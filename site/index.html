Command the campaign.

Sandtable is a digital adaptation of The Campaign for North Africa. You choose intent and issue orders. The computer checks the rules, handles uncertainty, protects hidden information, and keeps the complete record.

The eventual game

A commander’s week, without the paperwork.

One game turn represents one campaign week. Initiative determines who chooses the acting order; three Operation Stages then repeat movement and combat obligations.

  1. 1.0

    Read the theater.

    Inspect known formations, supply routes, reports, weather, and the limits of what your side is allowed to know.

  2. 2.0

    Set commander intent.

    Decide what matters: hold a port, preserve armour, threaten a route, or trade time for supply.

  3. 3.0

    Confirm exact orders.

    Staff turns intent into a concrete plan. Maproom exposes current legal actions; you review the order before it reaches the Umpire.

  4. 4.0

    Let the campaign answer.

    The Umpire resolves rules, seeded uncertainty, expenditure, breakdowns, contact, combat, and enemy reactions as those systems arrive.

  5. 5.0

    Study the dispatches.

    Chronicle records machine truth. Maproom reveals only the reports your side should receive; War Diary can later turn those events into human history.

Authority before intelligence

The model may advise. It never moves the pieces.

Sandtable keeps adjudication deterministic and treats every model response as an untrusted, versioned proposal. Hidden opposing state never leaves the authoritative plane.
