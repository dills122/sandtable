## Reachable branches and ledger consequences

Enumerating the literal RulesInput fixture over both Morale pairs, both assault pairs, legal retreat dispositions and all six capture dice produces **39 reachable combinations within the adopted dice-result and retreat/refusal/capture policy** of `(differential, required-retreat, refusal, raw-engaged, captured-role)` across differentials `−2/−1/0/+1/+2` with counts `9/6/8/8/8`. In every enumerated chart-result combination (excluding voluntary surrender), attacker final TOE loss lies `0..3`, defender `0..3`, capture `0..3` and at most one role captures. For `−1`, capture is absent; at `−2`, capture can affect attacker and coexist with defender retreat; at `0/+1/+2`, capture can affect defender and never coexists with retreat because the defender sum tests are disjoint. Raw Engaged can coexist with required Retreat; final relationship must honor retreat priority. Both roles can lose zero while either Retreat or Engaged remains actionable.

| Branch | Deterministic result and settlement duty |
| --- | --- |
| No required retreat | Record `not-required`; compute joint losses, optional positive custody, then Contact/Engaged from surviving original participants and final positions. No retreat CP/RP. |
| Required one-hex retreat, accepted | Record exact route before losses; charge defender `3→4` CP after losses. Both original units survive (`TOE≥7`). If the certified sole target unit vacates, award attacker 3 RP after its loss DP. Raw Engaged is ignored for final relation. |
| Required retreat, refusal or scripted deadline fallback | Record one unfulfilled hex; defender loss percentage increases 10 points before floor rounding. No movement CP or victory RP. A zero base loss can become one TOE. |
| Attacker or defender capture trigger | Draw that role's conditional share die, compute `ceil(final loss·share/100)` once, create one origin-bearing lot. If positive, resolve guarded relocation/one-TOE guard transfer **or** unguarded escape/reunion and retained future obligation. No duplicate casualty debit. |
| No positive capture | No custody decision, draw, lot or guard; still finish relationship and round closure. |

At this row state, committed attacker CP is `4+5=9`, defender `0+3=3`; fulfilled retreat yields defender CP4. Both begin Cohesion0, have no cost/retreat excess DP, and no source-exception `−4` differential. Each 3-TOE loss gives its role Cohesion `0→−3`; lower loss leaves 0. Fulfilled retreat gives attacker `min(10,−3+3)=0` if it lost three, or `0→3` otherwise. Refusal gives no RP. Final participant TOE is `10−loss`, with an additional **one** donor TOE transferred only if guard formation is chosen. Ammo remains zero. These operations are ordered by [result/settlement contract](../specs/combat-result-settlement-v1.md), [World7 contract](../specs/combat-world-settlement-v1.md), `CampaignCombatLossRetreat` and `CampaignCombatSettlementReceipts`; cause-specific CP/Cohesion receipts, conservation and future obligations must be retained.

## Subgate decision and remaining gaps

**Adopted chart arithmetic: pass for the 44-row input profile.** All five differential columns, all 360 selected cells including the explicit three-cell amendment, every enumerated retreat/refusal/Engaged/capture combination and the bounded CP/TOE/ammo/Cohesion equations have adopted rules and a deterministic receipt order. This narrower finding uses the all-row predicate audit and fixture enumeration; it does **not** select an RNG seed to avoid a bad outcome.

**Full source-legal settlement closure: fail/pending.** Land p25 §15.88 permits voluntary surrender, while [CMB-SET-001–008](../design/combat-settlement-disclosure-v1.md) offers only retreat/refusal and guarded/unguarded custody choices after chart losses. `CampaignCombatRetreatDisposition` accepts only `retreat`, `refuse-retreat`, `not-required`; `CampaignCombatLossRetreat.Losses` derives captive quantity only as a subset of chart loss. A 10-TOE participant electing surrender can exceed the certified `0..3` captured-loss bound and invalidate the at-least-seven-TOE survivor and one-guard assumptions. No source-backed timing, actor, resulting custody quantity, ledger effects, receipt type, fallback or bounded exclusion decision is adopted for this branch. Neither zero post-cost ammo nor the no-second-assault profile makes an already paid unit automatically surrender in this assault; that does not remove the separate voluntary option. This gap is present in the 44 candidate-positive states before any specific seed is selected. Do not call the 39 chart branches the complete physical choice tree.

**Ordinal-two executable certificate: fail/pending.** `CampaignCombatCertification.Admit` requires G2 ordinal one, and `CertifyInitialProfileFacts` requires `cycle.Ordinal == 1` plus initial-World comparison. Its `ProveSupport` uses current CP and covers all selected results, but no authenticated R03 ordinal-two caller currently invokes it. `CampaignCombatLossRetreat` projects typed receipts from a committed context, not from these terminal rows. The existing [result/settlement verifier](../specs/verify-combat-result-settlement-v1.py) checks first-round contract fixtures; it does not authenticate or replay any of the 44 R03 suffixes through an ordinal-two commitment. Guard upkeep and replacement training are retained as `retained-unimplemented` future obligations; any later crossing needs its own gate. One-hex route, custody relocation/reunion and participant provenance remain separate P01 subgates. Hence this report must not be cited as positive P01, native/public Combat admission or as a retreat-geometry certificate. The voluntary-surrender policy and its full settlement consequences need source-grounded adoption and a new exhaustive support proof before this subgate can pass.
