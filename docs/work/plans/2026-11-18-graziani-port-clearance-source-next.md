# Graziani port clearance: additional scope evidence

**Disposition:** Land rules §2.1 supplies additional general mode-scope evidence, but no newly identified official correction establishes a Land-only obstruction-clearance procedure, cost or timing. Keep clearance and recovery unadmitted; this is not a new policy.

The original [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 4 §2.1, describe §§6.0–32.0 as containing all rules necessary for the Land Game and §§48.0–58.0 as the Logistics Game, whose detailed supply replaces the Land-only abstraction. This supports the [scope review](2026-11-17-graziani-port-land-only-scope-review.md)'s caution against automatically importing §55's detailed procedures. It is introductory organization, not an express ruling that every effect in §55 is absent: §32.36 retains an efficiency condition and the scenario separately states Tobruk's status. Those specific references still need reconciliation with the general scope and conflicting initial values.

A different obstruction is specified in [Air/Logistics rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 28 / printed p. 24 §55.27: enemy air-laid mines reduce efficiency individually; a qualifying engineer in the controlled harbor rolls for each mine, removing it on 1–3. The text places removal in the Construction Stage and restricts that engineer's movement, voluntary CPA expenditure and other construction/removal work. This is a distinct mine procedure, not evidence that San Giorgio's ship obstruction clears by die roll, and does not establish mine eligibility or removal in Land-only play. It states no ammunition or Stores charge in this case; that observation alone does not settle other applicable costs.

The [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) §30.58 prints a reference to §50.2 for blocked ports, while §50.2 is an ammunition chart. **September 1979 errata §30.58 explicitly corrects that reference to §55.2**, as the [review](2026-11-18-graziani-port-clearance-source-next-review.md) subsequently verified and a [separate source check](2026-11-18-graziani-port-erratum-review.md) confirmed. This corrects the printed reference; it does not by itself establish which detailed clearance procedures apply in Land-only play. §30.58's personnel threshold is separate from §32.36's supply-unit threshold.
