## Trucks, supply and air

Scenario §60.43, PDF p. 3, assigns second/third-line truck pools to any Cairo hex (40 medium, 10 heavy), Alexandria (10 light, 20 medium), anywhere on maps (15 light, 40 medium, 5 heavy), and any air facility (5 light, 30 medium, 20 heavy). These are pools and placement constraints, not truck occupancy in every permitted location. First-line truck quantities accompany §60.41 rows; allocation and Land-only conversion require separate checks. General setup §59.44 constrains eligible on-map supply-truck placement; §59.63 treats air-facility trucks separately when air is abstracted. See [motorization analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-motorization-review.md).

Detailed-game §60.44, PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, locates supply at Mersa Matruh D3714, Sidi Barrani C4131, and Dump I; Dump I and one dummy may be placed anywhere in Egypt on Map C or D. Cairo and Alexandria have unlimited supply under that profile; air supply is distributed among air facilities. Country membership and actual choice are prerequisites, not inferred from map letter alone. Section 60.42 places planes, SGSUs and pilots at Commonwealth North African air facilities within capacity; this is an air-facility candidate set, not land-unit occupancy.

The [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-cw-extent-seeds-review.md)
notes that §60.5's facility inventory includes off-map sites; ownership,
eligibility and capacity still limit the candidates, and initially Italian
Tripoli/Tunisia boxes are not automatically Commonwealth options. Detailed
§60.44 also names a major repair facility at Alexandria and a temporary one
at Matruh; its Land-only replacement scope remains an interpretation.

For **Land-only**, §60.92(C), PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29, replaces §60.44: five regular supply units at D3714, one at C4131, three Dump I units together in any one Map D hex west of Matruh, and a group of three dummies under the Dump I rule; Alexandria and Cairo each have three regular supply units. Do not combine detailed and replacement tables. Exact eligible western hexes and group selections remain unproved. Section 60.93(C), Land and Air, adds two air-facility supply units and prints a `60.82 “C”` reference; that pointer needs separate interpretation.

## Scheduled movement into and out of scenario

Scenario §60.47, PDF p. 4, invokes the printed reinforcement track and prohibits replacement points before November 1940. The [Commonwealth chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf) §4.43a, PDF pp. 3–4, lists these GT/OpStage entries; none of its GT1–6 rows carries a `WD` withdrawal. This is a bounded observation of the normal schedule, not proof that no other rule can remove or move a unit.

| 1 / 3 | Polish Brigade | None stated |

Chart §4.43a defaults reinforcement placement to any Cairo hex(es) unless otherwise specified; same-parent arrivals may share a hex subject to stacking, while different parents must use separate hexes if possible. None of these six rows specifies another entry location. This identifies a candidate city, not a selected hex or guaranteed admission. Returning units may use Alexandria or Cairo, but none of these six rows is marked returning. [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) p. 31 §§20.12–.14 and scenario training requirements still apply. The Land §20.11 example conflicts with the chart and OA on 6th NZ Brigade's date; retain that unresolved conflict rather than assert an authoritative date without qualification. See [arrival scope and source crosswalk](2026-11-12-graziani-arrival-scope.md) and [independent timing review](2026-11-17-graziani-nz-arrival-followup-review.md). September [errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) §4.43a adds later entries, not a GT1–6 withdrawal in the inspected correction.
