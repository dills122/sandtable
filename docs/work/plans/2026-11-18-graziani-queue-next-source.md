# Matruh air-supply witness: Alexandria capacity bounds

**Date:** November 18, 2026. This bounded source check narrows one open destination-capacity gate in the [Matruh-to-Alexandria air-supply candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness.md) and its [review](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness-review.md). It does not select an actual setup, prove a legal shipment, or waive the other §32.37 conditions. Both examples require an independently established Alexandria major-city destination hex containing an eligible friendly division or brigade HQ.

## Source facts and bounds

- Scenario §60.92(C), printed p. 29 / PDF p. 5, gives Alexandria **three regular Supply Units**, each initially 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel. §60.41, printed p. 27 / PDF p. 3, lists the 2nd New Zealand Division HQ at Alexandria `E3613 &/or 3714`, without choosing its actual hex. Land §32.14, printed p. 43, states a maximum of **five Supply Units in a major-city hex**. Its wording for other hexes and [RUL-GRA-08](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-08-abstract-supply-stacking-bounded-planning-interpretation) remain separate; no general non-city cap is inferred here.
- **Three-unit bound:** if the only Supply Units at the actual qualifying HQ hex immediately before arrival are some or all of the three Alexandria units named by §60.92(C), then its existing count is at most three. Adding one Matruh cargo gives at most four, under the printed major-city cap of five. This holds without choosing how the three are distributed between city hexes. It is a conditional arithmetic result, not proof that no other unit is present, the HQ is eligible, or the cargo can legally arrive.
- **Additional initial units can change that premise.** Scenario §59.63(B) converts eligible second/third-line trucks into unattached Motorization Points in abstract logistics and ignores trucks available at air facilities when Air is abstracted. §59.66(C) permits each group of 30 other unattached Motorization Points in a hex to begin carrying an additional regular Supply Unit. §60.43 lists Alexandria with 10 light and 20 medium trucks, plus an `Anywhere, maps` pool of 40 medium and 5 heavy. A hypothetical placement concentrating the 20 eligible Alexandria medium trucks and at least 40 medium/heavy trucks from the anywhere pool in the chosen HQ hex could supply two additional initial units **if** those truck allocations, conversion, setup placement and supply-unit counting are independently accepted. With all three listed Alexandria units also in that hex, its count would be five before the proposed arrival; the printed five-unit major-city cap would leave no room for another. This is an illustrative contrast, not proof that all such placement choices are legal, selected or actually present. The source does not automatically place the anywhere points or extra units at the HQ hex.
- §59.65 permits initial supply overstacking only when unavoidable under scenario distribution and then prevents adding another Supply Unit while overstacked. It does not turn an optionally concentrated starting arrangement into an unavoidable exception by itself. The two hypothetical counts above do not require overstacking at setup; they distinguish two conditional pre-arrival states.

These bounds concern **Supply Unit counts**, not ordinary unit/hex stacking, source fuel, aircraft, city ownership or overall reachability. The existing candidate separately requires a usable Matruh origin, 12 Fuel from an origin Supply Unit, no enemy combat unit within five hexes of origin, the Commonwealth westernmost-division row condition, a qualifying destination HQ, an unused once-per-Game-Turn air allowance, correct phase and applicable stage movement lock under §32.37. Prior weather, optional placements and earlier actions can change the snapshot. A conditional empty-capacity result is not an actual shipment witness or proof of the other gates.

## Focused evidence needed next

1. Establish which actual Alexandria hex contains the HQ and whether it is a major-city hex, then count **all** real and dummy Supply Units under the accepted stacking/counting interpretation at the actual pre-arrival snapshot.
2. Resolve §59.66(C)'s application, the placement of Alexandria and anywhere Motorization Points, and whether any resulting extra units occupy that same hex. Record source units separately from optional extras and any later movement; do not assume three Alexandria counters exhaust all potential supply units.
3. Check legal origin and destination, prior events and every remaining §32.37 predicate before certifying a witness. Compare the conditional three-plus-one and five-plus-one cases with hidden-state-safe outputs and deterministic replay only after a ruleset decision. These are test ideas, not executed tests.
