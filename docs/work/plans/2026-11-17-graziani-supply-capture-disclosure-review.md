# Independent review: supply capture disclosure

**Disposition:** The [disclosure plan](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-disclosure.md) preserves the material distinctions in the checked rules. Its hold, audience matrix, and before/after tests are conservative implementation proposals, not printed disclosure rules. No source-backed reason found here to assert either blanket inventory disclosure or blanket secrecy.

- Land §32.13 says **any enemy combat unit** entering a Supply Unit's hex captures that unit, with its supplies used immediately and freely. Immediate/free use is an explicit consequence, not permission to postpone all use or simply hide an actionable outcome. The clause does not specify quantities, beneficiaries, stock debit, remainder, continuing ownership, or an inventory report. A selected procedure must give its intended user enough information and choices to perform the immediate use it requires; exactly which balances and which audience see them remain policy questions.
- Section 32.18 says dummy Supply Units are removed **upon discovery**. It does not list discovery triggers in that clause or specify capture/discovery event audiences. Do not equate discovery with combat entry or ZOC capture without a ruling, or suppress visible removal once the chosen procedure requires it. Different observable actions or outcomes can themselves disclose identity even without a real/dummy field.
- Section 10.16 requires the non-phasing player to tell the phasing player whether a particular unit exerts a ZOC if it could exert one into a hex and the phasing player's unit starts a Movement Segment there or moves into it. This conditional answer has a specified audience and subject; it does not establish general stock, identity, or spectator disclosure.
- Section 10.29 separately captures a qualifying non-combat unit alone in enemy ZOC at any time in the enemy Movement/Combat Phase if it has no strength of any type. It does not state enemy combat-unit entry or repeat §32.13's immediate/free-use clause. Whether an abstract Supply Unit qualifies, what “alone” means, and ordering against entry remain open. Mere adjacency establishes neither a qualifying ZOC nor capture. The section also restricts convoy entry and voluntary non-combat entry into certain enemy-ZOC hexes; legal occupancy still needs checking.
- Section 32.15 removes a real Supply Unit when **both** Ammo and Fuel reach zero. In the plan's partial/zero-balance tests, distinguish a zero in one resource from both-zero removal; do not treat a surviving both-zero unit as an ordinary valid state absent a separate ordering rule. Section 32.2 requires tracking points and names possible log sheets, but does not establish opponent access. Initial/maximum amounts do not establish remaining stock.

**Evidence and scope:** Independently visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 18 and 43, §§10.16, 10.29, 32.12–.18 and 32.2; local scan SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`. Same scan and edition as plan, not an independent edition or exhaustive errata review. No runtime or data changes; no gameplay tests.
