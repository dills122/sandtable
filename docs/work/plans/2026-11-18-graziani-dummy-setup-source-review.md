# Review: dummy tank placement and Land-only setup

**Disposition:** Supported within stated limits. No source-proven starting dummy-tank count, free placement, or survival rule follows from the cited cap and supply abstraction.

- Visually checked Land PDF p. 27 §§16.41–.43 and .46. Section 16.42 requires 10 Stores present in the placement hex, placement in the Construction Segment, and says there are no actual dummy-tank counters. Section 16.43 limits each player to three existing at a time and one in a hex, and bars Italian formations from using dummies even when mixed with German formations. These are placement, capacity and usage restrictions, not a grant of three at scenario start. Section 16.46's elimination conditions do not prescribe an initial quantity.
- Land p. 43 §32.11 tells players to ignore references to usage and expenditure of Stores, among other supply types, and applies the following supply rules. That is strong textual evidence against charging §16.42's ten Stores in the Land-only profile. The same passage does not expressly adjudicate whether the placement-hex presence phrase survives independently or how abstract supply would represent it. Treating either outcome as a completed rule would require an interpretation.
- Visually checked scenario PDF pp. 4–5 / printed pp. 28–29, §§60.7 and 60.92. The Land-only initial supply replacement lists dummy **supply** units: Axis two groups of three and Commonwealth one group of three, subject to their respective dump placement rules. Those are not §16.4 dummy tank battalions. Section 60.7's initial-construction text does not name dummy tanks; it does not by itself exclude another setup provision or general setup interpretation.
- A check of the available scenario OCR for `dummy` shows supply dumps/units and later minefield references; this word search can miss OCR errors, other spellings such as `dummies`, indirect instructions and unsearched errata. Even a perfect keyword search would not by itself establish a zero initial tank count. The note appropriately records initial number and location as unproven, with scenario and stage-specific predicates unresolved.
