# Independent source review: patrol details

**Disposition:** The candidate accurately records the principal printed patrol limits, procedure and disclosure boundaries. No material source error found in the checked cases. Its unresolved stage-history, cumulative point allocation, no-combat target and complete-loss distinctions are appropriate; none should be collapsed into an unqualified runtime rule.

| Check | Original source reading |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible points | Land §16.11 names recce type, CV/33, Mark VI Light, Panzer I, motorized infantry. September errata §16.11 additionally names Italian L/6, Commonwealth Stuart and mechanized infantry, including Panzergrenadiers. Eligibility is about designated TOE points, not the mere presence of a parent formation. |
| Limits and target | §§16.13–.15 set maximum **two points from any one origin hex**, maximum **three sent to one enemy-occupied target hex**, and exclude points from units at cohesion −8 or worse. §16.22 requires a target within five hexes and a path free of impassable terrain and enemy-controlled/occupied hexes other than a hex in the reconnoitered unit's ZOC. The text alone does not resolve whether the origin cap is consumed cumulatively over multiple patrols; §16.21 permits as many patrols as desired. |
| Weather and cost | §16.17 bars origin or target hex affected by rainstorm or sandstorm; it does not expressly extend that clause to every intervening hex. §16.16 normally costs one ammo and two fuel per point, at origin, regardless of result. Land-only §32.25 expressly makes patrolling require no supply expenditure. |
| Combat history | The §16.0 general rule says no anti-armor or close assault combat in the Operations Stage. §16.21 instead frames eligibility as the **phasing player** not engaging in either against enemy units; its target restriction covers a hex that has undergone any barrage or air bombardment, without repeating the preceding explicit stage qualifier. Candidate correctly retains these textual tensions and does not assert that a barrage elsewhere automatically blocks every patrol. |
| No-combat skip | §16.32 requires the non-phasing player to say whether the target has **no combat units**, treating AA/Flak and engineer units as noncombat for this check. On that condition, the phasing player skips **Patrol Survival Table** and suffers no patrol losses. It does not say to cancel reconnaissance and objective-loss resolution. This disclosure is not a statement that the hex has no units. |
| Survival / objective losses | §16.33 subtracts one from the survival die only if **all** points are from recce units; deductions including trucks are immediate and phasing player chooses unit type. No surviving patrol points means no information. §16.34 calls for an objective-loss roll after information or after all patrol units are eliminated, whichever first; loss choice is non-phasing player's, from combat units, else trucks, AA/Flak, engineer, HQ in that priority. If the patrol is eliminated in entirety, an objective-table Captured result becomes Eliminated. Do not automatically treat a fully captured or mixed captured/eliminated patrol as the printed “eliminated in entirety” case without a ruling. |
| Report | §16.51 requires historical designation, type, motorization, and “exact TOE Strength within two of the actual strength” at patrol time. Addendum §16.51 expressly says it does **not** identify component TOE point types, and its 8-point mixed tank example permits report of a six-point tank battalion. §16.52 gives non-phasing unit choice when more units exist than required, subject to §§16.53–.55; those cases set size preference, HQ/AA restrictions and phasing exclusions. Do not infer exact model composition, source stock or attached-truck counts. |
| Dummy tanks | §§16.41–.45 support defender-selected misleading tank identification and strength under their own conditions; no Italian formation may use them. Candidate properly leaves interaction between dummy-only hexes and no-combat skip open. |

The printed phrase about TOE strength is internally awkward: “exact” and “within two” appear together. A report of any legally chosen value in that range is not proof of actual exact strength; bounds, selection and publication protocol remain policy questions. Addendum §3.6's patrol disclosure withholds supply status and attached trucks except whether the unit is motorized. The reconnaissance table, survival table and objective loss table were not independently rechecked here; no numeric result, dice count beyond the inspected procedural rules, map reachability or information lifetime is certified.
