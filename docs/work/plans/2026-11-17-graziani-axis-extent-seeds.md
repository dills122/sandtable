## Air, trucks, facilities and initial supply

- **Air (§60.32):** aircraft may start at Italian airfields, landing strips, flying-boat basins and similar facilities within capacity; none start in Italy/Sicily and Crete is unavailable. SGSU placement has separate ownership/capacity rules. Scenario §60.5 lists initial air facilities; see the [facility inventory](2026-11-17-graziani-objective-supply-extent-seeds.md). Their eligibility and actual distributions remain conditional on ownership, capacity and mode. The [review](2026-11-17-graziani-axis-flexible-setup-batch-review.md) checks available types, pilots, SGSU and errata. Air is abstracted in Land-only mode; do not treat aircraft as land-counter seeds.
- **Second/third-line trucks and repair (§60.33):** Tripoli has 25 light, 140 medium, 40 heavy; anywhere in Libya has 30 light, 100 medium, 25 heavy; any air facility has 10 light, 50 medium. These are pools, not occupied hexes; §59.44 placement restrictions apply, and §59.63(B) excludes air-facility trucks when air is abstracted. Major repair at Tripoli; temporary repair at Tobruk and Benghazi. Facility names require map/box reconciliation, not invented coordinates.
- **Detailed supply (§60.34):** fixed dumps at Tobruk C4807, Bardia C4321, Benghazi, Derna B5925, Tripoli box and C0716. Dumps 1 and 2 and two dummy dumps may be placed in a Map C Libya hex not within four hexes of a Commonwealth unit. Additional air supplies distribute among Axis airfields where the applicable Air mode uses them; §59.61 excludes supplies and trucks at or for air facilities when only Air is abstracted. Each eligible area is a choice, not a selected dump or every-hex stock. Printed contents and dashes remain distinct; see original §60.34. Exact proximity, country membership, facilities and stacking remain to be checked.
- **Land-only (§60.92(B)):** replaces §60.34 rather than adding it. Fixed regular-unit locations: Tobruk 3, Bardia 2, Benghazi 1, Derna 1, Tripoli box 8, C0716 1. Dump 1 and Dump 2 each comprise three regular units, each group placed in one eligible Map C Libya hex outside the stated Commonwealth proximity; two groups of three dummies follow the dump rules. Derna ammunition is a printed dash, not an assumed zero. The [supply pilot](2026-11-12-graziani-supply-accounting-pilot.md) records contents; [independent flexible review](2026-11-17-graziani-axis-flexible-setup-batch-review.md) checks placement. Optional extra loaded motorization under §59.66 depends on actual eligible groups and location; it is not stock in every candidate hex.
- **Land and Air, logistics abstracted (§60.93(B)):** five additional regular supply units distributed among Axis airfields, distinct from Land-only. The printed reference to `60.82 “B”` remains unresolved. §60.91 lists no scenario-specific addition for air abstracted in Land and Logistics. Do not combine variant inventories.
