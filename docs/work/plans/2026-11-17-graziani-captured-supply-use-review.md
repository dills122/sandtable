# Captured abstract supply use: independent source review

**Disposition:** The [candidate note](2026-11-17-graziani-captured-supply-use.md) accurately preserves the uncertainty in §32.13's immediate/free wording. It does not establish zero depletion, automatic exhaustion, a range waiver, a CP waiver, or an ordinary supply debit merely by capturing. Neither §10.29's ZOC condition nor an attack alone is the enemy-combat-unit entry trigger stated in §32.13.

## Checks against Land rules

- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §32.13: a Supply Unit has no combat capabilities; entry into its hex by **any enemy combat unit** captures it, with its supplies used immediately and freely. The wording is “captured (and its supplies used immediately and freely)” and does not identify amounts, beneficiaries, physical accounting or duration. It does not expressly state a separate Capability Point waiver or whether “free” means no depletion. Note's candidate policies are interpretations, not source findings.
- §32.16 describes a **friendly combat unit** drawing within half its CPA along a route measured as a medium truck would traverse, except non-motorized infantry traces as infantry. It blocks traces through impassable terrain or enemy ZOCs unoccupied by friendly units. Capture does not expressly change the ordinary source's friendly status or waive this route. Even at the entered hex, a zero-distance geometric observation is not a determination of ownership, source qualification, or compatibility between immediate use and later triggers.
- §32.21 requires ammo for individual battalion-equivalents: non-phasing one point for assault, two for barrage, with phasing costs doubled; §32.22 says expenditure at combat. §32.23 says fuel-requiring units spend fuel the first time they move in an Operations Stage and additional fuel at the instant they exceed their CPA; §32.24 differentiates rates. Entry and the fuel trigger may occur in the same movement, so event order and availability matter. The quoted capture clause alone neither waives nor repeats these specific obligations, and no automatic refund follows from it. CP and consumable supply are distinct systems.
- §32.15 gives maximum 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel per Supply Unit and removes it when both reach zero. Conservation, immediate-use amount, and any surviving stock require an interpretation; neither emptying nor indefinite deferral follows expressly. Section 32.13's affirmative immediate/free phrase must remain visible when a conservative model cannot yet compute an outcome.
- Printed p. 18 §10.29 captures a non-combat unit if it is alone in an enemy ZOC during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase and has no strength of any type. The clause's other sentences address movement into enemy ZOCs. Supply Unit classification, meaning of “alone,” ownership, and the interaction with §32.13 require decisions. Mere adjacency is insufficient, and the entry-specific immediate-use parenthetical is not restated in §10.29. Printed p. 14 §8.13 prohibits entering an enemy-unit-occupied hex, with a cross-reference to §27.4; interaction with §32.13 is unresolved, not evidence that the entry event is automatically impossible.
