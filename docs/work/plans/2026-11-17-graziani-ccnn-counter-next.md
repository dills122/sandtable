# 1st CCNN headquarters: original counter check

**Finding:** the recovered counter scan identifies the 1st CCNN division HQ,
but its visible face does **not** specify a gun weapon system or starting gun
TOE strength. It does not close the existing source gap. Do not infer `65/17`,
zero guns, or a complete formation combat value from this counter. This is a
same-edition scan check, not physical-counter inspection, independent edition
or official clarification.

## Counter identity and marks

In the [counter scan](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF
**p. 2**, Counter Section **Nr. 3**, find the dark Italian Blackshirt block
in the upper half of the page. In the first row of that block, the first
counter at its left edge is immediately before `219Lgn` and `233Lgn`. Its
visible face reads **`1 CCNN`**, with **`(1CN)`** beneath; it has an infantry
symbol with **`XX`** above it and a parenthesized **`(5)`** to the symbol's
left. The face has no legible gun model, gun-point number, or separate
numeric attack/defense rating. Its black/green field and yellow text identify
the printed Blackshirt grouping; the meaning of every color or mark is not
independently asserted here.

Land §§9.11–.12 identify the parenthesized number on an HQ counter as its
stacking-point notation, with actual applicability depending on represented
units; `(5)` is **not** five gun TOE points. `XX` is the division-size symbol,
not an artillery model. The parenthesized text `(1CN)` is a counter identifier,
not the OA's `N` field. A face without a weapon model is not affirmative proof
the HQ has no weapon: the game uses OA sheets and unit characteristics for
TOE and the HQ can represent attached units. This inspection covers the visible
face in the supplied scan, not an unobserved reverse or all counter editions.

## Cross-check against original OA and chart

[Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 17
lists the 1st CCNN Division HQ as counter abbreviation `1 CCNN`, ID code `g`,
TOE/weapon field `N`, and arrival `D`. Its p. 4 key defines `N` for non-tank/
non-gun units as the maximum TOE from the characteristic chart, while also
requiring gun and tank TOE to be listed by weapon system even when assigned
to another unit. [Axis characteristics](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf)
PDF p. 26, §4.46b, give HQ `g` CPA 20, no intrinsic numeric combat ratings
in that row, and permission to assign **one artillery TOE point**. That is
capacity, not a stated initial gun or weapon model. The OA `N` versus the
weapon-specific listing convention is still an unresolved source interaction;
the counter's `(5)` does not adjudicate it.

The OA separately lists the 219th and 233rd Legion HQs and a 201st artillery
regiment. Their entries and nearby counter symbols describe **different
identities**. Their guns, equipment, ratings or points are not evidence of an
intrinsic gun on the 1st CCNN HQ. A division HQ can represent attached combat
units under Land §3.31; no inference that the whole formation lacks artillery
or combat capability follows from a sparse counter face.

## Errata and acceptance boundary

Visually checked [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf)
PDF p. 1, §4.44b entries and §4.46 note; also searched the four-page extracted
text for `CCNN`, `Camicie`, `blackshirt`, `65/17`, artillery and counter terms.
The checked entries do not identify a correction to this HQ's counter or a
weapon system/initial gun value. The §4.46 entry is a general note, not a
specific CCNN equipment correction. This bounded visual/text check does not
exclude OCR omissions, other rules, a later addendum or a different edition.

Keep both initial gun presence and, if present, type `unknown`. Any later
source or explicitly versioned ruling needs to address the OA `N`, the `g`
maximum and gun-listing convention without copying the 219th/233rd Legion's
listed equipment. Preserve a single HQ identity and distinguish its printed
stacking notation from artillery TOE, actual attachments and combat strengths.
No source content, runtime behavior or readiness flags changed; no gameplay
tests were run.
