# ZOC raw defense: gun position and ammunition

**Finding:** Land §10.15 explicitly tests raw defensive Close Assault Points and presumes **anti-tank units** Up Front for that purpose. Combat §§12.11–.12, .16 and .18 govern position and participation at a Combat Segment, but do not explicitly specify how to assign artillery position or ammunition eligibility for an earlier movement-time ZOC check. Counting all rated guns, discarding Back guns before a position exists, or assuming anti-tank's special presumption applies to artillery are competing interpretations, not established source facts.

## Source facts

Visually checked [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed pp. 12, 17–20 and 43, §§5.2, 9.16(a), 10.11, 10.15–.16, 10.21, 10.31–.32, 11.3, 12.11–.12, 12.16–.18 and 32.11–.22; the [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-zoc-raw-gun-position-review.md) additionally checked §15.12–.15 on p. 22; and [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) pp. 1–4. Same scans as prior work, not an independent edition or rights grant.

- §10.15 denies ZOC to unit(s) in a hex with fewer than ten **raw defensive Close Assault Points**, regardless of §10.11. It expressly presumes anti-tank units Up Front for this purpose. It does not expressly state an equivalent artillery presumption, ammunition test, or time at which artillery position is selected. Section 11.3 defines raw combat strength by TOE times the relevant rating before division by ten; §11.34 totals raw points first. This is a numerical threshold, not proof that every nominal rating contributes.
- §5.2 places movement before the Combat Segment's position determination, and §10.16 provides ZOC disclosure when a phasing unit starts a Movement Segment in, or moves into, a potentially controlled hex. Thus some ZOC determinations occur before the next combat-position step. §12.11 places artillery **involved in combat** Forward or Back as an entire unit; §12.12 says position can change only at the beginning of a Combat Segment. These clauses do not supply a default position for a movement-time ZOC evaluation, whether a previous segment's position persists for that purpose, or whether a hypothetical future combat position governs it.
- §12.16 says Back artillery cannot coordinate or split fire and makes a barrage-capable heavy-weapons unit whose guns are Back entirely Back and unable to participate in anti-armor or Close Assault. §12.18 permits Forward artillery to participate in anti-tank **or** Close Assault in that Combat Segment if it has the necessary ratings **and ammunition**. These are combat-participation rules. Neither explicitly says the movement-time §10.15 raw ZOC threshold is calculated from actual combat participation, nor does it remove the need to consider applicable supply and rating restrictions if another accepted rule connects them.
- The independent review identifies further **combat** conditions in §15.12–.15: pinned units and units retreating before assault into an attacked hex do not add defensive ratings; Back guns are normally unaffected by assault except overrun, Forward guns may participate; a unit must commit to add its rating; and an out-of-ammunition unit cannot assault offensively or defensively. These support considering a combat-eligible-strength interpretation, but do not themselves define the earlier §10.15 ZOC threshold or establish a hypothetical commitment.
- Land §9.16(a) calls Fort Maddalena's Italian garrison a heavy-weapons battalion in its stacking example. If its actual qualifying guns are Back under §12.16, the entire-unit combat restriction is material; the example alone does not say the unit has already chosen Back at setup or a movement-time ZOC boundary. Scenario, actual unit composition, any parenthesized rating and relevant ammunition rules remain separate. Do not assume a garrison's raw defense is always available or automatically zero based on a position not yet established.
- §32.11 replaces detailed supply usage in the Land-only abstraction with §32's own rules; §32.21 charges ammo for combat actions. The fact that a unit has a nominal defensive rating, or that its side has some supply, does not on these passages alone decide whether its points contribute to §10.15's pre-combat threshold. Conversely, §10.15 does not expressly waive ammo for artillery. Detailed-game and Land-only availability, unit draw, timing and known/unknown holdings require separate source and policy analysis.

Errata p. 2 §23.11 separately corrects engineer parenthesized-strength restrictions; no explicit §10.15, §12.16 or §12.18 correction found in the four-page package. This bounded inspection does not exclude later or indirect corrections.

## Competing policies and consequences

1. **Nominal available-strength reading:** count raw points from eligible present TOE and applicable ratings at the ZOC boundary without requiring a chosen future combat position. §10.15 names raw defensive points and specifically gives anti-tank a position presumption; this reading still needs a decision about ammo and other rating prerequisites.
2. **Position/participation reading:** count only TOE that could actually contribute to Close Assault at the boundary, applying a defined position and ammunition rule. This gives effect to §12.16/.18 but requires an extra default, persisted state or decision before combat; those details are not stated by the cited clauses.
3. **Proof-gated reading:** if the selected version requires unknown position, ammo, unit identity or special-rating status, return unknown rather than treating the points as zero or as definitely included. This is a safety/representation policy, not a source calculation.

For an illustrative hex with 6 nominal garrison raw plus 3 plus 3 nominal artillery raw, inclusion of all yields 12 and clearing §10.15's threshold; exclusion of both artillery terms yields 6, below 10. Whether those particular terms are available, mutually eligible, co-located or legal is not established by arithmetic. Either outcome also needs §10.11 stacking, cohesion, applicable exceptions and directional §10.21 entry/edge evidence. Anti-tank's explicit Up Front presumption should be tested independently from artillery and heavy-weapons position.

A future contract needs the authoritative snapshot and current phase, represented unit/TOE identity, applicable rating and ammo sources, position state or selected policy, version and replay behavior. Preserve raw evidence and unknowns; do not let hidden ammo or composition leak through legal-action lists, failure reasons, timing or subsequent state without an accepted disclosure protocol. That protocol must also honor §10.16's required disclosure of whether the relevant potentially projecting unit exerts ZOC when its stated movement conditions hold; do not suppress authorized ZOC disclosure as a generic privacy measure. Test pre-first-combat movement, position choices in successive Combat Segments, pinned/retreat/commitment cases, pre/post ammunition expenditure, qualifying and non-qualifying AT, Back heavy weapons, garrison/home stacking, exactly 9/10 raw, unknown values and side-safe replay. These are synthetic review vectors. No runtime, readiness, map data or gameplay tests changed.
