# Matruh assigned units and attachment capacity: source follow-up

**Status:** independent visual re-reading of the same scans and review of [RUL-GRA-03](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-03-matruh-initial-assignment-working-interpretation), not a new edition, admitted ruling, completed setup or runtime change. The adjacent Land rules clarify ordinary assignment and attachment, but no inspected source explicitly says how Matruh's exceptional errata-assigned battalions interact with its six-attachment sentence. Keep both capacity interpretations reviewable.

## Source observations

- [Scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF pp. 1–3 / printed pp. 25–27, §59.2 and §60.41: Matruh Garrison starts at D3714 with Essex, Durham Light Infantry and South Staffordshires following `Att`; §59.2 defines that indicator as attached but not assigned. It allows units **listed in the same starting hex** to be organized by the owner within formation and stacking limits, including free initial attachment/detachment. Section 59.2 separately permits a *listed unit exceeding formation limits* to operate, while preventing new subordinates from being assigned or attached; if subordinates detach, replacements may not be attached in violation of limits. This is not blanket immunity for an owner-created excess.
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF pp. 10–11: Matruh is a brigade-level headquarters, says no units may be assigned, and permits up to six attached units with reference to the maximum attachment chart. Buffs and Hampshire appear separately on the unassigned infantry sheet with `D` markers. [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1 §4.44b item 2, explicitly says those two start the campaign and Italian scenarios as part of, assigned to, Matruh Garrison. It does not state their attachment status, hex, or numerical interaction with the six-unit limit.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 9 §4.25 and pp. 29–30 §§19.11–.14, .2, .3, .4–.5: assignment and attachment are distinct. Assigned units can be elsewhere and can be attached to a different parent; a unit cannot have two assigned or two attached parents. Unless specifically stated otherwise, an assigned unit beginning in its parent's hex is attached by default. Section 19.3 says singular OA structures control assignment, not the normal chart. Section 19.4 and §19.5 describe extra **non-assigned** attachments over assignment limits. Errata PDF p. 2 §19.5 explicitly describes chart attachments as additional to normally assigned units, even if those assigned are elsewhere. That supports, but does not explicitly establish, applying the same distinction to the two exceptional Matruh assignees.
- [Common charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF p. 6 §19.5: Matruh's turns 1–12 row is six units, no tank and one gun class. The chart defines `unit` by full-strength battalion- or company-equivalent counter and separately permits division- and brigade-**equivalent** units two non-shell company-equivalents free of the maximum, subject to type limits. The OA calls Matruh a brigade-**level headquarters**; whether that phrase establishes the chart's brigade-equivalent condition must be expressly decided. Do not automatically grant the free-company modification, or silently deny it, based solely on its special row. Grouping, nested attachments (§19.46) and the three-company equivalence also require the accepted counting contract.

## Bounded policy and review questions

Retain RUL-GRA-03's proposed specific errata exception: Buffs and Hampshire have one identity each and begin **assigned** to Matruh, not merely attached. Treat them as exempt from the six *additional non-assigned* attachment slots while assigned to Matruh, whether co-located or elsewhere. Count the three listed `Att` battalions as additional attachments, conditional on the accepted chart counting, source strength and nested-unit rules. This relies on extending §19.4 and errata §19.5 to the newly exceptional assignees; the OA's Matruh-specific “up to six units may be attached” also reasonably supports a six-**total-attached** reading. The documents do not prove which wins. Under the alternative, count any assigned battalion only while attached and count it alongside non-assigned attachments, subject to other chart modifications. Under either reading detached assigned battalions remain assigned; they do not consume an *attached* slot merely by being elsewhere. Normal assignment-space accounting remains separate and requires a scoped exception to the OA's zero-assignment text.

Build setup in three distinct steps: preserve source-listed disposition and correction; determine actual locations from the complete source hierarchy, without assuming the erratum supplies a location; apply §4.25's co-location default unless specifically displaced, then offer §59.2 owner choices only for units actually listed in that same hex and within applicable limits. Review whether exceptional assignees are treated as listed in the parent's hex through source expansion; the erratum alone is not an explicit location. Do not assign Essex or Durham to Matruh merely because `Att` appears or infer their assigned parents from `23/70`.

Source-listed excess requires separate validation before using §59.2's special clause: identify the listed unit and the specific exceeded applicable limit, preserve the original over-limit roster, prohibit additions while its stated restriction applies, and allow normal operation. A setup action that creates additional excess is not made valid just because a different listed formation can begin over-limit. The text's restriction on replacing detached units is tied to whether the replacement violates limits; don't invent a permanent freeze after conformity. Resolve interaction of free initial reorganization with a source-listed over-limit state before permitting rearrangements that remove and replace subordinates.

Later reassignment matters: §§19.21, .26 and .28 constrain assignment; §19.26 permits specified Commonwealth brigade battalion shuffles but does not simply make a battalion independent. If an exceptional battalion is legally reassigned to another parent and remains or later becomes attached to Matruh, its exemption as a Matruh assignee under this proposal no longer follows; evaluate it as an additional attachment and validate all transition preconditions. Do not infer that reassignment or continued attachment is legal without checking those cases and the other formation's limits.

## Distinguishing counterexamples for admission

All cases are synthetic, assuming certified co-location, correct type, valid stacking and no nested attachments unless stated; they do not establish actual six-turn reachability or a source-listed initial roster.

1. Both exceptional assignees attached plus three non-assigned battalions: additional-slot policy counts three; absolute policy counts five. Both pass the numerical six-unit test. Detach one assignee: both preserve assignment, but absolute attached count falls while additional-slot count stays three.
2. Both assignees attached plus five non-assigned full battalion-equivalent attachments: additional policy counts five and passes the numerical test; absolute policy counts seven and rejects. This is a comparison vector, not proof five such legal units exist at Matruh or that stacking permits the combination. Both policies still enforce no-tank and one-gun-class limits.
3. Six non-assigned battalion-equivalent attachments with one assigned unit detached: additional count is six; bringing that assigned unit into its own parent passes the additional count but raises the absolute count to seven. Evaluate §19.41 co-location and the one CP cost **to each** unit for post-setup attachment; setup choice is separately free under §59.2.
4. Three scenario `Att` units plus separately located Buffs: do not default-attach Buffs at setup just because assigned; do not offer an across-hex §59.2 attachment. An actually co-located assigned unit defaults attached unless a specific exception or legal owner setup choice changes it. A non-assigned unit's later attachment normally costs two CP **to each** unit during the owner's Reorganization Segment (§19.42); distinguish §19.43–.44 detachment timing and cost.
5. A genuine source-listed over-limit formation may begin as listed; attempts to add or replace in violation fail, while a reduced, conforming formation follows the accepted normal rules. A newly chosen over-limit setup cannot cite that exception. Test atomic rejection, restore and replay.
6. Test two eligible non-shell company-equivalents under both interpretations of Matruh's brigade-equivalent status; test a third company and combined battalion equivalence, type restrictions and nested attachments with §19.46's §19.45 exception. Resolve counting semantics first; avoid treating every counter or every company as one automatic free slot.
7. A legally reassigned Buffs battalion attached to Matruh after reassignment is not automatically exempt under the selected policy; if capacity is already full, reject the transition unless a separately applicable rule permits it. Separately check alternate attached parent and detached assignment.
