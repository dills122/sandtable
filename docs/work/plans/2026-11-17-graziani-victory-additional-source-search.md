# Graziani victory: additional public source search

**Result:** No newly identified public SPI/designer clarification settles §60.81
convoy-route direction. This is a bounded search result, not proof that no
clarification exists. The existing [route proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-route-followup.md)
remains an interpretation; do not treat it as an official correction.
Search performed November 17, 2026, without contacting anyone or purchasing material.

## Sources checked

- [SPI magazine progress-report compilation](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv9/WIP_CNA_Done.pdf):
  contemporaneous material includes separately attributed development reports by
  designer Rich Berg and developer Marty Goldberger; the Goldberger report
  discusses a provisional movement sequence including transport/convoy movement. Searchable text checked
  for “Graziani”, “victory”, and “convoy”; no §60.81 directional clarification
  found. This is primarily prepublication development evidence, not later
  postpublication errata; OCR search can miss text and this was not a page-by-page
  visual audit.
- [Grognard game-article index](https://grognard.com/m-article-page-list.aspx?GameID=c045)
  lists September 1979 addenda supplied courtesy of Decision Games, plus an older
  errata entry and article index. Courtesy does not establish authorship. The
  [older text entry](https://grognard.com/errata1/cna.html) also identifies itself
  as September 1979 addenda; matching dates do not prove identical contents.
  Index listings alone do not establish completeness.
- [Moves 49, pp. 15–16](https://www.spigames.net/MovesScans/Moves49/CNAGPM49.pdf):
  Thomas G. Pratuch's postpublication game profile lists Graziani's Offensive
  in its playing-time table. Direct inspection of the accessible extracted text
  and searches for victory, convoy, Graziani and supply found no §60.81 route
  direction clarification. The author surveys game components and scenarios;
  this is not an identified designer ruling. Extraction errors limit the check.
- [CNA Living Rules](https://basmith.net/cna/) explicitly describes itself as
  unaffiliated, provisional, and incorporating September 1979 errata plus its
  own disputable rulings. It is useful for locating later interpretations, but
  neither a later fan conclusion nor its restated text independently proves
  official intent. No claim made here that its entire rulings corpus was checked.
- [Vassal module description](https://vassalengine.org/wiki_old/wiki/Module%3AThe_Campaign_for_North_Africa%3A_The_Desert_War_1940-43)
  says its charts incorporate September 1979 addenda. It does not identify a
  later official directional clarification on the inspected page.

Searches combined the game title, scenario name, §60.81, supply, convoy,
victory, errata, Moves and designer terms. Search-engine indexing, OCR, archived
or restricted forums, unscanned issues and private correspondence limit coverage.
No contact, purchase, or rights conclusion follows from these leads.

flagged attribution and source-identity limits incorporated above.
