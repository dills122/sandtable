# FG-01: Commonwealth warship identification in barrage

**Source result:** Land addendum **§12.23** says Commonwealth warships are always considered historically identified targets. Main §12.22 separately permits Commonwealth warships to be considered barrage targets, and §12.23 allows a barraging player to designate a specifically known unit rather than fire blindly under §12.24. The addendum supplies a warship-identification qualification to that target procedure; it does not itself publish a complete fleet manifest, authorize an otherwise illegal barrage, or prescribe a digital disclosure event.

| Source and trigger | Bounded source reading |
|---|---|
| [Land addendum §12.23](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 45 | The addition states Commonwealth warships are always considered historically identified targets. It names the target class; it does not list a ship's position, current condition, repair state or actual presence. It does not name a viewer or an announcement time in this sentence. |
| Land §§12.22–.24, p. 20 | §12.22 says Commonwealth warships **may be considered targets** for barrage. In §12.23 the barraging player states firing hexes; the opposing player supplies the number of battalion/company targets in each adjacent hex and each target's Gun/Armor/Infantry class, without having to state strength, exact type or designation. A specifically known unit may instead be designated. §12.24 otherwise uses blind type targeting and temporary IDs. Do not turn the adjacent-land-target count into an expressly specified ship manifest: the warship exception and its reporting format need to be read in context. |
| Land §12.0 procedure, p. 20 | At barrage resolution, the barraging player states the target by type/number or specific known ID and the Actual Barrage Points directed at it. The later declaration is separate from secret planning under §11.0 and the §12.23 response. It does not itself expose all ships, gun units or other hidden formation details. |
| Land §30.35, p. 42 | Artillery with Vulnerability Rating **6 or more**, and emplaced guns, may fire at enemy ships **in the same hex as the artillery**; the player notes that the guns fire at ships separately from any air bombardment. The case uses half raw artillery points as Bomb Points, with emplaced guns exempt from the halving. This is positive authority for a conditional gun-versus-ship attack, not a blanket land-barrage permission or evidence that any eligible pieces actually share a hex. Its same-hex procedure and §12.23's adjacent firing-hex report have different stated geometry; do not silently assume either provides a complete reporting procedure for the other. |
| Land §§32.71–.75, p. 44 | The distinct Land-only abstract Axis bombardment is limited to ships in **Alexandria Harbor**, plotted secretly in Commonwealth Fleet Assignment and executed in the named Tactical Naval Movement Segment, with section-E storm restriction and damage allocated among ships in that harbor. Its text does not refer to §12.23's land artillery selection procedure. No import of that procedure, attack availability, known ship location, attack plot or result follows from the addendum alone. |

Land §32.0's abstract mode is Land without both Air and Logistics; the independently checked [fleet-order ruling](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-15-abstract-fleet-attack-order-planning-interpretation) is a planning interpretation of the missing segment, not source authority to disclose a plot or fleet manifest. Existing [§§6–16 disclosure review](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-6-16-review.md) distinguishes secret planning, target response and resolution-time declaration. The warship addition does not override the partial limited-intelligence addendum's other timing or audience rules wholesale.

**Conditional discriminator:** If an actually eligible Commonwealth warship and a separately eligible Axis gun occupy the applicable same hex and the chosen rule contract permits the §30.35 action, compare naming the historically identified target with a blind temporary-ID land target. Establish ownership, location, weapon eligibility, range/terrain, phase, mode and current state independently; the sources inspected do not prove such a scenario action. Any side-facing projections, action options, logs or historic knowledge remain implementation decisions constrained by their own source rules.
