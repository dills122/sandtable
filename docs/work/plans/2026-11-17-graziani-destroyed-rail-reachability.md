# Graziani destroyed-rail witness: earliest reachability

**Disposition:** A witness could exist at setup only if independently established initial damage and Axis combat occupancy coincide on qualifying constructed track. A separately qualifying western rail use at the selected snapshot would demonstrate the restriction's practical effect; an attempted or completed use is not required for the prohibition itself to exist. The reviewed source does not establish that combination, or even an initially destroyed rail hex. It also does not expressly declare all initially constructed railroad undamaged. No earliest actual witness, first turn, or six-turn impossibility follows. RUL-GRA-26 selects a planning interpretation of a qualifying snapshot; it is not a reachability finding. See the [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-destroyed-rail-reachability-review.md).

## Source bounds

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28 §60.7, says the railroad runs to Mersa Matruh D3714 and ends there at game start. It specifies other initial construction and Tobruk's exceptional port efficiency, but does not identify a destroyed railroad hex or expressly state that all track is undamaged. A completed railhead describes construction extent; it does not by itself certify condition, exact per-hex topology, control, Axis occupancy, or an otherwise legal western movement. Conversely, omission of a damage marker is not affirmative proof that a specific segment is destroyed.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 15–16, 37 §§8.47, 8.76, 8.78 and 24.6–24.67 distinguish unbuilt track, constructed track and destroyed track. Unbuilt track has no function; destroyed rail cannot be entered by rail until repaired. The historical Benghazi–Barce line is excluded from use. Section 24.66 permits destruction by air bombardment or artillery barrage, or by a qualifying engineer, engineer-capable HQ, or infantry unit with at least three TOE strength points spending a complete OpStage in the hex without expending CP. It requires a destroyed railroad marker. This is a menu of mechanisms, not evidence any particular attack or uninterrupted stage occurs.
- Section 24.67 requires westward construction from the last completed hex with no gaps; §§24.61–.62 restrict new builders and time. Construction west of Matruh is therefore a separate possible history, not initial track merely because printed on the map. Section 24.65 restricts building and rebuilding enemy-controlled or occupied rail hexes. It does not establish initial damage or prohibit destruction there.

## Conditional chronology and proof gap

1. **Setup candidate:** Identify an actual constructed nonhistorical rail hex, independently establish its destroyed status and a qualifying Axis combat occupant at the selected snapshot, and identify a distinct usable rail hex west of it. Section 60.7 alone supplies none of those combined facts. The selected interpretation can evaluate such a proven state; it cannot create one. Initial deployed-force and map evidence would also need complete, source-backed comparison before excluding all setup candidates.
2. **Later candidate:** First prove a legal destruction event at a constructed rail hex. An eligible ground unit must satisfy the complete-stage and zero-CP conditions; artillery needs its own range, ammunition, target and outcome rules, and air bombardment needs applicable mode and outcome rules. Prove Axis combat occupancy at the selected evaluation snapshot, whether already present or arriving legally; to demonstrate a practical effect, establish an otherwise qualifying western rail use independently. The chronology may differ by method and side; no one-OpStage or first-turn outcome is established from the existence of §24.66 alone. In Land-only play, assess applicable abstraction and availability before invoking an air mechanism.
3. **Earliest claim:** Compare every legal setup and subsequent candidate under a fixed sequence, map, initial state, combat classification, destruction and timing policy. An example gives an upper bound only if every prerequisite is proven; exclusion of earlier snapshots needs exhaustive coverage. Current evidence supports neither bound beyond the start of the scenario. Six-turn horizon does not itself establish reachability or impossibility.

[RUL-GRA-26](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-26-destroyed-railroad-witness-bounded-planning) retains a proven previously constructed, destroyed rail hex as a §8.78 witness if occupied by an Axis combat unit; operational-witness remains an alternative. Both are interpretations: §8.76 does not expressly decide the nonlocal witness question. Neither makes the destroyed hex traversable by rail. West relation, use, qualifying track and snapshot timing remain separate decisions, as do path, occupancy, ZOC, capacity and privacy gates. A synthetic state distinguishing these interpretations is not a historical legal sequence.
