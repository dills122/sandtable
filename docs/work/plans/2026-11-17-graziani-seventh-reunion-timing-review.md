# 7th Armoured reunion timing: independent source review

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition:** Supported as a conditional sequence, not a movement or convoy witness. Under the stated default hierarchy and direct GT1 OpStage 1 entry, 1 RTR starts separate from its assigned parent. If legal movement after the first arrival brings them together and the assigned relationship still holds, §19.41 permits immediate attachment for 1 CP each; the first subsequent arrival phase is GT1 OpStage 2. No route, intact formation, or actual attacked convoy is established.

| Timing and cost | [Land §19.41](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 29, expressly allows an assigned, unattached unit to attach to its parent at any time in the same hex, charging one CP to each. Its example expressly includes either party moving into the other's hex and immediate attachment. Section 19.42 concerns a non-assigned unit and has different timing and cost; do not apply it to this conditional assigned reunion. Co-location, valid assignment, intervening movement and stacking remain independent conditions. |
| Stage sequence | Land §5.2, PDF p. 12, places Organization and Naval Convoy Arrival before each side's Movement and Combat Phase; OpStage 2 repeats the stage facets. Scenario §60.22 starts GT1 OpStage 1. Thus a reunion through movement after the OpStage 1 arrival cannot change that earlier arrival; OpStage 2 is the first **later phase**, provided attachment persists. This does not exclude some distinct, independently authorized pre-arrival co-location action; no such action is demonstrated. A phase alone does not prove a convoy arrives or is attacked. |
| Allowances | Commonwealth OA p. 6 assigns code `g` to 1 RTR and 8 Hussars and `d` to 7th Brigade HQ. Chart §4.46a, PDF p. 22, gives `g` CPA 25 and `d` CPA 20. Land §§6.13–.16 gives stage accounting, excess spending with disorganization, lowest-component parent CPA and no carryover. Brigade CPA 20 follows **only** for the assumed HQ plus 8 Hussars composition; recompute for actual attachments. Include each party's 1 CP and all prior stage expenditure. Land §6.26 also limits action at cohesion −26 or worse; exceeding CPA is not unlimited movement. |
| Shell and mode | Default shell conclusion remains conditional on actual attached hierarchy and qualifying strengths, as explained in [formation review](2026-11-17-graziani-seventh-armoured-convoy-witness-review.md). Later non-shell status needs both qualifying brigades attached at the relevant snapshot; 1 RTR's attachment alone does not establish it. Land §§32.61–.64, PDF p. 44, place the simplified convoy procedure in abstract logistics and its attacks in the Naval Convoy Phase. Apply that procedure only in its applicable mode; this timing check does not select a mode or resolve the 5% loss gap. Air plus abstract logistics has its own replacement procedure; see [mode review](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-mode-rounding-review.md). |
