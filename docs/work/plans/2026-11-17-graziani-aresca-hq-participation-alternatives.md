# Aresca HQ tank participation: alternatives

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition:** Source supports Aresca's nominal super-brigade equivalence and a shell at the listed attachment baseline. It does not explicitly decide whether Land §3.35's armored-brigade HQ restriction follows nominal identity or current shell-adjusted size. Keep both readings available; neither a CV33 rating nor a regiment label settles participation. This note selects no project ruling and changes no runtime data.

## Source and baseline

- [Land rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed pp. 6 and 17, §§3.31, 3.33, 3.35 and 9.21–.28: an HQ represents attached units and fights with their combined strengths; with no combat units attached it has zero stacking points, may use parenthesized ratings, is not a combat unit, and may not voluntarily attack. Section 3.35 says tanks assigned to armored division and armored brigade HQs may not be used in combat unless **no combat units are attached**. It does not expressly mention Aresca or super brigades. Section 3.31's combination does not itself settle whether the specific §3.35 restriction applies to the HQ's own tank.
- Section 9.22 explicitly treats Italian regiments at the next organizational level as brigade-equivalents and directs organizational identification by **printed stacking value** and §9.4, rather than designation or name. The [counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 1, shows Aresca tank regiment `III`, `(3)`; §3.33 says the HQ's printed number represents its full complement. [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) p. 2 §9.4 maps 3 to a full super brigade or division shell. Aresca's regiment HQ identity and full-complement number favor nominal super brigade, not a division shell. The chart calls any brigade shell one stacking point; this current value does not erase the printed `(3)`.
- [Axis chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 31 §19.32, gives the matching `(3)` tank regiment five tank-battalion assignment slots. Land §19.3 qualifies normal formation chart maxima for OA-noted singular structures. Axis OA PDF p. 6 lists Aresca HQ with one CV33 and four assigned battalions; it does not expressly call those four a singular maximum. Five is therefore the supported normal-chart denominator, subject to an applicable exception or correction, not an inference that five battalions are currently assigned or attached. See [assignment maximum source check](2026-11-17-graziani-aresca-assignment-maximum.md) and [review](2026-11-17-graziani-aresca-assignment-maximum-review.md).
- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.31, places Aresca at C3919 with all except XXI detached. At that listed baseline, XXI remains attached and is a combat unit; OA lists it at nine CV33, maximum nine for its `k` type. Under §9.26 fewer than two thirds of assignable battalions attached makes a brigade a shell; §9.27 excludes an attached shell subunit from the count for a full parent. One qualifying attachment out of five is a shell; four qualifying attachments are needed to reach full status. Assignment capacity, assignment, actual attachment and strength are distinct. Setup §59.2 allows same-hex organization within applicable limits, so a legal player-selected initial setup or later state may differ.
- Section 9.28 reduces a shell one level for close-assault unit differentiation **and any rule where unit size is important**, describing a brigade shell as battalion-equivalent. It does not say whether §3.35 identifies an HQ by permanent organizational type or by current size. A single current stacking point also does not by itself prove the tank can participate.

## Competing interpretations

| Interpretation | Attached XXI at listed baseline | Rationale and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nominal HQ category | Exclude HQ's own CV33 from combat under §3.35; assess XXI separately. | Read armored brigade HQ as an enduring nominal category, including super brigade; §9.28 changes size-dependent treatment without changing the HQ type. Strongly supported by printed full-complement `(3)`, §9.22, and the restriction's attachment trigger; super-brigade inclusion and category persistence are interpretations. |
| Current effective size | §3.35's brigade-HQ restriction does not apply while Aresca is a battalion-equivalent shell; assess the HQ tank under remaining rules. | Treat brigade status in §3.35 as a size-dependent condition governed by §9.28. This depends on that rule affecting the HQ category and on no other restriction excluding the tank. Do not automatically add its rating or assume the same answer when the parent becomes full. |
| Literal named HQ classes | Do not extend §3.35 to this named tank regiment unless another rule establishes the required category; assess remaining restrictions. | Emphasizes the text's armored division/brigade wording and lack of an explicit super-brigade/tank-regiment example. Must confront §9.22's express Italian regiment equivalence and printed-value direction; regiment name alone is insufficient grounds for exclusion. |

These are alternative readings, not different asserted scenarios. Even if §3.35 applies, it restricts **HQ-assigned tanks**, not all attached combat units. Axis §4.48 gives CV33 close assault 1 offensive / 2 defensive per TOE; the source-baseline nine eligible, unchanged XXI TOE yield 18 raw defensive points, while the HQ's one TOE would add two only if separately eligible under the applicable calculation. A weapon-system rating does not establish participation. Section 3.4's parenthesized-strength conditions and relevant errata, other combat rules, and the distinction between §3.35 combat use and a particular raw ZOC calculation remain separate checks; do not assert 18 or 20 as a complete hex or actual ZOC result. See [CV33 source check](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-aresca-cv33.md).

For an empty HQ, §3.35's attached-combat-unit condition changes, but §3.33 and parenthesized-rating rules still constrain use. For a full parent or another attachment, recompute qualifying attachments, actual tank strength, and other restrictions. Relevant unresolved questions are an authoritative classification of Aresca for §3.35, whether §9.28 changes that classification, any applicable singular structure or erratum, actual legal setup/attachment and strength, and whether the chosen combat restriction carries into the specific raw-ZOC calculation. A ruling must state these choices and test attached versus empty, shell versus full, and legal state transitions; no source passage inspected here establishes every answer.
