# Graziani Commonwealth facility setup: source review

**Date:** 2026-11-17. **Disposition:** placement and replacement caveats in [setup source check](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-facility-setup.md) are supported. Qualify its rounding statement and strengthen its per-hex evidence before using it as implementation guidance. The [independent planning proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md#commonwealth-initial-facilities) selects an all-hexes interpretation; that selection is a policy, not an unambiguous source fact.

## Findings

1. **Parenthetical and replacement scope remain choices.** [Land §22.31](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 35, lists Tripoli, Tobruk (for whoever controls it), “Alexandria and Cairo (all hexes).” The parenthetical follows Cairo; it can plausibly modify both cities, but the text does not explicitly say so. [Scenario §60.44](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed p. 28/PDF p. 4, places the Commonwealth major facility in Alexandria and a temporary facility in Matruh, without specifying an Alexandria hex. §60.92(C), printed p. 29/PDF p. 5, says Case 60.44 is replaced; its supply-status heading and replacement contents support a narrower supply-only reading but do not state that exception. Literal whole-case replacement removes Matruh's scenario sentence; §22.31 still independently lists Alexandria and Cairo. Scenario §61.36's Alexandria “either hex” belongs to Desert Fox, so does not settle Graziani placement. The planning proposal labels its narrower replacement and all-hex choices proposed; implementers must retain those labels and decide facility identity, placement, control and roll grouping separately.

2. **Per-hex grouping has stronger textual support than the setup note states.** §22.34 directs the reader to §22.25 for details of its percentage result. §22.25's field-repair example describes the percentage of vehicles of that type *in that hex*. This supports applying facility results per hex, especially alongside §22.32's facility-hex requirement and §22.35's hex-local supply costs. The example is specifically for field repair, however, and §22.34 itself says one die per type without explicitly stating scope across multiple facility hexes. Treat a per-hex rule as a supported interpretation, not an express standalone multi-facility instruction; test split lots and avoid inventing extra rolls from multiple facility identities in one hex.

3. **Single-point 10% exception has a chart/rule ambiguity.** [Common chart §22.8](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), printed/PDF p. 8, shows major-facility 75% on modified 0–1 and 50% on 2–4, as the setup note states. Percentages are outcomes, not numerical throughput. The chart marks temporary-facility 10% results with an asterisk and explains that a starred 10% result becomes zero for one TOE point; its major-facility 10% result at modified 8 lacks the asterisk. §22.25, referenced by §22.34, says to ignore a 10% result for a single TOE point without distinguishing columns. The note's unqualified exception should identify this discrepancy and require a versioned selection; test both interpretations at major modified 8. September errata §22.8 corrects the chart's final two die-modifier sentences, not this marker. Do not silently extend or exclude the exception based on the asterisk alone.

Destroyed tanks use separate rolls per point under §22.43 and chart §22.44; they are not the percentage lots above. These observations do not establish a fixed maximum throughput, an actual eligible initial attempt, or six-turn reachability.
