# Tobruk facility follow-up: source review

**Disposition:** Corroborate the [follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-facility-followup.md)'s bounded conclusion. The inspected sources do not settle post-control classification or establish an automatic transition. Retain the proposal as unadmitted.

- [Scenario §60.33](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, assigns the Italians a temporary facility at Tobruk; [Land §22.31](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 35, lists a major facility at Tobruk for whoever controls it. Neither passage specifies conversion, coexistence, or which classification survives a change of control. Land §24.81 also says major facilities already exist, which must be considered when evaluating a separate-emergence interpretation, but does not resolve the conflict.
- Land §24.81 distinguishes enemy-unit presence from ZOC and control; its “may be destroyed” wording does not prescribe a complete destruction procedure. Section 24.82 expressly permits engineer units, including HQs, to rebuild temporary facilities. Section 24.84 specifies rebuilding facilities in major cities whose city levels were reduced by bombardment; §24.87 permits rebuilding in enemy-controlled hexes. None of those provisions expressly supplies a complete recovery procedure for a temporary facility destroyed by enemy presence. Section 24.86 gives dismantling cost and proceeds, while §24.88 bars use during dismantling; neither states automatic conversion on control change.
- The [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2, supports the note's §22.34 and §22.8 corrections. It also corrects §25.15's reference to §22.34, relevant to the broader fortification/repair interaction but not a Tobruk classification ruling. No direct correction to §§22.31, 24.81–.88 or 60.33 found in that packet. This does not exclude indirect interactions or other errata.
- [Living Rules R-099](https://basmith.net/cna/rules/80-engineering) states scenario setup controls over the general facility list, without specifying a Tobruk control-change transition. [NJHarman's house rules](https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/) explicitly select temporary Tobruk and label that choice an interpretation. These are separate outside interpretations, not publisher corrections or evidence of designer intent.
