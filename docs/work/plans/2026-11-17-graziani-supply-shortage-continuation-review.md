# Graziani supply shortage continuation: independent source review

**Disposition:** The source allocation and shortage questions remain open. Land §32.37 supports the separate 12-Fuel origin-hex prepayment; it does not by itself establish pooling, self-payment, or shortfall outcome. Keep the vectors conditional and do not implement a default allocation policy on this evidence.

- Visually checked [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43. Section 32.37 limits air transport to supply units and one per player per game turn, during an OpStage's Organization Phase. It requires 12 Fuel before transport from any supply unit in the origin hex. It also states origin/destination type, proximity, direction and destination-HQ restrictions; the transported unit and its supplies may not be moved by the owner at all that OpStage, though it may be drawn on under §32.16. The sentence naming “any supply unit” is singular; it does not explicitly decide whether several units can combine payment or whether the unit being transported can be the source. Exact 12 is an amount witness, not proof of every other legality condition.
- Sections 32.15–.16 support separate balances, both-zero removal, Organization-only same-hex rearrangement, and friendly-source range for combat-unit draws, with trace restrictions. Section 32.23 places first land fuel expenditure at first movement and extra expenditure when CPA is exceeded; §32.24 identifies the applicable classes and costs. Neither passage explicitly specifies a tie-break, payment split, or a complete shortage protocol. Section 32.25 exempts patrol supply expenditure, and §32.24 also identifies movement uses that require no Fuel. The §32.0 general statement should not be read as a universal charge for every movement or combat-like activity.
- Scenario booklet printed p. 26 §§59.62–.66 describes initial abstract logistics and motorization. In particular, §59.66B provides for certain initially loaded attached Motorization Points to use their initial supplies before drawing from a supply unit. Its interaction with §32.11's abstract expenditure replacement and with the relevant Graziani setup is not settled by this inspection. Treat as a scenario-level applicability question, not an established override or proof that every expenditure must draw from a supply counter. Printed p. 27 §60.22 identifies Graziani's GT1/OpStage 1 through GT6/OpStage 3 span. These pages do not establish a general shortage algorithm.
- Visually checked [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 §32.0: its warning says the abstract rules were untested and gives no shortage algorithm in that note. No claim made about every erratum or subsequent clarification.

Fixtures assume applicable source type, event prerequisites and all unrelated legality independently established. A counter at zero of both resources is removed under §32.15, but timing against multiple pending claims and interaction with an air transport whose payment empties its carried counter still need a ruling. No inspected text establishes atomic rollback, simultaneous order priority, cancellation, partial action, or credit.
