# FG-01: Land sequence disclosure and planning

**Scope:** Visual census of the printed Land-only sequence [§5.2](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), pp. 11–12, including its Game-Turn preamble and all Operations Stage headings. It applies when playing Land without **both** Air and Logistics. The sequence explicitly states an initiative choice, secret combat planning and later probe announcements; many other steps specify an action or marker without stating all recipients. This inventory is not a digital visibility policy, a complete secrecy rule or proof an actual stage took place.

| Printed sequence and actor | Explicit source observation or its limit |
|---|---|
| I. Initiative Determination | Players determine the coming Game-Turn's initiative holder; whoever goes first in an Operations Stage is `A`, the other `B`. The outline does not itself describe a secret choice or an audience for each roll. §7.14 supplies the ordinary two-player rolls; scenario §60.6 fixes GT1 Italian initiative. Do not infer a draw from the outline alone. |
| II.A Naval Convoy Schedule | Axis plans specific cargo and routes, and future Axis replacements; Commonwealth consults its Production Table, determines replacement points two turns hence and plans arrival. The printed planning instruction does not expressly announce a cargo manifest or make every plan secret. [§§30–32 disclosure census](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-30-32.md) separately records §32.63's **arrival** lane announcement and §32.72's secret fleet-allowance roll. Production lead-time conflicts and initial preamble are separate questions. |
| II.B Tactical Shipping | Both players **plan** cargo transport between African ports. Axis coastal ships have counters; Allied coastal shipping is not counter-represented and is limited by port capacity. This sentence does not explicitly require a public manifest or establish that a move occurred. |
| III.A Initiative Declaration | The initiative holder **states** whether they will be `A` or `B` for that Operations Stage. This is an explicit statement of order for the two-player sequence; it does not expose unrelated intentions. |
| III.B Weather | Initiative holder rolls for weather. The outline names actor, not a complete audience protocol or actual result. |
| III.C Organization | Reorganization may attach/assign and detach units. Construction Completion removes/adds necessary markers; initiation/continuation notes working units. Training Completion notes units and Replacement Points completing a level and applies morale; initiation/continuation notes training units. Segments can be done in any desired order. These are actions, markers or records; the outline does not expressly publish a project identity, hidden contents or all training details. [Minefield review](2026-11-18-graziani-minefield-disclosure-source-review.md) and [§3.6 inventory](2026-11-18-graziani-limited-intelligence-source-inventory-review.md) separately address construction secrecy and entry-triggered reveal. |
| III.D Naval Convoy Arrival | Scheduled and actually arriving reinforcements, Replacement Points and Ammo Points appear at their designated ports or entrance hexes. Appearance is not an explicit permission to disclose their private composition, planned future cargo or every recipient's record. §32.63's lane announcement is a separate explicit abstract-convoy case. |
| III.E Fleet Assignment / Repair | Commonwealth assigns ships to sea or coastal hexes, then performs ship repair. The outline does not expressly announce the full assignment to an opponent. §32.73 separately has Axis plot an abstract fleet attack **secretly during Fleet Assignment**, executed in the same stage's named Tactical Naval Movement Segment. That segment is not separately located in this outline; [fleet-order ruling](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-15-abstract-fleet-attack-order-planning-interpretation) is a planning choice, not source-mandated disclosure. |
| III.F Reserve Designation | Player A designates units as reserve with a **Reserve I marker**. Physical status marking is explicit; contents of associated formations are not automatically exposed. [§§17–29 census](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-17-29.md) checks the corresponding reserve markers. |
| III.G.1–2 Movement / Breakdown | Player A may move eligible units and B may React if permitted; both sides check eligible vehicles for breakdown and broken-down vehicles are **indicated by a marker**. The marker does not automatically reveal its contents; see [marker review](2026-11-17-graziani-vehicle-marker-fog-review.md). |
| III.G.3(a–f) Combat | Both determine gun/armor positions; **both secretly plot** barrage, then execute it. Both **secretly assign TOE Strength Points** to anti-armor or close assault. A determines probes but need not reveal that decision at assignment; after resolving each close assault, A **announces** which assaults were probes. Anti-armor removes casualties and places Destroyed Tanks markers if necessary. The [§§6–16 census](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-6-16.md) separates earlier secret choices from §12.0 resolution-time target/Actual Barrage Points statement, §12.23 target response and §15.94 delayed-probe rule. Neither the marker nor an announcement implies the complete hidden composition of a formation. |
| III.G.4 Reserve Release | Player A may release reserves. No independent announcement wording or complete opponent-view instruction appears in this outline row. G.1–4 may be repeated subject to continual movement rules. |
| III.H, J, K, L | Player A moves eligible second/third-line trucks and POWs/guards; Commonwealth rail is available if A is Commonwealth; A may tow and repair eligible vehicles; phasing player may patrol subject to the outline's assault condition. The outline gives actors and actions, not a complete disclosure for each. Land §16.0 separately requires the patroller to announce Patrol Point count and target hex, with separate survival, reporting and objective-loss steps; see [review](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-6-16-review.md). |
| Player B; IV–VI | B undertakes F through L with `A` and `B` references exchanged; both repeat the first Operations Stage facets in stages 2 and 3, then the Game-Turn ends. Do not apply A-only disclosures to a fixed side when the role swaps, or assume a repeated contest where the source says stage facets. |

The opening planning, role statement, marker and secret-choice cases are distinct triggers. A printed marker does not authorize unrestricted inspection of its records; a silent sequence row does not by itself make every consequence secret. The [record-sheet review](2026-11-18-graziani-record-sheets-disclosure-review.md) and [partial limited-intelligence review](2026-11-18-graziani-limited-intelligence-source-inventory-review.md) supply separate boundaries. Detailed Air/Logistics sequences and scenario overrides need their own authority; none is imported here.
