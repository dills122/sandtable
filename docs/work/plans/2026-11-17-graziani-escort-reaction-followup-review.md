# Escort and Reaction follow-up: independent source review

**Disposition: revise before using the decision cases.** Source supports preserving separate rulings. Two phrases in the case matrix need tighter predicates; neither establishes a combined legality rule.

## Findings

1. **Declared assault target matters.** Printed §8.53(b) requires the adjacent moving enemy unit to announce a Close Assault **by that unit against the non-phasing unit**. The follow-up's “announced Close Assault” cases do not specify attacker and target. Amend both CPA 21/34 cases and the CPA 35 case to distinguish an assault by the adjacent mover against the reacting carrier from an assault against another unit. The latter is not sufficient by itself to satisfy §8.53(b). CPA 35 remains barred by the separate wording of §32.58(D), subject to its applicability and trigger.
2. **Define “escorted” at the relevant instant.** Section 8.53(a) says Truck Convoys **not stacked with friendly combat units** may never react. Matrix labels “escorted” and “unescorted” should state whether a friendly combat unit is stacked with the carrier when reaction is attempted, and should separately state whether the carrier is a Truck Convoy. Section 32.33's friendly Movement/Combat Phase requirement to begin and remain stacked is a different condition; it explicitly has no effect on normal non-phasing movement. Do not infer continuous reaction escort from the label.

## Confirmed source distinctions

- Printed p. 16, §8.98: attached trucks move with units in the owner's Movement and Combat Phase; unattached trucks move in Truck Convoy Phase. Either may be permitted to move in the opposing turn. Its capture-on-forced-excess sentence expressly concerns trucks moved in Truck Convoy Phase.
- Printed p. 44, §32.51: Motorization Points are Medium Truck Points in all aspects except modifications in Major Case 32. Section 32.58(A), (B), and (D) sets CPA 15, return to the last non-exceeding hex when forced to exceed CPA for any reason, and reaction unless the adjacent mover has CPA 35 or more. No assault prerequisite appears in (D). Its “for any reason” language makes a phase-overlap conflict with §8.98 material, while ordinary reaction occurs in the opposing Movement/Combat Phase under §8.5.
- Printed p. 15, §8.53(b): six-point differential at carrier CPA 15 begins at enemy CPA 21, conditional on the specified assault. Section 8.54's stated size exception concerns pinning and uses actual size rather than designation; carrier applicability remains unresolved. Sections 8.53(c–d) and 8.55 supply separate restrictions.
- Printed p. 43, §§32.32–.33: thirty points for real supply and six for dummy; attachment/detachment occurs in Organization Phase. The friendly phase rule does not identify a permanently fixed escort. Section 32.13 makes enemy combat entry into a supply unit's hex a capture event; it does not settle the full forced-return and cargo-ordering problem.
