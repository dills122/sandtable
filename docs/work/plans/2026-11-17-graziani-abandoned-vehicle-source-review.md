## Source interactions

1. §8.44 says vehicles except light trucks, recce-type units and motorcycle
   infantry may enter or leave salt marsh only by road or track. It then says a
   prohibited vehicle entering without using the track is abandoned, and prints
   cross-reference `5.33`. Identifying that reference with Logistics §53.3 is
   plausible, but not an inspected official correction. The road-versus-track
   wording in successive sentences also requires interpretation.
2. §53.31 starts with **“Trucks, or any prohibited vehicles”** in a salt-marsh
   hex and not on a road or track automatically abandoned. If read literally,
   “Trucks” may include light trucks, while §8.44 permits light trucks off road
   or track. The source does not expressly say the exemption from restricted
   entry also exempts them from §53.31 abandonment. The original note's vector
   comparing exempt categories should not pre-decide that outcome; preserve
   both candidate readings and mode applicability. Recce-type or motorcycle
   infantry are not automatically covered by the word truck merely because they
   are exempt in §8.44.
3. The surrender/capture clause requires an abandoned vehicle carrying
   infantry-type units **or** an artillery unit, plus an enemy combat unit
   within three traversable hexes counted from enemy to marsh and no friendly
   combat units within those same three hexes. The text places “at the end of
   the Movement Stage” directly after the no-friendly-combat-unit clause. A
   common end-stage snapshot for both enemy and friendly predicates is a
   reasonable candidate, but the grammatical placement does not expressly
   settle when the enemy presence is tested. The note's proposed timing vector
   should compare an enemy leaving or arriving before the checkpoint, rather
   than assume both tests have a printed identical temporal scope. Whether
   “those same three hexes” is radius or a particular route also needs care.
4. If no enemy is “around,” source says infantry is on foot and walking from
   the next Movement Stage and guns are abandoned. The range of “around” and
   a mixed enemy/friendly presence are not defined there. Do not award
   immediate captures merely from adjacency or conflate personnel/gun outcome
   with automatic ownership transfer of the vehicle.
5. §53.32 allows recovery by any engineer-capable unit in an adjacent
   non-salt-marsh hex, costs 10 CP to both units, treats the abandoned vehicle
   as having its full CPA, bars recovery if **either** unit is adjacent to an
   enemy combat unit, and attaches recovered vehicles to the recovering unit.
   This checks the note's transcription; details such as ownership, recovery
   timing, qualifying engineers and 10-CP aggregation are not settled here.
   §3.6 addendum keeps abandoned-marker contents secret.
6. Land-only §32.51 treats Motorization Points as Medium Truck Points except as
   modified, and §32.55 exempts those points from breakdown; neither inspected
   passage expressly equates abandonment with breakdown nor establishes that
   §53.3's complete physical-truck lifecycle applies in abstraction. Maintain
   distinct mode policy and marker identity; other vehicle types require their
   own classification. September errata p. 3 has no direct §53.3 correction,
   but that is a bounded inspection, not an official absence proof.

## Distinguishing vectors

* Light truck, medium truck, prohibited tank, recce and motorcycle infantry
  off-track; separately test valid road and track. Gate light-truck result on
  §§8.44/53.31 hierarchy and mode instead of making it an assumed exemption.
* Enemy at exactly three traversable hexes versus beyond, counted in printed
  direction; enemy enters/leaves before checkpoint; friendly unit within that
  range versus outside it. Keep route/region meaning and mixed states explicit.
* Recovery from adjacent non-marsh versus marsh, engineering eligibility,
  enemy adjacency to each endpoint, CP on both units, and privacy of marker
  contents. No automatic enemy ownership or unrelated capture inference.

No runtime changes or tests. These are conditional source-review vectors, not
proof of a legal Graziani path or incident.
