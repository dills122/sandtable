# Graziani fixed-hex six-side research coverage

**Finding:** All 33 distinct explicit coordinates in the [fixed-contact inventory](2026-11-17-graziani-fixed-contact-inventory.md) now have a six-side first reading and separate source review, including scenario-printed `B4827`. Both listed Alexandria hexes and all five Cairo location-summary hexes also have paired readings; Cairo `E1829` is already counted among the 33. Coverage means documents exist and address six sides, not that every side feature is resolved or certified.

## Method and limits

Compared the inventory coordinate list and city summary with local six-side notes and their review dispositions. A pair requires an initial note and a separate review inspecting its source observations; review may identify unresolved discrepancies or corrections. Reviews are informed checks of the same scan, not blind transcriptions or independent map editions. Links below identify the evidence, not official map or scenario errata. The [inventory review](2026-11-17-graziani-fixed-contact-inventory-review.md) independently checks the C-coordinate list but does not independently certify all other setup entries.

Separate scenario setup review is still needed; no inference about occupancy, ZOC, movement, contact or gameplay follows from this coverage audit.
