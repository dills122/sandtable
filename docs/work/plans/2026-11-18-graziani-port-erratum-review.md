**Result:** The corrected [port source note](2026-11-18-graziani-port-clearance-source-next.md) accurately identifies the September 1979 correction. Visually inspected [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2, right column, §30.58. Independently inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 42, §30.58: the printed reference for ports blocked by mines and similar causes is §50.2. Section 30.58 concerns personnel transport capacity and states a separate threshold for that purpose; this correction changes the cited case number, not its other wording.

The source note's distinction between this corrected cross-reference and unresolved Land-only clearance scope is warranted. This check does not independently verify every §55 provision, establish that all detailed Logistics procedures apply to abstract play, or rule out other corrections. Its observation that the earlier note and review incorrectly described the cross-reference as uncorrected is consistent with the corrected review's record; this is an informed reread, not a separate edition or blind transcription.
