# Graziani victory: public source search review

Reviewed November 17, 2026. No contact or purchase. All six cited URLs opened; the Vassal URL redirected to its corresponding retired wiki page.

## Findings

- **Attribution needs precision.** The [Grognard index](https://grognard.com/m-article-page-list.aspx?GameID=c045) says the September 1979 PDF is available *courtesy of* Decision Games; this does not itself attribute authorship or issuance to Decision Games. Prefer “available courtesy of Decision Games.” Its July 2021 posting date is not the addenda date.
- **Same title does not establish identical content.** The [older text entry](https://grognard.com/errata1/cna.html) identifies itself as “ADDENDA: As of September 1979,” and the index gives the PDF the same date. That supports describing them as entries for September 1979 addenda, but does not independently establish that their contents are identical or that no other dated clarification exists. No full comparison performed.
- **Berg/Goldberger wording could imply both authored discussion.** The [progress-report compilation](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv9/WIP_CNA_Done.pdf) contains a Berg-signed report discussing Axis convoy rules and identifies Goldberger as developer; another report discusses both men and anti-convoy air effort. These establish relevant development context, not necessarily that both men each wrote about convoy movement. The compilation is period material, mostly development reporting, not a later ruling. OCR is incomplete evidence.

## Corroborated scope

The [Moves 49 reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Campaign_for_North_Africa) names Pratuch, February–March 1980, and pages 15–16; article itself not inspected. The [Living Rules](https://basmith.net/cna/) explicitly says unaffiliated, provisional, September 1979 errata applied, and rulings disputable. The [Vassal page](https://vassalengine.org/wiki_old/wiki/Module%3AThe_Campaign_for_North_Africa%3A_The_Desert_War_1940-43) records September 1979 errata incorporated into charts and tables; this is module-maintainer evidence, not official rules authority.

Targeted web searches for §60.81, Graziani, convoy, victory, and errata produced no identified official directional correction. Searches and cited sources support only the bounded report of no newly identified clarification, not proof of absence or official intent. The Living Rules learning page offers a supply interpretation, but its unaffiliated status makes it no independent official correction. Search indexing, OCR, unavailable period material, forums, and private correspondence remain gaps.
