# Independent review: abstract supply capture policy alternatives

**Assessment: suitable decision material, not executable authorization.** The comparison preserves the distinct entry and ZOC triggers and labels consequential choices as policy. It does not establish a reachable Graziani capture, durable ownership, stock transfer, a discovery trigger, or a complete immediate-use operation. Keep capture unsupported until a compatible set of rulings defines an executable outcome.

## Findings and decision gates

1. **Immediate use needs a computable, non-vacuous result.** Land §32.13 states capture on enemy combat-unit entry and use of its supplies immediately and freely. Both allocation and charge-waiver alternatives still need to define what use occurs, who receives it, what “free” means, and how balances and remainder are treated. A zero allocation or waiver with no actual immediate use cannot silently satisfy the clause; if no compatible use can be computed, retain the unsupported gate. Neither ordinary expenditure nor full exhaustion follows automatically from the passage. Test partial and zero balances, no eligible recipient, and replay with exact allocations and accounting under the chosen rule.
2. **Exposure timing requires more than naming atomic events.** Section 10.29 says a qualifying unit is captured at any time during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase. Checking only before and after a compound operation can miss an intermediate exposure. Define state transitions, initial phase entry and phase exit precisely, including a unit already exposed when the phase begins; make relevant intermediate states observable to the rule, and test exposure created and removed within one larger action. Rechecking predecessor state needs deduplication and must not retroactively capture after a phase boundary.
3. **Sequencing and identity are policy decisions, not source tie-breaks.** Section 10.29 does not expressly repeat §32.13's immediate/free-use phrase. A strictly earlier ZOC capture therefore needs its own selected outcome before later entry can be adjudicated; owner and remaining target status cannot be assumed. For simultaneous predicates, combined resolution and ordered subevents are possible policies, but the latter must spell out which predecessor each rule sees and whether actual entry still targets an enemy Supply Unit. Assert one capture per identity and one required use where applicable, including retry and replay; do not treat this engineering invariant as printed text.
4. **Dummy discovery is unresolved.** Section 32.18 removes a dummy upon discovery, without enumerating discovery triggers in that passage. Neither enemy entry nor ZOC alone proves discovery on the reviewed text. Specify what observation discovers it, when removal occurs, and what each side may learn; do not create real stock for a dummy. Compare authorized observations across otherwise equivalent real and dummy histories, including legal-action availability, rejection/error behavior, event payloads and intelligence views. If withholding execution itself changes observable behavior, define a disclosure-safe unsupported path before admission.
5. **Entry and ZOC eligibility remain independent gates.** Section 32.13 specifies *any enemy combat unit entering*; neither adjacent presence nor non-combat entry is sufficient for that trigger. Section 10.29 separately requires an eligible non-combat unit alone in enemy ZOC with no strength of any type during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase. Its convoy and voluntary-entry restrictions matter to a legal witness. Test actual ZOC rather than adjacency, cargo and other occupants under chosen definitions, and an actual defender under the selected §8.13 entry interpretation. Synthetic states do not establish reachability.
6. **HQ precedent is narrow.** Section 3.36 requires an HQ with no combat values, parenthesized or otherwise, in a hex without combat units, placed in enemy combat-unit ZOC; it specifies instant capture, no CP expenditure for that capture, and one Prisoner Point. Its test should vary those predicates independently. It does not define §10.29's “alone” or establish a supply-capture cost, ownership or consequence.

The alternatives are deliberately incomplete. Version selected definitions, event ordering, disclosure and accounting together; reject policy combinations that cannot fulfill every applicable trigger. Record authoritative predecessor, phase, target, actual ZOC, capture grounds, discovery and use outcome; do not expose hidden identity or opposing stock through derived output.
