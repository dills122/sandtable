# Matruh initial supply: full-counter and air-payment boundary

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Bounded follow-up to the [Matruh-to-Alexandria abstract-air candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness.md), its [review](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness-review.md), and the [shortage continuation](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-shortage-continuation.md). Those notes establish a conditional separate-counter payment and preserve same-hex rearrangement as a separate event. This check derives what **initially full counters** imply for that candidate; it does not certify the move or select a transaction policy.

Scenario §60.92(C) replaces the detailed Commonwealth supply setup for Land-only and lists five regular Supply Units at D3714, each with 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel. Land §32.15 sets those same amounts as the initial and continuing maximum for each Supply Unit, allows no-cost rearrangement between Supply Units in the same hex during Organization, and removes a unit when both contents reach zero. Therefore, immediately after that listed placement and before any expenditure or other stock change, the five listed counters have **200 Ammo and 300 Fuel**, and none has spare Ammo or Fuel capacity. A capacity-respecting rearrangement among just those five cannot increase the balance of any one of them from its maximum. This does not prevent an offsetting exchange with no net balance change, nor does it exclude a separately established extra counter or earlier change.

For the existing conditional candidate, designate one counter as cargo and a different full counter as §32.37's 12-Fuel origin payment source. After that single debit, the source retains 40 Ammo and 48 Fuel; cargo retains 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel. The five-counter total is **200 Ammo and 288 Fuel** before any other change. No counter reaches zero of both resources, so this specific debit does not invoke §32.15 removal. An otherwise permitted subsequent rearrangement among co-located counters could refill the payer by moving 12 Fuel from another counter with sufficient stock, while leaving total Fuel at 288; that other counter would then hold 48. Whether a particular rearrangement may be interleaved after the required prepayment and before movement is not expressly decided here. Once cargo has moved away, the text's same-hex condition no longer supports transferring between that cargo and counters left at origin. A phase label alone does not prove either operation happened or establish a legal interleaving.

This arithmetic does not establish that the five-unit placement is legal under a particular stacking interpretation, that no other counters occupy Matruh, or that source and cargo retain initial balances until a chosen event. Destination capacity, HQ, enemy proximity, directional limit, allowance and actual sequence remain separate gates in the witness. Nor does the example resolve whether cargo may pay itself, multiple counters may split a payment, shortages, contested sources, or rearrangement and payment atomicity. If an earlier expenditure creates headroom, or an additional co-located unit has room, the full-counter observation no longer characterizes the whole stack.
