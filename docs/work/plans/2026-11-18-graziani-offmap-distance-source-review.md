# Ismailia off-map distance: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Independently rechecked the [off-map distance note](2026-11-18-graziani-offmap-distance-source.md) against the same original Air/Logistics, Land and scenario scans. **Disposition:** its distinctions between chosen flight path, chart shortest distance and western off-map land travel are supported. No material source correction found.

- Air §37.12, printed/PDF p. 7, counts a player-chosen path from base/air facility to mission target against the plane's range. Section 37.11 states range per direction and a transfer-mission exception. This is not a ground route. Section 36.5 describes off-map air facilities and their exceptions; §36.53 on PDF p. 24 lists Ismailia's `E3433` entry/exit and distance **9**. These cases do not themselves correct the worked example's intermediate wording.
- The p. 24 shortest-distance example expressly distinguishes the shortest calculation from actual flight. It prints four 33-hex terms through `B2629`, `C2629`, `D2629`, `E2629`, then **zero** northwest to `E3425`, 8 to `E3433`, and 9 east to Ismailia, total 149. The adjacent map/registration [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-map-registration-source-next-review.md) confirms the distinct `E2629` and `E3425` cells. The final off-map air leg agrees with §36.53; that agreement does not repair the zero term or identify an Ismailia ground connection.
- Air §37.42, PDF p. 25, is headed Land Distance Chart; its footnote states shortest distance excluding one endpoint and including the other, assuming an all-sea hex grid when necessary, and supplies the 33-hex adjacent-section same-number convention. Ismailia is not a matrix entry. The footnote's scope is that matrix; applying its endpoint count to every off-map air leg or actual chosen flight remains an inference.
- Land §§8.81–.85, printed/PDF p. 16, describe the Tripoli/Tunisia boxes and stage-based western land movement and map entry. Air PDF p. 24 prints the related §8.89 table for Tunis, Gabes, Tripoli, Tripolitania and Nofilia, by CPA category, in Operations Stages; Ismailia is not in that table. Scenario §60.5, printed p. 28 / PDF p. 4, lists Ismailia among **off-map** airfields. Neither western land table nor that facility entry supplies an eastern land route.

No source here selects a correction for the example, global sheet identity, an actual flight or Land-only §32.37 supply transport. The note appropriately limits its negative findings to inspected passages and does not claim later corrections cannot exist.
