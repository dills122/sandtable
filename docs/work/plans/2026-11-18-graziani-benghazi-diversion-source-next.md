# AR-07: Benghazi diversion witness boundary

**Result:** No eligible six-turn Axis whole-unit arrival was identified in the inspected sources. The original Axis reinforcement schedule begins GT9/OpStage 2; scenario §60.37 directs reinforcements to that track, while GT6/OpStage 3 ends Graziani. This bounds the ordinary schedule, not every possible exceptional introduction. A planned replacement or Supply Unit convoy is not itself the new whole-unit reinforcement required by §20.15. Do not enable or exclude this diversion for every scenario state on this evidence.

## Source checks and exact gate

Read the prior [independent Benghazi diversion review](2026-11-17-graziani-axis-benghazi-arrival-review.md) first. I re-inspected the original [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) pp. 6 and 31, [Axis schedule](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 2, [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF pp. 3–4 and [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF pp. 1–3:

- Land §20.15's condition is **no Commonwealth land combat units west of Mersa Matruh, inclusive**. It allows the Axis player to divert one battalion-equivalent combat unit or two company-equivalent combat units, with up to ten truck or Motorization Points assigned to arrive with that unit or its parent. It also forbids exceeding stacking restrictions or using Benghazi when its efficiency is worse than level 3, and says that use affects capacity that Operations Stage. It does not specify the package's reset frequency or exact capacity debit.
- Land §§20.0–.12 distinguish whole-unit reinforcements from TOE replacement points and place reinforcement entry at the beginning of Naval Convoy Arrival. Section 20.21 independently permits planned Axis replacement-point arrivals; that is not an identified whole-unit track entry. Scenario §60.37 plans September convoy runs on lanes 2, 3 and 6 and separately invokes the reinforcement track; a planned convoy is not a particular delivery or automatic §20.15-eligible unit.
- Axis §4.43b's first printed entry is Italian XXV Corps Artillery Regiment, GT9/OpStage 2, outside §60.22's six-turn horizon. Scenario §60.31 places units including the Benghazi Garrison, 2nd and 3rd CCNN Divisions at Tripoli and 4/10 Army in Tripolitania at **initial deployment**; their placement or later movement is not an arrival from the reinforcement track. The two Autoblinda 40 TOE points in the same setup are optional attachments or replacement points, not a demonstrated new whole-unit reinforcement.
- Errata p. 1 §4.44b provides missing OA sheets and an initial Matruh assignment; it does not print a six-turn Axis whole-unit schedule addition in that entry. The §4.43a schedule additions are Commonwealth GT28/64 entries. On p. 2 the sequential §20 entries inspected jump from §20.3 to §20.62; no direct §20.15 correction appears there. Page 3 §55.11 directs port handling to chart §55.3 and §55.3 discusses excess tonnage to Tripoli; neither explicitly resolves the separate §20.15 diversion condition. The existing [port comparison](2026-11-17-graziani-axis-benghazi-arrival-review.md) retains the conflict with the chart's general reinforcement-capacity language. This is not a search of later errata or all indirect exceptions.

## A bounded denial candidate, not an arrival witness

Scenario §60.41 lists the Matruh Garrison at **D3714**, with named infantry attachments; §60.5 identifies Mersa Matruh as D3714. Land §3.23 lists infantry-type units as combat units, and §3.31 says an HQ with attached combat units is considered a combat unit. Therefore, **if** an accepted initial formation record preserves an eligible Commonwealth land combat unit at D3714 at the actual diversion check, that unit lies at Matruh itself and the inclusive condition fails. This comparison needs no cross-sheet west ordering. It does not establish a legal in-window Axis reinforcement, the exact timing of a check, a completed setup, or continued presence after organization and movement. Matruh's assignment and attachment interpretation remains subject to [RUL-GRA-03 and its source conflicts](2026-11-12-graziani-formation-setup-conflicts.md); do not infer present attachments from unit names alone.

The positive condition is much stronger: an eligible actual arrival **and** absence of every qualifying Commonwealth land combat unit in the applicable west-of/inclusive region at the relevant state, plus package, stacking and efficiency requirements. A handful of observed empty hexes cannot prove absence. Hex ordering across sheets, the geographic interpretation of “west,” relevant off-map scope and unit classification require source-backed boundaries. Unknown location, map coverage or unit status remains unknown, not a negative finding. Separate candidate later arrivals, delays, alternative setup and optional introductions must be sourced before asserting the six-turn window has no possible whole-unit witness.

## Privacy and decision boundary

The trigger can depend on opposing information. An authoritative check may inspect complete state, but a player-facing action, error, timing, event or log must not disclose an otherwise hidden unit merely through availability or denial. Define the authorized disclosure and test indistinguishable visible histories before exposing the decision; this is an implementation requirement, not a printed disclosure exception. Capacity precedence, applicability of detailed port rules in Land-only mode, trigger scope, and disclosure remain unresolved decisions. No ruling is selected here.
