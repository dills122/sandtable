# Review: §32.3 supply movement source census

**Disposition:** All printed numbered cases §32.31–.37 are accounted for; no §32.38 or §32.39 appears before the §32.4 heading on the inspected page. One §32.37 wording qualification is needed. Coverage is not rule acceptance or an actual scenario action.

## Finding

The census describes §32.37's transported Supply Unit and supplies as not movable “**further**” in the OpStage. The printed case says they may not be moved **at all by the owning player that OpStage**, although draw under §32.16 is allowed. “Further” can imply only post-air movement is prohibited and leave earlier same-stage movement unrestricted. Preserve the source's whole-stage wording; whether and how a prior movement prevents the air action, and exact event ordering, remain interpretation questions. The [air witness review](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness-review.md) also records the full-stage restriction.
