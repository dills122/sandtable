## Mode and case census

Land §32.0 applies when playing the Land Game **without Air and Logistics** and introduces abstract Supply Units and Motorization Points; §32.11 says ordinary ammo/fuel/stores/water usage and expenditure are ignored and only the following supply rules apply. It does not itself establish an identical procedure for every mixed-game mode. September errata §32.0 calls the abstract rules untested; that is a source warning, not a correction resolving any particular case.
