# ZOC raw strength and gun position: independent review

**Disposition:** Candidate correctly distinguishes §10.15's anti-tank Up Front presumption from an unstated artillery presumption, and combat-segment position/participation rules from a movement-time ZOC check. Nominal strength arithmetic is not proof of eligible contributions. Two further close-assault cases, §15.12–.15, sharpen the competing readings but do not themselves specify how to compute an earlier §10.15 threshold.

## Original-source checks

- Land PDF/printed pp. 17–18 §§10.11, 10.15–.16: units in a hex with less than ten raw defensive Close Assault Points do not project ZOC, regardless of §10.11; **anti-tank units** are presumed Up Front **for this purpose**. The passage does not explicitly extend that presumption to ordinary artillery, state a separate ammunition requirement, or specify a pre-combat artillery position. It also does not say a currently unselected artillery position automatically contributes zero. §10.16 requires disclosure of whether a potentially projecting unit exerts ZOC when a phasing unit begins a Movement Segment in, or moves into, the relevant hex; privacy contracts must preserve that affirmative disclosure where applicable.
- §5.2 p. 12 places movement ahead of combat position determination. §12.11–.12 p. 20 positions artillery involved in combat as entire units Forward or Back, with changes only at the beginning of a Combat Segment. §12.16 restricts Back artillery coordination/splitting and says a barrage-capable heavy-weapons unit whose guns are Back is wholly Back and cannot participate in anti-armor or Close Assault. Section 12.18 permits Forward artillery to participate if it has the necessary ratings and **ammunition**. These are distinct conditions: §12.16's express entire-unit participation bar is for the described heavy-weapons case, not a quoted blanket statement that every Back unit's nominal ZOC contribution is zero.
- **Additional combat evidence:** §15.12 p. 22 bars pinned units and units retreating before assault into an attacked hex from adding their ratings to defensive Close Assault. §15.13 distinguishes Back guns, normally unaffected by Close Assault except overrun, from Forward guns that may participate. §15.14 requires commitment to add a rating to an actual Close Assault; §15.15 says out-of-ammunition units cannot Close Assault offensively or defensively. These are substantial evidence for a *combat-eligible-strength* alternative, but they discuss combat participation, not an explicit earlier §10.15 ZOC calculation. In particular do not turn a later voluntary commitment into an unannounced default or assume §10.15 ignores every restriction.
- §9.16(a) p. 17 calls the Fort Maddalena garrison a heavy-weapons battalion. If §12.16's described conditions actually hold, its **whole** unit cannot participate in the stated combat; the two-TOE × rating-3 nominal six may not automatically be treated as available in a combat calculation. Conversely no cited rule chooses Back at scenario setup or specifies whether that future combat position governs movement-time ZOC. Resolve its barrage-capable status, composition, timing, ammunition and state before selecting a pre-combat threshold policy.
- §§11.3 and 11.34 p. 19 define raw points from TOE and relevant rating and total raw points before converting to actual. They support the candidate's six-plus-three-plus-three **nominal** calculation; they do not choose which points are eligible for §10.15. Exactly ten satisfies the written “less than ten” exclusion, subject to §10.11, projection and all other rules. §32.11–.22's Land-only supply treatment and actual ammunition availability must be evaluated separately from detailed Logistics; an unrelated stocked unit is not automatic proof of usable ammo.

## Review outcome

The listed nominal, position/participation and proof-gated policies remain distinguishable. Define whether ZOC is computed from potential or currently eligible strength, how anti-tank's explicit presumption applies, and whether/how artillery position, pinned status, retreat history, commitment and ammo enter the particular snapshot. A policy may use unknown where prerequisites are missing, while physical ZOC disclosure and authorized information must follow their own accepted contract. Prior position, later position selection, late event correction and replay need versioned semantics; no source passage here requires every stage to reuse a prior combat position or permits treating a future choice as already made.

Test anti-tank separately from ordinary guns; no previous combat, Forward/Back heavy weapons, pinned/retreated units, known out-of-ammo and unknown ammo, thresholds 9/10 and corrected events, plus source-authorized §10.16 disclosure and audience-safe downstream behavior. These are synthetic vectors, not an admitted policy, reachable setup or test result.
