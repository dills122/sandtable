# Initial motorization: conditional medium-truck characteristics

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Follow-up to the [Giarabub allocation check](2026-11-17-graziani-giarabub-initial-allocation.md) and [Commonwealth initial subtotal](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-motorization-next.md). The checked **Truck Characteristics Chart §54.2** supplies a concrete medium-truck transport capacity and CPA for an infantry load. Together with Land §§8.92 and 32.51 it supports a *conditional* candidate for an actually motorized one-TOE infantry subject; it does not establish initial attachment, sufficient available points, a current Giarabub CPA or an accepted Land-only rule. An [independent same-scan review](2026-11-18-graziani-initial-motorization-source-review.md) confirms the conditional reading.

## Source evidence and the extra distinction

- [Common chart §54.2](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), printed/PDF p. 14, gives a **medium** Truck Point carrying infantry CPA **20** and maximum capacity **one Infantry TOE Strength Point**. Its legend says the CPA column is the Truck Point's allowance while transporting the listed item. In contrast its medium gun CPA is 15 and its supply CPA 30 is explicitly limited to trucks in convoy carrying supplies. Do not substitute either column for the infantry column, or import detailed-game fuel, breakdown or supply values wholesale into the abstract system.
- Land §8.92, printed/PDF p. 16, says a non-motorized unit being transported by trucks takes the appropriate transporting truck CPA. §32.51, p. 44, treats Motorization Points as Medium Truck Points except as modified within its major case. §32.53 considers normal units to have built-in transport; §32.54 separately permits motorizing leg infantry and prioritizes historically motorized infantry before other infantry-type assignments. Their interaction with Giarabub's specific counter/type and setup needs a ruling; §32.58(A)'s CPA 15 explicitly applies to Motorization Points **transporting a Supply Unit**, not automatically to points transporting infantry.
- The [previous Giarabub source check](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-giarabub-motorization.md) and [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-giarabub-motorization-review.md) establish the `hh` printed `8+` and one TOE point from OA `N` and maximum, while leaving its actual starting allocation unestablished. They do not assert that the plus alone supplies a numeric motorized CPA. If an accepted classification establishes that this one TOE is infantry for §54.2, the point is present, a Motorization Point actually carries it in the applicable mode, and no other applicable modification changes the calculation, **one medium-equivalent point and CPA 20** are a chart-based conditional candidate. Those premises are not proven by the chart or the historical `+`; even a fully verified capacity formula would not select an initial pool.
- Scenario §§59.63(A–B), 59.64 and 60.33 distinguish first-line conversion to attached points from second/third-line conversion to unattached points. The Italian `Anywhere in Libya` second/third-line listing provides 100 medium plus 25 heavy before conversion, not a specific Giarabub attachment. The [prior allocation review](2026-11-17-graziani-giarabub-initial-allocation-review.md) retains setup transfer and location as unresolved; this chart does not resolve those questions. The corresponding [Commonwealth source review](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-motorization-review.md) has a bounded 147 attached plus 115 unattached subtotal, not a single fungible point pool or a proof of legal placement.

## Conditional discriminators

1. Compare a proven one-TOE infantry unit actually carried by one eligible medium-equivalent point with the same printed `8+` unit lacking established transport: the chart and §8.92 can inform the former's candidate CPA, while the latter cannot inherit it from `+`. Establish parent representation, co-location, real source debit, mode and timing first.
2. Compare medium-point assignment to infantry against the supply-unit-specific 30-point minimum and §32.58's 15 CPA; they concern different cargo. Test one point against one qualifying infantry TOE, and reject a claim that this one point transports two infantry TOE **under the chart-based reading**, subject to all relevant rules and actual type classification.
3. Check historically motorized priorities and scarce/unknown assignments before using any Commonwealth or Axis setup selection; fixed source row provenance, optional initial cargo, attached versus unattached status and air-pool exclusion remain independent. No inference is made about either side's actual available or remaining points.

These are source-based comparison cases, not executed tests, legal allocations or reachability findings. Preserve the unknown if transport, classification, initial precedence, or a special exception is unresolved. No inference is made about previously merged blocker status.
