## Scope and rationale

Apply this proposed value only to the identified Sceferzen Oasis Garrison unit at its uncorrected initial `N` baseline: ID `dd`, one TOE of one maximum, before any separately admitted strength, composition or status change. Land §9.22 directs use of the printed stacking value with chart §9.4 rather than name/designation for organizational level. The original counter prints `0`, and §9.4 associates zero with a full company; Axis §4.46b separately classifies `dd` as infantry company-equivalent. The `N` full-strength baseline weighs against treating it as a below-half battalion shell. This conjunction supports the selected interpretation more strongly than the zero alone.

Preserve the actual counter's `G`, **`II` battalion size symbol**, printed **`0` stacking value**, OA's **Bn** wording, and chart's **Coy-Eq** classification as separate provenance. Section 9.22 does not explicitly resolve this exact `II`/`0` conflict or declare all size-sensitive rules identical; do not alter source facts or infer a formal erratum. The [source note](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sheferzen-size.md) and [independent source review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sheferzen-size-review.md) document Land §§4.21, 4.45, 9.16, 9.22, 9.26, 9.28 and 10.11/.15, common chart §9.4 and Axis §4.46b. They identify hash-verified same-edition scans, not a separate edition or rights grant.

The choice is limited to the qualifying baseline and organizational-level lookup. Proposed validity begins only after a complete initial setup snapshot confirms the identified source unit, `dd`, unchanged one-of-one `N` baseline, and accepted composition and attachment state. Mere passage of a phase, stage or turn does not itself expire a confirmed baseline. A change or correction to strength, maximum, composition, attachment, location, unit identity or relevant source content invalidates the baseline decision; missing or ambiguous evidence makes it unknown. In particular, losses, replacements and added TOE points trigger invalidation. Until a separately admitted re-evaluation or later-state policy establishes the size, size-sensitive consumers remain unavailable; do not assume either permanent company status or a new result on invalidation. This conservative invalidation policy is a project choice, not source text.

It does **not** select a general rule for shell status, garrison exceptions, away-from-home stacking, retreat, reaction, pinning, attachment, counter representation or every size-dependent effect. Another plausible reading gives the counter's `II` size mark or OA's Bn wording priority and treats `0` as a special garrison or shell-related value.
