# Graziani GT1–6: Land-only weather effects on movement and supply

**Result:** Weather can change Land movement costs, passage, breakdown and potentially abstract supply access; it is not merely a stage roll. Land-only §32 removes ordinary supply usage and substitutes its own fuel/ammo economy, while Motorization Points themselves are exempt from breakdown. Stored-fuel evaporation and application of movement weather to a hypothetical supply trace require explicit rulings. This is a bounded source analysis, not an admitted runtime contract or evidence of actual weather in any stage.

The [schedule](2026-11-17-graziani-weather-schedule-next.md) and [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-weather-schedule-review.md) establish the six dates, stage determinations, selected fall row and foul-location mapping, including season and startup qualifications. They do not establish which outcomes actually occur. This note does not repeat or alter those decisions. Every effect below is conditional on the stage outcome, location and applicable subject; sections unaffected by a storm have normal weather (§29.1).

## Direct source: Land-only supply and transport

- §32.0 applies when the Land Game is played without the Air and Logistics Games; it replaces ordinary supply expenditures with listed Ammo and Fuel costs. §32.11 says to ignore references to usage and expenditure of Ammo, Fuel, Stores and Water and that only the following supply rules apply. §32.15 gives each real Supply Unit initial contents and maxima of 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel Points. §§32.23–.24 prescribe fuel for specified land movement; they do not make all movements free or charge every unit equally.
- §32.16 allows a friendly combat unit to draw from a friendly Supply Unit within half the combat unit's CPA, measuring from unit to supply as a medium truck would traverse, except non-motorized infantry traces as infantry. Impassable terrain and enemy ZOCs unoccupied by friendly units block the supply line. §32.33 permits a Motorization Point-transported Supply Unit to move in one of the specified phases, subject to stacking conditions; §§32.32 and 32.58 specify transport requirements and a 15 CPA for those points. Section 32.55 excludes Motorization Points from breakdown, not from all movement restrictions.
- §29.34 reduces eligible Fuel and Water Levels by 5% during Weather Determination, including dumps and trucks, excluding well/oasis water and fuel/water actually consumed by a unit. §29.35 doubles unit water requirements; §29.32 also specifies construction-site water rations in hot weather. §29.53 fills depleted wells in rain, and §29.55 prohibits drawing water from wadis during rain. Section 32.11's supply substitution gives strong textual basis to omit ordinary water requirements and water expenditure in Land-only play; whether well state exists for a separate admitted purpose and whether §29.53 mutates it are separate mode questions. No §32 water balance or well model is established by the cited provisions.

## Interpretation requiring admission

1. **Hot stored fuel.** One reading applies §29.34 to abstract Fuel Points in Supply Units: evaporation is reduction of stored fuel, not necessarily usage or expenditure, and §32.15 retains fuel balances. Another treats §32.0/.11's replacement and “only” supply rules as excluding §29.34 entirely. Neither provision expressly resolves this interaction. Select and version a rule before applying losses; separately specify eligible on/off-map subjects, grouping, fractions/rounding and atomic timing. Do not use empty subjects as a substitute for missing state. Section 29.1 excludes Tunisia–Tripoli and other named off-map places from weather.
2. **Supply trace under weather.** Because §32.16 prices the route *as though* traversed by a medium truck or infantry, a plausible reading applies current terrain/weather movement costs and passage restrictions to the hypothetical route; another requires explicit authority before importing every movement effect into a non-movement trace. Neither §32.16 nor the weather cases expressly resolves that integration. For a purely illustrative legal route costing 6 CP in affected hex costs and a 10 CP draw threshold, doubling those costs would make 12 CP and fail the threshold under the first reading; this is not proof of an actual route, cost allocation or adjudication. Weather-modified trace costs also require the separate [half-CPA rounding issue](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-range-rounding-review.md), map identity, blocked terrain and ZOC inputs. No breakdown roll follows merely from a hypothetical trace.
3. **Actual transport.** Sections 32.31–.33 provide real Supply Unit transport; §32.51 generally treats Motorization Points as medium trucks except where modified. Applying accepted terrain and weather movement restrictions to actual motorized transport follows from those rules, subject to §32.55's breakdown exemption and the transport unit's applicable movement/phase rules. This does not by itself settle whether all vehicle-specific delta language applies to every abstract counter, or prove a permitted route or available fuel.

## Focused checks before admission

Check normal, hot, affected/unaffected sand and rain, and map E delta/non-delta cases separately; include exactly-half versus just-under-half CP for sandstorm breakdown, Motorization Points versus other eligible vehicles, and a rain wadi with and without a road. For supply, compare real movement with hypothetical draw, affected versus unaffected trace sections, a trace crossing an impassable rain wadi, mixed hexside costs, and selected half-CPA rounding. Compare both hot-fuel interpretations at zero and positive stored balances, fractional 5% loss and excluded off-map locations. These are design vectors, not executed tests or evidence of particular stage outcomes.
