# Matruh rail no-witness proof: independent review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Disposition:** No blocking discrepancy found in the bounded proof obligations. The example remains **unknown**: it supplies neither a complete negative certificate nor an established witness, and does not establish an available or historically reachable action.

## Checks

- Land §8.78, printed/PDF p. 16, applies to an Allied rail hex west of **any** rail hex occupied by an Axis combat unit. Its text supplies no same-line, route-membership, connectivity or distance restriction. The note correctly requires a geographic search beyond pinned route edges and identifies used hexes and west ordering as policy questions, not settled source facts. A full bound must include every potentially eastern playable position, including overlaps and unresolved seams; sheet letters alone cannot exclude a candidate.
- Land §8.47, p. 15, says unconstructed rail hexes are ignored for all purposes; §8.71 distinguishes the active Alexandria–Mersa Matruh line from the historic-only Soluch–Benghazi–Barce line. Section 8.76, p. 16, distinguishes destroyed rail from rail not yet constructed. The note correctly labels the destroyed-rail witness and historical-line exclusions as interpretations, preserves possible independently qualifying overlap, and leaves §54.4 and scenario overrides open. RUL-GRA-26 selects retained-witness for planning, not an official ruling.
- For each used hex and candidate position, the conjunction is west relation AND qualifying rail AND Axis combat occupancy. A false term excludes that candidate even if another term is unknown; all true establishes a witness even with gaps elsewhere; no true plus any unresolved potentially relevant candidate or coverage gap yields unknown. A negative requires complete relevant coverage with each conjunction false. Missing pinned rail is not a false rail term: source-supported absence or another independently false term is needed. This includes an unpinned, off-route, eastern occupied rail hex as a possible witness.
- The hypothetical two-hex use set is explicitly conditional. Route continuity, passenger, stage, construction and other movement conditions remain independent; the note does not infer actual reachability from the scenario rail end or candidate printed side. If an adopted use policy changes that set, repeat the quantification for every used hex.
- The disclosure gate covers enumeration through later observations and replay, not only an initial refusal. Equal authorized histories differing in hidden witness state must not expose that difference absent authorized disclosure; proof on authoritative state alone does not authorize public action. This is a planning boundary, not a verified protocol.

Useful future contract cases: unpinned eastern occupied rail with a proven west relation blocks; the same candidate with unknown rail and no other false term yields unknown; proven empty occupancy excludes it despite unknown rail; one proven witness blocks despite another unknown; all candidates independently excluded with complete bounds yields no witness. Also check equal-longitude and staggered-row comparisons, seam aliases, constructed-destroyed versus certified unbuilt rail, represented combat units, and paired hidden-state public traces. These are proposed cases, not executed tests.
