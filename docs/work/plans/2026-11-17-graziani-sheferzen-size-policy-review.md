# Scfzrn initial size policy: independent review

**Disposition:** Selection of `gra-scfzrn-initial-size-v1` for **planning** is warranted as a bounded project interpretation. It combines §9.22's explicit preference for printed stacking value over designation/name with §9.4's full-company row, the `dd` company-equivalent chart row and the initial maximum-strength evidence. The policy correctly preserves the conflicting `II` face and `Bn` name. This is not an official correction, executable rule admission, or proof of actual ZOC.

## Source and scope assessment

The [source check](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sheferzen-size.md) and [independent source review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sheferzen-size-review.md) support the quoted field values and source distinctions. In my earlier source review I visually checked Land §§4.21, 4.45, 9.16, 9.22, 9.26, 9.28 and 10.11/.15, Axis OA and §4.46b, common chart §9.4, and Scfzrn's counter face. Those same-scan checks identify the source hashes, not a separate edition or a rights grant.

Section 9.22 tells the player to use printed stacking value with the chart rather than a unit's designation or name. This does not expressly adjudicate a conflict with the counter's separate `II` organization-size mark. Section 9.4's zero includes full company and shell battalion, while §9.16's garrison stacking exception is location-specific. The selected source-baseline maximum of one TOE and the `dd` heading support the company option, but do not establish that any printed zero is a company or that every future use of this unit must retain that level. The alternative battalion interpretation and its consequences remain live.

The isolated `1 × 2 = 2` raw-defense arithmetic, printed zero and §10.11/.15 checks are accurately qualified: co-located eligible forces, current state and garrison treatment are independent. The policy does not settle §9.16's home/away or effective-value interaction with §10.11, optional Autoblinda assignment, map and border uncertainty, or phasing. It does not establish any attack or defense action, contact result, or six-turn reachability.

## Required contract details before admission

1. Define stable source-unit identity and provenance separately from mutable stack, role and size fields. Bind the policy version to the recorded source/content identity, original `II`, `Bn`, `G`, `0`, `dd` and baseline `N`, rather than overwriting any of them. Verify replay and unknown versions cannot silently substitute a different policy.
2. Define precisely when the **initial** eligibility check applies, how long a decision remains valid if the same baseline persists beyond setup, and which events invalidate or recompute it. A mere passage of time, strength/composition changes, attachment changes, loss or replacement, and unknown data need individually specified behavior. Until selected, fail closed for size-sensitive consumers rather than assume either permanent persistence or automatic loss at the first phase boundary.
3. Keep raw printed value, effective stacking under garrison rules, shell state and organizational level as distinct facts. Define each consumer's accepted inputs; a single integer cannot establish all four. In particular, §9.16's free stacking is not automatically a size conversion, and one TOE added by a special rule cannot silently change a maximum, shell status or baseline.
4. Preserve audience-safe behavior: unknown or changed unit state, optional choices and derived capability must not leak through availability, reason text, resulting actions, timing or subsequent observable traces. A generic error alone is not a complete privacy protocol. Actual stack composition and legal setup remain separately gated.

Test the proposed source baseline and its competing source readings, differing zero-value counters and shell battalions, home/away versus printed values, exact raw 9/10 threshold, enriched versus isolated stacks, uncertain/changed baseline and lifecycle boundaries, missing or conflicting source identity, policy version replay, and side-safe observable behavior. Tests are proposals, not executed evidence. No runtime, source content, readiness flag or gameplay tests changed.
