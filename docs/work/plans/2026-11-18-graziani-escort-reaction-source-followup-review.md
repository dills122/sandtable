# Motorized supply reaction and retreat: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Independently checked the [reaction/retreat follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-escort-reaction-source-followup.md) against the same original [Land rules scan](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 12, 15, 18, 21 and 43–44, SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`. The process and forced-return distinctions are supported; no material transcription error found.

- **Separate timing and trigger:** §8.51 describes reaction to an enemy combat unit moving adjacent. Section 8.53(b)'s six-or-more CPA difference restriction also requires the owner of that adjacent mover to announce a close assault by that mover against the non-phasing unit; its example says the unit could still retreat before assault. Section 13.0 places that different procedure after completed barrages. The attack-announcement condition belongs to §8.53(b)'s reaction restriction, not a general new prerequisite for all §13.1 retreats. Section 5.2 places the retreat step after barrage and before later combat steps.
- **Retreat scope:** §13.1 permits an unpinned non-phasing unit to retreat before assault except at cohesion −26 or worse. Section 13.21 calls this voluntary movement; §§13.23–.24 distinguish starting adjacent to an enemy combat unit from not starting adjacent. The latter is limited to four CP or one hex, whichever is greater. Broad “unit” wording does not expressly decide whether an abstract Supply Unit, assigned Motorization Points or their combination is the moving subject.
- **15 CP and reaction threshold:** §32.58 applies to **Motorization Points transporting a Supply Unit**: CPA 15, return to the last hex where CPA was not exceeded **if forced** to exceed it for any reason, and may react unless the adjacent mover's CPA is 35 or more. Section 13.21's voluntary description and §13.23's broad movement allowance do not explicitly resolve how §32.58(B) treats voluntarily exceeding 15, whether some other rule forbids it, or which object must return. Do not expand the forced clause into an unconditional over-15 outcome without an accepted interpretation. Section 32.55 exempts Motorization Points themselves from breakdown while preserving other breakdown rules.
- **ZOC and escort:** §13.26 states retreat must stop upon entering enemy ZOC and cannot go directly from one enemy ZOC to another; if the latter cannot be avoided, no retreat. Section 8.55 instead says a reacting unit may never enter enemy ZOC. Sections 10.26/.29 and 32.33 introduce further movement, convoy and non-phasing wording; their interaction and before-entry occupancy are not resolved by either statement alone. Section 8.53(a)'s unescorted Truck Convoy restriction expressly names reaction; §32.58(D)'s 35 threshold likewise names reaction. Neither clause alone proves a matching retreat prohibition or grants a retreat.

The follow-up's synthetic comparisons are conditional and do not establish a real carrier, legal route, fuel, eligible escort or reachable contact. Its scope preserves the substantive unresolved hierarchy, carrier/cargo identity and timing questions. Same scan, not independent edition; no new errata audit, runtime/data changes or gameplay tests.
