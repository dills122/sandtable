# Graziani terrain transcription policy: independent review

**Disposition (2026-11-17):** Accept the proposed evidence-gated manual approach as an interim project convention for disputed assignments, not as a printed rule or a completed correction. No universal area threshold or C0818 terrain assignment is established by this review. Retain the versioned v1 record for replay; treat its C0818 `clear` value as disputed, not as evidence of clear terrain. This review does not establish that existing consumers already fail closed on that value.

## Independent source check

- Land rules, printed p. 14, §8.35, distinguish directional slope and escarpment hexsides: splash contours are on the down side; ridges have no direction. The passage supplies no minimum intrusion, center, or majority test for terrain covering a hex. §8.31 distinguishes entry into hexes from crossing sides, and §8.33 requires a connecting road or track side for its movement benefit. These observations support separating area assignments, directional sides, and route connectivity.
- Original map PDF p. 1 terrain key visually distinguishes blank Clear, polygonal Salt Marsh, solid Desert, and palm-and-water Oasis; it also shows ridge, escarpment, slope and wadi as side features, and Village/Bir as a separate point symbol. Sample art illustrates feature types, but gives no numerical assignment criterion. A feature's splash or a spanning label must not silently become area terrain or a settlement locator.
- Independently viewed the policy's Map C crop at the stated resolution. Salt-marsh polygons cross into the C0818 cell and blue Giarabub Oasis lettering obscures part of it. The image supports visible intrusion, not an unambiguous center or quantified area. It does not by itself decide whether C0818 is a salt-marsh hex under the original rules or any proposed convention. This is a second reading of the same scan, not independent source corroboration.

## Gates and limits

The policy's source search, independent comparison, uncertainty handling, versioned identity and replay, and consumer tests are appropriate before a correction. Require the selected convention or bounded ruling to state what evidence qualifies, how conflicting readers are reconciled, and what remains unknown; preserve reader observations and rationale separately from the resulting assignment. Manual adjudication alone does not make uncertain artwork authoritative or prove a global rule.

For any future admission, explicitly distinguish unknown in the proposed data and behavior from the existing v1 `clear` value, and check affected consumers and source-world gates before claiming fail-closed operation. Test both resolved and unresolved disputed cells, old replay identity, overlay/hexside independence, route exceptions and affected movement, breakdown and combat consequences. No map data, runtime behavior or test coverage changes in this review.
