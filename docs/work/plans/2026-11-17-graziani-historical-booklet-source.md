# Campaign for North Africa historical booklet: source check

Checked 2026-11-17. Scope: original SPI *The Campaign for North Africa: The Desert War 1940–43* historical booklet, not its rules or a later redesign.

## Finding

Original boxed game included a separate historical background booklet. Thomas G. Pratuch's contemporary component survey, [“The Endless Sand,” *Moves* 49, p. 15](https://spigames.net/MovesScans/Moves49/CNAGPM49.pdf), appears to identify it as 16 pages by A. Nofi, R. Berg and J. Dunnigan, describes its army discussions and campaign overview, and separately identifies land rules and air/logistics rules. PDF text extraction reads “/6” and “Noji”; those characters have not been visually verified. The SPI-themed archive hosts that survey; the survey is evidence about the booklet, not the booklet itself. [LastDodo's component inventory](https://www.lastdodo.com/en/items/7841947-the-campaign-for-north-africa) independently lists a campaign historical background item alongside rules and charts. It credits Nofi, Berg and Dunnigan in game metadata, not independently as booklet authors.

No legitimately accessible full text of the original historical booklet from the publisher or a verified rights holder was found in this bounded search. This is not proof that no authorized copy exists. The [SPI archive's land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) and [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) are different documents; availability of those files does not establish permission or availability for the historical booklet. SPIgames.net is an enthusiast archive, not established here as publisher or rights holder.

[Decision Games' product page](https://shop.decisiongames.com/ProductDetails.php?ProductCode=P3034) describes a planned update of the SPI classic, not access to the original historical booklet. A [Grognard listing](https://grognard.com/m-article-page-list.aspx?GameID=c045) attributes access to the 1979 addenda to Decision Games; that attribution does not establish rights in the historical booklet. Current rights to the booklet and digital distribution remain unverified.

[Noble Knight's listing](https://www.nobleknight.com/P/2148003114/Campaign-for-North-Africa---Historical-Background-Book) identifies a separate SPI historical background book, out of stock when checked. Its metadata says 48 pages, conflicting with the contemporary survey's 16 pages; listing may contain an error or represent a different physical item. Do not treat its page count as resolved without inspecting a copy. No purchase or contact was made.

## Use in Graziani work

Treat the booklet, if lawfully obtained and verified, as historical context and contemporary design perspective, not authority for game rules, setup, unit values, or scenario data. Use original scenario instructions, applicable charts, and official errata for game-specific claims; cite independent historical sources for historical claims. Until verified material is available, do not infer Graziani scenario details from the booklet's existence or substitute a similarly named rules PDF.
