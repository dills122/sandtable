# Tobruk facility lifecycle proposal: independent review

**Disposition:** The [planning proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-facility-lifecycle-policy.md) preserves the source conflict and appropriately requires unknown-dependent transitions to remain unadmitted. Its initial one-temporary-facility state is an existing project profile, not a source-proven resolution of §22.31 versus §60.33. Privacy and event gates are proposed safeguards, not printed rules.

## Source checks

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.33 specifies a major facility at Tripoli and temporary facilities at Tobruk and Benghazi. [Land](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) p. 35 §22.31 lists Tobruk as a major facility for whoever controls it. Neither inspected clause explicitly explains whether the facilities coexist, replace each other, or change type at a control event. The proposal's lifecycle alternatives remain alternatives rather than established source outcomes.
- Land p. 37 §24.81 says temporary facilities **may** be destroyed by the presence of enemy units, while major facilities may not be destroyed **in that manner**. The text does not specify a mandatory automatic event, which actor decides, exact timing, owner reference, or effect on a separately interpreted major facility. Control, actual unit presence and opposing ZOC are distinct observations; Land p. 18 §10.27 permits a hex controlled by both players. A change in control alone cannot stand in for the required presence.
- Section 24.85 permits a temporary facility in a major city or village, at most two per player. It does not explicitly say an initial Tobruk temporary facility coexists with the general major one. Section 24.87 prohibits new temporary construction in an enemy-controlled hex but permits repair facilities to be rebuilt or dismantled there. Apply specific procedures and scope: §24.83 gives new temporary construction requirements; §24.84 describes rebuilding after major-city levels are reduced by bombardment, not a universal rebuilding procedure for every destroyed facility; §24.86 describes dismantling a temporary facility and §24.88 makes it unusable during dismantling. Those conditions do not resolve the identity conflict.
- Section 22.32 requires a broken vehicle or destroyed tank to begin the Maintenance Segment at a facility and not have been towed in that Operations Stage. Section 22.34 applies the correct temporary/major column to **broken vehicles**; destroyed tank repairs have distinct §22.4 rules. §§22.36–.37 provide weather and enemy-ZOC qualifications, including major-facility and major-city distinctions; do not generalize them into automatic repairs or availability.

## State and privacy review

The proposal separates facility identity/type, damage, presence, control and causal event order, and directs unknown-dependent outcomes to remain unavailable until source policy and complete authoritative history are established. That is a reasonable tri-state boundary provided **unknown is never converted to absent, undamaged, safe, or destroyed** in action generation, replay, state projection or later events. A side-safe result must also preserve observation equivalence across hidden presence/facility alternatives, including options, errors, event counts and downstream state. These are review requirements for a future implementation; no executable contract, completeness proof or test result is supplied here.
