# Ismailia off-map distance: adjacent source cases

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Bounded follow-up to [map registration](2026-11-18-graziani-map-registration-source-next.md) and its [review](2026-11-18-graziani-map-registration-source-next-review.md). The inspected Air and Land passages provide separate flight, shortest-distance and western off-map land procedures. They add useful counting context but **do not identify a correction** for the `E2629`–`E3425` zero-hex example, establish an Ismailia land route, or define a global map registration.

## Source comparison

- Air §37.0, printed/PDF p. 7, describes operational flight from one hex to another and distinguishes its actual path from a simple target placement; §37.12 counts distance from air facility to target along a path chosen by the player and compares it with the plane's range. §37.11 describes range per direction, with a transfer-mission exception. These are flight mechanics, not a road or land-unit movement rule. Section 37.12 does not independently explain how to count an off-map facility leg or correct the worked example.
- The “Determining Shortest Distance” text on Air PDF p. 24 explicitly says shortest distance is **not the actual flight path**; it instructs tracing two different 60-degree axes from origin and destination and counting to their convergence. Its Agedabia–Ismailia example names the `E3433` entry/exit leg, adds nine east to Ismailia, and includes the unresolved zero-hex step from `E2629` to `E3425`. The neighboring §36.53 off-map air facilities chart identifies Ismailia's entry/exit as `E3433`, distance 9. This is compatible with that one named air leg, not proof of a contiguous on-map ground path or a correction to the zero step.
- Air §37.42, PDF p. 25, labels its matrix **Land Distance Chart**. Its footnote defines matrix distance from one location **exclusive** to the other **inclusive**, even across a hypothetical all-sea grid where necessary; it also supplies the same-number adjacent-sheet 33-hex convention. The matrix itself does not list Ismailia. It is evidence of that chart's shortest-distance convention, not an explicit instruction that all off-map air legs or every actual flight path use the identical endpoint count. It does not resolve the inconsistent example or certify edge identity.
- Land §§8.81–.85, printed/PDF p. 16, describe western Tripoli/Tunisia boxes, transit boxes, Operations-Stage travel based on CPA, and map entry via the specified western edge. Air PDF p. 24's §8.89 table gives **Operations Stages** for Tunis, Gabes, Tripoli, Tripolitania and Nofilia at listed CPA bands. Ismailia is not an entry in that table. These western-box rules are a different movement abstraction; they provide no east-off-map Ismailia land-entry distance or connection in the inspected passages.
- Scenario §60.5, printed p. 28 / PDF p. 4, lists Ismailia as **off-map** among airfields; this is another source locator for the facility, not a printed on-map hex assignment. The [prior registration review](2026-11-18-graziani-map-registration-source-next-review.md) preserves the example's zero-distance problem and Land §4.1's separate overlay wording. Neither §37.12's path counting nor §8.89's western-box table decides a corrected intermediate hex, missing number, shared sheet cell, or land adjacency.

A future discriminating source would explicitly correct the worked example or specify the eastern facility's off-map counting and land access, with edition and provenance. The checked cases do not establish that no such source exists elsewhere. No decision about §32.37's separate abstract-air origin/destination geometry or an actual mission follows from this comparison.
