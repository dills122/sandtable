# Singleton ZOC threshold: independent review

**Disposition:** The [singleton source note](2026-11-17-graziani-singleton-zoc-threshold.md) correctly states a conditional no-ZOC result for a truly isolated combat unit with an applicable one-point value. It does not establish actual initial stacks. Keep attached-truck and convoy effects distinct and avoid treating stacking-limit exemptions as a complete §10.11 mixed-stack rule.

## Source checks

- [Land rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 17 §10.11, uses **more than one** stacking point both to describe a unit above battalion equivalent and in the separate hex-total alternative. Exactly one meets neither numerical expression. §10.15, continuing on p. 18, independently excludes units in a hex with less than ten raw defensive Close Assault Points and presumes anti-tank units Up Front for this purpose. Ten clears this exclusion only; it cannot itself create the size/stack qualification.
- Section 9.29 p. 17 says attached first-line trucks do not count towards unit organizational size or road-space stacking limits; truck convoys do not count against stacking limits except road space. Section 10.11 separately excludes convoys from exerting ZOC. These support the note's narrow attached-truck and convoy statements, not a universal derivation of §10.11's mixed-hex total from stacking-capacity exemptions. Parent representation and any additional combat units need separate adjudication.
- Visually rechecked original [counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 4 section 7: `1 RTR`, `2RflBde`, and `11 Hus` each show `II` and unparenthesized `1`. Crops: `/private/tmp/first-rtr-review/counter.png` and `/private/tmp/sheferzen-zoc-review/cw-counter.png`; prior reproducible crop parameters are in [1 RTR review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-first-rtr-zoc-review.md) and [Sheferzen review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sheferzen-unit-zoc-review.md). These are printed counter facts, not proof the actual occupied hex has only one effective stacking point.
- Raw baseline values 21, 12 and 18 and the stated scenario coordinates/trucks are attributed to the linked same-scan unit reviews, not newly recalculated against OA or scenario in this review. Under those baseline and singleton assumptions each clears the raw threshold but fails the one-point stack/size test. Actual legal co-location, representation, state, applicable garrison/free-stacking interpretation, cohesion and directional entry/terrain remain separate; a positive or negative actual contact ZOC is not established.
