# Rommel schedule and effects: independent source review

**Disposition:** The [source note](2026-11-18-graziani-rommel-schedule-source.md) correctly reads the ordinary GT20/2 entry, the six-turn window, initiative chart and §17.28's assault-specific adjustment. **Add one material scope comparison:** Land §31.0 item 3 states a separate, broader-worded morale effect whose subject and timing are not identical to §17.28. Do not silently merge the two or treat either as an actual Graziani effect.

- Axis chart §4.43b, PDF p. 2, prints `Gm: Rommel [DAK]` under **GT20 / OpStage 2**; its first ordinary land-unit entry is **GT9/2**. Scenario §60.22, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, starts Graziani GT1/1 and ends GT6/3. The §60.31 Italian initial deployment does not list Rommel. These facts bound initial listing and ordinary track only; they do not prove all exceptional/non-track paths absent or establish any later state.
- Common chart §7.2, p. 2, prints Commonwealth **3** for GT1–42; Axis **6** if the Rommel counter is on the Game-Maps, **3** for German land combat units but no Rommel on the Game-Maps, and **1** for the printed no-German-combat-units-or-Rommel condition. The footnote excludes the Map A Tripoli/Tunisia holding boxes for this purpose. Preserve the chart's different `land combat units` and `combat units` wording and its condition's grammar. Scenario §60.6, PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, grants Italian initiative for GT1 and begins ordinary determination in GT2; it does not determine each later outcome.
- Land §31.0, p. 42, calls for two dice at the beginning of each Game-Turn Rommel is present: a 12 sends him to Germany, drops Axis initiative to **3** for that turn and says he returns next turn unless another 12 occurs. This special wording does not add a separately printed German land-unit prerequisite to the reduction. Its timing/return and relationship to chart conditions remain interpretive.
- Land §17.23, p. 27, caps ordinary adjusted morale at +3 except §17.28. Section **17.28** adds **+1 adjusted morale** to Axis units stacked with Rommel at the **instant of Assault**; September errata §17.28 explicitly confirms +4 is possible. Section **31.0 item 3**, however, says if Rommel is in the same hex as a unit involved in combat during the Combat Phase, **all units in that stack** have morale increased by one. Its wording does not itself say Axis, adjusted morale, or instant of assault. These descriptions may be reconciled by scope or specificity, but the sources do not expressly choose a hierarchy here. Preserve both; no global extension, double bonus or automatic equivalence is established. Section 31.0 item 4 separately gives a possible +5 CPA if Rommel starts and remains with unit(s) for the entire Operations Stage; it is not an initiative or morale modifier.
