# Guarded dump raid equality: source review

**Disposition:** No material error found in the [equality discriminator](2026-11-18-graziani-raider-guard-equality.md). Its two guard-test dice and later attack die are correctly distinguished, and the example is explicitly hypothetical.

Land p. 39 §27.55(b) requires the two-dice sum to be **higher** than the guarding units' raw defensive assault strength; otherwise the raider is eliminated. Common chart p. 8 §27.91 instead allows survival and attack when the sum is **equal to or greater than** total raw Close Assault Defense Points, directing the player to ignore parentheses when calculating strength. For the stated hypothetical total 2 and guard dice 1+1, equality therefore separates the two printed outcomes. Under the chart reading, a **separate** die 1 destroys 10% of supplies; die 6 leaves supplies unaffected and eliminates the raider if combat units are present. The 100-to-10 arithmetic is conditional, not a setup stock or rounding rule.

The chart requires an opponent's real/non-dump/dummy report only after completing the raid without elimination. Land §27.56 instead immediately reveals a dummy after any raider–guard conflict if any raiders survive, turns off the supply-destruction raid, and retains other guard/elimination results. The note properly preserves these separate conditions and does not invent guard casualties or conflate a real dump with a dummy.

Land p. 43 §§32.11–.15 and .18 support the note's mode caveat: in Land-only play abstract Supply Units use dump counters and real units have ammunition/fuel quantities; an enemy combat unit entering captures a Supply Unit, while a dummy is removed upon discovery. These passages do not expressly decide the raider/abstract-dump interaction. The [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) bounded text search identifies §§27.16, .36, .88 and §32.0, not a directly numbered §27.55/.56/.91 equality correction. No precedence or exhaustive later-source absence follows.
