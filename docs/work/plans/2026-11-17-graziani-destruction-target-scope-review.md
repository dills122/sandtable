# Abstract supply destruction target scope: independent review

**Disposition:** The [target-scope note](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-target-scope.md) accurately identifies the tension between §32.17's hex-wide wording and per-Supply-Unit attempt limit. It does not establish subset selection, universal enemy co-occupancy, or automatic dummy discovery. Keep these as decisions before action admission. Privacy requires observation-equivalence checks in addition to a generic fail-closed response: merely withholding an offer when hidden state differs can itself reveal information.

## Source checks

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §32.17: players may try to destroy Supply Units they are with; pay half CPA of all units in the hex, rounding upward, and roll one die for each Supply Unit in the hex. A 1–3 destroys it; otherwise it is unaffected. Add one to the die if total stacking points in the hex during the attempt are at most one. Before any movement in a given sub-segment, only one attempt per Supply Unit per sub-segment. These words favor hex-wide treatment but do not explain a mixed attempted/unattempted set, authority, selection, or sub-segment identity. Do not assume the per-target limit expressly authorizes another action against only the fresh subset.
- Same page §§32.12–.15: real Supply Units use Supply Dump counters, have no combat capability and zero stacking value; §32.13 specifies capture when an enemy combat unit enters the hex, immediately and freely using supplies. Section 8.13 p. 14 states a unit may never enter an enemy-occupied hex, referring to §27.4 and aircraft distinctions. The interaction and its legal prerequisites warrant separate adjudication: do not silently conclude that the entry rule proves a contested hex legal, nor that §8.13 eliminates every relevant case or pending capture state.
- Section 32.18 permits one dummy for every three Supply Units received and/or initially held, moves it like real except for fewer motorization points, and removes it upon discovery; §32.32 gives 30 points for a real supply unit and six for a dummy. Neither specifies a destruction-attempt die for a dummy or makes the attempt discovery. The identical counter representation does not decide the target set, removal ordering or privacy semantics. Specific Logistics §54.12 discovery triggers are not automatically abstract-game rules.
- The [common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) p. 1 §6.3 gives one-third CPA for attempting to blow a supply dump without a mode label; §32.17 gives one-half CPA of all units in the hex. Neither cost formula independently defines abstract target scope. The separate [cost review](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-cost-hierarchy-review.md) records the precedence uncertainty; do not add both costs without a policy.

## Admission and evidence limits

If an actor's legitimate observation cannot distinguish two authoritative histories, compare visible action offers, rejection categories, event cardinality, identifiers, timing and subsequent observable RNG consequences; a generic error alone is not enough. The source does not specify this software privacy contract. Owner knowledge and discovery triggers must be established separately, and deterministic replay must preserve approved hidden outcomes without disclosing them through public traces. No source text requires a particular fail-closed UI or proves the proposed cases reachable.
