# Selected chart disclosure census: review

**Disposition:** No material source error found in the [census](2026-11-18-graziani-chart-disclosure-census.md). For exactness, its CW §42.53 row should spell out that the qualifying dice result is **equal to or less than the number listed for the number of planes**. The current wording leaves that comparator implicit; it does not assert the wrong outcome.

- Common p. 8 §27.91 says a raider completing a supply-dump raid without elimination receives a report from the opposing player identifying real, non-dump or dummy. On the guarded-dump test, chart survival uses two-dice sum **equal to or greater** than raw Close Assault Defense Points; the chart says to ignore parentheses when calculating strength. Land p. 39 §27.55(b) instead requires a sum **higher** than raw defensive assault strength. Equality therefore differs in printed text. Section 27.56 calls for immediate dummy revelation if raiders survive any raider–guard conflict; it does not itself say the chart's complete three-category report. Neither source alone resolves their interaction or automatic application to abstract Supply Units.
- The chart's convoy die 1–2 selection is asymmetric as reported: raided player determines truck type; raiding player determines cargo, with replacement points only if unavoidable. Its all-infantry-replacement convoy exception eliminates the raider without determining truck effect. Selection is not a full-manifest disclosure.
- Common p. 3 §16.7 gives the number of battalion-size-equivalent units about which the patrolling player receives reconnaissance information, including zero and All. It does not disclose the whole stack on every roll or prescribe a general opposing view.
- CW p. 31 §42.53 requires the Axis player, upon a dice result at or below the listed plane-count threshold, to inform the Allied player whether any naval convoy exists in the lane and, if present, report Small/Medium/Large. Its tonnage bands overlap and provide a possible Axis choice. This is detailed Air reconnaissance, separate from the Land-only lane-arrival announcement. September errata §42.53 points to the CW booklet. CW §32.47 itself is a die/supply-period table with no explicit opposing audience.

The source note's selected-chart and imperfect-OCR limits are necessary; this review does not establish a complete chart census, actual eligibility or a digital publication policy. Axis schedule/production pages were not independently re-read in this review.
