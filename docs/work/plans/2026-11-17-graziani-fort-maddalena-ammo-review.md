Scenario §60.31 lists Fort Maddalena Garrison at C3019 without a truck quantity on that row; §60.41 places 11th Hussars at C3020. Original map shows the two labeled hexes sharing a side. Thus C3019 is one hex from that listed Commonwealth force. Section 60.92(B)'s Land-only Axis Dump 1 and Dump 2 groups each must be in one Map C Libya hex not within four hexes of a Commonwealth unit. The adjacent C3019 fails that condition under any ordinary inclusive interpretation of one hex; the ambiguous exact four-hex boundary is irrelevant to this local exclusion. This does not exclude nearby draw sources or other supply categories.

Section 60.33 lists 100 medium and 50 heavy second/third-line trucks anywhere in Libya, alongside light trucks and separate Tripoli and air-facility allocations. Sections 59.63(B) and 59.66(C) derive unattached Motorization Points in the abstraction and permit one additional regular Supply Unit per complete group of 30 **other** unattached points in a hex. Section 59.44 gives separate placement restrictions for on-map supply trucks. Whether any group may occupy C3019 requires an accepted Libya-region interpretation, eligible conversion and allocation, stacking, owner choice and hierarchy between the optional unit and named-dump distance restriction. Neither source explicitly applies or exempts the named dump restriction to this separate additional supply. Air-facility motorization is separately treated and ignored when the Air Game is abstracted under §59.63(B).

Sections 59.66(A–B) permit an attached point not transporting men or guns to begin carrying one ammo or three fuel, additional to scenario dumps. The unit must expend these initial carried supplies first and may not draw a supply unit to meet expenditures while they remain; detachment is restricted while carrying them except when remaining attached to a subsidiary combat unit of that parent. A truckless garrison row neither proves such a qualifying point nor rules out every owner-selected legal setup. Preserve mixed-resource and detachment interactions for policy review.

Land §32.16 measures draw within half the friendly combat unit's CPA as a medium truck would traverse, except non-motorized infantry traces as infantry; impassable terrain and enemy ZOC without friendly units block the supply line. Axis chart `q` gives CPA 15, hence an unrounded half of 7.5. This value is not proof of a path, rounding rule, draw eligibility, source content or ammo on hand. Separately determine actual unit movement class, route direction and cost, threshold comparison, and any carried-initial-supply condition.

Section 10.15's raw-defense ZOC test, §12.16's Back heavy-weapons combat restriction and §12.18's Forward artillery ratings/ammo condition do not explicitly define a movement-time ZOC ammunition policy. No known supply choice, certain positive or negative ZOC, or attack obligation follows here. No explicit correction resolving these issues was found in the inspected errata; later or indirect authority may exist.
