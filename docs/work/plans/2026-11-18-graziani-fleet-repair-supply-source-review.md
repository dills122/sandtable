# Review: Commonwealth fleet repair and supplies

**Disposition:** No material source-transcription or timing error found in the [source note](2026-11-18-graziani-fleet-repair-supply-source.md). Its supply conclusion is bounded and its unresolved clock interactions remain open. One useful adjacent constraint: [Land §30.38](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 42, expressly allows ships to fire AA even while under repair; this does not decide whether their AA rating is restored as Gun Rating repairs.

- Land p. 43 §32.0 applies when playing Land without both Air and Logistics; §32.2's introductory exemption for Commonwealth Fleet and TOE points used to patrol is expressly for **that Case's purposes**. It is not an independent blanket exemption for Axis ships or every naval procedure.
- Land p. 42 §30.37 expressly repairs one Gun Rating level after six **consecutive** Operations Stages in Alexandria, without engineer or supplies, with work restarted if the ship moves before completion. Section 30.36 sinks and makes unrepairable a ship whose entire Gun Rating is eliminated; its below-half condition and greater-than-half repair threshold should remain distinct. Neither provision specifies repair-completion ordering within a stage or expressly addresses new damage without departure.
- Land p. 41 §§30.16 and .18 support two port stages per one in action, at most three consecutive stages at sea, and Valletta as a recovery port; travel between Valletta and Alexandria takes one stage and is not counted at sea for refit. Section 30.22 exempts offshore Gun Rating fire from ammunition, while p. 42 §30.25 requires the next two stages in **Alexandria** after offshore bombardment, excluding AA firing from that particular requirement. The source note correctly does not infer that Valletta qualifies for §30.37's specifically named repair location, or that separate clocks necessarily overlap.
- Land p. 44 §32.73 names the Tactical Naval Movement Segment for a planned fleet attack; it does not establish repair completion relative to that attack. Scenario §60.45, printed p. 28 / PDF p. 4, says initial combat ratings are unaffected, need no repair and ships cannot move until GT2 OpStage 1. [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 3 retains that first paragraph and discards the subsequent list. No actual later damage, eligible repair window or result follows.
