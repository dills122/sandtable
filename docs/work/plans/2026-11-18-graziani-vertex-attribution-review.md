# Siwa vertex attribution: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Disposition:** Supported within the stated source and visual limits. Retain uncertain incident sides as unknown. C0133–C0134 is a separate candidate omission, not a vertex ruling or authorized data correction.

Visually checked [Map A terrain key and Map C](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF pp. 1 and 3, and [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 14 §§8.33 and 8.35. The key depicts track as a single dashed line and unfinished road as parallel dashed lines. Section 8.33 requires movement between adjacent connecting road/track hexes through a road/track hexside to obtain the benefit. Section 8.35 puts slope and escarpment splash on the down side and describes ridges as directionless. Neither cited section nor the key supplies a tie-break for a route at a three-hex vertex, a dash-gap projection rule, or a multiple-side crossing convention. This finding is limited to those passages; it is not an exhaustive errata or ruling search.

The east crop shows a single dash crossing the C0133–C0134 shared side over the C0134 number, away from the vertices. The pinned [side records](../../../src/Cna.Core/Content/HistoricalMap/hexsides.v1.json) have track for C0130–C0131, C0131–C0132, and C0132–C0133, but no explicit C0133–C0134 pair. Sparse absence does not prove clear terrain; the image supports a candidate missing track, subject to source and provenance review before a data change.

The same scan shows crowding, terrain ink or dash gaps at the C0324, C0523, C0230 and C0426/C0427/C0527 junctions. The proposed unknown dispositions are warranted; pinned features do not independently adjudicate their crossings. At C0527, the single and paired dashes approach but do not visibly touch. These observations do not establish a complete route, seam crossing, movement legality or impossibility.
