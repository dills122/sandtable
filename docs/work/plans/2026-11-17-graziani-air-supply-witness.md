# Graziani abstract air supply: setup-based witness candidate

**Disposition:** A Commonwealth Mersa Matruh-to-Alexandria move is a plausible, setup-based candidate in GT1 OpStage 1's Organization Phase, not a proven legal play. Initial stocks can provide the 12-Fuel payment from a separate counter, avoiding the open self-payment and pooling questions. Enemy distance, the exact Alexandria destination and HQ, directional limit, startup sequence and actual intervening state still require certification. No distance, player allocation or intervening action is assumed here. This strengthens the amount-only example in the [shortage review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-shortage-continuation-review.md), without changing its disposition.

## Source constraints

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §32.0 apply abstract logistics and air when playing Land without the Air and Logistics Games. Section 32.37 itself permits each player to air-transport **one Supply Unit per Game-Turn**, in an OpStage Organization Phase; this is abstract capacity, not an aircraft count or a per-stage allowance. Both endpoints must be a major city, village, or Tripoli/Tunisia box. Before transport, spend 12 Fuel from a Supply Unit in the origin hex. No enemy combat unit may be within five hexes of the origin. An Axis shipment may not go farther east, or Commonwealth farther west, than the respective extreme hexrow occupied by a friendly division, shell or full. Destination requires a friendly division or brigade headquarters counter, not a battle-group HQ. The owner may not move the transported unit or its supplies at all that OpStage; it may be drawn upon under §32.16. This text does not establish a separate aircraft, airfield, or detailed-Air transport-capacity prerequisite.
- Section 32.14 prints five in a major-city hex and three in another type of
  hex **off-map**, with unlimited units in Tripoli/Tunisia boxes. Applying
  the three-unit limit on-map is the bounded RUL-GRA-08 planning
  interpretation, not unqualified source wording; Matruh's five-unit scenario
  setup and §59.65's unavoidable-overstack provision require separate
  reconciliation. Section 32.15 keeps separate balances, permits same-hex
  rearrangement in Organization, and removes a unit only when both Ammo and
  Fuel are zero. Do not assume a full destination can receive an additional
  unit, or use rearrangement to invent a particular allocation. See the
  [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness-review.md).
- [Scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.22, starts GT1 OpStage 1 and ends GT6 OpStage 3. Section 60.41 places Matruh Garrison at D3714 and 2nd New Zealand Division HQ in Alexandria E3613 and/or E3714; the latter wording does not select its exact hex. PDF pp. 4–5 / printed pp. 28–29 §60.92(C) replaces detailed §60.44 for Land-only: five real Supply Units at Matruh, each initially 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel; three each at Alexandria and Cairo, each initially 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel. It also lists one at Sidi Barrani and three together on map D west of Matruh. Detailed §60.44's unlimited city supplies are not the Land-only initial balance.
- For comparison, §60.92(B) puts three real units each with 15 Ammo and 50 Fuel at Tobruk C4807, two each with 10 Ammo and 40 Fuel at Bardia C4321, and eight each with 5 Ammo and 60 Fuel in Tripoli box. These are stocks, not proof of a legal air origin or destination. The setup's variable Dump 1 and 2 locations have their own restrictions; neither they nor any extra §59.66(C) unit may be placed by assumption.

## Candidate and remaining proof obligations

Consider one Matruh unit as cargo and a **different** Matruh unit as the 12-Fuel source, before either has spent fuel. That source's initial 60 would become 48, retaining 40 Ammo; the carried unit would retain its own recorded balance. This single-counter debit does not depend on pooling, self-payment, zero-stock removal or a shortage policy. Five units initially occupy Matruh, so the candidate removes one from a full starting origin rather than adding one there. Initial stock is not proof that this balance survives Weather or any other earlier operation: certify the actual snapshot and any applicable weather ruling first.

Select the actual Alexandria hex occupied by the 2nd New Zealand HQ and establish its qualifying city status, the applicable accepted capacity interpretation, and sufficient room immediately before arrival, including any optional or moved units. A four-unit pre-arrival ceiling is conditional on applying the proposed five-unit cap; it is not an unconditional source conclusion. The three initial Alexandria units do not themselves determine the HQ hex, their exact distribution, or all other occupants. Establish the HQ's qualifying status under applicable shell and HQ rules. Check that no enemy combat unit is within five hexes of D3714 at the actual transport time, including every legal setup choice; source setup labels alone do not establish measured distance. Check the Commonwealth westward extreme-division hexrow predicate against the actual represented divisions and accepted map-row interpretation; Alexandria's being a rearward city is not a substitute for that check. Establish legal placement, ownership, exact sequence and any setup choices, and show the player has not already used that Game-Turn's air allowance. Apply the stage movement lock and preserve its actual cargo balance after payment.

GT1 OpStage 1 lies inside the six-turn interval, but the source start statement alone does not settle all startup ordering or whether the necessary snapshot survives earlier stage events. If this candidate fails a gate, a later move requires a recorded legal sequence from setup through that later Organization Phase, including actual supply expenditure and unit/HQ movement; no later availability is inferred. A fully certified witness should name the exact origin and destination hexes, HQ, relevant division row, all enemy positions within the checked scope, fuel-source and cargo identities and balances, preceding events, capacity count, and resulting once-per-turn and stage-lock state. No such complete witness is established here.
