# Victory trace and convoy ZOC: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Independently checked the [trace source follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-victory-trace-source-followup.md) against the same original scans. **Disposition:** §10.29's pre-occupation distinction and contrast with §32.16 are accurately stated; no material source error found. Neither case expressly specifies the complete §60.81 victory-trace algorithm.

- **Actual convoy entry:** Land §10.29, printed/PDF p. 18, prohibits a Truck Convoy from entering an enemy ZOC unless that hex is **already occupied by a friendly combat unit**. Section 10.26 negates enemy ZOC for movement purposes when a friendly combat unit is present. Confirm the former's “already” and combat qualification; the later sentences of §10.29 concern noncombat units and specified capture circumstances. The first sentence is about **entry**, so it does not alone settle a convoy starting in that hex or whether an escort entering at the same instant counts as already present.
- **Local draw:** Land §32.16, printed/PDF p. 43, uses a medium-truck movement-cost analogy for the route from the friendly combat unit to a Supply Unit, with an infantry exception. It prohibits a line through impassable terrain or enemy ZOC unoccupied by **friendly units**, without specifying combat units in that exception. That language is not an express modification of §10.29's actual convoy-entry rule. The follow-up's hypothetical friendly Supply Unit example is conditional on that counter being accepted as a friendly unit for the local-draw exception; it does not prove an entire valid trace.
- **Scenario and abstract transport:** Scenario §60.81, printed p. 28 / PDF p. 4, uses convoy supply or a truck-convoy trace in the cited Italian and Commonwealth conditions, without expressly citing either Land case or deciding whether an actual movement or hypothetical path is required. Land §§32.32–.33 and 32.58 provide Motorization Point transport and its escort/CPA restrictions; they do not establish that every victory witness is such an actual movement. There is no basis here to read §32.16's broader friendly-unit phrasing as a direct correction of §10.29, or vice versa.

A known friendly combat occupant **before entry** satisfies the named §10.29 ZOC exception, not all terrain, occupancy, phase, fuel or convoy requirements. A noncombat occupant creates the proposed contrast only for the selected rule and classification; no path succeeds or fails globally without complete evidence and other legal conditions. The follow-up correctly keeps hypothetical trace policy, source endpoints, path direction, snapshot and disclosure separate.
