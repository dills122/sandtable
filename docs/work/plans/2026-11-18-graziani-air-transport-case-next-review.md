# Tripoli box air-supply case: source review

**Disposition:** Supports the [bounded case](2026-11-18-graziani-air-transport-case-next.md)'s initial figures, explicit box stacking exception and conditional payment arithmetic. No complete air shipment or off-map distance rule follows. Informed second reading of the same scans, not a separate edition.

- Scenario §60.92(B), printed p. 29 / PDF p. 5, lists eight regular Supply Units in the Tripoli box, each 5 Ammo and 60 Fuel. Their initial total is 40 Ammo and 480 Fuel before other changes. Land §32.14 expressly allows unlimited Supply Units in Tripoli/Tunisia boxes; the five-unit major-city-hex clause is separate. Neither says all other stacking restrictions disappear.
- Land §32.37 lists Tripoli/Tunisia box among permitted air endpoints and requires 12 Fuel from a Supply Unit in the origin hex before transport. **If** a distinct initially full co-box unit qualifies as payer, its 60 Fuel becomes 48 and the eight-unit total becomes 468 Fuel; its 5 Ammo remains, so §32.15's both-zero removal does not apply. This example avoids choosing self-payment or pooling but does not itself prove the box/“origin hex” mapping or an unchanged prepayment state.
- Scenario §59.66(C) explicitly treats Tripoli/Tunisia boxes as hexes **for that case**. It does not expressly generalize that convention to §32.37 payment or five-hex enemy proximity. Land §8.82 limits box entry to Axis units and Commonwealth airplanes; it does not itself supply the needed box-to-map distance or establish absence of every possible relevant enemy witness. Retain endpoint geometry and classification as separate gates.
- §32.37 also specifies destination division/brigade HQ, easternmost Axis division boundary, once-per-Game-Turn allowance, Organization timing and the transported unit's stage movement restriction while allowing draw. Source, actual state and earlier events must establish each predicate; eight stocked counters and unlimited box Supply Unit stacking are not a legal-transport witness.
