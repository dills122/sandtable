# Supply destruction: repeated movement/combat cycles

**Finding:** A new player command does not itself establish a new §32.17 sub-segment. Land §5.2 and §8.22 explicitly permit repeated movement/combat cycles, and §8.25 permits repeated combat even when neither side moves. That strengthens the need to model a real repeated cycle without assuming either that later destruction attempts require movement or that players may create unlimited empty cycles solely to retry destruction. The printed mapping of §32.17's “sub-segment” to §5.2's phase/segment/step outline remains unresolved.

## Original-source check

Visually re-read [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) PDF/printed pp. 12, 14 and 43, SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`. September errata SHA-256 `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`; the [independent cycle review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-supply-destruction-cycle-review.md) checked the September errata and found no direct §32.17 correction. No exhaustive later-errata search.

- §32.17 says players may attempt to destroy Supply Units they are with before **any movement in a given sub-segment of a Movement/Combat Segment**, pays one-half CPA of all units in the hex rounding upward, rolls once per Supply Unit, and allows one attempt per Supply Unit per sub-segment. The definition of the timing unit, target selection, dummy treatment and aggregation of CPA remain open. A failed roll does not silently refund cost or restore the once-per-subsegment allowance.
- §5.2, p. 12, calls G the Movement and Combat **Phase**, composed of Movement, Breakdown Determination, Combat and Reserve Release **Segments**. Player A may repeat segments 1–4, subject to §8.2, with each repetition including all four. The outline does not label a “Movement/Combat Segment” or identify the §32.17 sub-segment. Player B later undertakes F–L with A/B references exchanged. A phase, a four-segment cycle, a movement segment, and an individual unit move are not textually interchangeable identifiers.
- §8.22, p. 14, describes movement followed by combat and repetition while the phasing player wishes to move or have combat; §8.25 expressly allows repeated combat against a given enemy during one Operations Stage even if neither unit moves, subject to ammo and CP, and says combat can proceed when no units move. A no-movement combat cycle is therefore a real source possibility in that provision. It is **not** proof that any given combat-only cycle, destruction attempt, chosen target, or consecutive retry is legal: combat obligations, target, CP, ammunition, prior attempt scope and the unresolved mapping must be established.
- §8.23 allows phasing units ending a Movement Segment within two hexes of an enemy to move again, and restricts others subject to its reserve exception. September errata p. 1 corrects its final cross-reference from §8.22 to §8.23. Section 8.17 additionally limits voluntary CP expenditure for non-motorized units during their portion of the stage; its printed percentage is corrected from 50% to 150% in the same errata. Which expenditures fall within that limit requires separate adjudication; do not assume the destruction cost automatically permits an otherwise illegal action. It governs movement continuation; it does not say that destruction attempts count as movement or that a unit must physically move to start another combat. Apply its own scope rather than fabricating a universal destruction-retry exception or prohibition.

## Consequences and review vectors

Define a stable timing identity from the finally accepted interpretation, including game turn, Operations Stage, player portion and actual cycle/sub-segment boundary; log closure and movement occurrence. Repeated requests in one identified sub-segment cannot reset a unit's once-only limit or generate another die. A genuinely new source-recognized cycle with eligible no-movement combat may distinguish a per-cycle policy from a per-stage policy, but it does not by itself establish a new §32.17 sub-segment. Empty repetitions with no move or combat, a player-declared arbitrary boundary, later non-phasing reactions, and whether an opponent may destroy before the other's movement remain separate source/policy questions. Preserve unknown or unsupported until resolved.

After accepting a contract, test failed destruction followed by same-identity duplicate, a later actual movement/combat cycle, an otherwise valid no-movement combat cycle, and an attempted empty-cycle retry. Track the targeted Supply Unit across identity changes, mixed outcomes, CPA and RNG snapshots, and compare uninterrupted with replayed execution. Preflight complete target and stack data, but do not expose hidden supply/dummy identities through option lists, errors or outcome timing. These are synthetic discrimination vectors, not assertions that each sequence is permitted or reachable. No runtime, map, readiness or test changes.
