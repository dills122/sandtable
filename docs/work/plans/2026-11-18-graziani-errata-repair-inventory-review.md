# Independent review: September errata §§17–29

**Result:** The inventory's transcriptions match the visually checked errata. Two applicability details should accompany future use: §24.15's exception concerns **road/track stacking**, not the §24.12 CP interruption; §24.72 sits under an expressly **Air Game Only** heading. Neither changes the existing weather note's storm analysis on its own.

- Errata PDF p. 2 middle column §24.15 says §24.12 is an exception to §24.15's last sentence. Original Land p. 36 middle column §24.15 has one sentence joining one-project and all-stacking-rules clauses. Section 24.12 separately says construction units do not affect **road/track stacking limits**; that is the specific apparent exception to the general stacking clause. Its preceding CP-expense halt is a different statement. The inventory accurately avoids asserting a reset, but its juxtaposition with the CP halt could suggest the wrong application. Do not extend the exception to other stacking categories or one-project limits without further authority.
- Errata p. 2 §24.72 adds Commonwealth SGSUs and engineer-capable HQs as constructors of airfields and basins. Original Land p. 37 middle column headings §24.7 expressly say **Constructing Air Facilities (Air Game Only)**; §24.72 otherwise assigns airfields and flying-boat basins to engineer battalions and gives a separate eligibility list for landing strips and alighting areas. The correction adds to the former list; it does not establish a Land-only construction action, a specific existing unit, a site, or fulfilled material, time and control conditions. Exact scope of the phrase “with Engineer capability” for SGSUs can be preserved as printed rather than settled by punctuation.
- Errata p. 2 §28.17's change from 1 to 5 has a concrete context: original Land p. 40 right column requires one Guard Point at each detention camp and, when moving, one Guard Point per **1 Prisoner Point**. The fourth-line correction changes that moving ratio to one guard per **5**; it does not change the one-guard detention-camp sentence. Actual captures, rounding and scenario eligibility were not assessed.
- Remaining table entries match scanned text, including §20.3's SGSU reference and §20.72's one-month/planning-month correction. The former's cross-reference to Air §34.82 and §24.7's Air-only label warrant mode-specific review before treating them as Land-only actions. No proof of an actual repair, replacement, prisoner, raider, training or construction event follows. §29.1 and §29.61's contradictory week ranges are accurately retained.
