# Abstract supply destruction: actor and owner boundary

**Finding:** §32.17 permits “Players” to attempt destruction of Supply Units “they are with,” and says half the CPA of **all units in the hex** is expended. It does not expressly say friendly-only, owning player, phasing-only, or both sides; nor does it specify whose units pay when hypothetical opposing occupants coexist. Co-location, ownership, actor authority, timing and cost contributors must be determined separately. No contested legal state or action is established here.

## Checked source

- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §§32.12–.18, defines abstract Supply Units by Supply Dump counters, has no combat capabilities, and states §32.17's player/“with” and all-units cost wording. It calls for a die for each Supply Unit in the hex, prior to any movement in the given sub-segment, with one attempt per Supply Unit per sub-segment. No explicit ownership adjective limits target or CP contributors in this passage. “Players” alone does not prove both may act in the opponent's phase, and “with” does not precisely define actor identity or an enemy counter's eligibility. See [target scope](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-target-scope.md).
- The glossary §3.1 p. 5 defines a unit as a counter capable of expending Capability Points, combat unit by ability to engage, and attachment as same-hex representation by a parent. It uses friendly/enemy terminology elsewhere but contains no separate friendly-unit definition in that inspected glossary. Those definitions do not settle whether all co-located counters, represented components or opposing pieces pay §32.17's cost, or how a Supply Unit with its own special rules interacts with the generic definition. §6.14 p. 12 tracks each unit's CP across both players' portions. As the [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-actor-scope-review.md) notes, §6.16 prohibits transferring CP between units and carrying unused CP into the next Operations Stage. Neither rule settles whether an opponent may command a unit's expenditure or how the hex-wide cost is allocated; do not infer cross-unit pooling.
- §8.13 p. 14 generally says a unit may never enter a hex containing an enemy unit, with a stated §27.4 cross-reference and separate aircraft treatment. §32.13 p. 43 specifies capture on **enemy combat-unit entry** into a Supply Unit's hex and immediate/free use of its supplies. Their relation requires a rule hierarchy and legal state transition; the entry case neither proves an enduring mixed-side stack nor makes a specific entry automatically impossible. Non-combat entry, occupied defense, ZOC-only capture, subsequent ownership and dummy discovery are distinct cases. Do not simply assume the target remains enemy-owned after a successful capture.
- §10.29 p. 18 provides a separate conditional capture for a qualifying non-combat unit alone in enemy ZOC during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase with no strength of any type. Its applicability to Supply Units, the meaning of alone and any application of §32.13's entry-specific immediate use remain open. A qualifying ZOC is more than adjacency. See [independent ZOC review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-zoc-capture-followup-review.md).
- §5.2.G p. 12 assigns Player A a repeatable Movement and Combat Phase; Player B later undertakes F–L with roles reversed. §8.11 p. 13 permits certain non-phasing movement. Neither passage says §32.17 is a phasing-player-only action or permits a non-phasing player to initiate it. Its “before any movement” timing and undefined sub-segment cannot be mapped to actor authority by implication; see [timing proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-timing-policy.md), still unadmitted.
- The [common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) p. 1 §6.3 lists one-third CPA to blow a supply dump; §32.17 says half of all units' CPA. As §32.12 uses a Dump counter, scope and precedence require a choice; §54.14's detailed-dump phasing-unit procedure is not an express abstract actor rule. See [cost review](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-cost-hierarchy-review.md). The [prior errata review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-destruction-review2.md) found no direct §32.17 amendment in its bounded inspection; no exhaustive later-source search is claimed.

## Decision and verification gates

Before admission define a legitimate actor and phase, qualifying “with” relationship, target ownership and capture/discovery transition, affected unit and represented-parent set, CPA basis/rounding and permission to debit. Compare candidate friendly-only and literal all-occupants readings without asserting either is printed. Missing occupancy, provenance, actor or movement history must not become an assumed empty hex. If no reviewed choice and complete authoritative state establish the transaction, keep it unavailable under a side-safe observation protocol.

Synthetic comparisons: owner and non-owner in their own and opponent's phase; friendly mixed parent/components; hypothetical opposing occupancy before and after a valid entry/capture transition; non-combat entrant; ZOC-only capture; mixed real/dummy targets and an already-attempted unit. Validate payment, per-unit attempt identity and RNG atomically, with side-authorized offers and downstream events observation-equivalent across hidden alternatives. These are discriminating cases, not claims of legal reachability or executed tests.
