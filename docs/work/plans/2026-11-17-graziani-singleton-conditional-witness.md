# Conditional Commonwealth singleton setup witness

**Finding:** The printed counter values and stacking rules permit a **one-point singleton stack arithmetically** at each of C3320, C3520 and C3020, under the specific setup assumptions below. The checked sources do not yet establish that every assumption is jointly legal in a complete scenario setup, so this is a conditional candidate, not a demonstrated actual or fully legal witness. No optional co-location is selected.

## Candidate snapshot

At the scenario-start snapshot before movement or construction, assume the only combat unit or represented combat constituent at each listed hex is the listed counter. Assign each hex's listed first-line trucks to its sole assumed local unit, consistent with §59.42 if the singleton and unit eligibility assumptions hold; the clause permits division among units in that same hex when more than one is present; in Land-only mode use the applicable motorization-point conversion and avoid selecting any optional extra supply placement at these hexes. Put discretionary eligible second/third-line trucks, detailed dumps or other optional markers elsewhere as allowed. This is a proposed owner choice; it is not evidence that such an allocation is globally legal or was actually made.

## What stacking sources prove

[Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) pp. 16–17 §§9.11, .14, .22–.23 and [common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) PDF p. 2 §9.4 place a non-shell battalion at one stacking point; the counter value and battalion-equivalent source readings agree. A unit's historical name alone does not determine its equivalent. Land §9.29 says first-line attached trucks do not count toward unit organizational size or road-space stacking limits; truck convoys have a separate road-space exception. These provisions do not make a listed truck quantity an extra combat constituent. Their assignment and possible supply loads still require the applicable mode rules; convoy and free-stacking exceptions do not resolve every possible mixed-hex §10.11 total. A fully specified legal setup must check the actual terrain capacity under §8.37, not just the unit-side arithmetic.

Under these assumptions, each unit has one applicable stacking point and does not meet §10.11's strictly greater-than-one test standing alone, even though its nominal raw defense exceeds §10.15's ten-point floor. The result is conditional on no other qualifying unit or represented constituent and the relevant self-occupancy/stacking interpretation. It is not a legal movement, terrain-side, fog disclosure or combat-obligation finding.
