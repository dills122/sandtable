# Proposal: abstract supply destruction timing, v1

**Status: planning interpretation only; not admitted for runtime.** For Land-only abstract logistics, propose treating one complete, actually entered §5.2.G repetition of Movement, Breakdown Determination, Combat and Reserve Release as one §32.17 sub-segment. Its opening is before any movement in that repetition; its close is completion of Reserve Release. This maps the source's otherwise undefined term to the smallest explicitly repeatable movement/combat sequence containing both functions, and allows a later eligible repetition to have its own allowance. It is a project choice, not an official correction or proof that a retry is reachable.

The rationale and limits are in [source boundary](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-subsegment-source.md) and [independent cycle review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-supply-destruction-cycle-review.md). Land §32.17 uses “sub-segment of a Movement/Combat Segment,” whereas §5.2.G names a Movement and Combat **Phase** containing four **Segments**; §8.22 describes repeated movement/combat sequences. The source does not explicitly equate those terms. §8.25 allows combat with no unit movement, but does not authorize an empty repetition just to acquire another destruction attempt. The v1 mapping is defensible as a bounded reading only if the authoritative sequence can identify a real repetition independently of the destruction command. Until reviewed and the gates below are met, fail closed rather than manufacture a cycle or guess from command count.

The [independent policy review](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-timing-policy-review.md) finds a lifecycle gap: eligibility at the opening cannot depend on an independently valid later action already having happened. A bare phase-entry record could instead be manufactured for a retry. No commitment protocol is selected here, so this proposal remains unadmitted.

## Proposed contract

- Identify a timing window by ruleset/version, game turn, Operations Stage, phasing player portion, and authoritative cycle ordinal. Open it on actual entry into the cycle before any movement; permanently close its attempt window when any movement begins or the cycle passes its movement opportunity. Conservative interpretation: count either player's movement, including reactions, pending a separate ruling about non-phasing authority. Do not infer a new ordinal from an order, a rejected action, an individual unit move, or an unrecorded client declaration.
- An eligible pre-movement attempt records each affected Supply Unit and its result against that window even on failure. Do not permit another attempt for that unit in the same identity; a later actual cycle has a distinct identity, subject to every other gate. No attempt is authorized solely because the clock advanced or a prior attempt failed. The complete cycle, including combat and reserve-release transitions, must be valid under the separately accepted sequence rules; this proposal does not authorize otherwise empty repetitions. A valid no-movement combat cycle can be a distinct window under v1, but requires independently valid combat and resource history, not an assumption that one exists.
- An initially entered cycle with no movement has a candidate pre-movement opportunity; closing and later entry need actual authoritative sequence transitions and the unresolved commitment contract below. New player portion and new Operations Stage receive different identity only after those transitions are legal. These identities alone do not determine who can destroy, which targets are valid, or whether a later attempt is affordable. Closing when the movement opportunity passes without movement is an additional conservative policy choice, not a printed consequence of observed movement.
- Alternatives remain plausible: a single allowance for the entire phase, a movement segment per repetition, or some narrower unprinted sub-segment. A phase-wide choice would deny a v1 retry after a completed cycle; an arbitrary move- or command-based choice could admit extra retries. None is established by the checked text. If source or authoritative timing cannot distinguish these alternatives for a required action, mark that action unsupported rather than silently selecting an alternative at runtime.

## Admission gates

1. **Rule mode and version:** §32.17 is in Land-only abstract logistics. Common chart §6.3 lists one-third CPA to blow a supply dump without an explicit mode qualifier; §32.12 represents abstract Supply Units with Supply Dump counters. As the [source review](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-subsegment-source-review.md) observes, terminology alone does not remove the apparent overlap with §32.17's one-half CPA. Select mode, version and an explicit source-precedence or project cost policy before admission; do not silently merge or select costs or target scope.
2. **Capability and state:** authoritative scheduler must establish current phase, player portion, genuine cycle and movement history, including reactions and recovery after save/replay. Verify actor authority and co-location, legal supply counters, stack composition and modifier, and whether selection among co-located units or dummy handling is permitted by independently accepted rules. Unsupported cases fail closed; no timing choice supplies those answers.
3. **Resources and outcome:** separately resolve all-unit CPA aggregation and rounding, parent/component counting, available CP and resulting cohesion, one die per relevant Supply Unit, stock and carrier aftermath, and distinction between destruction, capture and dummy discovery. Validate complete transaction before debit and RNG; commit costs, rolls, attempt identities and outcomes atomically. Failed destruction still consumes its authorized attempt and cost. Do not declare an unsupported target set legal.
4. **Privacy and persistence:** store hidden identities, state, decisions, costs and dice in authoritative state; public options, errors, events and timing must not reveal real/dummy status or concealed opposing assets. Preserve version, identities and movement history through replay and reload; ensure duplicate requests cannot reroll or double-debit.

Before admission, define what establishes a genuine prospective cycle: for example, whether a validated planned action or an irreversible scheduler commitment suffices. Specify cancellation, failed validation, prevention by destruction and a committed action that never occurs, including whether the attempt, CP, RNG and disclosures roll back or remain committed. No option is chosen here; do not retroactively relabel an attempt or let the attempt itself establish the cycle. Record a causal watermark proving all prior movement history is complete before opening; pending or missing history blocks admission. Define handling of pending, cancelled and rejected moves separately from actual movement, and of delayed or corrected movement events, including replay. Identity must also account for capture, replacement and genuinely distinct counters without resetting an existing unit's allowance.

Section 6.21 generally permits CPA overruns with disorganization; §8.17's errata-corrected voluntary expenditure limit applies separately to certain non-motorized units. “Available CP” therefore cannot mean simply remaining below base CPA, nor an unrestricted overrun. Resolve applicable limits and resulting cohesion per unit before drawing. Likewise generic error text alone cannot ensure privacy: hidden target count or dummy handling may alter RNG consumption, downstream events, options or timing. Define side-specific permitted observations and an indistinguishable downstream protocol, or keep the capability unavailable.

## Synthetic checks before admission

Check failed attempt followed by same-window duplicate (no extra cost or roll); an attempt after either side's movement (closed); and independently justified later cycle with a new pre-movement window (v1 could permit a fresh eligible attempt once lifecycle gates are settled; phase-wide alternative would not). Contrast a properly supported no-movement combat cycle with an empty-cycle retry, a changed player portion, and a changed Operations Stage. Exercise pending, cancelled and prevented commitments; missing or corrected causal history; target selection, CP overrun and cohesion; mixed real/dummy downstream traces; distinct Land-only/full-logistics modes; interrupted transaction, duplicate command and replay. These are discrimination vectors, not executed tests or claims of reachable scenario states. No runtime change or admission follows from this proposal.
