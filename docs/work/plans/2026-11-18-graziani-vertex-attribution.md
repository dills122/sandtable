# Siwa route marks: vertex attribution source check

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Disposition:** The checked sources do not supply a vertex tie-break. Retain uncertain side assignments as unknown; do not infer all incident sides, or choose one from pinned records. C0133–C0134 is a distinct, visually supported track omission candidate, not a vertex case or an automatic correction.

[Map PDF](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf) p. 1 key distinguishes a single dashed track from paired dashed unfinished road. [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 14 §8.33, require movement between adjacent connecting road/track hexes through a road/track hexside to gain its movement benefit. Section 8.35 explains slope/escarpment splash on the down side and directionless ridges. Neither section nor the key specifies which incident side owns a route touching a three-hex vertex, how to project dashed gaps, or whether a vertex touch counts as multiple crossings. The down-side rule classifies a terrain feature's direction once its side is established; it does not assign route ink to a side. This is a bounded source check, not proof no later ruling or erratum exists.
