# Graziani 5% convoy result: reachability boundary

**Disposition:** no inspected source establishes an actual six-turn 21–40 Bomb Points attack, 5% die result, or legal cargo witness. The `55–64` cell is real and includes ordered roll `56`, but a chart-cell possibility is not a reachable scenario event. Initial Indian/Australian qualification is conditional and does not itself determine the westernmost qualifying division, lookup direction, a convoy arrival, or a 21–40 column. Keep 5% handling open; do not classify it as unreachable.

## Source-backed prerequisites

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §§60.22 and 60.37, runs GT1 OpStage 1 through GT6 OpStage 3 and has the Italian player plan the rest of September's convoys before starting using lanes 2, 3 and 6. It does not establish a nonempty load in a particular stage. §60.41 lists 4th Indian at D3615 and 6th Australian in training at Cairo and/or Helwan (E1430), with stated detachments; these are not by themselves non-shell certificates or completed player setup.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 44 §§32.61–.65, use actual arriving simplified convoys, announced lanes, and a Commonwealth attack. Section 32.64 looks up bomb points using the lane and qualifying division, then uses sequential dice on the separate air bombardment table. It does not prove that an arrival, attack or a specific die result occurs. Under §5.2's sequence the Naval Convoy Stage and scenario's GT1 OpStage-1 start require a separate initialization ruling; a hypothetical pre-start attack cannot automatically use an unproved deployment snapshot.
- [Axis chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 34 §32.66, defines a qualifying division as non-shell and east of, or in the same east–west hexrow as, at least one **other** Allied non-shell division. The route-2 and route-3 21–40 entries are Dxx01, with their next 41–80 entries Cxx16 and Cxx21; route 6 uses Dxx11 then Dxx01. The table says to locate the westernmost hexrow **beyond which** a qualifying division is situated. The direction/equality and cross-sheet ordering are not formally resolved by a source-certified geometry contract; do not turn a plausible reading into a fixed scenario result.
- [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF/printed p. 12 §41.5, Axis Naval Convoys row: 21–40 Bomb Points and sequential `55–64` give **5%**. Under the ordered-dice interpretation supported by Land §3.1 and §15.73, `56` is in that cell. The source's §32.65 spells out 10% and 20%-or-more treatments but does not explicitly state the separate 5% rounding and allocation result. A recognized 5% chart cell does not settle that loss policy or prove a nonzero physical loss.
- [Independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-five-percent-reachability-review.md) identifies errata PDF p. 3 §41.5 corrections to the **Barrage Points** heading alignment and final heading, plus a result rename. Those corrections do not explicitly amend the Bomb Points 21–40 Axis convoy cell. This distinction preserves the chart observation, not a reachability or loss-allocation proof. ## Conditional threshold illustration, not a legal witness

To distinguish lookup ambiguity from arithmetic, suppose an independently admitted geometry puts C's east–west index at `col`, D's at `33 + col`, and interprets a qualifying division **west of** a threshold as situated “beyond” it, with strict comparison. Under this *illustrative policy only*, a qualifying division at Cxx20 lies west of Dxx01 (index 34) but east of Cxx16 (index 16); route 2 would select its 21–40 row. Another distinct non-shell division at Cxx19 could satisfy the other-division condition, assuming both can occupy those rows in a lawful snapshot. No qualifying division farther west may select a different row. This synthetic pair is not a scenario placement, movement proof, boundary-equality ruling, or accepted implementation. Different interpretations of “beyond,” no-match handling and geometry can change the result.

A separately [reviewed initial pair](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-pair-next.md) conditionally makes 6th Australian at E1430 non-shell and qualifying using non-shell 4th Indian at D3615, under co-located attachments and candidate D/E geometry. The Australian is the eastern member; Indian does not qualify using that eastern division alone. A third non-shell division at or west of Indian, owner choices, shell status and current positions can change the westernmost qualifier. This conditional fact does **not** place the westernmost qualifying division in the synthetic Cxx20 band or prove the 21–40 row. It also does not prove a negative result: other divisions, scenario choices and later movement have not been exhaustively excluded.

## Remaining proof for a reachable 5% case

A positive witness must bind one accepted ruleset and concrete timeline to: legal initial placements and attachments for two distinct non-shell Allied divisions; a certified map/threshold direction and westernmost qualifying position in the selected route's 21–40 interval; all prerequisite movement, training and stage history; an actual legally planned, nonempty convoy on an allowed route at that snapshot; an allowed attack; and an ordered die result in `55–64`. The 5% *loss consequences* additionally need a selected §32.65 policy and cargo accounting. No reviewed evidence here supplies that complete witness. A genuine impossibility proof would instead have to exhaust every legal qualifying pair, route, movement, convoy and stage through GT6, including choices and source coverage; this review does not do so.

Focused future tests: source table lookup `56 → 5%` at 21–40; qualifying pair versus singleton/shell, a third division farther west, both threshold equality policies and unknown seam; actual versus hypothetical convoy, startup and later snapshot; correct route and replay with hidden cargo protected; and separately selected 5% rounding. These are synthetic test vectors, not executed results.
