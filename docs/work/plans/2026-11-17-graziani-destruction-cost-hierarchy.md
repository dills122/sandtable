# Supply destruction cost: source hierarchy

**Finding:** Land-only §32.17 directly specifies the abstract Supply Unit destruction procedure and half-CPA cost. The common §6.3 chart separately lists one-third CPA for attempting to blow a supply dump, without a printed mode qualifier. Logistics §54.14 explicitly supplies a matching one-third procedure for a different detailed supply model. This is strong evidence for treating §32.17 as the Land-only procedure, but the examined sources do not expressly say that §32.17 overrides the common chart, or that abstract Supply Units cannot also be chart supply dumps. Select the Land-only interpretation as a documented project policy, not an explicit source hierarchy. Do not silently charge both costs.

## Original evidence

- Land rules, SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`, printed p. 43 §§32.0, 32.11–.12, .17–.18: §32.0 applies when playing Land without Air and Logistics. §32.11 disregards references to consumption of ammunition, fuel, stores and water and says only the supply rules below apply. This is substantial scope evidence, but does not explicitly identify CP expenditure as supply expenditure or mention §6.3. §32.12 uses Supply Dump counters to represent abstract Supply Units. §32.17 calls for one-half CPA of all units in the hex, rounded upward, and a separate die for each Supply Unit in the hex, before any movement in the given sub-segment; each Supply Unit has at most one attempt per sub-segment. A roll of 1–3 destroys a unit; add one when total stacking points in the hex are at most one. The rule does not specify aggregate versus per-unit rounding or define a selectable subset of targets.
- Common charts, SHA-256 `51aa5a5bfdaca3d23794da71a45a830d98b798d060097cde6418126d6bd63bb0`, printed p. 1 §6.3: “Attempt to blow a supply dump” costs one-third CPA; CPA means unit's Capability Point Allowance. No mode qualifier appears on that row. Separate entries cover construction of real or dummy/non-dump supply dumps and demolition of other items. This row alone does not establish which action an abstract counter represents.
- Air/Logistics rules, SHA-256 `9e11678e26269525c25fd276a760e827a7cc738b987b39e89fe14295a7164cf4`, printed pp. 22–23, PDF pp. 22 and 27, §§54.11–.15: detailed dumps hold ammunition, fuel, stores and water; §54.14 permits one phasing non-gun unit per hex to attempt a particular dump per Player-Turn and charges one-third of that unit's **basic** CPA, rounded up. It permits specified extra CPA to adjust the roll, caps total attempt expenditure at basic CPA and forbids exceeding CPA to blow a dump, and uses a percentage-demolition table. It permits blowing dumps in any Operations Stage segment; a separate retreat-before-combat case limits expenditure to one-third and requires vacating regardless of result. Its base is the attempting unit’s basic CPA, whereas §32.17 refers to CPA of all units in the hex. These restrictions, targets and timing cannot simply be imported into §32.17. Their match to the common chart explains a plausible mode distinction without adding an express chart exemption.
- Land printed p. 36 §24.9: constructing a detailed real dump costs CP and stores in the Logistics Game, and dummy construction costs CP; supplies can be in a hex without a constructed dump. Physical counter representation, a constructed detailed dump and an abstract Supply Unit are therefore separate concepts, even though names and counters overlap.
- Land §32.18 authorizes abstract dummy counters at a stated ratio and removes them on discovery. Logistics §54.12 says detailed dummy dumps hold no supplies and specifies discovery on enemy entry or bombing/strafing. Neither establishes that every abstract destruction attempt discovers a dummy, or that a mixed real/dummy stack's outcomes may be publicly exposed. Do not infer an abstract destruction target, no-op or discovery rule from the physical counter face alone.
- September errata SHA-256 `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`, p. 2 §32.0 warns abstract rules were untested; §54.17 corrects percentage-table entries. No identified §6.3 or §32.17 correction selects a CPA cost. This is a bounded scan of the available errata, not proof no later clarification exists.

## Policy boundary and discriminating cases

Under a proposed §32.17-only Land policy, a single unit with CPA 15 would pay 8 CP under half-upward rounding; a one-third reading gives 5 CP. This illustrates the different costs, not a ruling on unit eligibility, current available CPA, or multi-unit rounding. Multiple units with different CPAs are needed to distinguish rounding each half separately from rounding an aggregate; neither interpretation should be asserted as printed fact.

Keep alternate readings visible: the chart could be treated as a separately applicable dump action, or as conflicting general text requiring adjudication. No examined rule authorizes adding one-third to one-half for the same attempt. Resolve separately the meaning of “all units,” resource availability, simultaneous target scope, actual sub-segment identity, rounding, dummy discovery and player-visible events. Tests for any chosen policy should cover cost 15 → 8, multiple affected units and rounding, repeat attempts in one versus later qualifying sub-segment, mixed hidden dummy/real counters without information leakage, and explicit rejection or handling when cost cannot be paid. These are decision tests, not evidence that unresolved interpretations are source facts. No runtime change is authorized by this note.
