# Graziani six-turn exclusions: independent source review

**Disposition:** No material transcription or scenario-boundary error found in the checked claims. Retain the batch's narrow bounds; this is not an exception audit or approval for runtime admission.

On 17 November 2026, independently inspected rendered original scans: [Commonwealth charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf) PDF pp. 10, 13, 20, 30–31; [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF pp. 3–4; [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 3. SHA-256 of the three downloaded files matched the batch's recorded hashes. Hashes establish identity, not completeness or interpretive correctness.

- Scenario §60.22 specifies GT1 Operations Stage 1 through the conclusion of GT6 Operations Stage 3. §60.47 prohibits Commonwealth replacement-point arrivals before November; it does not itself prohibit planning. §60.41 places the 6th Australian Division in training at setup, expressly less the 19th Brigade and both listed field artillery regiments.
- Chart §20.78C defines Date(s) as first/last **planning** Game Turn. All first dates in the batch's table match their printed rows, including 2-pounders GT5 and the four recce/light/cruiser rows GT3. §20.78B begins at GT3 and describes four-turn arrival; §20.78A begins at GT1. These are chart bounds, not proof that every corresponding unit, equipment point, phase, or other arrival source is absent. Tiger convoy GT32 is additional to chart replacements.
- §60.45's first paragraph prohibits moving initial Commonwealth warships until GT2 Operations Stage 1 while allowing fire. Errata removes the later conflicting text starting “The following units are available.” Chart §30.6's next scheduled row is 3/8, and its deploy note allows specified Malta placements. This does not establish an empty fleet phase or exclude attacks and other ship activity.
- OA p. 10 dates Selby conversion 2/12 and disbandment 3/21, with an earlier Close Assault movement exception. OA p. 13's Australian rows and setup `D` marks, and OA p. 20's two later French rows, match the batch. These dates do not eliminate other setup units, attachments, or distinct lifecycle rules.
- Chart §34.86 limits its monthly pilot-arrival roll to November 1940–October 1942 inclusive. Scenario §60.46 gives starting Malta pilots and permits construction increasing Malta air-facility capacity beginning October 1940. Neither claim excludes other air actions or initial facilities.

**Limits:** Did not independently inspect Map D's turn track or Land §32.81 in this review; September–October mapping and the Land-only AA rule remain dependent on linked prior source checks. Did not audit all errata, later corrections, special events, mode-specific interactions, pregame planning, or phase-entry rules. The Australian and French findings concern printed OA dates; scenario overrides and alternate sources still require separate proof.
