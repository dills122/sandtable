November 18, 2026. Compared every printed numbered case in §§32.7–32.9 with [register LA-19/20](2026-11-12-graziani-source-register.md) and the [current queue](2026-11-17-graziani-remaining-source-research.md). This is a bounded source census, not a scenario-ready, runtime or accepted-policy audit. The printed sequence is **§§32.71–.75, §§32.81–.84, then §32.9**, with no separately numbered §32.9 subcases on the inspected pages. The [fleet/AA review](2026-11-17-graziani-abstract-aa-fleet-review.md) already checks these rules; no reviewed reading by itself proves an action or closes every interaction.

Land §32.0 applies when playing Land without both Air and Logistics; abstract fleet attacks and AA changes remain printed procedures in that mode. September errata §32.0 warns the abstract rules were untested; it is not a correction selecting a particular policy. Different mixed-game combinations require their own source authority.
