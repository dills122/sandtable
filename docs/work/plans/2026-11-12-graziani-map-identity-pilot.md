## Potential map-seam conflict

Original Land §4.1 describes overlaying an `01xx` row on the preceding section's
`39xx` row. The original map's §37.42 land-distance chart note says matching
hex numbers on adjacent sections are 33 hexes apart. These descriptions may
imply different offsets if the row labels are interpreted as a simple axial
coordinate system. A [separately maintained project](https://tangled.org/glassparency.bsky.social/cna-agent-sim/blob/main/informatiion/CURRENT-CHECKPOINT.md)
also reports this conflict and identifies ambiguous coastal joins, but its
geometry and resolutions are not imported here.

Do not infer a seam transform or repair the chart from the two sentences alone:
the printed grid's orientation, row staggering, row numbering, overlapping
copies and edge alignment all need to be inspected together. Pick specific
physical features and named/numbered hexes on each adjacent scan pair, inspect
their overlaps, and compare explicit §37.42 distance examples. Record every
disagreement, source hierarchy, off-map/sea boundary and erratum; a test that
proves one pair does not certify all section joins. Version any accepted mapping
and verify reciprocal edges, alias identity, distance versus traversability,
scenario setup and route legality. Until then there is no admitted historical
topology or victory-supply route proof.

The map-B/map-C scans give a candidate seam hazard near Gazala. A separate
project reports a B5034/C5001 disagreement. Our low-resolution side-by-side
inspection initially suggested a coast-versus-sea difference, but a higher
resolution check showed the obvious coastal land label is **B4934**, not a
verified B5034. The corresponding C5001 is blue sea. The B5034 label and exact
overlap were not verified; the earlier coast-versus-sea reading is withdrawn.
A November 13 full-resolution recheck of the retained scan shows B5033 at the
lower edge and B5134 nearby, but does not show a B5034 hex or label on the
scanned sheet. C5001 is printed as sea. B4534/C4501 show a dashed route at the respective sheet edges,
B4734/C4701 show rough-patterned terrain, and B4934/C4901 each show coastal land
with a road. These three visual similarities are inspection leads, not proof
that the named hexes are aliases or that their terrain/hexsides agree. Some edge
hexes are partial or staggered; compare full geometry and the original rule's
`01xx`/`39xx` wording with the map distance chart before assigning a transform. Under a hypothesized offset of 33 in the second numeric
component, B5034/C5001 might denote one position, but the missing B5034 evidence
cannot establish a discrepancy. Reconcile the physical sheet join, coordinate
orientation, stagger and source corrections before selecting behavior.
This is an investigation target, not a completed seam mapping or an adopted
land/sea correction.

## Related initial-supply locations

Scenario §60.92 names Tobruk, Bardia, Benghazi, Derna, Tripoli box, two
player-placed Axis dumps and C0716; Commonwealth locations include Mersa Matruh,
Sidi Barrani, a player-placed map-D dump, Alexandria and Cairo. See the
[supply pilot](2026-11-12-graziani-supply-accounting-pilot.md) for counts and
placement conditions, which are not reproduced as topology facts here.

The Benghazi initial garrison row §60.31 and supply row §60.92 both print
**B4827**, whereas the location summary and §37.42 land-distance chart print
**A4827**. The newly inspected Map A visibly contains the Benghazi city/port and
its own printed location summary also identifies **A4827**. The city symbol
overprints the underlying hex number in the viewed scan; use the map legend and
adjacent hexes as corroboration, not a claim that the covered digits were directly
read. This is strong evidence for a scenario section-letter typo, but all are
records of the same edition, not independent expert review. Check applicable
errata and placement implications, then version any selected correction; do not
silently alias an unknown location to a different hex.

## Map D correction

The retained September errata p. 1 specifies an escarpment on the hexside between
D2228 and D2328. A November 13 visual check of the original Map D scan identifies
both numbered hexes; their shared slanted edge lacks the heavy escarpment symbol
shown on neighboring edges, consistent with the correction. This is a same-reader
check, not independent review or proof those locations or the corrected edge
are reachable in this scenario. Preserve a single reciprocal edge and verify movement, sight and supply
effects under whichever detailed rules actually apply; do not assume every
mechanic treats the edge identically. See register row ML-03.

## Map admission work

1. Verify the retrieved archival map and applicable correction set. Record period
   provenance, access/rights and independent reviewer; keep scans outside Git
   unless rights are explicitly resolved.
2. Double-enter each scenario setup, objective, arrival, port, facility and
   off-map location against its own source plus the map. Reconcile competing
   spellings, section letters, multi-hex places and legal placement regions.
3. Inventory every required location and edge reachable under the scenario,
   including terrain/hexsides, coast, road/track/rail, weather section and Delta
   subarea, ports and controlled/off-map transitions. This list is a work request,
   not a claim that it is exhaustive or that reachable scope is already measured.
4. Validate connectivity, valid boundaries, reciprocal edges, terrain/transport
   attributes, allowed map choices and source/errata references. A printed
   distance table is a comparison check, not evidence of a legal movement or
   supply route; its shortest distance may cross forbidden terrain.
5. Freeze source-neutral IDs, canonical serialization and content hash; fail
   closed for missing or conflicting facts. Test scenario references, illegal
   deployment, legal choice boundaries, location ownership and replay. Enforce
   access/disclosure boundaries without publishing hidden setup choices.

Map extent, total location and edge counts, rights, distances and full six-turn
reachability remain unknown. Unverified data must not be presented as a completed
historical map or used to claim victory or supply routes work.
