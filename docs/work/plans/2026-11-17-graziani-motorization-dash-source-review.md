# Abstract motorization dash: independent source review

**Disposition:** Candidate's September 1940 dash and January 1943 `2/4` readings are correct. The §58.5 chart gives no dash legend or numeric September value. The inspected Land, abstract-air and September errata passages do not resolve whether that dash means zero, inapplicable or unspecified. Calendar-indexing and startup remain separate decisions.

## Source checks

- [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF/printed p. 13 §58.5: the 1940 row shows dashes January through September, then `6/2`, `5/3`, `4/4`; 1943 shows `2/4` in January and dashes February through December. Its legend identifies the first number as Commonwealth and the second as Axis percentage of North African Motorization Points destroyed; the Axis legend explicitly includes Tripoli/Tunis etc. It does not explain a dash or print September `0/0`. The end-of-table pattern is compatible with multiple readings, not proof of a chosen convention.
- On the same page, §46.3's separate anti-aircraft table prints a result-zero row and dashes for unavailable result ranges. That supports only its own table structure, not a universal definition for §58.5. A dash is source data; do not silently replace it with a numeric zero or suppress a scheduled event.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 44 §§32.57 and 32.59: the first Naval Convoy Stage of each month determines the previous month's Motorization Point losses and refers to the monthly chart; §32.59 is headed Abstract Truck/Motorization Loss Chart. Land §32.57 specifies percentage of each player's total on-map Motorization Points, including Tripolitania/Tunis boxes, and side-specific removal areas. Those instructions do not explicitly resolve whether the chart month means the event month or the loss month, whether a partial pre-scenario month is processed, or how to interpret a dash. The chart's §58.5 numbering differs, though its contents and abstract-air cross-references make it a strong candidate for the intended table.
- [Scenario/abstract-air rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 1 / printed p. 25 §§58.42–.44, refer physical-truck loss calculation to §32.57 and §32.59's percentage chart. Section 58.42 separately says the owner chooses locations, subject to proportional truck-type and convoy/attached distribution; its hypothetical 10% example does not assign a month or explain `–`. Those physical-truck distribution instructions are not automatically Land-only Motorization Point rules.
- Bounded text search of [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) found no explicit §32.57, §32.59 or §58.5 correction; §32.0 warns abstract rules were untested. This does not exclude later or indirect authority.

## Consequences

Keep source dash, explicit 0%, an inapplicable period and an uninspected value distinguishable. The candidate's fail-closed planning recommendation is appropriate while event scheduling, month choice, dash semantics, rounding, pool snapshot, removal allocation and observation rules remain unadmitted; it is not an official source ruling that an event must or must not occur. Test October/September lookup only as conditional on the chosen index and startup policy; independently test numeric side order, denominator and pool-removal scope without importing physical-truck allocation into Land-only play.
