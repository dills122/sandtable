# Alexandria starting tank repair: source review

**Disposition:** Supports [scenario repair case](2026-11-18-graziani-repair-scenario-case.md)'s starting figures and bounded singleton ambiguity. No particular facility, die result, successful repair or precedence ruling follows. Informed second reading of the same scans, not an independent edition.

- Scenario §60.41 lists **broken-down** Alexandria vehicles: two TOE of A9 Cruiser and one TOE of A10 Cruiser tanks. The text does not allocate those points to a specific Alexandria hex. A broken-down-vehicle attempt uses §22.8 when otherwise eligible; the distinct §22.44 destroyed-tank table is not automatically applicable to that starting description. Facility presence and placement, phase, control, towing, weather and Land-only availability require separate evidence.
- Land §22.34 calls for a die per broken-down vehicle type at a facility and refers to §22.25 for percentage details. Section 22.25 rounds fractions up but disregards a 10% result when only one TOE point is being repaired. Chart §22.8's singleton note specifically refers to asterisked `10%*` results; its **major facility** 10% at adjusted die 8 has no asterisk. For a proven one-point A10 lot, applying the §22.25 exception yields zero; applying only the chart's starred-cell exception and ordinary rounding yields one. Neither source alone selects the hierarchy; actual type/hex grouping and all eligibility conditions remain separate.
- Chart §22.8's field tank `10%*` cells at adjusted 2–4 explicitly make a singleton zero. An otherwise eligible two-point same-type field lot at 10% rounds up to one. These are conditional arithmetic examples, not a selected die or proof that both A9 points are co-located and eligible.
- §22.34 adds one to a facility die for a major city's fortification reduced by one level and two for reduction by two or three levels. Raw die 6 plus an independently established +2 gives adjusted 8; the setup entry alone proves neither such reduction nor that roll. September errata §22.8 rejects the chart explanation's last two die-modifier sentences and directs the reader to §22.34; errata §22.34 separately removes its reference to §22.35, not the reference to §22.25. Retain the contested starting fortification and facility-location interpretations.
