# Graziani initial Alexandria repairs under possible rain

**Date:** November 18, 2026. This is a conditional scenario-grounded comparison of [weather/repair source analysis](2026-11-18-graziani-weather-repair-construction.md), not a claim that rain occurs, any unit is repaired, or a complete eligible attempt exists. The scenario actually lists broken-down tanks at Alexandria; whether a particular tank and a major facility share a qualifying hex at its Maintenance Segment remains to be established.

## Source facts and conditional case

- Scenario §60.41, printed p. 27 / PDF p. 3, lists broken-down Commonwealth vehicles at **Alexandria: 2 TOE of A9 Cruiser Tanks and 1 TOE of A10 Cruiser tanks**. It lists the 2nd New Zealand Division HQ at Alexandria `E3613 &/or 3714`; the placement text does not establish the exact location of each broken-down point or facility. Land §22.31 lists major repair facilities in Alexandria and Cairo with the parenthetical “all hexes”; whether that parenthetical covers both cities is disputed. Scenario §60.44 also mentions a major facility in Alexandria, while §60.92(C) replaces §60.44 for Land-only supply. The [facility setup review](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-facility-setup-review.md) retains these separate placement and replacement questions; neither an assumed all-hex facility nor a particular co-location is established here.
- Land §29.1 determines weather each Operations Stage and the foul-location table chooses affected map sections. Under the existing *planning* season selection, the fall row contains rain results; common chart §29.7 gives location die **3 = D and E**. Thus rain affecting Map E is a conditional table outcome within GT1–6 if the selected season, startup and stage procedures apply; no dice result is asserted. Rain is distinct from the table's Delta exception for **sandstorms**. Establish the selected outcome and actual affected hex before evaluating weather.
- Land §§22.13(d) and 22.36 exempt **Major Repair Facilities** from the stated rainstorm/sandstorm repair ban and weather effects, respectively. If an initially listed broken-down tank point is still present at the start of a Maintenance Segment in a qualifying major facility hex during rain, the weather ban alone does not prevent the facility repair. Section 22.32 separately requires the vehicle not to have been towed during that Operations Stage. Section 22.13's other conditions, relevant abstract-supply interpretation, control, facility condition, grouping and die procedure remain independent. Weather exception is not a successful repair or proof of an available command.
- For a field-repair comparison, §22.27 points to §22.13(d)'s weather restriction. A broken-down tank in a rain-affected hex **without a qualifying Major Repair Facility** cannot rely on the major-facility exception; field companion, towing, supply and other prerequisites are also separate. This comparison does not assume where the original tank counter was placed, that a field attempt actually exists, or settle how an elective field-repair procedure in a hex containing a major facility interacts with the exception.
