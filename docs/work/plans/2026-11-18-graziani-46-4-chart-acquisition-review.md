# Review: §46.4 chart acquisition

**Result: chart identification and arithmetic check out; qualify the synthetic target group.** The [acquisition note](2026-11-18-graziani-46-4-chart-acquisition.md) should specify that its 36 bomber-class aircraft form **one target group of planes on other missions**, because the §46.3 note limits this shift to that group type. Group size and aircraft class alone do not state the mission category. With that condition, 36 yields two right shifts under the note, versus one in the superseded §46.4 chart.

Independently inspected [common charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), printed/PDF p. 11 §46.4, p. 13 §46.3 note and p. 16 index; [Air rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 17 §46.0 procedure and §46.4 locator; and [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 3 middle column §46.4. Local common, Air and errata SHA-256 values match the acquisition note.

The located chart's four ranges and shifts match the note. The corrected §46.3 note says each target group is attacked separately; it counts bomber-class or transport-class aircraft, including combinations, in a group of planes on other missions, shifts one right per twelve exceeding twelve, and illustrates 24 as one shift. At 36 qualifying aircraft, 24 exceed the initial twelve, hence two shifts. The chart's broader label “non-fighter planes” and Air procedure's reference to non-fighters in the hex are not identical to the corrected note's group and class wording; the erratum expressly favors the §46.3 note over the §46.4 chart. Do not extend its count to all non-fighters or pool distinct groups without further authority.

This flak correction does not itself create a Land-only truck-loss rule. This comparison does not resolve their independent chart ambiguities, establish an actual 36-plane mission or assert an exhaustive crossreference search.
