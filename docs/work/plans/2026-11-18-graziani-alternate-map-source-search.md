# Map D/E alternate source search

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Bounded public search for a clearer or different-edition image capable of resolving rail class or vertex placement. **No verified independent, clearer D/E scan found.** The strongest apparent alternative, the SPI resource site's all-in-one PDF, contains the exact same embedded D and E JPEG bytes as its separate maps PDF in the retained local copies. The open leads below are not evidence of a different physical printing, scan or grant of rights.

Before searching, consulted [source rights status](2026-11-17-graziani-source-rights-status.md), [source register](2026-11-12-graziani-source-register.md), and the [remaining research queue](2026-11-17-graziani-remaining-source-research.md): source scans remain outside Git; hashes identify bytes, not authenticity, license or redistribution permission. User handles the rights inquiry. No contact was made and no scans were committed.
