# Cycle entry: independent source review

**Disposition:** The [source note](2026-11-17-graziani-cycle-entry-source.md) accurately distinguishes optional repetitions, required segment structure and supported no-movement combat from an unproven empty retry. No checked text defines a cycle commitment or maps §32.17's sub-segment to a repetition. Preserve the qualification that requiring an unrelated act is a project choice, not a printed prerequisite.

## Checked evidence

- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 12 §5.2.G, says Player A may repeat segments 1–4 as desired, subject to §8.2, with **all four** in each repetition. The text grants repetition; it does not make the first movement/combat phase optional or require each segment to contain voluntary movement, attack or release. Breakdown determination directs checks for vehicles and motorized units of both sides; an absence of movement/combat is not necessarily an absence of all procedural effects.
- Printed p. 14 §8.22 says the phasing player moves, resolves combat after movement ceases, and repeats until that player no longer wishes to move units or have combat. This assigns the described decision to the **phasing player**, not automatically both players, and supplies evidence about termination. It does not specify whether a stated wish, a planned action or a committed action creates a repetition, how a wish is withdrawn, or whether a destruction-only repetition is legal. Player B's later F–L portion in §5.2 has roles reversed; that is distinct from a non-phasing player's reaction within Player A's portion.
- §8.25 expressly permits an attack on the same enemy more than once in an Operations Stage without either unit moving, and combat when no units move, subject to ammunition and CP. It supports no-movement combat, not an inference that every proposed combat is legal, that an otherwise empty repetition is permitted, or that a new destruction allowance results. §8.23 and other movement constraints remain independently applicable.
- Printed p. 43 §32.17 specifies before any movement in a given sub-segment and at most one attempt per Supply Unit per sub-segment. Neither it nor the checked sequence identifies that sub-segment with a four-segment cycle, specifies actor authority during an opponent's portion, or resolves declaration and cancellation. Do not conclude a later actual combat automatically opens a retry; also do not read an explicit unrelated-action requirement into the text.

## Planning consequence

An authoritative timing contract needs a versioned identity, phasing actor, entry and termination evidence, a movement-history cutoff, and cancellation or prevented-action treatment. Opening before movement while proving a later action creates a causal design question; a command or phase-entry record alone is not printed proof of a genuine repetition. Keep an empty cycle, no-movement combat, both players' reactions and a subsequent player portion as distinct test cases. Source evidence supports neither automatic admission nor a universal prohibition for destruction-only repetition; fail closed if required history or a selected interpretation is absent.
