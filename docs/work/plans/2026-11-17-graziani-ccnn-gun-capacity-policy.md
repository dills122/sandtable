# 1st CCNN HQ: gun presence planning policy

**November 17, 2026. Proposed unit-specific interpretation; not an accepted ruling or runtime change.** One plausible reading is that 1st CCNN division HQ deploys with one artillery TOE point of unknown weapon system: OA `N` says the unit possesses its characteristics maximum, and ID `g` gives maximum one TOE with an artillery point permitted. An [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-ccnn-gun-capacity-policy-review.md) found insufficient basis to rank this above the competing unresolved-assignment reading. A project could select it as a versioned planning interpretation, but must retain the conflict and unknown weapon; the sources do not unambiguously establish the assignment.

## Evidence and conflict

The [source check](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-ccnn-hq.md) and [independent same-scan review](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-ccnn-hq-review.md) identify Axis OA PDF p. 17's `1 CCNN` HQ row as ID `g`, TOE and weapons `N`, deployed `D`. The OA key on p. 4 defines `N` as possession of maximum TOE on deployment for non-tank/non-gun units; characteristics §4.46b p. 26 gives maximum one and permits one artillery TOE point. Read together, these support one present point, without identifying its weapon. The key also requires gun points assigned to other units to be listed by number and weapon system; no such specific listing appears on this row. That omission supports the competing reading that presence is unresolved, not a proven empty point. Maximum capacity and permission alone do not prove presence. The two Legion HQs' 65/17 listings concern different units and cannot identify this one.

This is a synthesis of those cited readings, not a new source inspection or independent edition. Their bounded errata and counter checks do not settle the conflict; other material may exist. Preserve both readings and the existing [rulings](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md) unchanged. Neither one gun nor zero is promoted to an uncontested source fact.

## Proposed boundary

If separately reviewed and accepted, apply the one-point selection only to this HQ's initial deployment and record it as an interpretation, with unknown weapon identity and characteristics. Do not assign a model, caliber, equipment class, rating, ammunition requirement, mobility, or other weapon-dependent behavior from the word artillery alone. Do not treat unknown as zero, an empty slot, or a generic usable gun. Capacity, selected presence, and weapon identity are distinct facts; unit-specific evidence or a different versioned interpretation can change the selection.

Any consumer requiring weapon identity or characteristics remains unresolved until supported evidence and a separately reviewed policy supply them. The presence selection alone establishes no combat result, legal action, readiness clearance, or playable scenario. Before implementation, define unknown propagation, legal-action and visibility behavior, lifecycle after losses or changes, persistence/replay and ruleset-version binding, and focused tests; changing policy must not silently reinterpret existing saves. If such unknowns cannot be represented safely, retain the existing unresolved initial assignment rather than inventing an operational substitute. This document introduces no new model, content, runtime behavior, or RUL-GRA decision; no gameplay tests run.
