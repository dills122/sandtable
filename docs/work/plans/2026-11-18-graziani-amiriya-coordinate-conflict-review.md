# Graziani Amiriya coordinate conflict: independent review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Disposition:** Agree with unresolved source conflict and preservation of source-specific coordinates. No correction or alias established.

- Visually checked [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28: §60.5 lists Amiriya **E2212** in the first scenario group's initial Airfields table. PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29: §61.33 lists Amiriya **E3212** under airfields for the separate Desert Fox scenario group. The latter is contrary evidence, not an identified correction to §60.5; different scenario setups may differ.
- Visually checked [original Map E](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 5, at both coordinates. E2212 contains no named place dot in the inspected crop. Near the Amiriya label, a black circular mark lies inside E3212, immediately above Commonwealth training figures and below the E3312/E3313/E3212 junction. Its proximity to the label supports a possible named-place reading, but the crop does not establish its identity independently of the adjacent training artwork. The mark is not an identified airfield symbol; a named place dot would not by itself establish an airfield there. Label overlap alone does not place the point in E3312.
- Visually checked [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF pp. 1–4, including Maps and scenario entries; no direct correction of §60.5 or Amiriya coordinates found. Extracted-text search for `Amiriya`, `2212`, `3212`, `60.5`, and `airfield` found no coordinate correction. This bounded review does not rule out indirect corrections or other editions.

The [coordinate-conflict plan](2026-11-18-graziani-amiriya-coordinate-conflict.md) accurately separates scenario, map, and errata evidence. Preserve §60.5's E2212 for its source record and §61.33's E3212 for its own scenario; do not silently move the Graziani facility, equate the coordinates, or infer a printed airfield from the map mark. Any selected correction needs explicit precedence or a separately reviewed, versioned interpretation.

Review used independent visual renders of scenario pp. 4–5, errata pp. 1–4, and Map E crops at 300 dpi (E3212: x 1700, y 4650, 1100 × 900; E2212: x 1840, y 6320, 800 × 800). Same source scans as plan; this review is not independent edition evidence. No runtime or data changes; no gameplay tests.
