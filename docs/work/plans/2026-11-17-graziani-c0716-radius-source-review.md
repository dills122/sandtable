# Graziani C0716 radius: independent review

**Disposition:** The source row and geometric table check out under the stated inclusive, shortest-hex-step interpretation. They do not establish an adjudicated deployment area. No runtime admission follows.

## Source and applicability

The [scenario scan](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §60.31, lists `Within 3 Hexes of C0716: Saharan Det (I); Trucks: 5 light` as its own Italian initial-deployment row. The preceding `Anywhere in Libya` heading governs a separate row; this does not itself impose a Libya constraint on the detachment. Five light trucks are explicitly listed; neither five medium trucks nor a fixed detachment placement follows. The §60.34 fixed C0716 dump is a separate supply entry. Section 60 contains both Graziani's Offensive and the Italian Campaign; its setup is shared scenario-group material, not evidence of a separate later placement. Review used the same published scans, not a second edition or independent printing.

## Geometry check

On [Map C](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 3, the printed first coordinate is the staggered column. At C0716 its six adjacent cells are C0615, C0616, C0715, C0717, C0815 and C0816. For this local grid, odd first-coordinate columns connect to neighboring columns at last-coordinate offsets −1 and 0; even columns connect at 0 and +1. Applying those transitions in a breadth-first traversal from C0716 through three steps independently reproduces the plan's 37 cells, with ring counts 1, 6, 12 and 18 and its seven column ranges. For example, C0716 → C0815 → C0915 → C1014 is a three-step path; C0713 is three and C0712 four. This parity is local map geometry, not a verified global sheet-seam rule.

The table assumes origin inclusion and distance ≤3. Scenario wording does not explicitly define these endpoints, nor does it explicitly adopt the §37.42 distance-chart counting procedure for deployment. These are reasonable proposed interpretations, not a sourced ruling. A distance-three geometric candidate at Giaribub C1014 does not prove legal co-location, stack capacity, or player selection.

## Remaining decisions

Terrain, political boundaries, stacking, organization, unit-specific deployment rules and any scenario exceptions still need separate evidence before candidate cells become legal placements. Formation composition and whether components may deploy separately also remain open. Verify the precise treatment of the five listed trucks from general setup and transport rules before assigning points or attachment. September 1979 errata §60.31 concerns the separate XXI Corps artillery duplication; that limited check cannot rule out other corrections. The plan's cautions properly preserve these boundaries.
