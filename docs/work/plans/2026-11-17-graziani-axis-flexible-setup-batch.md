Adjacent setup choices matter even though outside the named subsections. §60.33 gives 30 light, 100 medium and 25 heavy second/third-line truck points anywhere in Libya, plus 25 light/140 medium/40 heavy at Tripoli and 10 light/50 medium at any air facility. Section 59.44 requires on-map supply trucks on a friendly combat unit, supply dump, or major city/town/oasis; §59.63(B) ignores air-facility trucks when air is abstracted. §59.63–.66 derive initial motorization and allow certain additional supply loads, with 30 other unattached motorization points in a hex able to start with an extra regular Supply Unit. These are contingent owner choices, not additional combat units or a free supply counter at every candidate. See [motorization follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-initial-motorization-next.md).

Section 59.2 allows organization of units already in the same hex within formation and stacking limits; it does not select a location or erase the regional restrictions. Distribution of the Saharan formation among radius cells remains unresolved. §60.34 detailed-game dumps and air supplies are replaced for Land-only initial supply by §60.92(B); do not add both tables. Exact distances to Commonwealth units, boundary membership, physical terrain, eligibility, counter identities, capacities, setup sequence and later corrections require independent adjudication before any proposed initial position is admitted. No runtime changes or gameplay tests.
