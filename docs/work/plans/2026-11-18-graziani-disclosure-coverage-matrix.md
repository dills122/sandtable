## Cross-inventory boundaries and next source checks

1. **§12.23 addendum versus main barrage/fleet cases:** visually compare Land p. 45's Commonwealth-warship identification addition with §§12.22–.24 and §§30.0/32.7, and any directly applicable erratum. The main census identifies the known-target exception; the addendum may identify a class of known targets, but does not alone prove a ship is an eligible land-barrage target, establish timing, or make fleet manifests public. The four inventories do not report this specific cross-comparison. A preliminary p. 45 visual read shows the addition exists; its mode/target interaction remains unreviewed here.
2. **§4.5, §§4.52–.55 sheets versus §3.61–.62:** compare the printed statement about keeping vital information secret and the specific Field Commander/TOE, vehicle, convoy and supply records against the map-stack and item-specific disclosure entries. Identify exact actor, field and any qualified exception; do not convert a description of what a sheet stores into an automatic opponent entitlement. These sections were not systematically compared in the four inventories. This matrix does not assert an extra secret or conflict without that comparison.
3. **Remaining addenda / scenario or chart exceptions:** inspect pp. 45–46 by numbered entry before making an exhaustive claim, then name a concrete conditional scenario/chart source if a specific disclosed fact is required. This is bounded inventory work, not a claim that an unexamined correction exists or every possible later source must be read.
