# FG-01: introductory Land §§1–4 disclosure pass

**Scope:** Visually scanned [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF pp. 4–11, covering §§1.0–4.6, for express audiences, hidden information, reports and relevant physical representations. This pass excludes re-inventorying addendum §3.6 and §§4.5–.55, already checked in the [limited-intelligence review](2026-11-18-graziani-limited-intelligence-source-inventory-review.md) and [record-sheet review](2026-11-18-graziani-record-sheets-disclosure-review.md). It compares their source scope; it does not create a digital role, view or permission policy.

| Source / topic | Printed statement and bounded interaction |
| --- | --- |
| §1.0, p. 4; §§2.1–.2, p. 4 | Introductory play overview and setup: the map counter lacks all information about its unit, so players track details such as current strength, attached subsidiary units, morale and supplies on sheets. This describes representation and preparation, not an express opponent right to inspect every sheet. Sections 1.0 and 2.1 identify no independent event-specific disclosure. |
| §2.3, p. 4 | Playtest role description gives the Commander-in-Chief responsibility for gathering, cataloging and disseminating enemy intelligence, and describes communication between teammates. This is a recommended team organization, not a specified opposing viewer, mandatory digital audience, blanket shared database or exception to §3.61. §4.51 separately says sheet grouping by player usage does not determine who actually uses each form. |
| §2.4, pp. 4–5 | Playing-time commentary; no express hidden-information or disclosure trigger identified in this bounded reading. |
| §3.1, p. 5: **Assault** | Glossary calls Assault ground combat in which combatants are **visible to each other**, unlike Barrage; it includes Anti-Armor followed by Close Assault. This is a broad physical-visibility description, not an instruction to reveal every identity, count, weapon or loss assignment. Compare §3.62's specific anti-armor and close-assault disclosures and §12.23's target identification rules, already in the [§§6–16 census](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-6-16.md). |
| §3.1, p. 5: **Desert Raider** | Glossary describes a raider as normally permitted to move on the game map **unobserved by the opposing player**. §§27.31–.33, .41–.44 and their timing/spotting qualifications remain the specific procedures, catalogued in the [§§17–29 census](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-17-29.md). “Normally” is not proof of perpetual concealment or an actual raider in a scenario. |

These classify what can be reported under another triggered rule; they do not on their own grant all attributes or an untimed world query. The addendum's separately reviewed §3.1, §3.4 and §3.6 preserve their own meanings. |

Counter corrections are catalogued in the [addenda census](2026-11-18-graziani-land-addenda-disclosure-census.md). |

The [review](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-1-4-review.md) notes §4.25's separate initial-setup rule: normally assigned units starting in the same hex are attached unless specifically stated otherwise, and subsidiary counters are not deployed. Apply the explicit setup exception and §3.6 rather than treating the representation rule as blanket disclosure.
| §§4.3–.49, pp. 9–10 | Charts, Organization at Arrival data and characteristics are described for finding arrival and unit facts. Printed reference material does not by itself authorize disclosure of every present composition, secret plan or opponent sheet field. Their use in a particular report or revealed target is governed by the applicable case. |
| §4.6, p. 11 | Game-parts inventory and physical production note; no event-specific audience or disclosure trigger identified in this bounded scan. |

## Source boundaries and errata

The [§3.61–.62 review](2026-11-18-graziani-limited-intelligence-source-inventory-review.md) limits opponent knowledge except where explicitly indicated and treats its examples as partial. The broad glossary descriptions above coexist with specific reconnaissance, barrage and spotting rules; no claim that those descriptions override the detailed triggers or authorize software event payloads is made. Physical markers, public rules text, record sheets, players' observations and actual unit state are distinct. No actual assault, raid, stack, disclosed report or six-turn witness is established.
