# FG-03: destroyed-tank capture, towing and captor knowledge

**Result:** A targeted check of Land §§14.53–.54 and 21.64/.67 adds a source-specific destroyed-tank distinction to the [vehicle-marker review](2026-11-17-graziani-vehicle-marker-fog-review.md). The rules distinguish capture-time relocation from later repair towing and give different direction wording; they do **not** expressly specify the captor's complete knowledge of marker contents, or clearly settle whether “three hexes” includes shorter distances. No capture or visibility policy is selected.

## Original text and bounded interaction

Visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF pp. 22 and 34–35, §§14.46, 14.51–.55, 21.51–.55, 21.61–.67 and 22.11–.14, and p. 45 addendum §3.6. Also checked the [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF pp. 1–3 for directly numbered §14.5, §21.6 or §22.1 entries. The [prior marker review](2026-11-17-graziani-vehicle-marker-fog-review.md) already compares §14.53 and §21.55 and leaves captor knowledge unresolved; [towing review](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-a-source-next-review.md) separately addresses §21.55 versus §21.67 for **broken-down vehicles**. This note checks the corresponding **destroyed-tank** linkage and does not claim an independent edition.

| Case | Source-specific observation | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capture choice | §14.52 says that when the enemy enters a destroyed-tank-marker hex with no other friendly combat units there, the enemy immediately chooses permanent elimination or capture. §14.53 says captured destroyed tanks **may be moved three hexes in any direction** at that instant; its three hexes may not include enemy units, enemy ZOCs or impassable terrain. | “May be moved” makes movement optional; the printed phrase does not expressly say **up to** three. Zero movement and a shorter moved distance are distinct cases. Do not substitute §21.55's explicit maximum-three wording for §14.53 without an interpretation. |
| Later towing and direction | §14.54 says, except for §14.53, destroyed tanks are treated as other units marked for possible repair and may be towed to a repair facility in the phasing player's Repair Phase. §21.67 says broken-down vehicles and destroyed tanks **if being towed** must be towed toward the nearest temporary or major repair facility. | §14.53's “any direction” describes the capture-time move; §21.67 gives a nearest-facility towing direction. Section 21.64 expressly cites §14.53 among towing-at-capture exceptions, making their interaction relevant, but these passages do not expressly decide whether the nearest-facility direction limits the instant relocation. Do not assume it does or does not. |
| Enemy-control condition | §21.64 generally prohibits towing a vehicle in an enemy-controlled hex but excepts towing at the instant of capture, citing §§21.55 and 14.53; it says friendly units negate enemy ZOCs in its scope. | That exception is not an express repeal of §14.53's separately stated restriction on the three hexes. Whether and how the capture hex, moved path, friendly occupancy and destination satisfy both requires a case-specific reading. It does not establish relocation eligibility by itself. |
| Hidden contents and repair | Addendum §3.6 makes broken-down, destroyed and abandoned vehicle-marker contents secret. §14.46 requires precise marker records; §§22.11/.14 allow controlled eligible repairs, including captured vehicles repaired separately, with additional restrictions. | None of these inspected passages gives a complete post-capture audience list or a blanket permission to publish all models and quantities. Conversely, repair-by-type and the captor's choices create an information need; no printed passage here defines its minimum disclosure. Preserve authoritative records and choose a side-safe decision/disclosure policy rather than inferring full or zero captor knowledge. |

The [abandoned-vehicle source review](2026-11-17-graziani-abandoned-vehicle-source-review.md) separately identifies §53.31/.32's salt-marsh procedure and unresolved abstract-mode interaction. Neither the destroyed-tank entry rule nor broken-down adjacency capture is automatically its abandonment procedure. Tank destruction, breakdown and abandonment remain distinct categories.

After an accepted interpretation, compare an otherwise eligible destroyed marker with **no relocation**, relocation of one or two hexes, and exactly three; vary nearer/farther facility direction and enemy-ZOC route with explicit occupancy. Separately compare equivalent outward histories with different hidden lots and specify what the captor needs to select a permitted repair or elimination action. These are proposed discriminator cases, not source-proven legal moves, revelations, gameplay tests or evidence of a Graziani capture.
