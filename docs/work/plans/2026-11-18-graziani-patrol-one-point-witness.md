# One-point patrol: fixed setup candidate

**Result:** The source names a non-reconnaissance infantry battalion and an enemy infantry formation at nearby fixed starting hexes. **A legally available one-point patrol is not yet proved:** the battalion's actual initial motorization, an admissible patrol path and stage conditions remain gates. The candidate narrows the witness search; it does not establish an actual die result, prisoner or disclosure.

## Candidate and evidence

- Scenario §60.41, [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, places **1st King's Royal Rifle Corps** with other Commonwealth units at **C3922**, and lists 15 medium and five heavy trucks at that location. Section 60.31 places the **2nd Libyan Division** with an attached tank battalion at **C3920**. These are fixed source setup rows, not a claim that both remain in place at the Patrol Phase.
- [Commonwealth OA/charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf) PDF pp. 5–6, 22–23: the 7th Armored Division row identifies 1st KRRC as `n`, `N`, `D`. The OA key defines `N` as maximum TOE for a non-tank/non-gun unit and `D` as deployed at start. The `n` row is an **infantry battalion-equivalent**, maximum six TOE, CPA `10+`; the `+` key says historically enough trucks were supplied to motorize its components. These entries identify non-recce infantry with a source-baseline six points, subject to applicable setup corrections. They do **not** prove which of the listed starting trucks or abstract Motorization Points are actually assigned, or that the unit is motorized at patrol time.
- [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 5 lists the 2nd Libyan Infantry Division's six `x`, `N`, `D` infantry battalions. This supports an enemy combat-unit target candidate at C3920 under the scenario's listed baseline, not a guarantee of its state after intervening actions. [Initial-contact review](2026-11-17-graziani-initial-contact-review.md) separately checks the fixed C3920/C3922 map pair: they are two hexes apart, with C3921 between, rather than adjacent. Distance alone does not certify a passable patrol path or negate an enemy-controlled intervening hex.
- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF p. 26 §§16.11–.14 permits motorized infantry to supply Patrol Points and sets maxima of two from a hex and three to a target; the checked provisions do not impose a two-point minimum. Therefore **if** KRRC is motorized and all other conditions hold, selecting exactly one of its infantry TOE points is a non-recce allocation within those maxima. §16.22 also requires an enemy-occupied target within five hexes and a permissible path; §16.15, §16.17 and §16.21 add cohesion, weather and combat/bombardment conditions. This note does not prove those gates or decide the scope of stage history.

The [one-point loss analysis](2026-11-18-graziani-patrol-interaction-followup.md) records the survival table's conditional 1 killed / 1 captured result on adjusted 6, the non-recce modifier distinction and unresolved excess-loss/prisoner handling. With an actual eligible one-point KRRC patrol against a target requiring a survival roll, raw 6 would be an appropriate discriminator **if** no other modifier changes the result; no roll is asserted here. Check initial allocation and motorization rules, an exact passable path including escarpment and enemy ZOC, contemporaneous occupancy, weather, cohesion and prior combat before calling this a scenario witness. Do not infer a legal action or reveal private target state from the printed starting rows.

The [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-patrol-one-point-witness-review.md) notes §16.22 expressly excepts a hex in the target unit's own ZOC from its general enemy-ZOC path restriction. Apply that exception when assessing C3921; it does not establish the remaining terrain, path or phase gates.
