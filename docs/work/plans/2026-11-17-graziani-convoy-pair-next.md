# Initial Indian–Australian convoy qualification: conditional pair

**Result:** Under the two documented co-located/default-attachment baselines,
a selected legal 6th Australian location at Helwan `E1430`, and the candidate
33-column map translation, **6th Australian qualifies** for §32.66 using the
non-shell 4th Indian Division at `D3615` as the required other division: the
Australian location lies east of it. This is a conditional geometric and
formation result, not proof that those two states coexist at an actual Naval
Convoy lookup, that Helwan is a legal full setup placement, or that a
particular bomb-points column or attack is determined.

## Evidence and assumptions

1. Scenario §60.41, printed p. 27 / PDF p. 3, lists 4th Indian Division at
   **D3615** and 6th Australian in training at **Cairo and/or Helwan (E1430)**,
   with stated detached/excluded components. The coordinate belongs to the
   Helwan reference in the printed phrase; it does not locate every possible
   Cairo choice or assert one shared location for the entire formation.
2. [Initial division analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-divisions-next.md)
   and its independent review record 4th Indian as non-shell under a baseline
   with the qualifying 5th and 11th Indian Brigades attached at D3615.
   [Australian analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-australia-shell-next.md) and its
   independent review establish a conditional non-shell baseline with its
   qualifying 16th and 17th Australian Brigades and their deployed battalions
   co-located and attached. Each has two qualifying brigades out of three
   assignable slots; §9.26's division-shell test is 50% **or less** attached.
   Reorganization, stack legality, placement and current composition remain
   owner/state dependent. Training alone is not a §9.26 shell criterion in the
   inspected text; it does not waive other restrictions.
3. Original Axis chart §32.66, PDF p. 34, defines a qualifying Allied division
   as a **complete (non-shell)** division east of, or in the same east-west
   hexrow as, at least one other Allied non-shell division. Its `Cxx01` example
   explains that the final two digits are the east-west hexrow and `xx` can be
   any north-south hex. Thus compare the chart's sheet and **last two digits**,
   not the first pair of digits or north/south position. The rule refers to
   divisions at the relevant lookup, not only their original OA assignments.
4. Land §4.1, printed/PDF p. 7, orders sheets A–E west to east but its literal
   `01xx`/`39xx` overlap wording conflicts with physical edge labels. The
   [geometry comparison](2026-11-17-graziani-seam-geometry-comparison.md)
   documents the candidate **33-column** translation, supported but not fully
   certified by other evidence. Use it as an explicit hypothesis, not as a
   newly established original overlay or proof of a traversable seam.

For a reproducible arithmetic example, set Map D's column offset to zero and
Map E's to +33. The candidate `D3615` has east-west index **15**;
`E1430` has index **33 + 30 = 63**. Thus the hypothetical Australian is
48 column positions east of the Indian. The origin is arbitrary; the relative
ordering is what matters. Hex staggering and source rules governing exact
same-row equality still need their own treatment, but cannot reverse this
large difference under the specified transform.

The [map identity pilot](2026-11-12-graziani-map-identity-pilot.md) separately
lists candidate Cairo city cells `E1730`, `E1829`, `E1830`, `E1930`, `E1931`;
under the same transform their indices are 62–64, also east of D3615's 15.
That arithmetic does not certify Cairo membership, placement, stacking, or all
possible choices. Helwan E1430 is the concrete scenario-printed example, not
a claim that the owner chose it or that every other potentially legal Cairo/
Helwan location was exhaustively enumerated.

## Direction and lookup limits

The comparison is **asymmetric**. In a snapshot containing only these two
non-shell divisions at the stated positions, the eastern Australian has the
Indian as a qualifying other division. The western Indian does **not** qualify
on the basis of the Australian to its east. A third non-shell Allied division
at or west of the Indian could change which division is the westernmost
qualifying one. A hypothetical same-east-west-hexrow pair can qualify in either
direction under the chart wording, but needs a separately certified row
mapping and two distinct qualifying divisions.

The chart asks for the westernmost applicable hexrow relative to a qualifying
division in a selected convoy lane; the word **“beyond”**, equality at the
printed threshold, the full candidate division set, and the map transform need
an accepted lookup policy. This pair alone does not determine which division is
westernmost in actual play, which chart row is selected, a bomb-points column,
attack eligibility or a die result. Check all divisions, not just these two.

Normal Land §5.2 puts a Naval Convoy Stage before each turn's first Operations
Stage, while §60.22 starts Graziani at GT1 OpStage 1. OA `D` means deployed at
GT1 OpStage 1, so a hypothetical pre-OpStage-1 convoy cannot automatically
consume those deployments without an accepted initialization and startup
policy. If the preamble is skipped, a later lookup uses that later
**authoritative** position and composition, not frozen GT1 baselines. No
minimum attack, mandatory move out of training or initial convoy event is
implied. Pair status and any error/preview must respect side-specific fog.

## Distinguishing vectors

- Given a certified snapshot with 4th Indian non-shell at D3615 and 6th
  Australian non-shell at E1430, and explicitly selected `D+0/E+33`
  geometry, Australian qualifies using Indian. With only these two, Indian
  does not qualify using Australian. Compare the reverse predicate to catch
  accidental symmetry.
- Retain the division denominator of three; make either division shell or
  unknown through a separately legal/unknown attachment state. A shell cannot
  serve as the required non-shell other division; unknown cannot silently
  become absent, shell or non-shell. Training alone does not flip status.
- Test a separately certified same-hexrow pair and a third eligible division
  west of Indian, including distinct identities and westernmost qualifying
  selection. Test equality versus “beyond” under each proposed threshold
  policy; never equate candidate row arithmetic with certified seam movement.
- Exercise GT1 initialization versus a later actual convoy snapshot, legal
  Helwan/Cairo placement and owner reorganization, changed map/ruling identity,
  stale state, replay and side-authorized observations. A missing qualifying
  source or unadmitted transform yields unresolved, not a guessed bomb column.
