# Review: bounded official-clarification search

**Disposition:** The cited leads support the bounded conclusion and authority distinctions. No new authoritative clarification found in this limited review. Search results, inaccessible text, and same-scan material do not establish that no later clarification exists.

- [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) opened in the web reader as four pages, dated September 1979; first page identifies the Map D 2228/2328 escarpment correction. Its text also includes a separate §8.37 terrain-chart correction, so the absence claim must remain limited to the four named cases and search method. Web `find` returned no match for `4.1`, `37.42`, `8.41` and `29.56`, as the note states. Local `pdftotext` search of the hash-matched copy `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb` found literal substring `4.1` three times **inside other case numbers** (`24.15`, `24.12`, `54.17`); it found no literal occurrence of the other three strings. The two search methods differ; neither result is a full visual or semantic review of all errata. In particular, a substring search alone is not a case-identifier search.
- [Grognard listing](https://grognard.com/m-article-page-list.aspx?GameID=c045) opened and dates its entry July 2021, labels it September 1979 addenda and credits Decision Games courtesy. Its [linked PDF](https://grognard.com/scans/CNA%20Addenda%20Sep%2079.pdf) opened as a four-page PDF with no machine-readable lines; this review's attempted screenshot returned a cache error, so its actual printed pages and byte identity were not independently compared. Posting date is not a later clarification date.
- [Decision Games product page](https://shop.decisiongames.com/ProductDetails.php?ProductCode=P3034) opened and describes an update in design stage, changed procedures and updated map; the visible description does not resolve the named original cases. It is a product description, not proof of a correction to the original or of source ownership, release date or permission.
- [Living Rules](https://basmith.net/cna/) opened and identifies itself as a provisional, unaffiliated restatement with separately recorded rulings. Those rulings may be useful leads but are not publisher or designer clarification. The [Land rules PDF](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) opened in the web reader as 47 pages with no readable extracted lines; this review did not re-inspect all in-book addenda.

A small independent search for the title with `29.56` rainstorm, `37.42` map, and `4.1` map overlap surfaced original/derivative rules and unrelated results, not a verified later official answer.
