# SQ-02: engineer HQ minefield construction candidates

**Result:** Two setup-listed division headquarters provide a concrete source case for construction eligibility. Both OA rows mark engineer capability, but §24.31 explicitly names **Commonwealth HQ engineers** alongside engineering units. Whether an Axis engineer-capable HQ is covered by the broader “Engineering unit” phrase is not expressly settled by these passages. No minefield, legal start or resource debit is established.

## Source case

| Candidate or rule | Printed evidence | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commonwealth HQ | Scenario §60.41, printed p. 27, places 4th Indian Division at **D3615**, with specified detachments. Its OA on Commonwealth chart PDF p. 15 lists 4th Indian Division HQ with superscript `E` and `D`. | The OA key p. 5 identifies `HQE` as headquarters with engineering capability; setup, parent representation and current position still need admission. |
| Axis HQ | Scenario §60.31 places 1st Libyan Division at **C4020**. Axis OA PDF p. 5 lists 1 Libyan Infantry HQ with superscript `E` and `D`. | This supports a source-listed engineer-capable HQ candidate, not an independent engineer counter or proof it can build at an action snapshot. |
| HQ capability | Land §23.14, p. 36, gives HQs with `E` by their stacking points engineering capability; §23.23 says engineer units and HQs with engineering capability may help build fortifications and other listed projects. | General engineering capability is not by itself an explicit permission to build every type of construction. |
| Minefields | Land §24.31, p. 36, permits any Engineering unit **or Commonwealth HQ Engineers** to construct minefields. §24.32 specifies one full Operations Stage, completing at the beginning of the next Construction Segment. §§24.35–.36 constrain terrain, one field per hex, major cities and enemy-controlled hexes. | A Commonwealth engineer HQ is expressly named. The sentence does not explicitly say whether an Axis `E` HQ counts within “Engineering unit”; §23 distinguishes engineer counters and engineer-capable HQs. Compare interpretations without deciding from nationality alone or assuming exclusion. |
| Resources and mode | §24.33 lists ordinary real-field construction costs of 15 Stores and 15 Ammo; §24.34 lists three Stores and no Ammo for a dummy. Land §32.11, p. 43, replaces ordinary supply usage/expenditure in Land-only play and states only its following supply rules apply. | The source comparison does not provide a separate Land-only construction-cost conversion, prove free construction or authorize an ordinary Stores ledger. Select an applicable abstraction policy separately. |

The two candidates differentiate HQ nationality and source wording without claiming either starting hex is suitable. Under a candidate interpretation that treats only the explicitly named Commonwealth HQ class as covered, an otherwise eligible 4th Indian HQ could qualify on that actor criterion and the Libyan HQ would require an independent engineer unit. Under a broader interpretation, an Axis `E` HQ might qualify as an Engineering unit. This is a policy discriminator, not proof of either legal action or a source-mandated exclusion.

Before an actual construction test, establish current HQ identity and engineering status, action authority, terrain, enemy control, stacking, weather, CP history and uninterrupted timing, resources under the accepted abstract mode, and any scenario-specific precedence. §60.7's start-of-game minefield statement and railroad parenthetical concern setup; they do not prove either candidate has already built one. See the prior [minefield source follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-fortification-followup.md) and [railroad-pipeline review](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-c-source-2-review.md). No setup state, expense, project or scenario reachability is asserted.
