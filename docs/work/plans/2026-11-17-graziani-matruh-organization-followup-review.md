# Matruh organization follow-up: independent source review

**Disposition:** No blocking factual error found in the scoped claims. Keep initial assignment and capacity unresolved; neither the counter notation nor the historical schedule settles them. The general statement in Land §19.2 that units enter already assigned is relevant context, but does not identify either battalion's parent or establish an exception for either one.

- Scenario §60.41 lists Matruh at D3714 with Essex, Durham Light Infantry and South Staffordshires after `Att`. Scenario §59.2 explicitly defines that indicator as attached but not assigned to the listed parent. Its `23/70` notation therefore cannot by itself establish present assignment to 23rd Brigade. OA p. 9 note f says Essex and Durham were not assigned to 23rd until June 1941 (3/38); note e describes 23rd's dissolution and reformation. Neither identifies their earlier assigned parent. OA p. 10 names Durham among units assigned to 22nd Guards at various times, but supplies no applicable starting date. These passages do not prove independence.
- Land §19.24 permits players to institute or ignore OA historical assignment shifts, within §19.21 restrictions, and requires a chosen shift during the listed month. The June note is historical evidence, not an unconditional automatic game event. This check does not decide whether a particular attempted reassignment meets all other rules.
- OA p. 10 says Matruh is brigade-level, forbids assignments, and permits up to six attached units, referencing the Maximum Attachments Chart. September 1979 errata §4.44b item 2 expressly assigns 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshires to Matruh at the start of the campaign and Italian scenarios. Graziani is in the Italian scenario group under §§60.0, 60.21–.22. The erratum conflicts with the OA prohibition; it does not itself state either battalion's hex or attachment.
- Land §19.4 and errata §19.5 describe additional attachments above normal assigned levels; §19.13 allows a unit to be assigned to and attached to the same parent, while §19.2 says detached assigned units still occupy assignment space. The six-unit Matruh text can also be read as a total attachment limit. Neither passage expressly reconciles that specific limit with the two exceptional assignees. Scenario §59.2 protects a listed formation exceeding organization limits under its restrictions and permits free setup attachments within formation and stacking limits; it does not independently locate the erratum's units or authorize player-created excess.
