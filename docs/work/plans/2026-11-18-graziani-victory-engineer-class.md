# VI-07: engineer capability and victory combat-unit classification

**Result:** A headquarters with engineering capability is not automatically the same classification as a separate engineer counter. For a concrete source candidate, scenario §60.31 lists the **2nd Libyan Division at C3920 with IX(L) tank battalion attached**; its OA identifies the division HQ with superscript `E`. If that attachment and the tank's combat status persist at the relevant snapshot, Land §3.31 supports treating the HQ representing it as a combat unit. This does not prove an objective occupant, qualifying convoy supply or a victory. The result follows from a represented combat attachment, not engineering capability by itself.

## Source comparison

I visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 5–6, 26 and 35–36, §§3.1, 3.23, 3.31–.33, 16.32 and 23.11–.14; [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF pp. 3–4 / printed pp. 27–28, §§60.31 and 60.81; [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 5; and [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 2 §23.11. This is a targeted follow-up to the [victory combat-unit review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-combat-units-review.md), not a rereading of every type or scenario state.

## Source-backed conditional candidate

Scenario §60.31 places **2 Libyan Division (I)** at **C3920** and lists **IX(L)** as attached. Axis OA p. 5 lists **2nd Libyan Infantry HQ** as `HQᴱ`, ID `b`, `N`, `D`, and an **IX(L) Tank Bn** with `D`; the OA key identifies `D` as deployed. The scenario identifies IX(L) as a tank attachment, and §3.23 includes tank-type units. Under a source-consistent setup interpretation where that combat battalion is genuinely attached and represented by the HQ, §§3.31 and 23.14 support combat-unit status for that **engineering-capable HQ**. That status depends on the attachment, not the `E` mark; verify constituent identity, setup legality and state before using it. C3920 is a starting setup location, not a proved strategic or decisive objective occupation.

Compare an otherwise eligible HQ with its actual tank attachment against a separate engineer counter on its own, and separately an HQ with only a noncombat engineer attached. The last case does **not** satisfy §3.31 merely by assuming the engineer is a combat unit; special HQ weapon rules or other exceptions must be checked independently. These are classification comparisons, not legal game-state constructions, victory results, a complete engineer census or proof a particular engineer has detached.
