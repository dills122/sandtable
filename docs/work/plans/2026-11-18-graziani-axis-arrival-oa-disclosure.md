# FG-01: Axis arrival schedule key and OA key

**Result:** The previously unreviewed-for-disclosure Axis schedule key and Italian Organization at Arrival (OA) key describe how to read printed force information; neither inspected passage explicitly announces a reinforcement's actual contents to the opponent. They do not make those facts automatically secret either. This is a bounded complement to the [selected chart disclosure census](2026-11-18-graziani-chart-disclosure-census.md), which inspected Axis pp. 2 and 33 but explicitly left other chart pages and reinforcement instructions unexamined.

## Method and source boundary

Visually inspected the retained Axis chart PDF pp. 3–4 at 120 dpi, including the end of §4.43b and the full Italian OA legend; no explicit secret, reveal, opponent audience or announcement was identified in those passages. This is a positive reading of the key and a bounded negative for those pages, not a full Axis chart OCR or an exhaustive search for other rules/errata. The chart's p. 3 schedule continues from previously inspected p. 2; it is not a new finding about all arrival categories. The September errata text search for `4.43b`/`4.44b` finds OA corrections under §4.44b; it does not by itself establish that no indirect disclosure correction exists. No external or later-edition source was checked.
