# Rail west-order decision input: source review

**Disposition:** The [decision input](2026-11-18-graziani-rail-west-decision-input.md) correctly treats center, chart-row and partial relations as unselected alternatives. Its same-sheet geometry distinguishes them, but neither the chart nor the map comparison establishes §8.78's universal comparator or a cross-sheet transform. Hidden witness coverage and disclosure remain separate admission gates.

- [Land §8.78](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 16, bars Allied railroad movement on a rail hex west of any rail hex occupied by an Axis combat unit. It does not expressly define individual-hex center ordering, chart-row equality, cross-sheet registration, or which phases of route use count. The existential witness blocks only when the chosen relation and all qualifying predicates are established; no witness cannot be concluded from a partial occupancy, rail or classification survey.
- [Axis chart §32.66](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 34, defines `Cxx01` using map section, any north–south hex, and east–west hexrow suffix. Its qualifying-division condition explicitly includes a division in the same east–west hexrow as another; that equality belongs to the chart's division test, not automatically to §8.78 or the chart's separate “beyond” threshold. Route 2's first four threshold labels are `Dxx01`, `Cxx16`, `Cxx01`, `Bxx16`. They do not independently register individual cells across sheet edges.
- [Map D](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 4, shows the center of D3515 left/west of D3615 while both identifiers end in `15`. On the proposed registered-center reading the former compares west; a strict different-row reading using the chart's suffix grouping does not identify it as west on that witness alone. Neither observation shows those cells are eligible rail or actually occupied. A partial model can instead leave the pair unknown until its own evidence is accepted. The illustrated seam case likewise cannot be adjudicated from sheet labels alone.
- The note appropriately separates selected [RUL-GRA-26](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-26-destroyed-railroad-witness-bounded-planning)'s bounded track-witness planning interpretation from still-open west and use semantics. A proven qualifying witness can suffice even with other unknown candidates; proving no blocking witness requires complete relevant coverage and decided comparisons. Hidden occupancy must be assessed in authoritative state; action offers, rejections and timing may disclose an unseen witness even if its identity is withheld. Paired authorized observations and downstream effects need explicit treatment before exposure. This is a software privacy requirement, not a printed §8.78 rule.
