# Independent review: morale +4 erratum

**Result:** Source supports the note's distinction between Basic Morale and assault-specific Adjusted Morale. Within the checked §17.23/.28 and September clarification, the explicit exception to the +3 adjusted cap is Rommel's +1 for stacked Axis units at the instant of assault. The 6th Australian example and conditional arithmetic are correct; neither proves a reachable +4 or completed training.

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 27, §§17.21–.23, .27–.28: close assault or probe adjusts Basic Morale by Cohesion; §17.23 caps Adjusted Morale at +3/−3 and explicitly cites §17.28 as exception. Section 17.27 selects the applicable Cohesion under the Largest Unit Rule for mixed Cohesion, not a second +4 exception. Section 17.28 further adds 1 for **Axis units stacked with Rommel at the instant of assault**. [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 left column, §17.28 confirms final +4 possible. A capped +3 plus 1 illustrates the exception; the source does not independently establish the prerequisite +3, Rommel's presence, stack, assault, or a Commonwealth beneficiary. This is bounded to the checked rules and erratum, not an exhaustive all-source exclusion of other modifiers.
- Land pp. 27–28 §§17.31–.36 distinguish parenthesized untrained and designated maximum ratings and prescribe one increase for each six Operations Stages of training, up to designated Basic Morale. The same-hex instructor, its fitness/strength, location, CP interruption and exact credited stages remain separate requirements. These are Basic Morale changes, not automatic final assault modifiers. Section 17.1 also assigns a battalion or brigade its parent formation's Basic Morale subject to attachment/independence exceptions; a specific subsidiary's representation requires checking those facts.
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 13 lower Australian panel, visibly states 6th Australian Division `+1 (−3)`. [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §§60.22, 60.41, places the listed division in training at Cairo and/or Helwan with the specified exclusions and runs GT1/1 to GT6/3. Under explicit assumptions of **−3 at start, no prior credit and 18 qualifying stages**, 18 ÷ 6 = three increments, giving 0. Whether all 18 qualify or the final increment is available during versus after its last stage is not proven. Setup “in training” does not itself establish instructor qualification, individual location or prior progress. The +1 designated maximum requires four increments from −3 and is not reached by those three increments.
