# C0716 air-fuel counterexample: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Independently checked the [C0716 witness](2026-11-18-graziani-scenario-witness-next.md) against the same original scenario, Land and map scans. **Disposition:** the six-versus-twelve arithmetic and its conditional scope are supported. One placement phrasing below needs care; it does not affect the calculation.

- Scenario §60.92(B), printed p. 29 / PDF p. 5, lists **one regular Supply Unit at C0716 with 20 Ammo and 6 Fuel**. Land §32.37 requires 12 Fuel before air transport from a Supply Unit in the origin hex. If the listed counter remains there with 6 Fuel and no other eligible origin-hex Fuel exists, the total is 6, so the required 12 cannot be supplied even under an aggregate-payment interpretation. This does not decide self-payment, whether multiple units may combine, or an actual phase snapshot.
- Section 60.92(B) lists Dump 1 and Dump 2 as two three-unit groups, each regular unit with 40 Ammo and 50 Fuel. Their footnote permits each in a qualifying Map C Libya hex outside the specified Commonwealth proximity. The follow-up correctly treats co-location at C0716 as unproved. Its phrase “separately placed” should mean **separately listed/selected groups**, not an asserted rule that the two must occupy different hexes: the inspected footnote does not expressly impose distinct hexes. Country, proximity, stacking, other initial choices and any earlier changes remain independent.
- Map C's blue palms at the C0716 cell match the key's oasis symbol; the numbered locator is partially obscured and relies on neighboring labels and grid. Land §32.37 names major city, village and Tripoli/Tunisia box as origin/destination categories. Oasis alone does not establish a qualifying village, and this local view does not certify the absence of every overlapping settlement mark. Fuel arithmetic does not establish an eligible endpoint or a legal air move.
- Scenario §60.31 places the Saharan Det **within three hexes of C0716**, with five light trucks in that setup entry; it does not require their exact placement in C0716 or combine them with the separate supply entry. A hypothetical second origin Supply Unit with at least 12 Fuel can satisfy the amount from one source, if otherwise eligible, but proves no other transport condition.
