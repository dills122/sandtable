# Independent review: C0716 radius map interiors

Reviewed 17 November 2026 against [Map C](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 3, using an enlarged rendering of the same scan as the [interiors transcription](2026-11-17-graziani-c0716-radius-interiors.md). This is a second visual reading, not an independent map edition or a blind review. Scope: the 37 listed cells and their visible interiors, not rules effects.

**Disposition:** No definite conflict found in the listed colors, marsh marks, oasis symbols, or Giarabub dot. All 37 listed cells are on the printed sheet. The grouped readings are plausible as observations of artwork, subject to the following limits.

- C0613–C0618: yellow and uncolored areas share each cell. The irregular boundary is artwork, not a hex boundary; color presence alone does not establish a whole-hex classification.
- C0814, C0914 and C1014: brown artwork occupies visible parts of the cells near their western edges. C0914's brown area extends noticeably inside the outline, so “at western edge” should not be read as proof that artwork is confined to the edge or as an adjudicated edge type.
- C0816–C0818: yellow marsh pattern is visible, but large blue “El Qaqa” and “Giarabub Oasis” lettering masks portions. C1015's smaller “Giarabub” label also crosses marsh artwork. Presence of pattern is supported; complete absence of other small marks under overprints is not.
- C0716 and C0718 contain blue palm oasis symbols. Dashed track crosses both; the “El Qaqa” name lies across nearby cells. The name's position alone does not prove a separately marked settlement or assign its full text to one hex. C1014 contains the blue Giarabub village/bir dot; its label extends into C1015. The scenario spelling difference noted in the transcription is not resolved by label placement.
- C0713–C0714, C0719, C0813, C0815 and C0915 show no apparent colored interior terrain symbol in this scan. That is a limited visual observation, not proof of blank terrain, no route, legal placement, or movement cost. Dashed tracks and printed hex numbers overlap portions of several cells.

This review did not verify terrain rules, side-by-side edge classifications, setup eligibility, stacking, paths, distance endpoint interpretation, or other editions and errata. In particular, matching visual marks cannot turn the geometric candidates into legal deployment cells. Retain the transcription's caveats and investigate ambiguous features against a clearer source before using this as a complete terrain inventory.
