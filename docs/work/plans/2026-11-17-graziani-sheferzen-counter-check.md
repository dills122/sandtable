# Sheferzen and 2nd Rifle: counter-face check

**Finding:** Scanned counter faces show `Scfzrn` with printed `0` and battalion-size `II`; `2RflBde` shows printed `1` and battalion-size `II`. Under the counter key's stacking-number convention these are zero and one stacking point, respectively. The `G` on Scfzrn denotes garrison status, a separate mark. These readings do not establish current raw defense, initial stacking, projection across the shared hexside or compulsory combat.
