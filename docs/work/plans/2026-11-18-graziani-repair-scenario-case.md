# Alexandria starting tanks: repair table boundary

**Date:** November 18, 2026. A concrete Graziani setup case supplementing the [repair resource matrix](2026-11-13-graziani-repair-resource-matrix.md), [facility review](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md) and [availability follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-repair-availability-followup.md). The printed singleton tank and repair table expose a specific 10% exception question. No repair roll, source precedence, city-hex allocation or reachable later damage is selected.

## Fixed starting facts and separate gates

Scenario §60.41, printed p. 27 / PDF p. 3, lists **two TOE of broken-down A9 Cruiser tanks and one TOE of broken-down A10 Cruiser tanks at Alexandria**. They are broken down, not destroyed; the Broken Down Vehicle Repair Table §22.8, rather than automatically the destroyed-tank table §22.44, is the relevant starting table if a repair attempt is otherwise permitted. The setup entry does not specify the exact Alexandria hex for each point. Land §22.31 lists Alexandria among major repair facility locations; the scope of its “all hexes” parenthetical and §60.44's replacement under §60.92(C) remain separately contested in the [facility review](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md). Co-location with an applicable major facility, unit control, towing, phase, weather, prior events and the unresolved Land-only availability issue must all be established at the actual attempt.

Section 22.34 calls for a die for **each type** of broken-down vehicle undergoing facility repair and directs readers to §22.25 for details. Subject to all other accepted grouping and eligibility rules, the two A9 and the one A10 are separate type lots, not a single three-point lot. Section 22.25 states percentages are rounded up, but a 10% result is ignored when only one TOE point is undergoing repair. Chart §22.8 similarly rounds percentage results up and marks certain `10%*` cells with a singleton-to-zero note. Per-type rolling and rounding depend on the actual eligible lot in the relevant hex and do not follow merely from the scenario's city-wide totals.

## Concrete unresolved 10% distinction

The §22.8 **major facility** column prints an **unstarred 10%** at adjusted die **8**, while its starred singleton exception explicitly refers to `10%*`; §22.25's one-point exception instead says 10% without a star, and §22.34 points to §22.25 for details. If exactly the initial one A10 point forms an eligible major-facility lot and the adjusted result is 8, a reading carrying §22.25's singleton exception into facility repair gives **zero**, whereas a reading applying the chart's star-specific exception only to starred cells would round 10% of one point up to **one**. These are competing interpretations of the listed cases and chart, not claimed outcomes or a selected ruling. Other rules and chart explanations could affect precedence.

The adjusted 8 is not asserted as reachable at initial Alexandria. Raw die 6 plus an independently established +2 facility modifier is a conditional route to that row. September errata §22.8 says the chart explanation's final die-modifier sentences are wrong and refers to §22.34; §22.34 ties modifiers to the amount a major city's fortification level has been reduced. Scenario §60.7 and Land §25.12 leave initial city fortification precedence contested in the [facility review](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md). Neither the scenario starting entry nor this arithmetic proves that a qualifying two-level reduction, raw die 6, particular facility location, or later attempt actually occurs. Do not apply the chart's superseded modifier language to fabricate adjusted 8.

Separately, for a hypothetical eligible **field** A10 singleton, §22.8's starred 10% at adjusted die 2–4 explicitly gives zero; an eligible two-point A9 lot at 10% rounds up to one. These illustrate the importance of separating type and count, not proof that a field companion, resource availability, same hex, phase or roll exists. Field versus facility repair, exception applicability, elapsed phases and private status need independently accepted rules and state.
