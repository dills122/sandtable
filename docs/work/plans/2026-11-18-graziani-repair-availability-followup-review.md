# Land-only repair availability follow-up: source review

**Disposition:** Supports [follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-repair-availability-followup.md)'s distinction between field-repair companion, abstract supply draw and later movement. The checked passages do not decide whether §22.13(b)'s repair-supply availability condition survives §32.11, or supply an abstract repair witness. Same-scan informed review, not an independent edition.

- §22.22 allows field repair when a broken-down vehicle is stacked with a friendly unit of any type, including its explicit Squadron Ground Support Unit example; the latter does not create an Air Game unit in Land-only play. §22.13 separately supplies other prohibitions. §21.44 says a broken-down vehicle cannot participate in combat. §3.33 describes an HQ with no combat units attached as noncombat; §3.36 adds a separate conditional capture rule for an HQ with no combat values. Neither case establishes that any particular HQ survives or is available in a real repair hex.
- §32.16 grants Supply Unit draw to a friendly combat unit, with its stated half-CPA, traversal and blocking conditions. A qualifying noncombat companion can meet §22.22 without itself being that draw subject; other combat units may still exist within range. This is not proof that §32.16 governs §22.13(b), that the companion must draw, or that repair otherwise succeeds. Displacement, a surviving separately defined availability condition, and an expressly chosen draw-based interpretation remain distinct.
- §22.5 treats repaired vehicles as Replacement Points of their type, excludes training and says they must have fuel to move again. It does not say that this later movement requirement itself is a debit or availability test at the repair attempt. §§32.23–.24 set abstract fuel expenditure for the specified moving subjects; applying those cases to a particular repaired point still requires the replacement and movement rules. The planning proposal concerning repair-supply displacement is not a printed ruling.
