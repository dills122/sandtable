# Review: dummy-tank victory scope

**Disposition:** [Source note](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-b-source-5.md) is supported within its stated limits. Section 16.43's Italian-formation restriction is not a demonstrated universal Axis ban or an automatic victory-classification rule.

Independently visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF pp. 5–6 and 26–27 §§3.1, 3.23 and 16.0, 16.41–.46, and [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF pp. 3–4 / printed pp. 27–28 §§60.31 and 60.81. Local SHA-256 values match: Land `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`; scenario `fa12488e59552d7b0ff0d5abefa891d4b81af0492256bec2d25f8d61ef040a66`. This informed check uses the same scan edition.

- §16.41 makes dummy tank formations tank-battalion-equivalent in size, zero CPA and worthless in combat; §16.45 describes their intelligence-deception function and worthlessness alone. §16.43 says Italian formations may not use dummies, even mixed with German formations. It does not expressly prohibit all German formation use or resolve every mixed/control identity question.
- §16.46 eliminates a dummy tank battalion when its formation undergoes any Close Assault, and separately specifies an anti-armor condition when no armor remains. This does not establish an actual assault or determine a terminal snapshot before one. §16.42's ordinary Stores cost and Land-only abstraction require their own scope decision.
- §3.1 explicitly lists dummy tank formations among things considered combat units in certain situations. This does not say which §60.81 predicate they satisfy. The Italian strategic and decisive rows name combat units; the other rows use their own wording. The note properly leaves specific subject, formation, location and supply conditions unresolved.
- Scenario §60.31's Italian initial force list and an ordinary track beginning after GT6 do not establish absence of all German or dummy introduction paths. The source note preserves this limit and distinguishes supply-unit dummies from dummy tanks.

No complete later-errata, alternate introduction, hidden-state or runtime audit. No data changes or gameplay tests.
