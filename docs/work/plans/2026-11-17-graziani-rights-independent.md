# Graziani source rights: independent evidence and next steps

Research snapshot: November 17, 2026. This is an evidence and release-planning note, not a legal opinion or a grant of rights. No permission request has been sent. The [RC inventory](2026-11-12-graziani-playable-rc-inventory.md) G10 and MVP-GATE-02/07 remain open.

## Provenance and ownership leads

- The [1979 Land Game rules scan](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) carries a 1979 copyright notice naming Simulations Publications, Inc. (SPI). It identifies the original claimant for that booklet, not the current owner or scope of later transfers. Check notices and credits on each chart, map, scenario, counter sheet and errata separately.
- Decision Games advertises [North African Campaign](https://shop.decisiongames.com/ProductDetails.php?ProductCode=P3034), an update of the SPI classic, including updated map, charts and rules. [Grognard's listing](https://grognard.com/m-article-page-list.aspx?GameID=c045) credits Decision Games for courtesy access to the September 1979 addenda. These make Decision Games a strong first licensing contact, not proof that it can grant every original, digital, adaptation, trademark, or third-party right. A [secondary account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Campaign_for_North_Africa) says Decision Games acquired CNA rights, but does not establish the chain or precise scope.
- [Decision Games contact details](https://shop.decisiongames.com/AboutUs.php) list PO Box 21598, Bakersfield, CA 93390 and 661-587-9633; its [store](https://shop.decisiongames.com/) lists Sales@DecisionGames.com for order help. Ask to be directed to rights/licensing; do not assume sales staff can grant rights.
- Publicly reachable [SPI scan](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) and [addenda transcription](https://grognard.com/errata1/cna.html) establish access, not a general redistribution or adaptation license. Matching file hashes establish byte identity, not ownership, authorization, or independent provenance.

## Third-party free-license lead

[CNA Living Rules](https://basmith.net/cna/) describes itself as an unaffiliated, provisional restatement with redrawn maps and retyped tables. Its [site footer](https://basmith.net/cna/rulings/register) states rules CC-BY-SA-4.0, data CC0 and code MIT; its [data conventions](https://basmith.net/cna/data/) say `data/` includes tables, errata overlays, map-sheet metadata and organisation records. These are the site's representations and license offers for its contributors' material, not evidence of authority to waive any SPI/successor rights. Verify repository identity, exact file, version, license, contributor chain, source and applicable license obligations before any import. Preserve attribution, license notices and changes where required; review CC-BY-SA share-alike scope and compatibility with Sandtable's current [all-rights-reserved status](../../../README.md#license). CC0 does not itself settle underlying rights. Do not assume every map or file is covered by `data/` or that its completeness, accuracy or rulings match Graziani.

## Use-specific review

The [U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html) distinguishes game methods from sufficiently original rules text and board artwork; [17 U.S.C. § 102(b)](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html) excludes ideas, processes and systems from copyright protection. This general U.S. distinction does not decide protection for a specific chart's selection or arrangement, a coordinate-by-coordinate map reconstruction, scenario data, rewritten prose or use in other jurisdictions. Titles may raise separate trademark questions. Obtain qualified counsel's review of actual materials and intended distribution; do not treat independent wording or a redraw as automatic clearance.

| Proposed surface | Evidence and disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Original scans, prose, graphics, map or counter art | No project redistribution license identified. Obtain written authorization from a verified rightsholder covering actual reproduction, modification, display and distribution, or counsel-approved alternative basis, before release. |
| Transcribed tables, scenario setups, orders of battle, map cells and derived configurations | Classify exact fields, source and degree of selection/arrangement or expression; send sample and planned use to counsel/rightsholder. No blanket permission requirement or exemption established for every value. Preserve provenance and keep G10 open pending a documented decision. |
| Independently implemented mechanics and historical facts | Document sources and authorship; review any copied expressive detail or distinctive selection. Mechanics/facts distinction alone does not clear a full source-derived package. |
| Living Rules files | Check exact upstream license and downstream obligations; independently assess underlying rights and resulting content. A public license covers only rights its grantor can license. |
| Names and marketing | Ask counsel to assess descriptive reference, logos, endorsement and trademarks in intended jurisdictions. No permission or trademark clearance established here. |

## Concrete next steps

1. Build a release manifest of actual source-linked assets and derived artifacts: source URL/edition/page, original notice, local path and hash, transformation, author, intended package/display, scope and jurisdiction. Include already tracked map, setup, supply, truck and errata work; hashes do not replace provenance. Separate raw scans from derived data, prose and original code.
2. Research registrations and recorded transfers with the [U.S. Copyright Office public records](https://www.copyright.gov/public-records/), using title, SPI and possible successors; retain search dates, records and gaps. Ask Decision Games to identify current owners and confirm its authority over original rules, charts, maps, errata, scenario and digital adaptations, including any excluded rights. Public records or a reply alone may not prove a complete chain.
3. Prepare a reviewable permission request identifying six-turn Graziani, representative assets and exact uses: transcription, adaptation, machine-readable data and map redraw, source and binary distribution, hosting/display, commercial/noncommercial use, attribution, territories, duration, sublicensing and any intended open-source license. Ask for written terms, fees, restrictions and evidence of authority. Obtain separate grants for third-party rights if identified; silence or access to scans is not permission. No outreach authorized by this note.
4. Have counsel compare desired packaging and any available license against actual artifacts and intended jurisdictions, decide each surface's basis, and record approved scope, attribution, restrictions and outstanding questions. If clearance is unavailable, remove/replace affected material or defer distribution, then review replacement independently.
5. Keep scans outside Git and preserve existing release gates while this decision is unresolved. Record approved evidence and perform a fresh-package/notice check before closing G10 and MVP-GATE-02/07; do not infer release approval from this research.
