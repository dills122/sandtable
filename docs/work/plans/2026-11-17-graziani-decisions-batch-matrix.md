## Stages and evidence gates

1. **Select policy.** Record decision owner, selected alternative, rejected alternatives, source locators, consequences and new immutable version. Convoy non-monotonicity and rail origin/contact tension require affirmative disposition. For victory and facilities, retain existing bounded rulings without extending their scope. Independent reviews use the same editions; they are not independent-edition corroboration.
2. **Specify contracts before implementation.** Define authoritative inputs, coverage certificates, three-valued predicates and short circuits, action/transaction identity, ordering, deadlines where applicable, durable state, correction and replay. Rail's local ZOC gate passes on certified no ZOC or a known qualifying occupant even if ZOC is unknown; known ZOC fails only with certified absence of qualifying occupants; otherwise unknown. A passing gate does not prove full legality. No facility outcome follows from a control event alone.
3. **Authorize observations.** For each player, observer and intelligence-plane audience, define permitted facts and timing. Compare histories identical under each audience's authorized observations but differing in hidden state: option lists, response categories, timing, events and counts, identifiers, terminalization, state freeze, retries, later actions, evidence repair and visible downstream state. Generic pending text or a fixed deadline alone does not prevent conditional-publication leakage. If no safe playable protocol exists, keep the relevant public boundary and subsequent observable behavior independent of hidden inputs; internal unknown/fail distinctions do not authorize disclosure.
4. **Admit only with evidence.** Source and contract review, complete map/occupancy/history coverage, deterministic synthetic tests, privacy paired-history tests and recovery/replay checks must pass under the selected version. Tests below discriminate choices; they do not prove historical reachability. Missing evidence keeps the affected capability unavailable, with no invented outcome.

## Minimum distinguishing checks after policy selection

- **Convoy:** empty versus unknown; ceil versus floor and pooled versus separate groups; 5%, 10% and 25%; singleton and ambiguous model allocations; interrupted post-draw recovery, duplicate command and no partial delivery.
- **Rail:** origin, intermediate and destination, with both traveler classes, stationary combat and non-combat occupants, certified absence and incomplete coverage; compare lone combat origin against ordinary contact/break-off. Exercise destroyed/unbuilt track, enemy occupation, construction/control, direction and pickup/dropoff without presuming multiple actions legal; replay and hidden-state pairs.
- **Victory:** same versus different holder/hex, evidence of history rather than terminal presence alone, witness plus unrelated unknown, blocked path plus unknown alternative, false conjunct plus unknown conjunct, route boundary completeness and replay. Paired histories include whether play stops or later actions are accepted.
- **Facilities:** initial profile; control change without enemy presence, presence without exclusive control, coincident events and departure/re-entry; preserve identity/damage under each selected lifecycle. Separate construction, rebuild and dismantle from vehicle/tank repair; test missing presence and paired hidden-state observations, interruption and replay.
