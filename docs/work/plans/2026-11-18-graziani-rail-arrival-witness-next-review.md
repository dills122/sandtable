# Rail arrival witness: source review

**Disposition:** The note's distinction between a conditional Phase-D fixture and a demonstrated Commonwealth Supply Unit arrival is supported. No actual qualifying rail journey or rule precedence follows.

Visually checked [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed pp. 12, 15 and 43–44. Section 5.2 places Naval Convoy Arrival Phase D after the start of each Operations Stage and Commonwealth Rail Movement Phase J later in the sequence. Phase D lists arriving reinforcements, replacement points and Ammo Points; it does not expressly list the abstract Supply Units of §32.42. Section 32.31 allows Supply Units to move by land or rail in the OpStage they arrive, while §8.73 requires a rail-using unit to start the OpStage on a rail hex; §32.34 retains normal rail rules. The possible tension depends on an arrival after Stage start and does not by itself establish an exception to §8.73.

Section 32.42 places Commonwealth Supply Units in Cairo, with a conditional Alexandria placement option if more than three are received in one Game-Turn; §32.43 excludes the scenario starting Game-Turn from its ordinary arrival statement, and §32.45 plans real unit receipts four Game-Turns ahead using arrival-period column. Visually checked [Commonwealth chart §32.47](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 31: period I runs through April 1941 and die results 1–6 yield `1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3`. One such result alone cannot cross the stated more-than-three threshold. This does not exclude other categories or accumulated receipts without further source proof and does not specify the exact placement phase or rail hex.

A new unit placed after Stage start on an otherwise eligible rail hex makes a useful conditional comparison: the literal start-location test and a separately adopted arrival exception can differ. Both require independent evidence for the placement timing, location, route and remaining eligibility; no later receipt or transfer automatically proves them. The initial-stock distinction is appropriate, but scenario setup, §20.12 and errata were not independently rechecked in this review.
