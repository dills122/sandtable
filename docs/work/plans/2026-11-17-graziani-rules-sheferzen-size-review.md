# Sheferzen size hierarchy: independent source review

**Disposition:** Confirm the [size note](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sheferzen-size.md)'s source readings. The `0` face, full-strength baseline and `dd` company-equivalent chart row together support a **provisional** company-equivalent interpretation. Section 9.22 explicitly prefers printed stacking value over designation or name for organizational level, but does not expressly resolve this particular counter's `II` size symbol, garrison exception, or every size-sensitive rule. Preserve all source fields and alternatives.

The Scfzrn counter shows `G`, `II`, and unparenthesized `0`; Land §4.21 identifies the corresponding marks as garrison indicator, organization size and stacking value, while p. 8's key assigns `II` to battalion. Axis OA p. 14 calls Sceferzen Oasis Garrison a battalion and gives `dd`, `N`, `D`; its chart §4.46b p. 26 places `dd` among infantry company-equivalents, with maximum TOE 1 and defense 2. Land §4.45 p. 10 and Axis OA key p. 4 describe `N` as maximum starting TOE. These are concurrent printed statements, not an official correction of the face or chart.

Land §9.22 p. 17 directs use of the counter's printed stacking value, cross-referenced with §9.4, rather than the unit's designation or name when determining organizational level. Common chart §9.4 places full company at 0, full battalion at 1, and shell battalion at 0. Section 9.26 defines a battalion shell as below half its maximum TOE, and §9.28 reduces a shell one level for unit differentiation and other rules where size matters. At the conditional `N`/one-TOE starting baseline a below-half shell is not established; consequently full-company row and `dd` heading lend weight to that candidate. Zero by itself does not prove company size, because the table also contains shell and HQ cases.

Land §9.16(a) gives `G` units free stacking in their assigned city/village, with an example where a garrison battalion is zero there and one elsewhere. It is a separate location-specific rule from the counter's printed value. Neither it nor §9.22 says in terms how to resolve this particular `II`/`0`/`dd` conflict, the effective value away from home, or whether all size-dependent uses share one answer. Retain those questions and actual movement eligibility. The errata scan contains no explicit Sceferzen size correction found in this review; other sources or later corrections may exist.

Under the isolated one-TOE baseline, raw defense is 2; §10.15 excludes units in a hex below 10 raw defense from projecting ZOC. The printed singleton `0` also does not meet §10.11's more-than-one stacking condition. These are conditional single-unit observations, not an adjudication of a complete stack or of §9.16's interaction with §10.11. Legal co-location, unit state, entry/edge and phase remain separate.
