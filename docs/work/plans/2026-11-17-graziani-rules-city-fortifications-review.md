# City fortifications: independent source review

**Disposition:** corroborate [city-fortification analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-city-fortifications.md)'s principal source conflict and lifecycle distinction. Retaining built-in city levels is a reasoned planning interpretation, not an explicit exception in §60.7. Suppressed-at-setup versus destroyed-and-rebuildable requires a separate decision; no automatic restoration is stated. Add the §24.47 removal rule and preserve uncertainty about the reach of §60.7's railroad parenthetical.

## Source check

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, §60.7, under **Construction on the Map at the Start of the Game**, states there are no minefields, fortifications, or pipeline, followed by a parenthetical exception for those provided by the railroad. Its wording does not explicitly distinguish constructed counters from inherent city fortifications. The parenthetical's grammatical scope and what the railroad actually provides are not resolved merely by the heading; do not silently apply the exception to all three categories or assert its exclusive scope without further evidence.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 36, §24.41 distinguishes counter-represented fortifications and built-in major-city levels which may be rebuilt when destroyed. Section 24.44 bars new fortification construction in major cities and certain other terrains, and from scratch in enemy ZOC. Section 24.45 permits rebuilding fortification and major-city levels destroyed by air bombardment or artillery barrage using §§24.42–.43; rebuilding may occur in enemy ZOC. Neither explicitly identifies a scenario-suppressed level as destroyed, nor states that one reappears automatically.
- Section 24.42 requires an engineering-capable unit and an infantry battalion of at least three TOE to spend three complete Construction Segments without CP expenditure; completion occurs at the beginning of the fourth. Section 24.43 prints 30 Stores Points, while Land-only treatment remains separate. Section 24.48 limits building or rebuilding to one level at a time and caps major cities at original level two, with Alexandria/Cairo at three. Additional interruption, weather, and construction eligibility rules are not displaced by this summary.
- Section 24.47 says emplaced fortifications may not be voluntarily removed and may be reduced only by air bombardment or artillery barrage; no other combat type affects them. Its application to an initially scenario-suppressed *built-in* city level is part of the source-precedence decision, not proof that §60.7 could not define a different start. Include the no-voluntary-removal boundary when testing an admitted lifecycle.
- Land p. 38 §§25.0 and 25.12 treat major cities as fortifications, with level two except Cairo/Alexandria at three; villages are not inherent fortifications. Sections 25.14–.16 discuss reduction, rebuilding and level zero. Section 25.21 says fortifications do not affect movement or breakdown. Sections 25.23–.24 add specified level-three air and pinned effects; §23.24 p. 36 explicitly excludes major cities from its engineer close-assault adjustment. These do not establish a specific city is reachable or attacked.

The construction heading and Land's built-in-versus-additional wording support the existing choice to retain city levels at setup. The unqualified scenario sentence also supports a contrary setup reading. If selecting absence, distinguish it from destruction and define whether or how rebuilding is possible; if retaining levels, preserve valid later reductions and rebuilding rather than making level permanent. Certified city identity, source hierarchy, separate construction and combat rules, abstraction, replay and ruleset identity remain gates. Test a level-zero city after actual damage separately from a scenario-suppressed one and include the voluntary-removal constraint.
