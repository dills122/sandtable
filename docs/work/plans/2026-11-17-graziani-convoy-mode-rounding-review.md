# Independent review: convoy thresholds by game mode

**Disposition:** Core comparison supported. Land §32.65B covers 25% as “20% or greater”; Air abstract-logistics §47.65 names 20% and then 30% or greater, leaving 25% outside its stated branches. Both have no explicit 5% branch. This is a textual gap, not proof of a particular loss outcome or of missing rules elsewhere. No policy admitted.
