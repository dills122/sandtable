## Monthly motorization loss

**Printed constraints.** Land §32.57 determines the previous month's loss at the first Naval Convoy Stage of each month. Its denominator is that player's total on-map motorization at that time, explicitly including Tripolitania–Tunis boxes; removal is from map E for Commonwealth and map A including its off-map region for Axis. The chart's 1940 row has a dash for January through September, then October 6/2, November 5/3 and December 4/4; its legend identifies Commonwealth/Axis percentages. The matched §58.5 chart title differs from §32.59's title, but the side ordering, percentage and related §58 references support this identification. The dash has no displayed definition. Neither §32.57 nor the chart states a rounding rule or what happens if the allowed removal region has fewer points than the calculated loss. Do not treat the off-map inclusion in the Axis removal region as proof that other off-map points are included in the denominator.

**Proposed bounded policy for review.** Identify §58.5 as the source chart; use the *previous* calendar month as its lookup key, because §32.57 expressly describes loss for that month. Represent a dash as `unspecified`, distinct from numeric zero; block adjudication when encountered until a separately versioned ruling or authoritative clarification supplies its meaning. For a numeric rate, calculate against a single stage snapshot, preserve the exact numerator and denominator until rounding, and retain the side and source month in the event. This proposal does not settle whether the first October stage reads as an initial-month exception, nor ordering of arrivals against the snapshot; those must be separately decided from the sequence rules.

**Alternatives and consequences.** A current-month lookup uses October's 6/2 at the first October stage, but needs an explanation for the text's previous-month clause; choosing it must not rewrite the printed source. Treating a dash as zero or as “no event” produces the same immediate loss in a simple case but differs in event history and may imply different first-month behavior. Neither is established by the chart. For numeric fractions, floor, ceiling, nearest and cumulative fractions are distinct choices. Land §32.65 explicitly rounds motorization losses down for 10% convoy results and rounds all conveyed items down for results of 20% or more, subject to its own minimum; that is useful analogy, **not** a rule for §32.57. Floor is a defensible candidate if consistency is desired, but must be labeled a house interpretation. For example, 6% of 25 is 1.5: floor gives 1, ceiling gives 2; no silent floating-point rounding.

If an exact computed obligation exceeds eligible points, alternatives include stop as unresolved, cap at eligible points, or change the removal region. Prefer stop until an admitted policy specifies a result: capping reduces losses, and expanding the region contradicts the stated restriction. Preserve owner choice among eligible points if applicable; don't invent a deterministic location order as a source fact. Also define whether points attached to units or supply can be removed and how any resulting transport illegality is handled before executing removal. §32.56's assigned-unit loss/capture and §32.65's convoy loss are separate events, not additional calculations of the monthly percentage.

## Dummy supply and initial placement

**Printed constraints.** §32.18 calls these dummy Supply Units, permits one for every three received and/or present at scenario start, says they move like real supply units with less motorization, and removes them on discovery. §32.32 specifies six motorization points for a dummy versus 30 for a real unit; §32.34 explicitly counts real and/or dummy supply units toward the two-unit rail limit. §32.14 gives supply units zero stacking value but caps their number at five in a major city and three in any other type of hex “off-map,” and exempts Tripoli/Tunisia boxes. Its grammar does not clearly establish a cap for ordinary on-map non-major-city hexes. Scenario §60.92(B) separately supplies two groups of three Axis dummies, each placeable according to Dump 1 and 2 rules; §60.92(C) supplies one group of three Commonwealth dummies under Dump 1's rule. These are explicit quantities, not proof that those quantities are added again to a general-ratio allocation.

**Proposed bounded policy for review.** Retain the existing [RUL-GRA-08](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-08-abstract-supply-stacking-bounded-planning-interpretation) choice of three in an ordinary on-map non-major-city hex, explicitly as interpretation. Count real and dummy units alike for this cap, supported by the general name and the explicit rail example; do not disclose dummy identity to an opponent through an error, legal-action list or event. Treat the scenario's specific initial groups as the available starting dummy allotment, rather than automatically adding a second ratio-derived starting allotment. Calculate future §32.18 eligibility separately once receipt, carryover and replacement semantics are chosen. This favors specific setup text and avoids duplicating grants, but neither the scenario nor §32.18 expressly resolves whether general initial eligibility adds more; retain additive and ratio-only alternatives for review.

Under this proposal, three real and three dummy units cannot share an ordinary capped hex; each group must independently satisfy its stated placement rule. The printed text says the dummy group may be placed according to the dump rules; it does not explicitly require sharing the real dump's hex. Test whether any legal distinct hexes exist from certified map and opposing setup before concluding initial excess is unavoidable. Scenario §59.65 allows initial excess only when unavoidable under distribution instructions, forbids adding supply while excess persists and requires normal restrictions once the distribution conforms. It excludes transporting motorization points from **hex stacking limits**, not the supply units carried. The five real units at Matruh remain a separate required §59.65 case; its source-listed placement does not itself settle the general on-map cap or dummy rule.

Scenario §59.53 prohibits non-active *dummy dumps* in air-facility hexes. Its applicability to §32.18 abstract dummy Supply Units is not established merely by their similar names; decide it separately before asserting such a restriction. Likewise, §59.66(C) permits additional starting real supply units transported by qualifying unattached motorization points. Decide whether those real units enter the ratio calculation and whether the scenario's fixed groups exhaust all initial dummy entitlement; do not silently infer either result. Placement, loading, ratio and cap choices interact and must be evaluated together.
