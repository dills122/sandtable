# Graziani facility geography: independent rule and crosswalk review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Disposition:** corroborate the bounded [mode-scope note](2026-11-18-graziani-facility-geography-mode-scope.md). Its 30 cells are leads, not a complete endpoint universe or proven shipments. No cell is finally excluded merely by its pin or facility status.

## Primary rule

Independently rendered and visually read [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 43, §§32.0 and 32.37. Section 32.0 applies to Land without Air and Logistics. Section 32.37 requires **both** origin and destination to be a major city, village or Tripoli/Tunisia box; the destination must contain a friendly division or brigade headquarters counter, expressly excluding battle-group headquarters. The division governing the directional limit may be shell or full; that is a separate condition from the destination HQ. The rule permits one supply unit per player per Game-Turn in an OpStage Organization Phase, requires 12 Fuel from any supply unit in the origin hex before transport, bars origin when an enemy combat unit is within five hexes, and limits eastward Axis or westward Commonwealth transport to the corresponding extreme friendly division hexrow. It prohibits movement by the owner of the transported supply unit and its supplies during that OpStage but expressly permits draw under §32.16. These readings support the note; §32.37 itself specifies no airfield, aircraft, or detailed-Air capacity requirement. Other applicable constraints remain separate questions.

## Target and pin check

Their pins match the stated groups: **21 village, 2 bir, 7 null**. No extra or missing target found. This checks transcription, not the original map or correctness of its settlement classification. In particular, a `bir` pin does not establish village equivalence, a null pin does not disprove a qualifying city or village, and a village pin does not itself certify the rule's endpoint. Other cities, villages, and Tripoli/Tunisia boxes remain candidates; initial occupancy is not a permanent exclusion.
