# Matruh initial organization: source follow-up

**Disposition:** Original scans confirm the separate printed attachments and the
errata assignment, but do not identify Essex's or Durham's starting assigned
parent or settle whether Matruh's six-unit ceiling includes its exceptional
assignees. No new official ruling or runtime change. RUL-GRA-03 remains a
working interpretation, not a source-proved answer.

## Findings

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf)
  §60.41, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, puts Matruh at D3714 and lists 1st Essex,
  1st Durham Light Infantry, and 1st South Staffordshires after `Att`. Section
  59.2, PDF pp. 1–2, defines this as attachment without assignment to that
  listed parent; the parent identifier can remain on a counter after assignment
  changes. Thus `23/70` does not establish initial assignment to 23rd Brigade,
  nor does `Att` establish any different assigned parent or independence.
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf)
  PDF p. 9, 70th Division note f, dates Essex and Durham's historical
  assignment to 23rd to June 1941 (3/38). Note e says the 23rd had been
  dissolved in August 1940 and reformed in May 1941; neither note specifies
  their Graziani assigned parents. Land §19.24 makes OA historical assignment
  shifts optional, subject to the cited restrictions and timing: note f is not
  itself an unconditional future automatic reassignment rule. The 22nd Guards
  note on OA p. 10 names Durham and Buffs among units assigned at various
  times, without identifying their Graziani starting assignment.
- OA p. 10 explicitly says Matruh is a brigade-level HQ, no units may be
  assigned, and up to six units may be attached. [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf)
  §4.44b item 2 expressly says 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshires start the campaign
  and Italian scenarios assigned to Matruh; scenario §§60.0–60.22 include
  Graziani in that group. This is a direct exception conflict with the OA.
  Assignment does not alone establish their hex or attachment.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf)
  §§19.11–.14 distinguish assigned and attached parents. Section 19.4 and
  errata §19.5 describe chart attachments as additional to normal assigned
  units; the latter explicitly allows assigned units to be elsewhere. This
  supports applying six additional slots to Matruh, but its OA wording also
  supports six total attached units. Neither source expressly reconciles the
  exceptional assignees with that specific maximum. Detached assigned units
  still occupy assignment space; attachment count and assignment space remain
  separate questions.
- Scenario §59.2 allows free initial attachment and detachment among units
  listed in the same hex within formation and stacking restrictions, and
  separately protects listed formations already exceeding limits under stated
  restrictions. It neither proves Buffs/Hampshire are listed at Matruh nor
  makes an owner-created excess legal. Land §4.25's co-location default needs
  actual location and applicable exceptions before application.

## Remaining decisions

Preserve five identities, source-listed attachments, errata assignment, unknown
initial parents and owner-selected setup separately. Resolve source location
and default attachment of Buffs/Hampshire; six total versus six additional
capacity; chart type, company and nested attachment counting; and the
source-listed excess interaction before admitting dependent legal actions.
See [capacity follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-followup.md) and
[company review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-company-review.md) for alternatives
and distinguishing cases. Do not infer an initial parent from a later historical
shift or infer placement from assignment.
