# Patrol history policy: independent review

**Disposition:** retain [proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-patrol-policy-proposal.md) for revision and admission review. Its past-boundary, stage-wide and target-window selections are coherent **as project interpretations**, with source alternatives and privacy gates correctly retained. No official source settles those choices. Before implementation, tighten the history completeness boundary and distinguish the Land-only supply exception.

## Findings

1. **Completed-event filtering needs a closure proof.** The proposal excludes unresolved and declared-but-not-executed events, and separately requires complete event coverage. This is safe only when the phase boundary establishes that every earlier potentially qualifying action has reached a final state, and its event stream is complete through the chosen position. An earlier action that has begun execution but has an unknown or pending outcome cannot simply disappear from the slice and produce a known absence. Define a stage/phase watermark or equivalent causal proof, including asynchronous air or combat events, corrections and replay. A deliberately cancelled action with a proven no-occurrence result may be excluded; a missing completion or ambiguous partial execution is unknown until separately classified. Add vectors for unresolved earlier action, late completion or correction, and duplicate events. Do not silently apply later evidence to a committed patrol; specify correction and replay.
2. **Keep the Land-only supply exception explicit.** Land §16.16 p. 26 charges ammo and fuel for patrol in the detailed rules, but §32.25 p. 43 says patrolling requires no supply expenditure in the Land-only abstraction. The proposal lists “supplies” among other prerequisites and tests; this is acceptable as a profile-dependent gate, but should not become a generic supply availability requirement for Graziani Land-only. This does not decide other CP or point-eligibility rules.
3. **Privacy must cover whether a patrol takes effect.** The proposal correctly identifies action enumeration, errors, timing and pending versus rejected responses as potential leaks. Include resulting observations, RNG draws, casualties, event count, subsequent state, and later history repair as observable traces. Successful patrol necessarily produces authorized consequences; an equivalence claim must say which effects and disclosures the audience is entitled to observe. If one hidden history allows execution and another blocks it, an identical immediate message alone cannot guarantee equivalent subsequent play. Choose an authorized disclosure or a public capability gate/other complete protocol before admitting the action.
4. **Preserve occurrence and state separately.** A completed barrage with no effect or a defensive anti-armor event may have source significance despite zero losses; proposal rightly leaves event classification open. Keep initiator, participants, combat type, target, phase and execution status, and test a target that changes occupants. A patrol's own losses should not be silently classified as §16.0 anti-armor or close assault solely because they are called combat losses; determine types from actual underlying events. Unknown classification cannot count as known absence.

## Source and scope

Visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed pp. 12, 26–27 and 43: §5.2 places A's patrol before B's own F–L; B may react and both players can participate during A's combat. Section 16.0 uses stage-wide past occurrence, while §16.21 refers to the phasing player's engagement and does not repeat a time window in its target-bombardment clause. Section 16.17 separately bars storm-affected origin or target; §16.22 supplies target and path constraints. Section 32.25 supplies the Land-only patrol-expenditure exception. Inspected [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) pp. 1–4; §16.11 expands eligible types, with no direct §16.0/.21 correction found in this package. Later or indirect authority may exist.
