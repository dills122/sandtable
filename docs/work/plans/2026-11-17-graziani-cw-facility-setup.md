# Graziani Commonwealth repair facilities: placement and capacity source check

**Finding:** Land-only §60.92(C) literally replaces all of §60.44, whose last paragraph places a Commonwealth major facility at Alexandria and one temporary facility at Mersa Matruh. The selected [RUL-GRA-07](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-07-bounded-facility-and-victory-planning-policies) instead retains Matruh under a narrower supply-only interpretation. The independent general major-facility list still names Alexandria and Cairo, but does not unambiguously establish how many Alexandria hexes contain facilities, or the full scope of per-hex roll grouping. Neither a facility counter count nor a numerical repair throughput follows from the city list alone.

## Source facts

- [Scenario §60.44](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed p. 28 / PDF p. 4, ends with a major repair facility in Alexandria and one temporary facility in Mersa Matruh. It does not specify the Alexandria hex. The same page gives Matruh as `D3714` and identifies Alexandria airfields in both `E3613` and `E3714`; the latter is an **air** facility list, not a repair-facility placement rule.
- §60.92(C), printed p. 29 / PDF p. 5, under Commonwealth Initial Supply Status, says Case 60.44 is replaced by the following, then lists abstract supply units and no repair facilities. Literal whole-case replacement removes its Matruh sentence; the heading supports, but does not expressly establish, replacement limited to supply paragraphs. The selected planning interpretation is not an official correction. Land §22.31 independently names major facilities regardless of whether §60.44 survives.
- [Land §22.31](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 35, lists Tripoli, Tobruk (for whoever controls it), **“Alexandria and Cairo (all hexes)”**. The parenthetical directly follows Cairo; applying it to Alexandria too is plausible, but its scope is not explicit. The existing [facility proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md#commonwealth-initial-facilities) states all hexes for both cities; that is an interpretation requiring explicit selection, not a settled source fact. The [city location-summary review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-city-hex-scope-review.md) lists Alexandria `E3613/E3714` and Cairo `E1730/E1829/E1830/E1930/E1931`; those entries do not independently decide repair placement, occupancy or control.
- Scenario §61.36, same p. 29, describes the **different Desert Fox scenario** and says Alexandria “either hex” and Cairo have major facilities. This corroborates that a printed scenario can use an Alexandria hex choice, but does not amend §60.44, specify whether that choice applies to Graziani, or resolve the general rule's parenthetical.

## Repair scope and open decisions

Land §§22.32–.34 require the broken-down vehicle or destroyed tank to begin the Maintenance Segment in a facility hex, without towing during that Operations Stage. Section 22.34 calls for one roll per broken-down vehicle type and refers to §22.25 for details. Section 22.25's field-repair example rolls by type and determines the percentage of that type **in that hex**. This cross-reference supports per-hex grouping for facility repairs; city-wide pooling has weaker textual support. Whether every detail of the field example transfers to every facility vehicle type, and how to group multiple facilities in the same hex, still need a contract decision.

[Common charts §22.8](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), printed/PDF p. 8, gives percentages rounded up; the major column gives 75% at modified 0–1 and 50% at 2–4, for example. These are outcome percentages, **not a fixed vehicle capacity**. Its temporary-facility 10% entries carry an asterisk whose note treats an attempted single TOE point as zero; its major-facility 10% entry has no asterisk. Yet §22.34 refers to §22.25 for details, and §22.25 states a general single-TOE 10% exception. Whether that rule applies to the unasterisked major result remains a source conflict to decide, not a uniform chart fact. September errata §22.34 removes its reference to §22.35; §22.8 corrects its last two die-modifier sentences by directing readers to §22.34. Neither change expressly resolves this asterisk issue. Section 22.43 instead requires a separate die per destroyed tank point, using chart §22.44. Inspected clauses do not establish a fixed maximum number of vehicles per facility. Other rules, supply, control and stacking may constrain particular attempts; their absence here is not proof of unlimited capacity.

Before setup admission, explicitly choose parenthetical scope, facility identity and whether Alexandria's major designation occupies one chosen hex or both, and confirm control and mapped location. Specify Cairo's facility representation and per-hex grouping consistent with §22.34’s §22.25 cross-reference, plus same-hex facility grouping and the unasterisked major 10% result, without multiplying opportunities or inventing throughput. Keep Matruh presence tied to the selected §60.92(C) interpretation; compare literal replacement. Preserve type, location, scenario and ruleset provenance. Test vehicles of the same type split across city hexes, one versus multiple facilities, single-point rounding, distinct destroyed-tank rolls, towing and maintenance timing, control, and replay. Repair supply abstraction, weather, ZOC, fortification and later control-change rules remain separate; no initial eligible attempt or actual six-turn reachability is established here.
