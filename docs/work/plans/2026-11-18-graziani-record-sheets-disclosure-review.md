# Record sheets and disclosure: independent source review

**Disposition:** The [record-sheet comparison](2026-11-18-graziani-record-sheets-disclosure.md) accurately distinguishes form descriptions, usage and the specific §3.6 disclosure limits. No blanket opposing access or blanket secrecy follows from the cited passages; no material transcription error found.

- Land §4.5, printed/PDF p. 10, gives keeping vital information secret from other players as one reason for the sheets. Section 4.51 calls the four groups an “owning” player's usage grouping but expressly says this is a presentation decision with **no effect on who actually uses what**. Neither statement classifies every field or settles all teammate access.
- Section 4.52, pp. 10–11, describes the Field Commander control sheet and TOE sheets, including CP, cohesion, strengths, ratings, attachments, truck and supply information. Its instruction that the player and “other Players” have no problems reading the recorded information appears in a paragraph on how information may be written. The note correctly retains this wording and its relationship to the specific secrecy rules as an interpretation, rather than reading it as a universal opponent sheet-inspection grant.
- Sections 4.53–.55, p. 11, describe Rear Area, Air Marshal and Logistics forms and their users and records, including convoy cargo/location, vehicle repair, POWs, squadron missions, trucks and supply dumps. Those descriptions establish what forms track, not an audience for every field. Land §32.2, p. 43, expressly allows the Supply Dump, Truck Convoy and TOE sheets for abstract Ammo/Fuel tracking; it does not transfer every detailed-Air or Logistics action into Land-only play.
- Addendum §§3.61–.62, p. 45, restricts opposing counter status, composition and other attributes except as expressly allowed, describes its examples as **partial**, and permits inspection of map stacks while distinguishing their contents. Its explicit categories include marker contents, POW/camp/guard numbers and Axis nationality, construction item, and truck/Replacement Point numbers and types. The note properly leaves capture, precise viewer and trigger interactions open; an otherwise allowed source disclosure is not suppressed merely because a value also appears on a sheet.
- [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) §19.5 concerns additional attachments and keeping their information on TOE Log Sheets; it does not expressly grant opposing inspection. Extracted-text search did not identify a directly numbered §4.5–.55 correction; indirect and later corrections remain outside that bounded result.
