# Fort Maddalena and 11th Hussars: initial ZOC baseline

**Disposition:** At printed standalone starting strengths, Fort Maddalena's garrison has six raw defensive Close Assault Points, below §10.15's ten-point threshold; 11th Hussars has eighteen, but its printed one-point counter does not independently exceed §10.11's stacking threshold. Both fixed scenario hexes are adjacent across a blue checkered line interpreted elsewhere as a national boundary; its exact movement/ZOC effects and other setup units remain unproved. These are conditional source baselines, not a complete initial state or proof of compulsory combat.

## Printed placements and units

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §§60.31 and 60.41, fixes Fort Maddalena Garrison at C3019 and 11th Hussars at C3020 with three medium trucks. The latter carries `7Spt/7` in its scenario identification; 7th Armored HQ at D3612 is listed with all its separately listed division units detached. Preserve assigned/attached distinction; §59.2 allows legal same-hex setup reorganization but does not relocate fixed entries.
- [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 14, Minor Garrison Units, lists Fort Maddalenna Garrison Bn, `FtMad`, ID `q`, `U@2`, `D`, basic morale −3, and identifies its location C3019. Section 4.46b p. 26 classifies `q` infantry battalion-equivalent, maximum TOE 3, CPA 15, AA 1, barrage 6, anti-armor 4, vulnerability 4, offensive/defensive Close Assault 3/3. `U@2` supplies a two-TOE initial baseline; `2 × 3 = 6` raw defense before other units or adjustments. It is not below half of maximum under Land §9.26's battalion-shell definition. The printed [counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf) PDF p. 1, section 2, shows `G`, `II`, and `1` at `FtMad`; under Land §9.16(a) garrison units stack freely in their assigned city/village, so distinguish the printed value from its home-location stacking effect.
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 6, 7th Armored Division, lists 11th Hussars `11 Hus`, ID `ll`, `U@6 TOE`, `D`, basic morale +2. Section 4.46a p. 23 groups `ll` as reconnaissance battalion-equivalent with CPA 45, armor protection 1, Close Assault 2/3, maximum TOE 8; other listed combat-rating cells are dashes. Its conditional raw defensive total is `6 × 3 = 18`; six of eight exceeds the shell threshold. Counter sheet PDF p. 4, section 7, `11 Hus` shows `II`, `1`, and `7Spt/7`. OA understrength notation supplies a point count, not an independently verified armored-car model or assurance a later actual force has six points.

## Stacking, setup and border

Land §§10.11 and 10.15 (PDF/printed pp. 17–18) require a qualifying unit or stack above one stacking point and at least ten raw defensive Close Assault Points for a ZOC, subject to other rules. A standalone Fort Maddalena garrison at the stated two-TOE baseline fails the raw threshold regardless of whether its special home stacking treatment counts here. A standalone 11th Hussars at six points satisfies the raw threshold but its printed one-point counter alone does not satisfy §10.11's greater-than-one threshold. Actual co-located, attached or otherwise represented units and source-specific stacking may change the result; do not convert these arithmetic examples into an isolated-stack fact.

Original [Map C](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 3, 600-dpi crop `(x=6400,y=10200,W=2200,H=1800)`, shows C3019 immediately west of C3020, with the blue checkered line following their common vertical side. `3020` is visible; fort name and linework overprint part of `3019`, supported by adjacent grid sequence and the scenario's separate coordinate. The line resembles the national-boundary lead supported by regional country labels in [Sheferzen border research](2026-11-17-graziani-map-sheferzen-border.md); its symbol identity, precise country classification and independent movement/§10.21 effects require an accepted map/rules policy. A line on their common side is not automatic proof of impassability, clear terrain, bidirectional ZOC or a mandatory attack. The separate dashed routes in the vicinity are not by themselves proof of crossing this side.

If an accepted border/region policy places the fort in Libya, §60.31's `Anywhere in Libya` units can be possible co-location candidates, subject to their actual identities, valid setup, limits and source corrections; do not assume they were placed there. The same §60.31 gives two special Autoblinda points, at most one per unit, or a Tobruk replacement alternative. A hypothetical single defensive-2 point added to the understrength preexisting fort garrison would arithmetically give 8 raw, not 10, but unit-composition, maximum-TOE, setup and contribution rules must be resolved before treating it as legal. Other units could raise stack points and raw strength beyond either singleton baseline. Initial snapshot, owner setup choices, occupancy and observation rights must all be explicit.
