# TR-01: Cairo instructor candidate

**Result:** The original setup and OA identify a named infantry battalion-equivalent at Cairo as a possible instructor for a 6th Australian infantry trainee. Unlike the 6th Australian listing, its setup line does not say it is training; actual later status remains unproved. Its source-baseline strength exceeds §17.35's one-third threshold, but **actual instructor eligibility and co-location are not proved**. This narrows the earlier [training source gap](2026-11-18-graziani-training-source-next.md); it does not establish a credited training stage or decide replacement training.

## Source comparison

- Scenario §60.41, [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, lists 6th Australian Division in training at **Cairo and/or Helwan**, excluding 19th Australian Brigade and the two specified field-artillery units. The same setup separately lists **2nd King's Own Royal Regiment** in Cairo. These lines do not specify which Cairo hex contains each unit or every trainee's final placement.
- [Commonwealth charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 11, list `2 KOR`, ID `m`, `N`, `D` under unassigned British infantry-type units, with Basic Morale +1 and no parenthesized second rating on that sheet. The OA key on PDF p. 5 defines `N` as maximum TOE for non-tank/non-gun units and `D` as deployed at the first stage of turn one. PDF p. 22 classifies ID `m` as an infantry battalion-equivalent with maximum **six** TOE. This is source evidence for the candidate's initial baseline, not proof of its current state at a training checkpoint or that its printed regiment name automatically settles every representation question.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 27–28 §§17.1 and 17.31–.35, distinguish designated/untrained Basic Morale and require an instructional combat battalion at designated Basic Morale, in the trainee's hex during each training Operations Stage, with at least one-third assigned TOE strength. Infantry divisions or brigades may use an infantry-type battalion; a unit with tanks needs a tank battalion. Assuming six assigned and six present qualifying points, 2 KOR's six exceed the two-point arithmetic threshold; its sole +1 OA rating is evidence consistent with already-designated morale, not proof against an applicable setup or attachment exception.

For a candidate source-backed snapshot, select an actual eligible **infantry** trainee and a Cairo city hex containing both it and 2 KOR, establish current combat classification, assigned and actual TOE, applicable Basic Morale and attachment effects, and verify required presence during the relevant stage. Whether uninterrupted presence is required remains an interpretation. The scenario's alternative Helwan and Cairo locations cannot be collapsed into automatic co-location. Section 17.33's no-CP requirement applies to the **training unit**; instructor timing and movement must be examined separately. The printed training label does not prove legal continued training, no interruption or a completed six-stage increment. This infantry candidate is not a tank instructor and does not resolve the separate §20.43 replacement-point interaction.
