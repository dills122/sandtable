# Destruction actor scope: independent review

**Disposition:** The [actor-scope note](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-actor-scope.md) correctly retains actor, ownership and timing as separate unresolved questions. Neither the general movement sequence nor the capture rule expressly resolves who may invoke §32.17 or whose CP is charged.

## Original source checks

- [Land](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §32.17, says players may attempt destruction of Supply Units “they are with”; the player expends half the CPA of all units in the hex, rounded upwards, and rolls a die for each Supply Unit in the hex. The attempt precedes any movement in a given sub-segment, once per Supply Unit per sub-segment. This does not expressly specify phasing status, target ownership, the meaning of “with,” or which side's units bear the charge. It also does not expressly authorize a player to select a subset of all Supply Units.
- Section 32.13 says a Supply Unit lacks combat capabilities and is captured if an enemy combat unit enters its hex, with supplies immediately and freely usable. Section 8.13 p. 14 generally prohibits entering a hex containing an enemy unit and cites §27.4, with separate aircraft treatment. These are distinct texts requiring an interpretation of interaction; neither demonstrates a durable opposing stack, an automatic general exception, or a particular subsequent ownership representation.
- Sequence §5.2.G p. 12 assigns Player A's Movement and Combat Phase and optional repetitions subject to §8.2; Player B later performs phases F–L with roles exchanged. Section 8.0 and §8.11 p. 13 describe non-phasing reaction/retreat opportunities. Neither assigns §32.17 to the phasing player alone nor explicitly authorizes the opponent to initiate it. An undefined sub-segment and “before any movement” also leave the relevant movement history unresolved.
- Section 6.14 p. 12 counts each unit's CP across both players' portions of an Operations Stage. Section 6.16 says CP do not transfer between units and unused CP do not carry to the next Operations Stage; it does not itself resolve whose expenditure an opposing player may command, nor provide an allocation for a hex-wide aggregate. Do not infer cross-unit pooling from §32.17.

The note's proposed actor, ownership, occupancy, resource and privacy gates are policy requirements for an executable interpretation, not printed affirmative rules. Potential §10.29 ZOC capture and the common chart cost interaction rely on their linked separate reviews; they do not settle actor authority here.
