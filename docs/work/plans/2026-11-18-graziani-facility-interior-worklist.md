# Graziani facility interior review worklist

**Scope.** Partition of the 30 on-map air facilities listed in the [scenario extent crosswalk](2026-11-18-graziani-scenario-extent-crosswalk.md), using existing notes and reviews only; no new map scan. Facility designation and coordinate below come from the scenario list. A map locality dot, name, or other symbol is a separate observation and does not by itself establish a printed air-facility symbol or its capacity. This is not a terrain, placement, control, or legal-action certification.

**Method.** “Reviewed observation” means an existing independent same-scan review explicitly locates at least one identified feature in the target interior. It does not mean the entire interior or both terrain and locality were reviewed. “Ambiguous” means an existing review specifically raises an interior attribution, symbol, or coastal classification question; a positive observation can coexist with that question. “Not found” means no independent review establishing an interior terrain or locality observation was found in the cited facility reviews and targeted prior reviews; it does **not** mean no map feature, no first-reader observation, or no scan exists. Side readings do not settle interior contents. Reviews use the same map scan, not an independent edition.
