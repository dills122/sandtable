# Land-only weather: repair and construction interactions

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Bounded source analysis, not an admitted runtime rule or evidence that a storm or repair occurs in Graziani GT1–6. Prior [facility control review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-facility-control-review.md) already identifies §§22.13(d), 22.36–.37; [repair policy](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md) proposes an abstract-supply interpretation; [weather effects](2026-11-18-graziani-land-weather-effects.md) records construction prohibitions. This note adds the express storm interruption and restart language in §24.22 and separates it from vehicle repair and its own timing. It does not reopen settled source transcription as a new ruling.

## Source facts

| Subject | Printed cases | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vehicle repair | §22.13(d) bars repairs when a hex has rainstorm or sandstorm, except Major Repair Facilities. §22.27 points field repair to that restriction; §22.36 says temporary facilities may be affected and major facilities never are affected by weather. | Major-facility weather exception alone does not establish an otherwise legal repair. Facility identity, applicable repair type and remaining conditions matter. Section 22.37's temporary-facility **major-city exception concerns enemy ZOC**, not weather. |
| Repair timing and other gates | §22.12 places repair in the phasing player's Repair Phase. §22.13 also lists enemy control, unavailable supplies and towing in that phase. §22.32 separately requires a broken vehicle or destroyed tank to begin the Maintenance Segment at a facility without towing during that Operations Stage. | Do not merge the general phase towing limit with the facility rule's stage-wide wording, or assume storm exception waives control, supply, towing, type or timing requirements. Destroyed tanks use §22.4 and its explicit special exceptions. |
| Construction in storms | §§29.42 and 29.54 prohibit construction in the stated storm conditions. §24.22 expressly says no construction in a hex affected by sandstorm or rainstorm: that Operations Stage does not count toward construction time, but construction does not thereby stop entirely. If a unit involved in construction leaves a hex during either storm, construction stops completely and must start again from the beginning. | An affected stage with the unit remaining is not automatically a reset. A departure in storm is not merely a paused stage. Determine the actual unit, hex, weather and departure before applying either result. |
| Construction timing | §24.11 initiates and undertakes construction in the Organization Phase's Construction Segment, completing at the beginning of a subsequent segment; construction time includes its starting Operations Stage. §24.12 says expending any CP halts construction and that Operations Stage does not count. | §24.12's CP-based halt and §24.22's storm-departure restart are separately worded; do not infer the same reset from every halt. §5.2 places Weather Determination before Organization, with the phasing player's Repair Phase later. Section 24.11's completion-at-segment-start versus §24.22's no-construction-in-storm wording leaves exact completion on a newly storm-affected stage an interpretation to choose. |
| Repair-facility construction | §§24.81–.84 distinguish new temporary construction from rebuilding; §24.83 requires an engineer for three Construction Segments, and §24.84 describes a particular major-city rebuilding sequence. §§24.86–.88 separately cover dismantling, including inability to use a temporary facility during dismantling. | Building or rebuilding a facility is construction; repairing a vehicle is a separate §22 process. Whether §24.22's interruption/restart wording also governs §24.86 dismantling needs an explicit interpretation of that activity and segment, rather than an assumed answer. |
| Weather and supply abstraction | §24.21 requires 10 water points at each construction site in hot weather, while §29.32 also specifies hot-weather construction-site water rations. §§24.13–.14 and individual construction cases specify ordinary supplies. §32.0/.11 substitute Land-only supply rules and disregard ordinary usage/expenditure. | The prior [repair-resource matrix](2026-11-13-graziani-repair-resource-matrix.md) distinguishes expenditure from §22.13(b)'s availability condition. Existing `graziani-repair-abstract-v1` is a **proposed** broader displacement choice, not a source-established rule. Apply the same careful question to construction prerequisites and hot-water accounting; no abstract stores/water balance or charge follows automatically. |

## Decisions and verification still needed

1. Select how storm status at Construction Segment start interacts with completion already due under §24.11; distinguish earned prior stages from a storm stage, and specify the observable status before any completion event. Decide timing when a construction participant leaves an affected hex, including multiple participants and projects spanning adjacent hexes. The cases do not spell out every multi-hex attribution or event ordering.
2. Define the treatment of dismantling and other actions called repair or rebuilding by their actual source case. Facility repair of vehicles at a major facility during a storm is a weather exception, not permission to construct there. Preserve facility type and its independently unresolved [Tobruk control lifecycle](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-facility-control.md).
3. For repair, choose the Land-only treatment of §22.13(b), individual supply debits and any separate abstract availability witness; independently retain weather, towing, control and repair-type gates. For construction, decide any surviving material-availability and hot-water requirements without assuming the repair proposal answers them.
4. After decisions, test synthetic normal/hot/rain/sand states; field versus temporary versus major facility; temporary facility in a major city under storm versus enemy ZOC; towed in phase versus earlier in stage; paused stage with unit remaining versus departure and restart; CP expenditure without storm; completion due as storm begins; and new/rebuilt/dismantled facilities. Check exact boundaries, resource effects, stage order, idempotency and replay. These are proposed checks, not executed tests or proof of reachability.
