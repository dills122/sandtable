# Independent review: fleet, AA and road-stacking census

**Disposition:** No material transcription or omitted numbered case found in the inspected §§32.7–32.9. The census is bounded source coverage, not an implementation or scenario acceptance. One useful precision: §32.74(a) counts the selected column from the table's left side; the die is a column selector, not itself a number of bomb points. The census's wording is consistent with that, but implementers should retain the indexing and consult the chart rather than derive points arithmetically.

| Cases checked | Independent finding |

Census distinguishes allowance from stage cap and leaves sequence mapping and actual state open. |

Their existing reviews do not prove actual six-turn availability, a legal target or acceptance of unresolved policy. The census correctly limits §32.0 to Land without **both** Air and Logistics; no general mixed-mode authority follows. September errata §32.0 warns the abstract rules were untested, while its numbered sequence does not directly correct these cases. Chart §41.5 correction is separately identified in the cited review. This does not establish absence of all indirect or later corrections.
