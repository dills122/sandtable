## Source check

Visually inspected the [scenario scan](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF pages 3–4 (printed pages 27–28) and all four pages of the [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf). Local SHA-256 verified before reading:

- Scenario: `fa12488e59552d7b0ff0d5abefa891d4b81af0492256bec2d25f8d61ef040a66`.
- Errata: `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`.

Scenario page 27, upper left, §60.22 names Graziani’s Offensive, ends at conclusion of game turn 6, OpStage 3, and points to case 60.71 for victory. Immediately following, §60.23 names the Italian Campaign, ends at the end of game turn 20, OpStage 3, and points to 60.72. Page 28's §60.7 begins at bottom of center column and finishes at top of right column: it addresses initial construction and has no numbered 60.71 or 60.72 subsection. The following §60.8 is first scenario group victory conditions; its §60.81 is headed Graziani’s Offensive, and §60.82 is headed the Italian Campaign. The latter includes a location points table and a separate supply-line victory condition, unlike §60.81's named Italian and Commonwealth victory levels.

Errata page 3's right column lists §59.2, §59.45, §60.31, §60.32 and §60.45 before moving to §61.38 and §61.41. No explicit correction of §60.22, §60.23, §60.71, §60.72, §60.81 or §60.82 was found on any of the four pages. This negative finding applies only to this package; other official material may exist.

The two named scenario-to-heading matches and identical one-section offset support mapping the six-turn scenario to §60.81 and the twenty-turn scenario to §60.82. They are internal consistency evidence from the same scenario scan, not independent editions. Preserve original 60.71 reference in provenance and label substitution as interpretation. The scenario's turn endpoint alone does not settle event ordering, routes, quantifiers, simultaneous results, privacy or completeness of map/control evidence; none is admitted by this review.
