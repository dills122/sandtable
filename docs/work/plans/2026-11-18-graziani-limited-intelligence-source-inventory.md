# FG-01: limited-intelligence source inventory

**Scope:** Land addendum §§3.61–.62 is explicitly a **partial** list, not an exhaustive action-by-action privacy contract. This inventory records its printed examples and selected directly relevant patrol/ZOC rules and September corrections. “Viewer” means the player described by the source, not an invented digital audience. Unspecified timing, visibility and mode interactions remain questions; a dash does not mean information is universally public or secret. No new runtime or disclosure policy is selected.

Land §3.61 restricts knowledge of an opposing counter's status, composition and attributes except as expressly indicated in rules. Its introductory passage describes land combat totals being revealed without identifying the units producing them; §3.62 immediately distinguishes the following cases. See also [victory publication review](2026-11-18-graziani-victory-publication-source-review.md).

| Printed case, Land p. 45 §3.62 | Viewer / trigger | Source-described information and remaining interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Map | A player examining a stack on the game map. | May examine the counters; the passage distinguishes what is **inside** them. It does not settle which owner records, hidden attachments or prior observations are visible. [Vehicle-marker review](2026-11-17-graziani-vehicle-marker-fog-review.md) distinguishes visible marker from contents. |
| Anti-armor fire | Opponent observing participants in an assault in a hex. | Types of tanks and self-propelled guns and presence of armored recce and/or halftracks actively participating are described; numbers, origin hexes and which TOE points absorbed damage are not. Resolve interaction with each actual combat procedure rather than revealing a complete formation. |
| Close assault | Opponent observing unit types actively participating in Close Assault or forced into it by Overrun. | Unit types, but not numbers, weapon systems or what took losses. The introductory aggregate-only statement and specific type exceptions need to be preserved together. |
| Prisoners | Captor receiving prisoners and captured equipment. | The entry says prisoners cannot be questioned for additional intelligence. It does not settle every authorized record or later capture disclosure. |
| Patrol | Patrolling player receiving a patrol report. | No supply status or attached-truck detail, except whether the unit is motorized. Main §16.51 and its addendum give additional bounded disclosures below; do not infer actual trucks or fuel. |
| Broken-down vehicles / destroyed tanks / abandoned vehicles | Observer of a marker. | Marker **contents** are secret. Capture, repair, lot identity and partial-marker events need separate authority; this sentence does not settle exactly what a captor learns. [Marker review](2026-11-17-graziani-vehicle-marker-fog-review.md). |
| POWs / POW camps / guards | Opposing observer; no separate timing given in the entry. | Numbers are secret, as is nationality if Axis. It does not say all camp counters or positions are invisible. |
| Construction | Opposing observer; no separate completion trigger given. | The item undergoing construction/demolition is secret. The entry does not specify whether every construction marker, stage or completed effect stays hidden. |
| Trucks and Replacement Points | Opposing observer; no separate trigger given. | Numbers, types and other details are secret. Do not infer that existence or a known visible counter must also be concealed. |
| Air reconnaissance | Air reconnaissance observer. | Receives less detail than Patrol; this entry does not enumerate a complete report. Detailed Air applicability is separate in the Land-only profile. |
| Planes: air-air combat | Players observing opposing planes engaged in air-air combat. | Exact aircraft makes are knowable; pilot ratings are secret until the instant of combat. This does not establish a Land-only aircraft event. |
| Planes: bombing/strafing | Land units without AA TOE Strength Points, or an SGSU, in the mission hex. | Are not entitled to numbers or types of aircraft, only the mission type. The sentence does not itself specify what a unit **with** AA points is entitled to know. Detailed Air and SGSU applicability require separate mode checks. |

## Directly related source exceptions and unresolved scope

| Source and trigger | Printed boundary | Interaction not settled here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Land §16.51, p. 27, successful patrol report; p. 45 §16.51 addendum | Non-phasing player reports historical designation, unit type, motorized status and strength within two of actual. Addendum says patroller does **not** identify types of constituent TOE points. | Selection, exact noise policy and allowed identity detail must respect §§16.52–.55 and actual result; approximate strength is not an exact disclosure. [Patrol addendum check](graziani-patrol-review/independent-addendum.md), [patrol details review](2026-11-17-graziani-patrol-details-independent.md). |
| Land §§16.52–.55, p. 27, patrol report selection | Non-phasing player normally chooses which units to reveal when more are present than the result requires; provisions qualify size, HQ/AA disclosure and phasing player's exclusions. | Do not replace these conditional rules with a universal all-units report. Actual eligible report, source-specific exceptions and side privacy remain case-specific. |
| Land §16.32, p. 26, patrol survival check | Non-phasing player must state if no combat units are present at target; AA/Flak and engineers do not count as combat units **for that check**. | This disclosure/classification is not a general combat, victory or all-state rule. Dummies and other types need their own cases. |
| Land §10.16, p. 18, potential enemy ZOC | Non-phasing player must inform whether a particular unit exerts ZOC when it **could** exert into a hex and a phasing unit begins a Movement Segment there or moves into it. | Exact timing and eligible-unit/terrain conditions are narrower than blanket revelation of all opposing ZOCs. Disclosure on unsuccessful attempts or unrelated actions is not specified by this clause. |
| Land §3.6 planes and §32.0, p. 43 | §32.0 applies abstract Air/Logistics rules when playing Land without those games. | The detailed-Air disclosure examples remain source evidence but do not by themselves create aircraft, pilot or mission actions in Land-only play. Identify any independently retained abstract event before applying them. |
| September errata §16.11, p. 2 | Adds Italian L/6, Commonwealth Stuarts and mechanized infantry/Panzergrenadiers of either side to eligible patrol types. | This changes who may patrol, not the content of §16.51 reports or proof a particular unit is present. No directly numbered §3.6 correction was identified in the inspected errata text pages; this is not an exhaustive indirect or later-errata search. |

The table identifies printed exceptions and unknown scope; it does not turn an allowed disclosure at one trigger into permission at every trigger, or replace a source rule with an inferred channel policy. Mixed nationality, capture, actual patrol selection, weather and mode, and currently observed versus remembered information need their own bounded checks. Some existing reviews cover patrol, marker content and victory publication; other §3.62 entries above are source inventory, not claims of independent full-system review or actual scenario reachability.
