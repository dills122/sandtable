**Disposition:** The six-entry inventory and its stated coverage of the two named notes match the inspected page and current notes. The inventory does not establish a clearance policy or a complete census of all corrections.

Visually inspected [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2, right column. The entries following §29.61 are §30.5, §30.55, §30.57, §30.58, §30.59 and §32.0, followed by §34.72. No §32.4 entry appears in that intervening range on this page. The case labels and substance in the inventory match: §30.5 points to §56.0; §30.55 permits transfer after barrage/bombardment despite the stated CP restriction; §30.58 points to §55.2; and §30.59 relocates the chart to §55.3. The §32.0 note warns that abstract rules were untested; it supplies no replacement mechanics.

Section 30.57's text supports the stated incoming/outgoing SP distinction, 10% maximum-tonnage reduction, Tobruk arithmetic and reciprocal supply effect. The source literally says **Air incoming capacity** and **at least** one-third, as the inventory preserves. Its first sentence states the reduction for the stage, while the worked Tobruk example describes the reduced maximum for the turn; the example should not silently settle that time-unit difference or establish actual throughput. Applicability to abstract Supply Units and its relation to chart §55.3 remain separate from transcribing the erratum.
