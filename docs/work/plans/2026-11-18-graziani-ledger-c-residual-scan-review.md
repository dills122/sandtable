# Review: French Marine reinforcement-track comparison

**Disposition:** Source comparison supported. The later infantry battalion has a real printed **OpStage** discrepancy, not a different Game-Turn, and neither inspected schedule row establishes a conversion or withdrawal of the original company. No material source error found.

- Commonwealth schedule §4.43a PDF p. 4 prints **GT62/2** for 1 Free French Brigade HQ and four named infantry battalions; **GT63/3** for 2nd NA anti-tank company and 1st Battalion de fusiliers; and **GT65/3** for 1st Battalion d'infanterie de marine, five medium trucks. The schedule key defines the columns as Game-Turn then Operations Stage. Neither latter row carries a `WD` or `Rtn` marker or names the initial company.
- Commonwealth OA PDF p. 20 lists 1st Battalion de fusiliers, `1 Fslrs`, ID `ff`, four light AA, **3/63**; its note calls it 1st battalion de fusiliers marine. It lists 1st Battalion d'inf de marine, `1 Marine`, ID `r`, `N`, **1/65** under 1 Free French Brigade. Thus the former agrees with its schedule row; the latter conflicts on stage with **65/3**. The common unit name/parent supports comparison, but does not choose a precedence or prove implementation arrival.
- OA PDF p. 21 lists `1st Company, Fr Mtr Marine`, counter `1`, ID `v`, with note describing a company of 1st Free French Motor Marine Battalion. Its printed `3/65` arrival is separately corrected to `D` by September errata §4.44b item 3, PDF p. 1; that company correction does not expressly correct either later battalion's stage. Scenario §60.41's C3926 placement and §60.22's GT6/3 endpoint are corroborated by the previously reviewed scenario source. The checked rows do not by themselves prove a transition, withdrawal, simultaneous survival or independent later identity.

**Scope:** This is a useful bounded check against the previously excluded schedule category, not an exhaustive unnamed withdrawal search or proof no indirect/later correction exists. Both disputed battalion stages are far beyond Graziani's six-turn endpoint; the discrepancy does not change its printed initial company placement.
