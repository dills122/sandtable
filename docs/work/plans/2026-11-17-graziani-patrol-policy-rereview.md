# Patrol history proposal: policy re-review

**Disposition:** coherent as a bounded planning interpretation, subject to clarification of the mixed known-failure/unknown case below. Not approved for runtime admission: causal completion, event classification, air sequencing, correction/replay, privacy and the remaining patrol rules still need executable contracts. Source alternatives remain live; this is not an official correction or evidence of six-turn reachability.

The revised [proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-patrol-policy-proposal.md) addresses the earlier [review](2026-11-17-graziani-patrol-policy-review.md)'s three principal findings:

- It requires a phase-scoped causal watermark or equivalent proof, treats pending or ambiguous earlier actions as unknown, and defers late completion/correction handling to a versioned replay protocol. The proof must cover the **current patrol cut**, not require player B's later actions to finish before A patrols; its use of a phase boundary permits that interpretation. Concrete producer watermarks and phase ordering remain unimplemented.
- It expressly distinguishes §16.16's detailed-game expenditure from §32.25's Land-only no-expenditure rule, without claiming other eligibility or CP rules disappear.
- It expands privacy review beyond response strings to execution effects, observations, RNG, casualties, event counts and corrections. It recognizes that identical immediate messages cannot hide a later successful patrol versus refusal, and leaves audience entitlement or a complete public gate as an admission requirement. This is an identified gate, not a demonstrated side-safe protocol.

One logical point needs clarification before implementation: the proposal says *any* known qualifying earlier combat makes patrol ineligible, but also says a pending or ambiguously classified earlier action makes the relevant predicate unknown. When both occur, these statements can conflict. For a three-valued existential prohibition, a fully verified qualifying occurrence establishes known ineligibility even if another event remains unknown; a known qualifying target bombardment similarly establishes that target's ineligibility. Unknown prevents a **pass** when no known disqualifying witness exists and coverage is incomplete. Alternatively, intentionally make all incomplete histories unknown; state that stricter evaluation explicitly. Neither internal distinction may be exposed without the separately accepted privacy contract. Add tests for known disqualifier plus an unresolved unrelated event, unknown alone, and complete verified absence.

The chosen stage-wide past-event window, phasing order and current-stage target window remain internally consistent as planning choices. The source's narrower phasing-player wording and unbounded target-history reading remain possible. §32.25 applies to patrol supply *expenditure* under Land-only rules; it does not settle other action prerequisites. Event classification, especially no-effect attacks and patrol losses, remains a separate gate. Equal visible histories still require a complete design for action effects and future progression, not merely generic errors.

This is a re-review of the revised proposal and prior same-scan source checks, not a new primary-source inspection, runtime verification, independent edition or legal-rights finding. No code, gameplay test or readiness state changed.
