# Saharan Detachment (I): source composition and trucks

**Finding, 2026-11-17:** [Scenario §60.31](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, lists **Saharan Det (I)** within three hexes of C0716, with **5 light trucks** on that same setup row. This is one formation entry, not an additional combat counter alongside its OA constituents. It does not fix a particular hex, list five medium trucks, or allocate individual trucks to individual subordinate counters. The separate C0716 supply dump is not the detachment's fixed location.

[Italian OA §4.44b](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 11, lists the following seven counters under **The Saharan Detachment**, basic morale **+1**, all arrival `D` (deployed). OA key on PDF p. 4 defines `D` as on-map at GT1's first Operations Stage; `N` as maximum TOE for non-tank/non-gun units; `U@2` as two starting TOE points. Indentation indicates assignment to the parent; it should not be silently conflated with a proved current attachment or a separate setup location.

Values from Italian unit characteristics §4.46b, Axis charts PDF pp. 26–27. Parenthesized HQ values carry the chart's conditional-use restriction. `+` on **hh**, not on the detachment HQ or the infantry battalions, means that unit historically had enough trucks to motorize its components; its CPA changes while motorized. It does not specify truck type, exact truck count, or authorize adding an independently quantified pool to the scenario's five light trucks. Distinguish the Sahara motor company from the Giarabub motor-MG battalion, a separate OA entry on PDF p. 14.

These are baseline OA values. Scenario §60.31 also permits the Italian player to attach each of two `ww` Autoblinda 40 reconnaissance TOE points to any unit, at most one per unit, ignoring normal composition restrictions; alternatively they may deploy in Tobruk as replacements. This scenario-wide choice can potentially augment a Saharan unit, but the scenario does not select that allocation. Other applicable attachment, location, and stacking rules require separate adjudication.

The battery's explicitly listed deployment is five weapon TOE points despite the `mm` chart's normal maximum of four. The §4.46b key permits maximum TOE to be exceeded only due to arrival/deployment strength; preserve the five listed points without silently changing normal maximum to five. Gun/AA figures are game TOE points, not a demonstrated count of individual physical weapons. Scenario §§59.2 and 59.42 require first-line trucks to be assigned to units in their listed hex; §59.45 permits eligible initial loads or motorization without cost, but does not choose them. Under §59.63(A), these light trucks contribute zero abstract motorization points by the stated medium/heavy conversion; other sources are not ruled out. [Land §4.25](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) defaults assigned units starting in their parent's hex to attached, unless specifically stated otherwise. Whether constituents may start distributed across this radius remains unresolved; neither forced co-location nor free dispersal follows from these passages. Resolving loads, towing, motorization and current attachment requires further rules and choices; neither full detachment motorization nor extra trucks follows from `hh` alone.
