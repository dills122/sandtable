# 7th Armoured shell threshold and 5% convoy candidate

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition:** Initial 7th Armoured is a shell under the specified default organization; a later reunion with 1 RTR gives a concrete *conditional formation target*, not a demonstrated legal movement or convoy witness. The [5% follow-up review](2026-11-17-graziani-five-percent-witness-followup-review.md)'s seven-index calculation remains conditional and pair-restricted. No legal six-turn witness or impossibility follows.

## Source-backed formation threshold

Scenario [§60.41](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, puts 7th Armoured Division at D3612 and explicitly detaches its earlier listed units: 1 KRRC and 4 RHA at C3922, 3 RHA at C3721, 1 RTR at C3520, 2nd Rifle Brigade at C3320, and 11 Hussars at C3020. The [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 6, associates 4th Armoured Brigade with 6 RTR and 7 Hussars, 7th Armoured Brigade with 1 RTR and 8 Hussars, and 7th Support Group with its listed infantry, artillery and reconnaissance components. `D` is deployment, not evidence that separately placed units are attached to D3612.

The OA gives 6 RTR and 7 Hussars ten tanks each and 1 RTR and 8 Hussars seven each. Code `g` has a ten-tank maximum (Commonwealth chart §4.46a, PDF p. 22); all four are above the [Land §9.26](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) battalion-shell cutoff, assuming printed initial strengths. Commonwealth §19.31, PDF p. 24, gives the GT1–18 type-I armoured division two armoured brigade slots and one support-group slot, and each type-I armoured brigade three tank battalion slots. Land §§9.26–.27 make a division a shell at **50% or less** attached brigade-equivalents, and exclude a shell brigade from its count. Thus two qualifying brigade-equivalents of three are needed. An ordinary three-slot armoured brigade needs two qualifying battalions; the separate two-battalion exception must not be discarded for other formation types.

Under the source-default co-location and attachment baseline at D3612, 4th Armoured Brigade has 6 RTR and 7 Hussars: **2/3**, non-shell. 7th Armoured Brigade has 8 Hussars only: **1/3**, shell. The support group's named combat components are elsewhere and its HQ alone does not establish a non-shell brigade. Division count **1/3** is therefore shell. This is an explicit baseline, not every legal owner setup: [§59.2](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, allows organization of units listed in the **same hex**, subject to formation and stacking limits. Land §4.25's default applies subject to exceptions; §19.13 requires actual attachment in the parent's same hex, not merely assignment. Separately located 1 RTR cannot be silently attached initially. Flexible placement of the specified map D/E artillery, anti-tank and AA units does not by itself prove a second qualifying brigade or a legal substitute.

An accounting check: moving one of the two qualifying tank battalions from 4th Brigade to 7th would leave counts 1 and 2, so does not by itself produce two non-shell armoured brigades. Whether any reassignment is legal is a separate question; Land §§19.26, 19.41–.46 govern assignment, attachment and substitution. In particular §19.45 concerns substitution for a missing assigned unit, not a general permission to substitute for any detached unit. Additional attachment limits are separate. The support-group chart lists two infantry slots plus other arms; its shell calculation, especially the two-battalion exception and actual attached units, needs a fully specified organization rather than a blanket two-of-three assumption.

## Conditional later candidate

A concrete organizational target is reunion of 1 RTR with 7th Armoured Brigade, with 8 Hussars still attached and qualifying, while 4th Brigade retains both 6 RTR and 7 Hussars. **If** all four battalions and both brigades are legally attached, retain the printed qualifying strengths, and the hierarchy and stack are legal at one snapshot, both armoured brigades are 2/3 and the division is non-shell at 2/3. This is a testable threshold, not proof that 1 RTR can reach and attach to the division within the scenario. Land §19.13 requires same-hex attachment; §19.41 gives the assigned-unit attachment conditions and cost during play. Land §§9.14 and 9.3–.35 impose terrain and timing-dependent stacking limits; attached formation counters and shell status affect stacking. Do not infer a legal stack from four individual counters or assume attachment works at a distance. Any later move, reorganization, losses and the current location of each parent and child must be recorded.

For a **synthetic lookup only**, retain the review's strict west-of, westernmost-qualifier and 33-column transform hypotheses. With 7th Armoured non-shell and 4th Indian non-shell, their initial D-relative indices are 12 and 15. To select lane 6's 21–40 Bomb Points band `1–10`, both named divisions must end at or west of index 10; net changes are at least **2 and 5**, respectively. The second-westernmost qualifying position must also remain in the band. Ties mean two distinct divisions in the same east–west row, not necessarily the same hex. These seven index differences are neither a radius, a distance in traversable hexes, movement points, nor a path. No endpoint, route, travel time, legal stack, line of supply, or convoy event is asserted. Formation assembly may require additional travel; other non-shell divisions may change the selected order statistic and pair-restricted bound.

The closest candidate therefore needs, at the *same actual Naval Convoy Arrival snapshot*: a complete legal attachment and strength record for both divisions and every other relevant division; certified map rows and threshold/equality policy; legal paths and phase-by-phase stack, movement, supply and enemy-state checks; an actual preplanned permitted lane-6 convoy and Commonwealth attack; and sequential `55–64` on common chart §41.5 (for example `56`). Scenario §60.22 starts at GT1 OpStage 1 and ends GT6 OpStage 3; ordinary convoy-stage sequencing and any pre-start convoy snapshot require the separate startup decision identified in the review. A 5% cell still needs a separate whole-loss allocation and rounding policy. None of these gates is satisfied merely by the arithmetic or by assuming a unit can move within a stated radius.
