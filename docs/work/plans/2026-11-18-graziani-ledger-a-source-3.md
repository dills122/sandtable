# LA-07 / SQ-07: C3922 motorization priority discriminator

**Result:** The printed C3922 setup provides a concrete case for the open interaction between scenario owner-selected first-line allocation and Land-only priority for historically motorized infantry. It does **not** prove that 1 KRRC is motorized at setup or patrol time, determine its assigned quantity, or decide whether §32.54 overrides the scenario's allocation language.

## Source comparison

- Scenario §60.41, printed p. 27, lists 1 KRRC and 3rd Coldstream Guards together at C3922, with **15 medium and five heavy** first-line trucks for that location. Scenario §59.42, p. 26, says listed first-line trucks must be assigned to units in their listed hex and may be divided among those units as the player desires. Section 59.63(A) converts each medium and heavy first-line truck into one attached Motorization Point, hex by hex; it gives no point for light trucks in its example. Thus the C3922 row is a **20-point listed input**, not proof of each unit's allocation or usable carrying capacity.
- Commonwealth OA p. 6 identifies KRRC as `n`, `N`, `D`; p. 11 identifies Coldstream Guards as `p`, `N`, `D`. The §4.46a chart p. 22 gives both an infantry battalion-equivalent maximum of six, with KRRC CPA `10+` and Coldstream CPA `10`. The chart key p. 23 identifies `+` as historically supplied with enough trucks to motorize its components. These are printed candidate baselines, not a certified present strength, assignment or transport.
- Land §32.54, p. 44, says Motorization Points sufficient to transport **all historically motorized infantry units** (CPA 8+ and 10+) must be assigned before assignment to other infantry-type units. Section 32.51 treats these points as medium Truck Points except where modified; §32.53 also mentions transport of units and 0+ guns. The text does not itself specify a per-hex priority boundary or establish that a physically distant attached pool can be freely reassigned at setup.

## Discriminating case and limits

Consider a candidate initial distribution assigning some C3922 points to Coldstream while leaving KRRC short of sufficient transport. A reading applying §32.54 to initial allocation would reject that ordering **if** the assigned points are legally available for KRRC and its historical category is established. A reading giving §59.42's player-selected first-line setup precedence, or applying the priority only after setup, needs to explain its treatment of the general abstract rule. The two passages do not expressly select either hierarchy. “All” also raises a separate scope question about historically motorized infantry at other locations; neither a single C3922 pool nor a global freely transferable pool follows automatically.

This is a **conditional policy discriminator**, not a claim that an allocation is legal or happened. The C3922 source row also lists artillery and another gun unit; assignment to non-infantry and their own transport prerequisites are not decided by §32.54's comparison of infantry types. Establish exact unit representation, eligible points and carrying requirements, organization/attachment limits, and applicable setup-specific precedence before asserting either a minimum for KRRC or free surplus. A `10+` historical label is not itself proof of current motorization. §59.66(B)'s optional initial loads apply only to attached points not initially carrying men or guns; without the distribution and carriage state, no loaded-point total can be inferred. The [prior allocation review](2026-11-18-graziani-motorization-allocation-followup-review.md) distinguishes later transfer of parent-attached trucks from assignment out of an unattached pool; do not use that transfer as proof of this setup allocation.

This case can also inform the [KRRC patrol candidate](2026-11-18-graziani-patrol-one-point-witness-review.md), but would still require actual stage state, target, route and patrol conditions. No legal patrol, cargo assignment or game action is established.
