# Commonwealth motorization arrivals and §60.47

**Finding:** Original sources do not unambiguously settle whether the Commonwealth's pre-November Replacement Point arrival ban also blocks motorization received at the medium-truck replacement rate. Two defensible readings yield different results. Neither establishes an actual GT1–6 arrival, amount, die result, or conversion of a scheduled truck row without its own legal event and timing.

## Printed evidence

| Source | What it says | Scope consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scenario §60.47, p. 28 | Commonwealth uses reinforcement track, bringing units where/when listed; **no Replacement Points may arrive before November 1940**. | Straightforward prohibition for Commonwealth replacement-point arrivals through GT6 October IV. Does not expressly name motorization or trucks. |
| Scenario §60.92(A), p. 28, Land Game only | Each player's ability to bring in Motorization Points equals ability to bring in Medium Truck Points; one truck point equals one motorization point. | Ratio links the *ability to bring in* the two categories; does not expressly say whether the resulting motorization is itself a Replacement Point for §60.47, or create an independent entitlement. |
| Land §32.52, p. 44 | Additional Motorization Points are received as reinforcements per scenario instructions. In addition, players may bring in Motorization Points as replacements at a rate equal to ability to bring in Medium Truck Replacement Points. | Expressly calls the latter motorization **replacements**; distinguishes that source from scenario reinforcements. No instruction that §60.47 must apply identically to both sources. |
| Land §20.0 and §20.2, p. 31 | General rule defines replacements as TOE strength points of particular classes; §20.2 lists infantry, gun, tank and recce points. | Truck/motorization points are not expressly listed in this definition; do not claim this list alone resolves every usage of the broader term. |
| Commonwealth production chart §20.78A, PDF p. 30 | Truck production rolls by type; result arrives four game-turns later. | Evidence of a medium-truck production mechanism and lead time, not a specific observed roll. Other source lead-time conflicts and initial planning phase remain unresolved. |
| Commonwealth land reinforcement track §4.43a, PDF p. 3 | GT5/1 7 Indian Brigade row lists 5 light and 20 medium trucks; GT6/3 6 NZ Brigade row lists 25 light, 85 medium, 5 heavy. | Printed accompanying trucks belong to whole-unit reinforcement rows. These are distinct from rolled production and do not alone prove their Land-only motorization conversion or eligible receipt; apply §32.52, §60.92 and source-specific scenario treatment. |

Scenario calendar is GT1 September III, GT2 September IV, GT3–6 October I–IV; scenario ends after GT6 OpStage 3. Thus “before November” covers all six turns, but says nothing conclusive about actual post-scenario events. No zero die roll should be inferred from a barred arrival.

## Competing bounded readings

The [independent source review](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-replacements-review.md)
adds that §32.51 treats motorization as Medium Truck Points except as modified,
and §20.74 expressly uses Medium Trucks as a replacement example. Both
strengthen the first interpretation; the second is not equally established
by §20.2's narrower list alone. Neither settles scenario-provided reinforcement
motorization, the arrival event or the other gates below.

1. **Ban rate-based replacement arrivals.** §32.52 specifically describes the new Motorization Points as replacements, and §60.47 bars replacement-point arrivals. Treat this source as unavailable before November even if a truck production roll or plan exists. Separately assess whole-unit scheduled truck/reinforcement rows; do not suppress them solely from this conclusion. This is the conservative no-premature-replacement reading, not an express source clarification.
2. **Allow motorization as its own category.** §60.47 names Replacement Points while §20.2 defines replacement classes without trucks or motorization. §60.92 uses medium-truck ability as a rate equivalence, and §32.52 gives motorization a distinct representation. Under this reading the prohibition applies to replacement TOE points, while a legally established medium-truck-based motorization arrival may occur before November. This depends on resolving whether the rate refers to hypothetical production eligibility or actual ability after scenario restrictions and any scenario-specific treatment of truck points; it does not grant an automatic positive receipt.

Do not conflate rate with delivery: even under the second reading, compute only from a lawful plan/production event, retained dice, date, scenario entry policy and any relevant source limits. §5.2 says two turns for Commonwealth replacement scheduling while §20.21 and the truck chart say four, and GT1 begins after the normal Naval Convoy Schedule Phase. Preserve those separate gates; neither interpretation permits assuming the missing pregame plan, a positive number, or a free same-turn delivery. Land-only motorization can receive reinforcements independently of rate-based replacements under §32.52, but exact mapping of listed accompanying light/medium/heavy trucks requires a separately approved crosswalk.

## Negative and boundary vectors

- In both policies, bar Commonwealth ordinary infantry/gun/tank/recce Replacement Point arrival during October; do not block a scheduled whole-unit reinforcement solely because it arrives before November.
- A valid recorded GT2 medium-truck production plan under four-turn chart timing nominally targets GT6. Compare the two interpretations: rate-based motorization is barred in the first; eligible only if all rate and arrival prerequisites are proven in the second. No actual GT2 plan or roll is asserted.
- Unrecorded production or uncertain pregame phase remains unknown, not zero. A roll in the current turn cannot automatically deliver points in the same turn. Do not force a roll just to fill a missing record.
- Keep GT5 and GT6 listed accompanying-truck rows distinct from §20.78A production. No direct 20- or 85-point motorization event until conversion, training, ownership and arrival semantics are independently certified. Do not convert light/heavy quantities through a rule that specifies medium equivalence without separate authority.
- At the November boundary, test eligibility anew if the campaign continues; scenario ends GT6. Distinguish pre-November receipt ban from planning for later and never infer post-scenario receipt as a Graziani outcome.
