## Material correction

The inventory's §4.44b row says the four-page PDF does not contain the omitted rosters. **Incorrect:** PDF p. 4 visibly embeds sheets headed 63rd Cirene, 61st Sirte and 62nd Marmarica divisions, with unit, counter, TOE/weapons and arrival columns. Errata p. 1 says those sheets were omitted from the original OA and points below; it does not mean they remain absent from this PDF. [Earlier six-turn inventory](../../research/2026-10-18-graziani-six-turn-surface-inventory.md) already records this correction. Presence of scans does not establish independent transcription, provenance beyond this copy, or rights. Correct the row and avoid treating the roster absence as a current source blocker.

## Confirmed scope

- **§59.45, PDF p. 3:** It states broadly that trucks may be loaded as the player wishes, then separately permits supplies above listed availability in trucks at scenario start. Inventory correctly preserves the extra-supply distinction and limits the second permission to starting loads. This text does not state a numerical maximum or resolve Land-only abstract conversion; neither absence proves unlimited stock or any specific Giarabub allocation. [Supply pilot](2026-11-12-graziani-supply-accounting-pilot.md) already flags extra truck loads and the abstract interaction; [six-turn inventory](../../research/2026-10-18-graziani-six-turn-surface-inventory.md) also explicitly records additional supplies on starting trucks. Thus source fact is already documented, while the more precise comparison with listed availability is useful.
- **§60.31, .32, .45, PDF p. 3:** Inventory accurately identifies duplicated XXI Corps Artillery, Z501 correction, and first-paragraph-only fleet correction. These entries do not specify CCNN HQ gun or Giarabub motor pool. This narrow observation does not establish absence elsewhere.
- **§4.44b, PDF p. 1:** Inventory accurately identifies the two Matruh battalions, French company arrival D, reconnaissance legend and German artillery ID. Re.2000 language is explicitly qualified in scan. §§4.44a, 4.44c and 4.46 descriptions match the inspected passages. These entries do not establish the disputed Italian HQ gun or pool.
