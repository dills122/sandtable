# VI-05: scenario-specific victory timing comparison

**Result:** Another scenario group explicitly defines an early automatic win, while the separate Race for Tobruk scenario explicitly scores its central objective at scenario end. These comparisons show that the original book uses distinct timing language for different scenarios; they do **not** decide whether or exactly when to evaluate Graziani's §60.81 predicates, nor do they authorize importing either outcome policy.
