# Saharan Detachment: independent source review

Reviewed 17 November 2026. Scope: cited original Axis charts, scenario setup and Land rule scans, plus September 1979 errata; review of the two companion Saharan documents, not a complete rules adjudication.

## Findings

1. **Optional setup augmentation needs explicit consideration.** Scenario §60.31, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, permits the Italian player to attach each of two `ww` Autoblinda 40 reconnaissance TOE points to any unit, one point per unit, ignoring normal composition restrictions; alternatively, deploy them in Tobruk as replacements. This option is not specifically allocated to the Saharan Detachment, but its broad wording leaves it available as a potential setup choice. The seven listed OA entries and their normal initial values are therefore the baseline, not proof that no unit can receive an optional additional point. Applicability of other attachment, location and stacking rules warrants separate adjudication. The companion documents do not mention this scenario-wide exception.
2. **Placement and truck allocation remain open.** Scenario §59.2 permits division of listed units when more than one hex is listed for a group; §60.31 gives this single listed formation a radius, not explicit individual hexes. The text reviewed does not unambiguously answer whether its assigned constituents may be initially spread within the radius. First-line trucks must be assigned to units in their listed hex (§59.2, §59.42); how that interacts with a distributed radius setup is not specified by these passages. Neither forced co-location nor free independent dispersal is established here.

## Confirmed against scans

- Scenario §60.31 places `Saharan Det (I)` within three hexes of C0716 and lists `Trucks: 5 light` on that row. The C0716 supply dump is separately listed in §60.34. §60.22 begins on Game-Turn 1, Operations Stage 1. The row does not assign a truck type other than light or choose individual recipients.
- Axis Italian OA §4.44b, PDF pp. 4 and 11, shows basic morale +1 and seven entries, all `D`: HQ `b` N; 1 and 2 Saharan/Libyan battalions `x` N; Sahara machinegun battalion `z` N; Sahara motor machinegun company `hh` N; Sahara artillery battery `mm` with 3 × 65/17 Gun and 2 × Light AA; and Meharisiti camel cavalry `yy` U@2. OA key defines `N` for non-tank/non-gun units as maximum TOE and `U@2` as two TOE points on deployment. Indentation denotes assignment, not automatic attachment regardless of position.
- Italian characteristics §4.46b, PDF pp. 26–27, matches the companion table's codes, CPA, close-assault values and maximum TOE. The battery's five listed weapon TOE points exceed its normal maximum four; the chart key allows excess due to arrival/deployment strength. The `+` on `hh` states historical supply with enough trucks to motorize its components and changes CPA when motorized; it supplies no truck count or type. It does not establish an additional setup truck pool.
- Land §4.25, PDF/printed p. 9, defaults assigned units starting in their parent's hex to attached unless specifically stated; attached units are not separately represented on the map. Scenario §59.2 permits organization of units listed to start in the same hex within organization and stacking limits. Neither passage alone settles distribution across the radius.
- Scenario §59.63(A), PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, counts medium and heavy first-line trucks as one attached abstract motorization point each, unless specifically modified by scenario instructions. These five light trucks contribute zero under that conversion; this does not rule out other sources. §59.45 allows eligible trucks to start transporting supplies or motorizing troops or AA without cost or penalty; it does not select recipients or loads.
