# PR #377 initial-contact research scope

**Finding:** Merge commit `35e32e8051141f1a67a87011cd483bb5021bb51b` implements a bounded **geometric separation** in its admitted fixture, conditional on its cited source distances and paths; it does not prove C3320, C3520 or C3020 are singleton combat stacks, decide Zones of Control, or settle first-combat duties. The [singleton source note](2026-11-17-graziani-commonwealth-singleton-proof.md) is compatible with that narrow geometry claim: each hex has one explicitly listed formation, while an exhaustive legal-occupant proof remains open.

## What the merged change and tests establish

- `CampaignSourceInitialContacts.TrySoluchMechiliToFtMaddalenaBound` uses published §37.42 distances Soluch A4130→C3019 = 61 and Mechili B4921→C3019 = 41, subtracting a C-sheet graph path length from C3019 to the other location; a remainder greater than one yields **not adjacent**. This is a triangle-inequality bound provided the chart distance and graph path are valid and refer to the same locations. An independently checked chart triangle is inconsistent: Soluch–Fort 61, Fort–Wadi Natrun 68, and Soluch–Wadi Natrun 130 imply 130 > 61 + 68. At least one value or shared interpretation is inconsistent, but the triangle does not identify the wrong cell or prove this separation false. Thus the Soluch bound is conditional pending a source policy or independent witness; see [distance triangle review](2026-11-17-graziani-pr377-distance-triangle-review.md). A path length is an upper bound on shortest local distance; a visually verified path, not independently proving its optimality, suffices for this exclusion. The Bardia/Soluch pair is not used because its candidate has a published-distance conflict. The method is gated by `useHistoricalMap`, raw source IDs and a conflict check. It can apply to additional reachable C hexes, not only the seven test rows.
- `Cna1979HistoricalMapTests.SoluchAndMechiliAreProvenNotAdjacentToEveryAdmittedForwardHex` exercises both directions for 14 pairs: two rear anchors and C3520, C3020, C3721, C3922, C3926, C4131 and C3320. Another test pins the stated Bardia conflict and absence of those flags for the selected anchor pairs. These exercise implementation results; they are not independent primary-source transcription of every underlying edge or a legal-stack test.
- `GrazianiRetainedFormationTests.CairoBrigadeUsesOneCounterAndRetainsOriginalWorld` changes C3617/B4921 from unavailable to resolved not-adjacent. Its title concerns a Cairo formation; it is not proof of singleton status at the three C contact hexes.
- `GrazianiSourceAuthorityTests.IntegratingEveryAdmittedClusterBeforeOwnerFinalizationRevealsRealRemainingBlockers` calls `Derive` for both sides on a particular integrated world and updates its expected blockers from five to four, removing `source-initial-contacts-unavailable`. This is a fixture-specific observed assertion, not a source finding that all setup choices, legal combat effects or other worlds have been resolved. Remaining fixture blockers include battlefield, control, CCNN characteristics and grouped movement; their presence is not itself a singleton diagnosis.
