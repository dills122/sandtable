# Victory trace: friendly units in enemy ZOC

**Date:** November 18, 2026. This source follow-up addresses one question in the [route review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-route-review.md): does an enemy ZOC on a hypothetical victory-convoy path require a friendly **combat** unit, or does any friendly unit suffice? The review already flags the two alternatives; this note adds the explicit truck-convoy entry text of Land §10.29. Neither source case expressly says which test §60.81's hypothetical victory trace uses. No policy or actual reachable witness is selected.

## Source distinctions

- Scenario §60.81's Italian strategic and decisive conditions describe being supplied **by convoy from** a source; Commonwealth tactical describes tracing supply **by truck convoy back to** Alexandria or Cairo, with later conditions referring to supply as above. These formulations do not expressly cite §10.29 or §32.16, establish a particular real dispatched convoy, or resolve the trace direction and quantifiers. [Direction follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-convoy-direction-next.md) retains those separate issues.
- Land §10.29 explicitly says Truck Convoys may not **enter** an enemy ZOC unless the hex is **already occupied by a Friendly combat unit**. Its following sentences separately restrict noncombat units entering an unoccupied enemy-ZOC hex and address capture in specified circumstances. Land §10.26 says a friendly combat unit in a hex negates enemy ZOC **for movement purposes**. These are source rules for movement; the wording “already occupied” and their scope must not be silently replaced with merely some friendly unit being present.
- Land §32.16 instead describes a friendly combat unit's local supply draw within half its CPA, measured as a medium truck would traverse, with a non-motorized infantry exception. It bars a line through impassable terrain or an enemy ZOC **unoccupied by Friendly units**. Its text does not add “combat” to that friendly-unit exception. Nor does the local-draw case expressly define §60.81's scenario victory trace.
- Land §§32.32–.33 and 32.58 govern a real Supply Unit carried by Motorization Points, including the Movement/Combat Phase escort condition and 15 CPA for those points. They do not prove that every §60.81 convoy witness is an actual Supply Unit movement or settle whether a moving escort satisfies §10.29's **already occupied** condition at entry. Identity, ordering and applicability require their own decision.

## Distinguishing conditional alternatives

Consider a synthetic enemy-ZOC path hex with a friendly Supply Unit that is established to be a friendly unit but **not** a friendly combat unit, no other friendly unit there, and all other route facts independently known. Applying §32.16's literal friendly-unit exception to a local-draw test would not block that hex solely for lack of a friendly unit. Applying §10.29's actual Truck Convoy entry rule would not satisfy its already-present friendly combat-unit condition. These are results of different cited rules, not proof that either governs a victory condition, that a Supply Unit supplies a trace witness, or that the full path succeeds. Confirm any counter's combat/noncombat classification separately.

Possible victory interpretations include applying actual truck-convoy entry restrictions; using an independently specified hypothetical route rule informed by §32.16; or another explicit source-backed interpretation. Also resolve endpoint/source treatment, whether an escort must be present **before** entry, directional passage and snapshot timing. A known friendly combat unit can satisfy the cited occupancy wording only if its status and timing are established; an unverified unit, ZOC, occupation, alternative route or map boundary cannot be normalized to a definite result. A no-match conclusion requires complete relevant source and path coverage. The current candidate's combat-unit-only neutralization is a project choice with a direct movement-rule analogue, not a textual correction to §32.16 or conclusive §60.81 semantics.

Suggested checks after a versioned decision: compare friendly combat unit, friendly noncombat Supply Unit, no friendly unit and unknown classification on the same enemy-ZOC hex; present before entry versus arriving with transport; entry versus starting in the hex; local draw versus actual convoy versus hypothetical victory trace; known alternative path; and both direction choices. Preserve private occupancy and ZOC in authoritative evidence and apply an independently selected disclosure policy. These are unexecuted synthetic checks, not reachability or gameplay claims.
