# Commonwealth rail ZOC: bounded planning proposal

**Status:** propose `gra-rail-zoc-v1`, not an admitted rule, official correction, or implementation. [Independent policy review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-zoc-policy-review.md) recommends deferring admission until the ordinary movement/contact tension and other gates below are reconciled. This selects one interpretation only for Commonwealth rail movement and its ZOC gate. Full rail legality, visibility and source-coverage contracts remain separate prerequisites; unsupported actions remain fail-closed.

## Source and rationale

[Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 15 §8.73 bars starting in or entering an enemy ZOC while using rail, and separately bars entry into an enemy-occupied hex. On p. 18 §10.26 negates the **effect** of enemy ZOC for **all movement purposes** when a friendly combat unit is present; it does not remove the ZOC or expressly require a different unit. The same page's §10.29 requires a friendly combat unit **already** in a hex for convoy entry, but does not expressly govern rail. The [source analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-rail-zoc.md) and [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-rail-zoc-review.md) preserve the competing readings, source hashes and related conditions.

Apply §10.26 to rail because its explicit all-movement wording is broad, while retaining §8.73's separate enemy-occupancy ban. At origin, allow a combat traveler to count itself: it is already physically present and §10.26 does not require another unit. This means the origin ZOC condition alone will not bar a qualifying combat traveler. At later entries, require an independently present friendly combat unit: the traveler cannot negate a prohibition by arriving before its arrival is legal. The distinction is a project choice, with §10.29 supporting already-present timing by analogy rather than establishing a rail rule.

## Candidate evaluation

1. Evaluate the selected traveler and route against one authoritative, versioned state and legal action boundary. At the origin, a known friendly combat unit present in that hex, including the traveler itself or another selected traveler already there, negates the enemy ZOC's movement effect for this origin check. A supply or other non-combat traveler does not qualify by itself. Classification must follow an independently admitted combat-unit contract.
2. At each subsequently entered hex, a friendly combat unit qualifies only if present before this rail movement action and not among its travelers. A traveler, including one proposed to be dropped off earlier in the same action, cannot bootstrap entry for itself or another traveler. This bounded snapshot choice avoids dependence on ordering simultaneous cargo. Pickup/dropoff actions and multi-batch sequencing need a separate contract; do not infer that this proposal authorizes them. An occupant which has actually completed a prior independently valid action can qualify in the later action's state, subject to all other stage rules.
3. A certified absent enemy ZOC passes this gate. A certified present enemy ZOC passes only with a qualifying friendly combat occupant under the applicable origin or entry rule. If enemy ZOC or qualifying occupancy is unknown, the result is **unknown**, except when a known qualifying occupant makes unknown ZOC irrelevant to this gate. An unqualified present ZOC is a known failure. Use §10.21 projection restrictions and certified boundary/incoming-ZOC coverage, not mere adjacency or absence of a loaded counter. Do not turn unknown into absence.
4. Independently apply enemy occupancy, rail continuity, construction/damage, §8.78's west-of-Axis-occupied-rail limit, timing, CP, stacking, directional/capacity and other restrictions. This proposal neither selects later-stage CP treatment nor waives §24.65's separate prohibition on building or rebuilding enemy-controlled or enemy-occupied rail hexes. Unknown prerequisites remain unknown; no admissible overall command follows from this ZOC gate alone.

Retain physical ZOC, its movement effect, and control separately. Do not alter other combat, retreat, truck convoy, supply draw, construction or victory rules by reusing this local policy. Action identity, stage history and snapshots must support deterministic replay and ruleset-version checks before execution.

## Alternatives and admission gates

A strict reading of rail-specific §8.73 bars rail in every enemy ZOC even with a friendly combat occupant. Another interpretation requires a **different** friendly combat unit at origin; it preserves more independent effect for the origin bar but adds a condition to §10.26's wording. A broader entry interpretation lets the traveling combat unit qualify on entry; it risks circular authorization. None is corrected by inspected errata. Preserve alternatives and source tension for review rather than claiming source certainty.

The independent review identifies a further tension in the selected self-origin reading: §§10.23–.24 and 8.62–.65 impose enemy-ZOC or contact movement restrictions and break-off costs on combat units already present. Applying self-presence generally could negate those effects. Restricting this proposal to rail prevents silent changes elsewhere but does not explain why §10.26's all-movement wording would be treated differently, or reconcile break-off with rail's no-other-CP condition. Resolve that relationship and review the distinct-protector alternative before admission; otherwise keep this gate fail-closed.

A complete contract must prove route endpoints, map and edge coverage, all incoming ZOC, occupant identity/classification, rail state, §8.78 direction and control, stage and transport legality. It must also specify pickup/dropoff, sequential action boundaries, later CP use and repair after new evidence. The selected rule cannot be admitted just because one synthetic path works. Define audience-specific action responses and event observations: §10.16 names Movement Segments and does not automatically license disclosures in the separately named rail phase. Equal authorized histories must not reveal hidden ZOC or units through option lists, errors, timing, event ordering or unknown-versus-blocked responses. An internal three-valued result does not itself authorize exposing which state occurred. If a privacy-safe and playable outcome cannot yet be specified, hold the rail action unavailable at the relevant public boundary.

## Distinguishing vectors

Unexecuted synthetic tests, assuming all other inputs certified and legal:

- At origin in enemy ZOC, lone combat traveler passes this gate; lone non-combat traveler fails; a non-combat traveler with another friendly combat traveler present passes. A different-unit origin policy gives a different answer to the first case.
- On intermediate or destination entry in enemy ZOC, a stationary friendly combat unit passes; no unit or only a non-combat unit fails. An in-transit combat traveler, including one proposed for earlier dropoff in the same action, cannot make the entry pass; a separately completed prior action may change the next action's snapshot if otherwise legal.
- Enemy-occupied entry remains barred even with a friendly combat occupant. A friendly occupant does not clear enemy control for §24.65. Check §8.78, destroyed track, stage CP and capacity independently.
- Unknown projection without an established qualifying occupant gives unknown; a known qualifying occupant makes unknown projection irrelevant to this ZOC gate, but not enemy occupancy or other gates. Certified no ZOC passes this gate even with unknown occupancy; a known unqualified enemy ZOC fails only when absence of qualifying occupants is covered and certified. Test incomplete occupant identity/classification, incoming ZOC beyond loaded map bounds and §10.21 terrain-limited projection. Compare the same lone-combat-origin case against ordinary movement/contact and break-off expectations before selecting a consistent policy.
- Compare hidden-state-paired histories through legal-action presentation, responses, timing, emitted events and replay. Test ruleset mismatch and stage rollover. These cases do not prove historical reachability, acceptance or passing tests.
