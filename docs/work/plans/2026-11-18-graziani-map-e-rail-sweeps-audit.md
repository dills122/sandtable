## Baseline and earlier reviews

The [reviewed pin inventory](2026-11-18-graziani-pinned-map-e-rail-inventory-review.md) reports 143 incident rail records and 142 distinct E endpoint IDs, including one open side. All 35 sweep pairs are reported as literal pinned `railroad` records; their 38 IDs are within that baseline. Only `E1229` overlaps the earlier **41 endpoint candidates** (36 in the [crosswalk](2026-11-18-graziani-rail-eastern-witness-crosswalk.md), plus five in the [fork followup](2026-11-18-graziani-rail-eastern-candidates-next.md)). Thus sweeps name 37 additional baseline endpoint IDs; the union has 78 IDs. This is endpoint membership, not 78 certified rail hexes or a side-review total. `E1229`'s sweep pair is unknown even though the earlier crosswalk includes that endpoint from other readings.

The reviewed [Suez/Bilbeis batch](2026-11-18-graziani-suez-rail-source-batch-review.md) has eight distinct pairs and nine endpoints; the reviewed [delta/Tanta batch](2026-11-18-graziani-delta-rail-source-batch-review.md) has seven pairs and eight endpoints. Neither repeats a sweep pair; C and delta share `E3027`, while Suez shares no sweep endpoint. Suez and delta share no pairs or endpoints. Together with A/B/C these batches name **50 unique pairs and 54 unique endpoints**. Only `E1229` overlaps the earlier 41-ID set, so the combined endpoint union with that set is **94 of the 142 pinned E IDs**, leaving 48 baseline IDs outside these named sets. This arithmetic does not establish that those 94 IDs, or any particular connecting sides, are fully surveyed.

Of the 50 pairs, 49 are reported with pinned `railroad`. The delta review identifies the remaining source-positive pair, `E2927–E3027`, as a source-versus-pin discrepancy: its pin contains `unfinished-road` and `track`, not `railroad`. Its endpoints still occur in the pinned rail endpoint inventory via other records. This audit does not propose a data correction. Vertex qualifications in Suez and the unresolved junction in delta remain as documented; no inference about adjoining sides follows.

## Limits

These are selected local readings, not a survey of omitted or unpinned sides. Neither absent pin nor unresolved crossing proves no rail; zero pinned unfinished-railroad records does not establish construction state. No continuous usable network, sheet seam, off-map continuation, service, destruction state, occupancy, or unblocked §8.78 outcome follows. Negative proof still needs justified geographic bounds and sheet registration, coverage of all potentially qualifying rail, construction and destroyed-rail policy, and complete fixed-snapshot Axis combat occupancy. A qualifying occupied eastern rail witness may establish a block despite other unknowns; see the [crosswalk's negative-proof requirements](2026-11-18-graziani-rail-eastern-witness-crosswalk.md).

Documentary set reconciliation only; no fresh scan, runtime or map-data changes, or gameplay tests.
