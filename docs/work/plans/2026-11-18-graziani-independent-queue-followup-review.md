# Abstract air supply arrival timing: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Independently checked the [queue follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-independent-queue-followup.md) against the same original [Land rules scan](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 11–12 and 43–44, SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`. No independent edition or exhaustive errata search. **Disposition:** the conditional Phase C-before-D result is supported; no material source correction needed.

- **Shared stage timing and actor:** §5.2 places Organization Phase C before Naval Convoy Arrival Phase D, before player A's F–L followed by player B's F–L. Section 32.37 permits **each player** to air-transport one Supply Unit per Game-Turn in an Operations Stage's Organization Phase; it does not assign that action to the later A/B movement phases. The outline lets Organization segments be undertaken in any desired order but does not establish a separate actor ordering or every sub-action ordering within Phase C. Do not infer initiative-dependent access or simultaneous actions from the follow-up's C-before-D conclusion.
- **Arrival identity:** §32.42 sends Axis Supply Units by convoy; §32.63 places convoy attacks in the OpStage Naval Convoy Phase. For the follow-up's explicitly assumed actual Phase D arrival, that unit and fuel first arriving with it cannot be available in the already completed Phase C. This does not prove a particular convoy, arrival phase mapping, delivery, or lack of earlier stock. The 12 Fuel required **before** transport may come from any Supply Unit in the origin hex under §32.37; it need not come from the transported unit. Whether multiple sources can combine payment is not settled here.
- **Later stage and movement mode:** A later Organization Phase makes a previously arrived unit a candidate only if all applicable gates and the one-per-player-per-Game-Turn limit permit. A new stage in the *same* Game-Turn does not reset that allowance; a stage in a later Game-Turn is a different case. Section 32.31 expressly permits a Supply Unit to move by **land or rail** during its arrival OpStage. That does not authorize air movement earlier in the same stage, and §32.37 bars the owner from moving an air-transported Supply Unit or its supplies at all in its transport OpStage while allowing §32.16 draw.
- **Startup and other side:** §32.43 excepts the starting Game-Turn of a scenario from its ordinary statement about Supply Unit arrival in all Game-Turns; it does not alone settle the scenario-start preamble or an actual GT1 shipment. Section 32.42 describes Commonwealth placement but does not independently establish the same Phase D timing for that placement. The candidate properly leaves both cases conditional.

The follow-up's origin/destination, friendly HQ, enemy-distance, directional and fuel gates accurately summarize §32.37 without proving that any are satisfied. A record of the source unit and origin fuel at the actual Phase C boundary remains necessary; later arrival cannot fill that earlier snapshot. No runtime, data or gameplay tests changed.
