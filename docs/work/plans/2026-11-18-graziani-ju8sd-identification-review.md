# Review: errata `Ju8SD` identification

**Disposition:** The [source note](2026-11-18-graziani-ju8sd-identification.md) supports Ju88D as a strong contextual candidate, not an explicit official correction. The separate FW220 versus Fw. 200 C discrepancy remains unresolved. No material transcription or mode error found.

- [Air §§43.11–.13](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 14, name **Ju88D** in §43.11's 75% Mediterranean-base list alongside He 111 and FW220. Section 43.12 gives the Italy/Sicily location until `1/35`; §43.13 uses broader `Ju88's` in the later Crete distribution. These are detailed-Air basing rules, not evidence of aircraft or missions in Graziani Land-only state.
- [September errata §43.12](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 3, visually prints the middle designation **`Ju8SD's`**, alongside He111's and FW 220's. It identifies the bombers referred to but does not explicitly say the middle glyph is an error or replace it with Ju88D. Main §43.11 supplies a close named contextual match; interpreting the erratum as Ju88D is a source-supported inference, not a printed correction.
- [Axis chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 2 lists **Ju. 88D**, Bomb 33, under the German bomber/flying-boat/transport/reconnaissance group; p. 1 lists **Ju. 88c** under fighters/fighter-bombers, and p. 2 also lists **Ju. 87B** and **Ju. 87D**. This confirms the cited matching row and distinct nearby designations, not an exhaustive official synonym list. The chart's **Fw. 200 C** differs from §43.11/errata **FW220**; no equivalence follows merely from this comparison.
- The inspected [counter PDF](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf) p. 2 Section 3 German aircraft area uses generic aircraft faces; it gives no readable `Ju8SD` or `Ju88D` identification in that area. This is not a full counter inventory or proof no other printed type label exists.
