# Aresca HQ tank participation alternatives: independent review

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition:** Supported as a bounded comparison, not an authoritative ruling. No inspected passage explicitly resolves whether §3.35 includes this super-brigade HQ or whether §9.28 changes its category for that restriction. The nominal, current-size, and literal-class readings remain interpretations; the third is particularly constrained by §9.22 and cannot rest on the regiment name alone.

## Source findings

- [Land](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed p. 6 §3.35 restricts combat use of tanks **assigned to** armored division and armored brigade HQs unless the HQ has no combat units attached. It does not prohibit the attached combat units themselves from fighting. Section 3.31's combined strength does not expressly override this specific restriction. The trigger is any attached combat unit, not the number that qualifies toward full status under §9.27; an attached combat-unit shell can therefore trigger §3.35 if the HQ class is covered, even though that shell does not count toward full-parent status.
- Land p. 17 §§9.22 and 9.28 and [common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) p. 2 §9.4 support distinguishing printed organizational identity from current shell equivalent. Section 9.22 directs players to printed stacking value and §9.4 for organization. Section 9.28 treats a brigade shell as battalion-equivalent for close-assault differentiation and rules where unit size matters; it does not expressly answer whether §3.35's HQ category is such a rule. The chart's one-point any-brigade shell entry does not alone establish tank eligibility. Counter identity `(3)` is taken from the linked prior visual review, not independently checked here.
- [Axis chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) p. 31 shows five tank-battalion slots for the `(3)` tank regiment. OA p. 6 lists Aresca HQ, one CV33 and four battalions; it marks three attached elsewhere. Subject to the normal-chart match and any applicable singular-structure exception, one qualifying attachment out of five is a shell and four qualifying attachments are the arithmetic full threshold. The threshold does not prove availability or legality of four attachments. [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) p. 3 §60.31 lists all except XXI detached at C3919. OA lists XXI at nine CV33. Listed baseline and later legal player-selected organization must remain distinct.
- The candidate correctly treats 18 as conditional raw defensive points for nine eligible XXI CV33 at two each, with the HQ's potential two assessed separately. It does not establish a complete hex total, actual combat result, or ZOC. Weapon rating, §3.35 combat participation, raw ZOC counting, §10.11 stacking/size prerequisites, cohesion, and terrain are distinct questions. Empty HQ exception does not grant unrestricted attack: §3.33 and §3.4 still apply.

## Implementation checks after a ruling

A versioned ruling should specify nominal classification, whether shell equivalence changes §3.35 applicability, and whether that combat restriction affects raw ZOC counting. Focused deterministic tests should independently cover:

1. Listed baseline with XXI attached: classify shell and calculate XXI separately from HQ tank; assert HQ contribution only as the selected ruling permits.
2. Covered HQ with an attached combat-unit shell: §3.35 attachment trigger remains true even if §9.27 excludes that unit from full-status counting. Compare no combat units attached, including zero stacking, no voluntary attack, and applicable parenthesized-rating conditions.
3. Five-slot synthetic parent at three versus four qualifying attached battalions: shell/full boundary, plus a shell subunit excluded from qualifying count. Check legal attachment and assignment separately; do not assume four available at scenario setup.
4. Losses, detachment and reattachment or other legal transitions: recompute strength and status without changing nominal identity. Test full parent and empty HQ under the chosen interpretation, and raw combat versus ZOC eligibility as separate assertions. Preserve deterministic replay under the versioned ruling.
