# Royal Tank Regiment arrival: later scenario records

**Result:** A later original scenario assigns units with the same printed 2 RTR and 7 RTR names to particular brigades. It supplies a dated comparison, but no arrival-stage parent, mandatory reassignment or continuous identity for Graziani. The existing 2 RTR and 7 RTR arrival questions remain open.

The [organization follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-organization-batch-followup.md) and [closure review](2026-11-17-graziani-batch-closure-check-review.md) document Commonwealth GT2 OpStage 3 2 RTR and 7 RTR arrivals. Commonwealth OA PDF p. 11 puts both under **Unassigned Armor-Class Units**, both with `3/2` arrival markers; 2 RTR's note `b` says it was assigned to **4/7 Armored Brigade upon arrival**, whereas no such note is attached to 7 RTR. Prior comparison leaves the effect of the 2 RTR historical note, assignment versus attachment, entry and reorganization timing unresolved. The already-checked Land §19.26 example is not a dated 7 RTR arrival instruction.

A targeted scan check found a later independent **scenario setup record**, not an independent edition: [Operation Crusader §62.31](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 7 / printed p. 31, starts units with matching names in these positions:

- Within one hex of C4807, **32 Army Tank Bde**, `Assg:` 1 RTR, 4 RTR and **7 RTR**.
- At **C4111**, **7th Armored Bde**, `Assg:` 7th Hussars, 6 RTR and **2nd RTR**.

Section 62.2 starts that scenario at GT57 OpStage 3, long after Graziani's GT2 arrival. Section 62.32 separately lists 7 RTR's tank strength with parenthetical `32nd Army, Bde 12`; it does not supply an instruction for GT2. That printed parenthetical and the §62.31 `32 Army Tank Bde (2)` notation have not been reconciled here.

The later setup supports preserving scenario-specific assignments as explicit records: it neither proves that similarly named counters are continuous survivors of a GT2 playthrough nor says when or how any reassignment occurred. In particular it cannot establish 7 RTR's GT2 parent, or choose whether 2 RTR's OA note creates an automatic assignment, an optional historical shift, or a conflict with normal sequencing. Copying the later brigade to the earlier arrival or treating their difference as proof of a particular transition would manufacture a rule. A discriminating source would be an applicable GT2-specific scenario instruction, authenticated correction, or explicit assignment timing/exception; until then preserve separately printed schedule, OA, scenario setup and relevant rules for review.
