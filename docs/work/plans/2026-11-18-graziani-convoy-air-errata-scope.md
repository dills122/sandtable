# Axis convoy Air errata: Land-only scope

**Result:** Air §41.67's upward rounding is a concrete alternative to Land §32.65(A)'s downward rounding of Motorization Points at 10%, but neither the shared table nor the September clarification establishes importing the Air procedure into Land-only play. Air §41.65G's landed-shipment clarification identifies completion of its detailed air-attack procedure; it does not supply an actual Graziani manifest, arrival stage, destination, or port-capacity decision. Neither erratum resolves the Land 5% gap or selects an arrival policy.

## Printed source and distinguishing example

- [Air rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 12, §§41.64–.67: a convoy must be located by strategic reconnaissance before an attack; §41.65 orders reconnaissance, air assignments, air combat, flak and bombing. At G bombing is resolved and Axis Naval Convoy Missions completed. Section 41.66 reads the result from the common table as cargo percentage lost; §41.67 divides losses by cargo type, rounding fractions **up**, grouping truck replacements together and tanks and guns separately by nationality. Section 41.68 separately says convoy bombing never affects listed reinforcement arrival.
- [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 3 left column, §41.65G says all shipments are considered landed when Axis convoys are completed; §41.67 clarifies the percentage applies to each listed cargo type. The latter does not alter the host case's upward rounding or explicitly address abstract Supply Units and Motorization Points.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 44, §§32.61–.65: abstract cargo comprises Supply Units, Motorization Points and Replacement Points; §32.61 refers lane assignment to §56.0 and says no tonnage limitation. The Commonwealth may attack each arriving convoy separately; §32.64 uses §32.66 and the Air Bombardment table, then §32.65 modifies the result. For 10%, (A) applies only to motorization and replacements, rounding the former down and latter up. At 20% or greater, (B) applies to all items, rounding down with a separately worded minimum. This is an express table reference, not an express reference to §41.65 or §41.67.

For a **synthetic**, otherwise eligible convoy with one abstract Motorization Point and a 10% table result, Land §32.65(A)'s stated rounding gives **zero** motorization loss. If, purely to compare procedures, one were to map that point to one detailed truck replacement point and apply Air §41.67, 0.1 rounds **up to one** truck replacement point lost. The hypothetical mapping is not established by these cases. Land §32.51 treats Motorization Points as medium trucks except as modified there, and §32.52 separately ties replacement arrival rate to medium-truck replacement ability; neither makes detailed Air cargo processing automatically applicable. Land's 10% clause also excludes Supply Units, whereas Air's clarification applies percentage to each listed detailed cargo type; abstract Supply Units and detailed supplies are not shown to be identical categories. This comparison distinguishes source procedures, not actual eligible cargo, attack, result, or six-turn reachability.

The [rounding source follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-rounding-source-followup.md) already identifies §41.67 as a possible Air-abstract fallback because Air §47.64 expressly refers to Axis Naval Convoy Bombing. That reference and §47.65's own modifications do not by themselves make §41.67 a Land-only fallback. Land §32.65 names 10% and 20% or greater, leaving a printed 5% result's rounding unexpressed; Air §41.66 describes results in tens of percent, although the common table includes 5%. Corrected §41.67 does not bridge that gap or establish the result's reachability.

## Arrival boundary

Errata §41.65G connects “landed” to completion of the detailed Air convoy sequence, after bombing in G. Land §32.63 calls its targets **arriving** convoys and locates attacks in the Naval Convoy Phase; it does not expressly repeat the detailed mission sequence or the erratum's landing sentence. Their different formulations do not alone establish whether Land-only delivery occurs before or after abstract losses, waive lane/port restrictions, equate landing with usable supply, or determine results when no attack is chosen. Section 32.61's lane-assignment reference to §56.0 does not expressly incorporate §41.65G. Actual stage, prior plans, cargo, destination and any mode-specific capacity remain separate from this source comparison. The [Axis motor arrival review](2026-11-18-graziani-axis-motor-arrival-sixturn-review.md) retains these gates and does not prove delivery from chart availability.
