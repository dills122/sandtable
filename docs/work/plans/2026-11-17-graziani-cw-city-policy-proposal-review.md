# Commonwealth victory city-source policy: independent review

**Disposition:** Retain [proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-city-policy-proposal.md) as a bounded planning choice, not an accepted rule or runtime contract. Its per-objective, independent city and hex, and friendly-control choices are explicit interpretations. Two interfaces need precise binding before admission: multi-hex objective selection and compatibility with a route policy using shared resources. No runtime or tests changed.

## Findings

1. **Bind subject identity and possession to route evidence.** The proposal selects one subject per listed location, but delegates resolution to a location and possession contract. For a location with several qualifying hexes, that contract must say whether it selects one fixed hex or permits an existential choice; it must also specify whether the route starts at the same hex that satisfies possession. A deterministic choice of one qualifying hex could fail where another has a valid route. Conversely, combining possession at one hex with an unrelated route from another could produce a false match. Preserve take/hold history and multi-hex semantics as separate dependencies; add a synthetic two-hex case with only one possessed hex routed, swap the routed hex, and compare the accepted binding with a separate-witness alternative. No particular binding follows from this source review.
2. **Define compatibility with joint resource constraints.** The proposal gives each subject an independent existential city/hex witness and says witnesses do not consume shared capacity, while leaving shared-resource constraints to route policy. Independent individual paths do not prove a jointly feasible set of shipments when two paths compete for a single truck or capacity. On admission either restrict compatible route policies to non-consuming connectivity, or specify a separately versioned row-level joint feasibility predicate, its interaction with subject results, and its three-valued proof requirements. Test two individually feasible traces competing for one resource, a feasible joint allocation, and unknown capacity. Do not call the conjunction of independent witnesses a complete actual-shipment verdict.
3. **Source choice and control remain independent decisions.** Mixed-city and same-city/different-hex vectors distinguish per-subject city choice from common-city and common-hex alternatives only with every other predicate fixed. Requiring friendly control and allowing any certified member hex are additional interpretations; initial stock, arrivals, the location summary, and chart representatives do not certify present control, membership, or a route. Exercise friendly, hostile and unknown control independently from endpoint obstruction and inventory. Do not import the Italian strategic “any hex” parenthesis or RUL-GRA-22's holder binding into Commonwealth wording.
4. **Certify witness and exhaustion scope.** A positive route witness requires accepted directed edge, terrain, weather, endpoint and blocker semantics, including all relevant incoming interference, on the same snapshot. An irrelevant unknown candidate does not defeat a complete witness. A negative needs complete membership, eligibility and potential continuation coverage, including outside-map/seam possibilities; exhausting loaded or certified candidates alone is insufficient. Test an apparently clear route with unknown incoming ZOC, a fully certified alternate, and no witness with an unknown boundary. Known-false row predicates dominate unrelated unknowns under RUL-GRA-07, without turning the masked trace into false.
5. **Disclosure includes terminal behavior.** Authoritative evaluation may use hidden control, units and routes; player and intelligence-plane observations require separately authorized projection. Compare paired histories with the same authorized observations and different hidden blockers, including offers, reasons, IDs, event counts and timing, retries, verdict availability, freeze, subsequent actions and downstream effects. If authorized disclosure cannot accommodate differences, externally observable evaluation and terminal behavior must not depend on those hidden inputs. Redacting the witness alone is insufficient.

## Evidence and limits

Compared the proposal with the [Commonwealth source follow-up and review](2026-11-17-graziani-commonwealth-victory-trace-followup-review.md), [route candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-route-followup.md), [route review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-route-review.md), [matrix](2026-11-12-graziani-victory-matrix.md), and [RUL-GRA-02, 07 and 22](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md). RUL-GRA-02 selects §60.81 as a working cross-reference interpretation; RUL-GRA-07 selects tri-state result composition for planning; neither accepts this source or route selection. The matrix lists tactical Sollum, Halfaya Pass and Siwa; decisive Bardia, Fort Maddalena, Sidi Omar, Sollum and Siwa; strategic Tobruk. Carrying lower-row locations into later rows is a distinct policy. The reported wording does not settle the tracing actor, per-objective quantification, common city or source hex.

This review relies on those same-edition source readings; it did not independently inspect original scans or search further errata. Synthetic cases are proposed discrimination tests, not executed tests or proof of reachable play, official interpretation, or release readiness.
