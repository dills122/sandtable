## What remains uncertain

- No feature record means no feature recorded, not verified clear. Even a visible track or exposed thin grid leaves concealed features and rules effects open. In the radius western ledger every side retains fine-mark uncertainty; contact C3419–C3320 remains obscured by lettering, and blue checkered marks need rules treatment. No reviewed-side total is a count of certified empty sides.
- Interior artwork and side facts differ. C0718 oasis and C0818 marsh, other radius terrain assignments, and halo C0919 oasis need clearer evidence and applicable assignment rules. Halo interiors do not close exterior edges or ZOC.
- At Matruh, D3615–D3516 and D3516–D3517 are missing-rail candidates; D3615–D3616 rail and D3516–D3616 track remain disputed or obscured. The two positive seed crossings do not establish route service, control or closure.
- Scenario-printed B4827 and map-identified A4827 remain distinct; local readings do not authorize an alias. Seams, coastal/physical boundaries, distance-chart conflicts and source rights remain separate from the counted pairs.

## Next highest-leverage source work

1. Obtain a clearer or independent map source and assignment rules for radius oasis/marsh discrepancies, checkered marks and obscured C3419–C3320; resolve each as supported, conflict or unknown. Review possible incoming forces and directional ZOC outside the contact envelope.
2. Trace each actually required Matruh road/rail route from rule-defined endpoints through every side and branch, starting at the eastern junction and its disputed rail pairs. Check control, availability, occupancy and ZOC separately. Local marks alone cannot close a route.
3. Inventory selectable, geographic and anywhere placements, arrivals, objectives, facilities and supply origins, then trace rule-reachable exits and relevant seams. Prioritize a seam only when a required placement or route crosses it; verify registration, both sides and identity. Preserve unresolved distances and coordinate conflicts.
4. For every proposed bounded area, document six-side status, terrain and directional effects, physical boundaries, entries/exits and potential external forces. Expand scope or retain a blocker wherever movement, incoming ZOC, placement or required route reaches beyond it. Record provenance, disagreement and reviewed absence explicitly before any versioned admission.

A **bounded** claim can be evaluated against enumerated consumers and rules, but requires evidence that all relevant exterior interactions and routes are accounted for; a geometric halo is not a wall. A **full-map** claim additionally needs source-backed coverage of all relevant sheets, every applicable side and cell, overlaps, seams, boundaries and corrections. The 7,073/20,365 data denominators are useful for scale, not certified full-map denominators or a percentage complete. Neither path alone settles non-map scenario readiness.
