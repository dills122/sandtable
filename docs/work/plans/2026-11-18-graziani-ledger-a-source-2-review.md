## Rule check

[Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF p. 35 supports the note's central distinctions:

- §22.65 gives the specialized squadron a **movement rate** of 20 CP for towing broken-down or destroyed tanks back to a facility, up to three tank TOE in addition to reserves. Its “turn in which they tow” bars the squadron from attempting field repair; it does not expressly settle whether “turn” means Game-Turn. Specify *movement rate* when repeating 20 CP, so it is not read as a flat towing debit. The source prints `DTS`, unlike nearby `TDS`.
- §22.66 bars a squadron that exceeded CPA in a given Operations Stage from attempting repairs in the **ensuing Operations Stage**, while still permitting towing; §22.63 separately bars voluntarily exceeding CPA. Keep this squadron restriction distinct from the ordinary subject's §22.13(c) Repair-Phase towing restriction and §22.32's Operations-Stage facility timing.
- §22.62 permits the stated field-repair die modification for co-located broken-down tanks and repair on a die 1 for destroyed Commonwealth tanks; §22.41 identifies §§22.6–.7 as exceptions to the ordinary facility-only rule for destroyed tanks. §22.7 treats the German Mobile Tank Repair Squad the same except it repairs only German tanks. This does not establish same-phase towing and repair permission.
