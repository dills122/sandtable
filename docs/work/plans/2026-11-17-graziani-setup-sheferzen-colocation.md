# Sheferzen initial co-location: geographic and setup gates

**Disposition:** the obvious §60.31 **anywhere in Libya** pool does not establish a legal additional unit at C3419: the source map places that hex immediately on the east side of the blue boundary lead, and the map's regional labels support identifying that side as Egypt. Boundary identity and cell classification remain source-based interpretations needing review. A separate §60.31 option offers an armored-car TOE point to any unit, subject to one per unit, but its interaction with the garrison's maximum TOE is unresolved. No actual player choice, legal augmented garrison, second unit or full initial stack is established.

## Original source evidence

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §60.31 fixes Sheferzen Garrison at C3419. Its separate **Anywhere in Libya** row lists 1/1AR, 2/1AR, 3/1AR, 4/1AR and corps-artillery entries. The row has a repeated 4/1AR and XXI entry with punctuation/identity problems; do not double count. The other geographic rows shown include anywhere on Map A or B and within three hexes of C0716, not an unrestricted Map C pool. This bounded comparison does not prove no other rule, correction, unit category or valid setup action can add a unit.
- The [original map](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf) PDF p. 3 depicts C3419 on the **east** of an alternating blue checkered line following its western side; the neighboring fixed 2nd Rifle Brigade's C3320 also lies east. A wider p. 3 view shows the same line between large `LIBYA` and `EGYPT` labels farther south, Libya west and Egypt east. The [boundary source research](2026-11-17-graziani-border-mark-next.md) and [review](2026-11-17-graziani-border-mark-review.md) qualify the map-key mismatch: the line's national-boundary identity is a strong geographic inference, not an explicit matching key symbol or a certified complete national-region dataset. A city hex touching a border side is not automatically on both sides under a definition supplied here. If this boundary reading and east-side membership are admitted, the Libya-only list cannot be placed at C3419 merely by choosing that hex. A rejected or unknown region classification must not be treated as a verified Libya hex.
- Scenario §60.31 also grants two TOE points of Autoblinda 40 reconnaissance and permits attaching each to any unit, but not both to the same unit, ignoring regular unit-composition restrictions; alternatively deploy them as regular replacement points at Tobruk. This is a **point assignment choice**, not a free extra counter or automatic co-location. The broad “any unit” language suggests the garrison as a candidate, with at most one point, but does not establish that assignment as legal. The `dd` garrison already has its one-TOE maximum: Axis chart §4.46b pp. 26–27 limits exceeding the maximum to arrival/deployment strength, while Land p. 30 §§19.6–.61 limit augmentation beyond the ID-code maximum. The scenario's exemption from regular composition restrictions could override that limit, count as a deployment exception, or leave the cap intact; it does not explicitly name maximum TOE. The [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-setup-sheferzen-colocation-review.md) retains these alternatives. Whether the two points can be split between assignment and Tobruk replacement, and their actual assignment, representation, stacking and supply consequences remain open. Axis chart p. 27 §4.46b note 3 identifies `ww` as Autoblinda 40; its row gives close assault 3/2. **If** one such point can legally be included and contributes that defensive rating without modification, raw defense would be `2 + 2 = 4`, below §10.15's ten-raw threshold. This is conditional arithmetic, not proof of a legal augmented unit, actual stack strength or ZOC.
- Scenario §59.2, PDF pp. 1–2 / printed pp. 25–26, says listed parent/subordinate details come from OA except scenario changes and permits same-hex units to be organized at scenario start within formation and stacking limits. The same-hex disposition clause permits attachment/detachment choices among units **already co-located**; it does not by itself relocate a fixed starting unit to C3419 or override a geographic placement restriction. Listed units exceeding organizational limits may operate, subject to the specific restrictions printed there. Any later movement is subject to phase, movement and combat rules and cannot be used to rewrite the initial placement snapshot.

## Consequences and open work

The fixed C3419 garrison plus C3320 rifle unit are source placements, not proof of final isolated stacks. Do not assert the garrison necessarily remains alone: resolve all scenario-applicable flexible placement categories, corrections, regional and city membership, possible initial composition, point assignment and legal same-hex reorganization, including stacking before concluding the positive or negative case. The nearby [unit/ZOC baseline](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sheferzen-unit-zoc.md) gives the garrison's unaugmented `1 × 2 = 2` raw defense, below ten, and the rifle's `6 × 2 = 12`, but §10.11's size/stacking threshold, map side, entry and later state are independent. C3419 being on the Egyptian side does not itself establish an impassable border, ZOC, control or a compulsory attack.

Synthetic future tests: on a certified regional model compare a Libya-only candidate in a known Libya cell, the inferred Egypt C3419 cell and an unknown-border cell; no unknown-as-Libya fallback. Separately compare the scenario-override and maximum-cap interpretations, one vs two extra points assigned to a single unit, zero-point and Tobruk replacement choices, unresolved mixed disposition, conditional raw-defense arithmetic, same-hex organization without relocation, stacking and duplicate identities, then initial vs post-movement ZOC and replay. These are unexecuted vectors, not assertions that all candidate setups are otherwise legal.
