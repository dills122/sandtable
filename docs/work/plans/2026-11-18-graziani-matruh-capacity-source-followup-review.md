# Matruh attachment chart: type-definition review

**Disposition:** Supports the [source follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-matruh-capacity-source-followup.md)'s narrow chart reading and unresolved questions. The chart's defined `Tank` is narrower on its face than a generic reference to every tank point, but the modifier requires companies to comply with type restrictions and equates three company-equivalents with one battalion-equivalent. These texts do not independently establish that any particular tank company can be attached to Matruh. This is an informed recheck of the same scans, not an independent edition or a new ruling.

- **Chart wording:** §19.5's Matruh GT1–12 row gives six units, “no tank, 1 gun class.” Its key defines a unit as a full-strength battalion- or company-equivalent counter and defines `Tank` specifically as battalion-equivalent units composed of tank TOE Strength Points. Its modification offers division- and brigade-equivalent units two non-shell company-equivalents at no cost to maximum attachments, states three company-equivalents equal one battalion-equivalent, and requires compliance with type restrictions. The conversion is printed equivalence; the chart does not explicitly specify how aggregating mixed companies affects the `Tank` type definition. Non-shell, full-strength, company-equivalent and zero-stacking are not interchangeable claims.
- **Shells and qualification:** §9.26 makes an ordinary battalion a shell below 50% of maximum TOE Strength Points attached, with a separate below-25% artillery threshold. §9.28 reduces a shell one level for unit differentiation **on Close Assault** and for any rule where size matters, including a battalion shell as company-equivalent. Whether that reduced shell meets the chart modifier's separately stated **non-shell** condition remains a rule interaction, not automatic free-company eligibility. §9.25 governs certain zero-stacking companies/batteries and contains its own parent-attachment and major-city exceptions; it does not itself grant attachment capacity.
- **Parent and exceptional assignment:** Land §19.5 applies its chart to units of the indicated size and type whether or not their organization is standard. It does not by itself settle Matruh's current brigade-equivalent status. The Matruh OA calls it a brigade-level HQ, says no units may be assigned and allows up to six attached. September errata §4.44b item 2 assigns Buffs and Hampshires to Matruh; §19.5's separate erratum says chart attachments are additional to normally assigned units even if those units are elsewhere. The six-total versus six-additional reconciliation remains an explicit source-precedence question. Assignment alone does not prove physical co-location or current attachment.

Check actual parent size, shell status, strength, unit type, nesting and applicable scenario setup before applying any interpretation. These source distinctions do not establish a present tank company, a specific legal attachment, or a numeric roster.
