# Abstract-air origin proximity: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Independently checked the [origin classification follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-supply-source-followup.md) against the same original [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 5–6, 26 and 43–45, SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`. **Disposition:** the proximity, classification and mode distinctions are supported; no material source error found.

- **Origin test:** §32.37 prohibits air transport **from** a hex if any enemy combat unit is within five hexes of that origin. It does not require an attack or ZOC. Given an accepted distance convention, a qualifying enemy at distance five meets the stated boundary; one at six does not meet this particular gate. This does not decide same-hex counting, cross-sheet registration or any other transport condition.
- **AA and patrol context:** §3.23 includes anti-aircraft-type units among combat units. Section 16.32 explicitly treats AA/Flak and engineers as noncombat **for the patrol-survival check**; it does not expressly redefine them for §32.37. The text supports the follow-up's refusal to carry that exception over automatically. Classification and existence of the particular unit remain separate questions.
- **Land-only removals:** §§32.81–.83 prescribe different Commonwealth, German and Italian removals and restrictions; §32.84 states that the anti-air modifications apply to anti-air TOE Strength Points and Replacement Points, not non-AA points merely possessing an anti-air rating. The latter does not by itself preserve an AA-type counter removed by a more specific clause, or settle its later arrival. Check the actual side, identity and stage state instead of treating the scenario list or rating alone as presence.
- **HQ, engineers, dummies and supply:** §3.1 recognizes context-sensitive combat status for HQs, engineers and dummy tank formations. Sections 3.31/.33 distinguish an HQ with attached combat units from one with none. Section 32.13 says a Supply Unit has no combat capabilities. These observations do not establish an automatic classification for every exceptional counter or prove a specific nearby witness; patrol treatment is not an air-transport ruling.

The follow-up accurately labels its 5/6, HQ and AA examples as conditional, not known Matruh state or an authorized information disclosure. Hidden proximity may need adjudication without exposing a unit's identity; these source cases do not supply a complete digital disclosure policy.
