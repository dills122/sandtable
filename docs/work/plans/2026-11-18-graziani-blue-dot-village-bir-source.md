# B5229 Tmimi: Village/Bir source distinction

**Result:** The retained map's blue dot and key establish a **Village/Bir** symbol at Tmimi `B5229`, but the checked sources do not explicitly choose **village** versus **bir** for that place. There is meaningful source evidence for both terms being operationally distinct in detailed water rules; the scenario's generic `City/Village` heading is context, not a per-row adjudication that Tmimi is a village. Do not replace the shared symbol with a selected subtype without a source-backed interpretation.

A discriminating source would be an original or official keyed place list **explicitly** assigning `Tmimi/B5229` to village/town or bir for the relevant rule, or an applicable official clarification that all shared-dot locations use one rule category. A geographic source or ordinary-language reading of the name alone need not settle the game's classification, and the `City/Village` list is not an explicit individual water-column assignment. Pending evidence, preserve the shared mark and the competing application interpretations; actual well state, route, facility and availability are independent.
