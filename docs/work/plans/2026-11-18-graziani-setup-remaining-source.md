# Amiriya setup: later airfield-complex record

**Result:** A third scenario setup distinguishes the named Amiriya location from a player-placed group of six nearby airfields. It is evidence that the source sometimes uses an area placement around that name; it does not correct either earlier scenario's explicit coordinate or prove where the named location is.

The open [Amiriya comparison](2026-11-18-graziani-amiriya-coordinate-conflict.md) identifies first-group §60.5 **Amiriya E2212** and second-group §61.33 **Amiriya E3212** as different airfield listings; neither is an explicit correction of the other. A bounded search of `docs/work/plans` for the later complex wording found no discussion, so I inspected [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 10 / printed p. 34, **§63.33 Commonwealth Air Strength**, Air Facilities paragraph. It instructs the Commonwealth player to place **six airfields within one hex of Amiriya**, calling them the Amiriya complex. That sentence gives no coordinate for Amiriya and does not enumerate the six chosen hexes. Its air-strength table likewise names the complex rather than giving it a hex. Section 63.2, PDF p. 9 / printed p. 33, begins that later scenario group at GT102 OpStage 1.

The entry supports treating a named place and multiple nearby airfields as potentially distinct concepts in **that later setup**. It does not establish that §60.5's one printed airfield is one of those later facilities, that either prior coordinate is the named place's correct hex, that all six airfields are distinct hexes, or that an independent subsequent scenario revises an earlier scenario. “Within one hex” also does not resolve inclusion of a center hex, stacking, or permissible placement without their governing rules. Land-only survival of these later Air facilities is outside this check. No Amiriya map mark, seam, alias, actual player choice, or Graziani facility allocation is newly admitted.

To discriminate the unresolved Graziani coordinate, seek an authenticated correction explicitly addressing §60.5, an independently identified original source naming the place or facility with an applicable coordinate, or a reviewed map-key/mark identification that addresses the earlier conflict. A later scenario's relative placement alone is insufficient.
