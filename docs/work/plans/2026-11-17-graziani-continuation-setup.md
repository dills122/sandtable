# Graziani continuation: Matruh roster expansion and location inference

**Disposition:** Scenario setup notation and the erratum's OA scope support a **conditional D3714 baseline candidate** for 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshire. This is stronger than inferring location from assignment alone, but is not an explicit hex correction, an official clarification, an accepted project ruling, or proof of legal final attachment. Preserve location as unknown until the inference and its source-conflict treatment are independently reviewed and adopted. This check adds a testable interpretation to the existing [Matruh source review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-organization-followup-review.md); it does not overturn that review's conclusion that the erratum itself gives no hex.

The [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-continuation-setup-review.md)
supports only the conditional candidate. It emphasizes that `Att` means
attached but not assigned, a detached unit need not occupy another hex, and
the proposed ordering of default attachment before free setup organization
is a project interpretation rather than explicit sequencing.

## Evidence and inference

1. [Scenario setup key](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 1 / printed p. 25, §59.2 says starting forces are generally listed by parent and the OA chart supplies assigned subordinates; composition differences are noted in parentheses. Its `Det.` explanation says assigned detached subordinates are listed under the parent and separately in their own hexes. This describes a roster-expansion convention, not a universal assertion that all assigned units must always occupy their parent's hex. The same page says `Less` identifies original assignees absent from the listed roster and `Assg` adds originally unassigned subordinates; §59.2 on PDF p. 2 permits setup organization of units listed in the same hex, within applicable limits.
2. [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1, §4.44b introduces its numbered corrections as applying to OA charts, reinforcement tables and related order-of-battle information. Item 2 says 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshires start the Italian scenarios as part of, explicitly assigned to, Matruh Garrison. This scope makes a corrected OA expansion a plausible interpretation, rather than a mere inference from a historical parent label. It conflicts with the [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 10, which forbids assignments to the Garrison; p. 11 lists both battalions as unassigned and deployed. A specific-erratum exception is a prerequisite, not an already established official amendment to every affected printed clause.
3. Scenario §60.41, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, places Matruh at **D3714**, explicitly naming Essex, Durham and South Staffordshires as `Att`; it does not name Buffs or Hampshire on that row or elsewhere in the inspected §60.41 land list. Compare its 7th Armored Division row: it explicitly detaches the division units listed earlier at separate locations. This example illustrates the key's notation; it does not prove Matruh's omission was deliberate or that its erratum repaired every location omission.
4. **Conditional derivation:** if the erratum is applied as a specific correction adding both deployed battalions to Matruh's starting OA roster, and §59.2's normal expansion applies without an omitted detachment or other exception, the parent entry supports both battalions at D3714. Land §4.25, PDF/printed p. 9, makes assigned units starting in their parent's hex attached by default unless specifically stated otherwise. Scenario §59.2 offers a free same-hex organizational choice subject to limits; ordering the default before that choice is a proposed interpretation, not expressly sequenced. Neither independently supplies the missing location; both depend on the location inference and accepted precedence.

## Alternatives and impact

- The erratum may correct assignment alone while leaving placement unspecified or omitting a required detached placement. The printed Matruh OA prohibition and unassigned OA rows are direct conflicts. Section 59.2 describes the normal notation, but the erratum does not explicitly say D3714, attached, or amend the scenario list. Absence of a separate `Det` entry is evidence for the candidate, not conclusive proof against a printing omission. A different authoritative correction or selected interpretation could change both location and attachment.
- Preserve three scenario-stated `Att` units separately from the two erratum assignees. Essex and Durham's `23/70` identifier does not prove their then-current assigned parent. Do not make the extra two battalions automatic attachments solely from `assigned`, silently invent a location, count duplicate copies from their old OA rows, or treat six attachment slots as settled. See [capacity](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-followup.md) and [company](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-company-review.md) for independent constraints.
- A later admission decision should compare at least: corrected-OA expansion to D3714 with default attachment; same-hex legal detachment during setup; assignment with unknown or separately proven location; a separate explicit `Det` override; the OA prohibition conflict; and whether each attachment-limit interpretation permits the baseline and final choice. Reject or retain unknown for missing location instead of manufacturing a hex. Preserve each identity once and track initial source baseline separately from owner choice, attachment and later movement. These are proposed vectors, not executed gameplay tests.
