# Matruh limit policy: independent review

**Disposition, 17 November 2026:** Retain as a bounded planning selection. Clarify the scope of type restrictions and the unresolved interaction between listed excess and optional setup changes before using the checks as an executable contract. Neither issue establishes an official six-additional ruling or authorizes runtime admission.

## Findings

1. **Type-gate scope needs an explicit decision.** The [policy](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-limit-policy.md#counting-and-type-gates) retains Matruh's no-tank and one-gun-class restrictions under both candidates, but does not say whether they govern all actual attachments, only the additional attachments counted under A, or some other set. A's exemption from the numerical count does not itself answer that separate question. Keep the restrictions in view; identify their applicable population and source hierarchy before deriving legality, including for an exceptionally assigned unit actually attached to Matruh. This is an unresolved interpretation, not evidence that either gate disappears.
2. **Grandfathering and reorganization need a transition boundary.** The policy correctly distinguishes an actual source-listed formation excess from an excess created by owner choice. Its instruction to reject newly created excess does not decide how free same-hex reorganization interacts with a pre-existing listed excess: for example, removal followed by replacement, a changed roster still above the limit, or a move to conformity and a later addition. The [capacity follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-followup.md) explicitly leaves that interaction open. Preserve the listed state and §59.2's restrictions; defer a transition whose legality depends on this unresolved interaction rather than treating every still-excess result as automatically permitted or prohibited. Test conformity and replacement separately.
3. **Make example premises executable before admission.** The arithmetic examples are useful comparisons, not setup findings. Explicitly bind them to Matruh's GT 1–12 row and an accepted full battalion-equivalent cost, with no claimed free-company adjustment or other exception affecting the numerical comparison. In particular, five versus seven is a base-count distinction, not proof of an actual legal roster or an unconditional rejection of a grandfathered listed state. Test the disputed modifier independently, including unknown eligibility and strength, as the policy already proposes.

## Supported boundaries

- The OA's Matruh-specific six-attached wording and errata §19.5's general additional-to-assignment explanation support competing readings. The [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF pp. 1–2, separately assigns Buffs and Hampshires and describes chart attachments as additional to normally assigned units; it does not expressly reconcile that exceptional assignment with Matruh's ceiling. Selection A is therefore a planning choice, not a source-proven ruling. Candidate B counts an assignee only while actually attached; assignment elsewhere is not an attachment.
- Three scenario `Att` battalions establish listed attached identities, not three proven chart slots or assigned identities. The errata assignment alone establishes neither actual attachment nor location. Apply the co-location default only after establishing location, and apply owner setup choices only when their same-listed-hex and other conditions hold. Do not infer an assignment maximum or shell denominator from the number of named assignees or six attachments.
- The [continuation review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-continuation-review.md) and [company review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-company-review.md) support retaining chart cost, nested-unit handling, dynamic equivalence, shell denominator and free-company eligibility as open questions. A base numerical pass is not complete legality. Supply stacking's unavoidable-distribution exception does not establish a formation-capacity exception.

## Review scope

Compared the policy with the linked continuation, capacity follow-up and company reviews. Independently checked the errata text; the other scan claims above rely on those reviews, not a fresh visual inspection of every source page. No exhaustive later-errata search, runtime validation, setup/data change or test run. Admission still requires a versioned hierarchy and counting contract, source-supported setup and focused tests.
