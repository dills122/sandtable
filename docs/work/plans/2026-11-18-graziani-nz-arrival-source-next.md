# 6th New Zealand arrival: reinforcement cross-check procedure

**Date:** November 18, 2026. The [NZ follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-nz-arrival-followup.md) and [same-scan review](2026-11-17-graziani-nz-arrival-followup-review.md) already identify §60.47's track instruction, the track/OA `3/6` brigade versus `3/2` artillery, and Land §20.11's contradictory `3/2` brigade example. This bounded pass adds Land §§4.41–.42's general schedule/OA procedure as relevant source evidence. It **does not find an official correction** of the discrepancy or resolve source precedence; RUL-GRA-04 remains a planning interpretation.

## Additional original-rule locator

Land §4.41, printed/PDF p. 9, says reinforcement schedules list names and dates of newly arriving land units, while OA sheets list organization and parent arrival dates. §4.42 directs the player to consult the applicable schedule for the current Operations Stage, then find the OA for each arriving unit. Its explanatory example describes using the OA arrival date as a cross-check against the schedule. It further says a parent arrives with its listed lesser units unless a lesser unit has a later arrival date and/or is excluded on the track.

This strengthens the source basis for treating the **track and OA as intended cross-checks**, rather than casually treating one as unrelated data. For 6 NZ Brigade, the existing reviewed chart readings agree on stage 3 of turn 6 for the brigade and its three battalions, while both give the 6th NZ field artillery stage 3 of turn 2. However, §4.42 does not declare a general priority rule against a conflicting specific rulebook example, establish that either printed date was corrected, or make track and OA independent editions. It also does not make the separately listed artillery arrive twice or establish the ownership/attachment of any later reinforcement. The [scenario's §60.47](2026-11-17-graziani-nz-arrival-followup-review.md) and §20.11's general sentence already favor using the track; §4.42 adds procedure, not an official decision between those and §20.11's contrary example.

Land §20.11, printed/PDF p. 31, expressly describes both brigade and field artillery arriving September `3/2` immediately after its track-directed general sentence. Those two statements remain genuinely in tension with the reviewed schedule and OA. No actual die, transport, training status or movement follows from either date. Compare one brigade event at GT2/OpStage3 against one at GT6/OpStage3, keeping the GT2/OpStage3 artillery event separate; do not create two brigade arrivals. Scenario endpoint GT6/OpStage3 and phase/arrival ordering still require the separately reviewed timing contract.
