# C4131: initial motorization capacity comparison

**Result:** Under the conditional medium-truck chart reading, full-strength **2nd Scots Guards** needs **six** Motorization Points to carry its six infantry TOE; the ten medium trucks printed at C4131 convert to ten attached points for that hex, leaving an arithmetic difference of four. The colocated **31st Field Artillery** has six 18-pounder TOE, each with printed CPA 15, and §32.53 says normal units have built-in transport. The source therefore does **not** establish that another six points must be allocated to those guns before the artillery can move. Actual assignments, priority across other infantry, and how built-in transport interacts with this unit's represented composition remain open.

## Source chain

- [Scenario §60.41](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, lists **2nd Scots Guards (I)** and **31st Field Artillery Regiment (4 In)** together at C4131, followed by **10 medium trucks**. [Scenario §§59.42 and 59.63(A)](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, assign first-line trucks within the listed hex and convert each medium or heavy truck into one attached Motorization Point. This is ten points attached within that hex, not ten independently assigned to each unit. See [independent Commonwealth setup review](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-motorization-review.md).
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 11, identifies `2 SctGds`, ID `p`, `N`; p. 5 key defines `N` as normal maximum TOE for non-gun units. The [unit characteristics chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 22 §4.46a, gives `p` infantry maximum **6**, CPA **10** without a plus. Section 32.54's priority for historically motorized infantry (`8+` or `10+`) therefore does not identify this `p` row as a priority recipient; it also does not establish allocation of every point or priority over guns.
- Commonwealth OA PDF p. 15 identifies `31 Fld`, ID `x`, **6 × 18-pounder**; unit chart p. 22 gives `x` artillery maximum **6** and unit CPA **20**. Gun characteristics §4.47, PDF p. 27, lists 18-pounder CPA **15**; the [review](2026-11-18-graziani-c4131-motorization-source-review.md) corrected an OCR-derived §14.47 locator. [Land §32.53](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 44, allows Motorization Points to transport units and guns with CPA `0+`, while treating normal units as having built-in transport. These facts require preserving the gun's and unit's distinct CPAs and the built-in-transport clause; they do not prove a six-point extra requirement for moving these CPA-15 guns. Applicable unit/gun CPA and group movement remain separate readings.
- [Common chart §54.2](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), p. 14, gives medium truck capacity **one infantry TOE** or **one artillery TOE**, with CPAs 20 for infantry and 15 for guns. Land §32.51 treats Motorization Points as medium trucks except where its major case modifies them, and §8.92 gives a non-motorized unit carried by trucks the relevant truck CPA. Conditional on applying that chart and carrying every present infantry TOE, six `p` TOE require six points and yield an infantry-carrier CPA candidate of 20. This is capacity arithmetic, not proof of actual assignment, a legal move or a blanket CPA for the mixed stack. If someone proposes independently carrying all six gun TOE as well, the chart's capacity arithmetic would be six additional points, total twelve versus ten; that calculation does not establish either need or eligibility for that additional carriage.

## Boundary cases

1. Six present Scots TOE and six eligible assigned points: chart capacity exactly met; four of this ten-point subtotal remain arithmetically. Five points are insufficient for all six under the one-per-TOE reading. Actual strength, lawful assignment and other demands must be established independently.
2. Six 18-pounder TOE at CPA 15 with no extra points: examine normal built-in-transport and unit/weapon CPA rules before treating movement as unavailable. Compare an explicitly established exceptional `0+` gun, not an assumed `0+` status for these guns.
3. Compare points already attached to another formation or loaded under §59.66(B), with eligible available points in the same hex. Scenario §59.64 and Land attachment rules govern later redistribution; §32.54's historic-motorization priority is not an automatic source allocation to Scots Guards. The scenario's separate second/third-line pool cannot be silently merged into this initial ten.

No actual setup assignment, player choice, transfer, move, route or CP expenditure is asserted. Initial TOE can be affected by specific scenario exceptions, rule precedence and representation; verify them before admission. No independently complete competing-priority inventory or Land-only unit/weapon CPA policy is claimed.
