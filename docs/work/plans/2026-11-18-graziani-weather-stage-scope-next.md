# WT-03: opening-stage cross-map storm exclusion

**Result:** In a single foul-weather determination, the printed location table never selects Maps C and E together. This gives a concrete first-stage discriminator for two scenario-listed starting locations: a rainstorm cannot affect both **C3721** and **E1829** at once under that table. The same section-selection exclusion holds for sandstorms, with the additional Map E delta qualification. No die value, storm or actual game state is asserted.

## Source-backed checkpoint

- Scenario §60.22, [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, starts GT1 Operations Stage 1. Section 60.41 lists **1st Royal Northumberland Fusiliers at C3721** and **16th Infantry Brigade (70) at E1829**. The source locations belong to distinct printed map sections. This 16th Infantry Brigade is not 16th Australian Brigade.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 12 §5.2, put initiative declaration then weather determination before Organization and either player's movement/combat block. Thus, conditional on entering the scenario at that printed stage and retaining those fixed setup placements, weather is determined before normal Organization or movement changes their positions. This is an opening-checkpoint source candidate, not proof that a runtime fixture has admitted those counters or its startup policy. Section 60.6 gives the Italian side initiative throughout GT1; it does not choose the weather result.
- Land p. 41 §29.1 calls for a location die after a sandstorm or rainstorm and gives normal weather to unaffected sections. [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) PDF p. 8 §29.7 lists C for location results **2, 4 or 6**, and E only for **3**. No printed location result includes both. Therefore a single opening-stage **rainstorm** cannot select both listed sections; if C is selected E is normal, and if E is selected C is normal. A different result may select neither. For **sandstorm**, section-selection exclusion also holds, while the table's delta exception can further treat an E delta hex as normal; whether E1829 is a delta hex is not determined here.

This is stronger than simply counting the 18 possible stage entries: it rules out one joint foul-weather assignment at the named opening positions, conditional on the published location table and an admitted stage. It does not rule out storms in both sections in **different** Operations Stages, hot weather across all maps, or later unit movement between sections. It does not establish the season row, actual weather roll, location roll, unit status or any storm effect on an action. The [schedule review](2026-11-17-graziani-weather-schedule-review.md) documents the fall-row selection and season conflict; [contact-weather review](2026-11-17-graziani-contact-weather-scope-review.md) covers other Map C locations but does not certify this cross-section opening pair. Actual startup, terrain/delta identity, path, phase-specific restrictions and deterministic replay remain separate gates.
