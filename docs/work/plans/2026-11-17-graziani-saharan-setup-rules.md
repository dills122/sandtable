# Graziani Saharan Detachment: setup rule boundaries

Research date: 17 November 2026. This is a bounded reading of the same original scans used in prior research, not an independent edition or a final ruling. No runtime admission follows.

[Scenario §60.31](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, lists the Italian **Saharan Det (I)** under “Within 3 Hexes of C0716” and lists **5 light** trucks on the same initial-deployment row. The row is separate from “Anywhere in Libya”; the fixed C0716 supply dump in §60.34 is also separate. Section 60.22 starts Graziani on Game-Turn 1, Operations Stage 1. This row describes initial deployment, not a post-start movement allowance or a requirement to move to the chosen hex.

## Formation and placement

- Scenario §59.2, PDF p. 1 / printed p. 25, directs players to the OA chart for subordinates assigned to a listed parent, subject to scenario exceptions. Its Hex Location paragraph permits division of **listed units** among hexes when more than one hex is listed, subject to stacking. Here the row names one formation and a radius, rather than explicitly enumerating multiple hexes or listing separate subordinates. Whether this paragraph authorizes independent distribution of the formation's constituents across radius cells remains an interpretation, not an explicit grant. Conversely, the row alone does not explicitly say every constituent must occupy one hex.
- [Land §4.25](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 9, says assigned units beginning in their parent's hex are attached by default unless specifically stated, and attached units are represented by their parent rather than separate counters. This conditional does not establish that all assigned units must start in the same hex. Scenario §59.2, p. 26, permits initial organization of units listed to begin in the same hex within formation and stacking limits; it does not independently authorize relocation. Composition, initial attachment, valid detachment, stacking and terrain remain separate checks.
- Prior [radius inventory](2026-11-17-graziani-c0716-radius-source.md) and [independent geometry review](2026-11-17-graziani-c0716-radius-source-review.md) derive 37 cells using distance ≤3 including C0716. Scenario wording does not explicitly settle inclusion of origin or distance-three boundary. The distance-chart counting instruction §37.42 is not an explicit setup definition. Treat inclusive endpoints as a proposed interpretation, not a confirmed ruling; geometry alone does not establish legal placement.

## Trucks and mode

Scenario §59.2, p. 26, and §59.42 require listed first-line trucks to be assigned to units in their listed hex, with owner-selected division among units in that hex. They do not expressly explain how the five points are apportioned if a radius row's formation can start in multiple hexes, whether all must accompany a single selected location, or how this row interacts with other eligible co-located units. Do not silently pick one allocation or duplicate the five points. In the detailed logistics game §59.45 allows trucks to start carrying supplies or motorizing troops or anti-aircraft units without cost, subject to applicable rules; this does not itself prove a particular load or legal recipient.

For the Land-only abstract logistics profile, §59.63(A), p. 26, derives attached motorization hex by hex from **medium and heavy** first-line trucks, one point per truck. The five listed light trucks therefore add **zero** under that conversion, rather than five abstract motorization points. This does not establish absence of other eligible points or additional sources. The §59.64 post-start alteration of initial motorization distribution is a separate rule, subject to its exception and Land attachment/detachment rules; it does not settle setup distribution.

## Evidence limits

Visually checked scenario pp. 1–3 and Land p. 9 using the linked scans. The [prior radius review](2026-11-17-graziani-c0716-radius-source-review.md) checked September 1979 errata §60.31 only for the separate XXI Corps artillery correction; other editions, indirect rules and later corrections remain possible. OA composition, organization interactions, exact legal cells, setup hierarchy, truck allocation and any subsequent movement remain unverified. No gameplay tests run; documentation only.
