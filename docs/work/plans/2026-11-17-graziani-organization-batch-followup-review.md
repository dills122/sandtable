# Graziani Commonwealth arrivals: organization follow-up review

**2026-11-17 — visual source review.** Reviewed the
[follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-organization-batch-followup.md) against the
original scanned pages. No material contradiction found in the bounded claims
checked. This review read the follow-up before the scans: it is an independent
visual check, not a blinded independent transcription or a rules decision.

| Check | Visual finding |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule and legend | Commonwealth charts PDF p. 3 prints GT2/OpS3 6th NZ Field Artillery, GT4/OpS3 3rd Hussars `[7]`, GT5/OpS1 7 Indian Brigade `[4 In]` with 5 L/20 M trucks, and GT6/OpS3 6 NZ Brigade with 25 L/85 M/5 H trucks. PDF p. 4 says bracketed information includes parent formation and assigns NZ units except two railroad engineer battalions to 2nd NZ Division. This supports the follow-up's assignment inference, not a co-location requirement. |
| Armor OA | Charts p. 11 lists 3rd Hussars, 2 RTR and 7 RTR under unassigned armor-class units. The Hussars note calls its 9th Armored Brigade/10th Armored Division assignment and different tank mix later; 2 RTR's note says assignment to 4/7 Armored Brigade upon arrival; 7 RTR has no note. The schedule `[7]` and OA placement remain distinct evidence, requiring reconciliation before selecting game behavior. |
| Indian and Polish OA | Charts pp. 5 and 15 support the indentation key, 4th Indian Division placements and brigade-parent relationships. Page 21 lists eight Polish unit rows, including HQ, each at 3/1; its note describes taking orders from 6th Australian Division at campaign start. Neither note alone specifies a game attachment operation or cost. |
| Placement | Charts p. 4 says reinforcements arrive in any Cairo hexes unless otherwise specified, while returning units may be placed in Alexandria or Cairo. Same-parent arrivals may share a hex with desired attachments and detachments subject to stacking; different-parent arrivals must use separate hexes if possible, without exceeding stacking. Land p. 31 §20.14 gives Cairo default and the separate return option; scenario PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28 §60.47 directs use of the printed track. These do not prove a legal placement in a particular map state. |
| Mode-specific conversion | Scenario printed p. 28 §60.92(A) equates motorization-point ability to medium-truck-point ability, one for one, for Land only. Printed p. 29 §60.93(A) instead includes medium plus heavy for Land and Air. The 20 and 85 medium figures sum to 105, but the cited passage does not independently establish that sum as a scenario allowance for these particular arrivals. |
