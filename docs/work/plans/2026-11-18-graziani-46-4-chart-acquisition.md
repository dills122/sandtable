# Flak Adjustment Chart §46.4: located

**Result:** The original **§46.4 Flak Adjustment Chart is present** in [common charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), printed/PDF p. 11, right side. It is a four-row chart, not merely an index or rules reference. The common-chart index on p. 16 also identifies §46.4 and page 11. This closes the chart-location gap in [prior scope research](2026-11-18-graziani-air-supply-errata-scope.md); it does not establish a Land-only truck-loss rule.

The printed chart groups **non-fighter planes** as 1–23, 24–47, 48–71, and 72 or more, with AA player's favorable column adjustments 0, 1, 2 and 3 respectively. The same booklet p. 13 §46.3 note instead directs a right shift for every multiple of twelve bomber-class or transport-class aircraft above twelve, attacking each target group separately; its example expressly says 24 aircraft shift one column. The [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 3 middle column, says §46.4's chart is wrong and §46.3's notes correct. For a **synthetic single target group of 36 bomber-class planes on other missions**, the printed chart gives one shift while the §46.3 note gives two; actual game reachability is not established. The [review](2026-11-18-graziani-46-4-chart-acquisition-review.md) notes the mission category is a prerequisite. The two texts also name their populations differently, so do not infer that all non-fighter planes satisfy the note's bomber/transport category.
