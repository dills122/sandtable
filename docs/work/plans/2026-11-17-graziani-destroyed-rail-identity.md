# Graziani: destroyed railroad identity under §8.78

**Disposition:** Prefer retaining a previously constructed, destroyed railroad hex as a candidate §8.78 witness, as an explicit versioned interpretation. The text repeatedly calls it a destroyed railroad hex and prohibits rail entry until repair; it does not expressly settle the distinct nonlocal witness question. Do not equate destroyed with unbuilt or infer that an unusable witness is itself a usable route hex. No implementation or admission follows from this preference.

## Evidence and inference

- [Original Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 15, §8.47 directs players to ignore **unconstructed** railroad hexes for all purposes. Section 24.67, p. 37, says unbuilt railroad hexes do not exist or function until built. These express exclusions support rejecting a certified unbuilt printed hex as a witness, but do not say previously built and subsequently destroyed rail becomes unbuilt.
- Section 8.76, pp. 15–16, refers to destroyed rail hexes and forbids entering them by rail movement until repaired. Section 24.66, p. 37, describes how railroad hexes may be destroyed and marks the affected hex with a Destroyed RR Hex marker. Sections 24.61 and 24.63 distinguish new construction from rebuilding destroyed track and permit eligible engineers to rebuild destroyed railroad hexes. This terminology, marker and repair operation support continuity of identity through damage; they do not explicitly say whether a destroyed hex qualifies for the separate §8.78 condition.
- Section 8.78, p. 16, restricts Allied railroad movement use west of **any rail hex occupied by an Axis combat unit**; it does not expressly require the occupied witness hex to be operational. Reading its noun in light of §8.76 and §24.63 favors a retained-witness policy even while §8.76 independently bars entering the destroyed hex by rail. An operational-witness policy instead adds an operating-track condition; that condition is not stated in §8.78. Both remain interpretations rather than a documented official ruling. No conclusion here settles what counts as “use,” “west,” a combat unit, or the relevant state snapshot.
- Sections 8.71 and 24.6 separately exclude the historical Soluch–Benghazi–Barce line from use. Destruction does not establish that such historical ink becomes qualifying rail. Independently established overlapping operational or destroyed track needs its own classification. Unknown line identity, construction history, destruction state, occupant classification or geographic relationship stays unknown; absence of a record is not proof of absence. A known sufficient witness can establish an internal block despite other unknowns, subject to disclosure and action-boundary constraints below.
- Scenario §60.7 was previously visually reviewed as placing the initial railhead at Mersa Matruh: see [rail eligibility research](2026-11-13-graziani-rail-scope.md). That named endpoint does not certify individual hexes, destruction history or reachability. This pass did not independently inspect the scenario scan. The [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), p. 1, modifies §8.71 for Axis use and §8.73 for reorganization; it does not expressly amend §8.78 or establish a destroyed-witness exception. This is not a census of later errata.

## Decision and verification boundary

Record retained-witness as a proposed interpretation, separately versioned from construction status, operability, line identity, occupancy, geographic west and event timing. A valid destruction transition should preserve evidence of prior construction and mark track unusable; a valid repair should restore usability under independently established rules. Do not assert a change takes effect mid-action or mid-stage until the governing ordering is decided. Compare alternate operational-witness interpretation before adopting a ruling.

Synthetic vectors, conditional on otherwise certified legal context: (1) an eastern previously built undamaged rail hex occupied by an Axis combat unit blocks use of an independently usable western hex; (2) destroy only the eastern witness: retained policy still blocks while operational policy does not, and neither permits rail entry into the destroyed hex; (3) repair it and check both policies again; (4) substitute certified unbuilt track and check no witness, versus unknown track or destruction state and check unknown; (5) add another certified eastern witness and test existential behavior; (6) test historical-only ink, independent overlap, and unknown identity. Establish snapshots, completeness and legal lifecycle prerequisites independently. These vectors are not gameplay or six-turn reachability evidence.

Keep witness evaluation authoritative. Hidden off-route occupancy or track state must not be revealed through action enumeration, distinct rejection reasons, timing, state changes, replay or downstream behavior beyond an independently established audience and source-authorized disclosure. Compare histories with identical authorized observations but differing hidden witnesses, including transitions and any separately justified §10.16 disclosure. If no observation-safe action protocol is established, keep the public rail action boundary unavailable; an internal known block alone does not authorize a public explanation.
