# Aresca assignment maximum: independent review

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition:** Supported as a bounded interpretation. The inspected sources support five as the normal assignment maximum and hence four qualifying attached battalions as the §9.26 full-brigade threshold. They do not establish a four-battalion maximum merely from four OA entries. This is not an official ruling or an exhaustive errata review.

## Evidence and limits

- [Italian formation chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 31, §19.32, depicts five separate tank-battalion symbols for the `(3)` tank-regiment type; its legend identifies the symbols as tank battalions. The four-light/one-medium language is explicitly historical. The `(2)` row has three tank battalions and is a different type. [OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 6, lists Aresca as 1st Regiment HQ and indents XXI(L), LXII(L), LXIII(L), and I(M) beneath it. The reviewed plan reports its companion visual reading of the [counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 1, as Aresca `(3)` tank regiment; counter image could not be independently displayed in this review. Subject to that match, the chart supports five; OA's roster describes four assignments, not by itself an express maximum of four.
- [Land rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 29, §§19.2–19.3, distinguish assigned from attached units and state that normal chart entries are maximum assignment limits, while singular structures noted on the OA are limited by those structures instead. OA p. 6 notes describe historical operation and starting attachments; no explicit Aresca-specific singular four-unit limit appears there. Section 19.21 allows an independent unit already attached to fill an available assignment position during reorganization. A detached but still assigned unit occupies its original position (§19.28). This does not establish that a suitable independent battalion is actually available at setup.
- Section 9.26, p. 17, uses the number of battalions that *may be assigned* as denominator and the number attached as numerator; §9.27 excludes attached shells from the count. Five yields a full threshold of four, while a denominator of four would yield three. Keep assignment capacity distinct from current attachments: §19.4 permits some non-assigned attachments and §19.45 describes substitution for missing assigned units, subject to its own conditions. The four qualifying attachments need not be assumed to be the four OA-listed battalions.
- Section 19.62, p. 30, caps new battalions assigned to a surviving cadre that lost all its assigned battalions by the number listed on the OA. Its specific rebuilding language should not be silently applied as a general initial maximum, nor should this review settle its interaction with the normal chart in that later state.
- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §60.31, lists Aresca at C3919 with all except XXI detached, and the other three as attachments to other divisions. With XXI attached and non-shell, this baseline is one qualifying attachment out of five, hence a shell. Setup §59.2, PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, permits same-hex organization within applicable limits; it permits a different legal initial disposition and does not itself change assignment. If no combat unit remains attached, check empty-HQ effects separately. The Autoblinda exception concerns two individual TOE points, not another assigned battalion.

## Review qualification

Normal-chart conclusion depends on the absence of an applicable singular-structure exception beyond inspected passages. A five-unit assignment limit does not alone prove that five units may attach, or that a fifth eligible battalion can be obtained during the scenario. No runtime/data changes or gameplay tests.
