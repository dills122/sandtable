# M13/40 breakdown correction: Graziani scope

**Result:** September errata §21.12 corrects Italian **M13/40** Breakdown Adjustment Rating (BAR) from Land's `2R` example to `1R`. Axis chart §4.48 independently prints `1R` for that exact vehicle; its legend defines `R` as a rightward breakdown-table column shift. Inspected Graziani starting Libyan Tank Command M-series vehicles are **M11/39**, not M13/40. Selected named M13/40 arrivals examined below are after GT6. This is not proof that all replacement, production, capture, setup exception or other paths cannot introduce M13/40 within the scenario.

## Source comparison

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 33, §21.12 example prints M13/40 `2R`; §21.13 says BAR adjusts breakdown-points table column left or right. Section 21.11 applies breakdown to listed vehicles including tank TOE points, whether part of units or replacement points. Section 21.26's illustrative LII(M) example explicitly uses M/13s and +1 higher column; an example does not place that unit in Graziani.
- [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 §21.12, says M13/40 BAR is `1R`, as on charts. [Axis charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 29 §4.48, explicitly list M 13/40 `1R`, M 11/39 `1R` and CV 33 `2R` in separate rows. Chart legend identifies BAR as breakdown column shifts. `1R` is numeral one plus R, not a unit identifier or an infantry rating. Same-edition rule, correction and chart are not independent editions.
- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §60.22 runs GT1 OpStage 1 through GT6 OpStage 3; §60.31 places 1 CCNN with I(M), Maletti with II(M), Aresca with XXI(L), Trivoli and Libyan Tank Command HQ among its initial forces. Axis OA PDF p. 6 lists I(M) and II(M) each `7 × M11/39`, Tank Command HQ `1 × M11/39`, and named light battalions as CV33. The letter `(M)` alone does not identify an M13/40. This comparison does not audit every initial unit or all subsequent composition changes.
- Axis OA PDF p. 4 gives Ariete HQ and 132nd armored regiment HQ `1 × M13/40` each and VII(M), VIII(M), IX(M) `7 × M13/40` each, all `1/18`; its legend makes this OpStage 1, GT18. PDF p. 14 gives unassigned V(M) `7 × M13/40`, `3/13`, and LII(M) `7 × M13/40`, `3/55`. These particular OA dates follow the six-turn endpoint; date alone is not proof against distinct scenario rules, altered arrivals, replacements or other sources. No exhaustive OA or production audit performed.
