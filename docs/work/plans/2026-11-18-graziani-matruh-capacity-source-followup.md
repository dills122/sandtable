# Matruh attachment chart: type-definition follow-up

**Date:** November 18, 2026. The [company review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-company-review.md) and [capacity follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-followup.md) already establish the source conflict and keep company eligibility unknown. This pass checks the chart's type key against its company modifier; it finds a useful qualification for testing type restrictions, **not** a source resolution of six-total versus six-additional or Matruh's present brigade-equivalent status. Existing RUL-GRA-03 is a planning interpretation, not an admitted rule.

## Source evidence

- Common Maximum Attachment Chart §19.5, PDF p. 6, gives Matruh GT1–12 six units, “no tank, 1 gun class.” Its key defines `Unit` by a full-strength battalion- or company-equivalent counter and `Tank` specifically as **battalion-equivalent units composed of tank TOE Strength Points**. The chart's modifier separately allows any nation's division- and brigade-equivalent units two **non-shell company-equivalents** at no cost to maximum attachments; it says three company-equivalents equal one battalion-equivalent and the companies must comply with applicable type restrictions.
- This definition warrants a distinct question not answered merely by saying “no tank”: whether the chart-key battalion-equivalent definition means an individual tank company falls outside that prohibition, how multiple tank companies or a shell battalion reduced to company-equivalent are classified, and whether another applicable rule supplies a broader restriction. The modifier's type-compliance sentence and three-company equivalence remain operative text under any reading. Do **not** treat the narrower key alone as permission for any specific tank-company attachment, or assume every tank TOE point is banned without a chosen interpretation. Compare mixed companies and aggregate size only after their source-defined classification and chart hierarchy are decided.
- Land §9.28 says a shell is reduced one level for unit differentiation and other rules where size is important, giving a battalion shell as company-equivalent. Section 9.26 defines a battalion shell by less than half its maximum TOE points, with a separate artillery threshold. The modifier expressly requires its free companies to be **non-shell**; whether an apparent company-equivalent created by reducing a shell battalion can ever qualify requires reconciling those expressions and actual counter/type/strength identity. Do not simply equate company-equivalent, non-shell and full-strength. Land §9.25 separately treats certain zero-stacking companies/batteries and their stacking limits; zero stacking alone does not confer free attachment eligibility.
- Land §19.5 says the chart's limits apply to units of the indicated size and type regardless of whether their organization is standard. That is evidence the chart is not restricted to standard formations; it does not say that Matruh's nominal brigade designation satisfies the modifier's **brigade-equivalent** condition in a particular snapshot. Its separate row and §9.22/.26–.28 still require the hierarchy and shell-denominator choices already identified in the [company review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-company-review.md).
- Land §19.4 describes non-assigned attachments over assignment limits; September errata §19.5 calls chart attachments additional to normally assigned units, including assigned units elsewhere. Matruh OA PDF p. 10 instead says no units may be assigned and up to six may be attached; errata §4.44b item 2 assigns Buffs and Hampshire. None of the checked type definitions reconciles the exceptional assigned units with that special six-attached wording. Six-total and six-additional remain alternatives; do not infer location or present attachment from assignment alone.

## Conditional checks after interpretation

Compare a full-strength non-shell company with a shell battalion treated as company-equivalent, a nominal tank company, three company-equivalents and a battalion-equivalent composed of tank points; separately vary Matruh's established size, assignee attachment, applicable base ceiling and the population subject to the row's type restrictions. Apply nesting and legitimate setup/stacking constraints independently. These are distinction tests, not evidence that such units are present or legally attachable. Unknown class or source hierarchy cannot by itself prove either a grant or permanent denial.
