# Graziani continuation: independent rail restriction review

**Disposition:** The off-route eastern-witness inference follows the printed §8.78 when both hexes qualify and the western relation is established. Correct the unfinished-track caveat: §8.47 supplies directly relevant, stronger language. Preserve separate uncertainty for destroyed track, rail-line scope, direction and timing. No rail action or reachability follows.

## Findings

1. **Unconstructed is not source-neutral.** [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 15, §8.47 says unconstructed railroad hexes are ignored for all purposes; §8.76, p. 16, says rail hexes not yet in existence cannot be used until constructed. This supports treating an unconstructed printed railroad hex as no qualifying §8.78 rail witness, absent an overriding rule. The continuation plan's statement that the text does not expressly resolve unbuilt status omits §8.47 and overstates the policy freedom. Track construction state may still be unknown in a particular snapshot; unknown is not proof of construction or nonconstruction.
2. **Destroyed differs from unconstructed.** Section 8.76 prohibits entry by rail movement until destroyed rail hexes are repaired, but still calls them rail hexes. It does not expressly decide whether an Axis occupant on one supplies §8.78's witness. Do not transfer §8.47's unconstructed rule to destroyed track without further evidence. Also examine §8.71's historical Soluch–Benghazi–Barce line, which the original text says may not be used for anything and has no effect on terrain or movement, before treating every printed line as a qualifying witness; its interaction with §8.78 is not established here.
3. **Conditional off-route consequence is supported.** Section 8.78 bars Allied railroad movement use of a rail hex west of *any* rail hex occupied by an Axis combat unit. It does not require the occupied hex to be on the proposed route, or specify the direction of travel. Thus an established eastern qualifying witness blocks a western used hex even when the intervening and traveled portions are clear. Section 8.72 and §8.74 allow movement in both directions subject to limits; they do not create an eastbound exemption from §8.78. Geographic comparison, qualifying line/status, which hexes count as use, and the relevant snapshot remain separate interpretation or evidence questions; do not equate west with rail ordering or automatically certify endpoints from this conclusion.
4. **Keep ZOC and occupation distinct.** Section 8.73, printed p. 15, separately bars a traveler from starting in enemy ZOC and from entering enemy ZOC or an enemy-occupied hex while using rail. Section 10.26 concerns negating ZOC's movement effect in specified circumstances; it does not itself remove an Axis combat occupant or expressly waive §8.78. Neither result selects precedence between §§8.73 and 10.26. An off-route Axis non-combat unit alone is not the named §8.78 witness, although §8.73's separate entered-hex occupancy prohibition remains relevant.
5. **Tighten citations and CP wording.** Section 8.73 appears on printed/PDF p. 15, not p. 16. The September 1979 errata's addition says rail travelers may not **earn** reorganization points in that stage; it does not state a general prohibition on spending them. Section 8.77's stage-wide CP wording warrants reconciliation with §8.73 before choosing later-CP semantics. Section 8.77 permits pickup and dropoff subject to restrictions; it does not establish a sequential action protocol or the governing witness snapshot.

## Implications for tests

Keep off-route eastern combat witness versus off-route non-combat control, both travel directions, clear path, and route occupancy/ZOC cases separate. Add a certified unconstructed printed hex with an Axis combat occupant that does not qualify under §8.47, plus constructed and unknown-status controls. Test destroyed rail as a distinct explicit interpretive alternative rather than equating it with unconstructed. Include historical-line scope only after adjudicating its relationship to §8.78. All synthetic tests remain conditional on separately legal fixtures and do not establish historical reachability.
