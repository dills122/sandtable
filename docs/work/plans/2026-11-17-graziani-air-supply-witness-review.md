# Graziani abstract air supply witness: independent visual review

**Disposition: revise capacity statements; candidate remains unproven.** Land §32.14 prints three units in any other type of hex **off-map**, not in every other hex. Its on-map interpretation is disputed. The witness must not state an unconditional three-unit limit outside major cities or that five units fill Matruh. Preserve the alternative interpretations, the separate §59.65 setup exception and any adopted ruling. The printed five-unit major-city limit supports a destination check if the actual HQ hex qualifies as a major city; exact destination, occupancy and any applicable exception still require proof.

Visual recheck of [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 43, §32.37 confirms one supply unit per player per Game-Turn during an OpStage Organization Phase; origin and destination must be major city, village or Tripoli/Tunisia box. Before transport, owner expends 12 Fuel from any supply unit in the origin hex. No enemy combat unit may be within five hexes of origin. Commonwealth may not transport farther west than the westernmost hexrow occupied by a friendly division, shell or full; destination must contain a friendly division or brigade HQ counter, not battle-group HQ. Owner may not move transported unit or its supplies that OpStage, though §32.16 draw is permitted. The text does not independently impose aircraft or airfield capacity. Whether other applicable rules add constraints remains a separate source question.

Visual recheck of [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29 §60.92(C), confirms five regular units at Mersa Matruh D3714, each 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel, and three regular units each at Alexandria and Cairo, each 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel. Sidi Barrani has one at 40/60; three Dump 1 units at 40/30 share one map-D hex west of Matruh. Scenario PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.41 places 2nd New Zealand Division HQ among forces at Alexandria E3613 and/or E3714, without determining which hex. §60.22 gives GT1 OpStage 1 start and GT6 OpStage 3 conclusion. The selected Axis stock figures in the witness agree with §60.92(B), PDF pp. 4–5.

A separate initially full Matruh counter can arithmetically pay 12 Fuel, leaving 48 Fuel and 40 Ammo, with cargo balance unchanged. This is conditional on both counters remaining present and their stated balances surviving preceding events; it proves neither legal timing nor a legal shipment. Do not infer exact city-hex allocations, enemy distances, divisional row, HQ status, player choices, capacity or unused allowance. A certified witness needs actual setup choices and preceding events, exact endpoints, source and cargo balances, applicable stacking interpretation, destination HQ and occupancy, enemy-distance and directional checks, and resulting stage lock. No complete witness is established.
