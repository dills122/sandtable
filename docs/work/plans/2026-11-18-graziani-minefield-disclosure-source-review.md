# Minefield disclosure: independent source review

**Disposition:** No material transcription error found in the [source note](2026-11-18-graziani-minefield-disclosure-source.md). The construction secrecy, entry-triggered type reveal and distinct printed dummy-removal timings are supported. They do not establish a completed minefield, actual observation or a digital disclosure policy.

- Land addendum §3.61–.62, printed/PDF p. 45, denies knowledge of opposing counter attributes except where rules expressly indicate otherwise; its **partial** list permits examination of map stacks but says the item undergoing construction/demolition is secret. It does not expressly make the construction marker or hex invisible, or specify a completion-event audience. A visible marker does not itself disclose the secret construction item.
- Land §§24.31–.34, p. 36, permit engineer units or Commonwealth HQ engineers to construct regular or dummy fields; reverse side marks the type. Both require a full Operations Stage, with completion at the beginning of the next Construction Segment in the printed example. A regular field requires the listed start supplies and an Under Construction marker; a dummy uses the same procedure with no ammunition and three Stores Points. These detailed costs do not establish their abstract-mode application or authorize revealing an opponent's actual expenditure. §§24.35–.36 separately constrain terrain, one field per hex and enemy-controlled hexes; the note does not claim an actual legal project.
- Land §26.11, p. 38, prints identical fronts and differentiated reverse sides; §26.15 specifically flips a **friendly** field immediately when entered by an **enemy unit**, revealing real/dummy status. §26.23 specifies same entry cost for real/dummy and revelation on enemy entry; later entrants in that phase still pay the minefield entry cost. The source does not independently define a remote audience, pre-entry reverse inspection or implementation event history. Other applicable engineer-specific entry costs remain separate from the real/dummy comparison.
- §24.38 states dummy removal upon revelation; §26.14 states end of the **Movement Segment** in which the enemy unit spends CP entering; §26.23 says end of the **Movement Phase** and explicitly charges later entries during that phase. The difference is material because §5.2 permits repeated Movement Segments within a Movement/Combat Phase. These passages do not alone select a precedence, nor does immediate reversal necessarily decide removal time. §26.13 additionally requires an eligible engineer to begin the full stage in a real-field hex, remain there and expend no CP on movement; the note's summary does not purport to replace those detailed conditions.

Visually rechecked [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) pp. 36, 38 and 45, and §5.2 p. 12. This does not exclude indirect or later clarifications.
