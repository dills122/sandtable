# Singleton one-point units and ZOC threshold

**Finding:** In an otherwise isolated hex, an eligible combat unit whose applicable §10.11 stacking value is exactly **1** does not meet either printed “more than one” alternative, even if raw defense is at least ten. Adding an attached first-line truck does not increase organization size under §9.29. This is a conditional singleton result, not proof of the actual Graziani setup stack, all exceptions or a contact outcome.

## Source and baseline

- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 17 §10.11: combat units above battalion-equivalent, described parenthetically as **more than one Stacking Point**, exert ZOC; units in a hex with **more than one** Stacking Point also exert ZOC even without a larger-than-battalion unit. Truck convoys and HQs without attached combat units never exert one. One is not more than one. A separately qualified larger unit or sufficiently populated hex is a different case; don't rewrite the strict threshold as one or more.
- §10.15 pp. 17–18 independently excludes units in a hex totaling **less than ten raw defensive Close Assault Points**, with anti-tank units presumed Up Front for that purpose. Exactly ten passes this numerical exclusion, not necessarily the other conditions. §§10.12–.14 exclude named units/markers and units with cohesion −26 or worse; §10.21 has directional hexside and unit-entry restrictions. Passing raw defense does not create the stacking condition; the inverse is also true.
- Independent counter and OA checks read `II` and unparenthesized printed `1` for [1 RTR](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-first-rtr-zoc-review.md), [2RflBde](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sheferzen-unit-zoc-review.md), and [11 Hus](2026-11-17-graziani-fort-maddalena-unit-zoc-review.md). Scenario §60.41 lists them at C3520, C3320 and C3020 with five, ten and three medium trucks respectively. Under the separately documented unchanged standalone baselines, their raw defense is 21, 12 and 18 respectively; these pass §10.15 numerically while one applicable stacking point fails §10.11. Printed parent designations and listed trucks do not certify actual attachments, final stack, current status, or scenario reachability.

## Mixed and exceptional stacking

§9.29 says first-line attached trucks do not count in **any way** towards unit organizational size or road-space stacking limits; truck convoys do not count against stacking limits except road space, and §10.11 expressly denies convoys ZOC. Their presence is not an additional combat battalion. §9.15 gives informational markers and air units no Stacking Point value; §§10.12–.13 deny their own ZOC. Supply Units have zero stacking under §32.14. Do not infer a >1 threshold solely from these counters, nor infer that a marker's own ineligibility automatically answers all mixed-stack questions.

§9.14 governs terrain-based maximum stacking and §9.16 lists exceptions: a garrison stacks freely in its assigned city/village, with Fort Maddalena's example explicitly zero there and one elsewhere; pure AA stacks freely in major cities and specified numbers with air facilities; the scanned continuation also refers to units with `0+` CPA not presently motorized having zero stacking. Exception scope for §10.11's **hex total**, as opposed to capacity, is not expressly settled for every case. In particular [mixed-stack research](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-mixed-stack-zoc.md) retains printed versus home-effective garrison readings; don't turn printed numbers into an automatic sum.

Artillery is not automatically zero: §9.24 gives artillery units one stacking point except certain scattered batteries, while §10.15's raw-defense and position rules remain separate. A legal, separately represented and otherwise eligible co-located artillery or other unit may change the hex calculation; its type, attachment, effective value, combat contribution and state must be resolved first. §9.21 describes assigned-and-attached division constituents; §9.29 trucks and §19.41/.42 attachment rules do not license double-counting a parent and represented children. §9.26 defines shells and §9.28 reduces shell organization one level where size matters. A printed `1`, a shell's effective organization, stacking capacity, and the applicable hex total must remain distinct facts.

## Gates and tests

Determine actual legal placement, scenario mode and owner setup, unit and parent/constituent identities, shell and strength, eligibility of each co-located unit, and any free-stacking interpretation before deciding the aggregate. Then apply raw defense, cohesion and terrain/side independently. Unknown hidden setup or uncatalogued edge remains unknown, not a negative or positive ZOC. Compare synthetic `1` alone versus `1` plus attached truck; `1+1` with separately eligible artillery versus represented constituent; free-stacking and shell alternatives; raw 9 versus 10; denied cohesion and prohibited edge. Preserve replay and side-safe disclosure. These are not executed tests or claims of actual play.
