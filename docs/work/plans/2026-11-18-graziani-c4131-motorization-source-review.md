# C4131 motorization: visual source review

**Disposition:** The [comparison](2026-11-18-graziani-c4131-motorization-source.md) matches the inspected figures and preserves the conditional allocation boundary. **Correct one source locator:** the Commonwealth gun characteristics chart is **§4.47**, not §14.47, on PDF p. 27. Its 18-pounder row does visually print CPA **15**.

- Scenario §60.41, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, lists 2nd Scots Guards and 31st Field Artillery Regiment at C4131 with **10 medium** trucks. Scenario §59.42 assigns the first-line trucks to units at their listed hex; §59.63(A) converts each medium/heavy first-line truck to one attached Motorization Point. This supports the ten-point hex subtotal, not a ten-point allocation to each unit.
- Commonwealth OA PDF p. 11 prints 2nd Scots Guards, counter `2 SctGds`, ID `p`, TOE `N`, deployed `D`. The key on PDF p. 5 expressly defines `N` for **non-tank or gun units** as the maximum TOE for that ID. Unit chart §4.46a, PDF p. 22, gives `p` maximum **6** and CPA **10** without `+`. The inference of six at normal strength is supported subject to specific setup overrides; §32.54's priority expressly identifies historically motorized infantry with CPA 8+ or 10+, not this printed `p` row.
- Commonwealth OA PDF p. 15 lists 31st Field Artillery, counter `31 Fld`, ID `x`, **6 × 18-pounder**, deployed `D`. §4.46a p. 22 gives `x` unit CPA **20**, maximum six artillery TOE; chart **§4.47**, PDF p. 27, gives the **18-pounder Gun CPA 15**. Preserve unit and weapon CPA as separate source values; neither value independently establishes a mixed-formation movement result.
- Common §54.2 p. 14 prints medium-truck capacity of **one infantry TOE or one artillery TOE** and CPA 20 carrying infantry / 15 carrying guns. Conditional on applying that chart to abstract Motorization Points and carrying all six infantry TOE, six points provide the stated capacity, leaving four of the ten-point subtotal arithmetically; five are insufficient for all six. Land §32.51 treats points as medium trucks except as modified, and §32.53 distinguishes units and CPA 0+ guns while saying normal units have built-in transport. These passages do not establish a mandatory additional six-point allocation to the printed CPA-15 guns, nor a legal assignment or move. Any extra gun-carriage arithmetic remains conditional, as the note says.
