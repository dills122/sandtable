# Tobruk later-scenario port comparison: independent review

**Disposition:** Accept the comparison, with a wording boundary: §61.6 says
San Giorgio is still there; it does not independently say there that the ship
is partially sunk or blocking the harbor. Seven is stated as Tobruk's current
starting efficiency, not throughput or a newly established maximum. No
source-backed resolution of the 7/5 conflict follows.

1. Scenario §61.6 sets all ports to full listed efficiency except Benghazi at
   zero and Tobruk at seven, adding that San Giorgio is still there. Section
   60.7 separately states Tobruk at seven and expressly describes that ship as
   partially sunk and blocking the harbor. The later statement repeats the
   number and ship presence; attributing its blocked status requires the other
   cases, not §61.6 alone. These are two scenarios in the same source, not
   independent editions or evidence of an actual completed playthrough.
2. Section 61.72 is the later scenario's Land Game only abstraction, continued
   onto the next page. Its Commonwealth initial supply provision includes five
   regular Supply Units at Tobruk, each initially holding 40 ammunition and
   15 fuel. It does not state a different Tobruk efficiency or define a
   clearance procedure. This mode-specific setup supports distinguishing supply
   counters from efficiency; it does not itself settle which detailed port
   procedures apply in Land-only play, or modify Graziani's §60.7 setup.
3. Chart §55.3 independently lists Tobruk efficiency **5**, with separate
   stage throughput values of **1 incoming and 3 outgoing stacking points**
   and **1,700 tons**. Its footnote says the campaign starts below five due
   to San Giorgio. Air/Logistics §55.25 specifies a three-level reduction;
   §55.18 limits ordinary recovery and excepts obstruction rules. Neither the
   repeat of seven nor the ship's presence defines above-maximum capacity,
   chooses source precedence, or authorizes subtracting three again from a
   stated seven. Chart footnote's campaign scope should not silently be
   generalized as an explicit separate §61.6 correction.
4. September 1979 errata p. 2 §30.58 explicitly corrects Land's port-blocking
   cross-reference to §55.2. This strengthens the case for considering that
   section in Land-only scope, but it is not a Tobruk starting-value correction
   or a complete abstract-mode cost and recovery rule. See the separate
   [clearance review](2026-11-18-graziani-port-clearance-source-next-review.md).
