# Matruh free-company eligibility: independent source review

**Disposition:** Keep eligibility **unknown** where a decision depends on
Matruh's current brigade-equivalent status or the interaction of §19.5's
modifier with its special maximum. The counter provides strong evidence of
nominal brigade designation, but its printed `X`, `(3)` and `G` do not alone
resolve the current shell calculation or attachment rule. A static designation
reading remains a plausible, explicit alternative. No source-backed result
here proves either a grant or a permanent denial.

## What the original records resolve

- The original counter scan, PDF p. 4, British Counter Section Nr. 7, shows
  one `Selby`/`Matruh` counter between 1 Buffs and 1 Hamp, with an infantry
  symbol, brigade `X`, `G` and parenthesized `(3)`. This separately confirms
  the [candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-company-next.md) reading. `X`
  supports nominal brigade designation; `(3)` is printed HQ stacking notation,
  not three current battalions or the six-unit attachment limit. Land §9.12
  says an HQ with no attached combat units has zero stacking and a printed
  parenthesized value when representing its formation.
- Common chart §9.4, PDF p. 2, assigns three stacking points to a full super
  brigade **and** a shell division. Together with `X` and the Commonwealth OA
  p. 10's brigade-level HQ description, `(3)` strongly supports brigade rather
  than division as the nominal category. It is not an unconditional current
  equivalence verdict. §9.22 warns that brigade names can mislead and directs
  attention to printed stacking points and §9.4; §9.28 separately reduces a
  shell by one level for differentiation and other rules where size matters.
  Whether this chart modifier is one of those uses still requires interpretation.
- §9.16(a), printed/PDF p. 17, gives the marked garrison free stacking in its
  assigned city/village. `G` is a **stacking exception**, not a printed
  instruction that its effective organization is zero-sized, that the
  free-company modifier applies, or that attached units automatically consume
  no capacity. Determine location and the exception's interaction with the
  represented formation separately.
- Common chart §19.5, PDF p. 6, gives Matruh GT1–12 **six units, no tank,
  one gun class** and separately allows division- and brigade-equivalent units
  two non-shell company-equivalents without cost to maximum attachments,
  while retaining type restrictions. The chart does not expressly say whether
  this unusually described HQ takes that modification or how it interacts
  with its separate six-unit wording. It also defines `Unit` using a
  full-strength counter and battalion/company equivalence; whether that
  general key imposes a separate full-strength requirement on the modifier's
  specifically non-shell companies needs its own hierarchy decision. Do not
  silently equate non-shell with necessarily full strength.

## Why the current-equivalence calculation remains open

Land §9.26 defines a brigade shell by **attached battalions** relative to the
number that **may be assigned**, with a special case for a brigade whose
maximum is two battalions and at least one attached battalion is itself a
shell. Section 9.27 excludes attached shell subunits from the stated
prerequisites; the attached-versus-assigned distinction is material. The
Matruh OA says no units may be assigned; September errata §4.44b item 2 says
the Buffs and Hampshires are assigned. Errata §19.5 describes chart attachments
as in addition to normally assigned units. Neither supplies an unambiguous
maximum assignable battalion denominator for this exceptional HQ or expressly
settles its six-total versus six-additional conflict. Six chart attachments
must not be substituted automatically for six units that may be assigned.

Scenario §60.41 lists Essex, Durham and South Staffordshires as `Att` to
Matruh; §59.2 defines `Att` as attached, not assigned, and permits same-hex
setup organization within its constraints. These entries do not establish the
attachment state of the separately assigned Buffs/Hampshire, a complete legal
owner-selected composition, or every subunit's shell state. Nor does nominal
`(3)` prove an effective non-shell super brigade in all snapshots. Even once a
snapshot is known, decide whether §9.28's size reduction applies to §19.5's
eligibility and how the special Matruh maximum interacts with the modifier.

A **hypothetical** ordinary-brigade threshold example shows why a denominator
cannot be guessed: if six battalions may be assigned, three qualifying
attached is below two-thirds and gives shell status under the ordinary test;
if only three may be assigned, three qualifying attached meets the ordinary
threshold. Neither denominator is asserted for Matruh. Equality at two-thirds
is not “less than” that fraction; test the explicit two-battalion exception
and shell-subunit treatment separately rather than applying this example
mechanically.

An alternative policy can treat the OA's brigade-level HQ and `X` as sufficient
for §19.5 irrespective of effective shell size. That gives effect to the
specific Matruh row and organization designation, but needs an explicit
precedence decision against the dynamic-size reading; it is not compelled by
the counter. Failure to choose is **unknown**, not an automatic rejection of
all attachments: an independently proven type/stack/maximum violation can
still be rejected, and an otherwise verified attachment fitting ordinary
limits need not rely on the disputed modifier. Block only when the legal
result genuinely depends on unresolved eligibility or hierarchy.

## Focused distinguishing vectors

- With accepted fixed-organizational policy, eligible non-shell companies may
  use the modifier only subject to the separately accepted six-unit hierarchy,
  chart-key strength reading and type restrictions. Under a current-equivalence
  policy, compare a proven full brigade, a proven shell brigade, a detached
  subunit and an unknown Matruh denominator; do not derive a state from `X` or
  `(3)` alone.
- Test hypothetical 3-of-6 versus 3-of-3 assigned maxima, equality at
  two-thirds, and the special two-battalion/shell exception. Neither example
  proves those maxima or attachments occur in the historical setup.
- Keep `G` free-stacking at its valid location separate from effective size,
  organization and the company allowance; test away from the location and
  unknown garrison assignment. Test non-shell versus full-strength company
  under each accepted key interpretation, a forbidden tank and the separate
  one-gun-class restriction.
- Preserve each source-listed `Att` counter and each errata-assigned unit once;
  compare legally attached, detached-assigned and owner-selected setup states.
  A command requiring the disputed allowance fails closed without mutation;
  a separate proven illegal attachment remains illegal, while a fully proven
  ordinary-capacity case need not be blocked solely by this modifier.
