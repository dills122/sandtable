# Port erratum and mode scope: source review

**Disposition:** The [candidate](2026-11-18-graziani-port-erratum-mode-scope.md) correctly distinguishes the personnel and abstract Supply Unit thresholds and qualifies the 60% comparison. The corrected cross-reference does not itself settle clearance under Land-only abstraction. Section 30.5's introductory reference for sea supply is printed as **“Case 00.00”**, but September errata §30.5 corrects it to **§56.0**, as the [clearance review](2026-11-18-graziani-port-clearance-decision-input-review.md) subsequently verified. The original version of this review overlooked that correction.

I visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 42–43, and [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2:

- **§30.5/.58:** Section 30.5 expressly scopes itself to personnel. Section 30.58 says blocking does not affect personnel reception until the port is reduced to **less than** two-thirds (67%) capacity, rounded up; then it may neither receive nor send personnel. It separately says a port totally incapacitated by bombing cannot receive or send personnel. The strict comparator and printed rounding must be preserved; this check does not settle how rounding operates for a particular port or treat exactly two-thirds and 67% as interchangeable discrete states.
- **Erratum:** The printed §30.58 reference is §50.2; errata §30.58 changes its second-line reference to **§55.2**. This is a corrected reference to the harbor-blocking case, not an express rewrite of the personnel threshold or a complete Land-only clearance rule.
- **§32.36:** For Commonwealth sea movement of one Supply Unit per OpStage among Alexandria, Benghazi and Tobruk, the destination must have an efficiency level permitting use of **at least** 50% of listed capacity. It occurs in Naval Convoy Arrival and substitutes for any combat-unit transport from the origin and into the destination. Equality at 50% meets the printed destination comparator, subject to the other conditions. The text does not itself say a personnel threshold is identical or decide all interactions with other restrictions.
- **Comparison:** On the candidate's explicit hypothetical that 60% is established as the applicable measure for both thresholds, 60% is above 50% and below both two-thirds and 67%. This illustrates different threshold tests; no actual capacity calculation, port, transfer legality, control or clearance follows. In particular, do not calculate one percentage from the other without a separately accepted capacity and efficiency contract.
- **Mode:** Section 32.0's general rule concerns the Land Game without Air and Logistics, and §32.11 limits applicable supply rules. Their relationship to the corrected specific reference, remaining personnel transport, blockage, recovery, resources and timing is still an interpretation. Neither a blanket import of §55.2 nor its blanket exclusion follows from this comparison.
