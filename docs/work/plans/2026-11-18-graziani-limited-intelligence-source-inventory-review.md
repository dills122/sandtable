# Review: limited-intelligence source inventory

**Disposition:** The [inventory](2026-11-18-graziani-limited-intelligence-source-inventory.md) accurately presents the checked §3.61/.62 examples and selected patrol/ZOC exceptions within its stated limits. No material audience or source-transcription error found. The list is partial, not a complete visibility policy or an exhaustive review of all rule exceptions.

## Source checks

- Land addendum p. 45 §3.61 restricts knowledge of opposing counter status, composition and other attributes except as rules expressly indicate. §3.62 labels its examples a **partial** list. The map entry permits examination of a stack while distinguishing counter contents; it does not grant inspection of every associated record. Anti-armor and close-assault entries reveal the specified participant **types**, with their respective omissions of numbers, origin/loss assignment or weapons. These specific disclosures coexist with the introductory statement that land-combat totals are revealed without identifying the units producing them.
- The prisoner entry forbids quizzing; the patrol entry withholds supply state and attached trucks except motorized status; vehicle-marker contents, POW numbers and Axis nationality, construction/demolition item, and truck/replacement numbers and types are secret under their listed cases. None alone defines a complete capture audience, action timing or every digital projection. The inventory correctly avoids turning secret contents into a blanket claim that all corresponding counters are invisible.
- The plane entry permits players to know exact makes engaged in air-air combat while pilot ratings are secret up to the instant of combat. Its bombing/strafing sentence limits land units without AA TOE points **or an SGSU** in the mission hex to mission type, withholding aircraft numbers and types. It does not specify all entitlements of AA-equipped land units. These detailed Air examples do not alone establish a Land-only aircraft or SGSU action.
- Land §16.51 p. 27 describes report of historical designation, unit type, motorization and an “exact” strength **within two**; the p. 45 addendum withholds constituent TOE point types. The awkward printed wording does not justify publishing actual exact strength merely because it says “exact.” §§16.52–.55 qualify the non-phasing player's report selection and the phasing player's exclusions. Section 16.32's no-combat-unit disclosure treats engineers and AA/Flak as noncombat **for that check**, not for all rules.
- Land §10.16 p. 18 directs the **non-phasing player** to inform the phasing player whether a particular unit exerts ZOC if it could exert into a hex where a phasing unit begins a Movement Segment or moves into it. The case does not require an inventory of every possible enemy ZOC. September errata p. 2 §16.11 adds the listed eligible patrol types, not a general report-disclosure expansion.
