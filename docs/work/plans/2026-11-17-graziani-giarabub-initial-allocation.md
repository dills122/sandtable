# Giarabub initial motorization allocation

**Source finding, 2026-11-17:** General Italian trucks are available for owner distribution, but the inspected abstract-logistics setup text does not establish that second/third-line-derived points may be attached to Giarabub's motor machine-gun battalion before play. Nor does it establish a particular allocation, an empty general pool, or a zero initial effective allowance. Keep this distinction between the detailed truck game and the abstract motorization-point profile.

- [Scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §§60.22, 60.31 and 60.33: Graziani starts Game-Turn 1, OpStage 1; the Giaribub Garrison starts at C1014, with no truck figure on its row. Italian second/third-line trucks include **30 light, 100 medium and 25 heavy truck points** for anywhere in Libya. The Italian player may assign and place them within the stated restrictions; this does not select a destination or recipient. The separate Tripoli and air-facility allocations must not be silently moved into this pool.
- Same booklet, PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, §§59.42–.45: listed first-line trucks must be assigned among units in their own listed hex. Eligible supply trucks must be placed at a friendly combat unit, supply dump, or major city/town/oasis, subject to the listed exception. Trucks may start transporting supplies or motorizing troops without cost or penalty. These provisions support an owner-selected detailed-game setup subject to all applicable placement, transport and attachment restrictions; they do not themselves prove a specific Giarabub assignment or that a particular proposed global allocation is legal.
- Same page, §§59.62–.64: when logistics is abstracted, §59.63(A) converts each initial medium or heavy **first-line truck point** into one **attached motorization point**, calculated hex by hex; §59.63(B) calls points converted from second/third-line trucks **unattached**. The 100 medium and 25 heavy Libya truck points therefore correspond to **125 initially unattached motorization points**, not 125 points plus the original trucks. Air-facility trucks are ignored if air is abstracted. Section 59.64 permits changing initial distributions once the game starts under Land attachment and detachment rules, with the §59.66(B) exception. The inspected text does not expressly authorize converting that unattached pool into an attached setup allocation before game start, despite the broader detailed-game wording in §§59.45 and 60.33.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 44, §§32.51–.54: motorization points substitute for truck points and are treated as medium truck points except as modified; they transport units and supply units. Section 32.54 requires enough points be assigned to all historically motorized infantry (`8+` or `10+`) before assigning points to other infantry types. It is a priority, not a named source pool, an instruction to create points, or an explicit pre-game exception to §59.63(B). Its interaction with the initial attached/unattached distinction and distributed pools remains a rules interpretation question. It also does not prioritize infantry over all non-infantry uses.
- Land p. 16, §§8.92–.93, 8.96–.97: historical `+` indicates usual historical truck provision; actual motorization requires sufficient transport. Attached trucks come under the highest parent formation currently attached; the organization-phase rule governs attachment during play, with its stated exception. It does not establish a pre-start attachment from §59.63(B)'s pool. Preserve distinction among battalion, its parent and controlling owner; do not invent a separate battalion-owned pool or duplicate a parent's points. See [Giarabub source check](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-giarabub-motorization.md) for `8+` and OA identity.

**Implementation boundary:** Preserve source row, quantities and conversion provenance; record owner placement and any authorized attachment as separate choices. Do not infer that co-location automatically attaches a point, that §32.54 selects this unit's exact points, or that missing first-line figures prove no eligible co-located source. If a rules policy later permits pre-start transfer in the abstract profile, identify its authority, enforce capacity, placement, priority and ownership, and debit the existing pool once. Otherwise retain the initial uncertainty and use the applicable in-play attachment process. Actual sufficient transport and applicable CPA remain to be determined.
