Its flexible `Anywhere on Map D or E` land row lists 8th Medium Artillery, 65th and 149th Anti-tank, 9th Heavy AA, 15th Light AA and 57th Light AA, so that row itself cannot place those units at either Map C contact hex. Other named land rows are fixed or city/area-specific; no separate free Map C land-unit row appears in this passage. This is not a full audit of counter identity, parent constituents or later source corrections.

Scenario §59.2, PDF pp. 1–2 / printed pp. 25–26, permits initial attachment/detachment among units **listed to begin in the same hex**, subject to organization and stacking limits. It does not on its own relocate the separately printed C3520 and C3320 units or transfer an eligible Map D/E placement to Map C. Assigned and attached relationships and counter representation remain separate, and any permitted initial co-location must be established before applying the same-hex option.

Detailed §60.44, PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, places Dump I and a dummy dump anywhere in Egypt on Map C or D. In contrast, Land-only §60.92(C), PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29, replaces §60.44 and places the three real Dump 1 units on Map D west of Mersa Matruh, with the dummy group using that rule. The detailed placement is conditional on a reviewed Egyptian-region interpretation and actual owner choice; it does not authorize the replacement Land-only group at C3520 or C3320. Section 60.43's `Anywhere, maps` row concerns second/third-line trucks, not listed combat units, and §59.44 provides on-map placement restrictions for supply trucks. Land §§9.29, 10.11 and 32.14 separately distinguish trucks, convoy ZOC exclusion and zero-stacking supply units; do not infer a positive combat stack or a complete ZOC result from a dump or truck marker.

**Additional scope guard:** §59.66(C), PDF p. 2, permits qualifying groups of 30 other unattached motorization points in the same hex to begin carrying an additional regular supply unit. Whether an eligible group can legally be placed at either contact hex needs the separately applicable initial truck, allocation, grouping, mode, stacking and owner-choice rules. The contact note already reserves other supply/dummy placement; ensure its named §60.92(C) restriction is not read as an exhaustive ban on every other possible initial supply unit. This option does not itself create a combat counter or prove its effect on §10.11.

The separately reviewed single-unit counters and raw values support the conditional baseline analysis, not actual final-stack isolation, ZOC or compulsory combat. Source identity, same-hex unit presence and organization, possible corrections, national boundary interpretation, map-side coverage, entry restrictions and phase all remain gates. Proposed tests should include the optional §59.66(C) case if and only if its prerequisites are separately accepted, alongside fixed entries, D/E choice, both supply modes, replay and side-safe outcomes.
