# RUL-GRA-07 selected policies: independent planning review

**Scope:** Review of the selected policy text against its linked planning proposals and repair matrix. This review did not independently inspect the original scans or later errata. The linked blind readings are transcriptions of the same source edition, not independent authority. These choices remain contested planning interpretations, not official rulings, admitted runtime data, or scenario reachability proofs.

## Findings

1. **Repair scope needs an explicit exception gate before implementation.** [RUL-GRA-07](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-07-bounded-facility-and-victory-planning-policies) broadly displaces ordinary repair resource expenditures, while the [facility proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md#land-only-repair-supplies) specifically identifies §§22.26 and 22.35 and requires review of exact repair types and exceptions. The [resource matrix](2026-11-13-graziani-repair-resource-matrix.md#what-the-source-says) flags §22.38's construction-specific case and distinguishes §22.42's Logistics-only expenditure. Reading the short selection as a blanket exemption for every repair operation would exceed the reviewed evidence. **Action:** enumerate admitted operations and source-specific exceptions in the future contract; leave unreviewed operations unsupported. Test each admitted type, construction-specific rule, absent/present abstract supply and detailed-mode costs separately. The selected displacement of §22.13(b) is itself a broader interpretation, not a consequence proved by no-expenditure language.

2. **Victory composition requires the linked three-valued evaluation and privacy rules.** The [selection](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-07-bounded-facility-and-victory-planning-policies) summarizes unknown handling but does not repeat the [proposal's](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-independent-decision.md#proposed-result-composition-policy-gra-victory-result-v1) rule that a known false required conjunct makes its row `no-match`, otherwise an unavailable conjunct yields `unknown`; nor does it repeat retention of all row results and reasons or filtering authoritative evidence for player views. Treating any unavailable conjunct as an unknown row even when another is definitively false changes outcomes; exposing row reasons unfiltered can disclose hidden state. **Action:** explicitly incorporate these details in the contract and test false-plus-unknown, true-plus-unknown, known match plus another unknown, and player-specific views. Keep terminal ordering, route and row predicates blocked until independently specified.

3. **Setup policy boundaries need to survive translation into state transitions.** The [facility proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md#tobruk-repair-facility-initial-type-only) supports one temporary Tobruk facility initially and explicitly leaves control-change type unresolved; the [fortification proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md#initial-city-fortifications) permits reduction of city levels to zero and requires separate action-rule review for rebuilding. Neither supports automatic facility conversion on capture or a permanent city bonus. Alexandria and Cairo facility hexes and control also remain subject to map verification ([Commonwealth proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-facilities-independent-decision.md#commonwealth-initial-facilities)). **Action:** block dependent post-capture behavior until a separate decision; model fortification level as reducible state, and defer rebuilding until its prerequisites are specified. Verify mapped city hexes and control before populating setup.
