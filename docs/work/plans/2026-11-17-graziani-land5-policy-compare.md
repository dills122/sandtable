# Land-only Axis convoy 5%: policy comparison

**Status: November 17, 2026.** Recommend the bounded per-group ceiling below for planning, conditional on explicit acceptance of non-monotonic loss severity. This is a project choice, not a source ruling, ledger decision, executable contract, legal-load proof or reachability finding. If monotonic severity is required, leave 5% unsupported pending a comparison of the entire result hierarchy; do not silently rewrite the printed 10% or 20%-and-above branches.

## Evidence and scope

The [triage](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-choice-triage.md), [proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-five-percent-next.md), [mode comparison](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-mode-rounding.md) and [independent critique](2026-11-17-graziani-policy-batch-proposals-review.md) report that Land §32.64 names supply, motorization and replacement cargo, and the invoked chart includes 5%. Land §32.65 specifies 10% and 20% or greater, including 25%, but does not specify a 5% branch. The detailed Air procedure's separate upward-rounded groups are an analogy, not established Land-only authority; Air abstract logistics explicitly replaces Land's procedure in its own mode. The independent [reachability review](2026-11-17-graziani-five-percent-reachability-review.md) confirms the chart cell but not an actual eligible load or six-turn event. These reviews use the same editions; no independent edition or exhaustive later-errata search is claimed here.

For comparison only, assume one already-established attacked individual Axis convoy, a 5% result, complete real cargo, and accepted group membership. Let `n` be an integer group count. Groups are Supply Units, Motorization Points, and Italian tanks, German tanks, Italian guns and German guns, pooling models within each nationality and equipment class. All examples are synthetic, not proven legal loads. Empty is known zero; unknown or unclassified cargo is unsupported.

| Candidate | Concrete 5% operation | Consequence and source fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Per-group ceiling** | Each nonempty group loses `ceil(n/20)`, capped at `n`. | Preserves Land's abstract subjects and nationality distinction; borrows Air-style separate upward rounding by analogy. One supply plus one motorization loses both; one Italian tank plus one Italian gun loses both. Strong small-group penalty and possible 5%-versus-10% inversion. |
| **Combined ceiling** | Pool all supported points in the convoy, lose `ceil(total/20)`, then allocate that total among carried groups and models under a separately accepted chooser. | One supply plus one motorization loses one; one tank plus one gun loses one. Treats different cargo units as interchangeable for arithmetic and creates cross-category allocation authority not established by Land. Still inverts 10% for the first example because 10% loses neither. |
| **Per-group floor** | Each group loses `floor(n/20)`; empty groups lose zero. | One supply plus one motorization loses neither; 19 in one group loses zero, 20 loses one. Uses fractional downward rounding, but extends no printed 5% instruction. A mixed group load can escape loss entirely; not a general proof of monotonicity across categories or the other branches. |
| **Always zero** | All supported 5% loads lose zero. | Eliminates 5% losses and the first inversion; makes a nonzero chart result operationally equivalent to a zero result. A deliberate policy, not a deduction from silence, and does not establish monotonicity between other branches. |
| **One minimum for convoy** | For a nonempty supported load, lose exactly one carried point, allocated under a separate accepted chooser; empty load loses zero. | Preserves a visible effect while limiting small-load penalty, but ignores 5% magnitude for large loads and requires cross-category allocation. The one-supply-plus-one-motorization example still inverts 10%. A per-group minimum instead would lose two and is a different policy. |

The table fixes candidate definitions for comparison; other pooling, minimum or percentage-preserving variants need their own specifications. At 10%, the reviewed Land branch excludes supply and rounds the motorization fraction down. Therefore one supply plus one motorization loses zero under that branch but both under the recommended 5% candidate. This is a per-cargo and total-loss inversion, not merely a difference in point value. Setting 5% to zero avoids this witness but alone proves no global ordering of all branches or cargo effects. A monotonic redesign must compare fixed legal cargo and allocation across all outcomes, including 10%, 20% and 25%, and explicitly address supply's exception and each branch's minima; it would be a broader project policy, not an implied 5% fix.

## Recommended bounded choice

Select `gra-axis-convoy-5pct-v1-proposed` for planning only: apply `ceil(n/20)` independently to each listed nonempty group in one convoy, with zero for known empty groups and losses capped by carried quantity. This gives the printed nonzero result a consistent effect on each named cargo subject, respects the reviewed Land nationality/not-model distinction, and uses a disclosed Air analogy rather than asserting Air governs Land. Its small-group penalty and inversion are deliberate costs. Combined ceiling introduces a new allocation across unlike subjects; floor or zero can erase the result; convoy minimum also needs cross-group allocation and disregards the proportion for larger loads. None is source-selected.

This choice does not determine which model is lost when several allocations satisfy a group loss. A singleton allocation is unambiguous; otherwise remain pending until an authorized chooser and deterministic tie/replay contract are separately selected. Do not infer chooser authority from Land's 10% tank example. Preserve the existing 10% and 20%+ branches and their separate unresolved questions. Bind any later selection to immutable ruleset identity and isolate old saves.

## Admission and distinguishing checks

Before executable effects, establish legal convoy and lane, complete immutable cargo and classification including dummy status, qualifying divisions and geometry, threshold direction and equality, ordered dice, timing, and authorized allocation. Persist draw result and RNG cursor durably; commit losses and delivery atomically with deterministic IDs and deduplicated retry. A crash or unresolved allocation cannot reroll, partially remove cargo or deliver survivors; observations and response timing must not reveal private cargo. Unknown cargo cannot become empty or zero loss.

Proposed checks, not executed tests: compare groups of 1, 19, 20 and 21 points; one supply plus one motorization; one Italian tank plus one Italian gun; empty versus unknown groups; distinct convoys; ambiguous model and cross-category allocation; 5%, 10%, 20% and 25%; table zero versus 5%; and duplicate command or interruption after draw. Validate legal loads separately before treating any vector as gameplay evidence. No runtime, ruling ledger or readiness change is made by this note.
