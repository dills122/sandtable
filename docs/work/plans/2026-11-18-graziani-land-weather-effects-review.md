# Land-only weather effects: source review

**Disposition:** Supports [candidate](2026-11-18-graziani-land-weather-effects.md)'s bounded movement and supply reading and its explicit unresolved interpretations. No runtime policy follows automatically. This informed review uses the same scan, not a separate edition.

- Land §29.44 doubles movement costs for hexes with sandstorm; §§29.45 and 21.37(d) use the differently scoped half-CP formulations described. Their denominator, map/hex scope and treatment of side costs remain interpretation questions. §32.55 exempts Motorization Points themselves from breakdown; it does not exempt every other vehicle.
- §§29.55–.58 support the stated rain wadi, road, river and delta effects. §8.41's rain-swollen road-wadi passage says +2 CP and no additional breakdown cost, retaining ½ BP, while §§29.56 and 21.37(c) treat road breakdown values as track values in rain; precedence remains an explicit question. §29.58's second sentence says **units** already in delta at onset must remain that Operations Stage unless on road or railroad; do not silently narrow that sentence to vehicle or motorized units.
- §§32.0, 32.11, 32.15–.16, 32.23–.24, 32.32–.33, 32.51, 32.55 and 32.58 support the described mode, supply draw, real transport and exemption boundaries. Actual transport and the hypothetical §32.16 draw are distinct. Reading all weather costs into that hypothetical traversal remains an interpretation, not expressly established as a separate rule. Initial fuel and ammo balances under §32.15 do not alone settle whether §29.34's 5% reduction survives §32.11. Prior [hot fuel review](2026-11-17-graziani-hot-fuel-followup-review.md) and [sandstorm review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sandstorm-movement-review.md) retain the same uncertainties and relevant timing and privacy gates.
- Candidate is scoped to movement, breakdown and supply, not an exhaustive weather survey. For example §22.13(d) also limits repair in storm hexes except at Major Repair Facilities; no finding here admits or implements that separate effect.
