# Motorization allocation and transfer: source review

**Disposition:** Supports the [allocation follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-motorization-allocation-followup.md)'s distinction between initially unattached points and trucks already attached to a parent when a subsidiary detaches. Neither the checked cases nor the `8+` marking prove a pregame assignment to Giarabub or its current effective CPA. This is an informed same-scan review, not an independent edition or an admitted allocation policy.

- **Initial pools:** Scenario §59.63(A) converts each first-line medium and heavy truck to an attached Motorization Point, hex by hex; §59.63(B) converts second/third-line trucks in the same manner to unattached points, excluding air-facility trucks when Air is abstracted. §60.33's `Anywhere in Libya` row lists 100 medium and 25 heavy, plus 30 light: the **125** is a conditional medium/heavy conversion subtotal, not one automatically co-located Giarabub pool or a selected recipient. The Giarabub §60.31 row does not list a first-line truck quantity. Other eligible co-located entries, placement choices and current attachments need separate evidence.
- **Attachment versus detachment:** Land §8.96 says Truck Points can attach to a historically designated unit and are controlled by the highest parent to which they are currently attached. §8.97 ordinarily confines attachment to Organization, with an exception in the Operations Stage when the unit is attached to a parent. Its separate detachment exception concerns a parent detaching a subsidiary during Movement/Combat: some of the parent's already-attached trucks may accompany the subsidiary. The September errata's §8.97 clarification also describes trucks going along with those detached units. Neither is an express pregame conversion of otherwise unattached second/third-line points into a Giarabub attachment. The printed rule also prohibits complete detachment of trucks from a historically designated unit at cohesion minus five or worse; its application requires the actual state.
- **Abstract and loaded-point scope:** §32.51 treats Motorization Points as Medium Truck Points except where modified in its major case. Scenario §59.64 permits initial distributions to be changed once the game starts, under Land attachment/detachment rules and the §59.66(B) exception. The latter restricts detaching points while they carry the optional initial supplies: they may detach only while remaining attached to a subsidiary combat unit of the parent. These sources do not by themselves establish whether any point starts loaded, a particular transfer, or the interaction of every detailed-truck exception with abstract setup.
- **Priority and characteristics:** §32.54 requires sufficient points for historically motorized infantry `8+`/`10+` before other infantry-type assignments; it does not state priority over all non-infantry uses or select a specific pool. Axis OA p. 14 lists Giarabub Mtr MG Bn as `hh`, and the Italian characteristics chart p. 26 places `hh` under Infantry Coy-Eq with `8+` and maximum TOE 1. That supports the priority subject's classification but does not itself establish location, transport attachment or a numeric current motorized CPA. The [medium-chart review](2026-11-18-graziani-initial-motorization-source-review.md) retains CPA 20 as conditional on actual qualifying carriage and applicability, not an automatically assigned rating.
- **Later arrival is separate:** §32.52 describes scenario initial points, further points as scenario reinforcements and a Medium Truck Replacement Point rate. Scenario §60.92(A) describes the ability to bring in points based on Medium Truck Points. Neither sentence adds a second automatic grant to the initial 125 subtotal.
