## Alternatives and minimal discriminator

1. **Import detailed clearance:** Interpret the corrected reference as admitting applicable §55.26 ship-obstruction procedure, including its engineer, timing and detailed resource requirements, in Land-only play. This requires an explicit representation and accounting rule for Stores and the interaction with §32.11; the abstract Supply Unit model does not itself establish such a ledger. A variation importing the engineer and timing while replacing or dropping charges is a **different policy**, not unchanged import.
2. **Keep clearance unadmitted pending an abstract rule:** Retain the source blockage and any independently admitted efficiency effects, but treat a clearance request as unsupported/unknown until a versioned Land-only lifecycle and cost rule are chosen. This does not establish that an obstruction is permanent, that no engineer can ever clear it, or that costs are zero.

Synthetic distinguishing state: a controlled Tobruk harbor has a separately established outstanding **one-level ship-blocking effect**; an eligible friendly engineer is present, the relevant stage is reached, no other action prerequisite blocks clearance, and a proposed separately accepted resource representation proves availability and authorized debit of 25 ammunition and 10 Stores. Under alternative 1, the proposed §55.26 action can remove one blocking level when all imported requirements and authoritative ordering are fulfilled. Under alternative 2, that same request remains unsupported/unknown pending the abstract rule. The representation and its debit are assumptions for this comparison, not facts supplied by Land-only §32.11. Do not call this an actually legal Graziani state or infer a witnessed engineer, stock, access, port control, stage or route.

The proposed state assumes source-specific ship-blocking applicability and a valid single remaining level; it does not decide whether San Giorgio's initial three levels are individually removable, whether clearance implies a particular new efficiency, or how the scenario's printed 7 reconciles with chart maximum 5. For a real case, resolve those source and lifecycle questions before changing state. A mine is a separate distinguishing case requiring §55.27's roll and limitations if that procedure is independently admitted.
