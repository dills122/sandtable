## Finding

No direct contradiction was found in the selected policies or the four result-order exclusions. The exclusions explicitly assume exclusive current possession of the same objective hex in the same terminal snapshot; §10.27's shared ZOC control does not itself disprove that **victory-specific** assumption. Nor do the source notes prove it. The result-order analysis predates and does not discuss §10.27, so its conditional table must not be reused as a source-proven unconditional exclusion. Its independent review adds Fort Maddalena as a second overlap for one existing row pair, not a fifth pair.

## Practical admission checks

1. Specify contextual control separately from current occupation, last occupation, historical taking, continued holding and retaining; define initialization, capture, departure, substitution, recapture and terminal event ordering. Do not make §10.26's movement-only ZOC neutralization a general victory-control rule.
2. For each relevant objective and source endpoint, check friendly-only ZOC, hostile-only ZOC, both, neither, known last occupier without current occupant, current qualifying combat occupant, and incomplete ZOC or history coverage. Expected outcomes follow only from the accepted predicate; unknown evidence that can change the result remains unknown.
3. Exercise all four conditional row pairs under the accepted same-hex and snapshot semantics, including Fort Maddalena as a second overlap for Italian tactical/Commonwealth decisive; separately test strategic rows, nonnested levels, true/false/unknown combinations and RUL-GRA-07's false-conjunct dominance. Synthetic checks do not establish reachable states.
4. Bind predicate and evidence versions to immutable terminal snapshot and replay. Complete endpoint, map, incoming ZOC and route coverage for relevant positive and negative proofs; define side-safe verdict timing and terminal behavior, including paired histories, before exposing hidden control or route facts.
