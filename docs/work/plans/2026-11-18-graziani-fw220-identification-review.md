# Review: FW220 identification

**Disposition:** No material source error found in the [identification note](2026-11-18-graziani-fw220-identification.md). Its `Fw. 200 C` comparison is a contextual candidate, not an explicit source equivalence or an official correction.

[Air](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf) p. 14 §§43.11 and .13 each print **FW220**; §43.11 lists Ju88D while §43.13 uses broader Ju88 wording. The [September erratum](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) p. 3 §43.12 separately prints **FW 220**, preserving the digits. It does not state 200. [Axis aircraft chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) pp. 1–2's German characteristics groups contain a single Focke Wulf manufacturer row, **Fw. 200 C**, with range 205 and Bomb 14; no FW220 row is visible there. The German bomber context supports further comparison but not a unique identity proof. The two rulebook occurrences are from the same source, not independent corroboration.

The negative appropriately applies to the two inspected chart groups and not all rosters, counters, editions or supplements. Detailed Air basing does not itself establish a Land-only action, force availability or state. I did not independently repeat the note's full OCR searches or examine other editions.

Visually checked Air p. 14, Axis chart pp. 1–2 and errata p. 3.
