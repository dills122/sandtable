# Fort Maddalena border effects: independent review

**Disposition:** Bounded original-source check supports the candidate's distinction: the blue checkered mark on C3019/C3020 is a strong national-boundary lead, while the inspected §10.21 and terrain chart do not list national borders as an independent ZOC blocker or cost. This is not proof of clear terrain, legal entry, a certified border effect, actual projection or compulsory combat. One wording correction: the September §8.37 track correction is general track-cost guidance, not a correction of one particular track.

## Source comparison

- [Map C](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 3, 600-dpi crop `(6400,10200,2200,1800)`: checkered blue blocks overlay the C3019/C3020 common vertical side; the Fort Maddelena and `3020` positions and surrounding grid support the paired cells, with fort labeling overprinted. No broad black escarpment band or blue major-river mark is visible on that side. The checkered overprint can hide other line detail: do not label the side source-certified clear. The [regional crop](2026-11-17-graziani-map-sheferzen-border.md) places similar checkered boundary between Libya and Egypt; the map key's `Border` graphic is a plain gray grid sample, not an explicit matching blue-checkered legend. National identity and cell-interior membership are interpretive, and I did not independently trace every connecting boundary segment.
- Land PDF/printed p. 18 §10.21 expressly excludes projection through all-sea, major river, lake, and escarpment hexsides, and into an adjacent hex the projecting unit could not enter from its present location. It does not list national borders independently. Subparagraph (c) still requires a unit- and direction-specific entry decision; it cannot be answered solely by absence of an escarpment. Separately establish which actual stacks project under §§10.11 and 10.15 and the phase-specific obligation under §§10.31–.32.
- Map PDF p. 1 terrain key distinguishes broad black escarpment, blue rivers, track, and other marks. Original map §8.37 Terrain Effects Chart contains rows for the enumerated terrain and features, not a national-border row. This is a bounded negative observation: a table omission does not establish no scenario-specific, unit-specific, weather or other source restriction, nor does it identify all ink concealed by the border marking. Land §8.19's west-of-Marble-Arch restriction is a separate location-specific rule, not a general national-border ban.
- [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 1 §8.37 says **tracks** halve terrain cost rather than costing 1 CP; that is a general track clarification, not a newly proved track on this particular side. I found no Fort Maddalena boundary-specific correction in the searched errata text; the search is not exhaustive for indirect or later authority.

The candidate's proposed no-additional-border-cost interpretation is reasonable to keep for review, with explicit uncertainty rather than an official rule claim. Its fixed C3019/C3020 placements are source facts, not proof that the optional Libya pool or any other legal setup changes produce a qualifying stack. Under a region policy placing C3019 in Libya, consider possible co-location subject to actual setup, stacking and source reconciliation. The marked shared side and force placements make an initial scenario check relevant, but do not by themselves prove a reachable eligible ZOC or attack at any point in six turns. Preserve unknown edge and projection inputs and test both directions, genuine impassable features, alternate setup and authorized observations.
