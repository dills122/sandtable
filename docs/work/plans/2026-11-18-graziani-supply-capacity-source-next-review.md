# Supply capacity comparison: independent review

**Disposition:** Accept the later-scenario comparison. Section 61.72(D)'s
limit applies to regular Supply Units in one named distribution at setup; it
does not establish a general on-map supply-counter cap.

- Desert Fox §61.72(D), continued from printed p. 30 onto p. 31, distributes
  nine regular and four dummy Supply Units among the listed locations. The
  restriction is no more than **two regular units at any one location**. The
  sentence does not state a combined two-counter cap including dummies, nor
  a post-setup capacity rule. Other stacking and placement rules remain
  separate questions.
- The same clause separately specifies one regular unit at Benghazi, five at
  Tobruk, and two each at Cairo and Alexandria. Tobruk's five have 40 ammo and
  15 fuel each; Cairo and Alexandria's have 40 ammo and 60 fuel each. The
  two-regular restriction belongs to the subsequent nine-regular/four-dummy
  distribution, not these separately stated allocations. None establishes
  that the unrestricted units may be added to a listed distribution location
  in another scenario.
- Section 61.72 explicitly concerns Air and Logistics abstracted, Land Game
  only, in Desert Fox. Section 60.92(C) is Graziani's separate Land-only
  setup: five regular units at Matruh, plus a separate group of three regular
  units and a group of three dummies subject to the map-D-west-of-Matruh
  placement instruction. The later two-unit distribution does not amend that
  setup. Owner-selected placement and fixed units must still be assessed under
  any applicable initial-excess rule; this comparison does not establish one.
- Land §32.14 visibly includes “off-map” in its three-unit clause and
  separately names unlimited Tripoli/Tunisia boxes. Section 61.72(D) gives no
  correction to that wording. Neither a printed general three-unit limit on
  ordinary on-map non-major-city hexes nor unlimited capacity there follows
  from the later setup.
