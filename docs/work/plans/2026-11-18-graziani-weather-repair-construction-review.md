# Land-only weather: repair and construction source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Reviewed [repair and construction note](2026-11-18-graziani-weather-repair-construction.md) against the original [Land rules scan](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 34–37, 41 and 43, SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`. Same scan, not an independent edition; no new errata search. No material transcription disagreement found.

- **Storm interruption and departure, §24.22:** Confirm no construction in a hex affected by either storm, and that stage does not count toward construction time; the storm alone does not stop the project entirely. Separately, if a construction participant leaves a hex during a sandstorm or rainstorm, construction stops completely and must restart. The case says “a hex” and does not separately spell out multi-hex attribution or event ordering. The note correctly preserves pause versus restart; requiring a particular project's hex, participant and weather linkage beyond that wording is an implementation decision, not extra printed detail.
- **Completion and CP, §§24.11–.12:** Confirm construction is initiated and undertaken in the Organization Phase's Construction Segment, with completion at the beginning of a succeeding Operations Stage's Construction Segment and the starting stage included. Spending any CP halts construction and the stage does not count. The cases do not expressly equate this halt to §24.22's restart. Whether a project already due to complete at segment start may complete when the new stage has a storm remains an interpretation, not settled by either clause alone.
- **Repair exception, §§22.12–.13, 22.27, 22.32, 22.36–.37:** Confirm phasing-player repair timing, storm restriction and Major Repair Facility exception, and separate stage-wide towing language for facility repair. The §22.37 Major City exception to temporary-facility restrictions refers to enemy ZOC, not storms; it does not turn a temporary facility into a major one. Other control, supply, towing and type restrictions remain independent. Sections 22.41 and 22.6–.7 give the cited destroyed-tank exceptions.
- **Building and dismantling, §§24.81–.88:** Confirm distinction between new temporary facility construction, major-city rebuilding sequence and dismantling. Section 24.86 assigns dismantling one Construction Segment and provides specific consequences upon dismantling; §24.88 bars use while dismantling. Those terms support the note's question about applying §24.22 but do not explicitly answer it. Building facilities and vehicle repair use distinct cases.
- **Land-only supply, §§24.13–.14, 24.21, 29.32 and 32.0/.11:** Confirm construction supply and hot-water language alongside abstract supply substitution. The survival of a separate availability prerequisite versus expenditure is a policy question; no stores or water balance follows automatically from citing those general rules. This review makes no new admission on repair or construction resource policy.

The note accurately identifies remaining interpretation and execution gates; the source text does not establish an actual storm, project or repair in GT1–6. Documentation only; no code, data, runtime changes or gameplay tests.
