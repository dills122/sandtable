# Initial contact: C3920 versus C3922

**Bounded conclusion:** The specifically listed 2nd Libyan Division at C3920
and Commonwealth force at C3922 are **not adjacent at printed setup**, so
neither projects its one-hex ZOC directly into the other's listed hex. This
pair alone cannot create a §10.31 compulsory-attack obligation before movement.
This is not a claim that the overall setup has no contact, or that no later
combat is reachable. Other player-placed forces and the movement sequence remain
unexamined for that wider question.

## Original setup and physical map

Scenario §60.31 (PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27) places the 2nd Libyan Division and
attached IX(L) tank battalion at **C3920**. Section 60.41 (same page) places
3rd Coldstream Guards, 1st King's Royal Rifle Corps (KRRC), 4th Royal Horse
Artillery and 7th Medium Artillery Regiment at **C3922**. The scenario also
lists forces “Anywhere in Libya,” so the two fixed rows do not exhaust possible
nearby Axis setup. It gives Italians initiative for turn 1 (§60.6, PDF p. 4),
which is not itself proof of phasing order or chosen actions.

On [Map C](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 3, inspect the
Fort Capuzzo–Halfaya Pass strip at high resolution. The C3920 and C3921 hexes
are consecutively numbered with a shared side; C3921 lies between C3920 and
the Halfaya Pass hex, C3922, rather than C3920 sharing a side with C3922.
The scenario §60.81 (PDF p. 4) separately identifies Halfaya Pass as C3922.
Parts of the city artwork and escarpment obscure the C3922 map number: the
scenario locator and neighboring grid geometry support the identity, not a
claim that every overprinted digit is legible. Both hexes lie on the same map
sheet; no seam interpretation is needed for the **non-adjacency** conclusion.
Land §10.0 defines ZOC as the six immediately surrounding hexes and §10.31
requires an enemy unit to exert ZOC on a friendly combat unit for this attack
obligation. Therefore neither named fixed stack can impose it on the other
while both remain in their setup hexes, regardless of their strength.

## Source strengths: threshold evidence, not a full ZOC ruling

The following readings concern printed starting TOE under the chart's `N`
notation, subject to any scenario-specific modification and accepted setup
organization. They do not prove actual later strength or full ZOC eligibility.

- [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 5,
  2nd Libyan Infantry Division, lists six infantry battalions with ID `x`,
  `N`, `D`: II, VI and VII of 3rd Libyan Regiment, XIV, XV and XVI of 4th.
  Its key on PDF p. 4 explains `N` as maximum TOE for non-tank/non-gun units
  and `D` as deployed at game start. Italian characteristics §4.46b, PDF p. 26,
  gives `x` maximum TOE 5 and offensive/defensive close assault 1/1. Those six
  entries yield **30 raw defensive close-assault points** if all six are
  present at their listed strengths. The chart's attached IX(L) tank battalion
  is separate and is not needed for this arithmetic.
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf) PDF
  p. 6, 7th Armored Division, lists 1st KRRC as ID `n`, `N`, `D`; PDF p. 11,
  Unassigned British Infantry-type Units, lists 3rd Coldstream Guards as ID
  `p`, `N`, `D`. Its key PDF p. 5 defines `N` and `D` in the same bounded
  fashion. Commonwealth characteristics §4.46a, PDF p. 22, gives both IDs
  maximum TOE 6 and defensive close assault 2. Their two infantry entries
  yield **24 raw defensive close-assault points** at those strengths, without
  assuming artillery contributes to that number.
- Land §10.15's threshold is **at least 10 raw defensive close-assault points**
  in the hex, while §§10.11–.14 and .21 separately govern organization/stacking,
  cohesion and permitted projection. The above sums exceed the threshold but
  do not alone establish either stack's projected ZOC, or that it could cross
  any particular hexside. In particular, map terrain and escarpments, accepted
  stacking/formation representation, initial cohesion and scenario overrides
  must be checked for any proposed adjacent contact. Printed maximum and
  source initial `N` are not a substitute for adjudicated current strength.

## What remains to prove and test

A later contact requires an actual accepted movement or setup choice, legal
movement/reaction timing and costs, target hex ownership, and both the source
ZOC predicate and the relevant intervening hexside. In particular, do not infer
that moving either side into C3921 creates reciprocal ZOC: inspect the actual
sides, escarpment/terrain restrictions and qualifying stack at that instant.
Then check the identity of the phasing side, §10.32 exceptions, any required
holding-off/assault/retreat and the September §10.3 erratum. Separate Axis
“Anywhere in Libya” placements could create another contact, subject to setup
rules; no such placement is asserted or excluded here.

A focused source-backed pair test should reject direct C3920–C3922 adjacency
and direct ZOC in both directions while still allowing each hex's ZOC to be
assessed independently toward certified neighbors. A separate later-state test
must use a visually certified relevant hexside and accepted movement, stack,
cohesion and ZOC data; unknown edge or setup state remains unknown rather than
becoming an automatic no-combat result. Test unauthorized enemy information
separately from §10.16's permitted ZOC disclosure. These are proposed tests,
not executed results; no runtime or readiness change is made here.
