# Abstract supply stacking: “off-map” scope

**Finding:** Land §32.14 expressly prints a five-Supply-Unit limit in a major-city hex and a three-unit limit in any other type of hex **off-map**, followed by unlimited supply in the Tripoli/Tunisia region boxes. The inspected sources do not expressly supply a general three-unit cap for an ordinary **on-map** non-major-city hex. Applying one remains a project interpretation, not a silently corrected transcription. The text also leaves which off-map spaces qualify as hexes and the scope of the regional-box exception to be decided for each case.

## Original source comparison

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 43 §32.0, describes its Land-only abstraction; §32.12 represents Supply Units by Supply Dump counters. §32.14 assigns zero stacking value, permits five such units in a major-city hex, prints **“any other type of hex off-map”** for the three-unit limit, and permits unlimited Supply Units in the boxes of the Tripoli/Tunisia region. `Off-map` is present in the source. The sentence does not expressly say “on or off map,” classify all off-map locations, or define dummy inclusion. Zero stacking value and a separate per-hex count are different quantities.
- Land p. 16 §§8.81, 8.87 distinguishes the named Tripoli/Tunisia boxes and smaller In Transit boxes and says there are no stacking restrictions in the Tripoli/Tunisia and In Transit boxes. This is consistent with an unlimited interpretation for supply in those boxes, but §32.14's phrase **boxes of the Tripoli/Tunisia region** does not itself enumerate every named or In Transit box. Decide membership and the relationship of the two cases explicitly; do not impose the three-unit cap on an identified region box while ignoring the printed exception.
- [Scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26 §§59.62, 59.65–.66: abstract Logistics uses Motorization Points and Supply Units. Initial placement may exceed stacking limitations only when unavoidable under scenario distribution instructions, with a temporary bar on additions and normal limits thereafter; §59.66(C) calls Tripoli/Tunisia boxes hexes **for purposes of that provision** when distributing optional transported Supply Units. That explicit purpose does not define every box or off-map facility as a §32.14 hex, or create a numeric ordinary on-map cap.
- Scenario PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29 §60.92(C) explicitly places five real Supply Units in Mersa Matruh D3714; it also lists three in one allowed Dump 1 hex west of Matruh, among other distributions. Five at Matruh do not independently prove its major-city classification, that three applies to ordinary on-map hexes, or that optional owner-selected loads are unavoidable. Under the separately selected [RUL-GRA-08](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md), if accepted map evidence classifies D3714 as a village and the project applies three on-map, the five-unit starting placement requires the §59.65 exception; the literal `off-map` reading does not itself prove that excess. Trigger, additional loads and subsequent compliance require the chosen policy.
- Scenario PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28 §60.5 labels certain air facilities **off-map** with coordinate-like references (for example Abu Seier `E3433`). That demonstrates `off-map` is not exclusively a synonym for the Tripoli/Tunisia large boxes in all scenario text. It does not by itself establish a physical on/off-map supply placement, stacking cap, eligible hex or adjacency at those facilities. Land §4.26's holding boxes instead represent on-map hexes that attract counters; a holding box is not automatically an off-map region or new hex.
- [Common charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) PDF p. 2 §9.4 tabulates unit stacking-point values, not a substitute for §32.14's Supply-Unit count. The original map terrain chart §8.37 concerns terrain stacking points. Neither inspected chart explicitly fills an on-map supply-counter count omission. September errata §32.0 warns the abstract rules were untested; text-search of the [September 1979 package](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) found no explicit §32.14 correction. This bounded search does not exclude indirect or later corrections.

## Competing interpretations and admission

1. **Literal scope:** apply the expressly stated five major-city and three other **off-map hex** limits, plus the unlimited region-box provision; treat the ordinary on-map non-major case as unspecified until a further rule or policy is admitted. This preserves the printed words but still needs a precise model of off-map hex/box identity and whether the major-city phrase has any unprinted geographical restriction.
2. **Expanded three-unit policy:** treat `off-map` as erroneous or read the three limit to include ordinary on-map non-major hexes. This makes an operational local cap and supports the existing bounded RUL-GRA-08 analysis, but is a source-hierarchy interpretation. The wording does not prove a typographical correction or automatic five-unit Matruh exemption.

A versioned rule must distinguish on-map vs off-map hex, actual box type and purpose, major-city identity, real/dummy counters, initial unavoidable versus player-created excess, no-addition and termination conditions, and transported motorization. Do not treat unknown map identity or rule scope as clear capacity. Check both ordinary on-map and explicitly modeled off-map examples, named region and transit boxes separately, five fixed Matruh units versus optional extras, and replay/side-safe dummy observations. These are synthetic tests, not passing coverage or claims of initial ownership.
