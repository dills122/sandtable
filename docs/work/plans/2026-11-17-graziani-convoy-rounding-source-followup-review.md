# Convoy rounding source follow-up: independent review

**Verdict:** Candidate’s qualified Air fallback is plausible, not source-mandated. No basis found in these passages to import it into Land-only play or to choose an Air 25% branch. Preserve both gaps as policy decisions.

- Air §47.6 explicitly replaces Land §32.6; §47.64 directs convoy attacks to Axis Naval Convoy Bombing rules, while §47.65 modifies their results. Air §41.67 gives upward rounding and groups all truck replacement points together, with Italian and German tanks and guns grouped separately. September §41.67 clarifies that the percentage applies to each listed cargo type. This referral makes §41.67 relevant, but does not expressly say how its detailed types map to §47.63’s abstract cargo list or whether its rounding survives for unmentioned results. Section 47.65 expressly changes rounding for its named results.
- Air §47.65 names 10%, 20%, and 30% or greater. Its 20% branch excludes depot supply units; its 30%-or-greater branch changes supply handling. Neither 5% nor 25% appears in those branches. Applying detailed rounding at either value, or extending either neighboring branch to 25%, requires interpretation. Section 41.66 describes the table result as in tens of percent; the companion [mode comparison](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-mode-rounding.md) identifies 5% and 25% cells. That wording adds uncertainty rather than settling those cells’ abstract treatment.
- Land §32.64 directs the table lookup and describes the percentage of supply units, motorization points and replacement points destroyed. Section 32.65 explicitly groups tank and gun losses by nationality, rather than individual equipment type; its 10% and 20%-or-greater branches specify distinct rounding and minimums. The 5% fractional treatment remains unstated in these passages. Table referral alone does not establish an Air detailed-rule fallback.
- September §41.67 addresses percentage allocation, not a general rounding default or an explicit §47.65 amendment. Search of the local September scan’s extracted text found no §32.6 or §47.6 entry. This is bounded evidence, not proof against other editions, overlooked wording, or later corrections.
