# 1st CCNN HQ blocker: independent source review

**Disposition:** Agree with the bounded unknown-weapon and unresolved-initial-gun conclusion. The printed capital `N` has a defined strength meaning, not a gun-model meaning. Do not turn the missing model into either a 65/17 assignment or a zero-point fact. This is a review of the identified scans and passages, not proof that no other authoritative source exists.

- [Axis OA chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 17, 1st CCNN Division: the division HQ row reads counter `1 CCNN`, ID `g`, TOE and weapon `N`, arrival `D`. The 219th and 233rd Legion HQ rows each explicitly list `1 × 65/17 Gun`; they are separate units, so neither entry identifies the division HQ's gun.
- Axis PDF p. 4, §4.44b key, TOE & Weapon System(s): for non-tank/non-gun units capital `N` states that the unit **possesses** the maximum TOE for its characteristics ID on arrival/deployment. The same key says tank and gun TOE points, including those assigned to other unit types, are always listed by number and weapon system. Thus `N` is stronger than a mere blank or permission: a literal reading with ID `g` suggests one point at deployment. But the required gun-specific listing is absent, so this does not unambiguously establish a particular initial gun point. `D` marks deployment on the map at the start of GT1's first Operations Stage.
- Axis PDF p. 26, §4.46b, HQ ID `g`: CPA 20, maximum TOE 1, and permission to assign one artillery TOE point; no gun model, with dashes in rating columns. Lowercase `n` is a separate infantry battalion-equivalent ID, not this row's capital OA `N`. PDF p. 27 legend defines maximum TOE as capacity and does not identify this HQ's model. Neither capacity alone nor omission of a model proves the point is empty.
- [Counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 2, Counter Section Nr. 3, Italian Blackshirt block: visually checked `1 CCNN`, `XX`, `(5)`, and `(1CN)` on the face. No gun model or explicit gun TOE count is visible. Counter face alone does not establish whether a point is assigned. I did not independently check the stacking-rule interpretation of `(5)` in this review.
- [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1, §4.44b's six OA corrections and §4.46 note: visual review found no specific correction for this HQ. Read extracted text for all four PDF pages, including p. 3 §60.31 concerning the duplicated XXI Corps artillery; found no specific HQ gun or starting-strength correction. Extraction may miss text; this is a bounded check of this document, not an exhaustive search of editions or later rulings.
