# Commonwealth Fleet: abstract supply and ship repair

**Source result:** The checked rules give two independently stated reasons not to charge supplies for the specified fleet-repair procedure. Land §32.2 exempts the Commonwealth Fleet from supplies **for that case's purposes**; §30.37 separately says no supplies or engineer are required to repair one ship Gun Rating level. The latter does not remove the six-consecutive-stage Alexandria requirement, sinking restriction or other fleet constraints. This is a bounded source comparison, not a completed repair or a universal exemption for every naval activity.

## Source comparison

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §32.0, supplies the Land-only profile without both detailed Air and Logistics and substitutes that section's abstract supply costs. The §32.2 introduction specifically excludes the **Commonwealth Fleet** and TOE Strength Points used to patrol from supply requirements for **that case's purposes**. It does not expressly grant an exemption to Axis shipping or establish that every transport, facility or naval procedure is free of every other cost.
- Land p. 42 §30.37 states that a ship repairs **one Gun Rating level** by remaining in **Alexandria for six consecutive Operations Stages**, with **no engineer and no supplies expended**; if it moves before those stages finish, all work must start again. This is a direct fleet-repair rule rather than an inference from §32.2. §30.36 independently says a ship whose entire Gun Rating is eliminated is sunk and unrepairable; below-half port restriction has its own printed threshold. Neither provision specifies restoration of AA rating, reset after new damage while remaining in port, or exact attack-versus-repair completion order. See [damage review](2026-11-17-graziani-aa-damage-followup-review.md) and [fleet sequence review](2026-11-17-graziani-fleet-sequence-review.md).
- Land p. 41 §30.16 separately says naval units need no fuel, ammunition or water within its stated requirement of **two Operations Stages in port per one in action**, and no more than **three consecutive OpStages at sea**. Section 30.22 says ship Gun Ratings used for offshore bombardment need no ammunition; §30.25, p. 42, requires the next **two Operations Stages in Alexandria** after offshore bombardment. These are separate fleet-operation facts, not evidence that §32.2 waives port/rest or attack restrictions. Section 30.18 describes Valletta as a port for “recovery”; §30.37 expressly names Alexandria for repairing a Gun Rating level, so the former does not by itself demonstrate a Valletta repair option.
- Scenario §60.45, PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, says initial warships' combat ratings are unaffected and need no repair; the retained first paragraph also prevents their movement until GT2 OpStage 1. The [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) p. 3 keeps that first paragraph and deletes the later fleet list. Initial sound condition does not establish a later damaged ship, eligible six-stage window or repair completion.

## Remaining scope

The compared clauses do not expressly settle whether two required Alexandria port stages can also count toward a separate six-stage repair, whether an attack or damage without departure resets repair progress, or whether repair completion precedes §32.73's named Tactical Naval Movement Segment. Count-as-overlap and separate-clock readings are possible interpretations, not findings that the source mandates either. The §32.2 exemption does not decide those temporal interactions. Establish actual ship identity, current damage, sunk status, location, action/rest history and applicable phase order before asserting a repair; do not debit a Supply Unit for §30.37's printed procedure merely by analogy to land repair.
