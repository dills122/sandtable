# Independent review: Axis Benghazi reinforcement diversion

**Disposition:** Candidate's six-turn schedule conclusion, §20.15 package and Benghazi chart values are supported by inspected originals. Its mode and port-capacity qualification is necessary. No material source error found; cap frequency and exact spatial classification remain unproved rather than implicit.

| Check | Source reading |
| --- | --- |
| Normal destination and trigger | Land §20.15, p. 31, sends Axis reinforcements to Tripoli normally. If there are no **Commonwealth land combat units west of Mersa Matruh (inclusive)**, Axis may divert forces to Benghazi. The parenthetical supports treating Matruh itself as included; exact hexrow/geometry, unit classification, opposing knowledge and off-map scope still require board and state rules. |
| Size and transport | §20.15 gives one battalion-equivalent combat unit **or** two company-equivalent combat units, and up to ten Truck or Motorization Points assigned to arrive with that unit or its parent. It prohibits diversion that exceeds stacking or when Benghazi is worse than Level 3, and says use affects its capacity in that Operations Stage. It does not explicitly settle how often the package limit resets, every possible formation breakup, or excess transport disposition. Do not make a replacement-point shipment a whole-unit reinforcement merely because it is in a convoy. |
| Port chart | Common chart §55.3 p. 15 lists Benghazi efficiency 3, 2 incoming and 5 outgoing stacking points, maximum **2,500 tons**; those capacities are in distinct units. Chart text says reinforcements are never affected by port capacity or current efficiency, and also says stacking-point and tonnage handling have no effect on each other. Land §20.15's diversion threshold and same-stage capacity effect therefore require a source-qualified reconciliation; no specific capacity debit amount or dimension should be invented. |
| Initial efficiency | Scenario §60.7 says ports start at listed efficiency except Tobruk; thus Benghazi starts at listed 3 under these sources. Level 3 meets the “worse than Level 3” threshold, but later damage or a different state must be checked before a later diversion. |
| Track and horizon | Axis Land Unit Reinforcement Schedule §4.43b, PDF pp. 2–3, begins with Italian XXV Corps Artillery Regiment at **GT9 OpStage 2**. No GT1–6 whole-unit entry is printed on that schedule. Scenario §60.22 ends Graziani after GT6 OpStage 3, and §60.37 says Axis receives reinforcements per track. This bounds the printed ordinary schedule; it does not prove no separate scenario override, optional rule, delayed arrival or replacement convoy exists. |
| Arrival identity | §20.11 defines reinforcement as new whole unit and §20.12 places its entry at the beginning of the Naval Convoy Arrival Phase. Replacement points and §32.61 abstract convoy items are separate categories. Scenario §60.31 Benghazi Garrison and §60.92(B) one Benghazi supply unit are initial setup, not evidence of a scheduled whole-unit arrival. |

**Errata:** September errata §55.11 says to follow the port chart for port handling; §55.3 discusses possible excess shipping to Tripoli. Neither explicitly amends §20.15's diversion exception. Candidate correctly presents the apparent interaction for adjudication rather than treating either source as erased. Review does not establish all Land-only uses of the detailed port chart or a precise port-capacity event order.
