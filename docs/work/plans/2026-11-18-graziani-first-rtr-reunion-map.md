# 1 RTR reunion: map geometry and unresolved seam

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Finding:** No source-verified legal route or stage-one reachability finding. On the explicitly conditional 33-column registration hypothesis, C3520 and D3612 are at least **25 adjacent-hex transitions** apart; if both parties move, their combined transitions before meeting are at least 25. This geometric bound does not certify the seam, establish a CP cost, or rule out other movement modes. Original sheet registration remains a blocking unknown.

C3520's label and two diagonal dashed crossings are visible; [C3520 six-side review](2026-11-17-graziani-c3520-six-sides-review.md) identifies them toward C3620 and C3419. D3612 has overlapping road/rail ink, village dot and brown slope bands; [D3612 six-side review](2026-11-17-graziani-d3612-six-sides-review.md) leaves railroad class and one slope direction uncertain. Neither endpoint review proves interior path sides or traversal.
- On the [33-column candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-seam-geometry-comparison.md), use last two digits as column and assign D's column 01 global column 34. C3520 is column 20 and D3612 column 45. Each ordinary adjacent-hex step changes that column by at most one, so their difference is `45 − 20 = 25`; triangle inequality gives the same lower bound on the sum of both parties' steps to any common hex. This ignores terrain, blockers and legal access and is not a shortest legal route. It assumes the candidate registration and ordinary adjoining hex movement throughout; it cannot bound rail, naval or other non-adjacent transport without separate rules.
- C's edge shows railway and ridge near C3633 and a dashed route near C3534; D's edge shows similar features near D3601 and D3501. Their ordering supports a registration lead, but clipping and overprint do not establish exact identity, side crossings or continuity. [Independent seam transcription](2026-11-17-graziani-seam-cd-independent.md) and [northern anchor follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-cd-seam-anchor-followup.md) retain these uncertainties. Land §4.1's `01xx` / `39xx` overlay wording conflicts at wording level with the candidate 33/34-to-01 geometry; the source does not itself establish the corrected transform. The three existing bounded contract pairs are project policy, not proof of an entire seam or this route.

## Timing and admission

Visually checked [Land §19.41](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 29. An assigned but unattached subsidiary may attach to its parent at any time in the same hex for one CP **each**; its example permits either party to move into the other's hex and attach immediately. This condition does not grant movement, certify the actual assignment or parent location, or determine movement cost. Section 19.42 separately governs non-assigned attachment. The 25-transition conditional bound cannot be added directly to the one-CP attachment charge: terrain, movement class and applicable cost rules remain unproved.

To establish a witness, first reconcile original overlay wording and crossing identity, then document every consecutive side, terrain and route feature, movement mode and costs, current CP, weather, fuel/supply, stacking, enemy occupancy and ZOC, contact and breakdown, and dated parent/constituent state. A negative reachability claim requires exhaustive allowed movement and meeting alternatives, including both moving parties and eligible transport; unresolved seam coverage cannot be treated as impassable. GT1 OpStage 2 remains only the earliest conditional subsequent arrival opportunity described in the timing review, not a demonstrated reunion.
