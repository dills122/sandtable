## Placement clauses applied to each row

Chart §4.43a PDF p. 4 defaults reinforcements to **any Cairo hex or hexes** unless specifically indicated otherwise. Units listed as arriving or returning as part of the **same parent formation** may share a hex with desired attachments and detachments, subject to stacking. Those **not assigned the same parent** must arrive in separate hexes if possible, without exceeding stacking to satisfy separation. This is a conditional rule, not a requirement to put a whole named brigade in one hex or permission to exceed stacking. Chart p. 4 identifies parent information in brackets and says New Zealand units except two railroad engineer battalions are assigned to 2nd NZ Division; interpret each OA/track relationship at the placement boundary rather than silently equating assignment, attachment, and co-location.

Each row below comes from chart §4.43a PDF p. 3. “Default” means the printed row gives no special entry destination; it does **not** establish that an actually eligible Cairo hex exists, is controlled, has room, or is selectable under all other rules.

## Exceptions and remaining checks

Land §20.14 gives Cairo as the normal Commonwealth entry, explicitly names Layforce at GT19 and Tiger Convoy at GT32 as Alexandria exceptions, and permits a **returning withdrawn unit** at Alexandria or Cairo at the Commonwealth player's option, subject to stacking. Chart p. 4 gives the same returning-unit option. Neither named later exception nor a printed return marker appears in the six schedule entries above. This does not prove no other scenario override, unscheduled return or category exists. September errata p. 1 §4.43a adds GT28/2 and GT64/2 entries and corrects a GT76 reference; it does not expressly alter these six rows. Errata §4.44b item 3 changes French Motor Marine company to `D`; scenario §60.41 places that company at C3926, a separate initial-setup case, not an added Cairo arrival under the corrected reading.

Scenario §60.47 directs use of the reinforcement track. Section 60.22 starts GT1/1 and ends after GT6/3. Land §20.12 places reinforcements at the beginning of Naval Convoy Arrival, charges no CP for disembarkation or the initially placed hex, and permits movement in the arrival stage subject to other rules. These clauses do not settle city control, stack capacity, the meaning of “if possible,” attachment timing, or a legal next action. Cairo's city-hex membership and map coverage require separate evidence; no coordinate is selected here. A no-arrival conclusion for other stages also requires checking other categories and scenario exceptions.

Useful implementation checks after source and policy admission: compare legal co-location of same-parent arrivals against distinct-parent separation with enough eligible hexes, then insufficient eligible hexes without exceeding stacking; retain unknown parent, city membership, capacity or control as unknown rather than inventing a placement. Test GT2 artillery separately from the GT6 brigade under the selected timing ruling, replay the owner choice, and preserve side-authorized observations. These are proposed tests, not executed results.
