# Independent review: construction stacking erratum

**Result:** Source reading supported; no material correction found. Section 24.15 is one printed sentence containing both the one-project limit and general stacking clause. Errata §24.15 calls §24.12 an exception to the last sentence without identifying a clause. Section 24.12's explicit road/track stacking sentence supports the note's narrow interpretation; it does not expressly waive the separate project limit or general hex stacking.

Section 24.12 separately halts construction after CP expenditure and excludes that Operations Stage from construction time. Section 24.22 excludes a storm-affected stage without automatically stopping the project, but requires a restart if a participant leaves during the storm. The [note](2026-11-18-graziani-construction-stacking-erratum.md) keeps these distinct and does not assert that a CP halt resets accumulated progress.

Section 32.9 directs players to ignore road/track-only stacking-point restrictions in §9.0. Section 32.0 applies the section when Air and Logistics are excluded; scenario §60.92 labels that option Land Game Only. Thus the cited §9.33 road/track limit is independently displaced in this mode, while §9.14 and §9.3 terrain-hex limits remain subject to their own wording and exceptions. This establishes a mode-level rule, not a reachable project, valid setup, or universal exemption from all stacking.
