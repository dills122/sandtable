# Review: AR-06 arriving trucks and stacking

**Disposition:** Schedule values and §9.29 distinction are supported; the proposed road-space comparison needs a material Land-only qualification. No scenario placement or runtime behavior established.

## Finding

[Source note](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-b-source-next.md) conditions its comparison on conversion and movement, but does not identify Land §32.9. That rule directs players in the abstract Land-only rules to ignore the **road and track stacking-point restrictions** of §9.0. Thus §9.29's attached-versus-convoy road-space distinction is a valid reading of the general rule, but it does not by itself provide a road-stacking discriminator for the Land-only scenario. Separate a detailed-mode comparison from any Land-only test; identify §32.9's waiver explicitly and preserve ordinary hex stacking and other independent restrictions. This does not decide how scheduled trucks convert or whether an unattached convoy exists in the abstract mode. See also [road-stacking review](2026-11-17-graziani-road-stacking-review.md).
