## Findings

No material contradiction found between the synthesis and the three independent reviews. Its source conclusions retain the key limits:

- **CCNN:** Capital `N` means maximum TOE on deployment, not a weapon code. The [review](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-ccnn-hq-review.md) treats the literal reading as affirmative evidence for one point, while the required weapon-specific listing is absent. The synthesis preserves that tension and leaves both model and initial assignment unresolved; neither zero nor 65/17 follows. This corrects the handoff's characterization of `N` as an unidentified weapon code. Review inspected the same scans, not another edition; its scenario passage was not independently checked.
- **CV33:** [Review](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-aresca-cv33-review.md) supports unparenthesized 1 offensive / 2 defensive per TOE point, including light tanks. Nine eligible, unchanged XXI points yield 18 raw defensive points, not automatic ZOC. HQ participation requires a separate brigade-equivalence and restriction determination; actual-combat adjustments do not automatically alter the raw ZOC threshold. Synthesis preserves these distinctions and does not claim implementation is complete.
- **Giarabub:** [Review](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-giarabub-motorization-review.md) supports printed CPA `8+` and motorized unit classification, while finding no specific initial pool or effective initial CPA. General second/third-line resources exist; `unresolved-no-scenario-pool` is only shorthand for an unidentified unit-specific initial assignment. Synthesis preserves those limits and does not equate missing assignment with zero.

Two scope distinctions could be made more explicit for an implementation reader:

1. Handoff says Giarabub is an open verification item **not currently a `ReadinessBlockers()` entry**. Synthesis title groups all three as implementation blockers, though its final sentence avoids claiming any blocker has cleared. Treat the title as task grouping, not evidence that all three are current readiness entries.
2. Giarabub review distinguishes §32.54's **abstract-logistics** assignment priority from §8.97 attachment timing and does not reconcile these with first-line versus second/third-line setup. Synthesis mentions priority and separate trucks, but an implementation must not assume priority itself establishes attachment or a specific effective CPA. The source check additionally derives one initial TOE from OA `N` and maximum 1; synthesis lists maximum 1 without asserting initial strength. That omission does not negate the motor-pool conclusion.

Handoff's requested deliverables are met at source-research level: bounded unknown for CCNN, positive CV33 weapon rating, and qualified Giarabub recheck. They do not themselves establish runtime readiness, authorize a particular unknown-state policy, or exhaust later editions and rulings. No runtime or pinned data changed; no gameplay tests run.
