# Fort Maddalena artillery stack: independent source review

**Disposition:** the [candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-fort-maddalena-stack-candidate.md)'s printed unit values and nominal arithmetic check out. Neither example is a demonstrated legal setup or ZOC: §10.11's home-garrison aggregation, §10.15's treatment of gun position and usable ratings, regional membership and actual player choice remain unresolved. Apply the Forward/Back qualification to the **garrison itself** as well as to the two optional artillery units.

## Source and arithmetic checks

Visually inspected [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27: §60.31 fixes Fort Maddalena garrison at C3019 and lists 1/1AR and XXI/XXVI Corps artillery in `Anywhere in Libya`, with a separate duplicate/punctuation issue addressed in [corps-artillery review](2026-11-17-graziani-corps-artillery-review.md). Section 59.2's same-starting-hex organization option does not place or attach the optional units automatically.

[Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 14 lists FtMad as `q`, `U@2`, `D`; §4.46b p. 26 gives `q` close assault 3/3. OA p. 16 lists 1/1AR with three 75/27, XXI Corps with three 75/27, six 100/17 and three Light AA, and XXVI Corps with nine 75/27 and three Light AA, all `D`. The OA note calls the raggruppamento battalions independent, with no formal HQ. Axis §4.48 p. 29 gives 75/27 close assault 1/1 and 100/17 1/0. Assuming rated points are available and counted, `2×3 + 3×1 + 3×1 = 12` and `2×3 + 9×1 = 15`; both are **nominal raw arithmetic**, not established §10.15 results. The checked [counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf) PDF p. 1 shows FtMad `G`, `II`, `1` and the 1/1AR, XXI and XXVI artillery counters with one-point marks. Its scan is not an independently authenticated physical edition.

## Position, stacking and setup gates

Land §12.11–.12 p. 20 assigns each artillery unit involved in combat Forward or Back at the Combat Segment boundary, with the entire unit in the same position; §12.18 permits Forward artillery to participate in anti-tank or close assault if it has necessary ratings **and ammunition**. §12.16 additionally says a **heavy-weapons unit** with barrage capability whose guns are Back is treated entirely Back and cannot participate in anti-armor or close assault. Land §9.16(a) p. 17 explicitly calls the Fort Maddalena garrison the Italian heavy-weapons battalion. Thus the candidate's conditional `6` garrison raw term also needs the accepted relationship between §10.15's ZOC check, position/timing, ammunition and §12.16; do not assume it is always available merely because optional artillery is Forward. Light AA's parenthesized ratings, gun/AA abstraction and anti-tank Up Front rule likewise need separate treatment. The cited position cases govern combat; their exact temporal application to a pre-combat ZOC test is not resolved by their wording here. Neither automatically zero nor automatically include those terms.

Land §9.16(a) explicitly gives the Fort Maddalena garrison zero stacking at its assigned village and one elsewhere. The candidate's `0+1=1` and `0+1+1=2` are correct **under the selected effective-home aggregation**; §10.11 does not expressly choose effective rather than printed values for its more-than-one test. Preserve the [mixed-stack alternatives](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-mixed-stack-zoc.md) and independently reviewed ambiguity. Section 9.24's artillery-one rule has its own exceptions. The ordinary clear-hex limit, national-region inference, allowed optional locations, stacking, any parent representation and source identity all require admission before either setup is a legal witness. In particular, co-located independent counters are not automatically attached, and attached constituents must not be double-counted.

C3019's location west of the blue checkered boundary supports, but does not officially certify, Libya membership. Its C3019–C3020 side remains marked; §10.21 projection and unit-entry legality are separate, as are actual placement, current state, opponent, stage and combat obligations. Both nominal raw figures and a candidate >1 stack are necessary inputs under particular interpretations, not proof of a starting ZOC or an attack.
