# Independent review: Land-only road and track stacking

**Disposition:** The proposed waiver and synthetic six-versus-seven examples are supported, with the stated terrain, occupancy, mode and stacking assumptions. I found no material source error. Two wording refinements below keep interpretation distinct from printed language and prevent accidental application to other modes.

| Check | Source-supported result |
| --- | --- |
| Mode | Land §32.0 p. 43 explicitly applies when playing Land **without the Air and Logistics Games**. It is not demonstrated for every mode missing only one of those games. Scenario §60.92 labels this configuration Land Game Only. |
| Waiver | §32.9 p. 45 says ignore road and track **only** stacking-point restrictions listed in §9.0. §9.33 p. 17 applies a five-point road/track space limit when a motorized unit moves through friendly units on that road/track, and says a unit ending movement after road/track entry counts for later motorized units. Its move-around instruction enforces that limit; §8.34 also refers directly to §9.33. Both should not silently reinstate the waived five-point threshold. |
| Unit breakup | §9.34 prohibits breaking up a unit **in order to avoid exceeding the limits of 9.33**. Candidate's conclusion that this road-limit-specific purpose does not recreate a waived limit is a reasonable application, not a separately printed sentence saying §9.34 is deleted. Other attachment, movement and stacking constraints still apply. |
| Capacity examples | Terrain chart §8.37 has clear-hex stacking limit **6**; road and track rows show **5** with note 7 limiting it to units/truck points actually on road. Hence 4+2 fits clear-hex capacity after waiver; 5+2 cannot stop in that hex absent a relevant exception. Passing through without stopping is a different question. The occupied points must be road users in the counterfactual ordinary-rule comparison; values are synthetic, not an observed setup. |
| Wadi / major river | Land §8.41 bars rainstorm wadi crossing except road traversal and gives +2 CP. Chart note 10 explicitly also permits railroad, treated as road for this purpose, at two additional CP. Note 11 says a **motorized** unit may cross major river only by road or railroad, with no additional CP or breakdown points for the river crossing. The latter does not waive ordinary hex/road movement cost and should not be extended to all infantry. |
| Tracks / escarpment | Errata §8.37 says tracks halve terrain cost and printed fixed 1 CP is wrong. Chart note 8 addresses the exception for a vehicle descending an escarpment; §8.42 gives +8 CP and six breakdown points, and forbids vehicles moving up. These are independent of road stacking. Land §32.55 exempts motorization points from breakdown, not every vehicle they transport. |

**Additional scope:** Hex stacking remains governed by §§9.14, 9.3–.32 and applicable exceptions; movement and retreat cannot be certified solely from this waiver. §9.29's first-line truck and convoy wording is scoped by §32.0's replacement of trucks with motorization in Land-only play. §8.33 requires connected road/track hexes and a crossing side; a drawn feature alone does not establish an accessible legal route. Rainstorm restriction is for affected map sections, not a global weather assumption. Road crossing does not independently prove enemy ZOC projection or its negation. Map D road-edge assertions in the proposal are referenced to its separate map audit; this review did not independently rescan those two edges.
