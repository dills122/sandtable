# Axis XXI Corps artillery duplication

**Result:** Scenario §60.31 lists XXI Corps artillery twice in its “Anywhere in Libya” group; September errata §60.31 explicitly identifies that duplication. Axis OA and counter sheet independently support **one XXI Corps Artillery Regiment identity**, not two starting units. XXVI Corps Artillery is a distinct source identity; its unqualified scenario label has strong OA/counter support but the source's adjacent missing punctuation deserves preservation. The separate repeated `4/1AR` is not expressly resolved by this erratum.

## Exact scenario and correction

Scenario PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.31's relevant line prints, with its punctuation and line break normalized only for readability:

> Anywhere in Libya: 1/1AR; 2/1AR; 3/1AR; 4/1AR; XXI Cp; XXVI Cp 4/1AR; XXI Cp (Ar).

The first `XXI Cp` is unqualified; the second explicitly has `(Ar)`. The September 1979 errata p. 3 §60.31 says under “Anywhere in Libya” the XXI Corps Artillery is listed twice. Apply that correction to the two XXI references as **one underlying regiment**, preserving both source occurrences and the correction in provenance. Errata does not say that XXI should be replaced by a different corps or supply an additional counter. The exact placement remains the owner's legal choice **anywhere in Libya**, not a preselected hex, and depends on the actual map, setup and stacking rules.

There is no separator between `XXVI Cp` and the second `4/1AR` in the scan. The `4/1AR` label also appears earlier. September §60.31 identifies the XXI duplication only; it does not explicitly correct or explain this separate repeat or the punctuation. Do not automatically delete, create a second battalion, or reinterpret the latter as an XXVI formation child. Preserve it as a separate source issue.

## OA and counter cross-check

Axis Italian OA §4.44b PDF p. 16, **Unassigned Gun Units**, lists these separate rows:

| Identity | Counter abbreviation | ID | Initial OA composition | Marker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| XXI Corps Artillery Regiment | `XXI Cp` | `kk` | 3 × 75/27 Gun; 6 × 100/17 Howitzer; 3 × Light AA | `D` |
| XXVI Corps Artillery Regiment | `XXVI Cp` | `kk` | 9 × 75/27 Gun; 3 × Light AA | `D` |
| X Corps Artillery Regiment | `X Cp` | `kk` | 4 × 100/17 Howitzer | `D` |
| 4th Battalion, 1st Artillery Raggruppamento | `4/1AR` | `mm` | 3 × 100/17 Howitzer | `D` |

The OA key makes `D` deployed at GT1 OpStage 1; §60.31 gives actual scenario location and correction. OA note `c` says the raggruppamento is administrative with no formal HQ for its independent battalions. X Corps artillery has separate scenario placement at Soluch; its OA note `d` says stationed there but not a garrison unit. Other corps artillery identities, including XXII and XXIII at Tripoli, do not become aliases of XXI merely because their labels look similar.

Original counter scan PDF p. 1, **Counter Section Nr. 2**, lower half, right-hand block, artillery row immediately after the row containing `VI Aos`: individual printed counters read `XXICp`, `XXIICp`, `XXIIICp`, `XXVCp`, `XXVICp`, then others including `X Cp`. Each of XXI and XXVI has the artillery symbol, regiment-level mark and printed stacking value **1**. The row contains one of each identity; the visible scan supports the OA crosswalk, not a second XXI counter. The physically adjacent `XX Cp` has Benghazi garrison marking and is a distinct identity. Counter-sheet content does not itself specify a player's chosen deployment hex or prove all setup restrictions have been checked.

For Land-only play, §32.83(3) removes assigned **heavy** AA TOE from Italian `kk` artillery. The OA's stated **light** AA is not removed by that clause. Preserve gun models, light-AA type and source identity separately; neither this correction nor the counter label supplies the whole rules needed for firing, transport or later losses.

## Acceptance vectors

1. Parse two XXI setup mentions, link them to a single XXI OA/counter identity and the explicit erratum; reject duplicate independent placement, while retaining both source occurrences as evidence.
2. Preserve XXI, XXVI, X and 4/1AR as four distinct identities with their own OA composition; never merge XXVI into XXI or treat their similar `Cp` suffix as unique identity. The owner-selected valid Libya hex and stacking must be recorded, not invented.
3. Keep the second `4/1AR` and missing separator as unresolved source text. Do not extend the XXI erratum to claim an explicit second correction. A valid independent battalion counter is not proof that two copies should enter play.
4. Under Land-only AA rules preserve the printed Light AA in both `kk` corps regiments, distinguishing it from heavy AA and from the gun TOE. Verify replay and hidden setup views without leaking the chosen opponent placement.
