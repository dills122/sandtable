## Findings

1. **LA-09: narrow the answered prohibition.** “Loaded-point transfer prohibition” may imply a blanket prohibition on moving or reassigning the point. [Loaded-motorization review](2026-11-17-graziani-loaded-motorization-review.md) instead identifies a prohibition on transferring the **initial supplies to a Supply Unit**, plus a conditional rule allowing a cargo-bearing point to detach only while remaining attached to a qualifying subsidiary combat unit. The latter restriction lasts only while it carries initial supplies; ordinary attachment legality remains separate. Name these different subjects and keep other transfers/disposal unresolved.
2. **LA-02: bound the schedule claim by side and type.** The row's “six-turn conditional replacement schedule” follows a both-sides incoming-allowance statement, but its [replacement review](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-replacements-review.md) specifically checks Commonwealth replacement planning and its two named truck-bearing reinforcement groups; its [supply schedule review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-schedule-review.md) concerns abstract **Supply Unit** tables, not both sides' truck or Motorization Point arrival schedules. Specify the reviewed Commonwealth scope, and retain a concrete check of Axis and other applicable medium-truck arrival capability and the abstract crosswalk. Do not infer that the supply-unit schedule establishes motorization arrivals.
3. **LA-13 and LA-07: link later reviewed discriminators.** LA-13 correctly keeps same-hex trace unresolved, but [same-hex source review](2026-11-18-graziani-same-hex-draw-source-review.md) adds a directly checked detailed-logistics same-hex fuel analogy, which does not itself resolve §32.16. LA-07 cites the C3922 source note without its later [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-a-source-3-review.md). Add those links to make the best bounded evidence reviewable; neither closes the relevant policy or state questions.
