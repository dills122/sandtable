# §32.35: coastal supply mission capacity

**Source result:** The Graziani setup supplies a concrete capacity distinction. Axis coastal ships `A`, `B`, `C` each print **1,000 tons**, and `D` prints **2,000 tons**; §60.35 makes all Axis coastal counters available at Tripoli, initially empty under §59.54. Land §32.35 requires **2,000 tons per Supply Unit** and permits two ships on one mission. Thus `D` alone meets the printed tonnage of one unit; `A` alone does not; `A` plus `B` totals exactly 2,000 tons **if** the two-ship clause permits pooling one unit's required tonnage. This is a strong textual interpretation, not proof of a completed legal shipment. A real/dummy distinction, mission composition and detailed-mode carryover still require decisions.

## Sources and qualifications

- [Land §32.0, §§32.18, 32.31–.35](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43: §32.0 expressly concerns Land without Air **and** Logistics. §32.35 permits Axis coastal shipping, requires 2,000 tons per Supply Unit and says two ships may be used on one mission. It does not separately specify how to divide a Supply Unit across two holds or whether two fully loaded ships can carry two Supply Units in that same mission. Section 32.18 says a dummy moves in the same fashion as a real unit, with reduced motorization; §32.34 separately makes real/dummy explicit for Commonwealth rail. This supports checking the same sea tonnage for dummies, but does not expressly settle how an empty decoy is represented as ship cargo or whether loading itself reveals identity. Section 32.32's minimum-motorization sentence and §32.37's air-specific detachment create a separate interaction with sea movement; do not silently waive or impose it.
- [Counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 3, Section 5 Axis block: ship `A` and `B`/`C` are marked 1,000 tons and `D` 2,000 tons. [Scenario §60.35 and §59.54](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed pp. 27 and 26 / PDF pp. 3 and 2: all Axis coastal counters are available and start at Tripoli; ships begin scenarios unloaded. §60.92(B) supplies initial Land-only Supply Units, including Tripoli units. Co-location and tonnage are initial ingredients, not evidence an OpStage or loading occurred.
- [Detailed Logistics §§56.31–.35](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), printed pp. 24–25 / PDF pp. 28–29: each coastal counter shows capacity, movement-only CPA 50 and each sea hex costs one; movement is only in Truck Convoy Phase; ships cannot be attacked from air or land or enter neutralized or enemy-occupied ports. Loading at phase start costs 5 CP, unloading costs 5 CP, and a ship carries only one type of cargo at once; ships may unload at successive ports within CPA and require no fuel. September [errata §56.31](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 3 adds four Tripoli–Tunis boxes per stage. Section 58.1's coastal-attack exception is for **Land plus Logistics without Air**, not independent proof of Land-only treatment. Whether and how §56's detailed port, phase, single-cargo and movement rules apply to abstract Supply Units must be selected from applicable source hierarchy; a Supply Unit contains both ammo and fuel, so treating it as a detailed cargo type is an interpretation. The printed §32.35 two-ship provision does not itself provide loading timing or a destination.

## Conditional distinguishing cases

| Input | Capacity-only implication; remaining gate |
|---|---|
| One real unit, `D` | 2,000 = 2,000. One ship suffices numerically; require actual unit/ship location, eligibility, phase, origin/destination and route. |
| One real unit, `A` | 1,000 < 2,000. Insufficient by itself; no delivery. |
| One real unit, `A` + `B` | 1,000 + 1,000 = 2,000; two-ship permission supports pooled capacity, but identify assignment, any split and destination policy first. Compare with one unavailable or differently located ship. |
| Two real units, `D` + `A` | 3,000 < 4,000. Total tonnage insufficient even if pooling is permitted; no two-unit delivery from this pair. |
| Two real units, `D` plus another hypothetical 2,000-ton ship | 4,000 = 4,000; distinguishes total capacity from unresolved number-of-units-per-mission interpretation. Hypothetical ship is not a starting-counter claim. `D` plus `A` plus `B` also reaches 4,000 but uses three ships and does not establish one mission is allowed. |
| One dummy instead of a real unit | Compare identical ship and mission state under the selected interpretation of §§32.18/.35, without inventing real ammo/fuel. Maintain side-safe action, error and event views until an authorized discovery; a selected disclosure can produce later visible differences. |

These are arithmetic and proposed test vectors, not tested gameplay, a route, port-capacity determination, a lawful split or a six-turn delivery witness. Select mission/capacity, dummy, motorization and mode semantics before admitting behavior; check phase, loading, control, port state and reachability independently. Separate coastal ships from strategic convoy lanes and tonnage.
