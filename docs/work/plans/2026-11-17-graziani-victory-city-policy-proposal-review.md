# Graziani victory city policy: independent proposal review

**Disposition:** Defensible as a conditional planning choice, not source-mandated policy or implementation admission. The proposal preserves the material ambiguities and correctly separates capability from actual supply. No runtime changes or tests. This review compares the proposal with the cited source transcriptions and independent source review; it did not independently inspect the original scans or conduct a new source search.

## Findings and prerequisites

1. **Witness binding is a choice.** The reported §60.81 wording supports a combat unit holding the selected city objective that can be supplied by convoy; binding both properties to that holder is a reasonable direct reading. Neither that reading nor “any hex” specifies a formal identity predicate across time. A different capable unit on another city hex does not establish the holder's capability under the proposed policy. Keep the separate-witness alternative and temporal identity unresolved until explicitly ruled on.
2. **Membership and control are independent.** The location summary offers candidate city hexes, not a complete, playable city-membership or topology certificate. Neither its marks nor the distance chart's representative endpoints establish victory eligibility, control, legal convoy endpoints or a route. Resolve whether occupation and control differ, what counts as holding a selected hex, and who controls relevant origins and endpoints. Do not infer Commonwealth source semantics from the Italian parenthesis.
3. **History and timing remain material.** Taking and holding may involve prior events, substitution, loss, recapture and an evaluation boundary. A capable holder at a terminal snapshot alone does not prove the required history. Specify what evidence establishes each predicate, including continuity and allowed substitution, and capture the terminal boundary and cleanup ordering before evaluating it.
4. **Negative proof needs completeness.** Missing map, route, control or history evidence is unknown, not a failed conjunct. A verified disqualifying fact can establish a particular conjunct false only under the accepted predicate and sufficiently complete evidence for that fact; it does not certify unrelated predicates. Any wider result-composition policy must specify how known false and unknown interact and avoid deriving a final result from incomplete coverage.
5. **Terminal behavior is an observation.** Privacy comparison must include whether and when play stops, state freezes, a verdict becomes available, retries occur, or later actions are accepted, as well as offers, events, identifiers and rejection categories. A hidden-state-dependent unresolved/terminal transition can disclose opponent information even if rejection text is generic. Define authorized terminal disclosure and observation-equivalence through subsequent behavior; if no safe protocol is established, keep the externally observable evaluation and terminal behavior independent of hidden inputs. Internal results and evidence require side-specific filtering.

## Discrimination and evidence limits

The proposed one-city/one-hex, same-holder, non-chart-hex and temporal contrasts are useful synthetic policy discriminators, not proofs of reachable play. Add paired histories with identical permitted observations but different hidden capability or control, including one known-false conjunct paired with an unknown conjunct, and compare terminalization, retry and subsequent action behavior. Expected outcomes await accepted predicates and disclosure policy.
