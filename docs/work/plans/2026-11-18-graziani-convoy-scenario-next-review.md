# Starting training and convoy snapshot: source review

**Disposition:** Supports [scenario comparison](2026-11-18-graziani-convoy-scenario-next.md)'s conditional treatment and phase distinction, with one wording qualification below. No eligible trainer, movement, convoy or attack is established. Informed second reading of the same scans, not an independent edition.

- **Instructor duration needs a qualification.** Land §17.35 says an eligible combat battalion must be present in the hex with the training unit **during each Operations Stage of Training**. The original candidate said “throughout each training OpStage”; that is a plausible stricter interpretation but the quoted case does not expressly say continuous or specify the moment of checking. Keep intermittent presence, departure and return, and exact training interruption/credit as unresolved timing questions. The case also requires designated Basic Morale, at least **one-third** assigned TOE strength, and the stated trainee/instructor type conditions. This review originally misstated the strength threshold as one-half; the [training follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-training-source-next.md) corrected it.
- Scenario §60.41 lists the 6th Australian Division in training at Cairo and/or Helwan E1430, less the 19th Australian Brigade and two listed field artillery units. That status alone does not identify an eligible co-located instructor, complete a training stage, or determine current shell status. Land §17.32 includes Cairo and Helwan among training areas; §17.36 makes training optional in general, without expressly resolving cancellation of this particular initial scenario status.
- Land §5.2's Training Segment forbids units beginning or continuing training from voluntarily moving during the remainder of that OpStage, except Reaction. §17.33 says any CP expenditure in an Operations Stage interrupts training. Whether a particular reaction is legal and how interrupted progress is retained are independent; neither passage expressly makes all six training stages consecutive or resets prior credit.
- §5.2 places Organization before Naval Convoy Arrival and then A/B movement/combat phases. §32.63 places attacks on arriving Axis convoys in that arrival phase; Axis chart §32.66 uses non-shell Allied division positions and its separate qualifying-division condition. Land §§9.26–.27 address shell status through actual qualifying attached subunits, not the fact of training by itself. A later lookup requires the actual stage snapshot and any earlier changes, not an assumed initial pair or a newly invented convoy. The opening-stage preamble remains a separate ambiguity.
