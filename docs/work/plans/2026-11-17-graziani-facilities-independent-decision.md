## Tobruk port: no selected value

Scenario §60.7 explicitly sets initial efficiency **7** and says San Giorgio partially blocks the harbor. Chart §55.3 lists **5**, with a note that blockage makes initial efficiency lower; Air §55.25 specifies **three levels** of reduction. The blind entries agree on these values. Alternatives include scenario override 7, which exceeds chart maximum and needs an explicit capacity formula; or 5 minus 3 = **2**, which disregards the literal scenario value. No source here establishes which number is a misprint or how to reconcile them, so neither is selected. Do not normalize 7 to 5, apply the three levels to 7, or silently treat community preference for 2 as authority. Clearance costs and whether detailed §55.26 survives §32 abstraction are separate open decisions.

This is conditionally consequential in Land-only: §32.36 permits Commonwealth sea movement of one supply unit per Operations Stage to specified ports including Tobruk only if destination permits at least 50% listed capacity. Assuming 2 of a maximum 5, with no other modifiers, capacity is 40%, so destination fails; a hypothetical value permitting at least 50% passes that threshold, subject to all other rules. Commonwealth control and actual six-turn reachability are not established by this synthetic example. Future decision tests must cover initial capacity and bounds, threshold just below/at/above 50%, control, damage, clearance timing/cost, reinforcements separately, and deterministic replay. Do not claim 7's percentage until its meaning is decided.

## Tobruk repair facility: initial type only

Scenario §60.33 gives Italians a temporary facility at Tobruk and Benghazi; Land §22.31 calls Tobruk major for whoever controls it. **Proposed `graziani-facility-start-v1`:** at initial Italian-controlled Tobruk use exactly one temporary facility, taking the specific scenario setup over the general type list. Do not create a simultaneous major facility. Benghazi starts temporary; Tripoli starts major. This is a scenario precedence choice, not proof the general text is erroneous.

Alternative: §22.31's location rule makes Tobruk major from setup, or a temporary facility coexists with an inherent major one. Neither coexistence nor an automatic conversion has explicit support in these texts. **Control-change type remains unresolved**: a setup-only override followed by a major facility for a new controller and a continuing temporary classification are both plausible. Do not infer a facility capture/conversion event, construction cost, or owner-independent type from initial choice. Block dependent control-change behavior until independently decided. Major/temporary selection affects §22.34 table column, weather (§22.36) and enemy ZOC (§22.37), not port efficiency.

Tests: initial one-facility count and type in each named hex; compare major and temporary repair columns and storm behavior under otherwise identical legal synthetic states; no duplicate attempts; capture/control transition explicitly unsupported pending decision; distinguish port state from facility state; save/replay and ruleset mismatch rejection. §60.31 places Italian forces at Tobruk; actual eligible repair lots and later capture require separate reachability proof.

## Commonwealth initial facilities

Scenario §60.44 ends with Commonwealth major facility at Alexandria and temporary facility at Mersa Matruh, after its detailed supply paragraphs. §60.92(C) says **Case 60.44 is replaced**, under heading Commonwealth Initial Supply Status, and lists abstract supplies without facilities. Its literal scope therefore conflicts with preserving the final facility sentence; the heading and supply-only replacement are evidence for a narrower reading, not a textual replacement of only the supply paragraphs.

**Proposed `graziani-cw-facilities-v1`:** replace §60.44's detailed initial supply accounting with §60.92(C), retain one temporary facility at Matruh D3714 and the Alexandria major facility. Apply Land §22.31's independent general major facility list at Cairo (all hexes) and Alexandria (all hexes), subject to map/control verification; do not infer that §60.44's mention of Alexandria excludes Cairo. This follows existing [RUL-GRA-01](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-01-commonwealth-temporary-repair-facility) as a chosen scope interpretation. Alternative literal whole-case replacement removes Matruh; general §22.31 still independently lists Alexandria and Cairo. Neither reading establishes exact city hex identities without map verification.

Tests: Land-only setup has one Matruh temporary facility, correct separate abstract supply counts without detailed supplies or duplicate facilities; independently check city hexes and controller; compare literal replacement fixture lacking Matruh; validate scenario setup, repair eligibility and version hash/replay. Scenario §60.41 places broken-down Commonwealth tanks at Alexandria, so facility repair is potentially relevant immediately; this does not prove all prerequisites of a particular attempt.

## Initial city fortifications

Scenario §60.7 says no initial fortifications under heading construction on map at start. Land §25.0 distinguishes major cities considered fortifications from additional player construction; §25.12 assigns level 2 to major cities and level 3 to Cairo/Alexandria, excluding villages. Blind readers agree on values, not precedence.

**Proposed `graziani-city-fortifications-v1`:** read the setup heading as excluding separately constructed fortifications at start, retaining intrinsic major-city levels: 2 normally, 3 Cairo/Alexandria; villages have no inherent level. This preserves both the city rule and the setup's construction context, but §60.7's unqualified wording also supports zero initial fortification effects in all cities. Label this as a contested choice, not source proof. Construction, reductions to zero, repair modifiers and rebuilding remain governed by their separately verified cases; do not model an indestructible permanent city bonus. Land §25.16 permits reduction of cities to zero, and errata corrects §25.15's repair cross-reference.

Tests: named verified major city starts 2, Cairo/Alexandria city hexes 3, village 0, no separately constructed marker; compare zero-city alternative; combat benefits, reduction to zero and rebuilding only after source-specific action rules are resolved. Check mapped hex and relevant combat states separately; do not claim scenario combat reachability from synthetic fixtures.

## Land-only repair supplies

Land §22.13(b) separately bars repairs if supplies are unavailable; §§22.23–22.24 explicitly require no expenditure for field truck and recce/armored-car repair. §22.26 requires fuel for tank field repair and §22.35 requires fuel and stores for facility repair before rolling. §22.42 explicitly labels destroyed-tank fuel/stores expenditure Logistics Game only. Land §32.0 replaces ordinary supply expenditures with its own costs, and §32.11 says only its following supply rules apply. §32.16 defines supply-unit draw for friendly combat units; a broken-down vehicle is not automatically one. See [resource matrix](2026-11-13-graziani-repair-resource-matrix.md).

**Proposed `graziani-repair-abstract-v1`:** for Land-only repair, omit ordinary §22.26 and §22.35 debits and treat §22.13(b)'s ordinary repair-supply availability condition as displaced by §32.11's exclusive supply rules; require no invented abstract repair witness or charge. Apply all other applicable repair prerequisites, timing, weather, control, towing and type restrictions. This chooses the broader reading of §32.11; alternative retains §22.13(b) as independent eligibility, but requires a sourced definition of available abstract repair supplies that these cases do not give. The no-expenditure clauses alone do not establish that availability was displaced. Detailed Logistics keeps its source-defined costs and eligibility. Review exact repair types and exceptions before implementing any operation.

Tests: for separately eligible field truck, recce/armored car, tank, facility broken-down vehicle and destroyed tank, compare absent versus present abstract supply under otherwise identical conditions; no repair resource debit in either state under selected profile. Detailed mode requires its own applicable costs and source-defined failure consumption; zero-balance rejection must occur before RNG where expenditure is required. Include rain/sandstorm, enemy control/ZOC, towing, facility type, ownership, timing, separate die grouping, interrupted/rejected attempts, replay, idempotency and mixed-version rejection. A synthetic repair state verifies policy only; actual six-turn eligibility and availability remain separate evidence requirements.
