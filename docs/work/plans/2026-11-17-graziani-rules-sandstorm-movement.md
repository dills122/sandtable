# Sandstorm movement and breakdown: source interaction

**Finding:** Land §21.37(d) states the sandstorm breakdown threshold as at least 50% of a unit's **movement measured in CP expended**, within a particular movement context. This is stronger evidence for a fraction of actual movement CP than for half of the unit's printed CPA. Section 29.45's shorter “50% or more of its Capability Points for movement” wording still needs a versioned reconciliation, especially for phase/retreat/reaction boundaries, stops and already-resolved breakdown checks. Weather roll alone does not establish any affected route or result.

## Original evidence

Visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed pp. 14, 33–34, 41 and 44, §§8.31–.33, 21.11–.37, 29.1, 29.41–.46, 32.55. Land SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`. Text-searched [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), SHA-256 `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`, for §§21.37, 29.44 and 29.45; no direct correction found. This bounded search does not rule out other or later clarifications.

- §29.1 determines weather per Operations Stage and uses a location die only for sandstorm or rainstorm; unaffected map sections are normal. §29.41 excludes delta hexes from sandstorms and §29.46 says sandstorms stop in coastal hexes rather than extending into the Mediterranean. Exact map, delta and coastal classification remain separate source-backed inputs.
- §29.44 doubles **all movement costs for hexes with a sandstorm**. §8.31 separately charges for entering hexes or crossing particular hexsides, and §8.33 has road/track terrain interactions. The rule does not explicitly identify which side's weather governs a hexside cost when adjacent hexes differ, how to apportion a combined road/terrain/hexside charge, or whether a per-move rule applies to non-movement CP. A complete arithmetic policy is required; do not indiscriminately double the unit's whole stage expenditure or every hex on a route that merely touches sandstorm.
- §29.45 applies a one-column-higher breakdown adjustment when a unit spends 50% or more of its Capability Points for movement in sandstorm hexes during a given Movement Phase. §21.37(d) describes at least 50% of a unit's **movement**, calculated in CP expended, on a sandstorm game-map during a particular Movement Phase, Retreat, Reaction, etc. Its formulation supports a denominator of movement CP for the relevant context; it does not say half of the unit's nominal CPA. The texts differ in scope vocabulary and refer respectively to hexes and game-map; a multi-section route and precise check boundary need explicit adjudication. Do not infer every unit has an actual breakdown check merely from meeting the threshold.
- §21.11 limits which vehicles are subject to breakdown, §§21.24–.27 govern checks at stops, cumulative stage points across both players’ portions, a repeat check after reaching a higher column, and a threshold of more than three points. Section 21.32 combines weather and vehicle adjustments, and §21.33 bounds table columns. Cumulative breakdown points, the movement-CP fraction and the adjusted table column are distinct quantities; whether a new weather shift alone can affect repeat-check eligibility under §21.26, or only the checked column under §21.32, needs an explicit interpretation. §32.55 explicitly exempts abstract Motorization Points from breakdown, while saying other breakdown rules apply. Do not extend that exemption to actual tank or armored-reconnaissance TOE points without further authority. A resolved later movement cannot retroactively change a completed check without a specified timing/replay contract.

## Distinguishing vectors

Assume a 10-CPA eligible vehicle spends 2 movement CP in the relevant context, 1 in a sandstorm hex and 1 elsewhere: the §21.37(d) reading reaches 50% of actual movement while a half-CPA test does not. Compare 0.5/2, 1/2 and 1.5/2 using exact half-CP arithmetic after a separately chosen movement-cost policy. A no-movement context has no automatically defined ratio or breakdown event. Compare stops, repeated Movement Segments, Reaction and Retreat only after timing identities are fixed; test real vehicle points separately from abstract Motorization Points and ensure normal/unknown weather is not converted into sandstorm or clear.

For a storm boundary, evaluate a hex-entry cost, a separately identified shared-side cost and a road/track traversal in each direction under the selected attribution policy. Track raw component costs and weather attribution for replay, not merely a final rounded total. Decide whether the fraction uses weather-doubled movement charges and how it allocates side costs; test weather shifts with unchanged accumulated breakdown points, separately from a move that increases them. Certification, path legality, stage timing, weather visibility, eligible vehicle identity and ZOC remain independent. These examples are synthetic, not proof any route or weather event is reachable. The [independent source review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sandstorm-movement-review.md) corroborates these distinctions. No runtime, source data, readiness or tests changed.
