# Abstract supply destruction timing: policy review

**Disposition:** The proposal is a coherent *candidate mapping* from §32.17's undefined “sub-segment” to one §5.2.G four-segment repetition, and its no-extra-attempt rule is reviewable. It is not yet a concrete executable contract: “actually entered” and “independently valid later combat/movement” need a non-circular lifecycle, authoritative evidence and cancellation semantics. Do not admit a retry, resource cost or public option from this planning selection alone.

## Original wording and boundaries

[Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 43 §32.17, places the attempt before **any movement in a given sub-segment of a Movement/Combat Segment** and limits it to one per Supply Unit per sub-segment. PDF p. 12 §5.2.G instead calls Movement and Combat a **Phase** and identifies four repeatable segments, all of which a repetition must include; p. 14 §§8.22 and 8.25 permit repeated movement/combat and combat without any movement, subject to rules and resources. None explicitly equates §32.17's sub-segment with that four-segment cycle or grants unlimited empty repetitions. The proposed mapping and broader phase-wide alternative are therefore interpretations. Section 8.23's corrected cross-reference and §8.17's errata-corrected voluntary CP limit remain separate constraints, not a timing-unit definition.

The proposed cutoff on **either player's** actual movement is a conservative choice consistent with the unqualified wording, but source timing does not itself establish whether non-phasing players may initiate destruction. Define movement occurrence, source order and closure, including reaction/retreat; an unexecuted, cancelled or rejected movement request is not automatically a proven physical movement. Conversely, missing or unresolved movement history is not proof the opportunity remains open. If this version chooses to close the window when the movement opportunity is passed without movement, say that is a policy beyond simply observing an actual move.

## Required contract decisions

1. **Cycle reality and commitment:** The attempt happens at the opening, yet the proposal requires an independently valid later movement/combat cycle. A phase-entry record alone could be created solely to retry; requiring a later event that has not happened can also make the opening eligibility circular. Specify what authoritatively starts a genuine repetition, whether a valid planned action or irreversible scheduler commitment suffices, and what happens if that action is cancelled, fails validation, is prevented by destruction, or never occurs. Define rollback, costs, RNG and visibility for each outcome. Do not retroactively relabel a completed attempt or manufacture a cycle from an attempt itself.
2. **Stable identity and causality:** Ruleset, turn, stage, player portion and cycle ordinal are a useful proposed key only after the scheduler defines entry, closure, no-movement combat and replay/recovery. Establish a causal watermark for all prior movements before asserting a window is open, and handle delayed or corrected events explicitly. A command retry and a new counter identity must not reset a per-Supply-Unit allowance; define whether identity survives capture or replacement and when a genuinely distinct counter is present.
3. **Actor, targets and CP:** §32.17 permits players to destroy units they are with, calls for one die per Supply Unit in the hex, and charges half CPA of **all units in the hex** rounded upward. It does not expressly choose phasing versus non-phasing authority, subset selection, ownership, mixed real/dummy treatment, represented parent aggregation or the rounding allocation. Section 6.21 permits exceeding CPA with disorganization in general, while §8.17 imposes a separate corrected limit on certain non-motorized voluntary expenditure. “Available CP” therefore needs a rule-specific contract; do not automatically reject every cost beyond base CPA or treat all overruns as legal. Determine side, eligibility, all affected target IDs and charges before drawing.
4. **Outcome and observability:** Failure still consumes a valid attempt and cost; destruction, capture, dummy discovery and carrier/stock consequences are different transitions. A full atomic preflight needs sufficient hidden authoritative state before RNG, and a valid result must be committed or restored consistently after interruption. Generic errors alone do not prevent leakage when a hidden dummy, target count or owner choice changes RNG consumption, later state or event timing. Specify allowed side-specific disclosures, options and downstream traces. Keep the capability unavailable where unknown inputs or an observationally safe protocol are unresolved.

## Verification boundary

Test one same-window duplicate; a later independently justified real repetition; no-movement combat versus empty-cycle retry; a pending, cancelled or prevented cycle; already-begun movement by each player; missing causal history; corrected event/replay; changed player/stage; multi-target, actor/CP/overrun and dummy/capture/stock cases. These are discrimination vectors, not proof that any example is legal or reachable. The proposed mapping remains suitable for planning comparison, with source alternatives retained, but no runtime/readiness admission follows.
