They do not establish a complete eastern rail census, a certified D/E seam, or absence of an Axis witness. This is a synthesis of informed readings of one scan, not a new visual survey or independent edition.

These candidates are not a geographic east-of-Matruh proof: Map D/E overlap, hex coordinate geometry and chosen comparison rule must be reconciled before assigning any cross-sheet ordering. Nor does a local candidate require a continuous route to the western trip: §8.78's wording names **any rail hex** occupied by an Axis combat unit, without an express same-line or route requirement. Conversely, candidate printed ink alone is insufficient to prove a qualifying witness.

## State and interpretation boundaries

- Scenario §60.7 states rail ends at Mersa Matruh at setup; Land §8.71 names the Alexandria–Mersa Matruh line. These named endpoints do not establish every side, branch, or initial state in this inventory. Unfinished blue rail toward D3515 is a distinct branch, not evidence of usable track. The source batches do not survey all eastern branches, and off-route Map E rail may matter even without continuity to the selected movement path.
- Land §§8.47 and 24.67 say unconstructed railroad is ignored or does not exist until built. A **certified unbuilt** printed hex supplies no witness; unknown construction is unknown. Destroyed constructed rail remains a policy question: §8.76 bars rail entry but does not expressly erase the rail hex. Historical Soluch–Benghazi–Barce ink is excluded under the separately proposed interpretation, with independently qualifying overlapping rail assessed separately. See [witness scope and source citations](2026-11-17-graziani-continuation-rail-scope.md) and [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-continuation-rail-scope-review.md).
- For one fixed authoritative snapshot, a known qualifying eastern Axis combat occupant on qualifying rail can establish a block despite other unknown candidates. An unblocked result requires sufficiently complete geographic, rail, construction, occupant and classification coverage for **all** potentially qualifying eastern hexes, including off-route and outside this table. Unknown candidates remain unknown; absence from this finite list or pinned data is not evidence of absence. Destruction policy, line identity, representation of attached combat units, and the meaning of use and timing require versioned decisions.
- Internal three-valued evaluation does not authorize revealing hidden occupancy, classification or witness location to a player. Action availability, errors, timing and events require the separate audience-safe contract before a rail action is admitted.
