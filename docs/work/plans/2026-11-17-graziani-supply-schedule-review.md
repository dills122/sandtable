# Graziani six-turn supply matrix: independent source review

**Disposition:** Numerical table entries, calendar and ordinary lead-time
arithmetic match the inspected originals. Revise GT1 startup wording before
using the matrix as an executable schedule: an arithmetic slot is not proof
that its planning action is performed during the scenario. No historical die
result or delivery is established. This is a separate visual reading of the
same edition, informed by the matrix, not a blind or independent-edition check.

## Primary finding: GT1 planning is unresolved

Land §32.43 (printed/PDF p. 44) calls for planning at the **beginning** of each
Game-Turn and allows planning as far ahead as needed. §32.44 and §32.45 give
Axis two-turn and Commonwealth four-turn offsets. Scenario §60.22 (printed
p. 27 / PDF p. 3) starts Graziani at GT1 **Operations Stage 1**. Land §5.2
(printed/PDF pp. 11–12) places Initiative Determination and the Naval Convoy
Stage, including its Schedule Phase, before Operations Stage 1. Its separate
Naval Convoy Arrival Phase occurs *inside* each Operations Stage.

Therefore GT1 is the ordinary arithmetic planning slot for Axis GT3 and
Commonwealth GT5, but the inspected passages do not establish whether the
§32.43 beginning-of-turn planning is performed before entry, accounted for in
setup, or executed by some other accepted startup procedure. §5.2 refers to
§32.0 during convoy scheduling, but does not explicitly locate both sides'
§32.43 table rolls within a named phase. Do not assert a mandatory skipped
roll solely from sequence position, or silently run a new GT1 roll at OpStage
1. Scenario §60.37 requires Axis pregame convoy runs for the remainder of
September, but does not specify every abstract supply roll or Commonwealth
plan.

The arrival matrix's GT5 statement **“Both ordinary slots during play”**
overstates the evidence: Axis's ordinary slot is GT3, but Commonwealth's is
GT1 and needs the startup decision. The planning-side matrix's GT1 row and
GT3 arrival's Axis GT1 slot also need the same qualifier, as does its general
claim of planning obligations through GT6 insofar as it includes GT1. GT2–6
are normal in-scenario turn beginnings under the ordinary sequence, subject to
an accepted terminal and obligation policy. The text “Planning for possible
pre-start arrivals” should say **pre-start plans for later arrivals** if that is
its meaning: the exception and table do not establish pre-start deliveries.
Do not infer all GT2 pregame plans or GT1 plans are absent, present, or zero.

Land §32.43 excludes the starting turn from its statement about possible
regular arrivals, conditional on planning die rolls for other turns. That is
not proof that the scenario starts with no supply, that an additional exception
cannot exist, or that every later turn produces a positive amount. The source
permits additional long-horizon planning, so the ordinary slot is an arithmetic
reference, not an exclusive latest-or-only opportunity proved by the inspected
text. Axis §32.44 uses the planning month's letter and says the result is how
many real supply units the player may bring in; Commonwealth §32.45 uses the
arrival period. Neither table by itself proves successful transport or
placement. §32.42's Alexandria option requires **more than three received in
one Game-Turn** and specifies one per three; interpretation of partial groups
and aggregation remains separate.

## Acceptance vectors

- With a versioned direct-entry-at-OpStage-1 policy, GT1 ordinary slots for
  Axis GT3 and Commonwealth GT5 must not automatically create new rolls;
  require an explicit startup plan/initialization disposition. Under a
  separately accepted policy that performs the turn preamble, create each
  authorized roll once. Neither vector claims the source settles this policy.
- Check actual turn indices `1→3`, `2→4`, `3→5`, `4→6`, `5→7`, `6→8`
  for Axis and `1→5`, `2→6`, `3→7`, `4→8`, `5→9`, `6→10` for
  Commonwealth, with the GT1 qualification above and explicit future-target
  cutoff. No invented negative turn/date is required to represent unknown
  pregame plans.
- Exercise all six exact table rows on both sides, including actual Axis B
  die-1 zero versus missing roll; period I lookup follows target arrival date,
  while Axis letter follows planning date. Test a hypothetical period boundary
  separately from the 1940 scenario dates.
- Keep setup, available entitlement, selected cargo, convoy outcome and landed
  inventory distinct. Prevent duplicate startup rolls or deliveries after
  retry/replay. Test Alexandria receipt thresholds and all open placement,
  aggregate and carryover interpretations under their own accepted policies.
