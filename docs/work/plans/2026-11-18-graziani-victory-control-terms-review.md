# Independent review: contextual control terms

**Disposition:** The [source note](2026-11-18-graziani-victory-control-terms.md) correctly attributes the two additional control formulations to their respective contexts; neither is an express definition or precedence rule for Graziani §60.81. Shared ZOC control, last occupancy, and the separate Crusader scoring condition must not be collapsed into one universal predicate.

I visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 42, §§30.5 and 30.56. The troop-transfer section applies to Commonwealth personnel. Section 30.56 permits transfer between friendly-controlled ports, describing control as the **last player to occupy the hex**, and separately bars transfers when a port is incapacitated by enemy air bombardment. It does not in that parenthetical specify combat-unit occupation or say the last occupier must remain present. Control alone is not a complete transfer authorization: the other port, transport, capacity and timing conditions remain relevant.

I visually inspected [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 9 / printed p. 33, §62.8. The Operation Crusader victory section defines city/village control by occupation with a combat unit of any strength. It separately requires that unit to have sufficient supplies for one month and ammunition for one week, described as one round each Operations Stage. This scoring-specific wording supports the note's current-occupation comparison; neither it nor its supply requirement expressly changes Graziani.

I also visually rechecked scenario PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, §60.81. Italian decisive and tactical rows explicitly associate take/hold with “occupy”; tactical separately uses retain possession, and Commonwealth rows use take/hold with their own supply language. The source note correctly avoids declaring all those phrases identical to either §30.56 or §62.8. Its hypothetical shared-ZOC, known-last-occupier and no-current-occupant case distinguishes the printed predicates, conditional on actual ZOC projection and established history; it does not resolve precedence or victory.
