# SQ-06: recovery-counter identity and later-scenario erratum

**Result:** A specific previously uncompleted source crosswalk adds evidence for, but does not conclusively prove, identity between the GT24/3 `Tank Recovery Squadron` track entry and Land §22.7's one German `Mobile Tank Repair Squad` counter. The German counter sheet visibly contains a face marked **TANK RECOVERY**, and September errata §61.41 names a **German Mobile Tank Recovery Squadron** starting in Tripolitania. The erratum is for the later §61.41 setup, not Graziani's §60.31; it does not introduce that squad in GT1–6.

## Why this was a remaining check

The [specialized-squadron review](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-a-source-2-review.md) compared the track and §22.7 but explicitly retained their identity as a candidate, and did not inspect a counter face or §61.41 erratum. The [postchecks audit](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-a-postchecks.md) likewise identified the identity/exception as incomplete but did not name an applicable unexamined entry. Counter Section 4 and errata §61.41 are concrete retained-scan comparisons for that specific identity, rather than a speculative new game-state example.

## Original source comparison

- [Axis land schedule §4.43b](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 2, lists `Tank Recovery Squadron` in the German GT24 / Operations Stage 3 row. [Land §22.7](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 35, names a **German Mobile Tank Repair Squad**, says there is **one** such counter and treats it like the Commonwealth Desert Tank Delivery Squadrons except it repairs only German tanks. The track does not reproduce that full rule name, and §22.7 does not explicitly cite the track row.
- [Counter PDF](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), p. 2 Section 4, right block top row, has a face with a tracked-vehicle illustration and two-line label **TANK RECOVERY**, immediately right of `DSKA` and left of tank marker `A`; the section is labeled German and its header says one identical section. This supplies a concrete matching German recovery marker absent from the earlier crosswalk. The face does not print the complete §22.7 name or GT24/3, so the linkage is a **stronger candidate inference**, not an explicit source declaration that the three labels are synonymous. No exhaustive counter, reverse-face or edition inventory is claimed.
- [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 3, §61.41 adds that the **German Mobile Tank Recovery Squadron** starts in Tripolitania. [Scenario §61.41](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 6 / printed p. 30, is Axis Land Forces setup for the subsequent Desert Fox scenario; §61.2, PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29, starts that scenario at GT26/3. This later setup is compatible in chronology with the track's GT24/3 entry, but compatibility and similar naming do not themselves prove one-to-one identity. It is **not** a correction to Graziani §60.31 or evidence of a six-turn starting squadron.
- Graziani §60.22, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, ends GT6/3. Under the candidate crosswalk, the ordinary GT24/3 schedule lies beyond that window; the later setup addition supplies no alternative Graziani entry. Other exceptional introduction, actual setup, mode and source precedence remain unexhausted. Neither the observed face nor timing creates a legal tow or repair.
