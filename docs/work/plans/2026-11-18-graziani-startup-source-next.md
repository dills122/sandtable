# Graziani startup: setup motorization and coastal shipping

**Result:** Two scenario-wide setup clauses distinguish initial choices from later Operations Stage actions. They do not resolve whether a pre-OpStage turn preamble was played. In particular, an initial Motorization Point distribution is a setup choice; changing it after play starts follows applicable attachment rules, including an explicit loaded-supply exception. Axis coastal shipping starts empty. Neither fact proves any first-stage action occurred.

## Source cases

[Scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, §59.63 derives initial attached and unattached Motorization Points from scenario truck listings for abstract logistics. Section 59.64 permits changing initial distributions once the game starts under Land attachment/detachment rules, except for §59.66(B). Section 59.66(B) permits an attached point not initially transporting men or guns to begin loaded with one ammunition or three fuel points. It requires that unit to consume those supplies first and forbids transferring them to a Supply Unit; while carrying that initial supply, a point may detach only if it remains attached to a subsidiary combat unit of that parent. This exception is tied to that initial cargo, not a general ban on all redistribution.

[Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §32.32, separately place attachment and detachment of Motorization Points **to Supply Units** only in an Operations Stage's Organization Phase. Section 5.2.C, p. 12, includes Organization within each Operations Stage. These rules distinguish a selected scenario-start distribution from a subsequent supply-carrier reassignment; §59.64 does not itself authorize ignoring §32.32's phase boundary or §59.66(B)'s restriction. They also do not require a first-stage reassignment, settle all possible point-to-combat-unit attachment timing, or imply a pre-start Organization Phase. A first-stage reassignment is only a conditional possibility once all pertinent requirements are established. The scenario's choices and actual attachment/cargo state remain inputs, not defaults inferred from the printed phase.

Section 59.54 states Axis coastal shipping begins **all scenarios unloaded**. Section 60.35 places the Italian coastal shipping counters in Tripoli at game start. These are setup facts: a hypothetical preamble cannot be applied to the scenario snapshot in a way that silently replaces its specified starting location or loaded state. Conversely, empty starting ships do not prove that the Naval Convoy Stage was skipped or executed, and do not prohibit a separately lawful later loading. Distinguish initial empty ship state from a convoy plan or an actual shipment; no arrival or movement follows from these clauses.

## Boundary and verification

Scenario §60.22 starts Graziani at Game-Turn 1, Operations Stage 1. Neither newly checked clause specifies how the earlier turn stages are reconstructed or whether the full preamble is played. The [startup continuation](2026-11-17-graziani-startup-sequence-continuation.md) and [review](2026-11-17-graziani-startup-sequence-continuation-review.md) retain direct entry as the literal start and full preamble as an explicitly selected variant. These setup cases narrow the state that any variant must reconcile; they do not provide an official startup ruling.

Distinguishing checks for a future contract: preserve chosen initial loaded point and its restriction across startup/replay; compare a permissible initial distribution with an attempted later supply-point change outside versus inside Organization; preserve initially unloaded Axis coastal ships at their stated starting location. These are design vectors, not certified legal complete actions or gameplay test results.
