# 6th New Zealand Brigade arrival: independent source review

**Result:** Confirmed the reported conflict and the follow-up's qualified planning interpretation. No material transcription error found. The printed sources do not establish an official resolution; the choice of GT6 is a local ruling supported by the scenario's direction to use the track.

| Source visually checked | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, §60.47 | Directs use of the Commonwealth reinforcement track as printed, bringing units in where and when listed. Warns that some units need training and that other units must be assigned as training units; bars replacement points before November 1940. It does not expressly bar whole-unit reinforcements. |
| [Commonwealth charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 3, §4.43a | GT2 / OpS3 lists 2 RTR, 7 RTR, and 6th NZ field artillery. GT6 / OpS3 lists 6 NZ Brigade and trucks: 25 L, 85 M, 5 H. Column heading calls these units and attached trucks; applicability under abstract rules remains a separate question. |
| Same charts, PDF p. 18, 2nd NZ Division OA | Brigade HQ and 24th, 25th, 26th NZ battalions each show `3/6`; 6th NZ field artillery shows `3/2`. Consistent with track, but not an independent edition. |
| [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 31, §20.11 | General sentence directs whole-unit arrivals by track. Its next sentence explicitly places the brigade, its three battalions, and field artillery in September `3/2`. This conflicts with the brigade track and OA timing; no source here proves which printing was intended. |
| [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF pp. 1–4 | Visual review found no explicit correction to brigade timing or §20.11. §4.43a adds GT28/OpS2 and GT64/OpS2 entries and redirects a GT76/OpS2 reference to the added GT64 withdrawal. §8.86 separately corrects arrival-stage movement; it does not resolve the date conflict. This packet cannot rule out other or later clarification. |

Track-first selection is a defensible planning interpretation, not an official correction or a demonstrated universal priority rule. No basis in these sources for treating the example as an additional brigade arrival. Training, truck conversion and assignment remain separate decisions; this review does not authorize content or runtime admission.
