# Errata §43.12: `Ju8SD` identification

**Result:** Original [Air §43.11](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 14, explicitly names **Ju88D** among the German bombers subject to the Mediterranean-base requirement. The [Axis aircraft chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) p. 2 separately lists **Ju. 88D**. Together these strongly support Ju88D as the intended reference behind the [September erratum](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) p. 3 §43.12's printed `Ju8SD`, but neither source expressly corrects that spelling. Preserve the erratum transcription and label Ju88D as an inference, not an official correction.

- Air §43.11 requires at least 75% of the listed German He 111, Ju88D and FW220 bombers at Mediterranean bases. Section 43.12 specifies Italy/Sicily as the base location until 1/35; §43.13 describes later distribution using the broader `Ju88's` wording. The erratum explicitly identifies bombers “referred to” in §43.12 and prints `He111's, Ju8SD's, and FW 220's`. Context links the clarification to §43.11's named list; it does not independently spell `Ju88D`.
- Axis chart p. 1 §4.44c lists **Ju. 88c** as a fighter/fighter-bomber; its p. 2 continuation (printed heading §4.44b) lists **Ju. 88D** in the bombers/flying boats/transports/reconnaissance group, with Bomb 33. The German rows also include Ju. 87B and Ju. 87D. Ju. 88D is the unique exact §43.11 type matching the erratum's similar middle designation among these listed rows; similarity alone does not establish an official spelling correction or permit replacing other types.
- The same chart lists **Fw. 200 C**, whereas Air §43.11 and the erratum print **FW220**. That is a separate source discrepancy; this comparison does not silently correct it or establish that the two identifiers are equivalent.
- The retained [counter scan](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 2, section 3, German aircraft group, displays generic fighter, dive-bomber and bomber labels/silhouettes in the inspected area, not a readable `Ju8SD` or `Ju88D` designation. It therefore supplies no independent type identification here. This is an area inspection, not a complete counter inventory or proof no aircraft type appears elsewhere.

This source case concerns **detailed Air** Mediterranean aircraft basing, not automatic Land-only Graziani behavior. The relevant dates and actual German aircraft availability are separate state and scenario questions; no present aircraft, eligible mission, policy or runtime change follows. No exhaustive later-errata search or independent-edition comparison was performed.
