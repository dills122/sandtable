## Monthly loss

- Land §32.57 says the first Naval Convoy Stage of each month determines losses for the **previous month**, using the appropriate month of an abstract motorization loss chart. Slash-left is Commonwealth, slash-right Axis. Percentage applies to that player's total motorization points on the map **at that time**, including Tripolitania–Tunis boxes. Losses may be removed anywhere in map E for Commonwealth, or anywhere in map A including the off-map region for Axis. It does not specify how to round fractional point losses, nor how to satisfy a computed loss if eligible removal locations contain too few points.
- Land §32.59 names an Abstract Truck/Motorization Loss Chart. Common chart **§58.5**, titled Abstract Truck Loss Chart, has motorization percentage legends matching the side ordering. September 1940 is a **dash**, October **6/2**, November **5/3**. No legend on that chart defines the dash. §58.42 independently points to §§32.57 and 32.59 for the same percentage figures when applying abstract air losses to physical trucks; §58.44 calls its chart Abstract Truck Loss Chart. Those links make §58.5 a strong identification, but the printed numbering mismatch is not an explicit renumbering correction.
- At the first October monthly loss event, the instruction to determine the **previous month's** losses supports selecting September's dash. The instruction to select the **appropriate month** also leaves a current-month lookup reading; that would select October's 6% Commonwealth / 2% Axis. The chart and text do not state which reading is intended, nor whether the dash means zero, no event, unavailable data, or something else. Event ordering relative to arrivals, initial-month treatment, rounding and removal shortfall remain open. Neither interpretation supplies a defensible numeric result without further choices. Existing [calendar analysis](2026-11-12-graziani-abstract-logistics-crosswalk.md#monthly-motorization-loss-chart-identity-and-date-ambiguity) locates the first October stage in turn 3; this recheck did not independently verify that calendar.
- Land §§32.55–32.56 distinguish motorization from truck breakdown and specify separate losses/capture when assigned units are eliminated or captured. Do not duplicate physical truck breakdown losses or conflate the monthly percentage with convoy attack losses (§32.65).

## Dummy stacking

- Land §32.14 gives supply units zero stacking value, but separately caps their count at five in a major-city hex and three in any other type of hex **off-map**, with unlimited units in Tripoli/Tunisia region boxes. The literal words do not plainly impose the three-unit cap on ordinary on-map hexes. The [bounded existing planning interpretation](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-stacking-review.md) extends it to on-map non-major-city hexes; that is a choice, not an explicit correction.
- §32.18 calls the markers **dummy Supply Units**, permits one for every three received and/or in scenario setup, moves them like real units with less motorization, and removes them on discovery. §32.32 specifies 30 points for real and six for dummy transport; §32.34 explicitly includes real and/or dummy in rail's two-unit limit. These facts favor reading dummies as supply units for §32.14's count, but §32.14 does not expressly say so, and a dummy-specific exception is not stated. Do not resolve count, discoverability or setup exception silently.
- Scenario §60.92(B) gives Axis two groups of three dummy counters, placeable under Dump 1 and 2 rules; §60.92(C) gives Commonwealth one group of three under Dump 1's placement rule. Each corresponding real dump contains three units; placement scope is one eligible map-C Libya hex per Axis dump and one map-D hex west of Matruh for Commonwealth. Whether each dummy group may share a real group's hex without violating a chosen three-unit cap depends on whether dummies count and on the chosen on-map reading. Treat scenario's dummy quantities and §32.18's general ratio relationship as a further interpretation: automatic additive dummy grants are not established.
- Scenario §59.65 permits initial excess only when unavoidable under distribution instructions, bars adding supply units to that hex while excess remains, and requires normal restrictions thereafter once its distribution conforms. It explicitly excludes motorization transporting supply units from hex stacking limits; that does not itself exempt the carried supply units. A player-selected co-location of real and dummy groups is not automatically unavoidable. Determining eligible placements, dummy inclusion, real/dummy visibility and the end of any initial exception needs a consistent choice before validation.
