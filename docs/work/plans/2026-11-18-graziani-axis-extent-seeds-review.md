# Graziani Axis extent seeds: independent source review

**Disposition:** Two source-coverage corrections needed: known initial air facilities are described as unknown, and detailed air-supply availability needs an Air-abstracted qualification. Remaining inspected scenario inventory and bounded arrival claims match the scans. This is a separate visual reading of the same editions, not a complete legal-placement, movement, or arrival-exception audit.

## Findings

1. **Known air facilities.** The Air bullet says “Specific facilities and selected distributions remain unknown.” Scenario §60.5, PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, lists specific initial airfields, landing strips, flying-boat basins and alighting areas, with named or hex locations, and assigns Libya facilities to Italy initially. Section 60.32 allows placement at Italian facilities within capacity; this does not choose individual aircraft distributions. State that facilities are source-listed but not enumerated in this inventory, or include their eligible geographic seeds, while retaining capacity, ownership, off-map and mode qualifications. The present statement obscures known source geography.
2. **Air-abstracted supply.** Scenario §59.61, PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, instructs players abstracting only Air to ignore all trucks **and supplies** available at or for air facilities. The §60.34 bullet includes additional air supplies without this qualification, although the truck bullet correctly addresses air-facility trucks. Qualify those supplies as applicable when Air is played; §60.91 supplies no scenario-specific addition for Air abstracted. Land-only instead uses §60.92(B), and Land and Air with logistics abstracted uses §60.93(B).

## Verified scope

- Scenario PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27: all fixed §60.31 location rows, attachments and detachments, Tripoli and Tripolitania, Libya and A/B pools, Saharan Det radius, two Autoblinda points, and the 13 truck-bearing rows including Saharan Det match the printed source. Printed Libya pool duplication and B4827 remain source issues, not evidence for extra units. Map-side A4827 was not independently rechecked here.
- Same page: §60.32 air placement and §60.33 truck quantities and repair sites match; §60.34 fixed dump names, flexible Map C areas, dummy dumps and air-supply provision match subject to finding 2. The plan intentionally delegates quantities and does not enumerate facility locations.
- Scenario PDF pp. 4–5 / printed pp. 28–29: §60.91–.93 support the stated mode distinction, regular-unit counts, two flexible groups and dummy groups, Derna ammunition dash, and five extra Axis airfield supply units. The printed §60.93(B) reference is indeed 60.82 “B”. Section 59.66 makes extra loaded motorization contingent on qualifying groups and hexes.
- Scenario §60.22 sets GT1 OpStage 1 through GT6 OpStage 3; §60.35 places available coastal shipping at Tripoli; §60.37 specifies planning for the rest of September on lanes 2, 3 and 6. Axis chart §4.43b, PDF p. 2, starts the ordinary reinforcement table GT9 OpStage 2. Land §20.15, p. 31, sends reinforcements normally to Tripoli and allows conditional Benghazi diversion. Axis chart §20.66, PDF p. 33, prints first planning dates GT2 for Italian light AA, GT3 for CV L.3/CV 33/35 and GT4 for light trucks, with the stated two-turn nominal delay. These are schedule bounds, not proof of actual arrivals or exclusion of other mechanisms.
