## Original-source checks

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §60.31 places Sheferzen Garrison at C3419 and separately lists the **Anywhere in Libya** group. It also gives two Autoblinda 40 TOE points, each attachable to any unit, but not both to the same unit, while ignoring regular unit-composition restrictions; alternatively the points may be deployed at Tobruk as regular replacement points. The clause appears in Italian initial deployment. “Any unit” directly supports broad unit choice, but does not explicitly decide enemy ownership, legal location, exact point representation, maximum-TOE exceptions or whether separate attachment counts as another counter. Do not assert that this grants an additional freely placed unit counter at C3419. The Tobruk option and one-point-per-unit restriction need to be evaluated together; mixing some points assigned and some replacements is not expressly resolved by the quoted alternative.
- [Axis chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 26 lists `dd` maximum TOE 1 and defensive Close Assault `2`; PDF p. 27's chart key says the maximum may be exceeded only due to arrival/deployment TOE strength. Land p. 30 §19.6 says no unit may be rebuilt or augmented beyond its printed ID-code maximum; §19.61 similarly limits augmentation by replacements. A separately checked OA baseline is `N`, one of one TOE. Whether an extra point assigned under this scenario clause is a specific exception to that cap, an arrival/deployment exception, a different form of attachment, or still constrained by it is unresolved. “Ignoring all regular unit composition restrictions” is specific evidence favoring a broad exception but does not explicitly name maximum TOE. Therefore `2 + 2 = 4` assumes the point can be legally included and its defensive rating applies; do not call that a source-proven legal setup or use it to prove the whole stack is below threshold.
- [Map](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf) PDF p. 3, 600-dpi crop at `(6600,9000,2300,1800)`, shows `3419` immediately east of a blue checkered line on the 3418/3419 vertical side and `3320` southeast, across the locally visible diagonal from 3419. The checkered line is not on that visible shared diagonal; `Bir el Sheferzen` text obscures part of the diagonal. A 150-dpi regional crop `(700,2850,2100,1100)` shows `LIBYA` left/west and `EGYPT` right/east of that same distinctive checkered line farther south. I did not trace every intervening segment or establish a formally keyed border symbol; national-boundary identity and cell-interior membership remain well-supported interpretation, not a complete certified geography or movement rule.
- If that interpretation and an accepted hex-membership policy classify C3419 as Egypt, the Libya-only pool cannot choose it merely because it borders Libya. The explicit fixed garrison placement still stands. Neither inference excludes all other eligible setup categories, later arrivals or point choices; the source has separately specified placement options, and §59.2's same-hex attachment/detachment does not itself relocate units.
