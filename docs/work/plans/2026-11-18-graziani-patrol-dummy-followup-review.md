# Dummy-only patrol target: source review

**Disposition:** Supports the [follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-patrol-dummy-followup.md)'s central unresolved interactions. The cited rules do not explicitly settle dummy-only target occupancy, dummy classification for the §16.32 no-combat check, or a dummy-only objective-loss recipient. In particular, §16.45's deception language and §16.32's mandatory disclosure should both be preserved when evaluating a proposed interpretation. This is an informed review of the same rules scans, not an independent edition or an implementation ruling.

- **Classification:** §3.1 expressly names Dummy Tank Formations among items considered combat units in certain situations. §3.23 lists the ordinary combat-unit types and says certain non-combat units may count for particular determinations, including reconnaissance; it does not expressly say which classification applies to a dummy at §16.32. §16.32 expressly excludes AA/Flak and engineers for this purpose, but silence about dummies alone is not an affirmative classification. The glossary's general definition of a unit as a counter and §3.21's category listing with an explicit no-counter exception also make a counter-only occupancy inference unsafe.
- **Eligibility and disclosure:** §16.22 requires an enemy-occupied target and a qualifying path; §16.42 places a dummy in a hex without a counter, and §16.45 contemplates one alone. The cited text does not expressly define the exact dummy-only occupancy test. If the no-combat condition applies, §16.32 requires disclosure and skips the survival table with no patrol loss. §16.45 permits an apparent tank designation and chosen TOE strength when revealing information and says the patroller cannot know it is a dummy until attacking. Those statements create the stated conditional tension; neither silently overrides the other in the cited text.
- **Report and objective loss:** The §16.7 table has zero outcomes for some rolls with one or two net patrol points, so §16.45 alone does not guarantee a report. With three net points its entries are positive, subject to target, survival and report interpretation. §16.34 orders a combat-unit TOE loss or, if no combat unit, truck, AA/Flak, engineer, then HQ points; it does not expressly name a dummy-only recipient. §16.46 gives specified close-assault and anti-armor elimination cases, not an express patrol-table resolution. Do not infer a real TOE loss from an apparent strength.
- **Land-only qualification:** §32.0 replaces ordinary supply expenditure and §32.11 broadly directs players to ignore references to usage and expenditure of Stores; this is strong textual evidence against charging §16.42's ten-Store expenditure under the abstraction. The separate question is whether its in-hex Stores-presence condition has any surviving independent meaning when the expenditure is ignored. §32.25 waives patrol expenditure, not dummy availability; neither rule supplies a scenario starting dummy.

The September errata's §16.11 addition expands types eligible to patrol and does not resolve these dummy interactions. This was not an exhaustive search of later errata, scenario inventories, or all rules.
