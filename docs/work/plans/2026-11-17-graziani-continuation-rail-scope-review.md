# Independent review: continuation rail witness scope

**Disposition:** No blocking source discrepancy found in construction, destruction, historical-line, or nonlocal witness analysis. One classification detail merits a more explicit citation before implementation.

## Finding

- **HQ classification (§§3.31, 3.33, p. 6):** The plan correctly warns against discarding represented combat units, but understates §3.31: when any combat units are attached, the HQ counter itself is considered a combat unit. Section 3.33 says an HQ with no combat units attached is no longer considered a combat unit. State these express rules separately from remaining questions about parent representation, parenthesized strengths, and determination-specific exceptions; those questions should not obscure the explicit attached-HQ rule. Section 3.4 and September errata §3.4 address use of parenthesized ratings, not a blanket test of combat-unit status.

## Checked conclusions

- **Unbuilt:** §§8.47 and 24.67 explicitly say unconstructed railroad hexes are ignored for all purposes and do not exist or function until built. Excluding a certified unbuilt printed hex from §8.78 is supported; an unknown construction state remains unknown.
- **Destroyed:** §8.76 calls these rail hexes and bars rail entry until repair. Section 24.66 describes destruction and a marker. Neither expressly answers whether such a hex remains a nonlocal §8.78 witness. Plan correctly identifies retained-witness and operational-witness policies as interpretations rather than settled source text.
- **Historical line:** §8.71 says Soluch–Benghazi–Barce is historical only, may not be used for anything, and has no effect on terrain or movement. Section 24.6 likewise excludes use of the Benghazi–Barce line. Excluding its ink as an indirect witness is strongly supported but still applies those restrictions to §8.78; plan properly labels that application as an interpretation. Overlapping independent rail requires separate evidence.
- **Nonlocal predicate:** §8.78 says no rail hex west of any rail hex occupied by an Axis combat unit may be used by the Allied player for railroad movement. Its sentence does not specify a same-line, route, distance, direction-of-travel, or ZOC condition. Section 8.73 separately restricts rail movement into enemy occupancy or ZOC. Geographic west, use, timing, and qualifying line identity remain open implementation questions.
- **Errata:** September 1979 errata §8.71 permits Axis rail use under §54.4; §8.73 adds a reorganization restriction. Neither expressly changes §8.78 or revokes the historical-line restriction. This is not a census of later publications.
