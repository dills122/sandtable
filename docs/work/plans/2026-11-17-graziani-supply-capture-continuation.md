# Abstract supply capture: trigger and precedence continuation

**Disposition:** Keep capture unsupported for executable play until an explicit ruling covers entry legality, §10.29 eligibility and the immediate/free-use operation. Existing [capture analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-next.md), [independent check](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-independent.md), [ZOC review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-zoc-capture-followup-review.md) and [use review](2026-11-17-graziani-captured-supply-use-review.md) support this boundary. No source found in those bounded checks establishes a complete procedure; absence from those checks is not proof no other rule or correction exists.

| Case | Source-supported condition | Consequence and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combat entry | Land §32.13: **any enemy combat unit enters** a Supply Unit's hex. No stated “alone” or ZOC requirement in this clause. | Captures the Supply Unit; its supplies are used **immediately and freely**. Section 8.13 generally forbids entry into an enemy-occupied hex; its interaction with §32.13 needs a ruling. An attack, non-combat entry or adjacency alone does not establish this trigger. |
| ZOC exposure | Section 10.29: such a non-combat unit is **alone** in enemy ZOC at any time during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase and has **no strength of any type**. Qualifying ZOC and projection must exist; adjacency alone does not prove them. | Capture follows if all predicates and applicability hold. Abstract Supply Unit eligibility, whether cargo counts as strength, and “alone” with other counters or markers remain open. Entry is not stated as a prerequisite. The entry-specific immediate/free parenthetical is not restated here; its extension to this trigger needs a ruling. |
| HQ-specific case | Section 3.36 addresses an HQ with no combat values, parenthesized or otherwise, in a hex without combat units when an enemy combat unit places it in ZOC. Section 3.33 says a bare HQ is not a combat unit, yet may use parenthesized ratings. | Capture is instant, costs no Capability Points, and counts as one Prisoner Point. These HQ-specific consequences do not establish free supply capture or a supply prisoner outcome. This is not a definition of “alone” for §10.29. Non-combat status and zero stacking do not by themselves prove no strength of any type. |

Section 10.29 also separately bars Truck Convoys from entering enemy ZOC unless a friendly combat unit already occupies the hex, and bars non-combat units from voluntarily entering an unoccupied hex in enemy ZOC. Check these restrictions when constructing a witness; neither alone determines whether an abstract Supply Unit is “such a unit” for the capture sentence.

## Order and use decisions

1. Proposed transition discipline, not printed sequencing: determine legal occupancy and actual ZOC, then evaluate the applicable trigger against the state and phase **at that event**. If a qualifying ZOC capture is established strictly before a later entry, do not automatically capture the same unit again; determine the later entry's target and ownership from the adopted transition. If triggers arise in the same unresolved event, source passages above do not prescribe a tie-break. Do not infer that either always wins, or that moving adjacent necessarily triggers capture.
2. For §32.13, identify beneficiaries, amount and timing of immediate use, stock debit and remainder, and whether ordinary §32.16 draw conditions and §32.21–.24 expenditure rules apply or yield. “Freely” does not expressly specify a separate Capability Point waiver, zero depletion, total depletion, unlimited range or future use. CP and ammo/fuel are distinct; avoid treating an unspecified CP charge or waiver as a printed rule. Section 32.15 removes a unit when **both** balances reach zero; it does not establish how capture reaches that state.
3. Decide discovery/removal for dummies before claiming an executable outcome; §32.18 removes a dummy upon discovery but reviewed text does not enumerate every trigger. Section 32.56 separately captures assigned motorization points with a captured unit and permits enemy use; unrelated co-located points are not thereby assigned. Record stocks, assigned carriers, action ordering and audience-authorized observations atomically under a versioned policy; preserve replay and fog-of-war boundaries.

Distinguishing vectors, conditional on legal state and a selected interpretation: a combat entrant versus non-combat entrant; qualifying ZOC before entry versus same-event collision versus no qualifying ZOC; lone supply versus another counter and an actual defender; empty or partial stocks; a dummy versus real unit; assigned versus unassigned carriers; and a bare HQ with versus without parenthesized values. Check one capture per identity, exact stock/CP outcomes only after policy selection, and equivalent authorized views. These are proposed tests, not demonstrated reachable positions or test results.
