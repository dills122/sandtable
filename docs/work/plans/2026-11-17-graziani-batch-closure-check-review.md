# Graziani batch closure check — independent review

**Result:** The three bounded dispositions in the [candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-batch-closure-check.md) are supported, with additional timing and historical-note evidence below. No placement or assignment ruling follows from this review.

1. **Placement.** Commonwealth schedule key PDF p. 4 permits same-parent arrivals/returns to be placed together with desired attachments and detachments, subject to stacking; those not **assigned** the same parent must use separate hexes if possible. Land §19.13 distinguishes assigned from attached parents. Section 20.12 fixes reinforcement entry at the beginning of the Naval Convoy Arrival Phase and waives CP for debarkation and the first hex; §20.13 applies normal rules after arrival. Section 19.2 places new assignments and reassignments in the Reorganization Segment, while §19.42 gives ordinary non-assigned attachment timing and cost. These provisions narrow timing and costs, but do not expressly settle whether the schedule creates an arrival-specific exception or which assignment state governs its placement predicate. The entry and first-hex waiver is not an express waiver of attachment cost. Avoid inferring legal placement without actual eligible hexes, stacking, assignments and phase order.

2. **2 RTR.** OA PDF p. 11 lists it under unassigned armor and note `b` uses past-tense assignment on arrival to 4/7 Armored Brigade. Land §19.2 also says units enter already assigned; §19.21 requires an independent unit to be currently attached to a parent before assignment. Section 19.24 explicitly makes historical OA assignment shifts optional, subject to §19.21 and the listed month. Whether this arrival note is such a shift, a historical description, or a specific initial assignment remains an interpretation; §19.24 does not expressly identify this note. Scenario §60.47 says to use the reinforcement track as printed, bringing units where and when listed; it does not expressly decide that note's organizational effect. Section 59.2's free initial attachment/detachment applies to units listed to begin the scenario in the same hex, and does not plainly extend to later reinforcements. Do not infer automatic attachment, a free assignment, or optionality of this particular note solely from these passages.

3. **7 RTR.** The OA unassigned-armor row has no note and records `3/2`; §19.26's 2nd Armored Brigade example describes a possible swap, not a dated initial assignment. Section 19.66 separately illustrates 7 RTR filling a hypothetical 42 RTR vacancy in 1st Army Tank Brigade; that example likewise supplies no initial parent. Neither example closes the initial-parent question.
