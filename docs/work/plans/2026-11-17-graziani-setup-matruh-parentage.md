# Graziani setup: Matruh parentage and setup choice source check

**Disposition:** Bounded same-scan source check, not a new official ruling or independent edition. Scenario labels `23/70` do not establish Essex's or Durham's initial assigned parent. The scenario explicitly lists both as attached to Matruh before the owner's setup organization choice. The September erratum explicitly assigns Buffs and Hampshire to Matruh and conflicts with the OA's general no-assignment prohibition. Existing RUL-GRA-03 remains a planning interpretation, not a resolution supplied by these source pages.

1. Scenario §59.2, printed p. 25, defines the bold parent designation as identification for a unit that has been assigned to that parent **whether or not it still is**. Section 60.41, printed p. 27, places Matruh at D3714 and lists 1st Essex and 1st Durham Light Infantry with `23/70`, plus 1st South Staffordshires, following `Att:`. Section 59.2, p. 26, defines `Att.` as attached but not assigned to that listed parent. This proves the printed Matruh attachment and excludes assignment **to Matruh by that notation**; the `23/70` identification alone does not establish a present assignment to 23rd Brigade. This check does not establish any other initial assigned parent. See the earlier [OA note f check](2026-11-16-graziani-source-material-research.md#matruh-combat-characteristics) for the later assignment date.
2. Section 59.2, p. 26, allows the owner to reorganize units listed in the same starting hex, including free attachment and detachment, within formation and stacking restrictions. This supports preserving printed baseline separately from the eventual owner choice; it does not prove that all starting attachments survive that choice. The same page separately allows a listed unit already exceeding formation limits to operate normally, including itself being assigned or attached, while barring new subordinates and further attachments that violate the limits after detachment. Whether that clause applies to this Matruh setup, and how it interacts with the owner choice and erratum, remains unproved; do not reject every source-listed over-limit baseline automatically.
3. Errata §4.44b item 2 expressly says Buffs and Hampshire start campaign and Italian scenarios assigned to Matruh. Scenario §60.0 names the Italian scenario group, §60.21 says it has two scenarios and §60.22 identifies Graziani as one, so the textual scope includes Graziani. Commonwealth OA PDF p. 10 says no units may be assigned to the Garrison; PDF p. 11 lists both under unassigned infantry with `D`. The erratum is evidence of a specific correction but does not spell out how its exception interacts with the OA prohibition, attachment capacity, their placement, or §59.2's owner choice. Do not silently equate assigned with attached.
4. The OA p. 10 also prohibits voluntary movement of the HQ until it becomes Selby Force or the Operations Stage in which it is attacked in a Close Assault; normal conversion is GT12 stage 2, after Graziani ends GT6 stage 3 (§60.22). The actual attack may occur earlier; the printed wording does not by itself settle exactly when within the stage movement becomes legal, its persistence in later stages, or all restrictions on subordinate units. Do not encode an unconditional six-turn HQ movement ban.

## Consequences and remaining gates

Keep separate source baseline, assigned parent, current attachment, final setup choices and event history. Whether Essex or Durham has a different starting assigned parent remains unknown. Do not invent one from the label; do not invent Buffs/Hampshire's placement or attachment status from assignment alone. Capacity counting, exception precedence, costs, close-assault trigger timing and persistence require separately reviewed policy and relevant Land rules. This check does not certify full formation membership, reachable combat or movement legality.
