# Historical booklet source check: independent review

Reviewed 2026-11-17 against the linked [Pratuch survey](https://spigames.net/MovesScans/Moves49/CNAGPM49.pdf), [Noble Knight listing](https://www.nobleknight.com/P/2148003114/Campaign-for-North-Africa---Historical-Background-Book), [Decision Games product page](https://shop.decisiongames.com/ProductDetails.php?ProductCode=P3034), and local [setup source manifest](graziani-source-review/manifest.json).

**Disposition:** Evidence supports the draft's bounded conclusion, with one transcription qualification. Pratuch's p. 15 identifies a separate historical booklet, reports 16 pages and credits A. Nofi, R. Berg and J. Dunnigan; the web PDF text extraction reads “Noji” and “/6”, so visual verification of those characters was unavailable in this review. [LastDodo](https://www.lastdodo.com/en/items/7841947-the-campaign-for-north-africa) names Nofi, Berg and Dunnigan in game metadata and lists a historical background item; it does not independently establish booklet authorship. No booklet copy was inspected.

Noble Knight explicitly describes the historical book alone and lists 48 pages, with a back-in-stock notification and last-stocked date. Its listing conflicts with the survey's 16-page count; neither error nor different edition is established. Inspect a physical copy before resolving the discrepancy. Decision Games describes an update in design stage, not access to the original booklet. The local manifest pins scenario, errata, Commonwealth charts and land rules for a specific setup review; it neither lists a historical booklet nor determines distribution rights.

A targeted search for the booklet's full text did not identify publisher- or verified-rights-holder-authorized digital access. This is a limited search result, not proof of absence, ownership, or authorization of any third-party scan. No contact or purchase was made. Existing rules and errata access should not be treated as booklet access or permission. Historical context remains separate from authority for scenario rules and setup.
