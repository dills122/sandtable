# LA-19: initial non-AA tank points with an AA rating

**Result:** The original sources supply a concrete Italian starting candidate for §32.84's non-AA exception: II(M) is listed with M11/39 tanks, and the weapons chart gives that tank an AA rating. The exception supports retaining **non-AA tank TOE points** against the §32.8 anti-air modifications merely because of that rating. It does not establish an actual tank count after setup, permit a detailed Air action in Land-only play, or preserve any separately removed AA-type counter or point.

## Original-source comparison

- Scenario §60.31, printed p. 27, places the Maletti formation at **C3617** with II(M) tank battalion attached. Axis OA PDF p. 6 lists II(M) as tank battalion `l`, **7 × M11/39**, arrival `D`, with its note naming Maletti as the initial attachment. This repeats the specific identity already checked in the [M13/40 correction review](2026-11-18-graziani-m13-bar-erratum-review.md); the new comparison concerns its §32.84 classification, not a new tank roster census.
- Axis §4.48, PDF p. 29, lists the **M11/39** under Tank Weapon Systems, with **AA 1**, not as an AA weapon-system type. Land §32.84, p. 45, states that the foregoing AA modifications apply to AA TOE Strength Points and Replacement Points, not to non-AA points that happen to have an AA rating. Thus an M11/39 tank TOE point is a source-specific candidate for the latter distinction: the AA rating alone is not a reason to remove that tank point under §32.8.
- Land §32.83, p. 44, separately lists Italian AA units, AA TOE assigned to particular emplaced-gun units, heavy AA on specified artillery, and heavy AA replacements for modification or removal. Those rules require their own type/assignment decisions. §32.84 does not turn an AA-type point into a tank point, exempt an entire mixed unit from another specific rule, or establish later arrivals.

For a hypothetical accepted initial representation that includes an actual II(M) M11/39 tank point, compare that point with a separately identified Italian AA TOE point under the source-specific §32.83 categories. Preserve the tank point when applying §32.8 **solely** because it has AA 1; apply the relevant AA clause to the other point only after verifying type and assignment. This is a proposed classification vector, not an actual game event or proof of all seven points' availability at a later stage. Gun/tank losses, detachment, actual snapshot and counter representation remain separate.

An AA rating on a surviving tank also does not prove a Land-only anti-air action is available: §32.0 replaces detailed Air/Logistics play, and this bounded check does not derive an independent aircraft or AA combat procedure. No policy for other non-AA weapon types, captured AA, ship AA, fleet targeting or hidden-state disclosure is selected.
