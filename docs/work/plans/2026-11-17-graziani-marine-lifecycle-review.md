## Source reading

The [Commonwealth charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 20, place these rows under 1st Free French Brigade:

| Printed designation | Counter abbreviation | ID | TOE / weapon | Arrival | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st Bn d'inf de marine | `1 Marine` | `r` | `N` | `1/65` | `d`: Coloniale |
| 1st Bn de fusiliers | `1 Fslrs` | `ff` | `4 × Light AA` | `3/63` | `f`: 1st battalion de fusiliers marine |

PDF p. 21, under Unassigned Allied Units, lists `1st Company, Fr Mtr Marine`, abbreviation `1`, ID `v`, `N`, arrival `3/65`. Note `a` describes a company of the 1st Free French Motor Marine Battalion. [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1, §4.44b item 3, says the French Motor Marine Company's arrival date is wrong and should be `D`. The correction addresses that company; this page does not explicitly correct either brigade row or explain any relationship between the counters.
