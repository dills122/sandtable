- §60.41 places 1 KRRC and 3rd Coldstream Guards among C3922 units and lists 15 medium plus five heavy trucks there. §59.42 says first-line trucks must be assigned to units in their listed hex and may be divided as the player wishes. §59.63(A) converts each medium and heavy first-line truck into one attached Motorization Point, calculated hex by hex unless scenario-modified: the listed input is **20**, not evidence of a specific allocation or transport capacity. §59.66(B) applies its optional load to attached points not initially transporting men or guns; it does not establish a load here.
- KRRC's OA `n/N/D` and Coldstream's `p/N/D` crosswalk to infantry battalion-equivalents, maximum six TOE; the chart gives `10+` and `10` respectively. Its plus key describes **historical** provision of enough trucks. That baseline and the setup hex do not establish KRRC's present assigned transport.
- §32.54 directs sufficient points for **all historically motorized infantry** (`8+` or `10+`) before assignment to other infantry-type units. Its wording does not expressly define a per-hex priority boundary, settle the scenario-specific initial-allocation hierarchy or authorize remote transfer. Section 32.53 also describes normal units' built-in transport; the note correctly avoids deriving an individual transport quantity or ruling from that alone. The proposed Coldstream-versus-KRRC choice distinguishes interpretations only if points are otherwise legally available and all relevant setup and carrying rules are met.

The check does not resolve an initial distribution, loaded holdings, assignment and formation representation, other units' priority, actual target or patrol route. No exhaustive errata or runtime audit; no data changes or gameplay tests.
