# SGSU arrival erratum: independent review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Reviewed [SGSU arrival note](2026-11-18-graziani-sgsu-arrival-erratum.md) against [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 2, [common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) PDF p. 7, [Air](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF pp. 5–6, [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF pp. 28 and 43, and [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF pp. 2–4. Independent reading of same scans, not independent edition.

- **Correction and actual constraints:** Chart §20.3 prints an SGSU conversion entry of one infantry replacement point and supplies; errata says ignore that entry, SGSUs come as desired under §34.82, and require no replacement points. Air §34.82 specifies Naval Convoy Arrival Phase, Axis Tripoli/Tunisia boxes, Commonwealth Alexandria/Cairo hexes, enemy-action loss delay, nationality and aircraft-count restrictions. The note accurately avoids treating arrival as unrestricted or automatic. No replacement-point charge does not itself erase other supply or availability requirements; Air §35.14 separately gives SGSU operating supply costs.
- **Initial pools:** Scenario §§60.32 and .42 print 39 Italian and 14 Commonwealth SGSUs, respectively, within initial air-strength entries; §59.35 permits placement at owned air facilities subject to readying capacity. These counts do not fix particular selected placements or prove a new arrival. The note correctly distinguishes detailed-air setup from an established Land-only mechanism.
- **Abstraction:** Scenario §59.61 explicitly ignores trucks and supplies at air facilities when only Air is abstracted; §59.63(B) likewise ignores air-facility trucks in the stated abstract-motorization calculation when Air is abstracted. Air §35.0 describes SGSU counters as vehicles, medium trucks, but neither cited scenario provision explicitly names SGSU counters. This is relevant mode evidence, not enough by itself to declare all SGSUs removed, import all detailed-air arrival rules into Land-only, or decide their independent land role. The reviewed note's qualified boundary is appropriate.
- **Training:** Land §17.35 requires an eligible combat battalion at designated Basic Morale in the same hex each training stage, at least one-third assigned TOE strength, with stated type conditions. Air §§35.0 and .12 say SGSUs have no combat values or strength. Neither §20.3 correction nor these cases establishes an SGSU as the required instructor; no instructor, credited stages or replacement-training capacity follows from scenario's Australian training label. Reviewed note correctly retains the replacement-training cross-reference question.

Scope is named passages, not exhaustive later errata, all abstraction provisions, or proof of a six-turn event. Air §35.13 also states an eliminated counter may return one Game-Turn later under §34.8; any precise interaction with §34.82's **one complete Game-Turn** enemy-action restriction deserves its own timing analysis, not an inferred shortcut. No policy, data or runtime changes; no gameplay tests.
