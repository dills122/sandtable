# Review: French Motor Marine later scenario setup

**Disposition:** Scenario transcription and timeline supported. The new setup does not establish an intervening lifecycle. Qualify the note's statement that parent formation remains unproved: an earlier OA entry names a parent for its company row, though its linkage to this scenario listing is not established by the later setup alone.

- Visually checked [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29. Section 61.1 describes two Desert Fox scenarios using the same setup. Section 61.2 begins the race for Tobruk at Game-Turn 26, OpStage 3 and ends at the conclusion of Game-Turn 38, OpStage 3. Section 61.31's Commonwealth initial deployment lists **Msus B4004**, 1st Coy French Motor Marines, followed by **Trucks: 3 Medium**. This is a later scenario's starting row; it is not an observed survivor or truck transfer from earlier play.
- PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.41 separately lists 1st Coy French Motor Marines at C3926 and two medium trucks. Section 60.22 ends Graziani at GT6 OpStage 3. The similar names and later GT26 setup support comparison, not a proven unique counter or continuous unit identity, unchanged assignment, replacement, coexistence or transfer. The numerical truck entries establish printed setup allocations, not a history of specific trucks.
- The [prior transition follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-marine-transition-followup.md) records Commonwealth OA p. 21 note a as describing its company entry as a company of **1st Free French Motor Marine Battalion**. Its [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-marine-transition-followup-review.md) visually checked that row and note. Consequently “parent formation ... remain[s] unproved” is best read specifically as **the GT26 scenario row's linkage to that OA company row and to either later named battalion row**, not as an absence of any printed parent information for the OA row. The existing working identification for the Graziani entry is an interpretation, not proof of a later transition.
