# Fleet no-target and excess damage: independent source review

**Disposition:** No material error found in the [source note](2026-11-18-graziani-fleet-no-target-source.md). The printed opening fleet, synthetic chart calculation and bounded no-target/excess conclusions are supported; neither example establishes an actual attack or harbor state.

- **Attack and target:** Land §§32.71–.75, printed p. 44, limit attacks to Commonwealth ships in Alexandria Harbor, at most one per Operations Stage, secretly plotted in Fleet Assignment and described as occurring in Tactical Naval Movement. §32.74 selects a Bomb Points column by one die, adding two for a Campaign Game, then uses two sequential dice; its result is damage allocated among ships in Alexandria Harbor as Axis wishes. Map E rain or sandstorm bars the attack. Neither empty-target behavior nor disposition of damage beyond eligible ships' remaining ratings is expressly specified in these inspected clauses; the note correctly leaves phase mapping, weather and allocation choices open.
- **Damage:** Land §§30.34 and 30.36, p. 42, reduce a ship's Gun Rating by damage and declare a ship sunk and unrepairable when its entire Gun Rating is eliminated. Those sentences do not specify an overkill recipient or disposition, and do not establish a negative rating. The note properly distinguishes its hypothetical from the Ajax example and its separate source discrepancy.
- **Synthetic lookup:** Common chart §41.5 p. 12 places `43–66` in the **2** result for Commonwealth Fleet at Bomb Points **81–120**. Under the note's expressly non-Campaign example, initial column die **4** selects that fourth column; sequential **43** gives 2 damage. One eligible ship with one remaining Gun Rating point is an assumed state, giving a one-point excess; actual reachability, weather, target and dice are unproved. September errata §41.5 corrects the Barrage Points headings and a flak label, not this stated Bomb Points cell.
- **Starting ships:** Commonwealth chart §30.6 PDF p. 31 prints eleven initial ships: one battleship, three marked cruisers and seven destroyers. At most one marked cruiser and two destroyers may instead deploy at Malta, leaving **8–11 at Alexandria** on that schedule. Scenario §60.45 PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28 says the fleet deploys under the schedule, ships cannot move until GT2 OpStage 1, and their combat ratings initially need no repair; September errata §60.45 retains that paragraph and discards the later contradictory list. Starting presence is not proof of surviving eligible ships at a later attack snapshot, including within GT1 after any permitted attacks.
