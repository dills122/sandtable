# Tripoli loading-stage boundary: source review

**Disposition:** Supports [source-priority note](2026-11-18-graziani-source-priority-next.md)'s distinction between an actual box loading/unloading event, a setup-preloaded group and an arriving unit. The checked text does not resolve whether attaching Motorization Points to an abstract Supply Unit counts as loading, or whether setup loading consumes the opening OpStage. No legal movement or source hierarchy is selected. Same-scan informed reading, not an independent edition.

- Land §8.88 treats Tripoli/Tunisia boxes as unlimited-capacity Supply Dumps and bars moving a truck or Motorization Point in the Operations Stage in which it loads and/or unloads supplies. The case appears in the box provision; its scope and its interaction with an abstract Supply Unit assignment need their own interpretation. Unlimited dump capacity is not an unrestricted stacking or movement rule.
- Scenario §60.33 lists 25 light, 140 medium and 40 heavy second/third-line trucks at Tripoli. §59.63(B) converts eligible medium/heavy points to unattached Motorization Points and ignores air-facility trucks when Air is abstracted. §59.66(C) permits each eligible group of 30 **other** unattached points in a hex to start carrying an additional regular Supply Unit and expressly treats Tripoli/Tunisia boxes as hexes for that case. The word additional describes the optional supply grant, not duplicate identities: it does not establish that a given group was chosen or that any one of the eight §60.92(B) listed units is that extra unit.
- Scenario §60.92(B) separately lists eight regular Supply Units in the Tripoli box at 5 Ammo and 60 Fuel each. Co-location of stock and points is not a proven load or movement. Section 59.66(C)'s preloaded setup wording does not expressly timestamp a loading action in the first OpStage; distinguish a selected preloaded group from a later in-stage load without deciding whether §8.88 applies to either case.
- Land §32.32 requires 30 points for a real Supply Unit and limits attachment/detachment to Organization. Section 32.31 permits a Supply Unit to move by land or rail in its arrival OpStage; §32.33 imposes separate phase and stacking conditions. Neither cited permission explicitly waives §8.88 if its loading predicate applies, nor does arrival itself prove a load occurred. Actual carrier, cargo, event timing, off-map movement and the opening-stage sequence remain independently required.
