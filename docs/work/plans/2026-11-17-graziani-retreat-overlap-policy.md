# Conditional retreat overlap: bounded policy comparison

**November 17, 2026. Planning only; no policy selected or admitted.** The [independent source review](2026-11-17-graziani-retreat-overlap-source-review.md) supports a conditional overlap between Land §§15.34 and 10.36, not a source-resolved priority. This comparison concerns that overlap alone. It does not establish a historical Graziani position, a reachable scenario state, exhaustive errata coverage, or an operational routing rule.

## Evidence and boundary

In the [synthetic witness](2026-11-17-graziani-retreat-overlap-source.md), phasing motorized `P` is in non-motorized marsh defender `E`'s ZOC, cannot assault or otherwise attack `E`, has no qualifying Holding Off barrage, and has no other unit attacking `E` or applicable §10.32 exception. The premises of both prescriptions hold before their interaction is decided. The source review supports a geometrically possible one-hex step and a three-step route ending three hexes from `E`; neither establishes a uniquely required route. Land §10.36 specifies three additional disorganization points, movement CP costs, no doubling back, no retreat through enemy ZOC and surrender if forced into enemy ZOC, with §10.26's friendly-occupant qualification requiring attention. These are source findings, not evidence that §10.36 overrides §15.34.

Section 15.34's terrain-specific unable-to-attack and other-unit-attack condition and §10.36's inability to assault or Hold Off cover different predicates. Section 10.31 speaks of attacking enemy-occupied hexes projecting ZOC, not an obligation for every individual phasing unit to attack. Section 10.3 applies to the phasing player under the cited errata. A claim that one section is categorically more specific requires an adopted interpretation of their scope and interaction; the review identifies no express hierarchy.

## Boundary controls and admission requirements

- **Targets and quantification:** The witness has one enemy `E`. For multiple projectors, decide whether §15.34 is evaluated for each enemy the unit cannot attack and what another unit attacking that same enemy requires; separately decide whether §10.36 is evaluated per unit, enemy, or enemy-occupied hex. Determine which attack or Holding Off affects which predicate and target, including one enemy attacked and another not. These quantifiers require source review; the witness does not establish them. Test mixed targets, multiple projectors in one hex and separate hexes, and separate acting units.
- **Alternate attack and Hold Off:** A second unit attacking the relevant enemy changes §15.34's predicate; do not assume that alone determines §10.36. A qualifying Holding Off barrage changes §10.36's predicate, but whether it counts as an attack for §15.34's alternate-attacker wording needs source-backed interpretation. Distinguish who performs it and which enemy or occupied hex is affected. Mere availability, a different target, or an unqualified barrage cannot silently satisfy a same-enemy condition.
- **Retreat and cost:** Three hexes moved and ending three hexes away are distinct constraints; the witness demonstrates both, not a general routing algorithm. Define choice authority, path and destination legality, no doubling back, other ZOCs, friendly-occupied ZOC under §10.26, blocked and partially traversable routes, surrender trigger and timing, and whether state changes during retreat alter legality. Do not generalize surrender to every blocked path without an adopted basis. Test both distances, alternate legal paths, occupied-ZOC and forced-ZOC cases, no-path and partial-path cases, and all costs and state transitions.
- **Authority and privacy:** Authoritative adjudication may use full state to validate predicates and paths. Specify phasing and opposing views independently, including selectable choices, errors, state updates, event projections, replay and intelligence requests. Availability, enumeration, rejection, ordering and timing can reveal hidden units or ZOCs even when event text is redacted. Define side-safe representation and behavior when authoritative answers depend on hidden facts; test paired worlds indistinguishable to each side across every observable boundary. Intelligence proposals remain non-authoritative and receive only their permitted view.
- **Persistence and replay:** Bind an accepted interpretation and all dependent timing, route, cost, disclosure and failure choices to a versioned ruleset/configuration hash. Record authoritative inputs and outcomes sufficiently for deterministic replay; project redacted events independently for each audience. Existing saves retain their original semantics or undergo an explicit migration, never silent reinterpretation. Test save/reload and replay across the policy boundary.
