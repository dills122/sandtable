# 6th New Zealand Brigade arrival: source follow-up

**Result:** retain [RUL-GRA-04](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-04-6th-nz-brigade-arrival-working-interpretation) as a **planning interpretation**, not an official correction or admitted ruleset. Scenario §60.47 expressly directs use of the Commonwealth reinforcement track as printed; the track places the brigade at GT6, Operations Stage 3. The OA agrees. Land §20.11's example instead places it at GT2, Stage 3 alongside the field artillery. This remains a real textual conflict, not a demonstrated corrected misprint.

## Evidence and hierarchy

OA notation is stage/turn. | Corroborates both schedule dates and identifies four brigade rows; OA and track are records in the same source package, not independent editions. |
| Land rules p. 31 §20.11 | Whole-unit reinforcements arrive as specified on the reinforcement track; its following example describes both 6th NZ Brigade and its three battalions and 6th NZ Field Artillery in September `3/2`. | Example contradicts the track about brigade timing, even while the general rule points to that track. It agrees about artillery. The example is not a second brigade arrival. |
| [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), pp. 1–4 | §4.43a adds omitted GT28 and GT64 entries and corrects a GT76 reference; no explicit correction of this brigade date or §20.11 was found in this packet. | Apply relevant published corrections; these listed corrections do not settle the NZ conflict. Absence here does not prove no later or other clarification exists. |

The scenario's express track instruction and §20.11's own general direction support selecting the track over its conflicting example. That is a reasoned local choice, not a claimed universal printed rule that examples always yield to schedules. An alternative is to hold admission until a controlling clarification appears; applying the example's GT2 date is another possible ruling but conflicts with the scenario-directed track and OA. None authorizes both dates.

## Boundaries for a future decision

If the track interpretation is accepted and versioned, keep the artillery's GT2/3 event separate from the brigade HQ and all three battalions at GT6/3. Preserve the track's accompanying truck quantities as source data; determine applicable abstract motorization, eligibility, placement, training and assignment separately. Test no premature or duplicate brigade, all four dated records, artillery independence, accepted stage boundaries, checkpoint replay and rejection of wrong-date replay. Do not infer that arrival forces training or excludes otherwise legal movement; see [training-order analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-training-order-next.md). No runtime or content admission follows from this note.
