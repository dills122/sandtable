# Graziani abstract supply: shortage and order continuation

**Disposition:** The [expenditure review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-expenditure-review.md) correctly leaves source choice, shortfall, splitting and simultaneous debits unresolved. Section 32 also has a distinct air-transport prepayment (§32.37). Its origin-hex source and timing must not be silently replaced with the ordinary combat-unit draw rule. No inspected passage supplies a complete shortage or competing-orders algorithm.

## Source obligations and limits

Visual inspection of [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43, §§32.0, 32.11, 32.15–.16, 32.21–.25 and 32.37, same scan as the existing review:

- Section 32.0 replaces ordinary Land and Air supply expenditures with this section's abstract Ammo and Fuel costs; those points must be expended for units to move and fight, subject to specific exceptions: §32.24 exempts specified movement uses and §32.25 exempts patrol supply expenditure. Neither all movement nor every combat-like activity incurs a charge. Section 32.11 ignores references to use and expenditure of Ammo, Fuel, Stores and Water outside these rules and expressly says no attrition for lack of Stores, etc. Do not transplant a detailed-game shortage rule without identifying independent applicable authority. The attrition sentence alone does not settle every consequence of deficient abstract Ammo or Fuel.
- Section 32.15 tracks Ammo and Fuel separately, reduces them on expenditure and removes a Supply Unit when both reach zero. Rearrangement at no cost is permitted between Supply Units in the same hex during Organization; this permission does not itself authorize cross-hex transfer, splitting a single payment, or a transfer at combat time. Section 32.16 permits a friendly combat unit to draw from any friendly Supply Unit within the stated trace range and restrictions. Neither line states a tie-break, reservation order or treatment of a cost exceeding a selected counter's stock.
- Section 32.21 gives individual participating battalion-equivalent Ammo requirements, §32.22 fixes expenditure at the instant of combat; §32.23 fixes first land movement payment and additional payment at the instant CPA is exceeded. The source does not say whether an otherwise valid group may choose a subset to participate, whether multiple simultaneous claims have priority, or whether insufficient points cancel, delay, reduce or change an action. A processing order must not silently decide a contested result.
- Section 32.37 separately requires the owning player to expend **12 Fuel Points before transport**, from any Supply Unit in the hex of origin. It also limits air transport to one Supply Unit per player per game turn, specifies Organization timing and additional origin, destination, enemy proximity and direction conditions, and says the air-transported unit cannot move at all that OpStage but may be drawn upon. The source says “any supply unit in the hex of origin”; whether multiple counters may collectively pay, whether the carried unit itself may pay, and what happens on a shortfall are not expressly resolved there. This is not evidence that a Supply Unit's land-movement fuel uses the same source predicate.
- The [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-shortage-continuation-review.md) identifies [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed p. 26 §59.66B: certain initially loaded attached Motorization Points use their initial supplies before drawing from a Supply Unit. Its applicability to Graziani setup and interaction with §32.11 remain open. Do not assume it overrides the abstract rules, or that every expenditure necessarily draws from a Supply Unit.
- September 1979 [errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 §32.0, warns that abstract rules were untested; it does not supply a shortage algorithm in that note. Other unnumbered or later corrections are not excluded.

## Minimum accounting obligations and unresolved policy

For a future admitted ruling, preserve resource identity, source eligibility, balances and timing at each specified event; never debit an enemy, an inaccessible ordinary-draw source, a different resource or more than an authorized available balance. A legal source and sufficient aggregate stock do not prove a legal split or a unique selection. Preserve no-cost same-hex Organization rearrangement as a separate event, subject to capacity and other applicable rules. At zero of one resource retain the counter if the other is positive; at zero of both remove it, while separately deciding how removal interacts with pending claims. These are bookkeeping constraints, not a source-specified transaction protocol.

A versioned ruling still needs to choose source selection and player agency, whether splitting is allowed, contention and event order, atomicity and rollback, failure outcomes, intermediate zero/removal, and the behavior of a move already underway when its additional fuel becomes due. It must separately settle eligibility and source allocation for air transport and supply-unit movement, and whether initially loaded Motorization Points under §59.66B apply before Supply Unit draws. Record sufficient pre-event facts and resulting debits for deterministic replay; do not assert that source text prescribes a reservation system, negative balance, implicit credit, free activity, forced partial action, automatic cancellation, universal initiative order, or priority for either combat or movement.

## Synthetic test vectors

Fixtures assume all unrelated legality predicates independently satisfied; they are future acceptance cases, not played or executed tests.

1. One reachable friendly source has exactly 1 Ammo for a non-phasing battalion assault; compare 0 Ammo with identical geometry. First has the printed requirement; source alone does not specify the second outcome. Check expenditure at combat, not declaration.
2. Two reachable friendly sources hold 1 Ammo each; a phasing battalion assault costs 2. Contrast one source holding 2. Combined availability does not settle split payment. With two different eligible sources each holding 2, no printed tie-break selects either.
3. Two individually eligible assaults each cost 1 and share a source holding 1. Reverse input enumeration while holding event facts constant. A chosen policy must expose priority or unresolved contention and avoid inventing stock; the source does not establish which assault wins or what follows.
4. Tank battalion has exactly 2 Fuel for first eligible land movement, halts and resumes within CPA; compare crossing CPA with no remaining Fuel. First charge is due at first movement, additional points at the crossing. Amount and shortage consequence at that crossing remain open; do not retroactively assert automatic undo or free extra movement.
5. Supply counters in one hex hold complementary stock. Compare explicit Organization rearrangement against attempted pooling at a later combat instant; latter needs a separate split rule. Check maximum capacity and both-zero removal using atomic balances according to the selected policy.
6. An otherwise legal air transport has 12 Fuel at an origin-hex Supply Unit versus only 11; compare 6+6 on two origin counters and 12 on a counter elsewhere. Exact origin stock meets the printed payment amount; split and shortage remain open, and elsewhere alone does not meet §32.37's origin-hex wording. Check payment precedes transport and the once-per-player-per-game-turn limit.
7. Retain 0 Ammo/positive Fuel and positive Ammo/0 Fuel counters; remove 0/0. Test two claims against a counter becoming 0/0 without deciding claim priority by container iteration order. If air-transport payment empties its carried counter, separately test removal and transport interaction under an accepted ruling.
8. Compare a patrol with otherwise eligible movement, and §32.24 exempt movement uses with a fuel-requiring tank movement. Preserve specific exceptions; do not impose a universal charge from §32.0.
9. Compare an initially loaded attached Motorization Point in the circumstances of §59.66B with an otherwise equivalent point without initial supplies. First establish applicability to Graziani and interaction with §32.11; only then test initial-stock-before-draw order. Do not treat this fixture as an established override.
