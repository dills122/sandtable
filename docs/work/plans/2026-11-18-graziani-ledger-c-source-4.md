# TR-01: named Australian trainee and morale horizon

**Result:** The original OA identifies the **16th Australian Infantry Brigade** as a source-baseline trainee candidate for the previously identified Cairo instructor comparison. Under an explicitly hypothetical zero-credit start at its printed untrained morale, reaching its printed designated morale requires four six-stage increases: 24 qualifying Operations Stages. Graziani has only 18 Operations Stages. This arithmetic does **not** prove actual starting morale, zero prior credit, legal instructor co-location or an actual final morale.

## Source facts and conditional arithmetic

- Scenario §60.41, [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, starts the **6th Australian Division in training at Cairo and/or Helwan**, excluding the 19th Australian Brigade and two named artillery regiments. Its [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 13 lists the 16th Australian Infantry Brigade HQ (`16 Aus`) and 2/1, 2/2 and 2/3 Australian Infantry Battalions as `N`, `D`; the sheet prints Basic Morale `+1 (−3)`. It separately lists the excluded 19th Brigade and its battalions at `2/9`. The [prior roster comparison](2026-11-17-graziani-australia-shell-next.md) already identified these OA entries; this check uses the named 16th brigade to bound the training calculation. Do not confuse it with the separate 16th Infantry Brigade of the 70th Division.
- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 27–28 §§17.31–.35, identifies the parenthesized rating as untrained and the other as the maximum attainable through training. Each **six Operations Stages** of training raises untrained Basic Morale by one, never beyond the designated level. Section 17.35 permits an infantry division or brigade to use an infantry-type instructor battalion, with the stated same-hex, designated-morale and one-third-strength conditions. The [reviewed 2 KOR candidate](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-c-source-3-review.md) supplies a potential instructor, not a proven actual match.
- If a represented 16 Aus trainee begins with Basic Morale **−3**, has **zero previously credited training stages**, remains eligible, and receives 24 accepted training stages, the four increments are `−3 → −2 → −1 → 0 → +1`. Scenario §60.22 starts GT1/OpStage 1 and ends after GT6/OpStage 3: at most **18** in-scenario stage positions. Under the same zero-credit assumptions, even 18 qualifying stages supply at most three increases, reaching **0**. The source does not establish that every position is a legal training stage, that the scenario begins at precisely −3 with no prior credit, or how interruption and any previously credited partial interval are represented. Do not turn the conditional bound into an asserted scenario outcome.

The 16 Aus HQ/attached-battalion representation and resulting morale subject need admission under actual owner setup. Cairo and/or Helwan does not specify a shared hex with 2 KOR; its presence, state, strength and morale each require checking. Section 17.33's no-CP condition applies to the **training unit**, and the scenario's training label does not prove completion or continuous training. This is morale training, not a replacement-point schedule or an automatic restriction on optional untrained play. No source policy or actual movement is inferred.
