# Initial Motorization Points: attachment and transfer boundary

**Date:** November 18, 2026. This bounded check follows [Giarabub allocation](2026-11-17-graziani-giarabub-initial-allocation.md), its [review](2026-11-17-graziani-giarabub-initial-allocation-review.md), and the [conditional medium-truck chart reading](2026-11-18-graziani-initial-motorization-source.md). It finds no checked case that directly assigns an initial Motorization Point to Giarabub or settles a pregame transfer from the second/third-line-derived pool. The in-play truck attachment exceptions below distinguish particular transfers; applying them to the Land-only setup and a particular counter remains an interpretation, not a chosen rule.

## Checked source details

- Land §32.52, printed/PDF p. 44, says each scenario lists initially available Motorization Points, further points arrive as scenario reinforcements, and players may also bring them as replacements at a rate equal to their Medium Truck Replacement Points. It does not set an initial individual attachment, common unlimited pool or free redistribution event. Scenario §60.92(A) speaks separately of the **ability to bring in** one point per Medium Truck Point; this is not an extra grant from already converted initial trucks.
- Scenario §§59.63(A–B) convert first-line medium/heavy trucks to **attached** points hex by hex, and second/third-line trucks to **unattached** points; §59.64 allows initial distributions to be altered **once the game starts** under Land attachment/detachment rules, with §59.66(B)'s exception. The `Anywhere in Libya` row's 100 medium and 25 heavy are second/third-line trucks, hence a conditional 125-point unattached initial subtotal under that conversion, not a selected Giarabub assignment. Physical placement, available pool and recipient are separate facts.
- Land §8.96 permits Truck Points to attach to a unit bearing a historical designation and puts attached trucks under the highest parent formation to which they are currently attached. §8.97 ordinarily limits truck attachment to the Organization Phase, with wording excepting the Operations Stage in which the unit is attached to a Parent Unit. It separately limits detachment to the Organization Phase except when a parent detaches a subsidiary during Movement/Combat, when any portion of its attached trucks may accompany that newly detached unit. This last exception describes **already attached parent trucks**, not a direct statement that initially unattached second/third-line points become attached before play. It does not establish Giarabub's actual parent, the stated unit-attachment event or a legal transfer from that pool.
- §32.51 makes Motorization Points medium-equivalent except where modified in that major case; whether and how the §8.96/.97 truck exceptions carry over to this abstract setup is not explicitly specified by these cases. §59.66(B) further limits detachment of points while they transport optional initial carried supplies, permitting detachment only while remaining attached to a subsidiary combat unit of the parent. Do not apply the broad §8.97 detachment exception without reconciling that special loaded-cargo wording, nor assume any point starts loaded.
- §32.54 says historically motorized infantry (`8+` or `10+`) must receive sufficient points before assignment to other infantry types. The [prior counter/OA check](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-giarabub-motorization-review.md) records `hh` as `8+`; the priority does not say where points originate, how multiple recipients share scarce points, or whether §59.63(B)'s initially unattached status is overridden. It also does not state priority over every non-infantry use. A general `8+` marking is not a source-named motor pool or a completed attachment.

## Distinguishing cases after a policy decision

Compare a point already attached to a parent when a subsidiary legally detaches during Movement/Combat against a previously unattached point merely co-located with it; compare both before the game and after Organization begins, with and without §59.66(B) initial cargo. Check point ownership, historical designation, actual parent/attachment, competing priority subjects, source-pool conservation and the specific applicable phase. These are synthetic source distinctions, not proof that any route, setup allocation or transfer is legal. The prior one-infantry-TOE / medium CPA 20 example remains conditional on classification, actual carrying, chart applicability and absence of contrary modification.
