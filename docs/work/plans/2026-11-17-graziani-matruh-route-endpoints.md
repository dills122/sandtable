For a positive witness, record each ordered adjacent pair, seam identity,
terrain, feature and applicable state from one snapshot. One fully valid
witness can prove an existential route even with unrelated unknowns. To prove
no route, exhaust **all** eligible endpoint choices and continuations under an
accepted finite search domain, including possible branches and incoming ZOC
across boundaries; otherwise return unknown. A blocked road or missing record
alone cannot prove failure. A negative rail survey must likewise cover every
eligible rail branch, not merely the selected seed.

## State and policy gates

For actual Commonwealth rail movement, §8.73 requires a qualifying start on
rail, stage CP history and no prohibited enemy ZOC or enemy-occupied hex;
§8.78 separately restricts rail west of an Axis combat-occupied rail hex.
Resolve §10.26's friendly-combat-unit movement exception before deciding ZOC,
and check occupancy independently. §8.76 excludes destroyed and unbuilt
track; §24.65 separately bars construction or rebuilding in enemy-controlled
**or** enemy-occupied hexes. Check stage, actor, capacity, damage, construction,
control, stacking, phase and errata, including reorganization points; initial
railhead or map ink does not establish any of them. See [rail scope review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-scope-review.md)
and [ZOC review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-zoc-policy-review.md).

For victory convoy proof, decide and version source control, subject/unit
binding, actual versus hypothetical convoy, direction, terrain, range,
occupancy and ZOC; preserve the source's from-Tobruk language and distinguish
it from Commonwealth's trace-back wording. Do not import rail-specific gates
or local-supply range automatically. Bind permitted observations and result
timing to the privacy contract; unknown hidden occupancy or incoming projection
is not clear terrain. See [victory direction review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-convoy-direction-review.md)
and [policy review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-policy-review.md). The
[endpoint review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-route-endpoints-review.md)
independently checked these distinctions against the same source scans.
