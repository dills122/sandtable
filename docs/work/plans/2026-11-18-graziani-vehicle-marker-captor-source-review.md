# Vehicle-marker captor source: review

**Disposition:** The [follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-vehicle-marker-captor-source.md) accurately distinguishes destroyed-tank capture relocation, ordinary repair towing, broken-down-vehicle wording and marker secrecy. No material discrepancy found in the checked passages; captor knowledge and the two relocation/direction interpretations remain open.

Visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF pp. 22, 34–35 and 45, and [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 1–3 numbered entries. Verified local SHA-256: Land `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`; errata `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`. This informed reread uses the same scans, not an independently authenticated edition.

- §14.52 requires immediate choice between permanent elimination and capture when the enemy enters a destroyed-tank-marker hex without other friendly combat units there. §14.53 says captured destroyed tanks **may** be moved three hexes in any direction at capture and bars enemy units, enemy ZOCs and impassable terrain in those three hexes. “May” leaves movement optional; it does not explicitly say a one- or two-hex relocation is allowed. Preserve that distinction from §21.55's explicit **maximum** three hexes for captured broken-down vehicles.
- §14.54 treats destroyed tanks like other units marked for possible repair except for §14.53 and permits towing to a repair facility in the phasing player's Repair Phase. §21.64 limits towing from enemy-controlled hexes and explicitly excepts capture-time towing, citing §§21.55 and 14.53; §21.67 directs broken-down vehicles and destroyed tanks **if towed** toward the nearest temporary or major repair facility. These passages do not expressly decide whether §21.67 constrains the §14.53 instant relocation. Nor does §21.64 itself erase §14.53's separate path wording.
- §14.46 calls for precise marker records; addendum §3.6 says contents of broken-down vehicle, destroyed tank and abandoned vehicle markers are secret. §§22.11–.14 cover repair eligibility and separate treatment of captured vehicles. They do not specify a complete captor audience or an automatic public inventory. A type-dependent repair decision creates an information-design question, not proof that all or none of the lot must be revealed.

No directly numbered §14.5, §21.6 or §22.1 correction was found in the inspected errata sequence; this is not an exhaustive indirect or later-correction search. No legal capture witness, applicable Land-only abstraction decision, runtime/data edit or gameplay test was established.
