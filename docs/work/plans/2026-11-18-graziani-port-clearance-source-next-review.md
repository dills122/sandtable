# Graziani port clearance: independent source review

**Disposition:** Accept the note's Land-only caution and distinction between
mine and ship-obstruction procedures. The previously OCR-only §50.2
identification is confirmed in the original scan. September errata §30.58
explicitly corrects the cross-reference to §55.2.

1. **Cross-reference verified.** Land p. 42 §30.58 actually points to §50.2
   for ports blocked by mines, etc. Original Air/Logistics PDF/printed p. 21
   identifies §50.2 as the ammunition consumption rates chart; PDF p. 27 /
   printed p. 23 identifies §55.2 as blocking harbors. This makes §55.2 a
   intended reference: September 1979 errata PDF p. 2 explicitly corrects
   §30.58 to §55.2. This is a direct Land-rule cross-reference, not merely a
   hypothetical typo, and strengthens evidence for some port-blockage rules
   carrying into Land-only play. Section 30.58 states a personnel handling
   threshold; the corrected reference does not independently specify how to
   implement clearance costs or recover efficiency under abstract supply.
2. **Mode scope remains unsettled.** Land p. 4 §2.1 says §§6–32 contain all
   rules necessary for the Land Game and describes §§48–58 as Logistics,
   which substitutes a detailed supply system for the Land-only abstraction.
   Section 32.11 further limits applicable supply rules; §32.36 nevertheless
   requires an efficiency threshold for Commonwealth supply-unit sea movement.
   The general scope statements and specific efficiency reference do not settle
   the lifecycle or which detailed port rules, if any, apply. Keep any
   automatic recovery and obstruction clearance unadmitted pending a selected,
   versioned interpretation.
3. **Mine removal differs from ship clearance.** Air/Logistics p. 24 §55.27
   prescribes a separate die roll per air-laid mine, removal on 1–3, and
   Construction Stage restrictions on the engineer. Section 55.26 instead
   supplies engineer and resource requirements for removing blocking effects;
   §55.25 identifies San Giorgio's initial three-level effect. Neither the mine
   roll nor its unstated resource charge establishes the San Giorgio procedure
   or its Land-only availability. The source says “Construction Stage”; it
   should not silently be normalized to another phase without reconciliation.
4. **Off-map ports require separate scope.** Section 55.21 lists Bizerta and
   Tripoli along with Alexandria, Aboukir and Rosetta as ports that may not be
   *so blocked*, in its discussion of a controller obstructing a harbor.
   Section 55.27 separately addresses enemy air-laid mines. Do not generalize
   the §55.21 restriction to every kind of mine, port effect or recovery without
   further interpretation. Section 55.17 separately treats Bizerta in the
   Tunis Box and specifies its availability; none of these cases establishes
   a universal off-map exception or a Land-only clearance policy. Tobruk's
   scenario-specific obstruction remains a separate question.
