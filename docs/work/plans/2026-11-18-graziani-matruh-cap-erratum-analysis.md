# Matruh attachment cap: erratum and specific row

**Result:** Errata §19.5 expressly states the *general* maximum-attachment chart counts extra attachments in addition to normally assigned units, including when some assignees are elsewhere. It does not expressly say whether Matruh's two exceptionally assigned battalions count against its separate OA statement that up to six units may be attached. Both six-total and six-additional readings remain source-reconciliation candidates; no policy selected.

## Evidence and interpretation

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 29 §§19.11–.13, distinguish assignment from attachment. A unit can be assigned to one parent and attached to another, and an assigned unit need not be co-located. Section 19.4 says normally assigned units may attach/detach and describes additional non-assigned attachments over assignment limits. Section 19.45 separately addresses substitution for missing assigned units; §19.46 addresses nested attachments and its exception. Assignment, presence, attachment and chart count cannot be treated as synonymous.
- [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 §19.5, expressly explains that chart-listed attachments are additional to normally assigned units, even when some normally assigned units are elsewhere. Thus an absent assignee does **not** turn its normal-assignment place into an extra chart slot. The clarification supports six additional non-assigned attachments under a general-chart reading, not six total merely because assignees are absent. It does not explicitly mention Matruh, the two later-assigned battalions, or change its OA wording.
- [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF p. 6 §19.5, has an explicit **Matruh Garrison GT1–12** row: six units, no tank, one gun class. Its key and non-shell company modifier qualify how units and maximum attachments are counted; see [capacity source follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-matruh-capacity-source-followup.md). The row is a maximum-attachment entry, strengthening the general additional-attachments reading. The row itself does not say whether exceptional Matruh assignees consume the six when attached.
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 10, says Matruh is a brigade-level HQ, no units may be assigned, and up to six units may be attached, referring to the Maximum Attachments Chart. Errata PDF p. 1 §4.44b item 2 instead explicitly assigns 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshires to Matruh at campaign and Italian-scenario start. The latter creates a named exception to the no-assignment text, but does not expressly replace its six-attached wording, locate either battalion, or state whether either is initially attached. OA's specific six-attached wording supports a total-attached reading; the general correction's additional-to-assignees wording supports an additional reading. Neither directly spells out their interaction after the assignment correction.

For a **conditional distinction**, assume both exceptional assignees are present and attached, six otherwise eligible non-assigned units are attached, and all type, counting, timing and stacking constraints are satisfied. A six-total interpretation counts eight attached against six; a six-additional interpretation counts six non-assigned against six. This is an illustrative discriminator, not a legal setup or outcome. If either assignee is elsewhere or not attached, do not count it as currently attached; §19.5 still says normal assigned units elsewhere do not reduce the chart's extra attachments under the general reading. Whether the two exceptional assignees are “normally assigned” for that rule is part of the unresolved reconciliation. Free company equivalents, indirect attachments, and initial scenario exceptions require independent treatment.

Prior [organization follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-organization-followup.md) and [capacity source follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-matruh-capacity-source-followup.md) already preserve this conflict. This reading confirms their disposition rather than admitting either interpretation, assigned units' placement, a legal action, or blanket type eligibility. The row's GT1–12 period and OA's Selby transition at GT12 OpStage 2 need separate timing reconciliation if relevant; no timing ruling here.
