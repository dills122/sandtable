# Independent source check: abstract supply capture

**Finding:** Both capture notes preserve the essential entry and ZOC triggers and correctly leave stock allocation and precedence unresolved. One material refinement: §32.56 affirmatively addresses motorization assigned to a captured unit; the carrier outcome is not wholly unspecified.

| Topic | Independent reading |
| --- | --- |
| Entry trigger | §32.13 says a Supply Unit has no combat capabilities; if **any enemy combat unit enters its hex**, the supply unit is captured “and its supplies used immediately and freely.” The wording supports neither merely adjacent entry nor an invented free-use amount, resource replenishment, durable ownership or stock consumption algorithm. Immediate/free use applies to supplies; do not turn that phrase into proof every balance becomes zero or that use can be postponed without a ruling. |
| Occupied hex | §8.13 generally prohibits a unit entering a hex containing an enemy unit, with a stated §27.4 exception. §32.13 does not expressly state its priority. A narrow entry exception for otherwise undefended supply is a plausible **project ruling**, not printed precedence. It does not authorize entry with an enemy combat defender still present. Multiple noncombat occupants also need handling. |
| ZOC | §10.29 says a non-combat unit **alone in an enemy ZOC** at any time in the enemy Movement/Combat Phase is captured if it has **no strength of any type**. A Supply Unit's lack of combat capabilities is relevant but does not expressly define whether its contents count as strength or define “alone” among multiple counters. §10.21 limits ZOC projection; mere adjacency is insufficient. The ZOC capture text does not specify that the captured counter disappears or that §32.13's entry-based supplies clause automatically triggers. |
| Carrier | §32.32 permits Motorization Points to be attached/detached to supply units in Organization and requires 30 for a real unit, six for a dummy. §32.56 says if the unit to which Motorization Points are assigned is captured, **those points are also captured and may be used by the enemy**. Thus, if a real Supply Unit with motorization assigned is captured and these clauses apply, source supports capture of the assigned carrier points as well. This does not settle which points were assigned in actual state, carrier placement, instantaneous reuse, continued assignment, or dummy/discovery interaction. Do not extend the rule to unrelated co-located or already detached points. |
| Empty and dummy | §32.15 removes a supply unit when **both** ammo and fuel are zero. §32.18 removes a dummy upon discovery and does not enumerate discovery triggers in that case. Real/dummy privacy, event ordering and duplicate prevention require a reviewed protocol. |
| Initial scenario | §60.92(B–C), printed pp. 28–29, supports the cited Tobruk 3 × 15/50, Bardia 2 × 10/40, and Matruh 5 × 40/60; dummy groups and dump placement are separately printed. These source stocks do not prove capture reachability, current composition, initial carrier assignments, or lawful later stacking. |

**Test refinement:** Include a captured 15-ammo/50-fuel real Supply Unit with 30 assigned Motorization Points and an otherwise identical unit with unrelated co-located points. Under an accepted capture trigger and assignment reading, §32.56 captures the first unit's assigned points; do not silently transfer the unrelated points. Preserve 15/50 as pre-transition evidence, but defer specific stock changes until immediate/free use is adjudicated. Compare a ZOC-only trigger with entry, a remaining enemy defender, a dummy, and a carrier detached before capture. Test deterministic replay and audience-authorized disclosure separately.
