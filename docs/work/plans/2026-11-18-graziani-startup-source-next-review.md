# Graziani startup setup: source review

**Disposition:** The setup-versus-action distinction is supported. Initial choices, loaded-point restriction, empty coastal ships and Tripoli location do not decide whether pre-Operations Stage turn phases occur. No first-stage reassignment or shipment is established.

Visually checked [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF pp. 2–3 / printed pp. 26–27. Sections 59.63–.64 derive attached and unattached Motorization Points from initial trucks and allow distributions to be altered once the game starts under Land attachment/detachment rules, except §59.66(B). The exception applies to each attached point not initially transporting men or guns that starts with one Ammo or three Fuel Points: the unit consumes that initial supply first, may not transfer it to a Supply Unit, and may detach the carrying point only while it remains attached to a subsidiary combat unit of that parent. The detachment restriction lasts only while carrying those initial supplies. Section 59.66(C)'s different setup rule for unattached points and additional Supply Units should not be substituted for (B).

[Land §32.32](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 43, specifies Organization Phase attachment and detachment **to Supply Units**; it does not alone settle timing for every combat-unit reassignment. The conditional first-stage test therefore needs an eligible Organization Phase, point and target, and applicable cargo and other attachment conditions. A setup selection alone is not proof that a change subsequently happened.

Scenario §59.54 says Axis coastal shipping starts all scenarios unloaded; §60.35 says the Italian player may use all Axis coastal shipping counters and they start in Tripoli. Section 60.22 gives the Graziani start as Game-Turn 1, OpStage 1. Nearby §60.37 separately requires Italian convoy planning for the remainder of September before starting and limits available lanes to 2, 3 and 6. That is a planning instruction, not evidence of execution or a ruling on whether a particular preamble is played; its precise interaction with abstract-mode rules is a separate scope question. The note correctly keeps the scenario snapshot distinct from later lawful changes and does not infer a skipped or executed stage from empty coastal ships.
