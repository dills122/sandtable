# Matruh Garrison: free-company eligibility

**Conclusion:** the reacquired original counter prints brigade-size `X`,
parenthesized stacking value `(3)` and garrison `G`, providing stronger evidence
for nominal brigade equivalence than the OA label alone. It does not establish
Matruh's effective size at a particular snapshot or settle the free-company
modifier's interaction with the special attachment limit. Preserve
`free-company-eligibility = unknown` until the exceptional assignment
denominator, actual attachments and source hierarchy are reviewed. This is a
same-edition source reading, not an official clarification or admitted setup.

## Reacquired counter: November 17 follow-up

The [original counter scan](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf),

An [independent counter-sheet review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-counter-review.md)
corroborates the marks and distinguishes the printed `(3)` from the chart's
six-attachment Matruh and five-attachment later Selby limits.

Land §9.11 identifies the number on a counter as stacking value; §§9.12 and
9.26–.28 distinguish an HQ's parenthesized printed value from its current
represented formation and shell size. Common chart §9.4 places a full
**super-brigade** at three stacking points, a shell division also at three,
and a shell brigade at one. The printed brigade-size `X`, OA's brigade-level
description and `(3)` together strongly support the **nominal brigade-sized**
reading, rather than assuming this is a shell division. They do not alone prove
that Matruh currently qualifies as a non-shell brigade-equivalent for §19.5,
nor settle whether size for that modification is static or affected by §9.28.
The OA's no-assignment text versus the two errata assignments leaves the
ordinary shell denominator contested. Do not equate `(3)` with three present
attached battalions or use it as an unconditional current value.

Land §9.16(a) gives a garrison counter with `G` above its stacking number free
hex stacking in its assigned garrison city or town; the Matruh OA identifies
the garrison and its later conversion. That is a **hex stacking** provision,
not proof that attachment limits, type restrictions or the free-company
modifier are satisfied. Its interaction with represented attached units and
actual location still needs its own rule and state. A bounded text search of
the hash-matched September errata for `Matruh`, `Selby`, counter, §§9.1,
9.2, 9.4 and 19.5 found the previously discussed §4.44b assignment and §19.5
clarification; §9.16 corrects a paragraph label. It found no explicit change
to this counter's printed `(3)`, `G` or `X`. This is not proof against OCR
omissions, later corrections or another counter edition.

The chart's free-company text is an **attachment-maximum modification**, not
the same thing as hex stacking. It does not free a battalion from the limit,
waive the row's no-tank/one-gun-class restrictions, or authorize an otherwise
illegal attachment. Company-equivalent type, non-shell status, nested formation
and stacking rules remain independently required. The distinct issue of
whether Buffs and Hampshire consume any of the six base slots is addressed by
[RUL-GRA-03](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-03-matruh-initial-assignment-working-interpretation)
and its [capacity follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-followup.md);
the free-company modifier does not decide that precedence conflict.

## Alternatives and bounded behavior

1. **Organizational-level reading:** treat the Matruh HQ's explicit brigade
   level as sufficient for the chart's brigade-equivalent modification,
   subject to company, type, stacking and applicable maximum rules. This gives
   the administrative descriptor practical weight, but assumes level implies
   equivalent for this purpose despite §9.22's warning and dynamic shell rules.
2. **Current-equivalence reading:** require a source-backed brigade-equivalent
   status at the attachment snapshot, and decide whether shell-size reduction
   under §9.28 is relevant to this modifier. This respects the modifier's exact
   noun and §9.2; it still needs an interpretation of the observed counter,
   a Matruh-specific shell denominator and actual represented composition. The HQ's exceptional OA
   and errata cannot be reduced to a generic brigade without a ruling.
3. **Fail closed for the unresolved distinction (selected research boundary):**
   carry `free-company-eligibility = unknown`. A setup or command whose legal
   outcome depends on the allowance is unsupported, without mutation or a
   fabricated maximum. A separate, fully known disqualifier can still reject an
   otherwise illegal action; an attachment that fits all independently proven
   limits may be considered without pretending the modifier was disproved.
   Preserve candidate alternatives, initial owner choice and policy version for
   later admission. `unknown` is neither a grant nor a permanent denial.

This does not select a mathematical formula for a shell when the source's
ordinary denominator is contested or zero. Source-listed over-limit treatment
under §59.2 must be assessed against an actual established limit and listed
formation; it does not prove that optional free companies are already listed.
If a later counter/source reading and rule hierarchy establish the relevant
size, reopen the result with that evidence, rather than inferring it from the
historical label or the absence of a confirmed extra-company roster.

  eligible non-shell companies: the organizational-level policy can permit
  the company modifier if all other constraints hold; a no-modifier reading
  cannot. The selected unknown boundary cannot decide an action dependent on
  that modifier. These are synthetic comparisons, **not** a proof of legal
  co-location, available counters, six-turn reachability or an accepted
