# Land-only dummy tank placement and starting inventory

**Result:** Source gives a per-player simultaneous maximum and a construction-segment procedure, not an automatic allocation of three initial dummy tank battalions. Land-only text strongly supports ignoring the ten-Store expenditure, while whether an independent local Stores-presence requirement survives remains unresolved. The scenario's listed dummy **supply** units are a different category and cannot supply a dummy tank inventory.

## Placement and lifecycle

Visually checked [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF p. 27, §§16.41–.43 and .46, against p. 43, §§32.0 and 32.11:

- §16.42 says that bringing a dummy tank battalion into play requires expenditure of **10 Stores**, which must be present in the hex in which it is placed. Placement is in the Construction Segment; no actual dummy-tank counters exist. It does not separately specify a starting inventory or a cost-free setup exception.
- §16.43 sets a maximum of **three in existence at one time per player** and at most one in a hex; Italian formations may not use dummies, even when mixed with German formations. This is a concurrent cap, not a statement that three already exist, a three-per-scenario total, or an authorization to choose an initial quantity. §16.46 specifies elimination in stated combat situations; existence after a loss or subsequent placement requires the actual event and remaining applicable rules.
- §32.0 describes the Land-only replacement of ordinary supply expenditure. §32.11 directs players to ignore references to usage and expenditure of Stores and says only the following supply rules apply. Its wording gives strong support for not debiting §16.42's ten Stores in this mode. But §16.42 attaches the local-presence clause to the expenditure, and the cited cases do not expressly say whether a distinct presence condition remains or how one would be represented after abstraction. Treat that as an interpretation gate, not proof of free, unconstrained placement or a supply balance.

## Scenario boundary

Graziani's [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) §60.92, PDF pp. 4–5 / printed pp. 28–29, replaces ordinary initial supply status with Land-only Supply Units and lists dummy **supply units**. Section 60.7, visually inspected on p. 4, lists the named initial construction conditions; it does not mention dummy tank battalions. These distinct supply dummies and the §16.43 maximum do not establish any tank dummy at setup.

A text search of the available scenario OCR for `dummy` finds supply dumps/units and later scenario dummy minefields, but no dummy-tank occurrence; OCR can miss text, and I did not visually audit every initial-force row or all scenario and errata pages. Therefore **initial tank dummy quantity and location remain unproven**, not source-proven zero. A scenario-specific setup or official correction identifying tank dummies, or an accepted general initial-placement interpretation with its timing and location, would be needed before recording any. Later placement also needs a valid Construction Segment, applicable restrictions and a decision on §32.11's interaction with §16.42; no actual stage action or scenario reachability is established.
