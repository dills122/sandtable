# Destruction CPA basis: independent review

**Disposition:** Core ambiguity and arithmetic in [CPA basis note](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-cpa-basis.md) are supported. Add §4.25's setup default and distinguish its map representation from CPA accounting; qualify §32.21 as an ammunition-specific battalion-equivalent rule. No source-licensed allocation or rounding policy found.

## Source checks

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 5 §3.1: a unit is a counter capable of expending CP; attached means same hex and represented on the map by the parent's counter. Assignment and attachment are distinct, and a unit may be assigned to one parent but attached to another. Parent counters represent subsidiary units that may instead have separate counters; HQ definition excludes trucks from independent subsidiaries.
- Printed p. 9 §4.25 reinforces representation: an attached unit has no separate counter **on the map**. Unless specifically stated otherwise, assigned units beginning a scenario in the same hex as their parent are attached to that parent. This default is relevant to initial setup, but neither it nor the definition decides how to debit represented subsidiaries for §32.17. Avoid counting physical off-map counters as additional independent occupants or assuming that represented units cease to exist for all rules.
- Printed p. 12 §§6.11, .15–.17: certain gun units with CPA zero are treated as CPA 10 for purposes other than movement; a parent formation has the lowest CPA of its component units, regardless of higher constituent CPAs. The example concerns moving a division as an entity; the definition does not expressly settle a destruction charge on attached or separately placed components. CP are not transferable and unused CP do not carry into the next Operations Stage; motorized infantry may assume its carrying truck's CPA. Parent minimum is an allowance definition, not proof that a single parent expenditure debits every child or that components share a transferable balance.
- Printed p. 43 §§32.0, .12, .17, .21, .32–.33: abstract Land-only game replaces trucks with Motorization Points; Supply Units are represented by Supply Dump counters. §32.17 states one-half CPA of all units in the hex, rounded upwards, and one die per Supply Unit in the hex. It does not expressly define per-unit versus aggregate rounding, account allocation, ownership filter, or treatment of represented components. §32.21 counts individual battalion-equivalents supplied and illustrates a division expending ammunition by component counters; this is an ammunition rule, not a general CPA rounding or destruction rule. The general §3.1 unit definition and the special Supply Unit terminology also do not by themselves settle whether a Supply Unit enters the charge basis or how it would have a CPA.
- Synthetic 15 + 15 example is correct: aggregate half rounds to 15, separate halves round to 8 + 8 = 16. Neither number establishes who pays or which representation is legally present. Account for cost chart's separate one-third supply-dump entry as documented in [destruction source review](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-subsegment-source-review.md); §32.17's specific one-half does not by itself document a conflict-resolution rule.
