# Commonwealth contact singleton boundary: source review

**Disposition:** The [singleton note](2026-11-17-graziani-commonwealth-singleton-proof.md) correctly reports one named Commonwealth land entry in each of the three hexes, but does not claim a proven final singleton or first-combat stack. The bounded completeness caveat is necessary: the deployment rows and same-hex organization language alone do not exhaust represented constituents, optional setup choices or subsequent events.

## Source checks

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed p. 27/PDF p. 3 §60.41, explicitly lists C3320 2nd Rifle Brigade with ten medium trucks; C3520 1st RTR with five; and C3020 11th Hussars with three. The 7th Armored Division row at D3612 detaches its previously listed division units. Its `Anywhere on Map D or E` row names artillery, anti-tank and AA, and cannot by itself place one of those at a Map C contact hex. Other land rows identify locations or areas. That observation establishes listed baselines, not all legal co-located constituents or the players' final choices.
- Scenario p. 26/PDF p. 2 §59.2 permits freely organizing units **listed to begin in the same hex** within formation and stacking limits; it does not grant relocation between these separate rows. §59.42 requires listed first-line trucks be assigned among units in their own hex. §59.44 places eligible supply trucks at a friendly combat unit, supply dump or major city/town/oasis; §60.43 includes trucks deployable anywhere on the maps. These provide conditional non-combat overlays, not another identified combat unit. Do not infer exact truck allocation, unit attachment, or a singleton from the printed row alone.
- §60.44 p. 28/PDF p. 4 allows Dump I and a dummy in Egypt on map C or D in the detailed mode; §60.92(C) p. 29/PDF p. 5 replaces it for Land-only play with the stated map D group west of Matruh. Section 59.66(C) p. 26 separately permits qualifying sets of 30 other unattached motorization points to start with a regular Supply Unit. Its existence is conditional on actual eligible points, placement and stack; it does not prove extra combat stacking or a real chosen overlay.
- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) p. 27 §§16.41–.43: dummy tanks are battalion equivalents, worthless in combat, with zero CPA; bringing one into play costs **10 Stores present in its placement hex**, and placement is in the Construction Segment with **no actual counters**. At most three per player and one per hex; Italian formations may not use them. Neither this procedure nor the inspected deployment row proves an existing dummy at setup. Because §32.11 disregards Stores expenditure in abstract logistics, the construction prerequisite and its mode interaction require an explicit ruling, not an assumed free or impossible construction. Later construction or movement also cannot be folded into an initial setup snapshot.

To establish an actual singleton at a specified instant, inspect represented OA constituents and possible detachments, all relevant placement permissions and chosen setup, permitted overlays and legal stacking, then account for all intervening movement/construction. Preserve hidden-state and coverage gates. No checked evidence warrants asserting an additional combat counter or a universal absence of one. This review did not independently re-audit all OA units, errata or later editions.
