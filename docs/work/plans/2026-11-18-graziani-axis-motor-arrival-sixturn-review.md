# Review: Axis six-turn motorization arrival bounds

**Disposition:** The [crosswalk](2026-11-18-graziani-axis-motor-arrival-sixturn.md) accurately states the checked ordinary truck-chart dates, scenario horizon and Land-only conversion distinctions. Its bounds do not prove an actual shipment or that every other arrival source is absent. No material discrepancy found.

- Axis §20.66a, chart PDF p. 33, gives first listed planning dates and per-turn maxima light **4 / 4**, medium **6 / 20**, heavy **5 / 3**. Its legend says the date is the first turn on which the stated maximum may be planned and arrival follows two weeks later. On the chart's ordinary turn arithmetic these give light GT6, medium GT8 and heavy GT7 as earliest planned arrival targets, respectively. Maxima and nominal targets are not completed plans, cargo, lane allocation or deliveries.
- Scenario §60.22 runs GT1/1 through conclusion GT6/3; §60.37 requires the Italian player to plan convoy runs for the remainder of September before the game, limited to lanes 2, 3 and 6, and invokes the reinforcement track. The preplanning clause does not itself list a truck manifest or expressly waive §20.66a dates. The [independent Axis schedule review](2026-11-18-graziani-benghazi-diversion-source-next-review.md) checks the full ordinary schedule beginning GT9/2; that does not eliminate separately sourced exceptions.
- Land §20.63 and its addendum require Axis pool replacement planning at least two turns ahead and describe convoy scheduling. Land §32.52 separates scenario-specified reinforcement Motorization Points from replacement Motorization Points at the medium-truck replacement rate. Scenario §60.92(A), Land Game Only, relates incoming motorization ability to **medium** trucks; §60.93(A), Land and Air, instead says medium **plus heavy**. Starting conversion §59.63(A) separately counts both medium and heavy first-line trucks. None of these observations makes a light-truck GT6 plan an automatic Land-only motorization receipt.
- Land §§32.61–.62 include Motorization Points in simplified Axis convoy cargo, with no tonnage limitation, and restrict any one lane in one Naval Convoy Stage to three Supply Units and 20 Motorization Points, subject to replacement-pool chart limits. They do not establish that any particular points are available or delivered. Section 32.56's capture consequence is a different state change, not a new replacement convoy arrival.

Scenario overrides, pre-existing plans, repaired/returned or other introduction paths, abstract-to-detailed hierarchy and actual arrival stages remain separate checks. The Commonwealth §60.47 replacement restriction is not an Axis ban. The crosswalk correctly avoids treating the chart-derived bounds as a universal zero.
