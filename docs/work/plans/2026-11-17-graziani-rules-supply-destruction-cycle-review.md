# Supply destruction cycle: independent source review

**Disposition:** The [cycle note](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-supply-destruction-cycle.md) accurately distinguishes the explicitly repeatable movement/combat procedure from §32.17's undefined `sub-segment`. A no-movement combat is expressly possible, but the inspected text does not equate its cycle with a new destruction allowance. One relevant erratum corrects a cross-reference in §8.23; it does not itself define a §32.17 timing unit.

## Source check

Visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed pp. 12, 14 and 43, and all four pages of [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf). SHA-256 verified before inspection: Land `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`; errata `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`.

- §5.2 p. 12 identifies movement and combat as phase G, with four numbered segments: movement, breakdown determination, combat and reserve release. Player A may repeat the set subject to §8.2, each repetition including all four. Player B later performs phases F–L with roles exchanged. Neither the outline nor this reviewed passage names a `Movement/Combat Segment` or maps its sub-segment to a particular boundary.
- §8.22 p. 14 describes moving, resolving combat after all movement has ceased, and repeating while the phasing player wishes to move or have combat. §8.25 expressly permits more than one attack on an enemy in an Operations Stage without either unit moving, and permits combat even if no units move, subject to ammunition and CP. This corroborates a possible no-movement combat cycle, not unlimited empty cycles or an automatic new destruction opportunity. §8.23 governs whether phasing units can move again after ending a Movement Segment near or far from an enemy, with a Reserve exception.
- §32.17 p. 43 allows a player to attempt destruction of Supply Units they are with, expending one-half the CPA of all units in the hex rounded upward. It calls for a die for each Supply Unit in the hex, destruction on 1–3, and an added one to the roll if the stack's total is one or less. It locates the attempt before any movement in a given sub-segment of a Movement/Combat Segment, with one attempt per Supply Unit per sub-segment. The passage does not say that submitting a new command creates that boundary. Which units are selected, dummy behavior, CPA aggregation, non-phasing authority and identification of the sub-segment remain separate decisions.
- September errata p. 1 §8.23 corrects the fourth-line cross-reference from `8.22` to `8.23`; the printed p. 14 last sentence calls Reserve Status an exception to 8.22. Preserve this correction in any source transcription. Errata p. 1 also corrects §8.17's percentage, potentially relevant to independent CP constraints. Errata p. 2 §32.0 warns that abstract rules were not tested. No explicit §32.17, §8.22 or §8.25 correction was found in the four-page package; other or later material may exist.

## Implications

Do not equate request count, individual unit movement, phase, four-segment cycle or entire Operations Stage without an accepted policy. Record the chosen durable boundary, paid costs, per-unit attempt identity and RNG so duplicates do not silently retry; preserve unresolved scope until then. An eligible no-movement combat and an empty repetition must be separate tests, along with the corrected §8.23 reference, phasing side, failed rolls, replay and side-safe observable traces. The source check does not establish legal target selection, enough CP, an opponent's authority, or reachability of any test sequence.

This is a source review after reading the companion note, using the same scans, not a blind transcription or independent edition. Hash matching does not grant rights. No runtime changes, readiness changes or gameplay tests.
