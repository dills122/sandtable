# Commonwealth initial contact hexes: singleton proof boundary

**Finding:** The inspected scenario deployment list names one Commonwealth land formation at each of C3320, C3520 and C3020, and its flexible combat-unit row is restricted to maps D/E. This supports a **one listed counter baseline**, not a definitive proof that each final setup contains exactly one combat unit or that it remains alone at first combat. No source-proven alternate second combat counter was identified at any of the three hexes; unexamined constituent/counter interactions and optional setup rules prevent an exhaustive exclusion claim.

[Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed p. 27/PDF p. 3 §60.41 lists these separately, and gives 7th Armored Division at D3612 with the listed division units above detached. Its only explicitly flexible land combat-unit row says anywhere on map D or E, listing artillery, antitank and AA units; it does not by itself place any of these at the three C hexes. Other §60.41 land entries are named hex, city or area deployments. This reading has not exhaustively expanded every OA subordinate or searched all potential corrections, so it is not proof of all legal occupants.

## Organization and non-combat pieces

- Scenario printed pp. 25–26/PDF pp. 1–2 §59.2 permits free initial organization of units **listed to begin in the same hex**, subject to formation and stacking limits. It does not independently relocate a unit from D/E or another named hex. [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) pp. 5 and 9 §§3.1 and 4.25 distinguish assignment, attachment and physical counter representation, including a default initial same-hex attachment. One visible counter can represent multiple attached units; “one counter,” “one combat unit,” “one battalion equivalent” and a singleton ZOC stack are different claims. Actual roster, possible subordinate detachment and applicable organization limits need individual verification.
- Scenario §60.43 gives second/third-line trucks `Anywhere, maps`; §59.44 requires eligible on-map supply trucks at a friendly combat unit, supply dump, or a major city/town/oasis. That supplies a **conditional alternate non-combat co-location** at a listed friendly combat unit, subject to truck availability, mode, placement and stacking; it is not evidence of a second combat counter. Section 59.42 requires listed first-line trucks be assigned to units at their own hex; under Land-only §59.63 they translate into motorization points. Exact allocation and represented carrier are independent setup choices.
- Detailed §60.44 allows Dump I and one dummy anywhere in Egypt on map C or D; an actual example at any named hex requires separately proven Egyptian membership and owner choice. Land-only §60.92(C) replaces §60.44 with three Dump 1 supply units on map D west of Matruh and a dummy group under the same placement rule; those specified groups cannot establish a C-hex placement. Separately, §59.66(C) permits qualifying groups of 30 other unattached motorization points in a hex to start transporting an additional regular Supply Unit. Source truck availability and placement/stacking choices must be established before calling such an extra counter legal. Land §32.14 gives Supply Units zero stacking value; their potential presence does not prove an extra combat unit or change a combat-stack classification by itself.
- Land p. 27 §§16.41–.43 describes dummy tank formations as battalion equivalents worth nothing in combat, with CPA zero, brought into play in the Construction Segment by expending 10 Stores present in the placement hex, and **no actual counters**. The inspected initial setup does not explicitly grant one at these locations. The instruction to construct does not prove a dummy already existed at scenario setup; equally, a later construction opportunity and its costs require separate adjudication, including how §32.11 disregarding Stores expenditure interacts with §16.42 in Land-only mode; neither free nor impossible construction follows automatically before any first-contact assertion. Do not count a dummy tank as an additional physical combat counter or silently rule out an eligible later deception.

## Scope and next proof

September errata p. 3 §59.2 corrects abbreviations; §60.45 addresses fleet, not a verified correction to these three land entries. This is a bounded relevant-entry search, not an exhaustive addenda or later-edition audit. For a positive singleton proof, expand each unit's OA and representation under accepted setup rules, enumerate all initial eligible combat sources and scenario options, establish actual player choices and legal stacking, and specify snapshot time. Check supply/truck and dummy overlays separately; inspect later movement or construction before claiming first-combat singleton or ZOC. No runtime changes or tests.
