2. **Junction remains qualified.** At E3003–E3004 the blue centerline meets the vertical grid side just above its lower vertex, supporting that side's attribution, although ink thickness and proximity to the vertex limit precision. In an independently rendered 500 dpi crop, no distinct railway branch crosses the neighboring E2905–E3004 diagonal; the pinned `railroad` on that side is therefore not independently established by this scan. Near-vertex ink and symbol convention leave its ultimate attribution open. Do not infer an additional branch or remove data on this observation alone.
3. **Clipping and ordering limit inference.** The western portion of E3101 is absent from the scanned sheet, although its side with E3001 and its railway crossing are visible. That crossing does not verify a D/E seam, its adjacent hex, or continuous rail service. East-of-Matruh comparison remains conditional on the proposed sheet ordering; a qualifying witness additionally depends on the applicable construction/destruction, occupancy and snapshot rules. This finite survey cannot prove absence of other witnesses.
