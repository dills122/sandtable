# Review: Tobruk repair facility across scenario setups

**Disposition:** The three printed facility observations and GT26/GT102 start dates are supported. They are distinct setup records and do not supply a live change-of-control transition. Qualify the Crusader row's ownership: §62.5's Tobruk sentence itself does not name an owner.

- Scenario PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29 §61.36 explicitly gives the Commonwealth a **Temporary Repair Facility in Tobruk**, another in Mersa Matruh, and major facilities in Alexandria and Cairo. Sections 61.1–.2 describe two scenarios using a shared setup; the race for Tobruk starts GT26, OpStage 3. On PDF p. 6, §61.72(D) describes the Land-only Commonwealth initial supply replacement but cites **§61.35**, the adjacent truck case, rather than the supply case §61.36. Its effect on the facility sentence warrants separate interpretation; the setup row is not an unconditional Land-only ruling.
- PDF p. 9 / printed p. 33 §62.5 says there is a **Temporary Repair Facility in Tobruk**. Its following sentence separately permits the Commonwealth an additional temporary facility in any village or city; the Tobruk sentence does **not itself** state a controlling side. This is an initial-construction record, not an explicit conversion or proof of its owner without other setup/control evidence. Section 62.62's Land-only supply replacements do not expressly name §62.5; this limited observation does not decide every mode interaction.
- PDF p. 12 / printed p. 36 §63.45 explicitly says the Axis has a **Major Repair Facility in Tobruk** and temporary facilities in Bardia and Mersa Matruh. PDF p. 9 §63.2 starts both El Alamein scenarios at GT102, OpStage 1. PDF p. 12 §63.82(B) says Axis initial supply status §63.45 is replaced by its listed Land-only supplies; whether that replacement reaches §63.45's facility sentence remains unresolved.

These entries establish different printed scenario snapshots, including expressly Commonwealth temporary and Axis major examples. They do not prove identity, coexistence, capture, destruction, rebuilding, side-dependent type or continuity between snapshots. Existing §60.33 versus Land §22.31 conflict is not resolved by assuming any scenario is a recorded continuation of another. No new scenario-wide control or reachability check was performed.
