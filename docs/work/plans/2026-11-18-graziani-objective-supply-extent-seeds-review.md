## Findings

1. **Derna supply coordinate is printed.** §60.92(B), printed p. 29, explicitly lists `Derna (B 5925)` as an initial Axis supply location. The inventory's statement that the supply row needs separate coordinate reconciliation misses this direct evidence. Record `B5925` as the printed supply-row coordinate; whether that coordinate is a certified map identity remains a separate question. §60.5 independently lists the same coordinate for the flying-boat alighting area; the two source roles remain distinct.
2. **Italian combat-unit requirements omitted from abbreviated victory rows.** §60.81 strategic requires taking and holding either Alexandria or Cairo with a combat unit that can be supplied by convoy from any Map D hex. Decisive requires taking and holding both Mersa Matruh and Siwa with any combat unit that can be supplied by convoy from Tobruk. Add those stated qualifiers to the inventory or explicitly reference the fuller victory matrix in each row. The precise supply subject and multi-location application remain open; neither row establishes a route or an accepted algorithm.

## Checks

- All six §60.81 rows' named location sets and source alternatives match the printed rows. Italian tactical says Sidi Birani and retained Sollum, Fort Maddalena and Giarabub in supply as above to Tobruk. Commonwealth tactical names Sollum, Halfaya Pass `C3922` and Siwa `C0127` with a truck-convoy trace back to Alexandria or Cairo; decisive names Bardia, Fort Maddalena, Sidi Omar, Sollum and Siwa; strategic names Tobruk; both latter rows say supply as above. Alexandria/Cairo are alternatives, not two required endpoints. The rows do not make lower victory levels prerequisites. Parenthesis scope, subjects and route semantics remain unresolved.
- §60.92(B) prints Tobruk `C4807`, Bardia `C4321`, Benghazi `B4827`, Derna `B5925`, Tripoli box, two chosen dumps and `C0716`. Each dump is in one Map C Libya hex outside the stated Commonwealth proximity restriction; dummy groups follow dump placement rules. §60.92(C) prints Mersa Matruh `D3714`, Sidi Baranni `C4131`, a chosen Map D dump west of Matruh, dummy group, and supply units in both Alexandria and Cairo. City placement is not specified to individual member hexes. The inventory correctly marks Benghazi's source conflict and does not silently expand owner choices.
- All §60.5 airfield, landing-strip, flying-boat basin and alighting-area names, coordinates, unnamed entries and off-map labels match the inventory. Deversoir and Kabrit each print off-map `E1833`. Malta capacity is separately stated without facility-by-facility locations. Facilities initially belong by country, which does not itself certify map country membership.
- §60.7 prints railway endpoint Mersa Matruh `D3714` and Tobruk efficiency 7; neither establishes a certified railway network or resolves port-chart conflict. §32.16 concerns local supply draw, not explicitly the §60.81 victory trace. §32.33 retains a Truck Convoy phase for motorized supply units; §32.37 permits restricted air transport of a supply unit under Land-only abstraction, which does not turn §60.5 facility seeds into detailed aircraft or air-supply placement. §32.42 places later Commonwealth arrivals at Cairo with a conditional Alexandria allowance. No mode-specific facility stock or truck conversion should be imported into §60.92 from §60.93.

No runtime, map data or tests changed. Findings concern source transcription and qualification, not legal movement, control, route reachability or acceptance of a victory policy.
