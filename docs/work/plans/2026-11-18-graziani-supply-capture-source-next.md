# Abstract supply capture: timing and raider boundary

**Result:** No newly verified original-source rule found that decides durable ownership or carries §32.13's immediate/free use automatically to a §10.29 ZOC-only capture. The [capture/use analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-captured-supply-use.md), [ZOC follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-zoc-capture-followup.md), and [logistics priority follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-logistics-priority-source.md) already document the main conflict and raider entry. They do not support silently resolving it.

Section 10.29's qualifying exposure can occur **at any time during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase**, without requiring enemy entry. Section 32.13 associates capture and immediate/free use with **enemy combat-unit entry**. Thus a conditional ZOC-only event and a later hypothetical entry have distinct triggers. The source does not expressly say the stock became friendly, stayed enemy-owned, was spent, or was removed during the interval; nor does it expressly extend the entry parenthetical to the earlier event. Once captured, do not presume continued enemy ownership merely to manufacture a later entry-triggered capture. Decide applicability, ownership, use, and ordering together; no actual legal sequence is asserted.

The already reviewed §§27.42, 27.55–.56 permit the specified raid entry and resolve a dummy after any raider/guard conflict if a raider survives. Section 32.11's Land-only substitution and §32.12's Supply Dump representation leave it unresolved whether the detailed dump procedure applies unchanged to abstract Supply Units, and whether the raider qualifies as a combat entrant under §32.13. Section 27.55 also excludes major-city dumps; no blanket entry exception follows. As a further **unverified source lead**, an [online text extraction](https://www.scribd.com/document/1068442062/The-Campaign-for-North-Africa-text) attributes a separate weekly resupply procedure to §27.35 and says §27.14 raiders have no combat ratings. These are not equivalent to resolving combat-unit classification or showing that the weekly detailed-supply procedure survives §32.11; each needs original-page visual verification before being treated as a new source finding. This check adds no source-supported immediate capture or ownership rule.

A discriminating synthetic review case is a real Supply Unit alone in a verified qualifying enemy ZOC during that enemy phase, with no combat entry, followed by a potential entry only if permitted under the chosen ownership and occupancy rules. Compare with a separately established real-unit combat entry and with a guarded dump raid; check identity, counter classification, phase, intervening ownership and any immediate-use obligation before assigning outcomes. A dummy and a raid must follow their own discovery conditions. These are research vectors, not evidence of a reachable scenario state.
