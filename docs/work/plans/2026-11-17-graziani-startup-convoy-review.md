# Graziani convoy startup: independent source review

**Disposition:** retain the candidate's full-preamble and direct-OpStage-1
alternatives as bounded interpretations; neither is a source-proven startup
contract. Its GT1–3 calendar, normal offsets, September lanes and chart
values match this rereading. Tighten the wording of the starting-turn
exception and initiative handling, and extend startup checks to Commonwealth
planning. No source supports a fabricated pregame roll, guaranteed positive
arrival or replayed plan. This review uses the same scans and edition, not an
independent edition or historical ledger.

## Findings

1. **A conditional arrival expectation is not a guarantee.** Land §32.43
   says players plan at the beginning of each Game Turn, Axis two turns and
   Commonwealth four turns ahead, and may plan as far ahead as needed so
   Supply Units arrive in turns other than the scenario's starting turn **if
   planning die rolls permit**. The candidate otherwise preserves this
   qualification, but its GT1 row calls the provision an “ordinary
   future-arrival guarantee.” Replace that phrase with a *conditional
   planning/arrival expectation*. It does not require a positive result on
   every later turn, prove a specific die was rolled, or by itself forbid all
   starting-turn cargo. Section 32.46 B includes an actual zero, distinct from
   absent or unrolled planning. Section 60.92 starting stock is separate.

2. **Direct entry does not by itself fix the missing GT1 supply plan.**
   Scenario §60.22 starts at GT1 OpStage 1. Land §5.2's ordinary turn outline
   has initiative and a Naval Convoy Stage before that OpStage, with a
   distinct Naval Convoy Arrival Phase inside it. Section 60.37 separately
   requires pre-start Axis convoy planning for the remainder of September
   and restricts those plans to lanes 2, 3 and 6. It does not expressly say
   what happened to the entire GT1 preamble, the §32.44 lookup or October
   entitlements. The proposed full-preamble, already-accounted-for and direct
   entry interpretations remain viable. Section 32.43 locates supply planning
   at the *beginning of the Game Turn*; it does not identify a specific
   implementation phase or say exactly how to initialize it at this scenario
   boundary. A direct entry must therefore choose a sourced/versioned
   initialization or other timing interpretation, or leave dependent plans
   unknown. Do not treat the phrase “skipped GT1 plan” as proof the scenario
   intended that supply obligation to vanish, nor use §60.37's September
   runs alone as proof of an October-target supply roll.

3. **Preserve fixed first-turn initiative.** Scenario §60.6 gives the Italian
   player initiative for the **entire first Game Turn**. The candidate's
   full-preamble alternative says to execute “applicable GT1 initiative,” which
   should be explicit: applying the ordinary stage outline cannot introduce a
   random initiative contest that overrides this scenario provision. The
   initiative holder's A/B selection within each reached OpStage remains a
   separate decision. GT2 initiative follows its applicable rules; this audit
   does not prove the result or any particular subsequent player order.

4. **Offset arithmetic and month selection are conditional.** The Map D track
   gives GT1 September III, GT2 September IV and GT3 October I. For a normal
   two-turn Axis offset, a GT2 target points before GT1, and a GT3 target points
   to GT1. Neither establishes a pre-GT1 numbered track turn or an actual
   roll. If GT1 planning is performed using September's ordinary §56.4 chart,
   §32.44 chooses **planning month**, so §32.46's B column is applicable and
   has die 1–6 outcomes `0, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3`; the result is the number the
   player may bring in two turns later, not delivered cargo. GT2's unknown
   pre-start planning month cannot be assumed September solely from its target.
   Section 60.37's September lane instruction does not by itself establish an
   October lane restriction; the pronoun and scope in its following sentence
   should still be reviewed as source wording rather than rephrased as an
   explicit October waiver. The optional errata §56.29 chart variant is a
   separate applicability decision.

5. **Include Commonwealth GT1-dependent planning.** The candidate's stated
   scope is Axis, but the same §32.43 beginning-of-turn boundary affects the
   Commonwealth four-turn plan: GT1 is the ordinary slot for a GT5 arrival,
   just as GT1 is the Axis slot for GT3. A startup implementation cannot
   initialize, defer or skip only Axis and silently assume the Commonwealth
   record exists. For a valid Commonwealth lookup §32.45 uses the **arrival
   period**, unlike Axis's planning month. Treat this as a separate plan, die,
   target and privacy-sensitive state; do not conflate their table results.

6. **Monthly loss and first encountered stage are separate decisions.**
   Section 32.57 calls for determining previous-month motorization losses in
   the first Naval Convoy Stage of each month. With a full GT1 preamble, that
   stage is the first *candidate* encountered in September; under direct
   OpStage-1 entry, the next ordinary pre-operation Naval Convoy Stage is GT2
   September IV. Whether the latter substitutes for the first of the month,
   whether a partial September start triggers an event at all, and whether the
   chart key is elapsed month or stage month remain unresolved. Under the
   **proposed elapsed-month** key, September would consult August's dash and
   GT3 October would consult September's dash; neither dash is established as
   zero. A September cargo plan does not decide those questions. A retry or
   restored checkpoint cannot invent a new first monthly stage or a second
   application.

## Six-turn arithmetic cross-check

`Pregame` means the ordinary offset precedes the scenario start; it is not an
asserted track coordinate, pre-start event, plan or numerical value. Axis
planning month and Commonwealth arrival period must each be established before
their table lookups. GT1's own table-arrival exception and scenario setup are
separate from the later targets.

| GT4 | Oct II | GT2 Sep IV | Pregame |

After resolution, test GT1 Italian fixed initiative, both startup routes, GT2
preplan with explicit provenance, GT3 Axis and GT5 Commonwealth missing versus
initialized plans, actual B zero versus unknown, early preplanning without a
second roll, and GT2/GT3 monthly-loss alternatives. Independently test cargo
allocation, convoy attack/loss, receipt, duplicate submission, map/port and
privacy; a planning result does not prove a landed unit. These are synthetic
acceptance obligations, not executed tests or historical outcomes.
