# Matruh rail action: proof of no §8.78 witness

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Disposition:** No negative witness proof exists for the example below. Map E pinned-edge review is useful positive evidence, not a census of source rail or game state. A valid negative requires a bounded geographic and state certificate for every potentially relevant hex. This note specifies evidence still needed; it does not admit an action or establish historical reachability.

## Fixed example and quantifiers

Consider a hypothetical Commonwealth rail action from `D3714` to `D3715`, using exactly those two hexes, at a specified authoritative Operations Stage snapshot. Scenario §60.7 places the initial rail end at Mersa Matruh `D3714`; the [Matruh source coverage](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-nonlocal-coverage.md) identifies `D3714–D3715` as a candidate printed rail side. Neither fact establishes this action's legal route, stage, passenger, construction, or state. The example is a proof target, not a claim that the trip is available. If an adopted use rule includes other hexes, or the actual route differs, substitute its complete used-hex set and repeat the proof.

[Land §8.78](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 16, bars Allied railroad movement use of a rail hex west of **any** rail hex occupied by an Axis combat unit. It states no same-line or on-route condition. For each used hex `u`, test every potentially relevant position `h`: geographic `u` west of `h`, qualifying rail at `h`, and qualifying Axis combat occupancy at `h`, all at the selected snapshot. A single fully established conjunction blocks under this rule even if other candidates are unknown. A negative needs every conjunction false and candidate coverage complete. A false required condition excludes that candidate even when another condition is unknown; otherwise uncertainty persists. See [RUL-GRA-26 audit](2026-11-17-graziani-rul26-audit.md). This result addresses §8.78 alone; legal rail movement needs its other rules independently.

## Certificate needed

1. **Action and timing.** Record passenger, origin, destination, full route, every hex considered used, stage and ordering, snapshot identity, ruleset and policy versions. Adopt and justify the meaning of “use” at origin, intermediate and destination and the evaluation time; do not silently assume `D3714` alone is the target. Establish actual route continuity and the independent §8.73 occupancy/ZOC, CP and other movement conditions separately. The [rail/ZOC review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-rail-zoc-review.md) identifies unresolved precedence and stage-timing questions.
2. **Eastern geographic bound.** Choose a justified geographic west relation for each used hex, including staggered rows, equal longitude and boundary cases. Land §4.1, p. 7, orders sheets A–E west to east, but does not provide a universal per-hex comparator; its overlap description conflicts with observed grid evidence. Use the [west-geography review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-west-geography.md), not sheet letters or raw coordinates as an unproven formula. Determine the full playable domain and every sheet or overlap containing positions that could be east of either used hex; do not presume all of D, E, or an adjacent sheet falls inside or outside it. Document treatment of any relevant outside-map area under applicable rules and scenario; absence of evidence is not an exclusion. Resolve D/E registration and any other intersecting seam or retain affected positions as unknown. A nonlocal witness needs geographic ordering, not rail connectivity across a seam.
3. **Complete source rail inventory.** Survey the entire bounded area, including off-route branches, junctions, side and vertex ambiguity, obscured or clipped marks, and relevant sheet overlaps. Reconcile original [map](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), key p. 1 and sheets D/E pp. 4–5, with data, recording both positive and source-supported negative coverage and unresolved cells. Pinned-side enumeration cannot discover omitted rail; `E2927–E3027` is a source-supported omission from pinned `railroad` in the [Tanta review](2026-11-18-graziani-delta-rail-source-batch-review.md). The [E crosswalk](2026-11-18-graziani-rail-eastern-witness-crosswalk.md) and [Map E sweep C](2026-11-18-graziani-map-e-rail-sweep-c.md) list bounded observations, not exhaustive coverage. Map D Matruh junction discrepancies, other D branches, Alexandria overprint and the D/E clipped seam remain material leads. Exclude a position on geographic or occupancy grounds if independently proven false; otherwise unresolved rail identity prevents a negative.
4. **Construction and line identity.** Establish initial scenario state and all relevant construction transitions as of the snapshot, with provenance for each potentially occupied rail hex. Land §§8.47 and 24.67 exclude certified unbuilt railroad; unknown is not certified unbuilt. Printed track, a railhead or a marker alone does not prove full construction history. Under planning ruling RUL-GRA-26, previously constructed destroyed rail remains a witness candidate; damage alone does not negate it. This is a versioned interpretation, not explicit §8.78 language. Distinguish historic-only Soluch–Benghazi–Barce ink from independently qualifying overlapping rail; its proposed exclusion is likewise interpretive, and §54.4 interaction remains open. Apply construction/control rules and scenario overrides separately. See [scope and sources](2026-11-17-graziani-continuation-rail-scope.md) and [audit](2026-11-17-graziani-rul26-audit.md).
5. **Complete occupancy and classification.** At the same snapshot obtain authoritative occupancy for every position not otherwise excluded, including represented and attached units, and apply the adopted Axis combat-unit classification. Land §§3.23 and 3.31 distinguish combat types and an HQ representing attached combat units; an HQ with attached combat units cannot be dismissed as a non-combat counter. Resolve any determination-specific exceptions for §8.78 rather than substituting ZOC, attack capability or a strength threshold. An empty or nonqualifying occupant excludes a candidate; incomplete hidden occupancy or unresolved classification does not. Occupancy on the actual path is separately relevant under §8.73.
6. **Disclosure and replay.** Bind proof to immutable snapshot, event order and versioned geographic, rail and classification policies. Internal three-valued results do not authorize disclosure of hidden Axis information. Specify permitted observations across action enumeration, submission, acceptance or refusal, state effects, events, later observations, ordering, timing and replay; compare equal authorized histories differing only in hidden witness state. A uniform initial response alone cannot conceal divergent later outcomes. Until an authorized disclosure or observation-independent protocol is established, keep the affected public action boundary unavailable. See [RUL-GRA-26 audit](2026-11-17-graziani-rul26-audit.md).

## Proof status and next evidence

For this example, no fixed authoritative snapshot, actual passenger, adopted used-hex and west policies, complete geographic/source inventory, construction history or complete occupancy classification is supplied. Thus **unknown**, not “no witness.” A proven witness could establish a block before census completion; a negative cannot. Prioritize the D/E seam and Map D bounded survey, then complete source-based Map E omission and branch coverage, while specifying state provenance and the side-safe action contract. Do not infer a complete census from reviewed pinned edges or a continuous coastal route.
