# Supply capture: explicit occupied-hex exception

**Result:** Land §8.13's general ban on entering an enemy-occupied hex explicitly cross-references §27.4; desert-raider cases describe particular entries despite that ban. This is new bounded evidence about the scope of the general wording, not proof that ordinary combat units can enter an abstract Supply Unit's hex or that any raider captures one.

## Question and prior work

The [supply-capture continuation](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-continuation.md) and [review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-continuation-review.md) leave §8.13's occupied-hex prohibition versus §32.13's capture-on-entry clause unresolved. Neither analyzes §8.13's express §27.4 cross-reference or its raider/dump case. This follow-up checks those clauses, not all possible occupied-hex exceptions.

## Original-source comparison

- Land p. 14 §8.13 says a unit may never enter a hex containing an enemy unit, adding **“see, however, Case 27.4.”** This cross-reference is part of the rule; quoting the prohibition without it overstates its generality.
- Land p. 39 §27.41 describes spotting when an **enemy combat unit enters a hex occupied by a friendly desert raider**, among another trigger; §27.44 specifies the movement/spotting CP procedure. That is an expressly described enemy-occupied entry, but the occupied subject there is a desert raider, not a Supply Unit. It does not independently authorize entry into arbitrary enemy counters.
- In the other direction, §27.42 bars desert raiders from entering enemy-occupied hexes unless going to raid. Section 27.55 describes a raider entering a hex with an enemy supply **dump**, including a guarded dump, to raid; §27.56 addresses a dummy dump after any raider/guard conflict. These provisions describe the special raid procedure and its own result, not a general supply-unit capture action.
- Section 32.12 p. 43 calls its Supply Units abstract substitutes for supply points and represents them with Supply Dump counters. Section 32.13 says an enemy **combat unit** entering the Supply Unit's hex captures it, with immediate and free supply use. Section 32.11 says only the following supply rules apply in Land-only play. Representation by a Supply Dump counter does not alone establish that all §27.55 detailed dump-raid effects survive §32.11 or apply unchanged to the abstract unit, nor do these passages establish that the raider is an eligible §32.13 combat entrant.

The comparison rules out treating §8.13 as a source-wide absolute without exceptions. It does **not** settle whether §32.13 supplies a further specific exception to §8.13, whether a raider and an abstract Supply Unit may use the detailed raid procedure in Land-only mode, trigger priority, dummy discovery, or free-use accounting. No scenario raider position, legal path, weather, random result, or actual capture is established. Those remain conditional questions requiring separate source or project interpretation.
