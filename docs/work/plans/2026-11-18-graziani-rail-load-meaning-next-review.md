# Rail load meaning: source review

**Disposition:** The bounded finding holds: inspected passages do not settle whether §32.34 counts distinct Supply Units carried during the stage or peak simultaneous load. Neither reading is admitted by this review.

Visually checked [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed pp. 15–16 and 43. Section 8.72's opposite-direction example moves a unit west and supplies east; it does not illustrate cargo turnover. Section 8.77 permits pickups and dropoffs while stacking and direction restrictions are always observed and no unit using rail has expended CP that Operations Stage. Section 32.34 states any two real and/or dummy Supply Units in one direction as the total load for an Operations Stage, with normal rail movement rules. It does not explicitly say whether unloading frees a place for a third distinct unit. The §8.77 stacking restriction does not itself define the supply-unit cap: §32.14 assigns Supply Units zero stacking value.

Visually checked [Air/Logistics rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 27 / printed p. 23 §§54.31–.35. Section 54.35 says unloaded supplies may not be moved that Operations Stage; it does not expressly prohibit another cargo from taking their place. Section 54.32 gives 1,500 tons per stage in either direction, while §54.33 limits supply to one type at a given time. These detailed-supply provisions do not directly define the Land-only two-counter rule. The cited cases also have separate mixed-service wording: §54.31 says supplies and personnel cannot be moved at the same time, while §54.32 permits personnel one way and supplies another. That interaction is outside this turnover finding and should not be silently settled by it.

The hypothetical three distinct units with peak one is a valid discriminator between the two proposed accounting rules, conditional on all other requirements. It is not proof either interpretation permits an actual trip. The note properly distinguishes a count of boarding events from distinct units; repeat boarding and §54.35's possible mode scope need separate decisions. No original errata independently inspected for this review; the note's bounded negative errata search is not independently verified.
