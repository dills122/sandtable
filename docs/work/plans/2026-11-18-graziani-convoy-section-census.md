## Errata and coverage boundary

The checked errata sequence contains no directly numbered §32.6 correction; this is not an exhaustive indirect or later-errata search. Errata §41.5 corrects table headings/result wording; the [5% review](2026-11-17-graziani-five-percent-reachability-review.md) separately checked that the cited convoy cell is not explicitly changed. Errata §41.67 addresses percentage allocation in the detailed Air procedure; whether it supplies a Land-only 5% fallback remains unresolved, not an automatic import. Errata §56.25 permits arriving tonnage allocation to an OpStage unless otherwise designated; §56.29 offers a chart-letter reordering option. Their application to the abstract profile and startup must be evaluated against their scope, rather than presumed from proximity to §32.6.

All six numbered §32.6 cases and both §32.65 branches appear in LA-18's range; LA-16 covers advance supply availability and LA-17 separately covers motorization. The queue links independent source reviews of lane cells, result grouping, 5% and scenario qualification. No wholly unreviewed **numbered §32.6 case** was identified in this bounded comparison; that is not a complete table audit, a finding that every source exception is reviewed, or a readiness conclusion. Useful next checks should name an unresolved actual snapshot, map/ordering witness, rule priority or unexamined erratum—not merely repeat that the source pages were read. Planning, choices, private state and rolls are state; not having them is not a missing printed result.

The [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-convoy-section-census-review.md) notes that the grouping example following §32.65(B) explicitly uses a 10% result, so its placement after B alone does not limit it to 20%-plus results. It also distinguishes §32.64's “Commonwealth division” from the chart key's “Allied division”; the eligible-population relationship remains a separate question.
