# Review: Land §§1–4 disclosure pass

**Disposition:** Checked opening-rule descriptions support the census; no material audience or source-transcription error found. One useful §4.25 qualification belongs alongside its counter-representation row: unless specifically stated otherwise, assigned units that **begin a scenario in the same hex as their parent** start attached, and their individual counters are not deployed on the map. This is a conditional initial-setup default, not permission to disclose an actual attached roster; overrides, actual same-hex placement and §3.6 remain separate. The candidate's narrower representation statement is correct, but omits this setup-specific condition.

- Land §2.3 p. 4 describes the playtest division of work, including the Commander-in-Chief gathering and disseminating enemy information and examples of teammate communication. It is a role description, not an explicit opponent entitlement or an independently specified digital team database. §2.2 explains why unit information is recorded on sheets; the [record-sheet review](2026-11-18-graziani-record-sheets-disclosure-review.md) retains separate secrecy and exceptions.
- §3.1 p. 5 calls assault ground combat whose combatants are visible to each other, unlike barrage, and describes anti-armor followed by close assault. It also says desert raiders **normally** move unobserved by the opposing player. The census correctly avoids expanding physical visibility into all identity, strength or loss details, or making raider concealment permanent; §3.62 and the respective procedures set additional conditions.
- §3.22 p. 6 treats a mixed armored/unarmored artillery or anti-tank unit as two units for target purposes. The statement classifies targets; it is not an untimed inventory disclosure. §§4.21–.24 pp. 7–9 depict counter and marker faces, including minefield fronts/backs; §4.25 removes attached subsidiary counters from the map representation and §4.26 provides holding boxes for counters in a represented hex. These illustrations do not themselves grant unrestricted examination of reverse sides or sheets. The [limited-intelligence review](2026-11-18-graziani-limited-intelligence-source-inventory-review.md) and minefield rule supply the separate, scoped observations.
- The census explicitly defers the previously reviewed §§4.5–.55 and addendum §3.6 rather than claiming a full new audit of them. Its no-extra-audience findings in introductory descriptions are bounded; public reference charts, conditional physical markings and present private state remain distinct. I did not independently repeat the complete extracted errata search or examine every scenario/chart exception.
