# Independent review: Matruh attachment cap

**Result:** Sources support the note's two competing readings and conditional discriminator. The general §19.5 clarification favors treating chart attachments as additional; the Matruh OA's specific “up to six units may be attached” and subsequent assignment correction do not expressly settle whether the two assigned battalions consume that six. No legal eight-unit setup follows.

- [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 left column §19.5, says chart-listed attachments are additional to normally assigned units even when those assigned units are elsewhere. Page 1 §4.44b item 2 assigns 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshires to Matruh for the campaign and Italian scenarios. The two passages do not name their mutual treatment. In particular, calling the errata-assigned pair “normally assigned” for §19.5 is an interpretation, not a separately printed Matruh statement.
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 10, Matruh/Selby panel, states no units may be assigned but up to six may be attached, referring to the Maximum Attachments Chart; §4.44b supplies a contradictory assignment correction. [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF p. 6 §19.5, has a Matruh GT1–12 row: six units, no tank and one gun class. Its key defines unit by a full-strength battalion- or company-equivalent counter; the separate modifier permits two non-shell company-equivalents without cost to maximum attachments, with type restrictions and three companies equaling a battalion. Therefore raw counter counts are not automatically capacity counts. OA changes the formation at GT12 OpStage 2 while chart labels Selby GT13+; exact transition remains separate.
- For the proposed arithmetic, assuming **two assigned and six non-assigned units all individually count as one charged unit** and all are attached, six-total gives 8 against 6; six-additional gives 6 non-assigned against 6. This is a distinguishing synthetic state, not proof that the chart, stacking, type, parent, timing, free-company or scenario restrictions permit those attachments. An absent or detached assignee is not a currently attached unit; whether its assignment affects the contested accounting still depends on the interpretation.
- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §60.41 lists Matruh Garrison at D3714 with specified attached 1 Essex, 1 Durham Light Infantry and 1 South Staffordshires. It does not place the Buffs or Hampshires in that Matruh line. This does not by itself resolve their other placement, legal attachment, size counting or the cap conflict. Land p. 29 §§19.13 and .4 and p. 30 §§19.45–.46 distinguish assignment, attachment, substitution and nested participation. The erratum's assignment does not itself establish co-location or an attachment event.
