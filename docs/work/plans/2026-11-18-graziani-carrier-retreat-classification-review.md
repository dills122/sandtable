# Carrier retreat subject: source review

**Disposition:** Supports [classification follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-carrier-retreat-classification.md)'s unresolved carrier/cargo and non-phasing movement questions. No inspected case expressly selects a §13 retreat subject or establishes a legal move. This is an informed reading of the same scans, not a separate edition.

- Glossary §3.1 defines a unit as a counter capable of expending CP; §§32.12–.14 represent an abstract Supply Unit by a Supply Dump counter, without combat capability and with zero stacking value. Those facts do not themselves supply an untransported counter's CPA or decide the identity and cohesion status of the retreat subject.
- §§32.32–.33 require 30 Motorization Points for real cargo and six for dummy cargo, establish the Organization Phase attachment window, and speak of the Supply Unit being transported. Section 32.33 separately exempts normal non-phasing movement from its friendly Movement/Combat stacking restriction. It does not expressly authorize separate carrier/cargo retreat or settle the fate of cargo on §32.58(B)'s forced return. Section 32.18's same-fashion dummy movement likewise does not decide retreat eligibility.
- §32.51 treats Motorization Points as Medium Truck Points except as modified in that major case, and §32.58 assigns CPA 15 to points transporting a Supply Unit. §8.99 permits a Truck marker to represent unattached Truck Points, **unless all those points transport Infantry Replacement Points**; that exception is part of the source's scope. The case does not specify that an abstract supply allocation must have its own carrier marker. A point allocation, represented carrier, cargo counter and their coupled movement should remain distinct questions.
- §§13.0–.1, .21 and .23–.24 support the stated separate voluntary, non-phasing retreat procedure and adjacency-dependent CP limits. Its unpinned and cohesion conditions do not establish a supply counter's or points' pinning/cohesion representation. §32.58(D)'s adjacent CPA 35 condition says **react**, while (B) applies when points are **forced** to exceed CPA; neither resolves every voluntary-retreat and cargo outcome. Retain the earlier [reaction-versus-retreat review](2026-11-18-graziani-escort-reaction-source-followup-review.md)'s ZOC and hierarchy qualifications.
