# 6th New Zealand arrival: procedure cross-check review

**Disposition:** Supports the [reinforcement-procedure follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-nz-arrival-source-next.md). The schedule and OA agree on the brigade's GT6/OpStage 3 arrival; Land §20.11's example instead gives GT2/OpStage 3. The checked procedure supports cross-checking schedule and OA and the scenario directs use of the printed track, but neither is an identified official correction of the contrary example. RUL-GRA-04 remains a project planning interpretation.

- Land §4.41 describes reinforcement schedules as listing newly arriving land units and dates, and OA sheets as giving organization and parent arrivals. §4.42 directs the player to check the current schedule and then the relevant OA; its example explicitly calls the OA date a cross-check. It says a parent arrives with listed lesser units except those arriving later and/or excluded on the track. This is procedural evidence, not a printed universal precedence rule resolving a conflict with §20.11.
- Commonwealth reinforcement schedule §4.43a lists **6th NZ Field Artillery Regiment** at GT2/OpStage 3 and **6 NZ Brigade** at GT6/OpStage 3, with 25 light, 85 medium and 5 heavy trucks on the latter row. The 2nd NZ OA lists the 6th Brigade HQ and 24th, 25th and 26th NZ battalions at `3/6`, and 6th NZ Field Artillery at `3/2`. The schedule labels its columns GT/OpS; the OA uses OpStage/Game-Turn notation. The matching records are within one source package, not independent editions. Printed truck figures do not establish their abstract-mode conversion or an actual receipt.
- Land §20.11 first directs whole-unit arrivals by the track, then states that the brigade and three battalions arrive with the artillery in September `3/2`. Scenario §60.47 separately says to use the Commonwealth reinforcement track as printed. These are genuine competing textual statements; neither accepting the track for planning nor describing the later sentence as an example proves an official amendment. Artillery and brigade must remain distinct source subjects; no checked text establishes two separate brigade arrivals.
- Land §20.12 places reinforcements at the beginning of the Naval Convoy Arrival Phase and allows movement in the arrival stage; scenario §60.22 ends Graziani at the conclusion of GT6/OpStage 3. Their interaction with the selected date, scenario boundary and other applicable rules needs the separate timing contract. No actual arrival, training status, placement or action is established by a schedule row alone.
