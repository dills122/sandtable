# RTR later-scenario comparison: source review

**Disposition:** [Candidate](2026-11-18-graziani-arrival-remaining-source.md) accurately transcribes the two later assignments and preserves the uncertainty about Graziani arrival. Section 62.32's parenthetical is printed as reported; it should not be silently normalized to the adjacent setup's parent designation.

Visually checked [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 7 / printed p. 31:

- §62.2 starts Operation Crusader at GT57, Operations Stage 3. Section 62.31 lists **32 Army Tank Bde (2)** within one hex of C4807, assigning 1 RTR, 4th RTR and 7th RTR. Separately, C4111 lists **7th Armored Bde 7**, assigning 7th Hussars, 6th RTR and 2nd RTR. These are explicit initial assignments for that later setup, not dated transfer instructions for the earlier scenario.
- §62.32's tank row literally reads **“7 RTR (32nd Army, Bde 12): 4 Matildas”**. The parenthetical differs from §62.31's printed **“32 Army Tank Bde (2)”** notation. This check does not establish what the `12` means or whether it is an error; matching the names alone does not resolve the notation.

Visually checked [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 11: the **Unassigned Armor-Class Units** table lists 2nd Royal Tank Regt and 7th Royal Tank Regt with `3/2` arrivals; only 2 RTR has note `b`, which says it was assigned to 4/7 Armored Brigade upon arrival. That source wording does not alone settle how to implement the historical note alongside normal attachment, arrival and reorganization timing. The later setup does not establish the actual survival or transfer of an earlier scenario's unit, or 7 RTR's GT2 parent.
