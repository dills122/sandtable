# Graziani replacement training: source boundaries

**Disposition:** Replacement training is mandatory, with class-specific durations and an explicit arrival-stage allowance. Source does not settle how every morale-training instructor condition maps to replacement points, or what interruption does to accumulated progress. No replacement-training policy or legal six-turn witness is admitted by this reading.

## Printed facts and interaction

| Subject | Source-backed fact | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requirement and duration | §20.43 requires both sides' replacement points to train: gun one Operations Stage, infantry three, tank/AC/recce six. It describes the gun stage as uncrating. Arrival stage may count. | Its note separates replacement training from Commonwealth morale training; do not award a morale increase for replacement training. |
| Locations | §20.43 names Tripoli or any major city for Axis points and refers Commonwealth training to §17.32: Cairo city hexes, Helwan 1430, Alexandria city hexes, Amiriya, Abouqir or Deghelia. Errata §17.3 says Axis training centers train replacement points, not actual units. | Whether an Axis center is required, sufficient, or an additional constraint at each stated location is unresolved. Map identification and location control are separate questions. |
| Instructor | §20.43 refers to §17.3 and says rules for training reinforcements apply to replacements. §17.35 requires a combat battalion at designated Basic Morale in the trainee's hex during each training stage, at least one third assigned TOE strength; infantry divisions or brigades may use infantry-type battalions and units with tanks need tank battalions. | Replacement-versus-morale distinction, gun uncrating, replacement counter classification and Axis centers leave import of each condition unsettled. Center text does not expressly waive or provide a battalion. |
| Capacity | §17.35 excludes instruction battalions from stacking limits and allows each to train as many units as may be stacked in that hex. §20.43 equates five replacement points to a battalion when size is in doubt; §20.45 says five points equal a battalion for all purposes. | Neither clause specifies partial-point rounding, mixed classes, existing occupants, multiple instructors, or whether five points constitute an eligible instructor. No capacity number follows without applicable stacking rules and accepted interpretation. |
| Interruption | §17.33 says any CP expenditure during an Operations Stage interrupts that unit's training. §5.2 separately bars units beginning or continuing training from voluntary movement for the rest of that stage, except reaction. | Applying these unit rules to each replacement class depends on the cross-reference; permitted reaction is not automatically free of CP. Text inspected does not specify whether earned earlier stages survive interruption, whether stages must be consecutive, or how lost instructor/location affects credit. |
| Completion and absorption | §5.2 Training Completion Step notes units and replacement points completing a level of training; Organization segments may be ordered as desired. §20.41 requires immediate designation for eventual delivery, immediate reassignment if designated unit is eliminated or withdrawn, trained points and a same-hex receiving unit for absorption; §20.49 limits absorption to Reorganization. | Completion, initial designation and absorption are distinct. Organization precedes Naval Convoy Arrival, so ordinary ordering does not provide same-stage absorption for newly arrived points; arrival-stage credit's exact completion boundary and any exception remain unresolved. |
| Unabsorbed points and movement | §20.42 gives unabsorbed points Basic Morale −3, permits combat only in defense against Close Assault, restricts voluntary enemy-ZOC entry to friendly-occupied hexes, and prevents exceeding their CPA, which is that of their assigned unit. §20.46 describes points moving toward the front for assignment as truck convoys moving only in the Truck Convoy Movement Phase. | §20.44 also describes trained tank, gun and recce points moving independently or by Commonwealth rail. Interpret these provisions together before choosing a transport rule; neither establishes unrestricted same-stage use. |

Section 17.34's six stages per morale point and maximum Basic Morale govern morale training; they do not override §20.43's replacement schedule. Section 17.36 permits units to fight at untrained morale instead of morale training; it does not erase §20.43's mandatory replacement training. These distinctions constrain interpretation but do not settle the instructor cross-reference.

## Decisions and cases still needed

- Specify class- and side-specific instructor obligations, including gun uncrating and Axis centers; establish instructor strength calculation and actual eligible unit identities. For integral strengths, `3 × actual >= assigned` tests the printed one-third threshold without inventing a rounding rule.
- Resolve trainee grouping and stacking capacity for one, four, five and six points; mixed classes, pre-existing occupants, two instructors, and instructor replacement or absence partway through a stage. Compare center-only and center-plus-battalion readings explicitly.
- Define arrival-stage selection and credit interval, completion step, and later absorption. Compare one-stage gun and three-/six-stage points; distinguish designated, in training, credited, completed and absorbed states. Do not infer same-stage absorption from arrival-stage training.
- Test designated-unit elimination or withdrawal, unabsorbed counter restrictions and applicable movement phase. Resolve interaction between §20.44's independent/rail movement and §20.46's truck-convoy language before permitting transport.
- Decide effect of a CP-spending reaction, zero-CP reaction, instructor departure and return, or location change on current and prior credit; compare accumulated versus consecutive-stage readings. Unknown prerequisites block dependent decisions rather than prove failure or silently reset progress.
- Test exact and below one-third strength, wrong tank instructor, stacking exemption and capacity, permitted and disallowed locations, interrupted and resumed training, and replay around completion/absorption. These are proposed cases, not executed tests or claims of historical reachability.
