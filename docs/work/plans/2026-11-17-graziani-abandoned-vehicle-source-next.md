# Abandoned vehicles: salt marsh, recovery and mode boundary

**Finding:** Original Air and Logistics rules §53.3 provide a concrete abandonment and recovery procedure missing from the earlier marker-fog inventory. Its interaction with Land-only Motorization Points remains unresolved; do not import the entire detailed truck system into Land-only play or assume all abandoned markers are captured by adjacency.

## Printed rules

| Case | Source | Bounded consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salt-marsh entry | Land §8.44, printed p. 15, generally confines vehicles other than Light Trucks, recce-type units and motorcycle infantry to road or track in salt marsh. It says a prohibited vehicle entering without using the track, whatever the reason, is abandoned, with a reference printed as **§5.33**. | Prohibited entry and forced entry/abandonment are distinct from breakdown. The printed reference appears to point to §53.3, but this is an inference, not an official correction. Successive sentences' road-versus-track wording needs reconciliation; an exception to the entry restriction does not automatically resolve §53.31's separate abandonment rule. |

## Land-only boundary

§53.3 appears in the detailed Logistics section, while Land §8.44 directly invokes a reference printed `5.33`. Land-only §32.0 replaces trucks with Motorization Points; §32.51 treats those points in all respects as Medium Truck Points except as modified, and §32.55 exempts Motorization Points from breakdown. Those facts support checking §53.3 as a cross-referenced candidate, but do not conclusively establish that all detailed truck, abandonment, cargo and recovery procedures survive abstraction unchanged. The source does not explicitly say the breakdown exemption also exempts abandonment, nor that every motorization point becomes a physical truck counter. Non-motorization vehicles and guns require their own type and mode treatment.

## Conditional checks

1. A prohibited vehicle forcibly enters salt marsh off the connecting road/track: classify the §8.44 abandonment separately from breakdown. Compare light truck, medium truck, prohibited tank, recce and motorcycle infantry and a valid road/track crossing. Do not assume §8.44's light-truck entry exception settles whether §53.31's “trucks” includes it; mode and source hierarchy remain gates.
2. For §53.31, vary enemy combat unit at exactly three traversable hexes versus beyond three, friendly combat unit present versus absent in those same hexes, and an enemy arriving or leaving before the end-of-Movement-Stage checkpoint. Compare shared-snapshot versus separately timed enemy predicates rather than asserting the parenthetical applies to both. Preserve the three-hex region/route question. Distinguish passenger surrender, gun capture and vehicle control; no historical event is invented.
3. For §53.32, compare adjacent non-marsh engineer-capable unit against non-engineer and marsh-hex candidates; check enemy adjacency independently for both endpoints. Debit the stated 10 CP to each under a selected mode-applicability and cost policy; attach recovered vehicles only if the rule applies and the action is valid.
4. In Land-only mode, test eligible Motorization Points, a separately represented tank/gun and a physical truck in detailed mode as distinct classes. Do not import the medium-truck equivalence without reconciling the specific abstraction and marker identity. Preserve opponent privacy across equal observable histories.
