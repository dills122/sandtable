# Graziani rail: geographic west evidence

**Disposition:** Use a specific map-supported west comparison for a §8.78 candidate; do not promote a sheet-letter or coordinate formula to a general rule. The printed sheets give an orientation and local examples. Land §4.1 explicitly orders the sheets west to east, but its overlap notation conflicts with the visible grid. The distance chart supplies distance, not an independent directional or rail-use rule. A same-index-row comparison can be visually clear without a published algorithm for every pair.

## Source observations

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 7, §4.1 says map sections A through E are laid west to east, and shows that order. It says the following section's `01xx` row overlays the previous section's `39xx` row. It does not define a numeric west comparator for individual hexes or resolve the treatment of a hex spanning a directional boundary. Section 8.78, p. 16, uses west in the Allied rail restriction but does not give a coordinate formula.
- [Map sheets](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 4, Map D, show a compass with west to the left and a numbered, staggered grid. In the Mersa Matruh vicinity, D3615 lies visibly left/west of D3616; this is a local geographic observation for two distinct printed positions, not a ruling that either is a qualifying rail witness or route hex. Rail crossing at their shared side remains disputed in the [route reading](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-route-batch.md). The sheet also shows that numbers have two components: `36` is shared in this example, while `15`/`16` increase toward the right. A local comparison like this does not establish how to classify equality, half-column offsets, or all different-row pairs.
- [Air/Logistics rules and charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 25, §37.42, give shortest geometric hex distance, including hypothetical sea-grid crossings. The footnote gives identical numbered hexes on adjacent sections as 33 hexes apart, with D4102, C4102 and E4102 as its example. Alongside §4.1's sheet order, this is evidence for C-to-D-to-E west/east ordering of these matching-number examples, but applying the sheet diagram to exact hex positions is an inference, not an explicit §8.78 comparator. The footnote does not specify seam identity, traversability or rail status. The specific example is geographic only: no Axis occupant or otherwise valid Allied rail use has been established.
- The printed map edges use `xx33`/`xx34` and `xx01` in the overlap region, unlike §4.1's literal `01xx`/`39xx` wording. Prior [seam comparison](2026-11-17-graziani-seam-geometry-comparison.md) records this conflict and a candidate 33-column translation. Prior [distance decision](2026-11-17-graziani-map-distance-decision.md) records chart inconsistencies and unresolved source hierarchy. The text's west-to-east sheet order cannot alone certify a particular near-seam pair as distinct, aliased, adjacent or strictly ordered; do not compare sheet letters alone when overlap may matter. Nor does matching-number distance establish a translation for every pair.

## Application boundary

For a proposed nonlocal witness, first identify two distinct positions and independently establish their geographic order from a sufficiently clear source or an explicitly adopted, bounded policy. Then separately establish qualifying rail identity and construction, Axis combat occupancy, the chosen snapshot and what constitutes Allied rail use. Historical-line and destroyed-rail treatment are separate candidate-specific decisions, not qualifications stated by §8.78 itself; see the [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-west-geography-review.md). An established eastern witness can matter off route; the comparison alone proves neither a legal rail trip nor historical reachability. Unclear directional equality, overlap, or position remains unknown rather than automatically west or east. For destroyed and unbuilt rail distinctions, see [rail scope](2026-11-17-graziani-continuation-rail-scope.md).

A future comparator needs an explicit interpretation of strict west for equal or staggered positions, accepted map registration and coverage, and source reconciliation. Positive, negative and unknown synthetic cases should be distinguished from actual six-turn states; an incomplete witness search cannot prove absence. No geometry, runtime, content data or admission change follows from this note.
