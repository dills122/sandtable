# Supply destruction: sub-segment source boundary

**Finding:** The checked sources do not identify §32.17's “sub-segment” with a particular repeatable unit of the sequence. A movement/combat cycle is a plausible interpretation, not an explicit source definition. No inspected passage proves a failed attempt may be retried at the start of the next cycle, or that an empty cycle may be manufactured to obtain another roll. Nor does it prove one attempt per entire Operations Stage.

## Text and interaction

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §32.17, place the attempt **before any movement** in a given sub-segment of a “Movement/Combat Segment,” and limit attempts to one per Supply Unit per sub-segment. They specify one-half CPA of all units in the hex rounded upwards, and a separate die per Supply Unit. They do not name the sub-segment's start, end, or relation to another request or unit's move.
- §5.2 p. 12 instead names **G, Movement and Combat Phase**, with four numbered **Segments**: Movement, Breakdown Determination, Combat and Reserve Release. Player A may repeat segments 1–4 subject to §8.2, each repetition including all four. Combat contains six named **Steps**. Player B then performs phases F–L with A/B roles exchanged. The outline does not call the phase a Movement/Combat Segment or name its divisions sub-segments. Thus the repeatable four-segment cycle is real, but equating it to §32.17's sub-segment requires a choice.
- §8.0 p. 13 calls the ordinary movement unit the Movement Segment of the Movement/Combat Phase; its procedure moves units one at a time or in stacks. §8.11 also permits non-phasing movement in circumstances including reaction and retreat. Neither an individual counter move nor a command is identified as a new sub-segment. Whether “any movement” includes non-phasing activity, and which player can act in the other's portion, remain separate questions.
- §§8.21–.25 p. 14 describe repeated movement and combat, subject to movement restrictions; §8.22 says resolve combat only after all movement in each sequence has ceased. §8.25 permits repeat attacks and combat when no units move, given necessary ammunition and CP. A no-movement combat sequence is consequently different from a completely empty repetition, but neither explicitly grants a fresh §32.17 allowance. The source does not establish that a mere declaration, rejected action, or request creates a cycle.
- The [common charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF/printed p. 1 §6.3, list “attempt to blow a supply dump” at one-third CPA, without a mode qualifier, retry or sequence boundary. §32.0 applies to Land-only abstract logistics, and §32.12 represents abstract Supply Units with Supply Dump counters. As the [independent source review](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-subsegment-source-review.md) observes, terminology does not establish that §6.3 is inapplicable: its one-third CPA and §32.17's one-half require an explicit precedence interpretation or project policy. Do not silently merge or select the costs.
- The [independent cycle review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-supply-destruction-cycle-review.md) inspected the four-page [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf): §8.23 corrects its fourth-line cross-reference, and §32.0 comments on abstract rules, but no explicit correction to §32.17, §8.22 or §8.25 was found. This does not exclude another edition or later ruling.

## Implications and discriminating cases

Retain a versioned, durable identity for whichever timing boundary is eventually selected, along with movement occurrence, per-target attempts, cost and RNG. Reject duplicate attempts within the same identified sub-segment. Keep retry across an actual cycle, a valid no-movement combat cycle, an empty repetition, a new player portion, and a new Operations Stage as distinct policy questions; compare the source consequences before admission. Test a failed attempt and repeated request in one boundary, a real later cycle, a no-movement combat, an empty-cycle retry, non-phasing movement, and replay. Protect hidden target identity and avoid treating these synthetic vectors as legal witnessed play.
