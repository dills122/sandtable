# Independent review: conditional retreat overlap

**Date:** November 17, 2026
**Disposition:** Supported as a conditional textual overlap. No source basis found here for precedence, sequential retreats, a mandatory route-selection algorithm, or a historical Graziani position.

## Findings

- **Directional ZOC:** [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 18 §10.21(c), tests whether the *projecting* unit can enter the adjacent target hex from its current location. Its tank-to-marsh example confirms that direction. The witness assumes non-motorized `E` can enter clear `P` and meets the separate ZOC requirements; its ordinary hexside avoids §10.21(a–b). Therefore `E` can project into `P` without `P` being able to enter or project into `E`. The escarpment negative control is also sound: §10.21(b) blocks projection through that side in either direction.
- **Entry versus assault:** Page 15 §8.44 distinguishes restricted vehicle entry into marsh from the prohibition on motorized units and AFVs assaulting units defending there. Page 23 §15.34 also prohibits motorized assault against marsh defenders and frames its terrain restrictions in terms of full assaults or probes. A road or track permitting vehicle entry does not lift the assault restriction. The fixture separately assumes no barrage or other attack and no other unit attacks `E`; those assumptions are necessary to its §15.34 predicate.
- **Both predicates:** With `P` phasing, subject to no §10.32 exception, unable to assault or make a qualifying Holding Off barrage, and in `E`'s ZOC, the stated facts satisfy the textual conditions of both §15.34 and §10.36 before deciding their interaction. The [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), p. 1, excludes the non-phasing player from §10.3. Wording qualification: §10.31 requires *hexes containing enemy units exerting ZOC on friendly combat units* to be attacked; it does not itself require every individual phasing unit to attack. Section 10.36 separately describes a unit unable to assault or Hold Off. This distinction does not defeat the single-unit witness.
- **Distances and retreat:** For the listed axial route, distances from `E` are 1 initially and 2, 3, 3 after successive steps; all entered hexes are outside `E`'s adjacent ZOC. It travels three hexes and ends three hexes from `E`, matching §10.36's stated numerical constraints under the fixture's empty, clear terrain assumptions. Section 10.36 also specifies no doubling back, no retreat through enemy ZOC, surrender if forced into enemy ZOC, movement CP costs, and three additional disorganization points. Section 10.26 qualifies enemy ZOC effects for movement where a friendly combat unit occupies a hex. The route is a geometric witness, not proof that these passages specify a unique legal routing procedure or precedence over §15.34's one-hex retreat.
- **Scope:** The state assumes qualifying units and terrain; it does not establish an actual map location, roster, reachable scenario state, or exhaustive errata coverage. It is not evidence that this situation occurred historically or that either retreat consequence takes precedence.
