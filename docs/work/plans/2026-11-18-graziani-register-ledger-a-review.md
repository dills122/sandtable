# Graziani register ledger A: documentary review

November 18, 2026. Compared [ledger A](2026-11-18-graziani-register-ledger-a.md) with all 34 TI, SQ and LA rows in the [source register](2026-11-12-graziani-source-register.md), and checked selected linked reviews against the ledger's claims. This is an informed documentary audit, not an original-scan reread, exhaustive verification of every linked finding, runtime audit or acceptance of any rule.

## Findings

1. **LA-07: clarify what the subtotals count and link their actual evidence.** The ledger places 610 Axis and 262 Commonwealth beside a request to verify “per-hex first-line medium/heavy inputs,” which can be read as first-line subtotals. Both are bounded combined attached plus unattached totals: [Axis review](2026-11-17-graziani-initial-motorization-review.md) gives 305 + 305; [Commonwealth review](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-motorization-review.md) gives 147 + 115. The two links currently on LA-07 address conditional transport CPA and allocation, not those subtotal calculations. Label the sums as combined converted first-, second- and third-line contributions, link these accounting reviews, and retain location, air-facility exclusion, missing-category and allocation qualifications. This matters because LA-07's first-line attached pool cannot be inferred from the combined numbers.
2. **SQ-05: identify the specific actor/reorganization gate.** The register explicitly calls out errata §§8.71 and 8.73 and stage-specific reorganization, including separately authorized Axis use. Ledger's “actor restrictions” is too broad to show that this distinct source question survives. Name the errata, check whether the exception applies in Land-only mode, and test the relevant stage and actor separately from route and capacity. See [rail scope](2026-11-13-graziani-rail-scope.md). Do not infer an Axis rail phase merely from a catalog position.
3. **LA-13: retain the source-access boundary explicitly.** Register §32.16 requires per-unit supply access and range/terrain/ZOC conditions; the row concentrates on destruction, capture and dummies without naming the access conditions. Add a source and test action for a specific eligible drawing subject, range and route blocking, separate from destruction/capture. [Repair availability review](2026-11-18-graziani-repair-availability-followup-review.md) distinguishes the combat-unit draw subject from a field-repair companion and does not settle whether this draw governs repair.
4. **LA-17: retain the motorization Breakdown exemption and special movement.** The register expressly distinguishes Motorization Points from other Breakdown subjects. The ledger covers monthly loss, allocation and conservation but does not identify that exemption or special movement as remaining behavior. Add a mode-specific rule and test boundary, including other subjects' still-applicable Breakdown, without implying monthly motorization loss is Breakdown. See [logistics crosswalk](2026-11-12-graziani-abstract-logistics-crosswalk.md).

This check did not validate every link fragment. Sampled reviews for SQ-03, SQ-06, LA-07, LA-12, LA-16 and LA-18 support their bounded source qualifications except the LA-07 ambiguity above. No row is certified complete by this review. No runtime/data edits or gameplay tests.
