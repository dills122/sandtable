## What negative proof still requires

For a particular used rail hex and fixed authoritative snapshot, first select and justify geographic west relation, including equal-longitude and cross-sheet cases; establish relevant sheet registration and bound all potentially eastern hexes. Survey all potentially qualifying rail, including unsurveyed Map D branches, Map E off-route lines and any other sheets required by that bound. Resolve side/vertex and clipped-edge ambiguities, line identity and construction state with explicit provenance; certified unbuilt rail is excluded, while unknown state remains unknown. Apply a versioned policy for destroyed constructed rail and any historical or independently overlapping rail. Then establish complete snapshot occupancy and Axis combat-unit classification for every qualifying candidate, including attached combat units. A known qualifying occupied eastern rail hex may establish a block despite remaining unknowns; absence from this crosswalk or pinned data cannot establish an unblocked result. Timing, use, and audience-safe disclosure remain separate admission decisions.
