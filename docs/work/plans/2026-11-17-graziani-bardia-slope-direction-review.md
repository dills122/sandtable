# Independent review: Bardia slope direction

**Verdict: supported with a visibility qualification.** The exposed brown edge on the C4220/C4321 side splashes toward the Bardia hex, supporting C4321 down and C4220 up. City and AXIS overprint obscure part of the side and its adjoining junction; this is a visible-part reading, not an unambiguous reading of every mark.

## Evidence checked

- Independently rendered PDF p. 3 of [original maps](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), SHA-256 `8830ec78df489607a2cc7e2cbc8ddcc9457ecbad3b11df071a60d825dadfd030`, at 600 dpi, crop `-x 6800 -y 5800 -W 2000 -H 2000`. The `4220` label is partly crossed by the road, but readable. The Bardia hex label is obscured by artwork; its C4321 identity follows the surrounding printed numbering and grid, not a legible `4321` label. The side runs down-right from the junction above C4220 toward the vertex below the AXIS legend. Exposed irregular brown ink lies on the Bardia side of that diagonal, especially near its left end. Overprint limits certainty about the remainder and individual flecks at the junction.
- Map p. 1 terrain key shows brown one-sided splash for slope, brown splash on both sides for ridge, and dark gray splash for escarpment. The visible color and exposed splash support slope here; obstruction limits any claim that the opposite side is fully visible.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`, printed/PDF p. 14 §8.35, put slope and escarpment splash contours on the down side and give ridges no direction. Applied to the visible splash, C4321 is down and C4220 is up. This conclusion does not depend on pinned data.

No finding requires a runtime change. This check does not establish other sides or movement, road, combat, or ZOC effects.
