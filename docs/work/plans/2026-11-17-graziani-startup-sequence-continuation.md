# Graziani startup: sequence boundary and mode

17 November 2026. **Finding:** Scenario §60.22 starts Graziani at Game-Turn 1, Operations Stage 1. The normal Land-only turn has initiative and a Naval Convoy Stage before that stage. The Land/Air outline additionally has a Strategic Air Planning Stage before its Naval Convoy Stage. Direct entry at OpStage 1 is the literal scenario start; the sources do not expressly specify how earlier turn-1 stages relate to setup. Required pregame September convoy planning does not establish that those stages were played. A full preamble is an explicit project variant requiring its own state boundary, not the printed start or an official ruling.

## Source observations

- [Scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF pp. 1–4 / printed pp. 25–28: §59.2 describes forces, supplies and trucks available at scenario start; same-hex unit organization is subject to formation and stacking limits. §59.32 starts aircraft fueled and armed without supply cost; §59.36 forbids aircraft maintenance during the first OpStage of a scenario. §60.22 starts GT1 OpStage 1 and ends after GT6 OpStage 3. §60.32 independently forbids Italian plane refit until GT1 OpStage 2. These aircraft clauses do not authorize a pre-start maintenance step or bar land-vehicle and fleet repair merely by calling them repair.
- §60.37 requires Italian convoy runs for the remainder of September to be planned **prior to starting the game**, limits lanes to 2, 3 and 6, and directs consultation of the availability charts. This planning obligation is distinct from resolving an actual shipment, and does not say the whole Naval Convoy Stage has occurred. §60.36 restricts Italian strategic attacks on Malta to Availability Level I; it does not itself mandate a GT1 pre-start raid. Scenario §60.6 fixes Italian initiative in GT1; that is not a basis to roll a new winner.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 11–12, §§5.1–5.2: the outline explicitly applies to Land without Air and Logistics. Normal turn order is Initiative Determination; Naval Convoy Stage (Schedule, then Tactical Shipping); OpStages 1, 2 and 3. Naval Convoy Arrival is a separate phase **within** each OpStage, after Initiative Declaration, Weather Determination and Organization. Organization's reorganization, construction and training segments may be ordered as desired. The Commonwealth Fleet Phase includes fleet assignment and fleet repair; player A's later Repair Phase includes towing and maintenance, followed by patrol and player B's phases F–L. Both repair locations are inside the OpStage, not normal pre-OpStage phases. Their presence does not prove an eligible repair or successful outcome.
- [Air rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 3–4, §33.0: Land/Air turn order is Initiative, Strategic Air Planning, Naval Convoy, then first OpStage. Strategic planning includes designation, Malta availability, mission assignment and Malta raid. Its Naval Convoy Stage includes scheduling and convoy resolution; its OpStage has Weather, Organization, Arrival, Fleet and Land Support Air phases. Tactical Shipping is a segment of Organization in this outline, rather than the Land-only pre-OpStage phase. Land Support Air includes tactical aircraft maintenance before the player-specific land phases; Strategic Air Recovery follows OpStage 3. This is a separate mode's outline, not an extra stage silently inserted into Land-only §5.2. §59.36's first-stage aircraft maintenance restriction remains distinct from a question of whether any strategic preamble was played.
- [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 3, corrects §59.2 terminology, adds notes to §59.45, clarifies §60.31 and corrects §60.32's aircraft designation. Its §56.25 clarification concerns allocating arriving tonnage to an OpStage and §56.29 is an optional convoy-level rearrangement. These entries do not explicitly settle the GT1 entry boundary. This targeted check is not a claim that every later publication or all errata were exhausted.

## Conditional consequences

| Entry interpretation | Consequences needing an explicit contract |
| --- | --- |
| Full GT1 preamble: explicit variant | Retain §60.37 pregame September plans, use fixed Italian initiative, then carry out applicable mode's preceding stages. Reconcile plans and identities before any new scheduling or roll; do not duplicate commitments or assume a convoy, attack, loss or delivery. Land/Air strategic planning requires its own supported aircraft and mission state. |
| Direct GT1 OpStage 1 | Start at its Initiative Declaration and Weather phases; retain required September preplans and scenario setup. Earlier turn stages are not replayed under this interpretation. Any missing beginning-of-turn future planning or prior strategic allocation needs a sourced, versioned initialization policy or remains unknown; it is not automatically zero. The first OpStage's own Arrival, Fleet, and player repair phases remain in sequence. |

The literal scenario entry is OpStage 1. Normal full-turn ordering does not itself prescribe replaying earlier stages at that entry, and no inspected passage explains whether their consequences are in setup. Setup is a start snapshot: applying hypothetical preamble actions to it requires an independently specified state boundary to avoid changing initial placements twice. Conversely, the start snapshot is not proof that all preamble consequences were already resolved. Weather is determined within the first OpStage on either interpretation; no earlier weather result is established. First-month convoy-loss semantics and the GT1-to-GT3 planning offset remain separate open questions in [startup convoy analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-startup-convoy-next.md) and [first-month review](2026-11-17-graziani-first-month-review.md).
