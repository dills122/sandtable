## Three bounded checks

1. **Arrival placement predicate — source evidence narrows, does not close timing.** Commonwealth charts PDF p. 4, §4.43a's schedule key, distinguishes placing arrivals of the same parent together with desired attachments/detachments from requiring arrivals **not assigned the same parent** to use separate hexes if possible, subject to stacking. The latter explicitly says *assigned*: mere common attachment does not by itself establish the same-assignment predicate. Land PDF p. 29 §§19.13, 19.2, 19.21 and 19.26 distinguishes assignment from attachment, puts new assignment in the Reorganization Segment, and normally requires an independent unit already attached before assignment. An independent reviewer also identified Land §20.12’s waiver for entry and the first hex, which does not expressly waive attachment cost, and §20.13’s post-arrival provision; neither by itself resolves the order of the parent test and arrival attachments. These rules do not explicitly identify whether the schedule tests assignment immediately before entry, after any arrival-specific reorganization, or against printed OA at entry. Nor do they settle whether the schedule permits an exception to ordinary attachment costs and timing. **Disposition: source-supported constraint plus project/source precedence decision;** preserve alternatives and do not certify a placement without actual hexes, stacking, assignments and phase order. This sharpens the [prior follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-organization-batch-followup.md), not an independent review.

2. **2 RTR assignment on arrival — competing source wording.** Commonwealth OA PDF p. 11 lists 2 RTR among unassigned armor and note `b` states assignment to 4/7 Armored Brigade on arrival. Land §19.21 says an independent unit must be attached before assignment, and §19.2 places new assignments in reorganization; the schedule key permits desired arrival attachments but does not expressly waive that assignment sequence. Land §19.2 says units enter already assigned to a parent, creating a further tension with the unassigned OA heading. Section 19.24 permits players to institute or ignore historical assignment shifts subject to its restrictions and the specified month; whether it governs this arrival note is uncertain. The OA note may describe historical assignment, a specific arrival assignment, or an entry that leaves a player-controlled change under general rules; the listed evidence does not select one. **Disposition: open source/interpretation conflict**, not a proven free assignment or automatic initial attachment. Check controlling scenario and corrections before choosing; retain distinct assignment, attachment, location and cost. Prior [follow-up review](2026-11-17-graziani-organization-batch-followup-review.md) already checked the OA row; the general-rule comparison here is bounded and needs independent review.

3. **7 RTR parent — general example is not an initial assignment.** OA PDF p. 11 places 7 RTR in unassigned armor, without a note on that row. Land p. 29 §19.26 uses 4 and 7 RTR in an example of a player replacing two battalions in 2nd Armored Brigade, after which those two are assigned there. It describes a possible reorganization, not a dated GT2 arrival instruction. Land §19.66 contains a separate hypothetical involving 7 RTR; it likewise is not by itself a GT2 parent instruction. Neither example fills the initial parent gap or overrides the OA by itself. **Disposition: bounded source check;** leave initial parent and any legal later choice separate. A complete scenario/errata search or authoritative clarification could change the result. No claim that the inspected pages prove the absence of every exception.

The independent reviewer also flagged scenario §59.2’s distinction between setup and later arrivals: do not carry setup provisions into arrival handling without an applicable rule. These added qualifications follow that review; they were not all separately inspected in this closure check.
