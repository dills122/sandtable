# Land-only Axis convoy 5% comparison: independent critique

**Disposition, November 17, 2026:** The per-group ceiling is coherent as a bounded planning proposal, conditional on explicit acceptance of its non-monotonic outcome and group mapping. Evidence does not select it over the alternatives. Keep 5% unsupported for execution until the policy and its separate admission gates are accepted; if monotonic severity is required, compare the entire result hierarchy before choosing. No legal-load or reachability conclusion follows.

## Findings

1. **Arithmetic checks out under the stated assumptions.** For each nonempty integer group, `ceil(n/20)` gives 1, 1, 1 and 2 at counts 1, 19, 20 and 21. One supply plus one motorization gives two losses at 5% under the recommendation and zero at 10% under the reviewed branch: supply excluded, `floor(1/10) = 0` motorization. Combined ceiling and one-per-convoy each give one at 5%, also exceeding that 10% result; floor and zero give zero. One Italian tank plus one Italian gun gives two under separate-group ceiling and one under combined ceiling. These are synthetic vectors: their cargo legality and an actual attack remain unproved. Total point counts treat unlike cargo as a count, not an established measure of equal game value; the supply/motorization witness also shows a loss inversion in each category.
2. **Grouping is a policy choice, not a Land-derived six-group schema.** Land's reviewed language names supply, motorization and replacement points and distinguishes tank/gun losses by nationality over types. Its tank-only example does not establish separate tank and gun groups, nor application of that grouping at 5%. Air §41.67's separate detailed groups and upward rounding are an analogy; mapping abstract supply and motorization to groups is also a choice. The comparison's reference to respecting Land's nationality distinction should be read with those limits, not as source proof of the complete grouping. Other replacement classes and uncertain membership must block this bounded policy rather than disappear.
3. **Non-monotonicity requires a decision, not just disclosure.** Source material does not demonstrate that a 5% result should cause greater losses than 10%, and source silence does not mandate zero, floor or a ceiling. A requirement for monotonic severity would call for fixed legal cargo and comparable allocation across 0%, 5%, 10%, 20%, 25% and other reachable outcomes, including supply exceptions and unresolved minima. Even a zero 5% rule alone proves no such ordering. The present recommendation is safe to use only as a conditional planning option, not as an accepted ruling or executable contract.
4. **Admission remains separately blocked.** Establish accepted legal individual convoy, lane and immutable complete real cargo; classification and dummy handling; division eligibility, geometry, threshold direction and equality; timing and ordered dice; and allocation authority where several models can satisfy a group loss. Preserve drawn result and RNG cursor; atomically commit losses and survivors with deterministic IDs and retry deduplication, and prevent cargo disclosure through output or timing. No partial delivery, reroll or treatment of unknown as empty. Preserve 10% and 20%-plus branches and their unresolved grouping/minimum questions. Bind any later policy to immutable ruleset identity and isolate old saves.
