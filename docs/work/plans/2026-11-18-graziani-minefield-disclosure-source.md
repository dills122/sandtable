# Minefield construction and discovery: disclosure source check

**Result:** The printed rules distinguish a secret construction item from a minefield counter whose real/dummy side is revealed when an enemy unit enters. They do not explicitly define every opponent-facing construction, completion or digital event. Dummy removal timing has conflicting printed descriptions; do not silently select one.

## Printed triggers and audiences

- [Land addendum §3.61–.62](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 45: §3.61 restricts an opposing player's knowledge of a counter's status, composition and other attributes except where expressly indicated. Section 3.62 gives a **partial** list: a player may examine map stacks, while contents remain distinct; the item undergoing construction or demolition is secret. It does not say an Under Construction marker or its hex is invisible, nor specify that project identity becomes public merely because construction ends.
- Land §§24.31–.34, p. 36: engineer units or Commonwealth engineer HQs may construct real or dummy minefields. Their counters indicate real/dummy on the reverse; either takes one full Operations Stage, with a minefield begun at one stage's start completed at the next Construction Segment. A regular field requires the listed supplies at start and an **Under Construction** marker in the hex; a dummy is built in the same way with different supply costs. These clauses describe the constructing player's procedure; their differing costs do not themselves authorize disclosure of project type or expenditure to the opponent. Section 32.11's abstract-supply interaction remains a separate mode question.
- Land §§26.11 and .15, p. 38: all minefield counters share a front; their reverse distinguishes real from dummy. A **friendly minefield** is flipped to reveal its real/dummy type immediately upon entry by an **enemy unit**. This is an explicit entry-triggered type reveal; it does not specify earlier remote inspection, a public log, loss of hidden history, or all audiences for a derived digital event. Section 26.23 separately says entering an enemy dummy reveals it and still costs the same as entering a real one.
- Land §24.38 says a real field, friendly or enemy, is removed after an engineer or engineer-capable HQ spends an entire Operations Stage in the hex; a dummy is removed upon revelation of its status. Section 26.13 similarly covers real-field removal. For an **enemy-entry** dummy, §26.14 says removal at the end of the **Movement Segment** in which an enemy unit spends CP to enter; §26.23 says the end of that **Movement Phase** of the Operations Stage, and explicitly charges later entries in that phase. These are distinct printed timings alongside §24.38's general wording. Their precedence or reconciliation needs an explicit source or interpretation; immediate type revelation under §26.15 does not itself resolve removal timing.

The source supports a meaningful before/after distinction for a conditional constructed minefield: during construction, project item remains secret to the opponent; after an enemy unit enters the completed field, real/dummy type is explicitly revealed. It does **not** establish that the printed front-side appearance, project completion, or a particular marker was actually seen in a scenario, or prescribe a complete side-specific snapshot, action error, timing channel or event history. Section 26.15 does not independently identify a remote observer or implementation delivery channel.

## Errata and scope

The [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2, includes §24.15's separate clarification about §24.12 and stacking, and §24.72 on air facilities. The checked printed errata has no directly numbered §3.6, §24.31–.34/.38 or §26 correction; this is not proof that an indirect clarification or later edition does not exist. No real/dummy removal policy or source precedence is selected.
