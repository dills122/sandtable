# 1st RTR at C3520: source-baseline ZOC research

**Disposition:** the scenario and OA give a source baseline of seven A9 tank TOE points at C3520. The A9's defensive close-assault rating 3 gives **21 raw defense**, above §10.15's ten-raw threshold. An [independent counter review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-first-rtr-zoc-review.md) reads a printed stacking value of **1**, so the isolated battalion baseline does not meet §10.11's more-than-one condition. This does not establish the actual setup stack or positive ZOC.

## Source readings

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.41, explicitly places `1st RTR (7/7)` at C3520 with five medium trucks. The formation designation and listed trucks do not themselves settle all attachment or setup choices. Map C places C3520 adjacent to Sheferzen C3419; see [fixed-contact inventory](2026-11-17-graziani-fixed-contact-inventory.md) and [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-fixed-contact-inventory-review.md).
- [Commonwealth OA and charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 6, 7th Armored Division, lists `1st Royal Tank Regt`, `1 RTR`, ID `g`, `7 × A9`, `D`, under 7th Armored Brigade. This is its listed deployment TOE, not seven complete vehicles. PDF p. 22 §4.46a gives `g` tank battalion-equivalent, CPA 25, and a maximum of ten tank TOE (with chart's separate US-tank qualification). A seven-of-ten baseline is above the below-half criterion for a battalion shell in Land §9.26, subject to setup and state corrections; this does not itself establish a valid current attachment or whole stack.
- Commonwealth chart PDF p. 27 §4.47 lists A9 Cruiser CPA 25, AA 1, anti-armor 3, armor protection 1, close assault offense/defense 3/3, fuel rate 2 and breakdown adjustment 1R. Land §§11.31–.32, PDF p. 19, use TOE multiplied by applicable rating for raw strength. At the seven-A9 baseline and unmodified defensive rating, `7 × 3 = 21` raw points. Table values, actual usable strengths, supply and scenario rules must not be conflated with a completed combat calculation.
- [Counter sheets](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 4, were independently inspected in the [counter review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-first-rtr-zoc-review.md): the `1 RTR` / `7/7` face shows `II` and unparenthesized `1`, the latter the printed stacking value. That review supplies crop coordinates, source hash and the field interpretation. The original note's counter-face reading was unavailable; the value here is attributed to the independent review.

## ZOC and contact boundaries

Land §10.11, PDF p. 17, requires a unit above battalion-equivalent, described as more than one stacking point, or a hex with more than one stacking point. Section 10.15, pp. 17–18, independently denies ZOC to units in a hex totaling below ten raw defensive close-assault points. At the specific **isolated**, unchanged seven-A9 baseline, 21 clears the latter threshold, while the one-point battalion counter does not clear the former. Other legal units co-located at setup can change a whole-hex result; owner choices, unit state and accurate representation are not assumed.

Section 10.21 separately bars projection through specified terrain and into a hex the exerting unit could not enter. The C3520–C3419 shared side has a visible dashed track lead, not a certified complete side or a legal movement route. Assess direction, actual terrain/edge, entry, counter/stack identity, strength, cohesion and phase with complete coverage. Section 10.31–.32 and further combat rules determine obligations only after ZOC and relevant states are established. Adjacency or raw 21 does not alone prove projection onto the garrison, a contact marker, an attack, or a legal historical start.

Proposed focused vectors after policy review and admission: preserve the OA's seven A9 versus the `g` maximum ten and the independently read one-point counter; compare the isolated and enriched §10.11 stacking cases and 21 raw with 9/10 threshold cases; test projection over known/unknown sides and terrain restrictions, both sides' setup choices, stage timing, replay and audience-safe observations. These are not executed tests.
