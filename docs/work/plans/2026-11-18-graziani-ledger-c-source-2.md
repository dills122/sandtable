# CO-01: what the railroad provides

**Result:** A previously unchecked connection gives the railroad parenthetical in scenario §60.7 a concrete pipeline referent. It does **not** settle whether the scenario's separate no-fortifications wording suppresses intrinsic major-city levels, or whether its parenthetical has any other scope.

Visually checked [Air/Logistics rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 27 / printed p. 23, §§54.3–.34, and [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, §60.7. Local SHA-256 verified: Air `9e11678e26269525c25fd276a760e827a7cc738b987b39e89fe14295a7164cf4`; scenario `fa12488e59552d7b0ff0d5abefa891d4b81af0492256bec2d25f8d61ef040a66`. These are the same original-scan editions as previous work, not independent editions.

- Scenario §60.7 lists no minefields, fortifications or pipeline, followed by an exception for what the railroad provides; it says that railroad runs to Mersa Matruh D3714 and ends there.
- Detailed-logistics §54.33 explains that railroad hexes are themselves pipelines, in the course of saying water need not be transported by railroad. Section 54.34 separately reserves one Operations Stage per calendar month for water for railroad use. Thus the pipeline reading of §60.7's parenthetical has a specific printed referent, not just an inference from grammar. The two clauses do not, by themselves, establish a playable Land-only water or railroad-supply procedure.
- [Land §32.0/.11](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43, substitutes abstract supply when Air and Logistics are omitted and says only the supply rules below apply. This bounds use of the detailed-logistics provision as explanatory context; it does not authorize importing water costs or a pipeline action into Land-only play.
