# Tobruk repair facility lifecycle: bounded planning proposal

**Status: unadmitted planning policy.** Use the existing RUL-GRA-07 initial profile of **one temporary facility** at Tobruk for the scenario's starting snapshot. Preserve that facility's identity and damage as separate facts. Beyond that snapshot, fail closed on transitions whose result depends on the unresolved §22.31/§60.33 conflict or §24.81 destruction meaning; do not create, convert, duplicate or erase a facility merely because control changed. This is a conservative project boundary, not a claim that no major facility exists under the original rules.

## Source and alternatives

[Scenario §60.33](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed p. 27/PDF p. 3, specifies temporary facilities at Tobruk and Benghazi and a major facility at Tripoli. [Land §22.31](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 35, lists Tobruk as a major facility for whoever controls it. The [facility-control research](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-facility-control.md) and [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-facility-control-review.md) establish no express conversion, replacement or coexistence rule. Competing readings remain:

1. The scenario's temporary designation continues until a separately lawful destruction or replacement event; no major facility arises solely from control.
2. The scenario governs only the initial snapshot and the general major facility applies after a specified transition; timing, identity and damage carryover are unprinted choices.
3. A general major facility coexists with a scenario temporary facility; initial one-facility planning policy does not admit this, and the source does not explicitly establish it.

The initial profile alone selects none of these as the post-control source truth. Land §24.81 p. 37 says temporary facilities **may** be destroyed by enemy-unit presence; major facilities cannot be destroyed **in that manner**. The text does not specify whether destruction is mandatory, relative to which owner or controller, precisely when checked, or how its result relates to an independently claimed major facility. Do not convert “may” into a chosen automatic or discretionary event without review.

## Proposed state and transition gates

Track actual facility identity, type, location, originating scenario and ruleset version, owner/controller if established, damage/effectiveness, and destruction status separately from unit presence and hex control. Record pre-event snapshot, actual entry/presence, changes in control and opposing ZOCs, candidate destruction check, any adjudicated consequence, subsequent rebuild/dismantle and use in causally ordered events. A control event alone proves neither enemy presence nor destruction; §10.27 recognizes mutual control by opposing ZOCs. Presence alone does not select a destruction outcome, a new owner, or an automatic control transition. Do not publish a completed post-transition result until event order, authority, and source policy are selected and the necessary hidden state is available.

Land §24.85 permits temporary facilities in a major-city hex or village and limits each player to two; that supports examining coexistence but does not prove it. §24.87 bars **new construction** in enemy-controlled hexes while allowing facilities to be **rebuilt or dismantled** there. §24.83 governs new temporary construction; §24.84 describes rebuilding after major-city levels are reduced by bombardment, not a universal procedure for every destroyed facility. §24.86 governs dismantling a temporary facility and §24.88 makes it unusable during dismantling. Don't turn these permissions into automatic repair, assume resources under abstraction, or count a proposed replacement as existing. Facility repair of vehicles is separately gated: §22.32 requires a broken vehicle or destroyed tank to start the Maintenance Segment at a facility and not have been towed that Operations Stage; §22.34's type column applies to **broken vehicles**, while destroyed tanks use distinct §22.4 rules. §§22.13 and .36–.37 add enemy control/ZOC and weather qualifications, including specified major-facility and major-city exceptions. Reconcile those cases before making a legal repair claim. None of the construction rules establishes the identity or eligibility of a post-control facility.

Admission requires a versioned ruling selecting lifecycle and §24.81 event/actor semantics, control and presence observations, event ordering, ownership and damage persistence, legal construction/rebuilding/dismantling and repair eligibility, resources and abstraction, and replay. As the [independent policy review](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-facility-lifecycle-policy-review.md) emphasizes, unknown must never become absent, undamaged, safe or destroyed in action generation, replay, projection or later events. Hidden opposing presence and facility status must not leak through options, errors, event cardinality or downstream state; require observation equivalence across hidden alternatives and define side-authorized observations. Fail closed where coverage or privacy is missing. This note does not assert Tobruk is reachable or a control change occurs in the six-turn scenario.

## Synthetic discrimination checks

Compare initial temporary profile; control change without enemy occupancy; enemy presence without exclusive control change; coincident events; and departure/re-entry. Under each accepted alternative check facility identity, damage, no unchosen duplicate or replacement, and temporary versus major presence consequences. Separately test new construction, rebuilding and dismantling in enemy-controlled hexes, cap and costs, Maintenance Segment and tow requirements, weather/ZOC exceptions, unknown hidden presence, interruption and deterministic replay. These are proposed tests, not executed outcomes.

Source locators, same-scan hashes and errata limits are documented in the linked source and independent review; September errata contains no identified explicit §22.31, §24.81 or §60.33 correction in the checked package, not proof of no later ruling. No runtime changes or gameplay tests.
