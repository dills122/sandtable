# Independent review: Graziani combat-unit classification

**Disposition:** The candidate's source-based distinction between §60.81 combat units and §60.82's non-parenthesized Close Assault Rating requirement is supported. Its RNF, attached-HQ and supply-unit examples are properly conditional. No material source error found in the focused check; special intrinsic HQ weapons and objective supply/possession remain separate gates.

| Question | Source check |
| --- | --- |
| General definition | Land §3.1 defines a combat unit by capacity to engage other units and/or aircraft and notes HQs, engineers and dummy tanks can count in certain situations. §3.23 lists infantry, tank, recce, artillery, anti-tank and AA types; some noncombat units may count for specified determinations. This does not make every counter or every engineer/dummy a victory combat unit. |
| RNF / artillery witness | Scenario §60.41 places **1st Royal Northumberland Fusiliers (I)** at C3721 and **7th Medium Artillery Regiment** at C3922. Under §3.23 their printed infantry and artillery types are combat types. These are Commonwealth examples; they prove neither Italian victory qualification nor present possession of an objective. |
| Attached HQ | §3.31 says an HQ with **any combat units attached** is itself a combat unit, fighting with the combined strengths. §3.1's attachment definition requires same hex and representation by parent counter; assignment alone is insufficient. Scenario §60.31 lists 63 Cirene at C4120 with LXIII (L) attached; subject to actual starting structure and state this is a concrete positive witness. Neither that witness nor a roster link proves a victory-city occupant. |
| Empty HQ and own weapons | §3.33 says an HQ with no attached combat units is not a combat unit, may use parenthesized ratings, occupies zero stacking points, and may not voluntarily attack. §3.34 separately provides certain Italian HQs directly assigned artillery, uses their combat strengths and says they revert to noncombat when the guns are gone; §3.35 addresses certain armored HQ tanks usable only without attached combat units. The relationship between these explicit special cases and empty-HQ classification deserves a case-specific ruling; presence of a parenthesized rating alone is insufficient. Addendum §3.36 further clarifies that an HQ with parenthesized combat values may defend, without settling the victory predicate by itself. |
| Supply Unit | §32.13 explicitly says a Supply Unit has no combat capabilities. A lone real Supply Unit does not establish the required combat-unit occupant. Its capture/ownership state is not determined by this negative classification. |
| Scenario distinction | Scenario §60.81's Italian strategic condition requires a combat unit at Alexandria or Cairo, with supply from a convoy from map D; decisive condition refers to any combat unit at Mersa Matruh and Siwa, supplied from Tobruk. Other conditions use their own capture, hold and supply wording. §60.82 separately says places under its Italian Campaign victory calculation must be held by combat units with **non-parenthesized Close Assault Ratings**. It does not expressly amend §60.81. Qualifying a type or HQ is not proof of location, control, supply, timing, simultaneity or winner. |

**Scope caution:** §3.31 describes the HQ as a combat unit when it represents attached combat units, but does not itself answer whether an HQ counter's subordinate Close Assault Ratings satisfy the separate §60.82 parenthetical. Similarly, §60.81's use of “a combat unit” or “any combat unit” does not settle every multiple-objective occupancy question. Preserve unknown when attachment, special weapons, actual map position or supply facts are missing.
