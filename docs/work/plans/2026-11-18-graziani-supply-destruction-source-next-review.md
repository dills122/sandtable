# Supply destruction prior-expenditure case: source review

**Disposition:** The conditional arithmetic and cross-portion reading are supported. Keep the hypothetical in the same Operations Stage and retain the explicit CPA-8, single-basis and eligible-attempt assumptions. No attempt or payer allocation is established.

Visually checked [original Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 12 §6.14: its example names the non-motorized 1st Royal Northumberland Fusiliers machinegun battalion, CPA 8, retreating three hexes for 6 CP before assault. It gives two CP remaining before exceeding CPA when the Commonwealth portion of that same first Operations Stage begins. The text says the battalion spent no CP defending and Italians spent none on close assault because it retreated first. Section 6.14 counts expenditure across both players' portions of an Operations Stage; §6.16 separately says unused CP do not carry into the next Operations Stage, while cohesion levels do. The example does not establish an actual scenario play or a later action's eligibility.

PDF/printed p. 43 §32.17 states one-half the CPA of all units in the hex, rounded upward, as the expenditure, followed by one die per Supply Unit. Under the note's explicit hypothetical that the relevant basis is just a unit with CPA 8, the charge is 4, not half of its remaining 2 CP; 6 plus 4 is 10 if the entire charge is assigned to that unit. The source wording does not settle how a hex-wide expenditure is charged among multiple units. The unit's CPA 8 must still hold at the hypothetical action: §6.17 gives a motorized 1st RNF an illustrative CPA 20 if transported by medium trucks. Neither assumption establishes transport or an actual opportunity.

Section 32.17 places attempts before any movement in a given Movement/Combat sub-segment, with one attempt per Supply Unit per sub-segment; it does not by itself establish the hypothetical later opportunity or adjudicate its relationship to the retreat and later cycles. Section 6.0 permits exceeding CPA under certain circumstances with cohesion consequences, not unconditionally. The note correctly leaves overrun, timing and eligibility open. Public printed example does not establish actual player visibility or dummy discovery.
