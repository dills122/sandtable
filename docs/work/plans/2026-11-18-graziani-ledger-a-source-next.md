# SQ-06 source follow-up: towing destination and repair timing

**Finding:** Original Land §§21.62, 21.66–.67 and §5.2 give specific towing constraints beyond the register's phase outline. A towed broken-down vehicle or destroyed tank must move **towards the nearest temporary or major repair facility**; a broken-down vehicle that unsuccessfully undergoes field repair may not be towed in that Repair Phase. The outline places towing before maintenance. These clauses constrain an implementation, but do not establish which facility is nearest, a legal scenario route, or an accepted way to reconcile timing and exceptions.
