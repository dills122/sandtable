# Aresca HQ size and tank eligibility: independent review

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition:** Core reading supported, with one denominator qualification. The proposed restriction remains an interpretation, not an explicit Aresca ruling.

## Visual source check

- [Counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 1, section 2: Aresca bears tank symbol, `III`, `(3)`, and `LTC`; no `G` is visible. [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 6, lists Aresca (1st) Regt HQ with one CV33, followed by XXI(L), LXII(L), LXIII(L) and I(M); the last three are marked attached elsewhere. The counter and OA support nominal super-brigade reading, not a present three-point stacking claim.
- [Land](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed p. 17 §9.22 directs use of printed stacking value with §9.4 rather than counter name or designation. [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) p. 2 maps three points to full super brigade or division shell and one point to **any brigade** shell. Land §3.33 says HQ's printed points represent its full complement. OA's regiment HQ and counter's `III` favor the former identification, without independently proving a universal rule for every printed three.
- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) printed p. 27 §60.31 says Aresca at C3919, all except XXI detached; its other three battalions appear attached to named divisions. This supports initial XXI attachment. Section 9.21 distinguishes assignment and attachment, and §19.12 confirms mere stacking does not establish attachment.
- Land §9.26 uses **total battalions that may be assigned**, not the number currently attached. Four OA-listed battalions are a plausible baseline, but the OA list alone does not expressly establish the maximum for §9.26. Land §19.2–.3 points to formation-organization limits and notes exceptions for singular structures. State the four-battalion denominator as an assumption pending reconciliation with those limits. Conditional on four and only XXI attached, one of four is below two thirds; it is a brigade shell. Section 9.27 excludes attached shell subunits from the full-parent count; it cannot improve this result. Section 9.28 reduces a brigade shell to battalion equivalent where size matters. A later full/shell determination needs actual eligible attachments and their own shell status.
- Land §3.35 restricts tanks assigned to armored division and armored brigade HQs while **any combat unit** is attached. It does not explicitly identify Aresca or settle whether a super brigade or shell is within that phrase. Applying it to Aresca is defensible but requires a versioned interpretation. Land §3.31's combined-strength language does not explicitly override §3.35. If no combat units are attached, §3.33 specifies zero stacking, no combat-unit status, and no voluntary attack; parenthesized combat ratings have separate conditions in §3.4. Those conditions prevent treating the empty-HQ exception as blanket combat permission.
