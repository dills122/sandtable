# Commonwealth contact hexes: initial co-location evidence

**Finding:** Scenario §60.41 individually lists 1st RTR at C3520 and 2nd Rifle Brigade at C3320; its explicitly flexible Commonwealth **land-unit** row is limited to Maps D or E, not these Map C hexes. The listed five and ten medium trucks are not additional combat counters for §10.11. No reviewed source here identifies a definite extra Commonwealth combat unit initially at either hex. This is a bounded source result, not proof that every scenario or setup rule forbids all co-location or that either actual final stack is isolated.

Scenario §60.41 places `1st RTR (7/7)` at `C3520`, five medium trucks, and `2nd Rifle Bde (I; 7Spt/7)` at `C3320`, ten medium trucks, on separate lines. It lists 7th Armored Division HQ at D3612 with the division units listed above detached. Do not silently move the HQ, restore attachments, or treat a printed parent designation alone as a co-located counter. Section 59.2 permits the owner to reorganize units **listed to begin in the same hex**, within formation and stacking rules; it does not by itself authorize relocating separately listed C3520 and C3320 units into one hex.

The same §60.41 expressly gives an `Anywhere on Map D or E` choice for 8th Medium Artillery, 65th and 149th Anti-tank, 9th Heavy AA, 15th Light AA and 57th Light AA. Both candidate hexes are on C, so that row alone does not authorize placing any of these at either one. Its Cairo/Helwan and Alexandria rows have their own place restrictions; neither states C3520/C3320. This review did not exhaust all special formation addenda, player-supplied setup decisions or hypothetical alternate source corrections. Do not convert absence from these rows into a global proof of no possible additional combat unit.

## Non-combat placements and ZOC limits

Section 60.43 gives second/third-line trucks in several location groups, including an `Anywhere, maps` row, subject to applicable setup restrictions. Section 59.44 requires second/third-line trucks available for supplies on-map to be placed at a friendly combat unit, supply dump or major city/town/oasis. These sources merit separate map, mode and scope checks; their presence does not create a new combat unit. Land §9.29 says first-line attached trucks do not count toward unit organizational size and truck convoys do not count against ordinary stacking limits (road space excepted); §10.11 expressly says truck convoys never exert ZOC. Section 60.92's Land-only motorization abstraction requires its own allocation rules; do not count the detailed truck quantities again as separate combat stacking points.

Detailed-game §60.44 permits Dump I and one dummy dump anywhere in Egypt on C or D. Both contact hexes appear Egypt-side under the separately qualified [boundary reading](2026-11-17-graziani-map-sheferzen-border.md), making them **possible geographic candidates** under a reviewed region and placement policy. This does not establish either dump was placed there. For Land-only play §60.92(C) replaces §60.44's initial supply status: its Dump 1 group must be on map D west of Mersa Matruh, and the dummy group follows that rule. Do not apply detailed-game Dump I's C-or-D option to that replaced Land-only group. Section 59.66(C) separately permits each qualifying group of 30 **other unattached** Motorization Points in the same hex to begin carrying one additional regular supply unit; fewer than 30 cannot begin with one under that group rule. Whether such a group can legally be allocated at either contact hex depends on initial truck placement, conversion, grouping, mode, stacking and owner choices. This is a possible additional supply unit, not an additional combat counter, and §60.92(C)'s map-D restriction on its named Dump 1 group is not an exhaustive ban on all other sources. Any further supply or dummy placement needs its own source and policy review. Land §32.14 gives supply units zero stacking value and §10.13 excludes informational markers such as dumps from exerting ZOC; do not count an additional dump as a combat counter. Their exact interaction with the §10.11 hex-stack predicate still needs accepted representation and rules rather than inference from a marker alone.

The separately checked printed standalone baselines are one stacking point and 21 raw defense for 1st RTR, and one stacking point and 12 raw defense for 2nd Rifle. Each clears §10.15's ten-raw threshold but, standing alone, does not satisfy §10.11's more-than-one stacking condition. Adjacency to the opposing Sheferzen hex, a qualified friendly stack elsewhere, or a non-combat marker does not supply a qualifying additional combat unit at the same hex. Any actual ZOC still depends on final legal co-location, stacking interpretation, unit state, edge/entry and phase.

An [independent setup review](2026-11-17-graziani-commonwealth-contact-stack-review.md) confirms the bounded reading and identifies the §59.66(C) option above.
