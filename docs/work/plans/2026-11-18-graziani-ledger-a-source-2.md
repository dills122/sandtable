# SQ-06: tank recovery squadron exception and six-turn scope

**Result:** The original §22.6 recovery procedure gives a **turn-worded** restriction to the recovery squadron itself, distinct from the ordinary vehicle's Repair-Phase restriction. The normal schedules place the named Commonwealth tank delivery squadrons and German tank recovery squadron after Graziani's six-turn window. This narrows an exception lead; it does not prove absence of all exceptional introductions or settle the ordinary failed-field-repair timing question.

## Specific source comparison

- Land §22.65, printed p. 35, permits the Commonwealth recovery squadrons to tow up to three tank TOE points at a movement rate of 20 CP toward a facility, and says they may not attempt field repair **in a turn in which they tow**. The 20 CP is a rate, not a flat towing charge. The rule prints `DTS`; adjacent §§22.62–.64 call the counters Tank Delivery Squadrons (`TDS`). Section 22.66 says a squadron exceeding its CPA in an Operations Stage cannot attempt repairs in the ensuing Operations Stage but may still tow. Section 22.63 separately says it may not *voluntarily* exceed its CPA. These are restrictions on the specialized squadron, not a general permission for an ordinarily towed vehicle to be repaired.
- Land §22.62 gives a co-located squadron a field-repair modifier for broken-down tanks and a separate die condition for destroyed Commonwealth tanks. Section 22.7 says the German Mobile Tank Repair Squad is treated the same except that it repairs only German tanks. This source explicitly provides a specialized field-repair procedure for destroyed tanks notwithstanding the ordinary facility requirement in §22.41, subject to its own conditions. It does not say that the same tank may be towed and repaired in the same Repair Phase.
- The [Commonwealth reinforcement schedule](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf) §4.43a PDF p. 3 lists Desert Tank Delivery Squadrons A, B and C at **GT29/OpStage 2**. The [Axis schedule](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) §4.43b PDF p. 2 lists a Tank Recovery Squadron at **GT24/OpStage 3**; the [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-a-source-2-review.md) corrected this note's original GT26 transcription. The line does not print the full §22.7 name, so counter identity is a candidate crosswalk, not established here. Scenario §60.22 ends GT6/OpStage 3. These normal entries are after the scenario; initial setup, exceptional introduction, optional rules and separate counter identity are not exhaustively excluded.

The **turn** in §22.65 and **Operations Stage** in §22.66 are different printed scopes. Whether “turn” in §22.65 means the entire Game-Turn and how specialized towing interacts with repair-phase order require an explicit interpretation; do not replace either printed term silently. Ordinary §21.66's failed-field-repair bar and §5.2's towing-before-maintenance order remain as described in the [earlier source check](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-a-source-next.md) and [review](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-a-source-next-review.md); specialized squadron rules do not themselves resolve that ordering. Land-only repair supplies, actual squadron availability, unit identities, facility control and routes remain separate gates.
