# TI-05: Rommel schedule and six-turn initiative scope

**Result:** Rommel is absent from the printed §60.31 Italian initial deployment, and the ordinary Axis land reinforcement schedule lists **Rommel [DAK] at Game-Turn 20 / Operations Stage 2**. Graziani runs GT1/1 through GT6/3, so this scheduled arrival is outside its window. This narrows the normal source path; it does not establish Rommel's absence in every possible scenario state or exclude an unexamined exceptional introduction.

- [Scenario §§60.22, 60.31, 60.37](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27: the scenario ends at GT6/3; the enumerated initial Italian units do not name Rommel; §60.37 directs reinforcements to the track. [Axis chart §4.43b](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 2, explicitly lists `Gm: Rommel [DAK]` at **20 / 2**, after its first printed whole-unit row at 9 / 2. [Independent diversion review](2026-11-18-graziani-benghazi-diversion-source-next-review.md) checked the complete ordinary track, but not every exceptional introduction. Setup, ordinary arrivals, replacement-point cargo and optional/non-track paths must remain distinct.
- [Initiative chart §7.2](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), p. 2, gives Commonwealth **3** for GT1–42. Axis rating is **6** when Rommel's counter is on the Game-Maps, **3** for German land combat units but no Rommel there, and **1** under the printed no-German-combat-units-or-Rommel condition. Its footnote excludes the Tripoli/Tunisia holding boxes on Map A for this purpose. Preserve the different “land combat units” and “combat units” wording and its grammar; the ordinary Rommel schedule does not independently establish absence of all qualifying German units or settle a special non-land case.
- [Land §31.0](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 42, requires two dice at the start of each Game-Turn Rommel is present: a **12** puts him in Germany and makes Axis initiative **3** that turn, with return next turn unless another 12 occurs. Do not automatically award 6 merely because the on-map chart condition was once true, or require an additional German land-combat-unit witness for the §31.0 reduction. Exact application and return ordering remain as identified in the [initiative review](2026-11-17-graziani-initiative-ratings-review.md).
- [Land §§17.23/.28](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 27: the ordinary adjusted-morale maximum is +3 with a §17.28 exception; Axis units stacked with Rommel at the **instant of assault** gain +1 adjusted morale. [September errata §17.28](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2, confirms a final +4 is possible. This is a separate conditional combat effect, not an initiative modifier or a change to Basic Morale. Neither the schedule nor an hypothetical presence proves an eligible stack or assault.
- The [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-rommel-schedule-source-review.md) notes that §31.0 item 3 separately describes a +1 morale effect when Rommel is in the same hex as a unit involved in combat during the Combat Phase, for units in that stack. Its trigger and subject differ from §17.28's Axis/instant-of-assault wording; do not silently merge them or assume stacking effects without a rule decision.
- Scenario §60.6 fixes Italian initiative for all of GT1; ordinary determination starts GT2, as independently checked in the [initiative review](2026-11-17-graziani-initiative-ratings-review.md). The holder chooses order each Operations Stage. A scheduled GT20 arrival does not decide a GT2–6 contest roll, actual presence, Game-Map coverage or winning side.

Before claiming a six-turn Rommel state, source a specific exceptional introduction and verify its mode, timing and presence; absent such proof, retain unknown. Distinguish normal GT20/2 arrival from a conditional §31.0 Germany roll and from §17.28's co-location requirement. The reviewed source does not prove that no alternative exists.
