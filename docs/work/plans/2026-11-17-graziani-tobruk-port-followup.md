# Tobruk port: obstruction, repair and errata follow-up

**Disposition:** Preserve [RUL-GRA-10](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-10-tobruk-initial-port-efficiency-planning-interpretation)'s provisional 2/5 setup and the conflicting printed 7. No newly identified official correction selects a starting value. Harbor clearance, abstract-mode costs and later damage composition remain separate admission gates. A separate September erratum creates a material throughput interaction that should not be summarized as universally independent cargo capacities.

## Source check

The [independent port reading](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-port-independent.md) and [policy review](2026-11-17-graziani-rul10-review.md) document original scenario §60.7's **7**, chart §55.3's maximum **5** and below-five obstruction footnote, and Air/Logistics §55.25's **three-level** San Giorgio reduction. The 2/5 calculation chooses the chart and reduction over the explicit scenario value; the latter already includes the obstruction and should not have three subtracted again. Seven as starting level, 2/5, and other possible reconciliations remain distinct interpretations, not publisher corrections. A 7-based policy must also specify operation above the chart maximum. [Living Rules](https://basmith.net/cna/tables/port-capacity) favors 2/5; another [simulator's local ruling](https://tangled.org/glassparency.bsky.social/cna-agent-sim/blob/28e183a5b24d2d60b4c97004b01cdc1cc08c3b92/docs/rules/source-notes/initial-naval-setup.md) selects 5/5. Neither is official authority for this project.

This follow-up visually rechecked [Air/Logistics rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF pp. 27–28 / printed pp. 23–24, SHA-256 `9e11678e26269525c25fd276a760e827a7cc738b987b39e89fe14295a7164cf4`. Section 55.18 grants one ordinary recovery point per Operations Stage without enemy bomb loss, except §§55.26–.27, and forbids exceeding assigned maximum. Section 55.26 says friendly engineer battalions or Commonwealth HQ with engineering capability undo harbor blockage, one level per Operations Stage, at Tobruk cost 25 ammunition and 10 stores per level. It says engineers reverse §55.22, whose blocking procedure requires the eligible unit and supplies in the port hex during the Construction Phase. This cross-reference is relevant to a detailed-mode clearance contract, but does not itself decide how all blocking prerequisites transfer to clearance, whether Land-only mode retains the action or its resource costs, or how clearance combines with bomb damage or mines. The separate §55.27 mine-removal procedure should not silently replace San Giorgio clearance.

## Errata and throughput scope

Rechecked searchable text of [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), SHA-256 `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`. Section 55.11 directs use of chart §55.3 for what may be shipped; §55.3 addresses excess Axis arrivals at Tripoli. Neither explicitly corrects Tobruk's starting 7 or maximum 5. Search is limited to this packet and extraction; later corrections may exist.

**Additional conflict:** errata §30.57 expressly reduces maximum tonnage for the stage by 10% per incoming stacking point and gives Tobruk's 1 incoming SP reducing 1,700 tons to 1,530; outgoing SP have no effect. For ports with incoming capacity above one SP, receiving at least 50% of maximum supply tonnage reduces the SP level by **at least one-third**; supplies have no effect for a one-SP maximum. The minimum reduction and rounding require interpretation. Chart §55.3 instead says stacking-point and tonnage handling have no effect on each other; the [independent port review](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-port-independent.md) accurately reports that chart text, but it is not a complete cross-source policy. Section 55.11's direction concerns what may be shipped and does not explicitly settle this different conflict. The erratum is in the Commonwealth port-transfer section, so determine its operational scope, relation to chart and §55.15, ordering with efficiency reductions, rounding, and applicability to Land-only §32.36 before adopting a formula. Its 1,530 example uses the listed 1,700 figure and does not establish Tobruk's starting efficiency or actual initial throughput.

The [independent follow-up review](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-port-followup-review.md) checked searchable errata text but could not visually inspect the source pages. A later parent-session rendering of the hash-matched errata PDF p. 2 visually corroborated the 10% reduction, Tobruk example, 50% threshold and “at least” one-third reduction. That later check does not make the independent review visual, verify the chart, or decide mode and precedence.
