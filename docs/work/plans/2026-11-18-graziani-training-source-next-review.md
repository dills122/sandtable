# Independent review: training instructor threshold

**Confirmed:** I visually inspected original [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 28 §17.35. The printed minimum is **at least one-third of assigned TOE strength**, applying to battalions used as instructors; one-half is incorrect for this condition. The reviewed [training source follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-training-source-next.md) identifies that discrepancy accurately. The 2-of-6 example meets the printed fraction and is not proof of an actual instructor.

The same paragraph requires a combat battalion that has or has reached its designated Basic Morale to be in the trainee's hex during each training Operations Stage. Infantry divisions or brigades may use an infantry-type battalion; any unit with tanks must have a tank battalion present. Instruction battalions do not count against stacking limitations, and each can train as many units as may be stacked in the hex. These additional conditions are not satisfied by the strength fraction alone.

For integral actual and assigned strength, `3 × actual >= assigned` implements the stated threshold exactly, without choosing a separate rounding convention. This arithmetic does not determine which points count as assigned or actual, whether fractional or mixed strengths are possible, or whether the battalion meets type and morale requirements. The source paragraph does not explicitly prescribe those additional accounting decisions.

The [replacement-training analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-replacement-training-followup.md) documents §20.43's replacement-training cross-reference and its distinction from Commonwealth morale training. Whether each §17.35 condition applies to each replacement class and side remains an interpretation, including the Axis center and gun-uncrating cases; this targeted visual check does not independently settle that interaction. The scenario's 6th Australian training entry does not itself establish an instructor, co-location or strength. This review did not freshly inspect §20.43, the scenario, or errata; those statements rely on the cited prior source reviews.
