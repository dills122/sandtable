# Construction stacking: September erratum and Land-only scope

**Result:** In Graziani Land-only, §32.9 already removes §9.0's road/track-only stacking-point restriction for all units. Section 24.12 separately says units involved in construction do not affect road/track stacking limits; September errata §24.15 identifies §24.12 as an exception to §24.15's last sentence. Neither establishes that construction units disappear from ordinary terrain-hex stacking, waives the one-project rule, or grants a construction action. No new scenario-specific capacity or reachable project follows.

## Source cross-check

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 36, §24.12: if construction participants expend any CP during the Operations Stage, construction is halted and that stage does not count. Its separate following sentence says units involved in construction do not affect road/track stacking limits. Section 24.15 prints **one sentence** joining two clauses: at most one project per unit at a time, and construction units subject to all stacking rules. [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 middle column, §24.15 calls §24.12 an exception to its last sentence. The road/track sentence provides the specific apparent stacking exception; the erratum does not name a clause, abolish the project limit, or explain the boundaries of participation at every timing transition.
- Land p. 17 §9.33 describes the five-point road/track limit when a motorized unit using the road/track moves through friendly road/track users, including units that ended movement after using it. Section 9.14 and §9.3 describe separate terrain-hex stacking restrictions at their specified times. Section 32.9, p. 45, says ignore road and track **only** stacking-point restrictions listed in §9.0. Section 32.0, p. 43, applies that section when playing Land without Air and Logistics; [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed p. 28 / PDF p. 4, §60.92 labels its Air-and-Logistics-abstracted option Land Game Only. Thus §32.9's §9.33 waiver applies independently of whether a particular unit is constructing. The erratum does not remove general terrain capacity, setup rules, or other specific limits. See [road stacking source check](2026-11-17-graziani-road-stacking-next.md) and [review](2026-11-17-graziani-road-stacking-review.md).
- Scenario §60.7, same page, states no initial minefields, fortifications or pipeline apart from railroad-provided pipeline; railroad runs to Mersa Matruh and ends there. Those initial facts are not proof a construction unit occupies a road, that any project is underway, or that a proposed location is reachable and legal. Individual construction cases and scenario restrictions still govern choice, timing, weather, control, units and resources.

## Distinguishing cases

In a synthetic detailed-game comparison, where §9.33's five-point road/track restriction otherwise applies, a participating constructor is excluded from affecting that road/track limit by §24.12; the remaining applicable units and conditions still need assessment. In Land-only, §32.9 makes that particular §9.33 threshold inapplicable even when the unit is not constructing. Neither comparison permits stopping above the applicable **hex** stacking limit merely because a unit constructs or the road/track threshold is ignored. No concrete counter placement or stopping legality is established.

The CP sentence is a construction timing restriction, not the road/track exception. Land §24.22 separately says a storm stage does not count, and a participant leaving during a storm stops construction completely and requires restarting. Neither the erratum nor the road/track waiver settles whether a CP halt resets prior progress, when participation begins or ends for a stacking snapshot, or how multiple participating units and projects are attributed. Check those timings and any other relevant stacking exception before selecting a runtime policy.
