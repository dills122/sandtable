# Rail west-order source convention: review

**Disposition:** Supports [bounded source comparison](2026-11-18-graziani-rail-west-order-source.md). Chart §32.66 defines a same-suffix row grouping for its own lookup; importing it into Land §8.78 remains an interpretation. No global hex transform, seam registration or gameplay conclusion follows. Informed review of the same scans, not an independent edition.

- Axis chart §32.66 explains `Cxx01`: capital letter identifies the map section, `xx` permits any north–south hex, and final digits select the east–west hexrow. The chart expressly includes equality of east–west hexrow in its definition of a qualifying division. Its convoy route 2 column proceeds `Dxx01`, `Cxx16`, `Cxx01`, `Bxx16` through the first four listed thresholds. Those facts establish that chart's notation and lookup ordering; neither the division equality clause nor threshold ordering specifies a general individual-hex comparator or an exact sheet offset.
- On Map D, the printed D3515 and D3615 centers are visibly staggered: D3515 is left/west of D3615 in the map's north-up view, while both end in `15`. Their grouping by the chart's notation and their projected center positions thus answer different questions. This local observation does not establish that both cells are eligible, occupied or usable rail, or choose a comparator for §8.78.
- The [prior independent geography review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-west-geography-review.md) records §8.78's “west of” language and the §4.1 edge-number conflict, and explains why Air §37.42's same-number distance does not establish a coordinate translation. Those rulebook pages were not re-rendered for this review. Same-row equality is explicit in §32.66's division condition; it should not be transferred by assertion to §8.78 or to the chart's separate `beyond` threshold condition. Cross-sheet near-seam ordering remains unknown without a documented interpretation and registration evidence.
