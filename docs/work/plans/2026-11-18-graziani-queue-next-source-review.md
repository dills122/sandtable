# Alexandria supply capacity bounds: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Independently rechecked the [capacity follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-queue-next-source.md) against the same original scenario and Land scans. **Disposition:** the two conditional count examples and 30-point arithmetic are supported. Correction: this review previously misread the anywhere pool as 25 heavy trucks; the source prints 5. The 40-medium example and its arithmetic remain unchanged; neither example establishes an actual legal arrival or setup.

- **Named setup and major-city limit:** Scenario §60.92(C), printed p. 29 / PDF p. 5, gives Alexandria three regular Supply Units, each 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel. Section 60.41 lists the 2nd New Zealand Division HQ among forces at Alexandria `E3613 &/or 3714`; it does not select the HQ's particular hex. Land §32.14 gives five Supply Units per major-city hex. If the qualifying HQ's major-city hex contains only some or all of those three units, one additional cargo yields at most four. This does not assume a particular split or show that the actual pre-arrival state contains no other units.
- **Optional additional units:** Scenario §59.63(B), printed p. 26 / PDF p. 2, converts eligible second/third-line trucks by the §59.63(A) rule and excludes trucks available at air facilities when Air is abstracted. Section 59.66(C) allows each 30 **other unattached Motorization Points in a hex** to start with a regular Supply Unit. Section 60.43 lists Alexandria's 10 light and 20 medium trucks and the anywhere-on-maps pool including 40 medium and 5 heavy. Under the note's explicit hypotheses that the 20 medium and 40 qualifying anywhere-pool medium points can legally be placed at the same qualifying HQ hex and are eligible other unattached points, `20 + 40 = 60` supports two optional extra Supply Units. Light trucks are not counted. No source passage automatically chooses those placements, extra units or their continuation to the arrival snapshot.
- **Five-plus-one contrast:** If, additionally, all three listed Alexandria units and both optional extras are in that same major-city hex immediately before arrival, the five-unit limit is already filled. A sixth Supply Unit cannot be added under that stated cap. Section 59.65 allows initial overstacking only if unavoidable under scenario distribution; these assumed five do not exceed five, and optional concentration does not itself prove an unavoidable exception. Whether all actual real and dummy counters count for a particular stacking interpretation remains a separate question; the hypothetical five here are regular units.
- **Timing and remaining gates:** The three-plus-one and five-plus-one claims are separate pre-arrival snapshots, not simultaneous facts. Prior movement, Organization choices, changes to unit contents or identity, and any used Game-Turn air allowance must be accounted for. Section 32.37 additionally requires 12 Fuel from an origin Supply Unit before movement, qualifying endpoint and HQ, enemy-proximity and directional checks, and limits each player to one Supply Unit by air per Game-Turn. A conditional count alone cannot satisfy any of them.
