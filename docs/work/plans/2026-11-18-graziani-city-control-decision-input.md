# Commonwealth city-source control: decision input

**Disposition:** The already-cited source does not uniquely resolve RUL-GRA-25's shared-ZOC endpoint question. §10.27 supports calling a hex controlled by both players when both project ZOC; it does not establish that the planning policy's extra friendly-control endpoint requirement uses that definition exclusively. Scenario §60.81 does not expressly require or define friendly control of the Commonwealth city source. Section 30.56 defines control for troop port transfer by last occupation; §62.8 defines it by combat occupation for another scenario. Neither expressly governs this endpoint. No policy is selected here.

## Minimal discriminating choice

For this bounded comparison, hold city membership, snapshot, route, interference, all other eligibility conditions and evidence constant; assume those separately accepted contracts permit the illustrative route even with the hostile ZOC. Both candidates below use **current eligible projected ZOC**, not port-transfer last occupation or the Crusader occupation rule. They differ only in the effect of an opposing ZOC on this endpoint's *control eligibility*:

- **A — friendly ZOC sufficient:** endpoint passes this control condition when a Commonwealth unit projects a qualifying ZOC into it, including when Axis also projects one. This is consistent with §10.27's shared-control wording **if** that general meaning is selected for this policy. It does not assert that the route is unobstructed or that friendly ZOC is the only possible basis for control.
- **B — exclusive ZOC required:** endpoint passes this control condition when Commonwealth projects a qualifying ZOC and Axis does not. This is a stricter project interpretation; §10.27 does not say shared control becomes exclusive or that an opponent's ZOC erases Commonwealth control. Other control bases would need their own explicit treatment.

Synthetic state: one certified Alexandria city hex is the only eligible source candidate; a qualifying Commonwealth ZOC and a qualifying Axis ZOC both reach it, with no occupant. Assume complete evidence establishes both projections, the only-candidate inventory, a valid non-consuming trace and every other necessary condition, including an independently accepted interference rule that permits this route. Under A its endpoint-control condition passes; under B it fails. With complete exclusion of all alternative eligible endpoints and paths, A can supply a positive trace witness while B's subject trace is false. Without that complete negative coverage, B's failure at this candidate alone leaves the subject unknown if another source or route may work. The state is illustrative, not proof of a legal or reachable position, a complete source-city inventory, or a selected route policy. If accepted interference independently blocks the route in the state, both can fail overall and a different state is needed to isolate this control choice.

A neither establishes nor requires an occupant, last occupier, stock or actual shipment. B's additional exclusivity is not a source-proven correction. Neither candidate settles an endpoint with neither side projecting ZOC, historical control after departure, or an occupied endpoint; these require explicit semantics and evidence. A broader last-occupier or occupation-based interpretation could produce other results and is not equivalent to either candidate.

## Evidence and disclosure requirements

Before admission, define eligible projection and incoming-ZOC coverage, candidate city membership, endpoint obstruction and route rules, and three-valued proof on the immutable terminal snapshot. A valid source witness can establish the subject despite an unrelated unknown candidate; a negative needs complete relevant candidate and route-space exclusion. RUL-GRA-07 makes a row no-match when a required conjunct is known false, even if another conjunct is unknown; otherwise an unknown row keeps the overall verdict unresolved.

For privacy, compare two otherwise identical histories with the same authorized observation: one has Commonwealth-only projection at the endpoint, the other also has a hidden Axis projection. Under B the internal control result can differ; under A this particular endpoint result does not differ, though interference or other hidden facts may still affect the route. These are conditional paired histories: first establish that the differing projection can actually remain unobservable to the audience under the accepted fog-of-war model. If it can, verdict availability, timing, freeze, retries, action acceptance or subsequent behavior could reveal it even with witness details redacted. Define authorized disclosure or an observation-independent external protocol before admission; no claim that the hypothetical histories are reachable or actually indistinguishable follows here.
