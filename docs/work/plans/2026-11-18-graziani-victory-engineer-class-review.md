# Review: engineer capability and victory classification

**Disposition:** One material setup-identity error in the [source note](2026-11-18-graziani-victory-engineer-class.md). Its general distinction between a separate engineer counter and an engineer-capable HQ is supported, but the named C4020 tank-attachment candidate is not printed as stated.

## Finding: C4020 and C3920 are distinct

Scenario §60.31 prints **1 Libyan Division `(I)` at C4020** with no `I(L)` tank attachment on that row. The parenthetical `(I)` designates the infantry type; it is not `I(L)`. The next row prints **2 Libyan Division at C3920 with IX(L) attached**, identified there as tank `[T; Tvl/LTC]`. Therefore remove the claim that I(L) is attached to 1 Libyan at C4020; it cannot serve as the note's stated source-supported attachment.

A concrete alternative is **2 Libyan Division HQ at C3920**, whose Axis OA p. 5 row is marked `E` and `D`, with the scenario's IX(L) attachment. Axis OA p. 6 separately identifies IX(L) as a tank battalion and its note assigns it to 2 Libyan at start. If that battalion is genuinely attached and represented by the HQ at the relevant snapshot, §3.31 supports the conditional classification. This is still not proof of an admitted setup, actual terminal occupant or qualifying supply. Alternatively, a distinct, substantiated attached infantry component could be examined for 1 Libyan; do not simply replace I(L) by a guessed identity.

## Other checked points

- Land §23.11 calls separate engineer counters noncombat and without ZOC. September errata §23.11 reverses the original parenthesized-strength stacking condition: those ratings may be used only when **not** stacked with a friendly combat unit, and engineers may enter friendly-occupied enemy-controlled hexes. The source note represents that correction accurately.
- §23.14 says `E` HQs have engineering capability but are otherwise treated as HQs; §3.31 gives an HQ combat status when it represents attached combat units, not because of `E` alone. §16.32's engineer/AA exclusion is for the patrol-survival no-combat-units check. These do not by themselves decide every victory predicate or the separate intrinsic-HQ-weapon question.
- §60.81 names combat units in the Italian strategic and decisive cases. The other rows have their own wording; the note properly avoids importing that qualifier or §60.82's separate Italian Campaign wording universally.
