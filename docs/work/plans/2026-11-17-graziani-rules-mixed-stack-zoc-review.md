# Mixed stacks and garrison ZOC: independent source review

**Disposition:** Confirm the [mixed-stack note](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-mixed-stack-zoc.md)'s principal source readings and its two unresolved §9.16/§10.11 interpretations. A zero home stacking value is not stated to erase raw defense; neither raw strength nor printed stacking alone establishes projection. Qualify §19.41's attachment example as applying specifically to an **assigned, not yet attached** unit, and retain a wording tension between §§9.21 and 19.12 about the set represented by a parent.

## Source check

Visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) PDF/printed pp. 17–19 and 29; [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF pp. 1–3 / printed pp. 25–27; and [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) pp. 1–4. SHA-256 verified: Land `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`; scenario `fa12488e59552d7b0ff0d5abefa891d4b81af0492256bec2d25f8d61ef040a66`; errata `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`.

Land §9.16(a) says garrison units stack freely in their assigned city/village; its Fort Maddalena example assigns zero stacking points there and one elsewhere. Section 9.22 directs organization-size lookup using the **printed** stacking value and §9.4 rather than designation/name. Section 10.11 identifies above-battalion combat units as more than one stacking point and also permits units in a hex with more than one stacking point to exert ZOC, subject to exclusions. These clauses do not explicitly decide whether the home-garrison numerical exemption changes §10.11's aggregate, or whether it applies only to capacity. The proposed `0+1` effective and `1+1` printed interpretations are distinct and reasonable candidates, not source-selected outcomes. Nor does §9.16 say the home exemption sets raw defense to zero.

Section 10.15 independently excludes unit(s) in a hex totaling less than ten **raw** defensive Close Assault Points, irrespective of §10.11, and presumes anti-tank units Up Front for this purpose. Section 11.3 calculates raw strength as TOE times the applicable combat rating; §11.34 totals raw points before division for actual points. Thus a separately eligible `6+4=10` passes this numerical exclusion, while `6+3=9` does not. Which units contribute and whether a particular rating is available remain independently governed; ten alone does not prove ZOC.

Section 19.12 describes a parent counter representing an attached unit, which is co-located and functionally combined; §19.13 permits assignment and attachment to different parents, with the attached parent in the same hex but the assigned parent possibly elsewhere. Section 19.41 specifically treats a unit **assigned but not attached** joining its parent, removes its counter, and says immediate attachment does not affect the parent's stacking value unless the parent was a shell. Avoid extending that case automatically to every unassigned attachment or timing. Section 19.42 separately governs non-assigned attachment. Land §9.21 describes a division as representing units `assigned and attached` to its HQ, while §19.12 describes attached representation more generally; reconcile that wording when defining represented constituents and avoid assuming this source review resolves all parent cases. Section 9.26 defines shell states; §9.28 supplies the size reduction for unit differentiation and rules where size matters.

Scenario §59.2 allows organization of units listed in the same starting hex, within formation and stacking limits; it does not establish the chosen setup or relocate a Libya-only pool. Scenario §60.31 lists Fort Maddalena at C3019 and separately gives `Anywhere in Libya` placements. Country membership, each unit's eligibility, actual presence, target edge and directional §10.21 entry remain separate. No explicit correction of the mixed-stack question was identified in the inspected errata package; later or indirect material may exist.
