# Land-only supply carrier: retreat subject boundary

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Focused follow-up to the [escort/reaction analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-escort-reaction-followup.md) and [reaction-versus-retreat review](2026-11-18-graziani-escort-reaction-source-followup-review.md). The original source supplies useful representation and coupling facts but does not expressly classify a Supply Unit, its assigned Motorization Points, or their combination as the §13 retreating unit. No hierarchy or executable policy is selected.

## Representation and movement evidence

- Land glossary §3.1 defines a unit as a **counter capable of expending Capability Points**. Section 32.12 represents an abstract Supply Unit by a Supply Dump counter; §32.13 gives it no combat capabilities, and §32.14 gives it zero stacking value. These establish a counter and distinguish combat and stacking properties; they do not state an untransported Supply Unit's own CPA or expressly identify it as the §13.1 retreat subject.
- Section 32.32 assigns 30 Motorization Points to transport a real Supply Unit, six for a dummy, and says a Supply Unit without the minimum cannot be moved. Attachment/detachment is allowed only in an OpStage Organization Phase. Section 32.58 assigns a **15 CPA to Motorization Points transporting a Supply Unit**, not explicitly to an independent cargo counter. Section 32.51 treats Motorization Points as Medium Truck Points except as modified in §32.5. Section 8.99 permits a Truck marker to represent unattached Truck Points in a hex; it does not establish that a supply-transporting Motorization allocation must have a separate carrier counter under abstract rules.
- Section 32.33 speaks of a **Supply Unit being transported** by Motorization Points and permits its movement in one of two owner phases, with an express exception to that friendly Movement/Combat stacking restriction for normal non-phasing movement. This is evidence of coupled cargo movement, not express permission to select the cargo or carrier independently in §13, a rule for separating them during retreat, or a waiver of all other conditions. Section 32.18 says a dummy Supply Unit moves in the same fashion as a real one, with fewer Motorization Points, and is removed on discovery; that alone does not decide whether either subject can retreat.
- Section 13.1 permits any unpinned **non-phasing unit** to retreat before assault, except one at cohesion −26 or worse; §13.21 makes this voluntary movement and §13.23/.24 govern distance/CP by adjacency. Neither case assigns a cohesion value to a Supply Unit or its Motorization Points. A no-combat property is not itself the printed no-retreat condition, while “any unit” is not by itself proof that a point allocation with no expressly identified counter fits §3.1's definition.

## What still requires a decision

The text supports at least three distinct implementation questions: (1) whether the transported Supply Dump counter qualifies as the retreating unit using the assigned points' movement characteristics; (2) whether the assigned points themselves form a qualifying moving counter/convoy, carrying the Supply Unit; and (3) whether some other combined representation or restriction applies. The sources inspected do not explicitly answer those identity questions or state what happens to cargo if a carrier is returned by §32.58(B). The real/dummy point minimum and the Organization-only attachment window are evidence against silently treating cargo as an independent free mover or detaching points as an unconditionally available retreat action; the cited cases do not settle every exception or lifecycle.

If a future ruling permits such retreat, adjudication still needs an applicable representation for pinning/cohesion or a source-based treatment where no value exists, weather and terrain, §13.26 ZOC limits and their relationship to §10.26/.29, cargo coupling and the meaning of being **forced** above CPA. Section 32.58(D)'s adjacent CPA 35 threshold expressly concerns reaction, not a self-evident condition on §13 retreat. A counterfactual test varying an actual real versus dummy load, 29/30 or 5/6 points, and unpinned/pinned or missing cohesion data can distinguish policies only once those concepts are defined. No actual eligible carrier, legal path or scenario reachability is asserted.
