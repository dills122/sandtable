# Graziani map: additional public-source search

**Search date:** November 17, 2026. No newly identified, independently
attributable SPI or designer correction or alternate official printing was
found that settles either sheet registration or the high side of
D2228/D2328. This bounded search does not establish that none exists. Keep
[ML-02 and ML-03](2026-11-12-graziani-source-register.md) open, including the
[direction assessment](2026-11-17-graziani-d2228-escarpment-direction-next.md).

This is a proposed successor product, not evidence of a released alternate 1979 printing or original design intent. Availability is as observed on the listing, not a promised release. |

That is a modern implementation of the known correction, not an independently authored publisher direction. No module or protected scan was downloaded; the changelog does not state high side or establish seam identity. |

Neither article was inspected. |

The [September 1979 transcription](https://grognard.com/errata1/cna.html)
repeats the existing escarpment entry without directional wording; it is not
a new correction. [Prior addenda search](2026-11-17-graziani-addenda-search-followup.md)
documents the original packet and other copies. No fresh source above closes
the registration conflict or adds a directional splash to the original map.

Searches combined game title, SPI, map, overlap, reprint, errata, D2228,
D2328, escarpment and magazine terms; checked public indexed listings and
publisher product description. Results depend on search indexing and listing
accuracy. Magazine contents, physical copies, potential print variants and
unindexed publisher correspondence remain unexamined. Useful next evidence:
a dated publisher/designer document with exact issue, page and explicit
registration or high-side statement, or independently sourced original sheet
with identifiable edition and relevant marks, compared against the pinned scan.
A later redraw or geographically plausible contour alone cannot establish SPI
intent. No contact, purchase, protected scan download, map-data change or
runtime change occurred.
