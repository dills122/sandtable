# Independent audit of RUL-GRA-16

**Disposition:** Supported as a bounded, versioned interpretation. No material
source contradiction found in its stated choice. It preserves the principal
conflicts and does not claim an actual production roll or shipment. Admission
still depends on the unresolved gates named in the ruling.

- **Lead time:** Land §20.21, printed/PDF p. 31, calls for Commonwealth
  production arrivals to be determined four game-turns in advance. Both
  §20.78A truck and §20.78B infantry chart notes specify four game-turns.
  §5.2, p. 11, instead says two turns. Selecting four for the two named
  production categories is supported; it is a decision on conflicting text,
  not a publisher correction to §5.2. §20.21 has broader wording, but the
  ruling deliberately leaves other categories open. Do not silently extend
  this policy to scheduled unit arrivals or all replacement categories.
- **Lookup and timing:** Errata §20.72, PDF p. 2, corrects two months to one
  month and selects the month/turn of planning for the production tables.
  §20.78B/C explicitly key their dates to planning. Printed §20.73, p. 32,
  says random infantry and truck points depend on arrival date. Ruling
  expressly preserves this conflict. Truck §20.78A's period headings have no
  separate planning-date definition in its own note, so applying the errata
  to it is a selected interpretation, not independent chart confirmation.
  First-July-phase example in §20.72 and per-turn production chart wording
  do not by themselves settle every planning frequency, month boundary, or
  specific stage. GT1 scenario starts at OpStage 1 (§60.22), after §5.2's
  ordinary Naval Convoy Schedule Phase; no actual GT1 preamble is established.
- **Pre-November scope:** §60.47, scenario printed p. 28, forbids Replacement
  Points arriving before November 1940. §32.52 calls rate-based motorization
  points replacements; §32.51 treats motorization points as medium trucks
  except as modified in §32.5, and §20.74 uses medium trucks as an example
  when discussing replacement points. The selected ban is well grounded,
  while §20.0/.2 and §60.92(A)'s rate wording leave a competing construction;
  retain it as interpretation rather than source certainty. §32.52 separately
  provides for scenario-specified motorization reinforcements. Its separate
  source and whole-unit reinforcement rows cannot be suppressed solely by the
  chosen rate-based replacement ban; neither is automatically converted into
  an approved Land-only motorization receipt.
- **Execution boundaries:** §20.78A directs separate rolls for three truck
  types and caps Alexandria's share at 25% of total trucks produced that
  game-turn; its note distinguishes production from trucks as reinforcements.
  The abstraction's effect on that location cap and individual physical truck
  rolls is not settled by this ruling. A zero receipt, an unrecorded roll and
  an explicit zero outcome are separate states. Stage allocation and privacy
  need their own policy; do not infer a die result from the arrival ban.

## Conditional checks

1. GT2 Sep IV planned production under chosen +4 targets GT6 Oct IV, inside
   the pre-November ban; block the rate-based replacement receipt. The +2
   alternative targets GT4 Oct II, also banned. Neither case requires inventing
   a GT2 plan or roll.
2. GT3 Oct I +4 targets GT7 Nov I. The calendar and nominal target are valid;
   GT7 is outside Graziani's GT1–6 scenario. A hypothetical infantry dice
   total 3 selects three points in §20.78B's GT3–30 column. This does not
   establish that the planning phase was reached, that a roll happened, or
   that a later arrival actually occurs.
3. Give planning and arrival periods different production values; selected
   §20.72 interpretation reads planning period, while a separately versioned
   arrival-date interpretation reads the latter. Test specifically at a chart
   period transition, not by pretending the GT1–6 infantry rows differ: all
   eligible GT3–6 planning turns use the same GT3–30 column.
4. GT5/OpStage 1 7 Indian Brigade has 5 light and 20 medium trucks; GT6/3
   6 NZ Brigade has 25 light, 85 medium and 5 heavy (§4.43a chart PDF p. 3).
   Reject neither whole-unit event solely on §60.47; independently resolve
   the attached-truck conversion and other arrival requirements. A stored
   absent production plan remains absent, while an explicitly rolled zero is
   an observed result.
