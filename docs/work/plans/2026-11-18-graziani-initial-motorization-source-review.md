# Initial motorization chart: source review

**Disposition:** Supports the [conditional medium-truck reading](2026-11-18-graziani-initial-motorization-source.md). The chart, §8.92 and §32.51 provide evidence for one medium-equivalent point carrying one qualifying infantry TOE at CPA 20, subject to actual transport and applicable mode rules. They do not prove an initial allocation or current Giarabub allowance. This is an informed same-scan recheck, not an independent edition or an admitted runtime rule.

- **Chart values:** Common chart §54.2's medium row gives infantry CPA **20** and maximum infantry capacity **1 TOE Strength Point** per Truck Point. Its legend identifies CPA as the allowance while carrying the listed item. The row's gun CPA 15 and supplies CPA 30 use distinct columns; the supplies CPA has the explicit convoy-carrying-supplies footnote. The chart's fuel, breakdown and detailed-supply columns should not automatically be imported into Land-only.
- **Rules interaction:** Land §8.92 allows sufficient trucks to motorize a non-motorized unit, except Meharisti cavalry, and gives a non-motorized unit being transported the appropriate truck CPA. §32.51 treats Motorization Points as medium Truck Points except as modified in its major case. §32.58(A) fixes CPA 15 for Motorization Points **transporting a Supply Unit**, a separate cargo case. These provisions support the stated conditional infantry candidate, not a blanket CPA for every Motorization Point or proof that the detailed chart has no other Land-only qualifications.
- **Giarabub source classification:** Axis OA p. 14 identifies Giarabub Oasis Mtr MG Bn as ID `hh`, TOE `N`, arrival `D`; its p. 4 key says `N` supplies the chart maximum for a non-tank/non-gun unit. Axis §4.46b p. 26 places `hh` under **Infantry Coy-Eq**, with CPA `8+`, close assault 5/7 and maximum TOE 1. This is affirmative evidence for the follow-up's conditional infantry classification, despite the OA's battalion name; it does not alone settle every cargo or formation representation rule. The p. 27 key describes `+` as historical provision of enough trucks and says CPA changes when motorized; it does not name the new CPA or establish the present pool.
- **Allocation:** Scenario §§59.63(A–B) convert medium and heavy first-line trucks hex by hex to attached points and second/third-line trucks to unattached points, with an air-facility exclusion when Air is abstracted; §59.64 allows later redistribution subject to the stated exception and Land attachment rules. §60.33 lists 100 medium and 25 heavy anywhere in Libya, alongside 30 light, but does not assign that general pool to Giarabub. Scenario §60.31's Giarabub row names no first-line truck amount. §32.54's historical-motorization priority requires reconciliation with the actual pools, unit type and setup/timing; it does not by itself prove an individual initial assignment.

The comparison of one point against one qualifying infantry TOE is a capacity and allowance candidate, not a legal-placement or route finding. A non-motorized unit's printed `8+` is not evidence that it currently has sufficient attached transport; absence of a unit-specific source quantity is not proof that no general pool exists. Parent contents, co-location, assignment, any modified capacity, other constraints and a versioned interpretation remain open.
