# Graziani victory shared objectives: source review

**Disposition:** Supports the [result-order follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-victory-result-order-followup.md)'s four **conditional** cross-side row exclusions. Under its stated exclusive same-hex, same-snapshot possession assumption, the four combinations are correct; the printed rules do not independently prove that assumption or a cross-side tie-break. One additional overlap strengthens one listed pair: Italian tactical retains **Fort Maddalena**, which Commonwealth decisive also requires, in addition to their shared Sollum condition. It does not add a fifth row pair.

- **Sollum:** Scenario §60.81 requires Italian tactical to retain possession of Sollum C4021 and Commonwealth tactical and decisive to take and hold Sollum. This gives two incompatible row pairs only if both predicates require exclusive current possession of that same objective. The scenario's §60.5 lists Sollum C4021.
- **Siwa:** Italian decisive requires taking and holding (occupying) both Matruh and Siwa; Commonwealth tactical and decisive require taking and holding Siwa, with tactical printing C0127. Section 60.5 also identifies Siwa C0127. Under the same assumption, this yields two further row pairs. Scenario names, locators, occupation, supply and hold language must remain separate from the assumed formal predicate.
- **Fort Maddalena:** Italian tactical also requires retaining it and Commonwealth decisive requires taking and holding it. Section 60.5 identifies it as C3019. If both refer to that same single objective hex under the same exclusive possession assumption, it is a second conflict for Italian tactical versus Commonwealth decisive; the count remains **four distinct row pairs**, not five. Whether either or both objectives actually meet the relevant predicates remains unproved.
- **Entry versus possession:** Land §8.13 prohibits entering a hex containing an enemy unit while expressly referring to §27.4; it also notes different aircraft movement rules. That is evidence of an ordinary entry restriction, not a complete terminal control definition or a proof that extraordinary coexistence cannot occur. This review did not exhaustively analyze §27.4, aircraft, other exceptions, or the meaning and timing of take, hold and retain.
- **Result composition:** Scenario §60.22 ends Graziani at the conclusion of GT6 OpStage 3. Section 60.81 lists six side/level conditions without an explicit cross-side priority or no-match result; §60.82 is labeled for the distinct Italian Campaign. Land §5.2 orders A's player phases before B's but does not itself specify an earlier-wins scoring policy. The strategic rows use different named objectives; these lists alone neither establish nor exclude simultaneous fulfillment. Supply and all other row predicates remain independent gates.
