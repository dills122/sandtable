# Formation clarification: bounded public-source search

**Result:** No additional authoritative correction located for the 1st CCNN division HQ's initial gun or for a French Motor Marine lifecycle transition. Both questions remain open. Search on November 18, 2026 was bounded; unindexed material, inaccessible periodicals, indirect instructions and other sources may exist.

## Sources and access

- The publicly accessible [original Land Game rules hosted by SPI Wargame Resources](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed pp. 45–47, identify their in-book addenda as design/development corrections included at press time. The host's publisher operation is not established. Web-reader extracted text for the counter addenda, pp. 46–47, lists a correction for the **138th battalion of 2nd CCNN**, concerning its headquarters stripe; it does not identify the 1st CCNN division HQ gun. The reader's whole-document text search found `CCNN` at that entry and no `Marine` match. This is a text/OCR check, not a visual examination of every page or a search for unnamed indirect instructions. Counter stripe correction does not assign a weapon. The [prior CCNN independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-ccnn-independent-review.md) already checked original OA, Land rules and September errata; this check does not represent a new edition.
- [Moves 49 game profile, “The Endless Sand”](https://spigames.net/MovesScans/Moves49/CNAGPM49.pdf), by Thomas G. Pratuch, opened publicly in the web reader. It is a contemporary game profile, not an identified designer or publisher ruling. Reader text search returned no `CCNN` or `marine` matches; imperfect OCR and a keyword check cannot rule out indirect discussion. No formation clarification identified.
- [Moves article index](https://boardgamegeek.com/wiki/page/Moves_Article_Index) appeared in search results, but direct access returned HTTP 403. No contents inspected or claim made about material behind it.
- [Friend or Foe's CNA resource page](https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/) was publicly readable and credits Clay Stone for PDFs with errata applied. Its [integrated Land PDF](https://friendorfoe.com/d/CfNA/CNA%20-%20Rules%20Land.pdf) contains the already-known French company arrival correction. This is a third-party integrated document; no distinct original correction or authority for a lifecycle transition was identified. Its integration is not independent corroboration of the September packet.

Targeted searches combined the full title with formation names, headquarters, gun, OA, addenda, Moves, SPI and periodical errata. Results included player reports and derivative resources, which do not establish an official ruling. The [earlier CCNN search](2026-11-17-graziani-ccnn-additional-source.md) and [Marine transition follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-marine-transition-followup.md), including its later-scenario follow-up, retain their stated evidence and limits. Further useful evidence would be an authenticated publisher/designer correction or directly applicable original reinforcement/withdrawal instruction identifying the affected unit and transition.
