# Matruh attachment limit: bounded planning policy

**Status, 17 November 2026:** Retain RUL-GRA-03's six-additional reading as a **planning selection**, not an official ruling, admitted ruleset, complete setup or runtime authority. Six-total-attached remains a plausible alternative. This selection extends the general attachment erratum to Matruh's exceptional assignees; the Matruh-specific OA sentence can reasonably take precedence instead. Neither source expressly resolves that conflict. [Continuation and independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-continuation-review.md) provide the checked source boundary; [capacity follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-followup.md) supplies further locators and counterexamples.

retains separate questions about whether type restrictions apply to the

## Evidence and policy alternatives

The [Matruh OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 10, prohibits assignments and permits up to six attached units, referring to [chart §19.5](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF p. 6. The chart's Matruh GT 1–12 row gives six, no tank and one gun class. [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1 §4.44b item 2, specifically assigns 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshires to Matruh for campaign and Italian scenarios, despite the OA prohibition. Its p. 2 §19.5 says chart attachments are additional to normally assigned units, including assigned units elsewhere. The general statement and specific Matruh wording leave their interaction open.

| Candidate | Six-unit base allowance | Effect of an assigned unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A: six additional, selected for planning** | Measure qualifying non-assigned attachments against six, subject to chart counting and modifiers. | A unit assigned to Matruh does not consume this additional allowance, whether attached or detached. This applies the errata's general distinction to exceptional Matruh assignees. |
| **B: six total attached** | Measure all qualifying actual attachments against six, subject to chart counting and modifiers. | An assigned unit counts while actually attached, alongside non-assigned attachments; it does not count merely because assigned or elsewhere. This gives precedence to the OA's literal Matruh-specific ceiling. |
| **C: defer** | Make no choice where legality depends on A versus B. | Preserve both readings until source hierarchy and project admission are decided. |

A gives effect to both specific named assignments and the later general clarification, and matches the existing [working interpretation](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-03-matruh-initial-assignment-working-interpretation). B gives special wording greater weight. Neither is proved by the other. Selection A does **not** mean eight units necessarily occupy eight slots, establish any extra roster, or waive other limits. If A/B changes an outcome before policy admission, retain the result as unresolved rather than silently choosing at runtime.

## Assignment, attachment and setup

Assignment and actual attachment are separate relationships. The named erratum supports a scoped exception for those two assignees, not a general right to assign other units to Matruh. It does not specify an independent numerical maximum assignable to Matruh, a shell denominator, the units' locations, or actual attachment. Do not derive that maximum from the six attachment allowance or treat two named assignees as proof that two is the general maximum. Any later reassignment requires its own applicable authority and capacity checks; under A, exemption from Matruh's additional count ceases to follow if the unit is no longer assigned to Matruh, including if it remains attached there.

[Scenario §60.41](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, lists Essex, Durham Light Infantry and South Staffordshires under `Att`; §59.2 defines that as attached, not assigned. Preserve each identity once. Three listed battalions are a roster count, not yet proven three chart units. The assignees' assignment alone establishes neither co-location nor attachment. Once their actual starting locations are independently established, [Land §4.25](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) supplies the co-located default unless specifically displaced; owner-selected initial reorganization under §59.2 requires units listed in the same starting hex and compliance with formation and stacking limits. It does not move a unit across hexes. Later attachment and detachment require their own timing, cost and legality checks.

Section 59.2 separately permits a **listed unit actually exceeding** its applicable formation organization limits to operate normally, including being itself assigned or attached. It bars assigning or attaching new subordinates while the restriction applies and bars replacement of detached subordinates in violation of limits. Establish the listed formation, applicable limit and actual excess before invoking it. This exception does not legalize a new excess created by optional same-hex reorganization or prove that Matruh starts over limit. Supply stacking under §59.65 is separate and only permits initial excess when unavoidable under scenario distribution instructions; it supplies no formation-capacity exception.

## Counting and type gates

The chart defines its unit using full-strength battalion- or company-equivalent counters; three company-equivalents equal one battalion-equivalent. It separately allows division- and brigade-equivalent units two non-shell company-equivalents without charge to the maximum, subject to type restrictions. Matruh's brigade-level OA label and counter's `X` support nominal brigade designation, but do not settle present equivalence, shell denominator, interaction with its special ceiling, or whether the chart key imposes a separate full-strength condition on the modifier. See [company review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-company-review.md). Keep modifier eligibility **unknown** pending a decision; do not grant or permanently deny it by assumption. Resolve strength, equivalence, grouping and nested attachments before computing exact chart cost.

Both A and B retain the row's no-tank and one-gun-class restrictions, company conditions, valid stacking and other applicable rules. A numeric pass alone does not authorize an attachment. A proven independent disqualifier may reject an action; an otherwise proven ordinary-capacity case need not depend on the disputed modifier. Where legality requires unresolved counting or free-company eligibility, defer that action without mutation.

## Distinguishing checks before admission

These are hypothetical full battalion-equivalent, type-legal examples with verified locations, stacking and no nesting; they do not prove available units or reachable play states.

1. Two assignees actually attached plus three non-assigned attachments: A counts three against six; B counts five. Detaching one assignee preserves assignment, leaves A at three and reduces B to four. If an assignee is elsewhere, do not default-attach it.
2. Two assignees attached plus five non-assigned attachments: A counts five and passes the base numerical comparison; B counts seven and fails it, absent a separately applicable exception. Neither outcome establishes overall legality.
3. Six non-assigned attachments plus one detached assignee: A's additional count remains six if the assignee later attaches; B's total would rise to seven. Check actual attachment preconditions and costs independently. A legally reassigned former Matruh assignee attached to Matruh becomes non-assigned for A's count; check the complete transition and both formations' rules.
4. Test an actually listed excess separately from an owner-created excess: permit listed operation under §59.2's stated restrictions, reject newly created excess, and check detachment and replacement against the applicable limit without inventing a permanent freeze after conformity.
5. Compare independently established eligible and ineligible brigade-equivalent states, unknown shell denominator, non-shell versus shell companies, a third company, nested units and type restrictions. Test both interpretations of any strength condition; do not infer a precise cost from counter count alone.

Admission needs a reviewed, versioned source hierarchy and counting contract; established starting locations and owner choices; assignment and shell-maximum decisions; free-company and type handling; and focused setup, legal-action and replay tests, including atomic rejection of unsupported transitions. This document uses existing source reviews; it did not independently re-inspect scans or exhaust later errata. No runtime, setup data or tests changed.
