# Independent review: raider Reaction erratum

**Result:** The cited erratum and original scenario/rules support the note's bounded conclusion. No actual formed raider, spotting attempt or executable Reaction is established. One wording qualification: the erratum expressly states Reaction after any spotting attempt; calling that an **expansion** beyond original §27.36's §27.44 reference is a reasonable comparison, but the original's broad “if necessary” wording does not itself define an exclusive list of triggers. It is not proof that ordinary Reaction restrictions or phasing status are overridden.

- [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 middle column §27.36, prints the any-attempt clarification. [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), pp. 38–39, §§27.12–.13, .2, .32–.44, supports the note's formation conditions, German-only DSA, 50-CP limit, spotting triggers and elimination on a 6. §27.43's “either action” cross-reference includes §27.41's two differently attributed events; the note correctly leaves procedure/timing interpretation open. A failed spotting does not prove an otherwise legal Reaction. Ordinary §§8.51, .53–.55 are on p. 15 and include non-phasing/adjacency and enemy-ZOC limits, so their interaction with the correction remains material.
- The note correctly distinguishes visible placement after being spotted under §27.33 from §27.43's elimination, without inventing a surviving counter. The interaction of those printed statements, including timing of placement and elimination, is itself unresolved; §27.33 also permits placement on a raid. No display or movement consequence should be assumed solely from one clause.
- Scenario PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §§60.22 and 60.41 support GT1 OpStage 1 through GT6 OpStage 3 and 11th Hussars at C3020. A setup reference does not supply maximum Morale obtained through training or a chosen TOE removal. The review did not independently check OA composition, full reinforcement track, other potential recipients or initial deployment beyond those pages.
- Land p. 43 §32.0 applies to Land without Air and Logistics; §32.11 ignores ordinary references to usage/expenditure of ammunition, fuel, stores and water, and scenario p. 28 §60.92 labels this configuration Land Game Only. The source does not explicitly reconcile raiders' §27.35 supply visits, fuel and water consequences with that substitution. The note properly leaves the special interaction open rather than asserting a retained or waived requirement; no special mode text located in this bounded review rescinds §27.36's clarification.
