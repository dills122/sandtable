# FG-01: Land in-book addenda disclosure census

**Scope:** Visually inspected every numbered rules entry and lettered counter entry under **Addenda**, [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF pp. 45–46. This is an inventory of disclosure relevance, including bounded negative readings, not a claim all resulting observations are authorized in every mode. The [coverage matrix](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-coverage-matrix.md) and its subsequent [warship](2026-11-18-graziani-warship-barrage-disclosure-review.md), [record-sheet](2026-11-18-graziani-record-sheets-disclosure-review.md), [minefield](2026-11-18-graziani-minefield-disclosure-source-review.md) and [vehicle-marker](2026-11-18-graziani-vehicle-marker-captor-source-review.md) reviews cover related interactions. The addenda are printed within the retained Land booklet, not a separate newly identified edition.

## Errata and scope

The earlier [base review](2026-11-18-graziani-limited-intelligence-source-inventory-review.md) checks §16.11's patrol eligibility addition. No directly numbered correction to the other listed p. 45 addendum cases was found in this bounded exact-number text search; this does not cover indirect corrections, counter-specific errata, OCR omissions or later editions.
