# Tobruk abstract supply redistribution: source review

**Disposition:** Arithmetic and conditional same-hex Organization Phase example in [candidate](2026-11-18-graziani-axis-supply-source-next.md) check out. One source-scope clarification matters: §32.15 also prints an initial 40 Ammo / 60 Fuel quantity, while the scenario replacement setup prints a different quantity; the cited setup's explicit values should be retained as scenario text, with their relationship to the general initial quantity acknowledged rather than omitted.

- Visually inspected [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, §60.92(B): in Land Game Only, its replacement Axis supply status lists Tobruk (C4807), **3 regular Supply Units**, each **15 Ammo and 50 Fuel**. It is an initial setup, not a later stock observation.
- Visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43, §32.15: it says a Supply Unit **initially contains and may never contain more than** 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel, removes it when **both** are zero, and allows supplies to be rearranged without cost among Supply Units occupying the **same hex during the Organization Phase**. Section 32.41 says the number of starting Supply Units is scenario specified, while §60.92(B) explicitly replaces §60.34's initial supply status. These are strong scenario-specific grounds for using the printed 15/50 setup, but §32.15's differently worded initial quantity is an additional source interaction; the rearrangement example is conditional on accepting the specific setup values, not proof of a universal initial 40/60 rule or an independent precedence instruction.
- Three units at 15/50 total **45 Ammo and 150 Fuel**. `(40,60)`, `(5,60)`, `(0,30)` conserves both resources, stays within each stated cap, and leaves every unit with at least one nonzero amount. Two units with 40 Ammo would require 80, exceeding the starting total by **35**. The rules' permission is same-hex and Organization Phase; the example assumes eligible friendly units remain together with no intervening changes and does not establish a legal setup rewrite, reachable state or actual action.
