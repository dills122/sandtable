# Independent review: victory convoy direction

**Disposition:** The candidate accurately distinguishes the scenario's directional phrases from actual physical movement and from Land §32.16's local supply draw. Neither a delivered convoy nor a particular passability direction is specified as a complete victory algorithm. The additional source boundary below matters if a future policy chooses to model a physical Land-only convoy.

| Issue | Source check |
| --- | --- |
| Italian strategic | Scenario §60.81, printed p. 28, requires a combat unit at Alexandria or Cairo that can be supplied by convoy **from any hex on Game-Map D**. This is source-to-objective language; it does not state that an actual delivery, particular cargo or current vehicle must already exist. Interpret the quantifier and unit binding separately rather than assuming every D hex qualifies or that any one candidate alone establishes failure. |
| Italian decisive and tactical | The decisive condition describes Mersa Matruh and Siwa and a combat unit supplied by convoy **from Tobruk**. Tactical takes Sidi Birani and retains Sollum, Fort Maddelena and Giarabub, with supply as above. A binding and source/route policy still needs to say what is proved at each place. |
| Commonwealth | Tactical requires taking and holding Sollum, Halfaya Pass and Siwa while **tracing supply by truck convoy back to Alexandria or Cairo**; decisive and strategic refer to supply as above. This supplies textual evidence for an objective-to-source trace, but does not expressly say whether to apply directed movement restrictions in trace direction or in prospective delivery direction. |
| Physical movement | §8.98 says attached trucks move with their units and unattached trucks in the Truck Convoy Phase, with specified same-origin/destination/path grouping. §8.33 requires connected road/track hexes through the feature's crossing to obtain the benefit; §8.42 distinguishes up and down escarpment movement. These govern actual movement under their own applicability conditions, not an explicit declaration that §60.81 requires a performed convoy event. |
| Marble Arch | §8.19 says no Commonwealth **Land unit may be moved** west of Marble Arch (A2109), for any reason. The candidate correctly does not silently convert this actual-movement rule to a universal prohibition on every abstract trace. Conversely, the rule cannot be ignored for an actual qualifying Commonwealth land-unit movement. Whether abstract motorization or a hypothetical witness inherits it needs separate source and mode review. |
| Local draw | §32.16 uses a friendly Supply Unit within half the combat unit's CPA, with a medium-truck trace except for non-motorized infantry and with impassable-terrain and specified hostile-ZOC restrictions. The case does not say this is the victory convoy test or explicitly impose its range on §60.81. §32.51's medium-truck treatment for Motorization Points does not alone resolve the connection. |

**Additional physical-transport gate:** In Land-only rules §32.33 says a Supply Unit transported by Motorization Points may move in the owner's Movement/Combat Phase **or** Truck Convoy Phase, not both; to move in Movement/Combat it must begin and remain stacked with a friendly combat unit. This is relevant if a chosen victory policy requires an actual transported **Supply Unit**, but neither §32.33 nor §60.81 expressly equates that operation with a hypothetical victory trace. It does not establish that every convoy must carry a Supply Unit or that a route witness must include an actual shipment. §32.58 also gives Supply Unit-carrying motorization a CPA of 15; do not impose that range on a victory trace without a separately reviewed interpretation.

Directed-edge tests in the candidate properly distinguish source-to-objective delivery from objective-to-source trace and require complete coverage before a negative conclusion. Whether a particular terrain rule actually creates the synthetic one-way edge, and whether an eligible convoy must exist in the real authoritative state, remain separate proofs. A real source witness and a reported victory result must not expose hidden opponent units or paths.
