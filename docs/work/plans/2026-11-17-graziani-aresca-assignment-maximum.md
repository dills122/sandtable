# Aresca assignment maximum

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Finding:** The normal Italian formation chart gives Aresca's matching `(3)` tank-regiment type **five tank-battalion assignment slots**. Five is the supported §9.26 denominator under that chart, subject to any applicable singular-structure exception; four OA-listed battalions do **not** establish a four-slot maximum. No explicit Aresca-specific four-slot exception appears in the inspected OA and scenario passages. This is a bounded reading, not an official ruling or an exhaustive search of all later corrections.

## Evidence

- [Axis charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 31, §19.32: the `(3)` tank-regiment row has **five separate tank-battalion symbols**. Its historical note says four light and one medium. The adjacent `(2)` tank-regiment row has three slots; it is a different printed type. [Counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 1, shows Aresca as a tank regiment `III` with parenthesized `(3)`; [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 6, identifies Aresca (1st) Regiment HQ. This matches the five-slot row. The chart's historical light/medium description is not itself proof of a binding type restriction beyond its displayed tank-battalion symbols.
- [Land rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 29, §§19.2–19.3, distinguish assignment limits from attachments. Section 19.3 says chart shows maximum assignment of each type for normal formations, while OA-noted singular structures are limited by those structures instead. OA p. 4's key explains indentation as parent assignment; p. 6 lists XXI(L), LXII(L), LXIII(L), I(M) under Aresca. Its note `a` describes Libyan Tank Command operating mostly on paper with battalions attached elsewhere; note `b` identifies actual initial attachments elsewhere. Neither expressly names Aresca's four-item list a singular four-slot limit. The list is evidence of four historical assignments, not a statement excluding a vacant fifth slot. Section 19.21 permits an eligible independent, currently attached unit to be assigned into an available slot; present availability and CP/timing are separate from theoretical capacity.
- Land p. 30 §19.62 says an HQ that lost **all** assigned battalions may be assigned new battalions up to the number listed on the OA while it remains a cadre. This is a specific all-lost rebuilding condition and must not be silently generalized into an initial four-slot cap. Its relationship to the five-slot chart in that future state is a separate interpretive question; this finding does not assert a five-battalion rebuild under §19.62.
- [Scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §60.31 places Aresca at C3919 with all except XXI detached, and places the other three under their respective host divisions. This changes attachment, not the OA assignment or explicitly the chart maximum. PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, §59.2 allows the owner to organize same-hex units at setup within formation and stacking limits; therefore baseline XXI attachment is not necessarily the only player-selected initial disposition. The scenario's Autoblinda exception concerns individual TOE points attached ignoring composition restrictions, not an added assigned tank battalion or an explicit change to the five-slot denominator. Exceeding-organization setup provisions address a listed excess; no such Aresca excess is identified here.
- Land p. 17 §9.26 defines a brigade shell by fewer than two thirds of the battalions **that may be assigned** being attached; §9.27 excludes an attached shell subunit from the full-parent count. Under the five-slot reading, at least **four qualifying battalions** are needed for a full brigade, and baseline one attached is **1/5**, a shell. If XXI is detached by a legal setup choice, there are zero attached; assess empty-HQ consequences separately. Four as denominator would also make one attached a shell, but would erroneously make three qualifying attachments sufficient for full status.
