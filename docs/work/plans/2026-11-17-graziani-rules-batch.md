# Graziani Land-only rules: prioritized evidence ledger

**Status: November 17, 2026.** Consolidation and cross-check of linked source
research, not an independent scan reading, official errata, implementation
approval, or exhaustive six-turn reachability audit. Priority indicates the
next decision or proof dependency, not demonstrated reachability. Planning
rulings below remain bounded by their own admission gates. No runtime or
readiness change follows from this ledger.

## P0 — terminal adjudication and safe disclosure

- **Victory reference — narrowed.** Scenario §60.22 ends Graziani after GT6
  OpStage 3 and references nonexistent §60.71; named §60.81 matches Graziani,
  and the parallel Italian Campaign references exhibit the same offset.
  [RUL-GRA-02](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-02-victory-reference)
  selects §60.81 for planning. No explicit correction was found in the
  inspected September errata. [Source and review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-victory-reference.md).
- **Route and verdict — unresolved.** §60.81 names objectives and supply,
  but does not supply a complete Land-only route algorithm, endpoint,
  direction, ZOC, source-control, outcome-precedence or terminal-publication
  contract. [RUL-GRA-07](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-07-bounded-facility-and-victory-planning-policies)
  selects result composition for planning; [RUL-GRA-22](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-22-italian-strategic-city-witness-bounded-planning)
  selects one eligible city hex and same holder for the Italian strategic
  witness. Neither admits the route. One certified witness can prove an
  existential condition; one blocked path cannot prove it false while an
  alternative remains unknown. Bind the unit, subject hex, history, source
  and path to one witness and snapshot. Immediate verdict versus pending can
  expose hidden route knowledge even with redacted reasons. Resolve authorized
  audiences and timing along with map coverage and terminal order before
  admission. [Policy review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-policy-review.md),
  [city review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-city-hex-scope-review.md).

## P1 — recurring supply and movement decisions

- **Supply calendar — narrowed.** GT1 is September III; GT2 September IV;
  GT3–6 October I–IV. Land §32.43 plans Axis supply two turns ahead and
  Commonwealth four; §32.44 keys Axis by planning month, §32.45 Commonwealth
  by arrival period. Conditional 1940 Axis column B gives `0,1,1,2,2,3`,
  Commonwealth period I `1,1,2,2,3,3`, for die results 1–6. GT1's
  starting-turn exception and pregame plans do not establish actual rolls or
  deliveries. GT1 startup, pregame initialization, entitlement carryover and
  whether to plan beyond GT6 remain open. One ordinary Commonwealth period-I
  result alone cannot satisfy the “more than three received” Alexandria option;
  other receipts or carryover could. [Matrix and review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-schedule-next.md).
- **Convoy losses — partial resolution.** Land §32.65's 20%-or-greater
  branch includes 25%; its explicit branches do not specify 5%. The Air
  abstract-logistics procedure replaces Land §32.6 in that mode and has its
  own 5% and 25% gaps; do not import it as a Land correction. Ordered d66
  `56` is within the chart's 21–40 bomb-point 5% range; die identities are
  distinguished, not sorted. This lookup is not a legal six-turn witness or
  a loss rule. Resolve 5% grouping, allocation, rounding, threshold direction,
  actual qualifying divisions and convoy snapshot separately. [Mode comparison](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-mode-rounding.md),
  [dice](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-sequential-dice.md),
  [reachability review](2026-11-17-graziani-five-percent-reachability-review.md).
- **Rail — distinct rules.** §8.73 bars rail travel in/into enemy ZOC and
  enemy-occupied hexes; §10.26 negates enemy ZOC effects for movement when a
  friendly combat unit is present. Their precedence and self-occupancy are
  unresolved. Even an admitted movement interpretation cannot authorize
  construction in enemy-controlled or enemy-occupied rail hexes, prohibited
  separately by §24.65. Rail connectivity, stage-wide CP/reorganization limits,
  pickup/dropoff and source-side visibility remain separate. [Independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-rail-zoc-review.md).
  [RUL-GRA-14](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-14-commonwealth-rail-supply-capacity-planning-interpretation)
  addresses bounded capacity for planning, not these route questions.
- **Hot weather fuel — unresolved.** §29.34 specifies a 5% reduction to
  fuel and water levels with stated exceptions; §32.0/.11 replace supply
  procedures in Land-only play. Their interaction with abstract supply and
  fractional treatment is not settled. If applicable, 50 fuel yields 2.5
  exact loss, not a source-selected integer result. A block conditional on a
  private stock or fraction can itself reveal information; select effect,
  representation, rounding, replay and audience policy together.
  [Independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-hot-fuel-followup-review.md).
- **Motorization and initial supplies — planning choices, not full setup.**
  [RUL-GRA-08](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-08-abstract-supply-stacking-bounded-planning-interpretation)
  and [RUL-GRA-09](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-09-monthly-motorization-and-dummy-supply-bounded-planning)
  select bounded stacking, dummy and previous-month interpretations; September
  chart dash, future entitlement, placement and privacy remain open. Initial
  five Matruh supply units and §59.65's unavoidable-overstack clause require
  their own case; neither establishes a general exception. Source-audited
  non-air truck subtotals of Axis 610 and Commonwealth 262 are not complete
  setup or placement certificates. [Queue detail](2026-11-17-graziani-remaining-source-research.md#source-facts-and-decisions-still-needed).

## P2 — actions and lifecycle

- **Supply destruction — bounded choice, timing open.**
  [RUL-GRA-21](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-21-land-only-supply-destruction-cost-bounded-planning)
  selects §32.17 half-CPA cost for Land-only planning; §32.17 limits attempts
  per supply unit per sub-segment. §§8.22/.25 permit repeated combat without
  movement, but do not identify a new §32.17 sub-segment. Define boundary,
  allocation, authority, dummies and replay; a command retry does not itself
  prove another opportunity. [Cycle review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-supply-destruction-cycle-review.md).
- **Patrol — bounded choice, event completeness open.** Phase sequence places
  player A's patrol before B's later movement/combat; §16.0, §16.21 and the
  phase summary use different formulations. [RUL-GRA-19](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-19-patrol-history-bounded-planning-interpretation)
  selects a bounded history interpretation for planning. It still needs causal
  event completeness, expenditure and privacy gates, including unknown prior
  history and later enemy combat. [Timing review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-patrol-timing-review.md),
  [final policy review](2026-11-17-graziani-patrol-policy-final-review.md).
- **City fortifications — contradiction persists.** Scenario §60.7's no
  fortifications wording does not explicitly distinguish built-in city levels;
  Land §§24.41, 25.12 distinguish those levels from constructed counters.
  Retention at setup is an interpretation. Suppressed levels are not necessarily
  destroyed, and §24.45's rebuilding of levels destroyed by specified attacks
  does not automatically restore them. Decide source precedence and lifecycle
  separately. [Independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-city-fortifications-review.md).
- **Tobruk and port — separate conflicts.** Initial port efficiency 2 of 5 is
  the reviewed [RUL-GRA-10](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-10-tobruk-initial-port-efficiency-planning-interpretation)
  planning choice amid scenario/chart tension; throughput, incoming personnel
  and tonnage precedence are separate. Repair facility control, destruction,
  rebuilding and vehicle repair also do not imply one another. [Port follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-port-followup-review.md),
  [facility lifecycle review](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-facility-lifecycle-policy-review.md).

## Cross-packet work order

1. Preserve unknown separately from false or zero: missing supply plans,
   incomplete route coverage, unverified setup and undiscovered combat history
   cannot establish negative results. An actual table result of zero is distinct.
2. Specify one versioned terminal and audience contract before admitting victory,
   hot-fuel or patrol behavior whose pending state depends on private information.
   This is a shared privacy dependency, not evidence that the rules have identical
   timing or disclosure requirements.
3. Reconcile stage boundaries and durable identities for planning, convoy,
   destruction and patrol independently; do not infer a common retry boundary
   merely because each records an Operations Stage.
4. After policies and source coverage are accepted, add focused boundary,
   replay and side-safe paired-history tests. Test vectors in linked reviews are
   proposals, not test results. No tests or fresh source scans were run for this
   consolidation; linked independent reviews generally use the same editions,
   and source hashes do not confer redistribution rights.
