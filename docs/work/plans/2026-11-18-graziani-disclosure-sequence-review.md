# Land sequence disclosure: independent source review

**Disposition:** No material actor, trigger or audience error found in the [sequence census](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-sequence.md). Its source-backed statements are separated from physical markers, unspecified audiences, later case exceptions and actual stage state.

- Land §5.2, pp. 11–12, explicitly applies to Land without the Air and Logistics games. Its preamble has the Axis plan convoy cargo/routes and replacement arrivals, Commonwealth determine and plan replacement arrivals, and both players plan tactical shipping; it does not expressly announce every plan or prescribe a complete secrecy protocol. Other cases, including §20.0 replacement-die secrecy and §32.63 arrival-lane announcement, have their own scope.
- The initiative holder **states** whether to be A or B each Operations Stage; §7.14 describes each side's die and rating comparison, with ties rolled again. Scenario §60.6 fixes the Italian holder for GT1 before normal later determination. Neither source proves an actual later die or restricts every observation to one invented audience.
- Organization and training entries direct completion, notes and marker changes, with the three segments in any desired order; Naval Convoy Arrival distinguishes scheduled from **actually arriving** items. Reserve and Breakdown place markers. None of those outline entries alone declares every associated sheet, project item or vehicle-marker content public. Specific §3.62 and other cases remain independent.
- Both players secretly plot barrage before executing it and secretly assign TOE Strength Points to Anti-Armor or Close Assault. Player A decides probes and may withhold that decision at assignment; A announces which assaults were probes after each Close Assault is resolved. This is consistent with §15.94's delayed-announcement permission; §12.0's later target/Actual Barrage Points statement and §12.23 target response are distinct from the secret planning. Marker placement does not disclose a complete formation or casualty ledger.
- Fleet Assignment/Repair and §32.73's secret attack plot name separate steps, and the outline does not locate its Tactical Naval Movement Segment. The census correctly labels the selected [RUL-GRA-15](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-15-abstract-fleet-attack-order-planning-interpretation) as planning interpretation. B then performs F–L with role references exchanged; later stages repeat the first-stage facets. This does not mean a new initiative contest each stage or tie a role to a fixed side.
