# Matruh initial supply arithmetic: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Independently checked the [Matruh follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-matruh-supply-followup.md) against the same original scans. **Disposition:** quantities, conditional debit and full-counter arithmetic agree. The note correctly distinguishes printed initial same-hex placement from later co-location, balance and timing assumptions.

- Scenario §60.92(C), printed p. 29 / PDF p. 5, lists **five regular Supply Units at Mersa Matruh D3714**, each 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel. Those five share the named initial hex; this is explicit placement, not a derived co-location. Before any stock change they total 200 Ammo and 300 Fuel. The list alone does not exclude extra counters, other inventory or later changes.
- Land §32.15, printed/PDF p. 43, gives the same per-unit initial/maxima, permits no-cost rearrangement among units occupying the same hex during Organization, and removes a unit when **both** balances reach zero. Five initially full listed counters have no headroom. The scenario's five-counter placement does not by itself settle the separately noted stacking interpretation or later legal movement and capacity.
- Section 32.37 requires 12 Fuel before transport from **any Supply Unit in the origin hex**. Assuming a distinct initially full payer and cargo, the payer has 40 Ammo/48 Fuel, the cargo 40/60, and the original five total 200 Ammo/288 Fuel after the debit; no listed unit has both contents at zero. One other still-full counter can supply 12 Fuel to refill the payer under a separately permitted same-hex rearrangement, leaving that donor at 48 Fuel and preserving the 288 total. This calculation does not decide whether the specified interleaving between payment and transport is permitted, whether cargo can pay itself, or whether several sources can combine payment.
- Section 32.15 requires co-location **at rearrangement time**. Section 32.37 places abstract-air transport in an Organization Phase and bars the owner from moving the transported unit **and its supplies** that OpStage, while allowing §32.16 draw. These clauses do not establish that a payment, rearrangement or transport actually occurred. The restriction and applicable source/destination, enemy, HQ, direction and once-per-Game-Turn gates must be checked independently; a stock arithmetic witness is not a legal shipment witness.
