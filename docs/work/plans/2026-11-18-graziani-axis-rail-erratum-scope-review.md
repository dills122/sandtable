# Axis rail erratum scope: source review

**Disposition:** The [scope note](2026-11-18-graziani-axis-rail-erratum-scope.md) accurately preserves the checked actor, one-direction wording and unresolved detailed-versus-abstract and timing boundaries. One useful specificity is missing from its abbreviated §54.43 description: the 250 Stores and 100 Fuel per increment must be imported to Africa **and brought to a controlled operative rail hex**. Do not treat that stock requirement as satisfied merely by acquiring generic supplies. This is a detailed-rule condition, not proof it survives Land-only abstraction.

Visually inspected original [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) PDF/printed pp. 12, 15 and 43; [Air/Logistics rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf) PDF p. 27 / printed p. 23; and [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) p. 1. Local SHA-256 values checked before review: Land `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`, Air `9e11678e26269525c25fd276a760e827a7cc738b987b39e89fe14295a7164cf4`, errata `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`. Informed same-scan review, not independent-edition corroboration.

- §5.2 puts the Commonwealth Rail Movement Phase in player A's block when A is Commonwealth, then repeats F–L for B with roles exchanged; later Operations Stages repeat the same facets. It does not expressly name an Axis rail phase. §8.72 states one stack in each direction once per Operations Stage for the Commonwealth.
- Errata §8.71 directs Axis rail use to §54.4; §8.73 forbids rail-traveling units to **earn** reorganization points in that stage. Neither inspected erratum explicitly creates a Land-only Axis supply-unit allowance or says the restriction prohibits spending already earned points.
- §54.4 excludes the Barce line east of Benghazi and conditions Axis use of the Commonwealth coastal line on control and stock. §54.41 sets five contiguous controlled hexes and defines control by the last player's land combat unit passing through. §54.43 gives 300 tons in any one direction during an Operations Stage per specified stock increment, but also says rail movement occurs in the “Convoy Stage.” It does not equate that term to the player-block phase. §54.44 sets 900 tons of active stock equivalent per stacking point; §54.46 extends Commonwealth movement/use rules to Axis. The note correctly does not convert the tonnage into an abstract allowance.
- §32.0 applies when both Air and Logistics are omitted. §32.34 names Commonwealth supply units, up to two real and/or dummy as total load in any one direction for an Operations Stage, with normal rail rules applying. It does not name Axis. The source note's statement that this text alone does not authorize two opposite-direction supply loads is a bounded reading, not a complete resolution of the interaction with §8.72 or other traffic.

No scenario reachability, usable route, control, actual stock, rail privacy or complete rule hierarchy was established by this review. Scenario pages, further errata and runtime behavior were not independently audited here. No code/data edits or gameplay tests.
