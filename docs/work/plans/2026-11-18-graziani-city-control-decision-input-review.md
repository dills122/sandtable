# City-source control decision input: review

**Disposition:** The shared-ZOC minimal pair discriminates the proposed endpoint predicates under its stated route and evidence assumptions. It does not select a policy or demonstrate a reachable terminal position. Preserve its distinction between endpoint failure, a false trace and an unknown trace.

Visually checked [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 18 §§10.21, 10.26–.27, and [scenario §60.81](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28. Section 10.27 expressly describes a hex in opposing ZOCs as controlled by both; §10.21 restricts projection, and §10.26 negates hostile-ZOC effects **for movement** when a friendly combat unit is present. The scenario's Commonwealth victory rows name supply back to Alexandria or Cairo but do not expressly define friendly control of the source. Thus shared ZOC supports candidate A if that general meaning is adopted for the additional planning-policy condition; exclusive candidate B is a distinct stricter choice, not a printed consequence of §10.27. Neither statement proves all possible control bases.

With one certified candidate, both qualifying projections, complete coverage and an independently valid route that permits the hostile ZOC, A passes the endpoint test while B fails it. B's candidate failure makes the whole subject false only if every alternative source and path has been excluded and other composition conditions are met; otherwise unknown remains possible. Route interference is a genuine independent gate: if a chosen interference rule blocks the shared-ZOC endpoint, the two control predicates need not produce different overall trace results. Occupancy, past control and the no-ZOC state remain unadmitted rather than defaulting to either candidate.

The paired-history privacy test is properly conditional. Section 10.16 on the same Land page requires disclosure of a particular enemy ZOC when a unit begins or enters the relevant hex during a Movement Segment; it does not establish that every endpoint projection is observable at terminal evaluation. Conversely, it does not prove these hypothetical histories indistinguishable: accepted fog, movement history and any authorized terminal disclosure must decide that first. Compare verdict availability and behavior as well as witness text where histories are observationally equivalent.
