# Graziani abstract supply: capture and disclosure boundary

**Finding:** Land §32.13 requires capture and immediate, free use of a Supply Unit's supplies when an enemy combat unit enters its hex. Section 32.18 removes a dummy on discovery. Neither passage specifies a capture disclosure message, full stock inspection, discovery trigger, audience, or later visibility of the result. A captor must be able to perform whatever immediate use an accepted ruling requires; that practical requirement does not establish that every current or historical balance becomes visible. Conversely, treating the result as necessarily invisible would invent a privacy rule and could prevent the stated use.

## Source and limits

Visually checked [original Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 18 and 43, SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`. This is the same edition and scan as the linked work, not an independent edition or exhaustive errata search.

- §32.12 represents an abstract Supply Unit by a Supply Dump counter. §32.15 gives initial and maximum 40 Ammo and 60 Fuel Points, tracks expenditure, and removes the unit when both reach zero. Those limits do not reveal current stock. §32.2 requires players to track points; it does not say opponents can inspect logs.
- §32.13 states capture on **enemy combat-unit entry** and immediate/free use of supplies. It does not quantify use, identify beneficiaries, decide depletion or durable ownership, or prescribe information shown to either player. The [capture use analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-captured-supply-use.md) and [independent check](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-independent.md) leave these accounting questions open. General §8.13's enemy-occupied entry restriction and its relationship with §32.13 also need a ruling.
- §32.18 permits dummy Supply Units to confuse opponents, moves them as real except for lower motorization requirements, and removes them upon discovery. It does not say how discovery occurs, that entry or ZOC capture necessarily discovers a dummy, or what exact event reports reveal. Removal after an accepted discovery is a rule consequence; it is not evidence that every observer receives a real/dummy field. §32.32's differing motorization requirements likewise do not alone define public visibility of assignments.
- §10.29 provides a separate conditional capture for a qualifying non-combat unit alone in an enemy ZOC during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase with no strength of any type. Supply-unit eligibility, “alone,” ordering and whether §32.13's entry-specific use follows remain unresolved; adjacency alone proves neither capture nor discovery. See [ZOC follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-zoc-capture-followup.md).
- §10.16 requires the non-phasing player to inform the phasing player whether a particular unit exerts a ZOC when it could exert one into a hex and the phasing unit begins a Movement Segment there or moves into it. That conditional answer concerns ZOC, not a general disclosure of supply identity, dummy status or stock. It does not automatically extend to every phase or audience.

## Proposed implementation gates, not printed rules

1. Before enabling capture, select and version entry legality, trigger, phase and order; determine real/dummy discovery, disposition, accounting and applicable ZOC case independently. No automatic transfer of entry-use wording to ZOC capture. An unknown prerequisite stays unknown; do not manufacture a capture, discovery, zero stock or absence of a counter.
2. Specify observations for each role and moment: owner before and after; captor at discovery, capture and immediate-use decision; other non-owner or spectator where supported; and replay/history. Identify the evidence authorizing each field, including any project-selected policy where source is silent. Counter location or a visible removal may itself convey information; do not promise secrecy that the chosen observable procedure cannot maintain.
3. Define the minimum actionable information and choices needed for the **selected** immediate-use mechanism, including timing, eligible beneficiaries, permitted amounts and resulting balances. Reveal those to the captor as required by that mechanism; source does not itself establish a blanket current-stock reveal, nor does it forbid one. Separate a real unit's capture/use from a dummy's discovery/removal, with an explicit outcome when discovery timing is unresolved.
4. Until disclosure and use are specified, hold the relevant public action boundary unavailable in a way independent of hidden real/dummy state. A generic error alone is insufficient if action lists, acceptance, events, timing, later state or replay distinguish outcomes. This is a conservative project gate, not a claim that the printed rules require indistinguishability after a source-authorized discovery or capture.
5. Test both equal authorized histories with different hidden states **before** the selected disclosure and expected differences **after** it. Cover real and dummy, legal entry versus adjacency, conditional §10.29 case, §10.16's bounded ZOC answer, partial/zero balances, immediate-use choices, removal and duplicate prevention, each audience, and fresh replay. Include absence of unauthorized exact balances or historical facts where the chosen policy does not disclose them; do not suppress the outcome or information that the selected, source-consistent capture/use procedure requires.

No runtime or data change; these gates do not select an interpretation or establish playable supply capture.
