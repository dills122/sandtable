# VI-01: Sidi Birani / Sidi Barrani source identity

**Result:** Original scenario, location summary and map provide strong same-edition evidence for treating the Graziani Italian tactical objective printed as **“Sidi Birani”** as the place **Sidi Barrani at C4131**. This is an evidence-backed identity interpretation, not an explicit printed correction or a coordinate attached to “Sidi Birani” in the victory sentence. No victory predicate, map seam, route or runtime identity is admitted here.

## Source cross-check

I visually checked [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, [Air/Logistics booklet](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf) PDF p. 26, and [Map C](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf) PDF p. 3. The existing [victory matrix](2026-11-12-graziani-victory-matrix.md) leaves normalization a candidate; [C4131 six-side review](2026-11-17-graziani-c4131-six-sides-review.md) independently reads the same map scan. This comparison focuses on the named objective and locator, not road or coastal crossing details.

| Record | Printed evidence | What it establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scenario §60.81, Italian tactical victory | “Sidi Birani”; the sentence gives no coordinate for that name. | Exact victory-source spelling and objective wording. It does not expressly say Birani is an alias or identify C4131. |
| Scenario §60.5, Air Landing Strips | “Sidi Barrani” `C4131`. | Same scenario group separately places a similarly named landing strip at C4131; a facility listing is not itself a victory-location definition. |
| Scenario §60.44, initial supply | “Sidi Baranni” `C4131`. | A second spelling and same coordinate in the scenario's detailed-supply entry; its placement does not prove that detailed supply survives Land-only replacement. |
| Air/Logistics, PDF p. 26, Summary of Important Locations | “Sidi Barrani” `C4131`, marked in the Victory Condition column. | Independently printed location-summary record supports the victory association and coordinate, though it does not expressly quote §60.81 or identify “Birani” as an alias. |
| Map C, PDF p. 3 | Coastal blue settlement dot within the cell read as `4131`, alongside “Sidi Barrani.” The map label overlaps the printed hex numeral; adjacent `4130`, `4132`, `4030` and `4031`, the location summary and [prior review](2026-11-17-graziani-c4131-six-sides-review.md) support the cell reading. | The settlement and locator align with the summary. This is a local same-sheet observation; it does not establish all boundary features or legal access. |

The matching location, spelling similarity and summary's victory mark strongly support the proposed association. They do not prove which spelling is an authorial typo, identify a distinct official correction, or prove that the victory sentence's missing coordinate was expressly intended as C4131. Preserve the source forms in provenance and use a reviewed, versioned alias/identity choice before freezing content. Separate take/hold, occupation, supply, target timing and legal reachability remain open. No OA claim is needed to establish the map-summary comparison; the OA was not searched for alternative place names in this check.
