## Gates that remain

1. **Identity and topology:** establish actual sheet-edge registration and distinguish same physical hex, adjacent hex and disjoint hex; reconcile Land §4.1's `01xx`/`39xx` wording against printed edges and account for partial cells and stagger. A 33-distance observation or matching model cannot by itself add an edge, alias, or crossing.
2. **Feature and authority:** inspect each proposed reachable side, terrain and relevant corrections, including overprint and both directed uses. Route, road/rail continuity, ZOC and control need their own accepted source facts and rule contracts; a shortest geometric or air distance does not prove traversability.
3. **Source discrepancies and scope:** preserve the printed matrix and worked-example terms separately, including 24 matrix violations and the local zero issue. Seek a provenance-identified official correction or independently identified edition where useful; select any interpretive hierarchy explicitly. An outside source or matching hash is not automatically independent authority or a rights grant.
4. **Proof and admission:** identify candidate-specific reachable footprint, full continuation and incoming-ZOC coverage; retain unsupported boundaries as unknown rather than clear or impassable. Review versioned identities, both directions, privacy, replay and affected consumers before wider admission. No new readiness or runtime conclusion follows from the added distance evidence.
