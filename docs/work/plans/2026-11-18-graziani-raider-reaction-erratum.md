# Desert raider Reaction after spotting: Graziani source boundary

**Finding:** September errata §27.36 says Desert Raiders may use Reaction after **any Spotting attempt**. Original §27.36 already grants Reaction if necessary and references §27.44; the [review](2026-11-18-graziani-raider-reaction-erratum-review.md) notes the original does not expressly make that reference an exclusive trigger, so the size of any expansion is uncertain. Neither passage establishes a concrete legal raider or Reaction during Graziani's six turns. In particular, a successful §27.43 spotting eliminates the raider; the erratum does not expressly say an eliminated raider survives or moves. Section 27.33's spotted-placement wording creates a separate timing question. Preserve these tensions rather than treating every attempt as an executable move.

- **Formation and scenario:** §60.22 runs GT1/OpStage 1 through GT6/OpStage 3. Section 60.41 lists 11th Hussars (`R`) at C3020; prior [source review](2026-11-17-graziani-fort-maddalena-unit-zoc.md) identifies six recce TOE at the cited OA baseline. This is a candidate source of a point, not a formed LRDG. Sections 27.12–.13 require an existing Recce/armored-car unit that has reached maximum Morale **through training**, then removal of a TOE point during the Organization Phase. The setup row and available OA count do not establish that training history, current eligibility or an actual formation decision. The setup lists no LRDG explicitly; this bounded reading does not exclude other eligible sources or later formation. Section 27.2 limits Axis DSA formation to German armored Recce/AC units and one DSA at a time; no eligible German formation or arrival in this six-turn interval has been established here.
- **Trigger and outcome:** §27.41 describes a raider entering enemy-controlled ground or an enemy combat unit entering a friendly raider's hex. Section 27.43 requires the raider player to announce either event and roll: 6 spots and eliminates the raider; other rolls leave it unspotted. Section 27.44 requires a moving enemy entering the raider's hex to spend one CP to try to spot, with a once-per-Operations-Stage limit for a phasing unit. These texts describe different aspects of the procedure; the erratum does not settle whether their apparently mandatory/optional language or timing applies identically to both triggers. Nor does an enemy in the raider's hex itself prove a legally executable destination.
- **Movement and timing:** §8.5 describes ordinary Reaction as non-phasing movement in the opponent's Movement/Combat Phase; §8.51 ordinarily triggers on an enemy combat unit moving adjacent. The correction's “after any Spotting attempt” includes cases not expressly framed as ordinary adjacent-entry reaction, including the raider's own entry into enemy-controlled ground. Its interaction with phasing status, immediate movement order and the general restrictions in §§8.53–.55 needs interpretation; do not silently waive them or restrict the erratum to §27.44 alone. Sections 27.32–.34 describe hidden movement, placement on raid or successful spotting, no ZOC, and a special non-stop rule for enemy ZOC. Section 27.37 caps expenditure at 50 CP and eliminates a raider forced beyond it. Remaining CP, terrain, weather, legal destination, actual attempt and die result all matter.
- **Land-only:** §32.0/.11 replaces ordinary supply expenditure with the abstract system, while §27.35 describes weekly ammunition, fuel and water pickup, and §27.38 exempts raiders from other fuel consumption and breakdown. How this special detailed-supply procedure interacts with the abstract rules is unresolved; do not impose its individual debits unchanged or assume every prerequisite disappears. This does not independently remove the erratum's Reaction language. The designer's errata §32.0 warns that abstract rules were untested; it supplies no substitute policy.

A useful **conditional** case is a legally formed, surviving LRDG and a separately legal enemy combat unit entering its hex, followed by a failed spotting roll and a proposed Reaction. Establish the actor, formation history, point ownership, stage, movement, CP, destination and applicable Land-only supply interpretation first. The named Hussars entry supplies only a potential formation lead, not that complete witness. Compare successful spotting, the raider-initiated spotting trigger, and prohibited or insufficient-CP destinations separately; these are unexecuted checks, not outcomes or proof of reachability.
