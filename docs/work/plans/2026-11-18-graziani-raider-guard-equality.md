# Guarded raider-dump raid: equality discriminator

**Result:** At equality between the sum of two guard-test dice and the guarding force's raw defensive assault strength, [Land §27.55(b)](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) eliminates the raider; [common chart §27.91](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) permits it to survive that test and proceed to the separate attack roll. The sources do not select a precedence here. This is a conditional rules discriminator, not an observed Graziani event or a claim that this detailed dump attack applies unchanged to Land-only supply.

## Minimal conditional comparison

Assume, solely for comparison, a legally formed raider validly raids a guarded **real detailed supply dump** outside a major city; total applicable guard raw defense is **2**, and guard-test dice are **1 + 1 = 2**. This is a hypothetical value, not an identified unit or established legal position. Ignore parentheses in strength as specified by chart §27.91. Both dice and strength are at equality.

| Source applied at guard test | Consequence of those two dice | Separate subsequent die fixed at 1 for comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Land p. 39 §27.55(b) | 2 is not **higher** than 2, so raider is eliminated and the raid does not proceed to §27.55(a). | No subsequent attack roll follows this path; no 10% loss is established by this attack. |
| Common chart p. 8 §27.91 | 2 is **equal to or greater** than 2, so raider survives guard test and attacks under the next dump entry. | Die 1 destroys 10% of supplies; if raider completes raid without elimination, opponent reports real/non-dump/dummy status to raider. If the real dump hypothetically contains 100 supply points in the relevant total, 10% is 10 points; this arithmetic does not establish actual stock, rounding or its treatment across categories. |

The attack die is **not** one of the two guard-test dice. A different attack roll changes the later outcome: chart die 3–5 has no effect; die 6 leaves supplies unaffected and eliminates the raider when combat units are present. The chart's report is conditioned on completing the raid **without elimination**, so the equality pass by itself does not guarantee that report. Chart and §27.55 describe supply loss, not guard-unit casualties from this comparison; do not invent a defender combat loss. No fixed actual dice, stock, guard or action are asserted.

For a **dummy** dump, Land §27.56 separately says after any raiders–guards conflict, if any raiders survive and the dump is dummy, that fact must be revealed immediately and the supply-destruction raid is off; other guard/elimination results apply. Chart's three-category report is conditioned on completing the raid without elimination. These statements have distinct timing and conditions; their interaction, including subsequent die and survivor status, needs an explicit interpretation. A real-dump example does not claim dummy discovery, and neither report specifies full contents. See [chart census and review](2026-11-18-graziani-chart-disclosure-census-review.md) and [Land disclosure census](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-17-29.md).

## Mode and source boundaries

Land p. 43 §§32.0, .11–.13, .15, .18 says Land without Air and Logistics uses abstract Supply Units, represented by Supply Dump counters; §32.11 ignores ordinary ammo/fuel/stores/water usage and expenditure, §32.13 captures a Supply Unit if an enemy **combat unit** enters its hex, and §32.18 removes a dummy on discovery. Section 32.15 gives each real Supply Unit ammunition and fuel quantities, rather than establishing an unchanged detailed dump inventory. The source does not by itself settle whether this raider qualifies for the §32.13 predicate, whether guarded dump raid remains available in this mode, how detailed 10% destruction maps to abstract quantities, or how any discovery interacts with §27.56 and chart §27.91. Capture, raid, and discovery are distinct triggers; no automatic capture, full information or mandatory destruction follows. The [raider formation and mode note](2026-11-18-graziani-raider-reaction-erratum.md) leaves training, legal formation, movement and special supply interaction unproven.

[September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 2 has §§27.16, .36, .88 and §32.0, but no indexed §27.55, .56 or .91 correction in the retained text search. This bounded search is not proof against unindexed, differently numbered or later corrections; the §32.0 note says abstract rules were untested and supplies no equality resolution. No policy or source priority is selected.
