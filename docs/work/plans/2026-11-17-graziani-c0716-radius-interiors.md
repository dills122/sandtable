Map key identifies yellow as desert, polygonal yellow pattern as salt marsh, palm symbol as oasis, and blue dot as village/bir. “No colored interior terrain symbol apparent” describes scan appearance only; it does not determine rules terrain or exclude line features. Labels obscure portions of C0816–C0818 and C1015, and dashed tracks cross the oasis symbols, potentially obscuring small marks. Brown artwork near C0814, C0914 and C1014 intersects hex boundaries and extends into cells; exact terrain or edge classification and effects need separate review. “El Qaqa” and “Giarabub Oasis” labels span multiple cells, so label placement alone does not assign an additional settlement to each cell. C1018 is outside this disk, despite adjacent salt-marsh artwork; C0712 is also outside it. No candidate is excluded from the geometric set by this visual review. Setup eligibility, stacking, movement, routes and other legal effects remain unverified.
