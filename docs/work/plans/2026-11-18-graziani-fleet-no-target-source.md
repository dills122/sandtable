# LA-19: abstract fleet attack with no target or excess damage

**Result:** The checked original §32.74, fleet damage rule, chart and September errata do not expressly specify what happens to an already planned attack when Alexandria Harbor has no eligible ship, or to damage beyond the remaining Gun Ratings of all eligible ships. An initially empty harbor is **not** implied by the scenario: the printed starting fleet schedule places at least eight of eleven listed ships at Alexandria under its Malta options. This does not establish the harbor's state at a particular later attack.

## Printed boundaries

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF p. 44, §§32.71–.75, permit a limited Axis attack on Commonwealth ships in Alexandria Harbor, with a scenario-wide allowance and at most one per OpStage. The Axis plots it during Commonwealth Fleet Assignment; the rule names Tactical Naval Movement for execution. Section 32.74(a) uses a die to select an Air Bombardment table column, with a Campaign Game modifier; §32.74(b) uses two sequential dice, calls the result damage points inflicted on ships **in Alexandria Harbor only**, and permits Axis allocation as desired. It does not state an empty-target, unused-damage or retargeting procedure.
- Land p. 42 §§30.34/.36 says ship damage reduces Gun Rating and a ship whose entire Gun Rating has been eliminated is sunk and unrepairable. These clauses define a sunk state, not an explicit instruction to allocate surplus damage to already sunk ships, carry it forward, or discard it. Section 30.34's Ajax example is a specific ship-damage example, not an empty-harbor or overkill example; its counter/example Gun Rating conflict is separately recorded in [Ajax review](2026-11-17-graziani-ajax-counter-check-review.md).
- [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) p. 12 §41.5, Commonwealth Fleet row, gives a **2-damage** result for sequential `43` in the **81–120 Bomb Points** column. Under the non-Campaign reading of §32.74(a), a column die of 4 selects that fourth column. Hypothetically, if exactly one eligible harbor ship has only **one remaining Gun Rating point**, the table result exceeds the inspected target pool by one; the source lines do not state how to process the remainder. This is a distinguishing synthetic state, **not** proof that such a ship, target pool, eligible attack, weather or dice occur in Graziani. The [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) p. 3 corrects §41.5's barrage headings and flak terminology; it does not expressly supply an excess-fleet-damage rule in that entry.
- [Commonwealth fleet schedule](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 31 §30.6 lists eleven opening ships, with one of three marked cruisers and two of seven destroyers optionally at Malta; remaining ships deploy at Alexandria. Scenario §60.45, [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, refers to that schedule and says initial ships may not move until GT2 OpStage 1. September errata §60.45 retains its first paragraph and discards the later list. Thus 8–11 ships at Alexandria is a source-baseline consequence, conditional on accepted setup and no other modification. It does **not** prove a target survives at a later execution instant or that Malta choices, phase sequence and previous attacks had any particular outcome. See [fleet review](2026-11-17-graziani-abstract-aa-fleet-review.md).

## Unselected alternatives and checks

For an **empty harbor**, candidate policies include preventing an attack before committing it, resolving a committed attack with no eligible targets, or deferring until a target exists. These differ in attack allowance, dice consumption and what a player can infer from legality or events. The checked text does not choose one, nor establish whether hidden target presence is known at plotting or execution.

For **excess damage**, candidate policies include capping allocation at surviving Gun Rating and treating the remainder as unapplied, or rejecting/deferring an allocation until an explicitly authorized destination exists. These are contrasting project options, not printed instructions; test exact-capacity, one point above capacity, no target and mixed damaged ships only after choosing the contract. Do not allow negative Gun Rating, spill to a non-Alexandria ship, or create a repairable sunk ship merely by assumption. Whether to consume the plotted allowance and dice is a separate decision from ship-damage accounting.

A source-specific next check would locate a directly applicable published fleet-bombardment example or official clarification for either no target or overkill, with edition and exact case; the bounded source set here supplies no such example. Weather §32.75, phase mapping, Campaign modifier, actual hull identity, AA proportionality and repair/damage order remain independent gates. No implementation or policy selection is made.
