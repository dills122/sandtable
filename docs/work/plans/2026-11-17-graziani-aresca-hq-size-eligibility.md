# Aresca HQ size and tank eligibility

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Finding:** The printed counter and rules strongly support Aresca as a **nominal super-brigade-equivalent armored HQ**, making Land §3.35 a well-supported candidate for its own tank. They do not expressly name Aresca in that rule or settle whether its current shell status changes that application. This is a source-based interpretation, not an official ruling or an admission of a runtime rule.

## Evidence and distinctions

- [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 6 calls it **Aresca (1st) Regt HQ**, ID `d`, with **1 × CV33**. Its four listed battalions are XXI(L), LXII(L), LXIII(L), and I(M). The first three have nine CV33 each; I(M) has seven M11/39. The section is headed Libyan Tank Command. These are assignment and baseline facts, not current strength or proof of attachment.
- The [counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), PDF p. 1, section 2, Italian block, shows `Aresca`, `LTC`, tank symbol, **III** organization mark and parenthesized **(3)** stacking value. There is **no G** over its stacking value. III is the printed regiment designation; it is separate from the numerical stacking value. The scanned sheet is original-looking, not independent authentication of a physical piece.
- [Land](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed p. 17 §9.22 directs players to the **printed** stacking value and §9.4, rather than the counter designation or name, for organization level. [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) PDF/printed p. 2 §9.4 maps **3** to a full **super brigade** or a **division shell**. Land §3.33 says an HQ's printed stacking points represent its full complement. Together with the OA's regiment HQ and tank identification, this favors nominal super-brigade equivalent rather than a division shell. A parenthesized HQ value is not a claim that it currently occupies three points.
- **Garrison is a different fact.** Land §9.16(a) uses `G` to identify garrison units and provides free stacking in their assigned city/village; Aresca's face has no `G`. Neither its regiment label nor its `(3)` makes it a garrison HQ. Absence of that symbol is not a finding about other units, later game state, or every possible special rule.
- **Attachment and present size are different again.** [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.31 places Aresca at C3919, with **all except XXI detached**. The OA lists four assigned battalions and marks the other three attached to named divisions. This supports XXI remaining attached initially, subject to the scenario's actual setup interpretation. Land §9.26 uses the number of battalions that **may be assigned** as its denominator, not simply the number listed in the OA. If those four are the applicable maximum, one attached is below two thirds and the brigade is a **shell**; the OA list supports that baseline but does not explicitly establish the maximum. Section 9.27 excludes an attached shell battalion from the count needed for a full parent. Under §9.28 a brigade shell is treated as a battalion equivalent where size matters, and §9.4 gives it one stacking point. Confirm the permitted assignment maximum and actual attachment and shell status before fixing current size. Section 9.21 distinguishes assigned from actually attached units; same-hex presence alone does not prove attachment. Land §3.33 gives an HQ with no attached combat units zero stacking points. Later state must be checked separately.
- Land p. 6 §3.35 says tanks assigned to armored division and armored brigade HQs may not be used in combat unless the HQ has **no combat units attached**. On the nominal super-brigade reading, applying this restriction to Aresca while XXI is attached is a strong, conservative interpretation: do not automatically add the HQ's CV33 to XXI's defensive close-assault total. Section 3.31 nevertheless treats an HQ with attached combat units as a combat unit using their combined strengths; it does not make the HQ's own restricted tank eligible merely because the formation fights. If no combat unit is attached, §3.35's own condition changes, but §3.33, parenthesized ratings, and other use restrictions still matter.

## Remaining decision

Neither §9.22 nor §3.35 explicitly names Aresca, states whether a **super** brigade is included in “armored brigade HQ,” or addresses whether §9.28's shell reduction changes the HQ category for §3.35. Thus nominal brigade classification is strongly supported, but an unconditional source-proven eligibility verdict is not. An implementation needs an explicit, versioned interpretation and tests for attached versus detached XXI, shell versus full formation, and empty HQ; preserve printed `(3)`, `III`, nominal size, current size and attachment as separate facts. This finding does not establish an actual ZOC or change the independently sourced CV33 1/2 rating.
