# Supply destruction cost hierarchy: source review

**Disposition:** The [hierarchy note](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-cost-hierarchy.md) gives a defensible *proposed* Land-only reading, not an express source override. The specific half-CPA abstract procedure, matched one-third detailed procedure, unqualified common-chart row and shared counter representation are accurately distinguished. Preserve the unselected precedence and multi-unit rounding questions; no examined text directs both charges for one attempt.

## Source checks and qualifications

- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF p. 43 §32.0 applies to Land without Air and Logistics; §32.12 represents abstract Supply Units by Supply Dump counters. §32.17 specifies half the CPA of **all units in the hex**, rounded upwards, a die for each Supply Unit, and timing before any movement in the given sub-segment, with at most one attempt per unit per sub-segment. Neither target-subset permission nor rounding each unit versus an aggregate is explicit. §32.11's supply-expenditure scope is evidence of a distinct model, not express precedence over the CP chart.
- [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), printed/PDF p. 1 §6.3, lists one-third CPA for an attempt to blow a supply dump, with no mode qualifier on that row; the legend defines CPA as the unit's allowance. Naming alone cannot prove the row excludes an abstract Supply Unit, given §32.12. The two fractions have different stated bases as well as different numbers: one unit's CPA in the chart versus all units in the hex in §32.17. The source alone does not say to add them or choose one over the other.
- [Air and Logistics](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), printed p. 22/PDF p. 22 and printed p. 23/PDF p. 27, §54.14: only non-gun units may attempt; only one phasing unit per hex may attempt a particular dump per Player-Turn. Base charge is one-third **of the attempting unit's basic CPA**, rounded up. Further one-third or two-thirds may be announced before the die to modify it; total attempt spending cannot exceed basic CPA, and the unit cannot exceed its CPA to blow a dump. A co-located unit may attempt while retreating before combat, limited to one-third and required to vacate regardless of result. The final sentence permits blowing dumps in any segment of an Operations Stage. Keep the retreat case and general any-segment wording visible when specifying actor/timing; do not assume the ordinary phasing restriction alone settles every case. These detailed conditions and percentage loss procedure are not silently transferable to §32.17.
- The chart fraction and detailed rule support an interpretation that the chart summarizes detailed demolition; they do not prove that it is exclusive to that mode. A specific-procedure precedence choice for §32.17 is plausible but remains project policy, not a source-declared hierarchy. A single CPA 15 gives 8 versus 5 CP under the respective formulas, contingent on actor and eligibility; multi-unit cases and permitted overruns require separate rules and allocation choices.
- The reviewed note's errata and Land §24.9 references are useful scope evidence; this review makes no independent exhaustive errata or later-edition claim. In particular, mode, actor, movement history, all-unit charging, target selection, dummy handling, and visibility still gate any implementation. Section 6.21's general overrun treatment and corrected §8.17 limit also prevent treating simple remaining CPA as a universal resource rule.
