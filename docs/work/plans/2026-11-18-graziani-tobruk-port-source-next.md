# Tobruk port efficiency: later-scenario comparison

**Finding:** The original Desert Fox setup independently repeats a starting Tobruk efficiency of **7** with San Giorgio still present. This is another scenario statement in the same source, not an official correction of Graziani or evidence that 7 is throughput. The conflict with chart maximum 5 remains unresolved; [RUL-GRA-10](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-10-tobruk-initial-port-efficiency-planning-interpretation) remains a planning interpretation, not a source correction.

The [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 6 / printed p. 30 §61.6, states that ports start at full listed efficiency except Benghazi at zero and Tobruk at seven, with San Giorgio still there. Section 61.7 on the same page introduces scenario adjustments for play without Air and/or Logistics, and §61.72 specifically addresses Land Game only. This is a separate later scenario setup, not a Land-only-specific change to §60.7, a statement that Tobruk's undamaged maximum is seven, or a demonstrated continuation of an actual Graziani game. Repetition makes the printed seven less easily dismissed as an isolated occurrence; it does not determine source precedence or how above-maximum capacity operates.

The [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-tobruk-port-source-next-review.md) notes §61.6 itself says the ship is still present, not that it is blocked or partially sunk; those details come from other cases. Section 61.72 continues on the following PDF page with Land-only supply units, without expressly changing port efficiency or providing clearance. The chart's below-five statement explicitly concerns the campaign start, not an express correction of this later setup.

The existing [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-port-independent.md) already distinguishes chart efficiency **5** from the separate throughput columns (1 incoming and 3 outgoing stacking points; 1,700 tons) and records the three-level obstruction. No newly identified original source or official erratum in this bounded search changes that distinction or supplies a reconciled starting value, an above-maximum rule, Land-only clearance, or a six-turn witness. Searches for the scenario and chart sections, Tobruk efficiency 7, and San Giorgio errata found no additional official correction; third-party house rules are not authoritative. This is not an exhaustive census of later rulings.
