# Conditional retreat overlap policy: independent critique

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition: useful bounded comparison; revise admission criteria before selecting a policy.** The comparison preserves the source review's conditional overlap and does not claim an established hierarchy, automatic four-hex movement, or a historical position. Its alternatives are interpretations, not source-resolved results. The following choices need sharper definition before any deterministic rule is admitted.

1. **Quantify against the same enemy.** The witness has one enemy `E`, so both predicates can be assessed against that force without ambiguity. With multiple ZOC projectors, specify whether §15.34 is evaluated for each enemy the unit cannot attack, what “another unit attacks that enemy” requires, and whether §10.36 is evaluated per unit, enemy, or enemy-occupied hex. Identify which qualifying attack or Holding Off affects which predicate and target, and decide the outcome when one enemy is attacked but another is not. A different target, mere attack availability, or an unqualified barrage must not silently satisfy a same-enemy condition. These quantifiers and interaction are decisions requiring source review, not established by the single-enemy witness. Test mixed targets, multiple projectors in one hex and separate hexes, and separate acting units.
2. **Separate consequence from effects.** The terrain-priority candidate correctly leaves §10.36's three additional disorganization points open; it must also decide movement CP, movement-generated disorganization, other retreat restrictions, and any independently applicable §15.34 or general rules. Compulsory-combat priority needs the same independent accounting for §15.34 and other rules, not just §10.36's stated effects. Sequential treatment must define event order, evaluation snapshot, predicate persistence, distance and destination for each movement, charging per actual step versus flat additions, and what happens if the first movement makes the second impossible or unnecessary. Neither selecting a priority nor mentioning both rules automatically includes or excludes all their effects. Define CP availability and shortfall, disorganization thresholds and resulting state transitions, with no assumed free movement, automatic double charge, or invented fixed penalty.
3. **Make routing and failure explicit.** Distinguish three hexes moved from ending three hexes away; the witness demonstrates both, not a general route algorithm. Define choice authority, path and destination legality, no doubling back, other ZOCs, friendly-occupied ZOC under §10.26, blocked or partially traversable routes, surrender trigger and timing, and whether state changes during retreat alter legality. Do not generalize surrender to every blocked path without an adopted basis. Include both distances, alternate legal paths, occupied-ZOC and forced-ZOC cases, and no-path and partial-path cases in deterministic tests.
4. **Gate both sides and every disclosure.** Define the phasing-side and opposing-side views independently, including selectable choices, errors, state updates, event projections, replay, and intelligence requests. Full-state validation alone does not make a player-facing action safe: availability, route enumeration, rejection, ordering and timing can reveal hidden ZOC or units. Specify a side-safe representation and behavior when the authoritative answer depends on hidden facts, then test paired worlds indistinguishable to each side across each observable boundary. Do not treat redacted event text as sufficient if action availability or outcomes expose the same facts.
5. **Separate editorial acceptance from operational admission.** Record the selected interpretation, source conflict, assumptions and unresolved corrections; obtain source or editorial review without presenting that review as an official ruling. Bind all dependent timing, route, cost, disclosure and failure choices to a versioned ruleset/configuration hash. Specify retention or explicit migration of existing saves, replay inputs and per-audience projection. Keep this overlap blocked for paths requiring its outcome until those decisions and focused behavior, privacy and persistence tests pass; acceptance of a planning candidate alone does not establish scenario readiness.
