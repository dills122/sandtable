# LA-01: Land-only §32.5 motorization census

**Scope:** Bounded census of every printed numbered case in [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) §32.5, including the four lettered §32.58 cases, against [register LA-17](2026-11-12-graziani-source-register.md#initial-obligation-rows), LA-02/07–10/15/18 and the [remaining-source queue](2026-11-17-graziani-remaining-source-research.md). Visually rechecked printed/PDF p. 44; §32.59 is followed by §32.6. Land §32.0 applies to Land without Air and Logistics. A printed fact or prior review does not establish a selected interpretation, current allocation, legal action, delivered points or implemented behavior.

## Errata and next source work

The cited [motorization review](2026-11-17-graziani-motorization-dummy-review.md) also reports a broader extracted-text search; neither is proof against indirect, unindexed or later corrections.

All nine numbered cases and §32.58(A–D) appear above. LA-17 names the full range; LA-02/07–10 cover arrival and setup, LA-15 transport, and LA-18 convoy losses. This census identifies **§32.58(C)'s relationship to the specific combat CP clauses and sequence** as a focused next source check, not an assertion those clauses are absent or that the existing source note is wrong. A separate named source check is a proposed §32.51 medium-truck abandonment/recovery interaction. Policy acceptance, all mode interactions, completeness of scenario state and implementation remain open.
