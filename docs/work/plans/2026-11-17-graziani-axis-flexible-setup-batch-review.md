# Graziani Axis flexible setup: source review

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition:** Correct the Ba 65 refitted count before accepting the inventory; other checked statements below are supported within their stated limits.

## Finding

Scenario §60.32 lists **Ba 65: 10 available, 2 refitted**, not 10/6 as in the inventory. The 6 refitted value belongs to the following Ba 88 row. The September 1979 errata §60.32 corrects `2501` to `Z501`; it does not give a Ba 65 correction. The same scenario paragraph prohibits Italian refitting until Game Turn 1, Operations Stage 2; include that restriction if this row is used beyond placement inventory.

## Checked source boundaries

- Scenario §60.31 prints the Libya pool, including the second `4/1AR` immediately after `XXVI Cp`, as transcribed. Errata §60.31 says XXI Corps Artillery is listed twice; it does not explicitly resolve that second `4/1AR`. The Map A/B row and its nine artillery identifiers match. Saharan Det is within three hexes of C0716, with five light trucks. The two Autoblinda 40 TOE points are an attachment or Tobruk replacement choice, not independently placed land units.
- Section 60.32 supports facility-capacity limits, no starting planes in Italy/Sicily, unavailable Crete, all other listed plane counts, three/ten/fifteen pilots and 39 SGSU. Section 59.35 separately limits SGSU to owned facilities and their readying capacity; individual facility eligibility and capacity are not established by the pool. Section 59.32 states all planes start fueled and armed; ready planes are a portion of total available.
- Section 60.34 lists detailed-game dumps and air supplies. Its two starred dumps and two dummy dumps can be placed in a Map C Libya hex not within four hexes of a Commonwealth unit. Section 60.92(B) explicitly replaces §60.34 for Land-only: Dump 1 and Dump 2 each have three regular units, each 40 ammo/50 fuel, and each group is in one qualifying Map C Libya hex; two groups of three dummies follow their rules. C0716 separately has one unit with 20 ammo/6 fuel. Do not add the detailed supply table to that replacement.
- Section 60.93(B), for Land and Air with logistics abstracted, adds five regular units of 40 ammo/15 fuel each to Axis airfields and literally refers to `60.82 “B”`. Preserve the faulty reference as an open interpretation; do not assume it establishes the underlying allocation. Section 60.91 says `None` for scenario-specific air abstraction in Land and Logistics.
- Section 60.33 prints 30 light, 100 medium, **25 heavy** anywhere in Libya; 25/140/40 at Tripoli; 10 light/50 medium at any air facility. Sections 59.44 and 59.63–.66 support the inventory's qualified truck and additional-supply discussion. In particular, §59.66(C) requires each 30 other unattached motorization points to be in one hex for an optional additional regular unit. These provisions do not themselves choose hexes or validate contact, stacking, borders, aircraft capacity or all supply-placement interactions.
