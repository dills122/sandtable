# Captured abstract supply: immediate and free use

**Finding:** §32.13 expressly attaches immediate and free use of supplies to enemy combat-unit entry and capture. It does not specify a quantity, draw allocation, later balance, or express precedence over the ordinary range and expenditure cases. Treat a waiver of fuel, ammunition or range rules as a candidate interpretation, not a proven consequence of “free”; neither defer all use nor silently empty the unit as though printed.

The [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-captured-supply-use-review.md) confirms that §32.13 does not expressly waive Capability Point costs or say that supply use avoids depletion. CP and consumable supply are distinct systems; “free” does not settle either accounting rule by itself.

## Source boundaries

- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §32.13: a Supply Unit has no combat capabilities; if **any enemy combat unit enters** its hex, the unit is captured and its supplies used immediately and freely. This is an entry-triggered statement about supplies. It does not explicitly say whether every point is consumed, who chooses beneficiaries, what “freely” waives, whether remaining stock survives, or whether captured status grants a durable friendly supply source. A combat unit's entry itself has separate legality issues under §8.13 p. 14.
- §32.16 permits a friendly combat unit to draw from a Supply Unit within one-half of that combat unit's CPA, measuring a route with the specified movement-type assumptions; the route cannot pass through impassable terrain or enemy ZOCs unoccupied by friendly units. The same paragraph does not say capture creates or waives a trace, or decide whether the captured counter has become friendly at the instant of use. The unit entering the hex is at zero geometric distance from that counter, but range, route and ownership for *other* prospective recipients still need a policy. Do not assume ordinary eligibility is satisfied for every unit on the map.
- §32.21 charges ammunition by individual battalion equivalents: one Ammo Point for a non-phasing equivalent in Assault and two in Barrage, with a phasing unit requiring twice the appropriate non-phasing amount. §32.22 locates ammunition expenditure at the instant of combat. §§32.23–.24 charge fuel in an Operations Stage when required units first move, and again upon exceeding CPA; rates differ by tank, gun/recce, motorization and other categories. These printed triggers do not describe a combat or movement event at every capture, nor expressly say how the capture phrase overrides, fulfills or delays them. An entering unit may already have paid the relevant fuel for that stage; no automatic refund or second ordinary charge follows solely from capture.
- §32.15 caps each Supply Unit at 40 ammo and 60 fuel, and removes it when **both** quantities reach zero; moving points and allocating consumption need conservation under a selected interpretation. The capture text does not state that a 15-ammo/50-fuel stock necessarily becomes `0/0`, disappears, or is replenished. Nor does it authorize silently storing immediate use for an arbitrary later stage. If the phrase implies some use at capture, preserve that source requirement while the amount and recipients remain open.
- §10.29 p. 18 separately describes capture of a non-combat unit alone in an enemy ZOC during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase, if it has no strength of any type. Eligibility of a Supply Unit and meaning of “alone” require their own interpretation; enemy adjacency alone is insufficient. Section 32.13's immediate/free phrase is printed on **enemy combat-unit entry** and is not explicitly repeated in §10.29; neither an attack alone nor a ZOC-only event establishes entry. Whether the same use follows a qualifying ZOC capture is unresolved, not a demonstrated automatic consequence. See [independent capture check](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-independent.md).

## Candidate policies and proof limits

Plausible readings include: waive a normal charge for eligible immediate recipients; allocate some actual captured points immediately under ordinary expenditure/trace constraints; or apply another explicitly bounded capture-specific use. These are competing project interpretations, not an exhaustive list or source verdict. A waiver of expenditure, a free transfer with depletion, and no additional ordinary fuel charge because the stage cost was already paid have materially different accounting. Select actor, recipients, timing, quantities, trace and subsequent ownership together; where they cannot be established, fail closed without inventing a balance or public disclosure. Reconcile capture, discovery, assigned motorization (§32.56), no-duplicate debit and audience visibility separately.

Discriminating synthetic cases: entry before versus after the capturing unit's first fuel expenditure; entering unit versus another in-range, out-of-range or blocked-trace combat unit; immediate capture versus later combat; zero ammo with positive fuel; captured 15/50 stock under each candidate conservation policy; qualifying ZOC-only versus actual entry; and an undiscovered dummy. Check accepted actor/phase rules, conservation, causal ordering, replay, and side-authorized events. These are not legal-state or tested-gameplay claims.
