# Graziani first-month convoy timing: independent source review

**Finding:** Game-Turn 1 is September III 1940, and a normal turn's Naval
Convoy Stage precedes Operations Stage 1. If the opening follows the whole
Game-Turn sequence, the first encountered Naval Convoy Stage is turn 1 in
September, before the first Operations Stage. Scenario §60.22 instead says the
scenario starts with turn 1, Operations Stage 1; it does not explicitly say
whether the earlier turn-1 stage is played, skipped, or already accounted for
in setup. The sources checked do not settle that startup boundary or whether
§32.57 triggers at a partial-month scenario start. Do not assert a September
loss, zero loss, or first-October loss as established fact.

## Source observations

| Source | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| Scenario §60.1, printed p. 26 | Group begins 15 September 1940; June through early September are ignored. |
| Scenario §60.22, printed p. 27 | Graziani starts Game-Turn 1, Operations Stage 1, and ends after turn 6, Operations Stage 3. This wording names an operations stage, not an explicit initial Naval Convoy Stage. |
| Map D turn track, map PDF p. 4 | Track begins with turn 1, Sep III 1940, marked as beginning Graziani at stage 1; turn 2 is Sep IV; turns 3–6 are Oct I–IV. Track does not show Sep I or II game turns. |
| Land §§5.1–5.2, printed pp. 11–12 | Each Game-Turn has an Initiative Determination Stage, then Naval Convoy Stage (Schedule and Tactical Shipping), then first, second and third Operations Stages. Naval Convoy Arrival is a separate phase *inside* each Operations Stage. |
| Land §32.57, printed p. 44 | During the first Naval Convoy Stage of each month, determine the previous month's motorization losses, reading the appropriate month on its named chart. Text does not specify a scenario-start exception or define “first” relative to a mid-month start. |
| Scenario §§59.2, 59.62–.64 and 60.37, printed pp. 25–27 | Setup lists initial forces and determines initial motorization from listed trucks; Axis plans convoy runs for the remainder of September before play starts. This preplanning does not expressly waive the whole opening Naval Convoy Stage or §32.57. |

Land §6.14's example independently calls Game-Turn 1 September III, 1940.
The normal ordering therefore makes turn 1's pre-operation Naval Convoy Stage
the earliest *candidate*, not the separate turn-1 Operations Stage 1 arrival
phase. If §60.22 is interpreted as entering directly at Operations Stage 1,
the first subsequently encountered normal Naval Convoy Stage would be turn 2,
September IV; turn 3, October I is the first such stage in October. Neither
interpretation follows conclusively from the named sources alone.

## Meaning of “first” and ruling implications

“First Naval Convoy Stage of each month” naturally refers to the earliest stage
in that month in the game sequence, rather than the first stage encountered
after an arbitrary resume or retry. Here the published track itself starts at
Sep III; it does not provide a prior Sep I/II game stage. Yet a scenario start
at Operations Stage 1 could exclude turn 1's preamble, and the rules do not say
whether the first *encountered* September stage then substitutes for the
calendar month's first scheduled stage. Treat first-encountered-after-start as
a proposed policy, not as a printed definition. A resume must retain prior
resolution status rather than treating its first encountered stage as monthly
first.

If a September startup determination is ruled applicable, §32.57's previous
month would be August; the separate question of whether the chart key is the
elapsed month or the stage month remains open. Under the proposed elapsed-month
key, the September 1940 stage would consult August's dash; October I would
consult September's dash. The dash's meaning, numeric rounding, snapshot and
removal ordering remain unresolved as recorded in [RUL-GRA-09 review](2026-11-17-graziani-rul09-review.md).
These are conditional consequences, not official clarifications.

Before admitting behavior, choose and version startup entry, monthly-first
semantics, chart key and dash treatment; test turn-1 and turn-2 September,
turn-3 October, and save/resume or retry without duplicate or partial loss.
Keep the existing fail-closed gate while choices remain unaccepted.
