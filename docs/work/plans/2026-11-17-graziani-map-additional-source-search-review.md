# Graziani map: public-source lead review

**Review date:** November 17, 2026. Independent check of linked public pages in
[additional-source search](2026-11-17-graziani-map-additional-source-search.md).
No contact, purchase, module download, or protected map scan inspection.

## Findings

- **1995 player discussion: unverified.** The linked Google Groups URL returned
  HTTP 429 during this review. Neither the date nor the reported four pages and
  omitted Italian charts could be independently checked. Retain as a reported
  lead, not as independently verified evidence; retry the thread when accessible.
- **Decision Games: text verified; images uninspected.** The
  [listing](https://shop.decisiongames.com/ProductDetails.php?ProductCode=P3034)
  says design stage, pre-order, SPI-classic update, revised procedures, and
  graphic map update. These publisher statements establish a proposed successor,
  not a released 1979 printing or original design intent. Page text contains no
  relevant correction; linked product images were not inspected, so absence of
  relevant sheet images is not independently established by this review.
- **VASSAL: changelog verified; module uninspected.** The
  [page](https://vassalengine.org/wiki_old/wiki/Module%3AThe_Campaign_for_North_Africa%3A_The_Desert_War_1940-43)
  identifies maintainer and contributors and records the v0.93 map replacement
  and April 6, 2020 v0.94 addition under September addenda. Changelog does not
  identify high side or sheet registration. It is a maintainer's report, not
  independently attributable SPI direction; module content was not inspected.
- **Grognard index: entries verified; articles uninspected.** The
  [index](https://grognard.com/indexes/c045.html) calls the Chain of Command #8
  reduced-size maps a variant and identifies Pratuch's Moves #49 survey as
  analysis. It gives no author, page, image, or publisher attribution for the
  former. Index classifications do not establish article contents or origin.
- **CNA Living Rules: site claims verified.** Its
  [home page](https://basmith.net/cna/) calls the map its own redraw, says
  September errata are applied, describes disputed rulings and provisional
  rules, and disclaims affiliation. These are site claims; this review did not
  compare the redraw against original sheets.
- **September addenda: relevant transcription verified.** The
  [Grognard transcription](https://grognard.com/errata1/cna.html) specifies
  an escarpment at map D hexside 2228/2328 without stating its high side. This
  transcription is secondary evidence of the dated addenda, not a new correction.

These checks do not resolve ML-02 sheet registration or ML-03 high side. They
verify only the stated page content and access limits, not the completeness of
search results, physical editions, magazine articles, or unindexed records.
