# Sheferzen and 2nd Rifle: initial ZOC source baseline

**Disposition:** OA and characteristic charts give conditional baseline strengths, but neither counter alone is proven to project ZOC under all §10.1 predicates. In particular, Sheferzen's `dd` OA row is titled a battalion but the characteristics chart classifies `dd` as infantry company-equivalent. The 2nd Rifle row is `n`, a battalion-equivalent despite its name. Do not equate name with game size, ignore possible co-located setup choices, or infer an initial compulsory combat.

## Conditional ZOC analysis

Land rules PDF pp. 17–18 §§10.11, 10.15 and 10.21: §10.11 generally requires a combat unit above battalion-equivalent with more than one stacking point, or a hex containing more than one stacking point; §10.15 separately withholds ZOC from unit(s) in a hex totaling **less than 10 raw defensive close-assault points**. Both requirements and terrain/entry restrictions must be satisfied. Under source-baseline standalone assumptions and §11.3's rating-times-TOE calculation, `dd` has `1 × 2 = 2` raw defense and `n` has `6 × 2 = 12`. Thus the standalone garrison is below §10.15's threshold; the rifle unit clears that numerical threshold but being battalion-equivalent does not by itself satisfy §10.11's >1-stacking-point test. Confirm actual counter stacking values and any special garrison rules rather than inferring them from unit names. Combined stacks, initial player placements or additional admissible units may change both tests, so these are conditional single-unit baselines, not certified initial stack outcomes.

Even a qualified stack needs unobstructed projection under §10.21, including entry legality. The observed edge's name overprint leaves coverage unproved. §10.31–.32 obligations additionally depend on the friendly combat unit, phase and exceptions. Neither initial contact marker nor compulsory attack is established here. Check valid setup choices and co-location, counter and size distinctions, shell/attachment state, actual strengths, full edge evidence, ZOC and phase history before any definitive adjudication.
