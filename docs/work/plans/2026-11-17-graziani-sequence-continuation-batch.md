## Shared stage ledger consequence

Land §6.14, p. 12, assigns a unit's operations-stage CP allotment across both
players' portions, explicitly including retreat and reaction during the
opponent's portion. Section 6.16 says unused CP does not carry into the next
operations stage, unlike cohesion. Thus a B-side reaction during A's movement
reduces the CP remaining within that unit's allowance during the later B-side portion of the same stage; reset at the
player boundary would lose source state. Under §6.13 a unit may exceed that
allowance and incur disorganization points, so it is not a hard spending cap. Section 6.14's 1st RNF illustration
is a rules example, not a proof that its narrated attack and retreat occur in
an actual Graziani playthrough. Exact costing, overspend, disorganization,
state privacy and replay remain separate detailed-rule and runtime questions.

No source-proven globally empty phase is established here. Source positions,
conditional actions and completed legal witnesses remain distinct. [Independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-sequence-continuation-batch-review.md)
checked these sequence claims against the same scans, not an independent edition.
