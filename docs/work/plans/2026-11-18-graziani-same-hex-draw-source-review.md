# Independent review: same-hex abstract supply draw

**Disposition:** Accept bounded source comparison. The detailed-fuel erratum is a relevant same-hex analogy; it does not expressly define the Land-only §32.16 trace. Neither a zero-CP rule nor a nonzero-distance requirement is established by the checked passages.

- Visually rechecked [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF p. 43. Section 32.16 makes the range half the friendly combat unit's CPA, measures from unit to Supply Unit as a medium-truck traversal except for non-motorized infantry, and excludes impassable terrain and enemy ZOCs unoccupied by friendly units. Its examples describe nonzero ranges; they do not explicitly settle same-hex distance or endpoint treatment. Section 32.15's same-hex Organization transfer is **between Supply Units**, a distinct permission; §32.37 points back to §32.16 without a local-draw example.
- Rechecked [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 3 §49.14. It says a unit may take fuel from a source such as a dump or truck in its same hex while discussing fuel capacity. It does not address §32.16, abstract Supply Units, ammunition or zero-length path semantics. Land §32.0 applies its abstract rules when the Land Game is played without the Air and Logistics Games and replaces specified supply expenditures; §32.11 says only the following supply rules apply. These support the note's mode distinction, not a blanket claim that every detailed non-supply rule is superseded.
- Land p. 18 §10.29 addresses actual Truck Convoys entering an enemy ZOC and requires an already-present friendly combat unit; §32.16 instead describes a hypothetical trace and says friendly **units** for its ZOC exception. The two wordings do not establish identical endpoint rules. The note correctly leaves that interaction and the separate victory convoy trace unresolved.
- The D3714 example depends on scenario setup and an accepted action snapshot, not the fuel analogy. Its previously [reviewed source evidence](2026-11-18-graziani-draw-setup-witness-review.md) remains conditional on formation representation, stacking, stock, endpoint and other draw conditions. Neither alternative in the note asserts an executed draw or a printed prohibition.
