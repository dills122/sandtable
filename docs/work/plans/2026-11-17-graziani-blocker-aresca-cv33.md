# Aresca CV33 close-assault source check

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Finding:** CV33 has a printed close-assault rating of **1 offensive / 2 defensive per TOE strength point**. It is neither zero nor unassigned. This supplies the missing weapon-system rating; it does not by itself decide whether Aresca HQ's own tank contributes to a particular grouped ZOC calculation.

An [independent source review](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-aresca-cv33-review.md)
confirms the rating and identifies the regiment-equivalence and HQ restrictions
that must be considered separately from the weapon value.

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.31, places Aresca Regiment at C3919, with all except XXI detached, and five medium and five heavy trucks. Its separate entries attach LXIII(L) to 63 Cirene, LXII(L) to 62 Marmarica and I(M) to 1 CCNN.
- [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 6, Libyan Tank Command, lists Aresca (1st) Regiment HQ, ID `d`, as `1 × CV33`; XXI(L) Tank Battalion, ID `k`, as `9 × CV33`. Notes mark the other named battalions attached elsewhere. These are source baseline quantities, not proof of their later current strength.
- Axis OA PDF p. 26 §4.46b lists `d` headquarters with one parenthesized maximum tank TOE, and `k` tank battalion-equivalent with up to nine tank TOE. Their unit-row close-assault cells show dashes; they are not the assigned tank weapon-system table. PDF p. 29 §4.48, **Italian Tank and Gun Characteristics**, identifies `CV 33 (L3/35)` under Tank Weapon Systems and prints unparenthesized **1/2** in Close Assault Off/Def. The same row prints CPA 25, anti-air 0, anti-armor 0, armor protection 1, fuel rate 1 and breakdown adjustment 2R. Numeric zero in other columns does not make close assault zero. The light tank is expressly assigned a rating; no categorical exclusion follows from its classification.
- [Land rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed p. 6 §§3.23 and 3.31, classify tank-type units as combat units and explain that an HQ represents attached units. Section 3.35 says tanks assigned to armored division and armored brigade HQs cannot be used in combat while combat units are attached. Aresca is described as a **regiment** HQ, but Land p. 17 §9.22 explains that Italian regiments can be brigade-equivalents and directs players to printed stacking value and §9.4 rather than name alone. Whether Aresca is such an equivalent, and whether §3.35 applies to it, require a separately supported interpretation; the regiment label alone does not exclude that restriction. Do not treat the unparenthesized vehicle rating as unconditional HQ participation. Section 3.4 and the September 1979 errata's parenthesized-rating clarification may also matter when applying a parenthesized rating.
- Land printed p. 19 §11.3 calculates raw combat strength as TOE strength multiplied by applicable rating, then totals raw points before conversion to actual strength. At the listed baseline, XXI's nine CV33 TOE would therefore supply **18 raw defensive points** if eligible and unchanged. Aresca HQ's one TOE has vehicle rating 2, but its eligibility alongside attached XXI is not established by the rating alone. Section 10.11 requires a combat unit above battalion-equivalent or more than one stacking point in the hex, and says HQ units without attached combat units do not exert ZOC. Section 10.15 separately excludes hexes below ten **raw** defensive close-assault points; the 18-point baseline clears that numeric threshold only if XXI is eligible and unchanged. Land p. 23 §15.4 reduces **actual** close-assault strength for unsupported tanks; it does not by itself change §10.15’s raw threshold. Actual state, other eligibility and projection restrictions remain separate checks; do not automatically total HQ and XXI as 20 or infer a ZOC from the numeric threshold alone.
