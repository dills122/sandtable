# Tobruk repair facility: later scenario comparison

**Result:** Two additional official scenario setups expressly give Tobruk different repair-facility types under different sides. They strengthen the evidence that the printed type is scenario-specific; they do not prescribe a conversion when control changes during a continuing game.

## New bounded source comparison

The [prior follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-facility-followup.md) and [review](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-facility-followup-review.md) compare initial Italian temporary facility in §60.33 with Land §22.31's major facility for whoever controls Tobruk. They do not compare the following scenario entries. I visually inspected the [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf):

| Setup | Exact printed evidence | Bound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desert Fox §61.36, PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29 | Commonwealth has a **Temporary Repair Facility in Tobruk** and one in Mersa Matruh; Alexandria and Cairo are major facilities. §§61.1–.2 describe a shared setup for two scenarios and start the race for Tobruk at GT26, stage 3. | Another scenario's starting position, not an observed transfer from Graziani or an automatic response to control. The Commonwealth entry is in a supply section; mode-specific replacement wording must be checked separately before importing the whole section into Land-only play. |
| Crusader §62.5, PDF p. 9 / printed p. 33 | Construction at scenario start includes a **Temporary Repair Facility in Tobruk**, followed by a Commonwealth optional temporary facility and Alexandria/Cairo majors. | Explicit initial construction listing. This text supplies no procedure for changing Tobruk's type after a later control change. |
| El Alamein §63.45, PDF p. 12 / printed p. 36 | Axis has a **Major Repair Facility in Tobruk**, plus temporary facilities at Bardia and Mersa Matruh. §63.2, PDF p. 9, starts both scenarios at GT102, stage 1. | A later distinct setup, not proof of a live temporary-to-major transformation. §63.82 says §63.45's Axis initial supply status is replaced in Land-only play; whether that replacement also displaces the facility sentence is a separate scope question. |

These rows show that the original scenario book itself lists temporary Tobruk for the Commonwealth in one setup and major Tobruk for the Axis in another, in addition to the previously recorded Italian temporary setup. The [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-tobruk-transition-source-next-review.md) notes that §62.5 does not explicitly assign the Tobruk facility an owner in that sentence, and §61.72(D)'s cross-reference leaves Land-only treatment of §61.36 unresolved. The rows do not establish that facility type is determined solely by side, control, or date. Nor do they identify a single persistent facility, state whether two facilities coexist, prescribe destruction or rebuilding, or specify a transition on entry or control. The scenarios set independent initial positions; sequence across their dates is not an actual play history.

A directly applicable official transition or clarification of the conflict between §60.33 and §22.31 remains the discriminating evidence. Absent that, control state, scenario precedence, facility identity, enemy-presence destruction and recovery still need explicit versioned decisions. This check does not certify that a control change is reachable in the Graziani six-turn window.
