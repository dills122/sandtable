# Axis reinforcement diversion to Benghazi

**Finding:** No Axis whole-unit reinforcement is listed for Graziani GT1–6
on the inspected original Axis Land Reinforcement Schedule; its first entry is
GT9 OpStage 2. Scenario §60.37 refers to that track. Thus §20.15's diversion
is not shown to produce an in-scenario whole-unit arrival, although planned
replacement points and abstract convoy supplies are separate mechanisms.

## Original-source conditions

- Land §20.15, printed/PDF p. 31, says Axis reinforcements normally arrive in
  Tripoli. If **no Commonwealth land combat units are west of Mersa Matruh,
  inclusive**, Axis may divert forces to Benghazi: one battalion-equivalent
  combat unit **or** two company-equivalent combat units, plus up to ten Truck
  or Motorization Points assigned to arrive with that unit or its parent.
  This is an optional diversion of an eligible arrival, not new units or a
  general permission to send any number of replacement points there.
- §20.15 prohibits diversion if it would exceed stacking restrictions or if
  Benghazi has worse than Level 3 efficiency at that moment; its use also
  affects port capacity for that Operations Stage. “West ... inclusive”
  includes Matruh itself. Precisely classifying every unit, westward hexrow,
  off-map location and hidden opponent state requires source-backed board and
  representation rules; no current qualifying state is established here.
- Common port chart §55.3, PDF/printed p. 15, lists Benghazi **efficiency 3,
  in 2 stacking points, out 5, maximum 2,500 tons**. These are different
  quantities, not 2,500 extra motorization or the whole-game arrival cap. The
  chart says reinforcements are never affected by a port's capacity or current
  efficiency, while §20.15 specifically requires Level 3 and says this use
  affects capacity. Apply neither statement as if the other did not exist:
  a specific diversion gate and its effect on other same-stage port use need
  an explicit mode-aware reconciliation. The chart's ordinary efficiency value
  3 meets the §20.15 threshold if the actual state is still at that level;
  actual damage and scenario override must be checked. Scenario §60.7 says
  ports begin at listed efficiency except Tobruk, which separately has a source
  conflict; that exception does not change Benghazi's printed initial value.
- Land §20.11–.12 defines reinforcements as new whole units arriving according
  to the track in the Naval Convoy Arrival Phase. §20.0/.21 separates
  replacements, while Land-only §32.61 describes Axis abstract convoys of
  supply units, motorization and replacement points. A scheduled reinforcement
  is not automatically a §32.61 cargo item, and a convoy or reinforcement
  plan is not evidence that a whole-unit track entry exists. No automatic
  conversion between any of these event types is established here.

## Horizon and examples

Original Axis schedule §4.43b, chart PDF p. 2, starts **GT9 / OpStage 2**
with Italian XXV Corps Artillery Regiment. Its continuation and notes on PDF
p. 3 identify arrival dates and say attached units normally arrive attached,
with a specific §20.15 diversion exception. Scenario §60.22 ends Graziani
at GT6 OpStage 3 and §60.37 says Axis receives reinforcements as on the track.
Therefore no GT1–6 entry appears on that inspected whole-unit schedule; this
is not a claim that every future or independently sourced arrival is absent,
or that any delayed arrival or optional rule has been exhaustively excluded.
Scenario initial Benghazi Garrison (§60.31) and initial Benghazi supply unit
(§60.92(B)) are setup, not track reinforcements. A preplanned Axis convoy or
replacement from §§20.21/32.61 is not a counterexample to the schedule check.

Conditional rule vectors, **not actual Graziani arrivals**:

1. Later eligible one battalion-equivalent and ten accompanying assigned
   motorization points, no qualifying Commonwealth land combat unit in the
   forbidden region, current Benghazi efficiency 3 and valid stacking:
   §20.15's stated size, transport and principal gate conditions are met.
   Other arrival, allocation, mode and port accounting rules still apply.
2. Change only efficiency to a level worse than 3, or place a qualifying
   Commonwealth land combat unit at Matruh itself: block diversion under the
   stated §20.15 condition. Unknown location/classification or efficiency is
   unresolved, not proof that diversion is legal.
3. A single brigade-equivalent, three company-equivalents, eleven assigned
   motorization points, or an already-arrived replacement-point shipment is
   not established as a permitted §20.15 package. Test each independently;
   alternative breakup, excess-point destination and parent-attachment rules
   need their own source rather than inventing automatic clipping or reroute.
4. GT6 has a planned abstract Axis supply or replacement convoy but no
   independently established whole-unit track arrival: do not offer a
   Benghazi whole-unit diversion based merely on that convoy. GT9 is outside
   Graziani and is a schedule witness, not a claimed played event.
