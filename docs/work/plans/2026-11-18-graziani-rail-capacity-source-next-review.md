# Rail capacity and timing: source review

**Disposition:** The [source note](2026-11-18-graziani-rail-capacity-source-next.md) accurately preserves cumulative versus peak capacity and arrival timing as unresolved. The truck example does not establish a rail rule. The inspected texts do not expressly resolve precedence, demonstrate a legal trip, or authorize a specific runtime policy.

- Land p. 15 §8.72 places Commonwealth rail movement once per Operations Stage in the Commonwealth Rail Movement Phase and permits one stack in each direction. Page 16 §8.74 repeats the directional service; §8.75 limits rail movement to two stacking points and points separately to §49.3 for supply maximums. Page 43 §32.14 gives Supply Units zero stacking value; §32.34 limits the Commonwealth to two real and/or dummy Supply Units in one direction as the total load for a stage and retains normal rail rules. These clauses do not expressly reconcile the two-direction service with the one-direction supply limit or define whether total load means cumulative distinct boardings or maximum simultaneously aboard.
- Section 8.77 permits pickup and dropoff anywhere along a rail line, provided stacking and direction restrictions are always observed and no unit using rail has expended CP in that Stage. A drop-A, board-B, drop-B, board-C example distinguishes cumulative and peak readings, but source does not establish either outcome or general reboarding. Section 8.98's truck-convoy splitting and stops/starts example is explicitly another movement mode; it does not establish a rail turnover rule.
- Section 32.31 expressly permits a Supply Unit to move by land or rail in its arrival Operations Stage. Section 8.73 requires a rail user to start that Stage on a rail hex. For a unit arriving after Stage start, the tension depends on arrival and placement conventions; neither an automatic override nor a universal ban follows. Section 32.37's separate air-transport restriction on further movement by the owning player in that Stage constrains air then rail, irrespective of the general arrival permission. Actual origin, destination, phase, conditions and timing remain necessary.
- Section 8.73 says a rail user may not have expended CP for another reason during the Stage; §8.77 has its own no-CP wording. Neither inspected case illustrates post-rail expenditure. September errata p. 1 §8.73 adds a no-reorganization-points rule for units traveling by rail in that Stage; its text does not settle the ordering or validity of previously earned points. Avoid treating either chronology as established.
