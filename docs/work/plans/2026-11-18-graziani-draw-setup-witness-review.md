# Independent review: Matruh local-draw candidate

**Disposition:** Accept as a conditional co-location candidate, not a legal draw, admitted setup, or proof of an actual action. The named unit, five regular Supply Units and their printed starting location are supported by the checked sources. The zero-length trace and action-specific conditions remain open.

## Checks

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) §60.41, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, places Matruh Garrison at D3714 and lists **1 Essex** with 1 Durham Light Infantry and 1 South Staffordshires after `Att`. Scenario §59.2's attachment terminology and the [Matruh source review](2026-11-17-graziani-continuation-setup-review.md) distinguish attached from assigned; do not promote the separate Buffs/Hampshire assignment conflict into a finding that Essex is unlisted. Land §3.23 includes infantry-type combat units and §3.31 treats an HQ with attached combat units as a combat unit. This supports the candidate subject to actual component identity, representation and applicable setup rules, not an admitted action.
- Scenario §60.92 is headed Air & Logistics Abstracted (**Land Game Only**). Its §60.92(C), PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29, replaces §60.44 for Commonwealth initial supply and lists **five regular Supply Units at Mersa Matruh D3714**, each with **40 Ammo and 60 Fuel**. A different line separately supplies dummy counters. These are printed initial quantities, not evidence of stock at a later action. The scope of any separately mentioned repair facility is not decided by this check.
- Land §32.16, printed/PDF p. 43, uses a friendly combat unit's half CPA and measures from that unit to the Supply Unit as medium-truck movement, except non-motorized infantry traces as infantry. The line may not pass through impassable terrain or enemy ZOCs unoccupied by friendly units. The printed case does **not** explicitly define a zero-length trace, settle endpoint interpretation, or grant a legal expenditure merely because the pieces share a hex. The candidate correctly states zero cost and positive CPA as conditional assumptions rather than source facts.
- Section 32.14 literally includes the disputed “off-map” qualification; its exact reach is not settled here. Scenario §59.65 permits initial supply overstacking only when unavoidable from distribution instructions and limits later additions. These provisions and formation-capacity rules require an accepted setup interpretation; five printed units alone do not certify an admitted or legal stack. See [capacity review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-continuation-review.md).

Any future draw must also establish current friendly ownership, unit status, applicable CPA and trace rules, stock and a separately legal expenditure. A local draw is not the §60.81 victory convoy trace. No neighboring terrain, line, movement, range-rounding result or actual state is inferred here.
