# Independent review: supply motorization and combat CP

**Disposition:** The §11.25/§32.58(C) interaction and conditional Matruh candidate are supported as framed. No material error found in checked clauses. Neither co-location nor an announced engagement alone proves the exception's participation condition; the source does not specify a complete per-step checkpoint or carrier accounting model.

- **General versus specific charge:** Land §11.25, p. 19, applies CP expenditure to all units in a hex, including nonparticipants, for both phasing and non-phasing sides. Its examples use a division and withheld/barraging combat units; they do not independently classify a Motorization Point allocation as a §11.25 unit. Land §32.58(C), p. 44, specifically says Motorization Points **transporting a Supply Unit** need not expend CP when stacked with combat units **participating in combat**. The note correctly retains that classification question and does not waive the participant's own combat charge, a nontransporting allocation, or all stage costs.
- **Amounts and sequence:** §§11.21–.24 support the note's 3/2 CP non-phasing cases, 5/2/3 CP phasing cases and per-Combat-Segment maxima. §11.27's 1 CP exception requires a defending unit in a full non-Probe close assault with final differential minus four or worse. The note does not convert these into cumulative charges. Its cautious treatment of movement/retreat costs and §32.17 destruction CPA is appropriate; the exception does not by itself specify those interactions.
- **Candidate:** The prior [Matruh review](2026-11-18-graziani-draw-setup-witness-review.md) checks scenario §§60.41 and 60.92(C): Matruh Garrison with 1 Essex attached and five regular Supply Units share D3714 in printed setup. The subject note also cites §§60.43, 59.44, 59.63(B) and 59.66(C) for a possible separately loaded 30-point group. Treat that extra Supply Unit as conditional and distinct from the five; §59.65's initial stacking exception, legal distribution, component representation, assignment, an actual combat participant and combat must still be established. No current battle or reachable witness follows from the candidate. I did not independently re-audit the truck and optional-load clauses in this pass.
