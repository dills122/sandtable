# Graziani Commonwealth victory: city-source planning selection

**Status:** proposed bounded planning selection `gra-cw-city-source-v1-proposed`, not an official correction, accepted ruling, runtime admission or proof of reachability. Select one trace subject per listed objective, with independent city and eligible-hex choices. This selection makes explicit choices that §60.81 does not settle. It does not admit a complete convoy algorithm or victory verdict.

## Evidence and scope

The [reviewed original-source follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-commonwealth-victory-trace-followup-review.md) reports scenario §60.81 naming Sollum, Halfaya Pass and Siwa for tactical victory, followed by a truck-convoy trace back to Alexandria or Cairo; decisive and strategic use supply “as above.” It finds no stated tracing actor, city-hex rule or common-city choice. The [matrix](2026-11-12-graziani-victory-matrix.md) lists decisive objectives Bardia, Fort Maddalena, Sidi Omar, Sollum and Siwa, and strategic objective Tobruk. The Italian strategic “any hex” wording is not in the Commonwealth clause. The follow-up review visually checked the original scenario and Land scans and bounded September 1979 addenda; this proposal relies on that review, not a new independent reading or a different edition.

The [independent policy review](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-city-policy-proposal-review.md) prompted the witness-binding, resource-compatibility, proof-coverage and temporal disclosure requirements below. It used existing same-edition readings, not a new inspection of the scans.

Standard §60.44's unlimited city supplies do not carry silently into Land-only play: §60.92(C) replaces that rule with three initially stocked regular Supply Units per city, and §32.42 gives later Commonwealth arrivals at Cairo with an Alexandria allowance. Neither initial stock nor arrival placement proves present stock, control, a shipment or a qualifying victory endpoint. §32.16 local draw does not establish city connectivity. See the [source follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-commonwealth-victory-trace-followup.md) and [route candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-route-followup.md) for remaining source and route limits.

## Selection

1. Interpret “as above” as applying the trace condition separately to **each location listed in the row being evaluated**. Tactical uses its three listed locations; decisive its five; strategic Tobruk alone. Each location is a subject resolved under a separately accepted location and possession contract. For a multi-hex objective, that contract must specify whether one fixed hex or an existential set qualifies. Under the latter, select a hex satisfying the accepted possession and history requirements **and** starting its trace; a route from a different unqualified hex cannot be combined with possession. Under the former, trace from that fixed qualifying hex. The contract must define its quantifier before evaluation; an unresolved or incomplete qualifying set yields unknown when it can change the result. This binding is a planning choice, not a source-defined actor. It does not add lower-row locations to a higher row, require a particular holding unit as tracing actor, or decide occupation, combat-unit identity, taking or holding history.
2. For each subject independently, require an existential trace to **one** eligible city-source hex in Alexandria **or** Cairo. Different subjects may select different cities; even subjects selecting the same city may use different eligible hexes. Neither all city hexes nor a shared city or shared hex is required. Witness choices are independent of row order and consume no shared capacity. Admission of this version is limited to a non-consuming connectivity route policy; independent witnesses do not establish a jointly feasible actual shipment. Any shipment or capacity-based alternative requires a separately versioned row-level joint-feasibility predicate, including its interaction with subject results and three-valued positive, negative and unknown proof requirements, before use.
3. Candidate endpoints are certified member hexes of either named city, with friendly control under an independently accepted control predicate and compliance with separately specified endpoint obstruction rules. This control condition and allowing any certified member hex are planning interpretations, not express Commonwealth §60.81 rules; the Italian parenthesis and chart representatives do not establish them. The location summary is a candidate inventory, not a complete membership certificate. Unknown membership or control remains unknown. For this selection, no current stock, source Supply Unit, historical shipment or transport availability is added solely as an endpoint-eligibility test. This does not imply stock exists or preclude another route policy from requiring it.
4. Evaluate against the same accepted immutable terminal snapshot and authoritative state. A certified eligible endpoint plus a valid route witness establishes that subject's trace even if an unrelated candidate remains unknown. Positive proof includes accepted directed edges, terrain, weather, endpoint and blocker rules, including relevant incoming interference. A subject is known false only if complete membership, eligibility and potential route-space coverage, including seams and outside-map continuation, proves no eligible endpoint reachable; exhausting loaded or certified candidates alone is insufficient. An unknown candidate that could provide a route prevents that conclusion. No certified witness and no complete exclusion yields unknown. For the row conjunction, a known-false required predicate dominates a different unknown; otherwise unknown propagates, consistent with [RUL-GRA-07](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-07-bounded-facility-and-victory-planning-policies). This does not turn unknown into disconnection or prove masked predicates. Overall composition and terminal behavior remain separate.

This selects subject and source quantifiers only. Direction, hypothetical versus actual movement, weather, enemy interference, zero-edge traces, bounded negative proof, objective identity, snapshot timing and terminal ordering remain unaccepted dependencies. The [route candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-route-followup.md) is illustrative, not automatically adopted. If a dependency is unavailable, do not infer a positive or negative trace from this proposal alone. Bind this selection and accepted dependency versions to the ruleset and recorded internal evidence; changing any version must not reinterpret an existing save.

## Alternatives and consequences

- A single row-level force or one selected holder traces instead of every objective; a holding unit at each objective traces instead of the location. Each changes the quantifier and may change results without any change in city choice.
- One common city for the row rejects mixed Alexandria/Cairo witnesses; one common hex further rejects different hexes of the same city. Independent choices allow both. The printed alternative alone does not select among these.
- Restrict sources to chart representatives, or require a designated supply location, actual stocked Supply Unit, shipment or presently feasible transport. Conversely, omit friendly control. These choices can change eligibility and results; city name, initial supply and chart distance alone do not select one.
- Apply “as above” to a different set of subjects, carry tactical objectives into later rows, or require only a single aggregate trace. Each needs its own rationale; none follows merely from this selection.

## Admission and discrimination gates

Before runtime, independently review this selection and accept versioned location, possession, city membership, control, endpoint, route and terminal contracts. Certify relevant map and incoming interference coverage, directed route semantics and evidence sufficiency; define complete negative search and any shared-resource rules. Prove deterministic replay, checkpoint restoration and stable policy identity. A synthetic case does not establish a reachable board state or historical outcome.

Proposed tests, not executed:

- With all other predicates known true and complete source/route evidence, connect two required subjects exclusively to different cities: selected policy passes; common-city and common-hex alternatives fail. Connect them exclusively to different eligible hexes of one city: selected and common-city pass; common-hex fails. Give one subject only a valid non-chart city hex: selected passes, chart-only fails.
- For a two-hex objective, hold only one qualifying hex and route it, then move the sole route to the other, unheld hex. Under an existential possession contract the first passes this bound conjunction and the second fails when coverage is complete; a separate-witness alternative could pass both. Also test both held with only the second routed: an existential contract can pass where a fixed-first-hex contract fails. Taking and holding history must independently qualify each witness.
- For each listed objective in each row, independently remove its trace while preserving possession and all other traces; the row is no-match under complete evidence. Vary a holder-only or one-row-force alternative separately; do not add Halfaya to decisive or other lower-row objectives to strategic. Test possession without trace and trace without possession.
- Compare one valid witness plus irrelevant unknown candidates, complete disconnection from all eligible sources, unknown membership or control that could permit a source, and unknown route/seam that could permit a path. Expect respectively subject true, false, unknown and unknown. Pair a separately known-false row predicate with an unknown trace: row no-match while internal trace remains unknown. Unknown in a row without such false evidence stays unknown.
- Exercise friendly, hostile and unknown control independently of endpoint obstruction, membership and inventory, including an unknown non-chart hex and initial or arriving stock without proof of present control or route. Compare no stock or no actual shipment under the selected endpoint rule with a stock- or shipment-required alternative; route expectations require the independently accepted route contract.
- Compare two individually feasible traces competing for one truck, an allocation sufficient for both and unknown capacity. These are not tests of the non-consuming selection's shared feasibility: a shipment alternative must supply joint proof and return unknown when unresolved capacity could change its result. Test an apparent clear route with unknown incoming ZOC, a fully certified alternate witness and no witness with an unknown boundary; expect unknown, true and unknown respectively under accepted route rules.
- Record stable subject, chosen source and route witness internally; check duplicate evaluation, restore and replay. Before exposing offers, verdict availability, rejection categories, event counts or IDs, timing, retries, terminalization, freeze, later actions or downstream effects, define authorized player and intelligence-plane projections and compare paired histories with identical authorized observations but different hidden control, routes or units. Include whether and when observable evaluation or terminal behavior changes as evidence becomes available. Filter evidence and outcomes; if authorized disclosure cannot accommodate those differences, keep externally observable evaluation and terminal behavior independent of hidden inputs. A generic rejection or witness redaction alone is insufficient.

No runtime or test changes; no source-backed gameplay or release claim.
