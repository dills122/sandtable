`f000351177e0e482eb2799fad6e6e2581b400c9d80d4fe365436168c5eeed78b`.
In that crop the D3419/D3420 shared vertical side begins near `(326, 480)`;
the blue cross-tied railway crosses lower down. The two dark road borders
approach that upper vertex: the upper border merges with the vertex ink,
while the lower border meets the vertical grid below it, near `(326, 525)`.
Interpreting the strip between those borders as the road gives a centerline
intersection below the vertex and therefore a **candidate road crossing** of
D3419/D3420. Ink width, overprint, and scan resolution prevent a precise
independent location of the upper border at the junction.

The competing reading assigns the road through the adjacent side at the
three-hex vertex, or treats its side ownership as indeterminate because the
grid and road artwork merge there. The lower border by itself cannot exclude
that reading: a wide symbol touching two sides does not establish the game's
vertex convention. The [earlier batch review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-east-rail-batch-review.md)
and [extension review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-east-extension-review.md)

tie-break was verified in this check; the geometric midpoint is an inference,
not a printed mark or official ruling.
