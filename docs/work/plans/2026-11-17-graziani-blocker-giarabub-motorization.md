# Giarabub motor-machine-gun: source recheck

**Finding:** The source prints a chart CPA of **8+**, not an absent CPA; `+` denotes historical motorization and a CPA change when motorized. It also identifies a motorized machine-gun unit. The cited setup does not enumerate first-line trucks at Giarabub or explicitly allocate this unit a motor pool or current motorized CPA. Preserve that unresolved assignment; do not interpret it as a proved zero, a ban on assigning trucks, or absence of all relevant numeric facts.

The [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-giarabub-motorization-review.md)
confirms this limited conclusion while preserving available general truck
pools and separately permitted later attachment.

## Source evidence

- [Scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §60.31: `Giaribub (C1014): Giaribub Garrison` has no `Trucks:` quantity on that row, unlike several other deployment rows. PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, §59.42 and §59.63A, say listed first-line trucks are assigned within their listed hex, and each medium or heavy first-line truck gives one initial attached motorization point in the abstract logistics game. Neither case directly gives this battalion an individual point count. §59.64 permits changing initial distributions after game start subject to its exception.
- Scenario §60.33 separately provides second/third-line trucks, including **30 light, 100 medium and 25 heavy anywhere in Libya**. This is a player-distributed pool, not an explicit initial first-line assignment to this unit. Section 59.63B distinguishes these as unattached motorization points. Its existence precludes an unqualified claim that no trucks exist in or could be placed at Giarabub; no specific present attachment or effective CPA follows from its existence alone.
- [Axis charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 14, list Giarabub Oasis Mtr MG Bn with counter abbreviation `Grbub`, ID `hh`, TOE `N`, arrival `D`, under the C1014 garrison, morale 0. PDF p. 4 key defines `Mtr` as motorized, `N` as maximum listed TOE for a non-tank/non-gun unit and `D` as deployed at first stage. PDF p. 26, §4.46b, gives `hh` **CPA 8+**, close-assault offense/defense **5/7**, maximum TOE **1**. The `N` key and `hh` row therefore support an initial one TOE strength point. The p. 27 key states `+` means historically supplied enough trucks to motorize all components, and only CPA changes while motorized. It specifies neither a truck count nor the changed CPA by itself.
- [Air booklet](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 44 / printed p. 40, counter section 2: Giarabub's garrison motor-MG counter shows `G`, battalion mark `II`, stacking value `0`, and the motorized machine-gun symbol. Compare [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 7–8, §4.22 and §4.23: the former labels that symbol, and the latter summarizes counters. Land §9.16a, p. 17, describes the home-garrison stacking exception. These markings do not print a truck allocation or current CPA; `0` is the stacking value, not zero trucks or zero CPA.
- Land §32.54, p. 44, requires assigning sufficient motorization points to all historically motorized infantry (`8+` and `10+`) before assigning them to other infantry-type units. This establishes a priority constraint, not a numerical source allocation or automatic proof that this unit currently has a specific pool. Read with §59.63's first-line/second-third-line distinction and initial setup rather than assuming a quantified attachment from `+` alone. Land §32.51 treats motorization points as medium trucks except as modified; exact current allowance remains dependent on a supported motorization state and relevant transport rules.

## Boundary

`unresolved-no-scenario-pool` is defensible only as shorthand for **no identified unit-specific initial pool in the reviewed setup**, not as a finding of no available pool or no printed rating. A policy could decide how to handle the source's priority and setup interaction; this review does not make that ruling, establish when or how second/third-line points attach, or change runtime data. Scope is the cited sources and related keys, not a claim that every edition or erratum was exhausted.
