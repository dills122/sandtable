# Fort Maddalena border side: movement and ZOC evidence

**Disposition:** source supports interpreting the blue-checkered C3019–C3020 side as the Libya–Egypt boundary, not identifying it as an escarpment, river or automatically impassable feature. The inspected terrain chart and §10.21 contain no explicit national-border movement cost or standalone ZOC prohibition. This is a bounded negative search and geographic interpretation, not proof that every applicable rule allows entry or projection. Keep the marked side and its unadmitted effect distinct from an independently verified clear side.

## Original map and scenario

[Map C](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 3, shows the Fort Maddelena dot in C3019 immediately west of C3020, with alternating blue blocks on their common vertical side. The [independent contact review](2026-11-17-graziani-map-fort-maddalena-contact-review.md) confirms the side, labels and local overprint. No broad dark escarpment band or blue major-river symbol is visible on this side, although its blue marking and endpoints mean it is not certified feature-free.

A broader Map C view places the same distinctive checkered line between `LIBYA` to its west and `EGYPT` to its east. The map key, PDF p. 1, depicts a gray `Border` line but does **not** visibly reproduce the blue checks; a generic hex border is another possible interpretation of the key sample. Thus the political-border identification and C3019 west/C3020 east membership are supported inferences, not a fully verified symbol legend or regional polygon. See [prior boundary research](2026-11-17-graziani-border-mark-next.md) and its [review](2026-11-17-graziani-border-mark-review.md).

[Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, explicitly places Fort Maddalena Garrison C3019 and 11th Hussars C3020. Its distinct **Anywhere in Libya** Axis placement category makes regional membership relevant to additional setup choices; an accepted C3019-as-Libya inference may support considering that hex for a listed unit, but stacking, identity, eligibility and all other restrictions remain. A boundary-adjacent hex is not automatically in both countries. Explicit fixed deployments do not establish whether optional forces were placed, or grant a move across the line. This is not an exhaustive scenario/setup restriction audit.

## Land rule and terrain chart comparison

[Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 18 §10.21, lists ZOC exceptions for all-sea, major-river and lake hexsides, escarpment hexsides, and adjacent hexes the projecting unit could not enter from its location. It does not name a national boundary as an independent exception. Section 10.21(c) is unit- and direction-specific: independently check each unit's actual entry restrictions, terrain, edge, current state and relevant rules. This is distinct from §10.11/.15's stack/strength qualifications and §§10.31–.32's combat obligations.

Original map PDF p. 1 **Terrain Effects Chart §8.37** separately lists terrain, road/track, ridge, slope, escarpment, wadi and river effects; no political/national-border row is visible in that chart. Land pp. 14–15 §§8.31–.37 and 8.41–.47 describe movement and terrain effects; §8.19 separately restricts Commonwealth land units west of Marble Arch, not a blanket statement that national boundaries are impassable. Neither chart omission nor these bounded passages prove no other scenario or unit-specific restriction exists. Confirm the exact identity and effect of every coincident feature and use the accepted terrain and unit contracts before resolving entry. The blue checkered mark alone is not evidence of an escarpment, water, road, or ZOC blockade.

[September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1, has MAPS and §8.37 entries but no explicit Fort Maddalena border rule identified in the four-page package during this bounded review. Its §8.37 track correction concerns a distinct track, and no claim is made that all later or indirect corrections were checked.

## Future policy and tests

A versioned project interpretation could treat the national-boundary annotation as imposing no **additional** ordinary movement or ZOC penalty while still applying all other restrictions. That would be an inference from the inspected rules, not an explicit source sentence or presently admitted policy. Retain alternatives and fail closed when effect, region, unit entry or edge coverage is unknown. Test both crossing/projection directions with eligible and ineligible units, independently varying the national mark, an actual coincident escarpment or other feature, unknown coverage, and scenario Libya-only placement. Test explicit fixed placement separately from voluntary extra setup and keep opposing side information safe. These are unexecuted synthetic tests, not proof of a legal starting stack or compulsory combat.
