# Graziani rail: friendly occupancy and enemy ZOC

**Disposition:** Defer admission. A provisional, versioned movement interpretation could apply §10.26 to rail. At the origin, count a qualifying combat traveler already present; at each entry, require a qualifying friendly combat unit already present before the traveler enters. This addresses the inclusive movement language and avoids circular entry, but neither precedence nor entry timing is an explicit rail ruling. The self-origin reading has unresolved implications for ordinary movement and contact; do not select it or enable behavior without resolving those implications and remaining admission decisions.

## Evidence and limits

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 15 §8.73, require a traveler to start the OpStage on a railroad hex, prohibit it from being in enemy ZOC, and prohibit entry into enemy ZOC **or** an enemy-occupied hex while traveling. The same case bars CP expenditure for another reason during the stage. Section 8.77 says no rail traveler has expended CP in that stage, without repeating “for another reason.” Reconcile both cases and their timing before admission: neither a complete post-rail CP ban nor permission for later expenditure is established here. Section 8.72 places Commonwealth rail in its rail phase; §5.2 places that phase in the active player's block.
- Same scan, p. 18 §10.26: presence of a friendly combat unit negates the *effect* of enemy ZOC for **all movement purposes**. Rail is movement, making application to rail textually strong; §8.73's unqualified rail-specific wording leaves competing precedence possible. Section 10.27 recognizes opposing and mutual ZOCs; negating a movement effect does not erase physical ZOC or enemy control. Section 10.23 explicitly cross-references §10.26 for retreat. These provisions do not expressly establish rail precedence.
- Section 10.29 specifically permits truck convoy entry into enemy ZOC when already occupied by a friendly combat unit. That wording supports checking pre-entry presence as an analogy; it does not make its timing a printed rail rule. Section 10.21 limits ZOC projection by terrain and entry eligibility. Test actual projection, not mere adjacency.
- [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), p. 1 §8.73, adds that rail travelers cannot earn reorganization points in that stage; §8.71 refers to Axis use under §54.4. No explicit §10.26 correction or rail ZOC exception was found in this four-page errata. This is not proof that no other clarification exists.
- This follow-up relies on prior visual source transcription in [rail ZOC analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-rail-zoc.md) and [independent rail scope review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-scope-review.md), rather than a new visual reinspection of the scanned Land pages. The subsequent [occupancy follow-up review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-occupancy-followup-review.md) independently checked the same scan and identified §8.77 CP and pickup/dropoff qualifications. Errata text rechecked November 17, 2026. Both Land analyses use the same edition, not independent editions or a publisher interpretation.

## Bounded policy candidate

1. Compute physical enemy ZOC and qualifying friendly combat presence from authoritative state. For movement purposes only, apply §10.26 when presence is established. Do not infer qualification from ownership alone: classification, including parenthesized-strength units and other special cases, needs source-backed admission. Supply counters and dummy counters are not automatically combat units.
2. At the origin check, the traveler is already present. Count it if it is a qualifying friendly combat unit; the wording says **a** friendly combat unit, with no express other-unit qualifier. A non-combat traveler needs a separate qualifying occupant. This choice means the origin ZOC prohibition has no practical effect on qualifying combat travelers, a consequence to accept explicitly rather than conceal.
The self-origin reading also bears on ordinary combat movement: §§10.23–.24 and 8.62–.65 describe controlled-hex movement, contact and break-off for units already present. Applying self-presence generally could undermine those effects; negating a movement effect does not itself erase ZOC or Contact markers. Resolve each consequence and entry timing independently. A rail-only reading does not explain that difference; reconcile the clauses and rail’s no-other-CP condition, or retain a fail-closed gate. See [independent policy review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-zoc-policy-review.md).

3. Before entry into each intermediate and destination hex, check for an independently present qualifying friendly combat unit. Do not count any traveler in the current rail action to make an otherwise prohibited entry legal under a conservative pre-action snapshot. Section 8.77 permits pickup and dropoff, so this snapshot may exclude otherwise possible sequences; define those semantics and the one-stack allowance first. This pre-entry rule is a policy choice, not a conclusion compelled by §10.26. An alternative checks state at each entry and could count a previously dropped qualifying traveler. Neither its sequencing nor a ban on counting that traveler is source-proven. Define atomicity, pickup/dropoff ordering, stacking, direction and the one-stack allowance before choosing either contract.
4. Continue to reject enemy-occupied hexes under the separate §8.73 prohibition, even if a qualifying friendly unit is also present. Do not waive stacking, destroyed or unbuilt track, §8.78's Axis-occupied rail restriction, eligibility, timing, capacity, or CP conditions. Evaluate construction and repair separately: §24.65 bars work in enemy-controlled or enemy-occupied railroad hexes, and the movement effect does not remove that control.
5. Certified absence of ZOC passes regardless of occupancy; certified qualifying presence passes this gate even if ZOC is unknown. Known ZOC with certified absence of qualifying presence fails; otherwise incomplete projection or occupancy yields unknown. Certified absence requires complete relevant occupant and classification coverage. Defer admission or use an explicitly defined safe result; player-visible route candidates, rejections and events must not reveal hidden enemy or qualifying-unit state. Section 10.16's Movement Segment disclosure does not automatically establish disclosure during the distinct rail phase.

A stricter interpretation gives §8.73 priority and bars all projected enemy ZOC, including friendly-occupied hexes. It is plausible but not expressly stated as precedence and conflicts with the inclusive wording of §10.26. Keep interpretation and version visible in any future ruleset and replay data; neither policy is an official erratum.

## Distinguishing acceptance vectors

Use synthetic certified rail paths and hold unrelated restrictions legal. Expected outcomes below are for the **candidate**, not source-established rulings or executed tests.

| Location and known state | Candidate result for ZOC check |
| --- | --- |
| Origin: qualifying combat traveler alone, enemy ZOC | Pass; traveler is already present. |
| Origin: non-combat traveler alone, enemy ZOC | Fail. |
| Origin: non-combat traveler and separate qualifying combat unit, enemy ZOC | Pass, subject to legal co-location. |
| Intermediate or destination: enemy ZOC, no pre-existing qualifying friendly combat unit | Fail for combat and non-combat travelers; traveler cannot qualify its own entry. |
| Intermediate or destination: enemy ZOC, qualifying friendly combat unit already present | Pass for either traveler, subject to other restrictions. |
| Any affected hex: friendly non-combat occupant only | Fail unless origin traveler independently qualifies. |
| Any affected hex: unknown projection or unknown qualifying presence | Do not treat as clear; exercise chosen safe outcome. |
| Enemy-occupied entry with qualifying friendly presence | Fail separate enemy-occupancy check. |

Compare ordinary movement/contact and break-off against each origin interpretation before selecting one. Cover certified no ZOC with unknown occupancy, known qualifying presence with unknown ZOC, incomplete occupant coverage, phased pickup/dropoff and both sides’ observable trajectories. Compare a previously dropped qualifying unit followed by a later traveler under both snapshot proposals, conditional on independently established action legality; test prior and later CP expenditure under whichever §8.73/§8.77 interpretation is accepted. Also cover blocked §10.21 projection, legal versus illegal stacking, multiple route hexes with mixed presence, §8.78 independent of ZOC, destroyed/unbuilt track and stage-wide CP. Test observations and failures under separately decided disclosure rules, plus deterministic replay and version preservation. Compare a strict §8.73-precedence fixture to show that the two policies differ. Do not manufacture co-location where the world model or source rules disallow it. These vectors do not prove any actual Graziani rail route reachable.
