# Review: §§46.4, 49.13 and 49.4 errata scope

**Result:** No material discrepancy found in the bounded source comparison. The note correctly retains uncertainty about applying detailed no-fuel consequences to Land-only abstract supply and does not treat the §46.4 erratum as a truck-loss correction.

- Air/Logistics p. 20 §49.13 computes vehicle fuel use by each five CP or fraction and its Note exempts CP spent without actual hex-to-hex movement; September errata p. 3 says the Note refers only to land units. Land p. 43 §32.0 applies when both Air and Logistics are omitted, replaces ordinary supply expenditure with its listed Ammo and Fuel costs, and §§32.23–.25 give abstract movement and patrol rules. Those passages support the source note's limited comparison; they do not themselves settle how the detailed no-fuel condition maps to abstract shortage.
- Errata §49.4 adds the stated assault restrictions, normal defense and two-column adjustment. That adjustment is expressly for defending **tank-type** units without fuel, not automatically every mechanized or recce unit. Air/Logistics p. 20 goes from §49.3 directly to §50.0, supporting the finding that §49.4 is an addition on that page. Land §32.15 removes a Supply Unit only when both Ammo and Fuel reach zero; §§32.16 and 32.21–.25 do not expressly resolve the mapping or all shortage consequences.
- Air p. 17 §46.0 describes aircraft-density adjustment and §46.4 locates the Flak Adjustment Chart. Errata §46.4 says that chart is wrong and the notes under §46.3 correct. Common chart p. 13 §46.3 says each target group is attacked separately and gives one right shift per additional multiple of 12 bomber/transport-class aircraft above 12; its example makes 24 aircraft a one-column shift. The reviewed note accurately describes that correction's scope and identifies that the §46.4 chart itself was not located. No comparison of chart cells or global crossreference absence follows.
