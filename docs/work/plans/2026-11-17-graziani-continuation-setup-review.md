# Independent review: Matruh continuation inference

**Disposition:** Retain D3714 as a conditional candidate, not a source-established location or adopted ruling. The erratum corrects starting assignment; the scenario key makes roster expansion plausible, but the source conflict and an omitted exception remain material. Preserve unknown location pending a documented precedence decision. No runtime or data admission follows from this review.

## Evidence and limits

- [Scenario §59.2](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 1 / printed p. 25, says initial forces are generally listed by hex and parent, with the OA chart supplying assigned subordinates. It specifies `Less`, `Assg`, and `Det` annotations and separate listing for detached subordinates in their own hexes. A corrected OA roster containing both battalions, no applicable exception, and normal parent expansion therefore give a coherent D3714 interpretation. These are premises, not independently established facts about each battalion's hex.
- [Scenario §60.41](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, gives D3714 for Matruh Garrison and explicitly lists 1 Essex, 1 Durham Light Infantry, and 1 South Staffordshires under `Att`. Neither Buffs nor Hampshire appears by name in the land deployment list. The 7th Armored row explicitly identifies its separately listed units as detached; this shows notation in use, not proof that every omission was intentional. `Det` denotes detachment, not necessarily a different hex: §59.2 also permits same-hex detachment at setup.
- [September 1979 errata §4.44b item 2](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1, expressly says both battalions start the campaign and Italian scenarios assigned to Matruh. Its heading covers OA, reinforcement tables and other order-of-battle information. It gives no hex, attachment, or precise replacement text for Matruh's OA entry. Treating this scenario-specific assignment as a correction to the OA roster used by §59.2 is reasonable, but remains a precedence interpretation.
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 10, says no units may be assigned to Matruh, though units may be attached; PDF p. 11 places 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshire in the unassigned infantry table, with `D` in their arrival column. Erratum conflicts with both unassigned status and the prohibition. A specific exception for these two assignments is necessary; do not silently generalize it to all Matruh assignments or eliminate either identity from the roster.
- §59.2, PDF p. 2, defines `Att` as subordinate units attached **but not assigned**. Therefore the three explicitly named units are scenario-stated attachments, not evidence that they are Matruh assignees. Counter parent designations serve identification and need not indicate current assignment. Preserve assignment, attachment, location and identity separately.
- [Land §4.25](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 9, defaults assigned units beginning in their parent's hex to attached unless specifically stated otherwise. It does not place units in that hex. §59.2 permits free same-hex setup organization within formation and stacking limits. The proposed sequence of a default baseline followed by an owner choice is a useful model, but neither passage expressly establishes that temporal ordering; validate final attachment legality and capacity separately.

## Material alternatives

1. Interpret the erratum as correcting assignment only, while an omitted scenario detachment or placement remains unresolved. Both units can remain assigned without being colocated or attached; no alternate hex is established by these sources.
2. Apply corrected-OA expansion to D3714, then apply §4.25 only if no specific exception applies. Same-hex setup detachment is an additional legal-choice question, not proof of another starting hex.
3. Resolve the OA prohibition by a narrow two-unit exception, or select another explicit source-precedence ruling. The printed prohibition cannot simply coexist literally with the erratum's starting assignments.

Each alternative needs separate capacity and scenario-setup review. No claim that a particular detached placement exists, that six attachments are legal, or that an official clarification settled these conflicts.

## Review scope

Visually checked scenario PDF pp. 1–3, Commonwealth OA PDF pp. 10–11, and Land §4.25; read errata PDF p. 1. Source scans are the same editions referenced by the proposal, not independent corroboration. No exhaustive later-errata search, gameplay test, code or data change.
