# Review: warship barrage identification

**Disposition:** The [source comparison](2026-11-18-graziani-warship-barrage-disclosure.md) is supported within its stated scope. No material source or audience error found. The addendum's identification status does not independently establish ship presence, a legal attack, or an opponent-facing fleet manifest.

- [Land addendum §12.23](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 45, says Commonwealth warships are always considered historically identified targets. Land §12.22 p. 20 separately permits Commonwealth warships as barrage targets; §§12.23–.24 describe known-unit designation versus blind type/temporary-number targeting. The addendum gives no ship location, condition or named recipient. The opposing player's adjacent-hex battalion/company count and Gun/Armor/Infantry classification in the main §12.23 do not explicitly define a warship manifest.
- Land §12.0 p. 20 states that at barrage resolution the barraging player states the target and Actual Barrage Points directed there. This follows the separate secret planning described in §11.0; the former does not itself expose every source unit or ship.
- Land §30.35 p. 42 permits artillery with Vulnerability Rating 6 or more and emplaced guns to fire at enemy ships in the artillery's **same hex**, noting ship fire separately from air bombardment. It uses half total raw artillery points as Bomb Points, with emplaced guns exempt from the halving. The adjacent-hex §12.23 report and same-hex ship procedure are differently worded; their complete interaction is not expressly settled by these passages. No co-location, availability, attack or result follows from the note's conditional example.
- Land §§32.71–.75 p. 44 describe a separate abstract Axis attack against ships in Alexandria Harbor, including secret plotting, named execution segment and section-E storm restriction. They do not expressly refer to §12.23's target-selection procedure. Section 32.0 p. 43 applies to Land without both Air and Logistics; neither mode nor target eligibility is decided by the identification addendum alone.
