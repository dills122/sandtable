# 1 RTR reunion map: independent review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Disposition:** Conditional 25-transition lower bound supported; no source-verified route, movement cost, or stage-one reachability follows. No material correction found in the reviewed [map note](2026-11-18-graziani-first-rtr-reunion-map.md).

- **Initial positions and relationship.** Original [scenario §60.41](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3, explicitly places 1 RTR at C3520 and 7th Armored Division at D3612, detaching its units listed above. The [Commonwealth organization chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 6, lists 1 RTR beneath 7th Armored Brigade HQ. D3612 is the division's stated location, not a separate explicit brigade location; applying it to the brigade requires the noted default hierarchy and actual snapshot assumptions.
- **Arithmetic and two movers.** Under the hypothesized +33 column translation, C3520 has global column 20 and D3612 column 45. For ordinary adjacent steps, each step changes global column by at most one. For meeting column `m`, the two step counts sum to at least `|m − 20| + |45 − m| ≥ 25`. Integer rounding means at least one party must make 13 or more steps; it does not require each to make 13, nor establish that a 12/13 split is legal. This bound holds for any common meeting hex under those assumptions, not just travel to one endpoint. It does not cover non-adjacent transport or prove a traversable path.
- **Registration and physical map.** Original [Land §4.1](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 7, prints `01xx` over preceding `39xx`; it does not certify the hypothesized +33 transform. Endpoint crops from the original [map scan](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF pp. 3–4, show the C3520 label and dashed crossings, and D3612's overprinted features. These crops do not prove seam registration or interior crossings. The bound is therefore conditional on the stated candidate, including its adjoining-hex representation across the seam.
- **Attachment.** Original Land §19.41, PDF p. 29, permits an assigned but unattached unit to attach to its parent at any time when both are in the same hex, for one CP each; its note covers either party moving into the other's hex and attaching immediately. Assignment, attachment, movement legality and current locations are separate conditions. This rule supplies neither movement permission nor a cost per transition. Section 19.42 addresses non-assigned attachment separately.
