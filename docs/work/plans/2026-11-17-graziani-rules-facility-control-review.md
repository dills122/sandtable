# Tobruk repair facility: source review

**Disposition:** Confirm the companion [facility-control note](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-facility-control.md)'s source readings and unresolved temporary-versus-major conflict. The reviewed sources do not specify a conversion, facility duplication, or complete destruction lifecycle. Two additional construction clauses merit explicit treatment in any later policy: §§24.85 and 24.87 distinguish placement, building, rebuilding and dismantling.

## Evidence

Read [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) PDF/printed pp. 18, 34–35 and 37, and all four pages of [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf). - Land `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`.

Scenario §60.33 specifies a major Italian facility at Tripoli and temporary ones at Tobruk and Benghazi; §60.31 lists Italian units at Tobruk. Land §22.31 lists major facilities including Tobruk for whoever controls it, as well as Tripoli, Alexandria and all Cairo hexes. The scenario and general designation conflict at Tobruk unless an additional interpretation is chosen. Section 22.32 requires the broken-down vehicle or destroyed tank to begin the Maintenance Segment at a facility and not have been towed that Operations Stage.

Land §24.81 allows only temporary facilities to be newly constructed and states major facilities already exist. Both types may have effectiveness reduced or neutralized by bombing or barrage. Temporary facilities **may** be destroyed by enemy-unit presence; major facilities may not be destroyed **in that manner**. It specifies neither automatic versus discretionary action nor the owner-relative and timing boundaries. It supplies no express conversion or replacement instruction. Land §10.27 separately allows a hex to be controlled by both players when opposing ZOCs enter it; enemy presence and a change of exclusive control are distinct concepts.

Section 22.34 uses temporary and major columns for broken-down-vehicle repairs. Sections 22.36–.37 distinguish weather and enemy ZOC by facility type, including the major-city exception for a temporary facility. Section 22.13(a)'s enemy-controlled-hex prohibition and major-facility ZOC exception require reconciliation with §22.37 before use. Section 22.13(d) independently exempts major facilities from its rainstorm/sandstorm ban. These are facility repair conditions, not the separate port-efficiency question.

Additional clauses: §24.85 permits a temporary facility in any major-city hex or village and limits each player to two temporary facilities. This supports examining whether coexistence is permissible in principle, but does not say Tobruk initially contains both types or decide whether scenario §60.33 overrides §22.31. Section 24.87 prohibits new temporary construction in an enemy-controlled hex but allows all repair facilities to be rebuilt or dismantled there. Its interaction with §24.81 presence and §22.13 use is a separate issue; construction permission alone does not establish repair eligibility. Sections 24.83–.84 specify distinct construction and rebuilding costs and timing, which may require abstraction decisions.

Errata p. 2 corrects references at §§22.34 and 22.8. No explicit §22.31, §24.81 or §60.33 correction found in the four-page package; other material or indirect interactions may exist.

## Limits

This source review followed the companion note and uses the same scans, so it is neither blind nor an independent edition. Hashes do not establish rights. Source facts support keeping identity, type, presence, control, damage and lifecycle distinct, but do not choose a post-control policy or establish six-turn reachability. Test any accepted policy and its ruleset identity separately. No runtime changes or gameplay tests.
