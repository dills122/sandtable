# Land-only anti-air lifecycle, GT1–6

## Printed categories and timing

Land §§32.81–.84 (pp. 44–45) state removal **from play** without restricting the general commands to scenario initialization. The text explicitly says captured Axis anti-air TOE Strength Points are **immediately** removed when captured by Commonwealth. The other removal clauses do not explicitly state an event hook for later scheduled arrivals, captured Commonwealth points, or recreation. A continuous ineligibility filter is a plausible reading of “from play”; a one-time setup pass is another implementation interpretation, not an express source instruction. Do not represent either as a printed clarification.

| Category | Printed Land-only treatment | Later lifecycle limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commonwealth AA units; light/heavy AA TOE wherever assigned; AA replacement points | All removed from play, including replacement points (§32.81). Captured Axis AA TOE immediately removed. | Explicit captured-Axis rule is continuous. Later Commonwealth AA unit/point arrival is not separately timed in text. Scenario §60.47 says no Commonwealth replacement points arrive before November, irrespective of AA, subject to any applicable explicit exception. |
| German AA units ID `z` or `bb`; assigned light AA TOE; light AA replacement | Remove specified units and light categories (§32.82). Heavy AA TOE and heavy replacement unaffected. | No GT1–6 whole-unit entries in ordinary Axis track. No explicit captured-Commonwealth-AA or future-arrival hook supplied by this clause. |
| Italian AA units ID `ss`, `tt`, `uu` | Remove units (§32.83(1)). | Applies to named source classes, not every counter with an AA rating. Whether future arrivals must be filtered is implicit. |
| Italian emplaced gun ID `pp` | Remove its assigned AA TOE points (§32.83(2)). | Remove the AA points, not its unrelated guns or whole unit. |
| Italian artillery ID `kk` | Lose assigned **heavy** AA TOE points (§32.83(3)). | Light AA on these units is not removed by this clause; do not eliminate mixed artillery. |
| Italian heavy AA replacements | All lost (§32.83(4), naming 75 mm and 90 mm). | No GT1–6 planning from the ordinary heavy-AA production row anyway. |
| Italian light AA replacements | May be brought in at rate **one per month**, “effectively halving” available points (§32.83(5)). | An upper limit, not an automatic entitlement or arrival; production, planning, convoy and loss rules remain gates. Month and counting event need a policy. |
| Non-AA TOE or replacement points with an AA rating | Explicitly unaffected by these AA modifications (§32.84). | Preserve their actual type. Neither AA combat rating nor a counter's mixed payload alone makes every point AA. |

Scenario §60.41 lists Commonwealth 9th Heavy AA, 15th Light AA and 57th Light AA for placement anywhere on D/E; §32.81 covers those units. Scenario starting Italian positions and errata-derived mixed artillery require point- and unit-specific handling, not a blanket deletion of every AA-bearing formation. Distinguish previously source-recorded composition from admitted playable state.

## Replacement chronology and month boundary

Axis §20.66 Italian production chart (PDF p. 33) prints Light AA `15 / 2 / 2…24 / 6`, then `45 / 1 / 25` in Number/Max/Dates/Tonnage columns. Its key describes first planning turn and two-game-turn delay to arrival, while describing Max as “planned/brought in.” Under the ordinary schedule, earliest planning is **GT2**, earliest arrival **GT4**; actual plan, eligible lane, capacity and successful arrival are separate. Chart total is not a six-turn entitlement. The §32.83 one-per-month limitation conflicts with incompatible unmodified two-per-turn use; it does not say grant one automatically. Its “effectively halving” parenthetical also needs reconciliation with the chart's per-turn maximum; see the [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-aa-lifecycle-review.md).

The printed map turn track places GT1–2 in September III–IV and GT3–6 in October I–IV. Scenario starts GT1 OpStage 1 and ends GT6 OpStage 3 (§60.22). For an ordinary two-turn lead:

| Planning turn | Planning month | Nominal arrival | Arrival month | Within scenario? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GT1 | September | GT3 | October | No ordinary Light AA plan: production starts GT2. |
| GT2 | September | GT4 | October | Yes, conditional on actual valid plan and convoy. |
| GT3 | October | GT5 | October | Yes, conditional. |
| GT4 | October | GT6 | October | Yes, conditional. |
| GT5 | October | GT7 | November | After scenario. |
| GT6 | October | GT8 | November | After scenario. |

The two relevant readings differ at the boundary. If **brought in** means receipt month, GT4–6 collectively admit at most **one October receipt**. If it is counted against **planning** month under the production chart's “planned/brought in” shorthand, one GT2 September plan and one GT3 or GT4 October plan could notionally produce **two October arrivals**, subject to all other conditions. The print does not expressly settle which event debits the §32.83 cap, whether attempted but sunk shipment consumes it, or whether unused monthly allowance carries over. No extra allowance, roll, receipt or loss follows from this table. Preserve actual pre-scenario plans and alternative interpretations rather than silently assuming either ruling.

## Six-turn reinforcements and capture

Axis land reinforcement schedule §4.43b starts at GT9 OpStage 2; no ordinary listed Axis land reinforcement arrives GT1–6. This does not eliminate planned replacement shipments or scenario exceptions. Commonwealth §4.43a lists land arrivals at GT1/3, 2/3, 3/3, 4/3, 5/1 and 6/3; their listed names are Polish Brigade; 2 RTR, 7 RTR, 6 NZ Field Artillery; 1 Field Artillery; 3 Hussars; 7 Indian Brigade; 6 NZ Brigade. The schedule does not list an expressly named AA unit in those rows. Inspect constituent OA and any separately corrected source before claiming every arrival is AA-free. The French Motor Marine company is a corrected starting unit, not a seventh ordinary arrival.

Capture is directional: §32.81 expressly removes captured **Axis** AA TOE immediately on Commonwealth capture. It does not explicitly state the reverse for Axis capture of Commonwealth AA, and §§32.82–.83 distinguish German from Italian categories. If an attempted Axis capture concerns an already removed Commonwealth point, establish the legal predecessor state; do not fabricate one or generalize the captured-Axis clause. Scenarios involving later reintroduction are policy tests, not claims a historical capture occurred.

## Distinguishing tests for a future versioned ruling

1. Setup: remove the three named Commonwealth AA counters; remove Italian `ss`/`tt`/`uu`, `pp` AA points and `kk` heavy AA while retaining `kk` light AA and unrelated artillery. Apply German heavy and light cases separately; non-AA points with AA ratings survive §32.84.
2. A future otherwise scheduled prohibited AA unit or point: compare one-time-filter and continuous-filter policies explicitly. Require exact source type and timing; never silently accept either as a quoted rule.
3. Commonwealth capture of Axis AA TOE removes it immediately; reverse-direction capture remains unresolved by this explicit sentence. Do not remove captured non-AA points merely for AA rating.
4. GT1 plan rejected under ordinary Italian Light AA production; GT2 plan cannot become ordinary GT3 arrival. GT2-to-GT4 and GT3-to-GT5 exercise September/October boundary under each month interpretation, including two receipts in one month as rejection only under receipt-month policy. No automatic receipt on either date.
5. Decide whether the cap counts planning, shipment, an attempt, a lost
   convoy or receipt; the source does not settle that event. Include replay,
   failed convoy, unused September capacity and no rollover as separate
   undecided vectors. October limit must not affect German heavy or non-AA-rated
   points.
