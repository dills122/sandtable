# Graziani startup sequence: independent source review

17 November 2026. **Disposition: supported with two clarifications.** Direct visual reading of the cited scenario and sequence outlines supports the continuation's distinctions and its refusal to declare an official preamble ruling. No runtime, data, or readiness change authorized by this review.

## Findings

1. **Treat direct OpStage entry as the literal scenario start; label a full preamble an explicit variant.** Scenario §60.22 says Graziani starts with Game-Turn 1, OpStage 1. Land §5.2 and Air §33.0 place earlier stages before that point in a normal turn; neither cited outline expressly states how to reconstruct or handle them when a scenario begins there. §60.37 independently requires Italian convoy runs for the remainder of September to be planned before starting, using only lanes 2, 3 and 6. It does not say to execute a complete Naval Convoy Stage or establish arrivals. The plan's conditional framing is appropriate, but its opening description of an ambiguity should not imply that replaying the full preamble is equally explicit in the scenario text. A full preamble is a chosen startup policy requiring an independent state boundary and must not be presented as the printed start.
2. **Make mode-specific ordering tests cover the differences inside and after OpStages.** Land §5.2 places Tactical Shipping in the pre-OpStage Naval Convoy Stage; Air §33.0 instead places a Tactical Shipping segment in the Organization Phase, and has a Strategic Air Recovery Stage after OpStage 3. Air also inserts Land Support Air after Fleet and includes tactical aircraft maintenance there, before the player-specific land phases. The continuation correctly identifies the preamble difference but cites only Air p. 3 and its suggested exact-order test does not explicitly mention recovery or the changed tactical-shipping location. Include Air p. 4 and these boundaries when specifying a complete Land/Air turn; do not transplant the Land-only shipping order into Air mode.

## Confirmed details

- Scenario §59.36 bars maintenance on planes during the first OpStage of a scenario. §60.32 separately says the Italian player may not **attempt** to refit planes until GT1 OpStage 2. §59.32 starts all planes fueled and armed, including planes not refitted; that does not make every aircraft ready. These restrictions concern aircraft, not the in-stage fleet and vehicle repair phases. Eligibility and other restrictions still govern repair.
- Scenario §60.6 gives the Italian player initiative for the entire first Game-Turn. Both outlines then place Initiative Declaration before Weather in each OpStage. Land §5.2 places Organization, Naval Convoy Arrival, Fleet, and then player A's phases, including Repair, followed by player B's corresponding phases. Air §33.0 retains these broad boundaries but adds air-specific work and shifts phase lettering.
- Scenario §59.2 describes setup as forces, supplies and trucks available at scenario start and permits same-hex organization within stated limits. It does not establish whether hypothetical preamble consequences are baked into that state. Neither setup nor preplanning alone proves that a convoy, raid, loss, delivery, or earlier maintenance occurred.
