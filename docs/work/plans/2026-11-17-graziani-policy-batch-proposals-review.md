# Graziani policy batch: independent critique

**Disposition, November 17, 2026:** The three proposals are internally coherent as bounded planning choices, not source-established rules or executable contracts. Select the convoy and rail choices only with explicit acceptance of their outcome-changing consequences below; neither consequence is resolved by the source reviews. The victory recommendation can retain the already selected RUL-GRA-07 and RUL-GRA-22 and add the stated proof discipline, without selecting a route algorithm or publication protocol. No gameplay tests, reachability proof, runtime change or readiness change resulted from this review.

## Findings

1. **Convoy 5% is non-monotonic by construction; selection requires a design judgment.** For one Supply Unit and one Motorization Point, the proposed per-group ceiling loses both at 5%, while the described Land 10% branch excludes supply and rounds that motorization fraction down. This is a material gameplay consequence, not evidence that the 5% rule should behave that way. The proposal accurately exposes it and preserves the 10% and 20%-or-greater branches, including 25%; those qualifications make it reviewable, but do not make it historically established or establish that the synthetic load is legal. If monotonic loss severity is a required project property, do not select this policy: revise the hierarchy and compare affected branches explicitly. Otherwise an informed planning selection is coherent, subject to the listed admission gates and separate allocation decision. A floor, no-loss or altered grouping policy also needs an explicit choice; source silence alone does not select one.

2. **Rail origin self-presence effectively removes the starting ZOC restriction for combat travelers under this interpretation.** The selected broad reading combines §10.26's friendly-combat presence with §8.73, and the traveler is already present at origin. Thus every qualifying combat traveler can satisfy the proposed ZOC exception itself there, provided all other rail conditions hold. This is materially different from requiring another friendly combat unit and from treating §8.73 as a rail-specific prohibition. It is a defensible literal planning choice, not an explicit source ruling. The separate-occupant-before-entry rule avoids circular entry but is an additional project timing choice; §10.29's truck-convoy language does not establish it for rail. Selection is safe for planning only if both asymmetries are consciously accepted and retained in distinguishing tests; enemy occupation, construction/control, stage-wide CP and disclosure remain separate gates.

3. **Victory proof semantics do not close publication or route questions.** A certified witness for an existential predicate suffices even with unrelated unknown candidates; a negative result requires certified exhaustion of eligible candidates and continuations, unless a different required conjunct is already false. This is consistent with RUL-GRA-07's false-dominates-conjunction and unknown-row composition. RUL-GRA-22's same-holder, one-city-hex interpretation remains limited to Italian strategic victory; do not transfer its parenthesis or unit requirement to Commonwealth rows. History in a witness must be evidence contained in, or referenced by, the same immutable terminal snapshot, not an inference that taking and holding happened only at terminal time. Retaining these choices for planning is coherent, but does not authorize a verdict: conditional publication can leak hidden completion even with identical pending text or a fixed deadline. Audience authorization or an observation-independent protocol remains an admission blocker.
