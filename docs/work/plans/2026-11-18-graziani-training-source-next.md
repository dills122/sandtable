# Training instructor strength: cross-note source discrepancy

**Result:** Original Land §17.35 states an instructor battalion must be at least **one-third** of its assigned TOE strength, not one-half. This agrees with the earlier [replacement-training follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-replacement-training-followup.md). The later [scenario follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-convoy-scenario-next.md) and its [review](2026-11-18-graziani-convoy-scenario-next-review.md) originally misstated one-half and are now corrected. This targeted cross-check identifies a documentation discrepancy, not an official amendment or a new replacement-training policy.

Visually checked [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 28 §17.35. It requires an eligible combat battalion of designated Basic Morale in the trainee's hex during each training Operations Stage, and states at least **⅓** of assigned TOE strength for instruction battalions. It also gives type conditions, exempts instructional battalions from stacking limitations, and allows each to train as many units as may be stacked in the hex. For an illustrative instructor with six assigned and two qualifying actual TOE points, 2/6 meets the printed one-third threshold but fails a one-half threshold. This conditional arithmetic does not prove such a battalion exists, that all six points are of the correct type, or that any other instructor requirement is satisfied.

[Scenario §60.41](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, lists the 6th Australian Division in training at Cairo and/or Helwan, with the printed exclusions. That does not identify an eligible instructor or prove co-location, strength, morale or credited training. For **replacement points**, §20.43's cross-reference to §17.3 and its distinction from Commonwealth morale training leave the applicability of instructor and capacity provisions to individual replacement classes unresolved, as the earlier follow-up explains. The scenario's pre-November Commonwealth replacement-arrival ban does not itself establish the absence of initial points or all training; the [six-turn exclusion analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-six-turn-exclusions-batch.md) already records that boundary.
