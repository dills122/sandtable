## Direct source recheck

- Scenario p. 27 places Matruh Garrison at D3714 with three named attachments;
  the errata p. 1 assigns 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshires to that garrison, while
  Commonwealth OA p. 10 says no units may be assigned. Those statements are
  genuinely different. The OA's six-attachment limit is not itself an answer
  to whether exceptional assignees consume slots.
- The Commonwealth reinforcement track p. 3 lists the six turn/stage pairs as
  1/3, 2/3, 3/3, 4/3, 5/1 and 6/3. It specifies 5 light and 20 medium trucks
  at 5/1, and 25 light, 85 medium and 5 heavy at 6/3. Blank truck entries are
  not proof of zero. The OA sheets on pp. 11, 15, 18 and 21 were rechecked at
  group level against the track: the listed 6th NZ brigade HQ and three
  battalions have `3/6`; its field artillery has `3/2`.
- Scenario p. 27 directs victory to §60.71, while the next page section has a
  heading for the named scenario at §60.81. This check supports a correction
  of the pointer, not a decision about winner precedence or supply routes.
- Scenario p. 28 includes the Matruh temporary facility in §60.44; p. 29's
  Land-only replacement identifies §60.44 by number under a supply heading.
  The scope conflict is real: the numbered-case reading and supply-heading
  reading have different results.

The 21 candidate OA member rows were also checked against those four page images
for counter abbreviation, ID code, TOE marker and arrival marker: the 84
recorded field values agree with this same-reader check. These are OA rows, not
proof that every counter or its relationship is correctly admitted. This reread
does not independently verify them, source rights, setup choices, map geometry or
the complete six-turn arrival surface. No blank second-reading file is filled by
this review.

### Repair availability follow-up

Same-reader visual recheck of Land printed pp. 34 and 43 confirms §22.13(b)
uses an availability condition separately from the repair cost cases, while
§32.11 replaces ordinary expenditure and says its following supply rules apply.
A further implementation constraint is visible in §32.16: its supply-unit draw
rule is written for a friendly combat unit within range. The repair subject may
instead be a broken-vehicle marker, so that draw rule alone does not establish a
repair-availability test for every eligible subject. Do not silently substitute
movement/combat supply reach for §22.13(b). The availability decision and its
facility/marker cases remain open pending a separately reviewed ruling.

### Land-and-Air supply cross-reference

Same-reader visual check of scenario printed pp. 28–29 found another pointer
conflict: §60.93(B–C), headed Land & Air Games, says initial supply is as in
§60.82(B–C), then adds supply units at air facilities. The printed §60.82 is the
Italian Campaign victory section and has no matching supply subsections;
§60.92(B–C) immediately preceding §60.93 contains the respective Axis and
Commonwealth initial supply tables. A plausible correction is §60.92(B–C), but
it remains a working inference requiring independent review and errata search.
The additional air-facility units belong to §60.93's Land-and-Air mode and must
not be silently added to the Land-only §60.92 setup. This does not resolve the
separate §60.44 temporary-repair-facility replacement conflict.
The extracted errata text had no match for §§60.82, 60.92 or 60.93; this is a
search result, not proof that an image-only correction or other edition has none.
