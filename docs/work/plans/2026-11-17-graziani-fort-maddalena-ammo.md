# Fort Maddalena garrison: initial ammunition evidence

**Disposition:** the original scenario does not specify ammunition held at C3019 or an initial truck allocation for the Fort Maddalena garrison. Land-only setup offers conditional ways for the player to place or carry supplies, but no inspected source fixes an owner choice, a valid supply path, or ammunition availability for the garrison. The [independent source review](2026-11-17-graziani-fort-maddalena-ammo-review.md) corroborates these limits and the unrounded draw calculation below. Do not infer either supplied or unsupplied, or an initial ZOC, from the location and ratings alone.

## Source facts

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.31, lists Fort Maddalena Garrison at C3019 with no truck quantity on that line. [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 14, identifies FtMad as `q`, `U@2`, `D`; §4.46b p. 26 gives `q` maximum TOE 3, barrage 6 and close assault 3/3. These are source baseline and ratings, not an ammunition allocation. Scenario §59.42 p. 26 assigns listed first-line trucks to units in the **same hex**; §59.63(A) converts initial medium and heavy first-line trucks to attached motorization points hex by hex in the abstract profile. The absence of a truck figure on FtMad's own row does not prove no other eligible starting unit or owner-selected co-location could supply an attached point; neither is established.
- In Land-only, scenario §60.92(B), PDF pp. 4–5 / printed pp. 28–29, replaces the detailed §60.34 initial supply status with listed Supply Units, including two groups of three at Dump 1 and Dump 2. Each group is placed in one Map C hex in Libya **not within four hexes of a Commonwealth unit**; the separate dummy groups follow those placement rules. The fixed Commonwealth 11th Hussars starts at C3020, adjacent to C3019, so C3019 itself cannot host either *scenario Dump 1 or Dump 2 group* under that printed distance restriction. This is not an exclusion of all sources, all other hexes within draw range, later moves, additional setup supply units, or a determination of legal initial placement elsewhere. The printed distance wording's boundary and actual complete setup still need their own contract.
- Scenario §60.33 p. 27 provides second/third-line truck points, including **100 medium and 50 heavy anywhere in Libya**, plus light trucks; it also lists a Tripoli allocation and air-facility allocation. Under §59.63(B) and §59.66(C), p. 26, qualifying unattached motorization is derived from second/third-line medium and heavy trucks; each complete group of 30 **other** unattached points in one hex may optionally start transporting an additional regular Supply Unit. Air-facility points are separate and ignored when Air is abstracted. Section 59.44 restricts on-map supply truck placement to a friendly combat unit, supply dump or major city/town/oasis, with its off-map qualification. A C3019 group is a **candidate**, not an established fact: it depends on accepted Libya membership, eligible points, allocation, grouping, friendly-combat-unit/placement interpretation, supply-unit representation, stacking and other setup restrictions. Section 60.92(B)'s four-hex constraint expressly applies to its two named dump groups; whether it also constrains additional §59.66(C) units needs an explicit hierarchy decision before calling such a placement legal. Scenario dump and dummy grants cannot silently be duplicated by this option.
- Section 59.66(A–B), p. 26, lets an **attached** point not initially transporting men or guns start with either one ammo or three fuel, additional to listed dump supplies. The owner must consume these initial carried supplies first, and the text restricts detachment while they remain. This option requires a proven eligible attached point; no point or loaded ammo follows just from the garrison's own truckless setup line. Actual allocation, transport duties, same-hex reorganization, mixed-resource exhaustion and the option's detachment conditions remain distinct gates.
- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 43 §§32.15–.16, describe regular Supply Units and allow a friendly combat unit to draw from a unit within **half its CPA**, measuring a medium-truck route except for non-motorized infantry and applying stated impassable-terrain/enemy-ZOC conditions. At the source-baseline `q` CPA of 15, the exact unrounded half is **7.5**, before any separately accepted integer rounding or threshold policy. It establishes neither a source placement nor a legal path or available ammunition. Exact unit movement category, source eligibility, direction, route, contents, depletion and interactions with carried initial cargo require accepted rules and state. A geometric radius or a named dump alone is not a certified usable draw. No expenditure or actual player choice is selected here.

## Relation to possible ZOC

Land §10.15 asks for raw defensive Close Assault Points in the hex; §12.16 p. 20 says a heavy-weapons unit with barrage capability whose guns are Back is treated entirely Back and cannot participate in anti-armor or Close Assault. Section 12.18 permits Forward artillery participation with necessary ratings and ammunition. How those combat-position and ammunition clauses apply to an earlier or standing **ZOC threshold** is unresolved; §10.15 does not itself explicitly specify a supply test, and the checked sources do not authorize assuming either that ammo is irrelevant or that lack of ammo always removes raw strength for ZOC. See [stack candidate review](2026-11-17-graziani-fort-maddalena-stack-candidate-review.md). Effective/printed stacking, co-located units, §10.21 entry and side coverage and timing remain separate. No definite supply or combat result follows from this note.

Proposed tests after decisions: fixed dump group at adjacent-to-Commonwealth C3019 versus a potentially qualifying more-distant hex; named dump versus optional §59.66(C) unit and its source hierarchy; zero versus eligible attached loaded point; source path blocked/unknown, exactly half-CPA boundary, mixed initial cargo and separately selected Forward/Back and ammo readings; actual versus merely allowed owner placements, replay and side-safe responses. These are synthetic, unexecuted vectors, not claims of a legal historical setup.
