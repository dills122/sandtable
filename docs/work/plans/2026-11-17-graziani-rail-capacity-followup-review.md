# Graziani rail stock and capacity: source review

**Disposition:** Scenario stock and conditional three-stage arithmetic are supported as stated. Retain stage-wide counting, cumulative boarding, and the on-map three-unit ceiling as unresolved policy choices. No reviewed passage certifies any proposed journey.

## Findings

1. Scenario §60.92(C), PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29, replaces §60.44 for Land-only play: five regular Supply Units at Mersa Matruh D3714, each with 40 ammunition and 60 fuel; Alexandria and Cairo each possess three with those contents. Dump 1's three regular units and the separately listed three dummies follow the map-D-west-of-Matruh placement instruction. Scenario §60.7, preceding page, says the railroad runs to and ends at Matruh initially. Neither setup fact establishes an actual journey, later counter availability, or route legality.
2. Land §§8.72 and 8.74–.75, pp. 15–16, state one Commonwealth rail movement per Operations Stage with one stack each direction and a maximum of two rail-carried stacking points. Section 8.75 points to §49.3 for supply maximums; it does not itself cite §32.34. Land §32.34, p. 43, supplies the separate Land-only rule: two real and/or dummy Supply Units in one direction as the stage's total load, with normal rail rules applying. The proposed combined ceiling and rejection of a third counter traveling oppositely are interpretation, not an explicit reconciliation with §8.72. Given that selected ceiling, moving five distinct counters requires at least three stages; `5 → 3 → 1 → 0` also assumes each selected movement succeeds and stock has no other changes.
3. Section 8.77 permits pickup and dropoff anywhere along a line, subject to stacking and directional limits and the condition that no unit using rail has expended CP in that Operations Stage. It does not expressly define whether the §32.34 total counts distinct units boarded over the stage or peak simultaneous load. The plan appropriately labels cumulative counting conservative and leaves ordering and capacity interpretation open.
4. Section 32.14, p. 43, gives Supply Units zero stacking value, then prints five in a Major City hex and three in any other type of hex **off-map**. Its text does not expressly establish three in an ordinary on-map non-major-city hex. Consequently Matruh's five do not alone establish a printed on-map excess. The proposed on-map cap and its §59.65 exception must remain conditional; compliance timing and transition semantics remain open, as the plan states. Do not infer unlimited ordinary on-map capacity from this passage either.
5. The opposite-direction non-supply vector correctly limits its conclusion to the supply cap: remaining stack, rail and route rules still apply. Initial endpoint wording and setup quantities do not prove path connectivity, control, eligibility, intermediate stops, receiving capacity, or a historically available trip. The plan preserves these as gates.
