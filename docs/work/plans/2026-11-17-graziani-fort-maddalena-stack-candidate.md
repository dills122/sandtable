# Fort Maddalena: conditional artillery co-location vectors

**Result:** One illustrative `FtMad + XXVI Corps Artillery` stack has enough *potential raw defense* for §10.15, but under Land §9.16(a)'s express Fort Maddalena home-garrison example its effective stacking total is only one, below §10.11's strictly-more-than-one test. A separate three-counter illustration, `FtMad + XXI Corps Artillery + 1/1AR`, has a conditional effective total of two stacking points and a nominal 12 raw defense. The three-counter case distinguishes a potential threshold-satisfying stack from the fixed scenario baseline; **it is not a proven legal setup or actual deployment**.

## Evidence and calculation

- [Scenario §60.31](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, fixes Fort Maddalena Garrison at C3019 and lists 1/1AR and XXI/XXVI Corps artillery among units placeable `Anywhere in Libya`. September errata §60.31 identifies XXI Corps artillery's duplicate mention; use a **single** XXI identity. The adjacent XXVI/4/1AR text has a separate punctuation issue; its interpretation is an additional gate for the XXVI example. Section 59.2 permits owner-selected organization of units already in the same starting hex, within applicable formation and stacking limits; it does not force attachment or place any optional counter in C3019.
- [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 14, gives FtMad `q`, `U@2`, `D`. Italian characteristics §4.46b p. 26 gives `q` offensive/defensive Close Assault 3/3: conditional garrison raw defense `2 × 3 = 6`. OA p. 16 gives 1/1AR three 75/27 guns and XXI Corps three 75/27, six 100/17 and three Light AA; it lists them deployed, with the raggruppamento battalions described as independent and no formal HQ. XXVI Corps has nine 75/27 and three Light AA. The counter sheet PDF p. 1 section 2 shows 1/1AR, XXI and XXVI with printed one-point values; FtMad prints `G`, `II`, `1`. Preserve identities, initial TOE, and any later admitted attachment or source correction.
- Italian tank/gun chart §4.48, Axis PDF p. 29, gives 75/27 Close Assault 1/1 and 100/17 1/0. Thus 1/1AR's three 75/27 provide a **nominal** 3 raw defensive points, and XXI's three 75/27 another 3; its six 100/17 provide zero. Do not count parenthesized Light AA as an unconditional contribution. Under the assumptions that the source baseline points are present, applicable, and counted in §10.15, `6 + 3 + 3 = 12`. Similarly XXVI's nine 75/27 give nominal 9, so `6 + 9 = 15`. Artillery positions, ammo, gun-class and §10.15 raw-strength semantics are independent gates. Section 12.11–.12 assigns artillery Forward/Back at the Combat Segment boundary; §12.18 makes Forward artillery participation conditional on ratings and ammunition. Section 12.16 also makes a heavy-weapons unit with barrage capability whose guns are Back entirely Back and unable to participate in anti-armor or close assault; §9.16(a) identifies Fort Maddalena as a heavy-weapons battalion. Thus the same unresolved position, ammo and timing questions apply to the garrison’s nominal 6 as well as the optional artillery. Combat-position rules do not themselves specify the timing or inclusion rule for a pre-combat ZOC calculation: neither automatically zero nor automatically include these terms. These calculations do not prove an actual ZOC threshold at setup.
- Land PDF/printed p. 17 §9.16(a) specifically illustrates Fort Maddalena's garrison with **zero** stacking points in its assigned village and one elsewhere. Applying that effective value to §10.11's hex aggregation yields `0 + 1 = 1` for the two-counter XXVI illustration, below the strictly greater-than-one threshold, even though its nominal raw total is 15. For the two separate artillery units, `0 + 1 + 1 = 2` exceeds it and is below the ordinary clear-hex limit shown by map Terrain Effects Chart §8.37, **if** that terrain, stacking interpretation and unit state are admitted. Land §9.24 separately says artillery units generally have one stacking point, subject to its stated exceptions. Whether §10.11 counts exactly these effective home-garrison values rather than another representation is an explicit interaction to verify. §10.15's raw threshold is separately at least ten; no calculation establishes terrain/entry or other projection predicates.
