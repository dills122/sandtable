Coast and yellow artwork near the upper junction do not establish other features or their side membership. The B/C join, cell alias, and cross-sheet continuation remain unproved.

This independently locates the scenario facility, not a printed landing-strip glyph, current control, capacity, or road route.
- Literal search of pinned [hexsides](../../../src/Cna.Core/Content/HistoricalMap/hexsides.v1.json) finds neither `B4933` nor `B4934`. Pinned [hexes](../../../src/Cna.Core/Content/HistoricalMap/hexes.v1.json) contain `B4933` as clear, village, coastal, but **no `B4934` record**. `C4901` occurs in the pin; that does not establish an alias or adjacency. Missing records do not certify empty terrain or prove a missing playable cell. Resolve endpoint identity and representation before considering a new side record. The files cite `vassal:CNAv2.1.0`; this comparison does not verify edition equivalence.

Road-positive is the only supported side-feature observation here; other membership remains unknown. A future version must preserve that distinction and existing v1 replay identity, or defer admission. One crossing does not establish connected road hexes or legal movement, supply, reachability, control, weather, occupancy, ZOC, or timing. Consumer and boundary review and focused tests remain prerequisites, not completed work.
