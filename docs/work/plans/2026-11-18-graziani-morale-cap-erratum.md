# Morale +4 erratum and Graziani training

**Result:** September errata §17.28's +4 is an **Axis assault adjustment involving Rommel**, not a higher training ceiling. The 6th Australian training entry does not make this exception reachable for that Commonwealth formation. This bounded check does not establish whether Rommel can enter or participate in an assault during Graziani; general +4 combat reachability remains unproved.

## Source and bounds

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 27 §§17.1, 17.21–.23 and .28: Basic Morale and assault-specific Adjusted Morale are distinct. §17.23 caps Adjusted Morale at +3/−3 but expressly excepts §17.28. Section 17.28 adds +1 to Axis units' Adjusted Morale when stacked with General Rommel at the instant of assault. [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 §17.28, clarifies that final Morale +4 is possible. In that context a pre-Rommel +3 becomes +4; the clarification does not raise Basic Morale, amend Commonwealth training, or establish an Axis event.
- Land pp. 27–28 §§17.31–.36: parenthesized OA rating is untrained Basic Morale; other rating is designated maximum. Each six Operations Stages of training raises untrained Basic Morale one point, never above designated Basic Morale. Training requires an eligible same-hex instructor each stage and is interrupted by CP expenditure; the rule makes training optional in general. Distinguish replacement training under §20.43, as in [replacement follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-replacement-training-followup.md).
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 13, prints 6th Australian Division Basic Morale `+1 (−3)`. [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed p. 27 / PDF p. 3, §60.41 places it in training at Cairo and/or Helwan, excluding 19 Australian Brigade and 2/1 and 2/2 Australian Field Artillery. Section 60.22 ends at GT6 OpStage 3. Setup alone does not specify instructor, exact co-location, completed training or prior credited stages; see [training follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-training-source-next.md).
- Conditional arithmetic only: if starting at −3 with zero prior credited stages, and all 18 scenario Operations Stages qualify, at most three six-stage increments would reach Basic Morale 0 by the end. This is not an actual result or a finding that all stages qualify; startup credit, interruption, instructor and stage-timing issues remain separate. Under the stated source ceiling, even fully trained Basic Morale is +1, not +4. A Commonwealth assault's separate Cohesion modification remains subject to §17.23; the Rommel exception does not apply to it.

The scenario's initial Axis setup reviewed at §60.31 does not list Rommel; this alone does not exclude later entry, special arrivals or a later assault. [Initiative review](2026-11-17-graziani-initiative-ratings-review.md) likewise did not prove first-six-turn Rommel presence. Therefore do not promote the exception to a reachable Graziani witness or assert its absence from all possible play without a bounded arrival and assault audit.
