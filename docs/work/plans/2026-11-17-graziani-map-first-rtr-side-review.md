# C3419/C3520 track side: independent review

**Disposition:** The source scan supports the candidate's local track crossing and separate western blue-checkered mark. No escarpment band is visible on the shared diagonal, but the crossing dash obscures part of the grid line; this is not complete proof that no other feature exists. The side remains insufficient by itself to establish legal movement, either unit's ZOC or compulsory combat.
