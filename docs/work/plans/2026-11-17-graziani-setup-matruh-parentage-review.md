# Matruh parentage: independent source review

**Disposition:** Checked source readings support the companion [parentage note](2026-11-17-graziani-setup-matruh-parentage.md). RUL-GRA-03 remains a planning interpretation; source conflict and initial parentage are not settled. One additional §59.2 clause matters when evaluating an initially over-limit formation: it expressly allows such a listed unit to operate normally, subject to restrictions on adding subordinates. That clause does not by itself decide how the Matruh source conflict applies.

## Primary evidence

Recomputed SHA-256 before visually inspecting source scans:

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF pp. 1–3, printed pp. 25–27: `fa12488e59552d7b0ff0d5abefa891d4b81af0492256bec2d25f8d61ef040a66`.
- [Commonwealth charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF pp. 10–11: `f75d074727071025f3145b273921ec03882b8b57cb4a429485995d4cf5255981`.
- [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1, §4.44b item 2: `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`.

Scenario §60.41 places the Matruh Garrison at D3714 and lists Essex, Durham Light Infantry and South Staffordshires after `Att:`; Essex and Durham each carry `23/70`. Section 59.2 labels `Att.` as attached but not assigned to the listed unit. Its parent designation identifies a unit that has been assigned to that parent whether or not the assignment still holds. Thus the marks support the printed Matruh attachments, but do not prove a current 23rd Brigade assignment or another initial assigned parent. They do not describe the owner's eventual setup reorganization.

Section 59.2 permits the owner to organize units listed in the same hex and freely attach or detach them at scenario start, within formation and stacking limits. On the same page its separate **Listed Unit Exceeds Formation Organization Limits** paragraph permits such units to operate normally but prohibits new assigned or attached subordinates and prohibits replacement attachments violating limits after detachments. Whether that exception applies to any specific Matruh setup requires a separate ruling; it should be considered alongside the general setup clause and source conflict.

Errata §4.44b item 2 says 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshires start the campaign and Italian scenarios assigned to Matruh. Scenario §§60.0, 60.21–60.22 support applying the Italian scenario-group wording to Graziani. Commonwealth chart p. 10 says no units may be assigned to the Garrison and up to six may be attached, with the maximum attachments chart referenced; p. 11 lists Buffs and Hampshire under unassigned British infantry with `D`. These are conflicting printed statements. A specific exception is plausible but remains project policy, and the erratum does not state their placement or attachment status. Assignment is not synonymous with attachment.

Chart p. 10 also says the HQ may not voluntarily move until it becomes Selby Force or the Operations Stage in which it is attacked in a Close Assault, with conversion at game turn 12, OpStage 2. Graziani ends at game turn 6, OpStage 3 (§60.22). The possible assault exception therefore prevents an unconditional six-turn movement ban; exact trigger timing and persistence are not settled here.
