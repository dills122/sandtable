# Convoy rounding: source follow-up

**Finding:** Air §41.67 supplies a plausible base procedure for Air abstract
convoys because §47.64 directs attacks to the Axis Naval Convoy Bombing rules.
It applies the table percentage to each listed cargo type and rounds fractions
up, as clarified by the September errata. Whether that detailed-cargo procedure
continues to govern results omitted by §47.65 is an interpretation: §47.65
modifies results using abstract cargo and names 10%, 20%, and 30% or greater,
not 5% or 25%. Land §32.64 independently states that the table result is the
percentage destroyed, but §32.65 does not specify how to round a 5% result.
No checked general rounding rule settles both questions.

## Evidence and limits

* [Land §§32.64–.65](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf),
  printed/PDF p. 44: the table percentage applies to supply units,
  motorization and replacement points in the lane. The specific 10% rule
  excludes supply and rounds motorization down and replacements up; the
  20%-or-greater rule rounds down with a minimum. The language does not say
  what fractional treatment applies to 5%. Its final paragraph groups tank and
  gun losses by nationality over all types, rather than by individual type;
  its application at 5% remains uncertain. The instruction to
  consult the Air Bombardment table is not itself an instruction to import
  all detailed Air convoy rules.
* [Air §§41.66–.67, 47.64–.65](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf),
  printed/PDF pp. 12 and 18: §47.64 refers attacks to Axis Naval Convoy
  Bombing rules. §41.67 rounds fractional losses up across specified detailed
  cargo types, including all truck replacements together and tanks and guns
  separately by nationality. §41.66 describes the table result in tens of percent, while
  the printed chart includes 5% and 25%; that mismatch further complicates
  extending the detailed procedure to those results. The [September 1979 addenda](https://grognard.com/errata1/cna.html)
  clarifies applying the percentage to each listed type. This is stronger
  evidence for a possible Air-mode fallback than a generic rounding analogy.
  Still, §47.65 modifies attack results for different abstract cargo classes,
  so applying §41.67 at 5% or 25% requires a choice about its continued scope
  and how detailed types map to abstract classes. Neither fallback nor that
  mapping is expressly printed in §47.65. In particular, Air 25% is not
  expressly assigned to its 20% or 30%-plus branch.
* The addenda's §41.35 instruction to round upward concerns bombing supply
  dumps, not a universal rule. Land §11.32's nearest-integer instruction
  concerns calculation of combat strength; §17.56 rounds destruction down.
  Air §38.34 rounds refit fractions up. These contextual instructions do not
  establish a single general rounding default for convoy losses.
* The checked September addenda contains §41.5 and §41.67 entries, but no
  §32.6 or §47.6 entry. Search of extracted rule text for rounding and
  fractions is bounded by OCR quality; this does not prove absence of a
  general rule elsewhere or of later official corrections.

## Review implications

Treat §41.67 as a documented candidate for Air abstract-mode interpretation,
not as a proven correction or as a Land-only default. Any chosen policy must
specify affected cargo classes, aggregation, rounding, minimums, and behavior
for empty classes separately. Preserve game-mode identity and test 5%, 20%,
25%, and 30% independently. No gameplay outcome or source-mandated policy is
asserted here. See [mode comparison](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-mode-rounding.md).
