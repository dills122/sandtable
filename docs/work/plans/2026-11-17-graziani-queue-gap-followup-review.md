# Graziani training instructor criteria: independent review

**Disposition:** Accept the bounded §17.35 transcription for morale training; revise the replacement discussion before using it as a rule. Section 20.43 expressly distinguishes Commonwealth morale training from replacement training, while also referring replacement training to the rules for training reinforcements. The interaction deserves an explicit ruling; neither sentence alone settles whether every §17.35 instructor condition applies to every replacement class. No legal training witness or runtime admission follows.

## Findings

1. **Replacement distinction omitted.** Land §20.43's note says replacement training has nothing to do with Commonwealth training for morale. The candidate correctly identifies its cross-reference to §17.3, but omits this countervailing text. Read both together: replacement training has its own fixed durations and purpose; the extent to which the cross-reference imports §17.35's physical instructor requirements remains a policy question, including the gun uncrating case. Do not equate completed replacement training with a morale increase or infer a universal instructor exemption from the note.
2. **Five-point equivalence has another relevant statement.** Section 20.43 gives five replacement points per battalion when in doubt about size. Section 20.45 separately states five points equal a battalion for all purposes in its supply paragraph. Consider both when deciding size and stacking; neither expressly says that five replacement points form a qualified §17.35 instructor, nor specifies how partial groups round. The candidate's cautious wording is sound but incomplete as a basis for a future ruling.
3. **Capacity and timing need careful boundaries.** Section 17.35 says each instruction battalion may train as many units as may be stacked in that hex and instruction battalions do not count against stacking limits. It does not expressly settle how other occupants, multiple instructors, partial replacement groups, or instructor substitution affect that capacity. Presence is required during each training Operations Stage; the same instructor throughout consecutive stages is not specified. Do not infer a completion or interruption rule solely from this clause.
4. **Axis centers do not settle instructor identity.** September errata §17.3 says Axis training centers are used only for replacement points, not actual units. It does not explicitly dispense with instructors, state that a center itself is a battalion, or resolve the relation between center and §20.43's locations. Preserve these as open questions, alongside scenario-specific authority.

Section 17.35 otherwise supports the candidate's same-hex, designated Basic Morale, combat-battalion, one-third assigned TOE, infantry-type and tank-battalion readings. The threshold is “at least” one third; for integral strength, compare three times actual strength against assigned strength without prematurely choosing a separate rounding convention. This arithmetic does not establish how mixed TOE types or actual strength are calculated. Test exact threshold and immediately below, as well as tank and non-tank instructor types, location, stage presence and stacking; test replacement and center interpretations only after explicitly recording the chosen hierarchy.
