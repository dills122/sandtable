# Independent review: destroyed railroad identity

**Disposition:** Agree with retained-witness as a proposed interpretation, not an explicit ruling. No reviewed provision expressly removes a previously built hex's rail identity upon destruction or expressly exempts a destroyed occupied hex from §8.78. The text does not expressly decide that nonlocal witness question either. Keep the alternative operational-witness reading visible and version the decision.

## Visual source findings

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 15, §8.47 ignores unconstructed railroad hexes for all purposes. Page 37, §24.67 says unbuilt railroad hexes do not exist or function until built. Neither provision expressly classifies a built, later destroyed hex as unbuilt.
- Page 16, §8.76 calls them rail hexes that have been destroyed and prohibits entry by Rail Movement until repair. Its next sentence separately treats hexes not yet in existence until constructed. Page 37, §§24.61–24.63 distinguishes building new track and repairing or rebuilding destroyed track; §24.66 uses a Destroyed RR Hex marker. This supports continuing identity but does not explicitly govern §8.78. Section 24.65 also bars building or rebuilding enemy-controlled or enemy-occupied rail hexes; repair cannot be presumed while that condition holds.
- Page 16, §8.78 forbids Allied Railroad Movement use of rail hexes west of any rail hex occupied by an Axis combat unit. It states no operational condition on the occupied hex. A damaged witness remains a plausible reading of that wording; a reader could instead construe “rail hex” in this movement rule as usable rail. Neither reading is expressly settled by the reviewed clauses. Section 8.73 separately bars Rail Movement through an enemy-occupied hex or enemy ZOC, without settling the nonlocal condition.
- Page 15, §8.71 and p. 37, §24.6 exclude the historical Soluch–Benghazi–Barce line from game use. The [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), p. 1, clarifies Axis rail use under §54.4; its wording “rail lines” leaves scope against the historical-line exclusion unclear without reviewing §54.4. Do not treat historical ink alone as a certified §8.78 witness or claim a general unqualified prohibition on all Axis use. Overlapping independently established track needs separate classification.
- Errata p. 1 adds a §8.73 reorganization restriction and has no express amendment to §§8.76 or 8.78. This check does not establish absence of later errata, scenario exceptions, or other contextual rules.
