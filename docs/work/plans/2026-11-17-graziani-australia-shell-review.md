# Independent review: 6th Australian shell baseline

**Assessment:** The conditional 2-of-3 non-shell conclusion in `2026-11-17-graziani-australia-shell-next.md` is supported by the inspected scenario, OA, unit characteristics, formation chart, and §§9.26–.27. It is not an unconditional result for an unspecified player setup or later convoy lookup. No material error in the baseline arithmetic was found.

| Check | Independent source reading |
| --- | --- |
| Scenario location and exceptions | §60.41 prints “Cairo and/or Helwan (E1430),” in training, less 19 Aus Brigade, 2/1 Australian Field Artillery, and 2/2 Australian Field Artillery. Co-location is a source-permitted reading, subject to an actual legal setup and stacking; source does not force co-location. §59.2 allows a listed group distributed among more than one listed hex subject to stacking. The exact scope of `Cairo and/or Helwan` and complete stacking remain gates, not a certified concrete setup. |
| OA assigned brigade and battalion rows | Commonwealth OA PDF p. 13 lists 16 Aus HQ, 2/1, 2/2, 2/3 and 17 Aus HQ, 2/5, 2/6, 2/7 as `N`, `D`. It lists 19 Aus HQ, 2/4, 2/8, 2/11 as `2/9`, i.e. GT9 OpStage 2, beyond GT1–6. `Less` also excludes that brigade from initial scenario listing. Do not confuse 16 Aus with separately listed 16 Infantry Brigade (70). |
| Strength and denominators | OA key p. 5 defines N as maximum TOE and D as deployed GT1 OpStage 1; p. 22 gives infantry code p maximum 6. Each named battalion is 6/6, hence non-shell. §19.31 p. 24 gives three infantry brigade slots per infantry division and three infantry battalion slots per ordinary brigade. With both brigades and their three children attached, each brigade is 3/3 and division 2/3; absence of 19th does not reduce the allowable-slot denominator. |
| Training | Scenario specifies in training, and OA shows trained/untrained morale +1 (−3). Neither fact changes the §9.26 shell calculation on the cited evidence. This does not settle other training restrictions, completion or convoy eligibility at a later time. |
| Threshold and shell child | §9.26 makes a division shell at 50% or less, and an ordinary brigade shell below two-thirds. §9.27 expressly excludes a shell child from the requisite count and illustrates three assigned brigade slots with two shell brigades as a shell division. Thus the proposal's wording that one should “assess §9.27” is cautious but need not imply that this basic exclusion is unresolved: with exactly two attached brigades, if either is a shell, only one counts and the division is shell. Separately verify any substitution or unusual denominator before applying this vector. |

**Remaining gates:** §4.25 default attachment applies to assigned units starting in the same hex unless specifically stated otherwise; §59.2 gives owner organization latitude subject to formation and stacking. Prove placement of the parent, brigade HQs and each required subordinate, plus full stacking and any allowed substitute, before asserting actual initial status. `D` alone establishes deployment, not same-hex attachment after owner decisions. A non-shell Australian division alone does not establish a second qualifying division, §32.66's required row relationship, or initial convoy-stage timing. Preserve unknown when any required fact is missing.
