# Review: Land §§17–29 disclosure census

**Disposition:** The [census](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-17-29.md) accurately bounds the checked replacement, raider and prisoner passages; no material audience or timing error found. One adjacent raider choice deserves explicit treatment when extending this partial inventory: [Land §27.58](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 39–40, says on a convoy-raid die result of 1 or 2 that one truck point and cargo are destroyed, the **defender** chooses truck type and the **raider** chooses cargo type. This is an asymmetric choice in a specific outcome, not an express grant of the full cargo manifest or every hidden property. Applicability to abstract Supply Unit/convoy mode needs separate authority. The source further gives special handling where some or all trucks carry Replacement Points; no actual raid or choice follows.

## Verified boundaries

- Land §20.0 p. 31 permits **any Replacement die rolls** to be kept secret from other players, including teammates. It does not declare all replacement counters and consequences permanently invisible. Section 17.55 p. 28 places material-destruction attempts after voluntary surrender is declared. Sections 18.12–.13 and .21 use Reserve I/II marker faces to designate status; physical markers do not make every underlying detail public.
- Land §§27.31–.33 pp. 38–39 distinguish initial on-map raider placement, secret subsequent movement/location, and map placement when a raid or spotting occurs. Sections 27.41–.44 p. 39 contain both different spotting triggers and the awkward “if a Raider performs either action” announcement wording; the candidate correctly retains the attribution question. [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) p. 2 §27.36 says a desert raider may use Reaction after **any spotting attempt**, without by itself resolving elimination order.
- Sections 27.52–.55 specify the stated raid announcement or raider self-revelation. Section 27.56 reveals a dummy dump after any raider–guard conflict **if any raiders survive**; no earlier blanket reveal follows. Aircraft and airfield raids are detailed-Air subjects; applying dump procedures to abstract units remains a distinct mode question. Section 27.88 p. 40 removes SAS from physical sight when stacked with an LRDG and specifies escort to map placement, subject to the named interaction.
- Section 28.14 p. 40 puts Prisoner Point markers on map until departure and allows, rather than mandates, a camp marker with more than five standing points. Addendum §3.62 p. 45 keeps POW/camp/guard numbers and Axis nationality secret. Sections 24.56, .66 and .67 p. 37 identify optional End of Road, Destroyed RR Hex and Railhead markers; these do not override item-under-construction secrecy by implication. The [minefield](2026-11-18-graziani-minefield-disclosure-source-review.md) and [captor](2026-11-18-graziani-vehicle-marker-captor-source-review.md) reviews already cover the interactions attributed to them.

## Recheck

The added §27.58 row correctly records the defender's truck-type choice and the raider's cargo-type choice on the specified result, without inferring full manifest disclosure or Land-only applicability. Its locator should read **pp. 39–40**: the truck-type clause begins on p. 39 and the cargo-choice clause continues at the top of p. 40. The substantive omission is addressed; the page-span correction remains.
