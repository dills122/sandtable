# Graziani addenda search: four unresolved questions

**Search date:** November 17, 2026. No additional independently identified
official correction was found that settles the 1st CCNN HQ's initial gun,
Tobruk's port conflict, map overlay, or monthly motorization losses. This is a
bounded public-source search, not proof no later correction exists. None of
the four gates is closed by this search.

The September packet's correction of §30.57 addresses stacking-point
transport's port-tonnage effect; it does not choose the disputed starting or
maximum Tobruk efficiency. Do not conflate a related port rule with an answer
to that specific conflict.

## Community leads, not official corrections

- [CNA Living Rules](https://basmith.net/cna/) explicitly says it is
  unaffiliated, applies September errata, and adopts provisional rulings. Its
  [register](https://basmith.net/cna/rulings/register) indexes R-027 (Tobruk
  efficiency), R-092 (abstract truck loss timing/base/rounding), and R-002
  (map-art inconsistencies). Register status “accepted” means that project's
  ruling, not SPI approval. Individual pages returned cache-miss errors in
  this search; no detailed resolution or direct match to the overlay dispute
  is asserted here.
- [Friend or Foe house rules](https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/)
  proposes Tobruk efficiency 5 and changes the San Giorgio treatment; it also
  interprets scenario opening as starting without the strategic and naval
  stages. These are the author's choices, not official errata or independent
  proof of a printed correction. The site's [resource page](https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/)
  describes other PDFs as having errata applied; a later compilation is not
  itself a new publisher ruling.
- A search also found *The Desert Fox: Rommel's Campaign for North Africa*,
  a [different SPI game](https://vassalengine.org/wiki_old/wiki/Module%3AThe_Desert_Fox%3A_Rommel%27s_Campaign_for_North_Africa_April_1941-December_1942)
  from 1981. Its errata do not by title or topic alone correct this game's
  1979 rules.

## Scope and next evidence

Searches covered the game's title with errata/addenda, SPI, 1980–81, CCNN,
Tobruk, map overlay, and §32.57/motorization terms; checked Grognard's game
listing, the public source inventory and the in-book addenda. Indexing gaps,
offline magazine corrections, unsearched archives and unverified source
provenance remain possible. Next useful evidence is a dated publisher or
designer correction with exact issue/page and the relevant text, followed by
verification against the original scan and existing edition. Do not import
community values as source facts; keep any selected interpretation versioned
and distinguish it from official evidence.
