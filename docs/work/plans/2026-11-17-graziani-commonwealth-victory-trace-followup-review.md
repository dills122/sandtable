# Commonwealth victory trace follow-up: independent source review

**Disposition:** Core uncertainty and proposed policy separation supported. One relevant source distinction should be added before using the follow-up as a complete source inventory. No ruling or runtime admission follows.

## Finding

- **Include the Land-only initial and arriving supply rules in source-eligibility analysis.** Scenario §60.44 describes unlimited supplies at Cairo and Alexandria in the standard initial supply status, but §60.92(C) explicitly replaces §60.44 for Land-only play and gives each city three initially stocked regular Supply Units. Land §32.42 places later Commonwealth Supply Units in Cairo, with a stated Alexandria allowance when more than three arrive in one Game-Turn. Section 32.0 substitutes Supply Units and Motorization Points for standard supplies and trucks. These provisions supply relevant mode-specific context for a candidate requiring an actual supply source, but do not say which city hex terminates a §60.81 trace, require an actual shipment, or prove continued stock or control. Do not silently carry standard unlimited supplies into Land-only play or turn initial presence and arrival placement into automatic victory-route eligibility.

## Source checks

- Scenario §60.81 tactical wording names Sollum, Halfaya Pass and Siwa, followed by tracing supply by truck convoy back to Alexandria or Cairo; decisive and strategic say supply as above. It does not identify a tracing actor, city-hex rule, or common-city choice. The alternatives in the follow-up are plausible policies, not exhaustive formal consequences of the text. The Italian strategic “any hex” clause is absent from the Commonwealth wording. Later-row carryover and objective quantification remain separate choices.
- Land §32.16 measures local access from a friendly combat unit to a friendly Supply Unit, normally as medium-truck movement within half CPA, subject to impassable terrain and specified enemy ZOC restriction. It does not establish a city connection. §32.51 treats Motorization Points as medium Truck Points except as modified; §32.33 and §32.58 govern a transported Supply Unit, but do not expressly require a real transported unit or impose its CPA on the victory trace. Direction, route rules and actual-versus-hypothetical shipment remain open.
- The September 1979 addenda scan's scenario entries include §§60.31, 60.32 and 60.45, with no §60.81 entry in that section; its §32.0 note warns that the abstract system changes balance, without resolving the trace. This bounded check does not prove no clarification exists elsewhere or in another edition.
- The proposed mixed-city, same-city/different-hex, non-chart-hex, unknown and disconnected cases discriminate policies only after subject, source eligibility, route semantics and complete evidence are defined. Unknown evidence must not be treated as disconnection; the selected row policy may still allow a separate known-false predicate to dominate.
