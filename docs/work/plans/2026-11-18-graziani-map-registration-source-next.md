# Map registration: printed distance example

**Date:** November 18, 2026. A source-index follow-up to the [seam comparison](2026-11-17-graziani-seam-geometry-comparison.md) and [distance decision](2026-11-17-graziani-map-distance-decision.md), without re-reading the same unclear seam crops. **Finding:** Air/Logistics' printed worked example takes four 33-hex steps through matching section-local numbers. It explicitly says east for the first; eastward continuation is a supported reading. This supports that direction and spacing as a counting convention. It does not establish a sheet-edge alias or resolve Land §4.1. The same worked example contains a separate zero-distance wording problem, so it is not a clean global geometry oracle; see the [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-map-registration-source-next-review.md).

## Original source comparison

- [Air/Logistics](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 24, “Determining Shortest Distance,” Example 2, starts at Agedabia. The adjacent [Land Distance Chart §37.42](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 25, identifies Agedabia as `A2629`. The example proceeds **east 33 hexes to B2629**, then 33 each to `C2629`, `D2629`, and `E2629`. Its four 33s total 132. This is more specific about **eastward direction and the repeated addresses** than the chart footnote's general same-number/adjacent-sheet 33-hex statement. It supports, but does not independently certify, a 33-column eastward candidate.
- The next printed phrase says **zero hexes northwest to E3425**, followed by 8 hexes to `E3433` and 9 east to Ismailia; the printed sum is 149 (`4 × 33 + 0 + 8 + 9`). Under the ordinary reading that `E2629` and `E3425` identify distinct hexes, a zero-hex transition between them is not explained. It could reflect a printing or interpretation problem; the inspected sources do not identify which part should be corrected. The printed arithmetic alone is not evidence of a valid path or of the missing distance.
- Air §36.53, on the same PDF p. 24, lists Ismailia with entry/exit `E3433` and distance 9. This supports the example's last named leg as an **off-map air-facility** convention; it does not make Ismailia an on-map hex, resolve the preceding E2629/E3425 wording, or prove a ground route. The example is not an independent edition of that chart.
- Land §4.1, printed/PDF p. 7, directs the `01xx` row of a following sheet to overlay the preceding sheet's `39xx` row, with sections A–E west to east. The example's 33-hex counting and the map-edge evidence cited in the seam comparison do not explain how to translate that different wording into printed per-hex aliases. They do not prove which digit group is intended, a typo, a unique offset/stagger, or any specific seam crossing.

The shortest-distance procedure is an air-distance aid and explicitly is not an actual flight path. Its examples and chart may constrain comparisons but cannot themselves certify matching partial physical cells, land adjacency, terrain, road, rail, ZOC or legal traversal. The [distance decision](2026-11-17-graziani-map-distance-decision.md) separately records contradictions between printed chart distances; retain them rather than choosing an unproved correction. A future discriminating source would identify an actual same-position pair or measured grid vertices on both sheet margins with provenance, and reconcile the rule and chart wording; it must not infer absence of terrain from an unrecorded edge.
