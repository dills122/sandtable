§59.2 normally lists parent units; assigned subordinates require the OA chart, subject to printed exceptions. Where a group has multiple listed hexes, its owner may divide its listed units among those hexes subject to stacking. Units listed in the same starting hex may be organized by their owner within formation and stacking limits, including initial attachment or detachment. Listed first-line trucks must be assigned to units in their listed hex. These permissions do not themselves establish final composition or extend a named region.
