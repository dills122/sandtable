## Rules and state gates

Land §8.71 names Alexandria–Mersa Matruh as active; scenario §60.7 places the
initial terminus at Mersa Matruh D3714. This named line is not a side-by-side
topology proof. Land §§8.72–.78 provide phase, load, start, CP, enemy ZOC and
occupancy restrictions, prohibit use of destroyed or unconstructed rail,
and permit pickup and dropoff along the line. The interaction with friendly
combat-unit ZOC negation needs a ruling; §8.78's restriction west of an
Axis-combat-occupied rail hex is separate. Errata modify §§8.71 and 8.73;
see [rail scope review](2026-11-17-graziani-rail-scope-review.md). Land
§8.47 treats unconstructed roads as tracks and ignores unconstructed rail;
printed symbols alone do not establish scenario construction or damage.

For Land-only abstract logistics, §32.34 permits two real and/or dummy
Supply Units as total rail load in one direction per OpStage under normal
rail rules. Its relationship to §8.72's general movement in each direction
remains an explicit precedence question. Section 32.16's supply-range trace
uses medium-truck movement, or infantry movement for non-motorized infantry,
and excludes impassable terrain and enemy ZOCs unoccupied by friendly units;
it is not itself a rail move. Scenario victory convoy wording has its own
endpoint and modality questions; see [endpoint review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-route-endpoints-review.md).
Actual path, phase, eligible actor/load, terrain, construction, damage,
occupancy, control and ZOC require authoritative state and applicable
interpretations. A particular rail move needs its selected covered path;
the rules do not require a complete survey of the named line for every move.
