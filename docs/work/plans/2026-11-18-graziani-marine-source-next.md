# French Motor Marine: later scenario setup check

**Result:** An additional original scenario independently lists a 1st Company French Motor Marines at its later starting position. It extends the bounded setup comparison through the start of Game-Turn 26, but supplies no lifecycle instruction connecting that company to either later battalion row.

## Source evidence

[Prior follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-marine-transition-followup.md) and its [review](2026-11-17-graziani-marine-transition-followup-review.md) compared Graziani setup, three Commonwealth OA rows, and September errata, while explicitly leaving other scenario setups unsearched. I visually checked the [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29, §§61.1–.31:

- §61.1 describes the Desert Fox scenario group as two scenarios sharing one setup; §61.2 starts the race for Tobruk at **Game-Turn 26, Operations Stage 3**, and ends at turn 38, stage 3.
- Under §61.31 initial Commonwealth land deployment, **Msus (B4004)** lists **1st Coy French Motor Marines**, with **3 Medium** trucks. This is a separate scenario's starting listing, later than Graziani §60.41's C3926 company and two medium trucks.
- The similar name supports comparing those setup records; neither the new row nor these local introductory provisions state that a specific company survived an intervening playthrough, that its earlier trucks were transferred, or that it becomes either named 1943 marine battalion. An independently constructed starting setup is not an observed state reached from a different scenario.

The checked row adds a dated scenario occurrence and a different starting allocation. It does not resolve whether the company is later withdrawn, replaced, reconstituted, transferred, or coexists with the later OA entries. As the [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-marine-source-next-review.md) notes, an OA note explicitly names the parent of its company row; linking the scenario row to that OA row, unique counter identity across scenarios, and the relationship to later battalion rows remain unproved. Neither a full reinforcement/withdrawal track nor every scenario or later official correction was searched here. A direct official transition, withdrawal/replacement or precise counter/parent linkage applicable at the relevant date would discriminate the lifecycle alternatives; a ruling may instead be needed if such evidence is unavailable.
