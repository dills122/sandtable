# FG-01: selected chart disclosure census

**Result:** Common chart §27.91 adds an explicit post-raid report of a dump's real, non-dump or dummy status, with a trigger distinct from Land §27.56's immediate dummy disclosure. It also differs from Land §27.55(b) at equality in the guard test. Applicability to Land-only abstract Supply Units and source precedence remain open; neither case proves an eligible six-turn raid.

| [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) p. 8, §27.91, supply dump raid | If raider completes raid without elimination, **opposing player informs raiding player** whether dump is real, a non-dump, or a dummy. For a guarded dump, two-dice sum **equal to or greater than** total Raw Close Assault Defense Points lets raider survive and attack. | [Land §§27.55–.56](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 39, instead says guard sum must be **higher than** strength; otherwise raider eliminated. After any raider–guard conflict, surviving raiders cause a **dummy** to be revealed immediately and supply destruction is off, though other results apply. Land §32.12's abstract Supply Units differ from detailed dumps; do not presume chart dump procedure survives unchanged. ## Scope and provenance

Visually checked common pp. 3 and 8, CW p. 31, Axis pp. 2 and 33, and Land p. 39. Searched imperfect retained OCR of the common and CW chart booklets for secret/reveal/announce/inform/opponent and relevant table numbers to locate candidates; visually checked the above selected passages. Axis full OCR was unavailable, so its inspection is specifically the two listed pages. The OCR search can miss text; this is **not an exhaustive chart census** or proof of no other disclosure clauses. Combat calculation tables beyond the selected patrol and raid tables, other reinforcement instructions and chart pages, detailed modes, later errata and indirect inferences remain unexamined here. Checked retained September errata text for §§16.7, 27.5, 27.9, 32.6 and 42.53; only the §42.53 chart-location hit appeared in that bounded search, not proof of no other correction.
