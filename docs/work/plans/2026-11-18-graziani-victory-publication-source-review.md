# Victory terminal timing and disclosure: source review

**Disposition:** Supports [source boundary](2026-11-18-graziani-victory-publication-source.md). The inspected text establishes the scenario endpoint and limits knowledge of opposing counters, but does not specify a digital verdict event, its audience or a pending-decision protocol. This is an informed reading of the same scans, not an independent edition or exhaustive secrecy search.

- Scenario §60.22 ends Graziani's Offensive at the conclusion of Game-Turn 6, Operations Stage 3 and references §60.71; §60.81 is the printed Graziani victory list. Treating the latter as the intended reference is an interpretation, not a correction printed in §60.22. Section 60.81 states the listed conditions; the inspected text does not set a separate publication audience, digital event or explicit scoring snapshot.
- Land §5.2 places A's patrol after movement/combat, convoy, applicable rail and repair, followed by B's F–L; the third Operations Stage repeats the first stage's facets. The following End of Game-Turn statement announces completion but does not name a victory publication or cleanup procedure. This supports conclusion after both portions without resolving every separate stage-end obligation or an early-assessment interpretation.
- Scenario introduction §59.0's general designer commentary expects a player to know whether objectives were accomplished; it does not prescribe source-private witness disclosure or a terminal audience. Land addendum §3.61 restricts opposing-counter status, composition and attributes except as rules expressly indicate; §3.62 labels its examples a **partial** list and includes patrol's restriction on supply and attached-truck details. These passages do not establish a victory-specific disclosure exception or universal postgame secrecy. Do not infer absence of another rule outside this bounded inspection.
- The timing of an observable pending or final result can itself reveal hidden decision completeness. That is an application-design issue requiring a selected audience and timing policy; the inspected original cases neither prescribe such a policy nor establish what additional source disclosures may exist.
