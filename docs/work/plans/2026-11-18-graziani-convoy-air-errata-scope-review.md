# Review: Axis convoy Air errata and Land-only scope

Visually rechecked [Air rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF p. 12 §§41.64–.68, [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF p. 44 §§32.51–.52 and .61–.65, and [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 3 left column §§41.65G and 41.67 against [scope note](2026-11-18-graziani-convoy-air-errata-scope.md). The procedural sequence, cargo types, rounding, landing clarification and explicit references match the source. Land §32.65(B)'s separate minimum sentence remains grammatically uncertain; note does not apply it to the 10% example.

Synthetic arithmetic checks: 10% of one Motorization Point is 0.1, rounded down to zero under Land §32.65(A); conditionally mapping one point to one detailed truck replacement gives 0.1 rounded up to one under Air §41.67. Land §32.51's medium-truck treatment and §32.52's replacement-rate connection do not themselves establish that cross-mode cargo mapping. Section 32.65(A) names only Motorization and Replacement Points, while the erratum says each listed detailed cargo type; neither the arithmetic nor common table reference authorizes importing detailed processing. Existing [convoy census review](2026-11-18-graziani-convoy-section-census-review.md) records the cited 5% table cell and missing explicit Land branch; I did not re-render that chart here.

Air §41.65G resolves bombing and completes Axis Naval Convoy Missions; erratum adds that shipments are considered landed when Axis convoys are completed. Land §32.63 independently describes attacks on arriving convoys and does not repeat the detailed G sequence. Note correctly leaves stage, delivery, destination and no-attack cases open. It does not prove a live convoy, cargo, result or reachability.
