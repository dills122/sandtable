# Graziani victory city-hex scope: independent review

**Disposition:** Source reading supported with one wording refinement. No policy adopted; no map, route, or gameplay admission. Reviewed scenario PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28 and Air/Logistics PDF pp. 25–26 directly from local scans, November 17, 2026.

## Finding

- **Proposed test should preserve capability wording.** §60.81 says the Italian strategic combat unit **“can be supplied (by convoy)”**; the draft's final proposed contrast says “supplied” and “unsupplied.” Actual supply status and a qualifying capability to be supplied are not necessarily identical. State the test in terms of a combat unit that can versus cannot be supplied by the applicable convoy predicate; leave any actual-supply requirement to a separate ruling. This does not resolve that predicate or the separate Map D quantifier.

## Checks

- The Italian strategic clause says take and hold “either Alexandria or Cairo (any hex)” with a combat unit that can be supplied by convoy from any hex on Game-Map D. One city and one qualifying city hex is a reasonable reading, including application to Alexandria; parenthetical scope over both city names remains an interpretation. The indefinite combat unit links the actor and supply capability; requiring one unit on the objective to witness both is the stronger direct reading, not an explicit formal identity or temporal rule. An independent supplied unit elsewhere does not follow from the text. Taking, holding, substitution over time, and exact supply-capability evaluation remain open.
- The Commonwealth tactical row names Alexandria or Cairo as the destination of truck-convoy tracing; decisive and strategic rows refer to supply as above. These rows do not repeat “any hex.” No basis here to transfer the Italian parenthesis or to decide whether destinations must match across objectives. The draft correctly keeps those choices separate.
- Location summary lists Alexandria `E3613/3714` and Cairo `E1730/1829/1830/1930/1931`, each marked Major City and Victory Condition. Those marks apply at the location row, not a scenario-specific selection rule, verified topology, or proof of supply/control. Section 37.42 lists Alexandria `E3613*` and Cairo `E1829*`; the footnote identifies each as one major city hex, and describes shortest distances including sea-crossing geometry. It provides representative chart endpoints, not exclusive victory or convoy endpoints.
- Scope remains §60.81, specifically Italian strategic objective and Commonwealth source references. §60.82 concerns the separate Italian Campaign, and its combat-unit and supply wording cannot silently define §60.81. The source's named Graziani heading and the separate §60.22 cross-reference issue do not turn this note into an official erratum. Other scenarios, city rules, and route procedures require their own evidence.

## Evidence limits

Both scans are same-edition source material. Review verifies printed wording, listing, and footnote, not an independent edition, complete map membership, route legality, unit qualification, final timing, or control policy. No runtime edits or tests.
