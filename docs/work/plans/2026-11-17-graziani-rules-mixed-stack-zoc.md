# Mixed stacks, garrisons and ZOC thresholds

**Disposition:** Land rules use both a stacking/organization test and a separate raw-defense threshold for ZOC. A garrison's free stacking at its assigned city/village is explicitly illustrated as zero there, but the inspected sources do not explicitly say whether §10.11's aggregate ZOC test uses that effective zero or the counter's printed value. Nor do they say that free stacking erases a unit's defensive strength. Preserve both questions until a versioned policy and actual setup are reviewed; no initial contact outcome is established here.

## Source facts

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 17 §10.11: a combat unit above battalion-equivalent (described there as more than one stacking point) exerts ZOC; if a hex has **more than one** stacking point even without such a larger unit, the units in the hex also exert ZOC. The same case separately excludes truck convoys and HQs without attached combat units. Sections 10.12–.14 exclude other named categories and cohesion −26 or worse. The text does not explicitly settle every mixed-stack contributor's eligibility or whether garrison home stacking substitutes its effective value for its printed number in this ZOC count.
- §10.15, pp. 17–18: regardless of §10.11, unit(s) in a hex totaling **less than ten raw defensive Close Assault Points** do not exert ZOC, regardless of organizational size; for this purpose anti-tank units are always presumed Up Front. Ten is not *less than ten*, so it passes this numerical exclusion, subject to §10.11 and all other applicable rules. Section 11.3 p. 19 obtains raw strength from TOE times the relevant rating; §11.34 says raw points are totaled before division for actual strength. Section 10.15 says raw, not rounded actual points.
- §9.16(a), p. 17, says `G` garrison units stack freely in the city/village to which assigned. Its example expressly gives the Italian Fort Maddalena heavy-weapons battalion **zero stacking points in that village and one elsewhere**. The introductory clause frames this as ignoring stacking restrictions; the example also uses numerical zero. Section 9.22 directs organizational-level determination using the **printed** stacking value and §9.4, rather than a unit name/designation. Neither says explicitly that the garrison's home exemption erases its raw defense, nor specifies which stacking number §10.11's total uses. Keep printed, home-effective, shell-adjusted and organization-size facts separate.
- §§9.21 and 19.12–.13, pp. 17 and 29, distinguish parent representation from units merely assigned or separately stacked; an assigned unit need not be in its parent's hex. Section 9.21 describes a division representing units **assigned and attached** to its HQ, while §19.12 describes attached-unit representation more generally; reconcile that wording rather than assuming an identical represented set in all cases. Section 19.41 specifically addresses an **assigned but not attached** unit joining its parent: its counter is removed, and immediate attachment does not affect parent stacking value **unless it was a shell**. Section 19.42 separately governs non-assigned attachment; do not automatically extend §19.41's consequence to that case. Section 9.26 defines shell states and §9.28 reduces a shell one level for unit differentiation and other rules where size matters. Thus adding constituent printed values to an already-representing parent risks double counting; conversely, counting only its HQ's own intrinsic strength risks discarding attached constituents. Exact ZOC snapshot, recursive composition, shell transition, eligibility and raw contribution need a contract rather than an assumed calculation.
- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF pp. 1–2 / printed pp. 25–26, §59.2 permits players to organize units **already in the same hex** at setup subject to formation and stacking limits. Assignment, attachment and freely selected initial distributions are distinct; this wording alone does not move a Libya-only unit into another country, or establish any particular choice. Section 60.31 fixes the Fort Maddalena garrison at C3019 but also gives a separate Libya-only flexible pool. Candidate regional classification and admissible extra placements remain separately reviewed.

## Interpretations and distinguishing cases

For an otherwise valid friendly home stack containing a printed-one garrison and a separately represented printed-one battalion, two plausible ZOC counts exist:

1. **Effective-stack reading:** §9.16's explicit zero-at-home example applies to §10.11, so the garrison contributes zero to that count and the total is 1, not more than 1. A sufficient raw-defense sum would not independently overcome §10.11. This gives literal effect to the example's numerical zero, but the placement of §9.16 under stacking restrictions leaves scope to interpret it as capacity-only.
2. **Printed-size reading:** §10.11 counts the garrison's printed organizational value 1 and the other battalion's 1, total 2, even though the garrison occupies zero capacity at home. This preserves the printed-value/organizational-size distinction and makes §9.16 a capacity exemption, but §10.11 does not expressly say to disregard the example's zero for ZOC.

The sources do not select either. This synthetic configuration is not a claim that a particular unit is available or can legally be placed at C3019. Neither reading alone settles §10.15. For example, if separately determined eligible raw contributions are 6 and 4, the sum is 10 and passes the **raw threshold only**; 6 plus 3 gives 9 and fails it. Whether a specific garrison's rated defense is available for the sum in its current state, whether a particular other unit contributes, and how attached constituent gun/AA/parenthesized ratings and ammunition apply must be decided from the relevant rules. Zero stacking alone is not textual evidence of zero defensive strength. The same color, co-location, printed number or aggregate raw alone does not certify a unit's ZOC onto a target: §10.21's side and unit-entry restrictions remain direction-specific.
