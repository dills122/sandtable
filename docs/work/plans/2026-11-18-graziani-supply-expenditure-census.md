# LA-01: §32.2 supply-expenditure source census

**Scope:** Bounded inventory of the complete printed **§32.2 introductory text and §§32.21–.25** on Land p. 43, compared with [register LA-14](2026-11-12-graziani-source-register.md#initial-obligation-rows) and the [current queue](2026-11-17-graziani-remaining-source-research.md). The next printed heading is §32.3; no §32.26 or separately numbered subcase appears in this section on the inspected page. This is a source-coverage check, not a complete mode census, accepted interpretation, legal action or runtime audit.

§32.0 says this abstraction applies to the Land Game without Air and Logistics, replacing ordinary expenditure with listed abstract costs; §32.11 directs players to the supply rules below. Scenario §60.92 is the Group One Land-only adjustment. The relationship to other obligations, initial holdings and action prerequisites must be resolved individually, not inferred from this census.
