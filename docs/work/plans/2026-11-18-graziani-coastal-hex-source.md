# Coastal hex: bounded source-definition check

**Result:** The checked original passages and map key do not expressly say that a land hex qualifies as coastal solely because water or shoreline is in an adjacent hex. They also do not explicitly settle a shoreline touching only a vertex. The key illustrates coast with both land and water inside the outlined example, which is evidence for that pictured case, not a textual exclusion of every other case. The available checked evidence therefore does not alone resolve the project's `coastal` field for D3903, D3231 or B5229.
