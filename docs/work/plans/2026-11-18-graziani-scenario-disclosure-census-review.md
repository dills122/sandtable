**Disposition:** Dummy counts, source modes and the [census](2026-11-18-graziani-scenario-disclosure-census.md)'s bounded audience conclusions are supported. One wording refinement: scenario §60.92(B)/(C), printed p. 29 / PDF p. 5, says the dummy groups **may be placed** according to the relevant Dump 1/2 or Dump 1 rules. The census's “placed under” wording could imply placement is mandatory; preserve the explicit permissive wording without choosing a setup policy.

Section 60.92(C) replaces §60.44 and gives Commonwealth **one group of three** which may be placed according to Dump 1's rule. These are group/counter counts, not the two and one detailed-dump counts. Section 59.53 identifies non-active scenario dumps as dummies and excludes them from an air-facility hex; the Land-only replacement/representation interaction remains separately scoped.

Section 60.6 fixes Italian initiative for the first Game-Turn; §60.7 prints the initial minefield, fortification, pipeline, railway and port statements. None of these specific passages by itself defines a blanket opponent-facing live-state or dummy-identity disclosure. Land §3.6 and source-specific exceptions remain independently applicable.
- The no-new-disclosure conclusion is explicitly bounded to the inspected scenario sections and search terms; it is not an absence claim about other rules, hidden state, teammate protocols, indirect wording or later editions. ## Recheck of corrected wording

Visually rechecked scenario PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29. Corrected census now preserves **may place** for both Axis's two groups of three and Commonwealth's one group of three, with the respective Dump 1/2 and Dump 1 placement rules.
