# Abstract supply capture: bounded policy alternatives

**Status: decision material, not an adopted rule.** Keep capture unsupported for executable play until entry legality, §10.29 applicability and immediate/free use are resolved together. This comparison follows the [continuation](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-continuation.md) and its [review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-continuation-review.md); it does not establish a reachable Graziani capture or select an owner's post-capture status.

## Source constraints

[Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) §32.13 says **any enemy combat unit entering** a Supply Unit's hex captures it and its supplies are used **immediately and freely**. The clause does not require a ZOC or the unit to be alone. Section 8.13 generally forbids entry into a hex containing an enemy unit; the relation between those provisions needs a ruling. An attack, adjacency or non-combat entry alone does not satisfy §32.13.

Section 10.29 captures a qualifying non-combat unit **alone** in enemy ZOC, with **no strength of any type**, at any time during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase. Whether an abstract Supply Unit is “such a unit,” whether its cargo counts as strength, and what counts against “alone” remain unresolved. A qualifying ZOC must actually exist; adjacency and zero stacking alone do not establish the predicates. Section 10.29 also restricts Truck Convoy entry into enemy ZOC without a friendly combat unit already in the hex, and voluntary non-combat entry into an unoccupied enemy-ZOC hex. Check those restrictions in any witness. Its capture sentence does not expressly require entry or repeat §32.13's immediate/free-use phrase.

Section 3.36's instant HQ capture, no Capability Point expenditure and one Prisoner Point are HQ-specific. They neither define §10.29's “alone” nor supply capture's cost or consequence. Section 32.18 removes a dummy on discovery, but reviewed text does not enumerate all discovery triggers. Section 32.56 separately addresses motorization assigned to a captured unit; co-location alone does not establish assignment.

## Decisions and alternatives

These alternatives fill gaps in the checked text; none is presented as a printed rule. A complete policy must pick compatible choices and preserve the stated triggers, including immediate/free use on an established §32.13 capture.

| Question | Bounded alternatives | Effect and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry legality | **Specific exception:** allow otherwise legal enemy combat entry against a Supply Unit under §32.13, subject to a separately defined treatment of other occupants. **General prohibition:** retain §8.13's prohibition unless another established exception permits entry. | The first gives the capture clause an entry path without creating a general right to enter occupied hexes. The second does not erase §32.13 if a separately legal entry occurs. Neither establishes removal of defenders, mixed occupancy or reachability. |
| §10.29 applicability | **Inclusive:** treat abstract Supply Units as eligible non-combat units and explicitly define strength and alone tests. **Exclusive:** do not classify them as “such a unit” for this provision; keep §32.13 independently applicable. | Inclusive does not equate no combat capability with no strength or make all adjacent supply capturable. Exclusive is a project interpretation, not proof of an exemption. Test other counters, markers and cargo under the selected definitions. |
| Exposure timing, if applicable | **After each atomic state change:** evaluate phase, actual ZOC and all predicates after every relevant change. **Before and after each atomic change:** also evaluate its predecessor state, with repeated matching observations deduplicated. | Both must cover qualifying exposure at any time in the enemy Movement/Combat Phase. Define phase entry and exit, including exposure already present on entry, and expose every relevant intermediate state inside a larger action. A predecessor check must use its own phase and cannot retroactively capture across a boundary. Deduplicate repeated observations. A phase-end-only check is inconsistent with the stated timing. |
| Strictly earlier ZOC capture | **Immediate-use extension:** apply a separately specified immediate/free-use operation to a §10.29 supply capture. **Entry-only use:** do not apply that operation solely because §10.29 fired; specify the remaining capture outcome separately. | The extension is a policy, not an express §10.29 consequence. Either choice must define post-capture owner or control, target status, stock and disclosure before later entry is adjudicated; no particular outcome is assumed. Entry-only cannot invent free use or defer an actual §32.13 use. Once captured, evaluate a later entry against its then-current target and status; do not capture the same identity again merely because entry follows. |
| Same atomic event meets both triggers | **Combined resolution:** record both applicable grounds and resolve one capture, including §32.13 immediate/free use when its actual entry predicate is met. **Ordered subevents:** define and version an order with intermediate state; evaluate each trigger against that state, applying §32.13 if its entry predicate actually occurs against the Supply Unit. | The source does not prescribe a tie-break. Ordered subevents may change which predicates hold; specify the predecessor each rule sees, whether actual entry still targets an enemy Supply Unit and the resulting status before the next subevent. Avoid manufacturing or suppressing an entry to obtain a preferred result. Neither option authorizes duplicate capture or required use. These deduplication constraints are proposed engineering invariants, not printed rules. |
| Immediate use and remainder | **Actual allocation:** specify eligible beneficiaries and immediate amounts, account for any stock debit and retain or remove the remainder under an explicit rule. **Charge waiver:** specify which otherwise eligible immediate use is free, its relation to stock and any remainder. | Define actor, amounts, timing, range/trace, ordinary expenditure interaction and later status for either choice. “Free” alone does not establish no depletion, full depletion, a CP waiver, unlimited range or an ordinary debit. A zero allocation or waiver with no actual immediate use cannot silently satisfy §32.13; if no compatible use can be computed, including when no recipient is eligible or stock is zero, retain the unsupported gate. Do not postpone all use after an established §32.13 capture. §32.15 removes a unit when both balances reach zero. See [use analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-captured-supply-use.md). |
| Discovery collision | **Discovery first:** define which observation discovers a dummy and remove it on discovery before evaluating a remaining real target. **Capture-trigger observation:** define whether and when a qualifying trigger itself discovers a dummy, then resolve discovery/removal and any real capture accordingly. | Both require a source-compatible discovery ruling; neither assumes a marker's hidden identity or cargo. A discovered dummy is removed under §32.18. Define what each side may learn and do not issue a real-stock capture and dummy removal for the same identity. |

The alternatives are not exhaustive. In particular, an entry exception must specify how it interacts with a defender and applicable movement restrictions; a use rule must settle allocation and conservation before executable admission. A policy that cannot compute a source-required outcome remains unsupported, rather than silently producing a fabricated stock, ownership or public observation.

## Transition evidence and discriminating cases

For an adopted version, record authoritative event and phase identity, predecessor and resulting state, actual occupancy and qualifying ZOC, rule/policy version, trigger grounds, discovery state, target identity, assigned motorization, and any use allocation and balances. Apply one atomic outcome per identity; retries and replay must reproduce the same outcome without a second capture, debit or discovery. Publish only side-authorized observations; action availability, rejection and error behavior, event payloads and intelligence-plane views must not reveal hidden dummy identity, stock or opposing state. Compare otherwise equivalent real and dummy histories. If withholding execution would itself disclose hidden identity, define a disclosure-safe unsupported path before admission.

Use synthetic cases, conditional on independently established legal predecessors, to distinguish choices:

1. Combat entry, non-combat entry and adjacency without entry; add an actual defender and check entry legality separately.
2. Qualifying versus absent ZOC; enemy versus other phase; exposure already present at phase entry and exposure created and removed within one larger action; phase exit and predecessor rechecks; lone unit versus another counter, marker or defender; varying cargo and strength.
3. A strictly earlier §10.29 capture followed by entry versus both predicates in one atomic event; compare selected sequencing, one capture and immediate use only where justified.
4. Real versus dummy under each selected discovery rule, without presuming either identity from a visible marker; compare authorized views and retry behavior.
5. Partial and zero balances, assigned versus merely co-located motorization, and immediate recipients with differing range or trace; include no eligible recipient, check exact immediate allocations and accounting on replay, conservation and both-zero removal only under selected accounting.
6. Vary HQ combat values including parenthesized values, presence of combat units in its hex, and placement in enemy combat-unit ZOC independently; check §3.36 separately, including its stated CP and Prisoner Point consequences.

These are proposed decision tests, not demonstrated reachable states or executed tests. Preserve source facts and chosen policy separately in versioned provenance.
