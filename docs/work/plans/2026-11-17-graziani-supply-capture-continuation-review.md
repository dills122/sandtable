# Supply capture continuation: trigger review

**Disposition:** No material misquotation found in the three capture predicates. Keep capture blocked pending a ruling; the continuation correctly treats applicability, entry legality, use and event precedence as unresolved. Two scope refinements make the comparison more complete.

- **§10.29 entry restrictions:** The capture sentence follows separate restrictions: Truck Convoys cannot enter an enemy ZOC unless a friendly combat unit already occupies the hex, and non-combat units cannot voluntarily enter an unoccupied hex in an enemy ZOC. The continuation's instruction to determine legal occupancy and actual ZOC is appropriate, but the table does not name these restrictions. They matter to proposed witnesses and do not by themselves resolve whether an abstract Supply Unit qualifies as “such a unit.”
- **§3.36 consequence:** The HQ clause specifies instant capture, no Capability Point expenditure for that capture, and treatment as one Prisoner Point. The continuation accurately describes its predicates and limits it to HQs, but omits these consequences. They are HQ-specific and do not establish a free capture or prisoner outcome for supply.

**Precedence:** Section 32.13 explicitly states capture when any enemy combat unit enters the Supply Unit's hex and supplies are used immediately and freely. Section 10.29 states capture of a qualifying non-combat unit alone in enemy ZOC with no strength of any type during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase; it does not expressly require entry or repeat §32.13's use clause. Neither passage establishes a tie-break for simultaneous triggers or authorizes treating a later entry as a second capture of an already captured identity. The continuation's event-state and duplicate-avoidance guidance is a proposed transition discipline, not printed rule text. Section 8.13's general occupancy prohibition and any specific exception still require interpretation; no automatic priority follows merely from ordering the table.
