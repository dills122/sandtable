# NZ assignment example: September erratum and Graziani

**Finding:** Errata §19.14 identifies the example's two different parents: 1st King's Royal Rifle Corps (1 KRRC) would be **attached to 2nd New Zealand Division** and **assigned to 7th Armoured Division**. The original example is conditional: if detached and sent to support NZ, then absorbed by NZ. It is not a scenario setup instruction, a 6th NZ Brigade arrival, or an actual change of assignment. The correction illustrates an existing distinction in §19.13; no new general rule or independent policy follows.

| Evidence | Supported boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Land §§19.12–.14, p. 29; errata §19.14, p. 2 | Assignment and attachment can point to different parents simultaneously, each at most one; attachment requires the same hex and representation by the parent, assignment does not require co-location. Section 19.14 also permits a parent formation itself to be assigned/attached to a higher formation. The corrected last line names NZ as attached parent and 7th Armoured as assigned parent for the hypothetical 1 KRRC. It does not itself effect the attachment or waive limits. |
| Scenario §60.41, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 | 1 KRRC is listed at **C3922**, with `7Spt/7`; 7th Armoured Division is at **D3612**, with the previously listed division units detached. The 2nd NZ Division HQ and listed 4th NZ Brigade and supporting units are at **Alexandria E3613 and/or E3714**. This initial separation does not enact the example's later absorption by NZ. Nor does location alone settle every setup relationship, legal future movement, or available NZ attachment slot. |
| Commonwealth OA p. 6; Land §19.42 | OA places 1 KRRC within the 7th Armoured organization. The example's assigned-division shorthand does not erase the scenario's more specific `7Spt/7` organization or prove a new direct-parent edge. Attaching a non-assigned unit under §19.42 requires co-location, applicable §§19.4–.5 limits, and two CP for each participant during the owner's Reorganization Segment; its prior movement and all other applicable conditions must be established. Check nested-parent representation separately before choosing an executable attachment. |
| Commonwealth reinforcement schedule p. 3; OA p. 18; Land §20.11 p. 31 | Schedule lists 6th NZ Field Artillery GT2/3 and 6 NZ Brigade GT6/3; OA lists the field artillery `3/2` and brigade HQ plus 24th, 25th and 26th battalions `3/6`. The Land example instead puts brigade and artillery at `3/2`. Errata §19.14 corrects a **different example** and does not address either arrival date. Existing [arrival source review](2026-11-18-graziani-nz-arrival-source-next-review.md) retains the conflict and the track-based working interpretation; neither source is independent edition evidence. |

For future implementation, represent assignment and attachment as distinct relationships and verify their source-legal identity, co-location, cost, capacity and timing. That is already supported by §19.13 and related cases; the erratum adds names to this hypothetical, not a new 1 KRRC-to-NZ startup event, automatic later event, or a rule settling 6th NZ timing. No legal route, actual attachment, source-precedence decision or reachable witness established here.
