# Independent review: Commonwealth air supply division-row scope

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition:** One wording issue; conditional boundary arithmetic and scenario transcription otherwise supported within stated scope.

## Finding

The plan says categorically that neither a brigade HQ alone nor a detached divisional subordinate establishes a division's location. Land §32.37 distinguishes an occupying division from the separate qualifying division or brigade HQ counter, but does not define how to locate a dispersed division or explicitly exclude every subordinate as evidence of its location. State this as an unselected interpretation: a qualifying HQ or subordinate alone does not *prove* an actual division occupies that row; resolving formation occupancy needs a source or ruling. The plan already acknowledges this ambiguity elsewhere, so avoid implying the source settles it.

## Checked

Visually inspected Land PDF p. 43 / printed p. 43 and scenario PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27. Land §32.37 specifies westernmost hexrow occupied by a friendly division, shell or full, and separately requires a friendly division or brigade HQ counter at destination, excluding battle-group HQ. Same-row equality follows under the plan's explicitly conditional destination-boundary reading; the text describes transport and does not settle the origin-west case. No two-division or non-shell condition appears in §32.37.

Scenario §60.41 lists 7th Armored Division at D3612, 4th Indian Division at D3615, and 2nd New Zealand Division HQ at Alexandria E3613 and/or 3714 with other listed forces. Setup does not establish actual placement and formation status at the transport phase. A qualifying HQ alone does not prove an actual occupying division; the same-row deduction holds if that division is independently established at destination. Conditional arithmetic using a common west-to-east ordering is sound; no D/E sheet registration was verified. No endpoint, shipment, cross-sheet seam, or complete legality is established.
