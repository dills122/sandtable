# Independent review: Aresca CV33 close assault

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Verdict:** The draft's 1 offensive / 2 defensive CV33 rating is supported. No source evidence supports a categorical light-tank exclusion. The number resolves the weapon rating; it does not settle HQ participation or every condition for a ZOC.

Independent source inspection preceded reading the draft. On Axis OA PDF p. 6, Aresca (1st) Regiment HQ has ID `d` and `1 × CV33`; XXI(L) has ID `k` and `9 × CV33`. Scenario PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.31 puts Aresca at C3919, with all except XXI detached. Axis OA p. 26 §4.46b gives HQ `d` no intrinsic close-assault figure and permits one tank TOE strength point; battalion `k` permits nine. Axis OA p. 29 §4.48 explicitly prints `CV 33 (L3/35)` as **1/2** under Close Assault Off/Def. Both figures are unparenthesized. These are weapon-system ratings per TOE point, not a guaranteed effective formation strength.

The draft correctly treats HQ participation as unresolved. Land p. 6 §3.31 says an HQ represents attached combat units and fights with their combined strengths; §3.35 restricts use of tanks assigned to armored division and brigade HQs while combat units remain attached. Section 9.22 on p. 17 says Italian regiments can be brigade-equivalent for game purposes, with organization determined by printed stacking value rather than name. Thus the regiment designation alone does not establish whether §3.35 applies to this HQ; a specific counter/equivalence determination or explicit ruling is needed. This distinction does not alter the printed CV33 rating.

Land p. 19 §§11.32–11.34 support the draft's illustrative nine × two = 18 **raw** defensive points, before conversion to actual combat strength. This is conditional on unchanged and eligible XXI TOE. Section 15.4 on p. 23 applies a combined-arms reduction to **actual** close-assault strength of unsupported tanks; do not silently apply that reduction to the raw ZOC threshold. Section 10.11 on p. 17 separately excludes HQs without attached combat units from projecting a ZOC; §10.15 requires at least 10 raw defensive close-assault points, while §10.21 on p. 18 restricts projection across certain terrain. Other state and eligibility requirements still apply.

**Suggested draft clarification:** cite §9.22 alongside §3.35, and distinguish raw ZOC threshold from actual close-assault adjustments. Neither changes the rating finding.
