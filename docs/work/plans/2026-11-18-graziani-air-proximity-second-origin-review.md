# Tobruk starting proximity boundary: source review

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Independently rechecked the [second-origin note](2026-11-18-graziani-air-proximity-second-origin.md) against the same original scenario, Land rules and Map C scans. **Disposition:** setup values and bounded row-difference argument are supported. Keep the bound explicitly tied to the stated same-sheet geometric convention; it is not a complete opposing-force or cross-sheet exclusion.

- Scenario §60.92(B), printed pp. 28–29 / PDF pp. 4–5, lists **three** Axis regular Supply Units at Tobruk C4807, each with 15 Ammo and 50 Fuel. Section 60.41 places 2nd Scots Guards, labeled infantry, and 31st Field Artillery Regiment at Sidi Barrani C4131. Land §3.23 includes infantry-type units in combat units. These are setup entries, not proof that either counter and its composition persist through a later Organization snapshot.
- Map C shows the Tobruk grid and surrounding labels; city artwork obscures the `4807` numeral. The source's named locator and neighboring numbered cells support the identity; the printed number is not independently readable there. The [Sidi Barrani reading](2026-11-17-graziani-c4131-six-sides.md) identifies its numbered C4131 cell. The locally visible regular hex pattern supports a first-component change of at most one per adjacent step. On the stated regular **same-sheet** hex-count model, first-component difference `48 − 41 = 7` is a lower bound: C4131 cannot be within five of C4807 under that model. It is not an exact distance or a passable route.
- The other fixed Map C locations in §60.41 listed by the note are C3926, C3922, C3721, C3520, C3320 and C3020. Their first components differ from 48 by 9, 9, 11, 13, 15 and 18 respectively, all above five under the same model. This checks the listed fixed C locations, not all possible sources of enemy combat units or a complete setup-state audit. Map D/E choices, alternative placements, organization, later movement and unknown forces remain separate.
- A lower-bound argument using Map C row numbers is directly about paths represented within that same-sheet convention. A global distance interpretation permitting a cross-sheet shortcut, unmodeled boundary, alias or other nonlocal transition needs separate exclusion; “no seam used” in the proposed calculation is not itself proof that every competing path avoids a seam. This precision does not establish such a shortcut exists. Land §32.37 itself says an enemy combat unit within five hexes of the **origin** bars air transport from it, but does not define the complete coordinate metric in that sentence.

No positive enemy proximity witness was demonstrated in the bounded list, and that is not proof of an empty five-hex radius or a permitted air transport. Phase timing, 12-Fuel source, eligible origin/destination, HQ, direction and Game-Turn allowance remain independent conditions. No legal movement or disclosure decision follows.
