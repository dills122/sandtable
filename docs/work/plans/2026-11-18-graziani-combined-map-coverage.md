## Evidence limits

Paired readings are informed checks of the same scan, not independent-edition corroboration. Observations include positive marks, exposed grid, obscuration and conflicts; the batches use different conventions and their positive or unknown counts cannot be combined as equivalent classifications. Missing pinned records do not prove clear sides. The [facility audit](2026-11-18-graziani-facility-side-coverage-audit.md) retains the `D3516–D3517` railroad correction and other unresolved source-versus-pin questions. `C3419–C3320` remains obscured; blue checks, whole-cell terrain assignments, coast, seams and physical boundaries need their own evidence and rules treatment.

These totals establish neither complete routes nor setup, control, capacity, legal movement, incoming forces or ZOC. A bounded playable area requires rule-relevant entries, exits, placements, external interactions and routes to be accounted for; full-map certification additionally requires source-backed coverage of applicable sheets, features, seams, boundaries and corrections. No runtime or map data changed; no gameplay tests run.
