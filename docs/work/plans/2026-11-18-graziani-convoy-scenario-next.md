# Graziani convoy qualifier: starting training and later snapshots

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Bounded follow-up to the [Indian–Australian pair](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-pair-next.md) and its [review](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-pair-review.md). Scenario setup gives a concrete **in-training** status whose movement and trainer conditions matter before later convoy lookups. This is not a demonstrated eligible trainer, legal move, arrival or convoy attack.

## Direct source relationship

Scenario §60.41 starts the 6th Australian Division **in training at Cairo and/or Helwan (E1430)**, with 19th Australian Brigade and two named field-artillery units excluded. Land §17.32 identifies permitted Commonwealth training areas, including Cairo and Helwan. Section 17.35 requires an eligible combat battalion in the same hex during each training OpStage: a designated-morale instructor, at least **one-third** assigned TOE strength, with type requirements depending on the trainee. The earlier half-strength transcription in this note and its review was incorrect; see the [training source follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-training-source-next.md). The [review](2026-11-18-graziani-convoy-scenario-next-review.md) notes that continuous presence versus leaving and returning is not explicit. Setup's training label alone does not certify the actual instructor, co-location, its morale/strength or a completed training stage.

Land §5.2's Organization Phase includes a Training Segment: units beginning or continuing training may not **voluntarily move during the remainder of that OpStage**, except Reaction. Section 17.33 says expending any CP during an OpStage interrupts training. Section 17.36 says units need not train and may enter combat with their lesser morale; it does not expressly decide whether this scenario's initial “in training” entry can be canceled before a particular first-stage action. The Reaction exception is not a general promise that any reaction is legal, and an actual CP expenditure must be accounted for in training status. No source here says interrupted progress automatically resets to zero or that six training stages must be consecutive.

The Axis chart §32.66 tests current non-shell division positions for its convoy lookup; being in training is not itself the chart's shell test. Land §9.26/.27 use attached brigade equivalents and shell status. The division's position, representation and composition can change independently of morale/training, and §5.2 allows Organization reorganization before that OpStage's Naval Convoy Arrival Phase. A selected training status therefore does not freeze the particular Indian–Australian pair or guarantee that its initial formation baseline still applies when a convoy is attacked.

## Conditional time boundary

Assume a valid Organization snapshot where an eligible 6th Australian subject **continues training**, a qualified instructor and all other training prerequisites hold, and no applicable special rule changes the outline. Ordinary voluntary movement for that subject is then barred for the remainder of that OpStage under §5.2; the stated Reaction exception and other non-voluntary possibilities require their own rules. This constrains a proposed westward movement in that particular stage; it does not prove the subject remains at its location under every event or that it cannot be chosen not to train in a later stage.

For a later **actual** OpStage convoy lookup, §5.2 places that stage's Organization Phase before Naval Convoy Arrival and the later A/B movement phases; §32.63 describes attacking arriving Axis convoys in the Naval Convoy Phase. Thus use the actual division state at the applicable lookup after relevant prior-stage events and any preceding Organization changes, not the earlier setup label or an assumed later movement. If no convoy arrives, or the Commonwealth does not exercise its attack option, there is no attack lookup to certify. GT1's omitted-versus-played opening preamble remains the [existing startup ambiguity](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-pair-next.md); this observation does not create an opening convoy or force continued training.

A source-sensitive test can compare otherwise identical hypothetical stage snapshots: continuing valid training with an ordinary voluntary move request; an eligible Reaction and its CP/training consequences; a separately accepted no-training choice; and an Organization change that alters actual attached brigades before a later convoy lookup. All need the applicable action hierarchy, current unit identity, trainer, geometry and phase state. None is an executed test, fixed stage outcome, actual route or proof of a bomb-points row.
