# Sheferzen initial contact: flexible combat-unit placement

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Finding:** No reviewed source supplies a complete legal witness placing an extra Axis or Commonwealth combat unit in C3419, C3320 or C3520 at initial setup. Nor do the reviewed passages prove that every final stack is a singleton. The explicit placements are Sheferzen Garrison at C3419, 2nd Rifle Brigade at C3320, and 1st RTR at C3520. The strongest apparent flexible land-unit pools do not establish an additional occupant at these three Map C hexes under the qualified country reading below. This finding concerns initial setup, before movement or later construction; it does not settle first combat or ZOC.
