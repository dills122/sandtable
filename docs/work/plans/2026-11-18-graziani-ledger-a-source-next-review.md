# SQ-06 towing follow-up: source review

**Disposition:** The [follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-a-source-next.md) accurately reports the checked wording and sequence. Preserve one additional interaction explicitly: §21.55 permits capture-time placement in any direction, while §21.67 states a nearest-facility direction for towed subjects. The checked text does not itself resolve how these instructions combine. Do not apply the ordinary direction rule to capture-time placement without a separate interpretation.

Visually inspected [original Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 12 and 34, with independent renders. Verified local SHA-256 `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99` before inspection. This is an informed reading of the same scan, not an independent edition or exhaustive errata search.

- **Timing:** §5.2 K lists Towing Segment first, Maintenance Segment second; maintenance covers broken-down or destroyed vehicles not towed in that phase. §22.13(c) also forbids repair for vehicles towed during the Repair Phase. §21.66 says a broken-down vehicle that unsuccessfully undergoes field repair may not be towed in that Repair Phase. The note correctly retains the interaction rather than reversing the printed outline or inventing a later towing segment.
- **Subject and destination:** §21.67 states broken-down vehicles and destroyed tanks must be towed towards the nearest temporary or major repair facility if being towed. It does not specify distance metric, equal distances or inaccessible facilities on that line. §21.62 says only vehicles not undergoing field or other repair may be towed, and places towing in the “Repair Stage” except instances such as §21.55; preserve its printed stage wording rather than silently treating it as a separately defined phase.
- **Capture and route:** §21.55 permits captured broken-down vehicles to be placed (towed) at the instant of capture up to three hexes in any direction, with the path barred through enemy units, enemy ZOCs or impassable terrain. §21.64 separately restricts towing from enemy-controlled hexes, with capture-time exception, and states friendly units negate enemy ZOCs in its scope. These are distinct from proving a normal towing route or deciding whether the §21.67 nearest-facility instruction qualifies §21.55's any-direction wording. The note's capture-time versus later Repair Phase distinction is supported; the two direction provisions deserve explicit reconciliation as well.
- **Additional limits:** §21.63 gives a broken-down or destroyed marker 10 CP per Repair Phase and treats its towing as a medium truck, subject to its references to §§22.6–.7; §21.65 states towing has no fuel, stores or water cost and does not undergo Breakdown. These printed clauses do not establish an actual reachable facility or map route.

The follow-up appropriately leaves facility identity, applicable recovery exceptions, Land-only repair resources and actual scenario witness open. This review did not inspect §§22.6–.7, errata, all route rules or runtime behavior. No data edits or gameplay tests.
