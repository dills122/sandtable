# Port erratum: personnel and abstract-supply scope

**Finding:** Corrected §30.58 supplies a direct Land-rule link to harbor blocking, but its operative threshold governs **personnel**; it does not replace §32.36's distinct destination condition for an abstract Supply Unit. Neither the link nor this comparison selects a Land-only clearance lifecycle.

The [September 1979 erratum](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2, changes §30.58's reference to §55.2. Its host [Land rule](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed p. 42, is within §30.5 naval transport of troops. Section 30.58 says blockage does not affect receiving personnel until capacity is reduced below two-thirds (67%), rounded up; below that point the port may neither receive nor send personnel. The corrected reference points to harbor-blocking rules, but the correction does not change that case's personnel subject into Supply Units or prescribe a new supply threshold. Section 30.5's introductory statement separately says it concerns personnel and points elsewhere for supply; a separate §30.5 erratum corrects its printed `00.00` placeholder to §56.0 ([review](2026-11-18-graziani-port-clearance-decision-input-review.md)). Do not infer that the §30.58 threshold applies to abstract supply solely because both use a port.

Land §32.36, p. 43, separately requires the destination's current efficiency to permit use of at least 50% of listed capacity for the one Supply Unit sea transfer. It substitutes for combat-unit transport from the origin and into the destination, rather than declaring that all personnel transfers and Supply Unit shipments use an identical eligibility predicate. As a **conditional comparison only**, if a port's effective capacity has independently been established at 60% of its listed capacity for both measures, 60% satisfies the §32.36 destination threshold but is below §30.58's personnel threshold. This does not establish an available port, origin/destination control, a reachable trip, or how capacity was computed. Whether and how the substitution and other transfer restrictions interact requires its own ruling.

The corrected cross-reference strengthens a case for consulting §55.2 when adjudicating blockage in a retained Land transport context. It does not specify that every referenced detailed rule, resource charge or timing applies unchanged to the abstract game. Conversely, the abstract supply rule's limited supply scope does not by itself erase §30.58's separate personnel wording. Section 32.11's supply abstraction, §32.36's explicit efficiency predicate and the corrected cross-reference must be reconciled operation by operation. No precedence, clearance cost, automatic recovery or state-transition policy is selected here; see the [earlier source review](2026-11-17-graziani-port-land-only-scope-review.md) and [erratum check](2026-11-18-graziani-port-erratum-review.md).
