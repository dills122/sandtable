# Sheferzen: counter, size and stacking hierarchy

**Finding:** Land §9.22 expressly directs players to use the **printed Stacking Point value**, cross-referenced with §9.4, rather than a unit's designation or name, to identify organizational level. The original `Scfzrn` counter's printed `0` is consistent with §9.4's full-company row and the `dd` characteristics chart's company-equivalent heading, while the same counter's `II` size symbol and OA's “Bn” give battalion labels. This is strong source support for a company-equivalent **working interpretation**, not an explicit Sheferzen correction or a complete rule for every purpose. Preserve the conflicting source fields and the garrison/shell qualifications.

## Evidence and scope

- [Counter sheet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf) PDF p. 1, section 2, Italian right block, `Scfzrn` between `23 DPtl` and `FtMad`, shows `G`, `II` and unparenthesized `0`. [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) PDF/printed p. 7 §4.21 calls these fields garrison indicator, organization size and stacking value; p. 8's key says `II` is battalion. See the [counter-face check](2026-11-17-graziani-sheferzen-counter-check.md). The counter scan is an original-looking scan, not an independently authenticated physical piece.
- [Axis chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf) PDF p. 14 lists Sceferzen Oasis Garrison **Bn** with ID `dd`, `N`, `D`; p. 26 §4.46b groups `dd` under **Infantry Coy-Eq**, maximum TOE 1 and defensive close-assault rating 2. Land §4.45, p. 10, says `N` means it starts at its maximum possible TOE strength. Thus, conditional on that baseline and no specific correction, the available one TOE is full rather than a below-half-strength battalion shell; this does not by itself decide how to reconcile the size labels.
- Land §9.22, p. 17, warns against relying on unit designation/name and specifically tells players to use printed stacking value with §9.4 to determine organization. [Common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) PDF/printed p. 2 §9.4 gives a full company value `0`, a full battalion value `1`, and a shell battalion value `0`. Land §9.26 defines a battalion shell by less than half maximum TOE; §9.28 reduces a shell one level for unit differentiation and other size-relevant rules. A printed zero is therefore **not universally proof** of a company: shell and special cases matter. Here the conditional `N`/maximum evidence plus `dd` heading align with company-equivalent, but do not formally erase the `II` mark.
- Land §9.16(a) gives a `G` unit free stacking in its assigned city/village; its example gives a garrison battalion zero **there** and one elsewhere. Thus location-specific effective stacking and printed organizational value are distinct questions; do not apply a home-location zero as proof that every garrison is company-sized or use it to establish all away-from-home values. `Scfzrn` itself prints zero. Actual lawful departure and any changed organization remain separate.
- Land §10.11, pp. 17–18, requires a combat unit above battalion-equivalent (more than one Stacking Point) or a hex containing more than one Stacking Point, subject to other restrictions. §10.15 denies ZOC if the hex totals less than 10 raw defensive Close Assault Points. At the above standalone one-TOE/defense-2 baseline, raw defense is 2 and the face prints zero; both are conditional and do not account for other admissible co-located units, stacking interpretation, terrain or scenario changes. Counter `II` versus chart `Coy-Eq` does not by itself make the singleton qualify under §10.11. §10.21 projection and all other state still matter.

## Corrections, decision and tests

Text-searched [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) for `Scfzrn`, Sheferzen/Sceferzen, garrison, counter and relevant §9.2/§10.1 references; found no explicit Sheferzen size or stacking correction. Text extraction and spelling search may miss material; no claim of a complete physical-counter addendum search, later official correction, or unambiguous source precedence for all size-dependent rules.

A versioned implementation could follow the §9.22/§9.4 direction and chart heading as a provisional company-equivalent interpretation while retaining original `II`, OA `Bn`, printed `0`, garrison condition and provenance. Do not make that an official source correction, or generalize a singleton stack conclusion to setup choices or other time states. Independent review should test full vs shell, home vs away, counter value vs effective garrison stacking, same-hex additional counters, 9/10 raw-defense threshold, projection terrain and replay of the selected ruleset. No map, runtime, readiness or tests changed.
