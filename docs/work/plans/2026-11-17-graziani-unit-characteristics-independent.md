# Graziani unit characteristics: independent transcription

Source: Commonwealth charts PDF `/private/tmp/sandtable-cw-charts.pdf`, SHA-256 `f75d074727071025f3145b273921ec03882b8b57cb4a429485995d4cf5255981`, verified 2026-11-17. Page references below are PDF page numbers. Also checked scenario PDF p. 3 (printed p. 27) and September 1979 errata PDF p. 1. This transcription preceded comparison with existing research.

## Reading the charts

OA key, CW p. 5: `N` for non-tank/gun units means maximum TOE points for the ID code; `D` means deployed at the first operation stage of game-turn 1. Parenthesized morale is arrival/deployment morale; unparenthesized value is basic morale. Indentation in OA indicates parentage at arrival, not necessarily scenario attachment. ID characteristics are from §4.46a, CW pp. 22-23. Close assault values below are offensive/defensive. Dash means not applicable. A `+` after CPA means historically supplied enough trucks to motorize all components, not an arithmetic bonus; motorization changes CPA. Scenario placement and attachment are separate from OA grouping.

## 6th Australian Division

CW p. 13 gives basic morale +1, deployment morale -3. Its deployed infantry battalions all have ID `p`, `N`, `D`: 2/1 Aus, 2/2 Aus, 2/3 Aus under 16 Aus Infantry Bde HQ; 2/5 Aus, 2/6 Aus, 2/7 Aus under 17 Aus Infantry Bde HQ. ID `p` (§4.46a p. 22) has CPA 10, close assault 1/2, maximum TOE 6; `N` therefore implies 6 initial TOE points. No anti-air, barrage, anti-armor, vulnerability, or armor protection rating appears in this row.

The OA also lists 2/4 Aus, 2/8 Aus, 2/11 Aus under 19 Aus Infantry Bde HQ, each `p`, `N`, arrival 2/9 (operation stage 2, game-turn 9), not `D`. Scenario p. 3 places 6th Australian Division in training at Cairo and/or Helwan (E1430), explicitly less 19 Aus Bde and 2/1 and 2/2 Australian Field Artillery. Do not include those excluded units as deployed formation members from this setup.

Other deployed OA entries: 6 Aus Div HQ `a`, `N`, `D`; 16 Aus Bde HQ `f`, `N`, `D`; 17 Aus Bde HQ `e`, `N`, `D`; 6 Aus Div Cavalry `ll`, `N`, `D`. Chart: `a` CPA 30, no TOE maximum printed; `f` CPA 20, anti-armor 2, vulnerability 1, assault 0/1, max TOE 1; `e` CPA 20, anti-armor 1, vulnerability 1, assault 0/1, max TOE 1; `ll` CPA 45, armor protection 1, assault 2/3, max TOE 8. Thus N implies 1, 1 and 8 points for f, e and ll respectively; do not invent a numeric initial TOE for a where chart supplies no maximum. The OA lists 19 Aus Bde HQ `a` 2/9, 2/1 AFld `x` 6 × 25-pounder 1/11, and 2/2 AFld `x` 3 × 18-pounder plus 3 × 4.5-inch howitzer 1/7; their later arrival and scenario exclusion do not establish deployment guns for other units.

## Matruh-relevant battalions

Scenario p. 3 places Matruh Garrison at D3714, attaching 1 Essex, 1 Durham Light Infantry, and 1 South Staffordshires. September errata p. 1 §4.44b item 2 adds 1 Buffs and 1 Hampshires as assigned to Matruh Garrison for campaign and Italian scenarios. Scenario group/attachment does not make these infantry OA rows identical in ID or parent assignment.

OA p. 9 note f says Essex and Durham were not assigned to 23rd Brigade until June 1941; their scenario attachment to Matruh should not be confused with OA indentation under 23rd HQ. Scenario's `23/70` identifiers do not override this note.

## 1st French Motor Marine company

CW p. 21 unassigned Allied units: 1st Company, Fr Mtr Marine, counter abbreviation `1`, ID `v`, `N`, printed arrival `3/65`, basic morale +1. Note identifies it as a company of 1st Free French Motor Marine Battalion. Errata p. 1 §4.44b item 3 corrects arrival to `D`; scenario p. 3 places it at C3926 with 2 medium truck points. ID `v`: CPA `10+`, assault 1/1, maximum TOE 1, hence initial 1 TOE by N; other listed combat columns are dashes. `+` has motorization meaning above; it does not itself encode a numeric motorized CPA. Do not infer a gun allocation from these rows.

## Comparison with prior research

The independent values above agree with [source material research](2026-11-16-graziani-source-material-research.md) for the Australian and five Matruh battalions, and with [arrival scope](2026-11-12-graziani-arrival-scope.md) for French identity, ID, strength, CPA, assault and erratum. No material numerical disagreement found in those compared passages. Existing research correctly distinguishes scenario attachment from OA parent labels and identifies a separate assignment conflict: after comparison I inspected CW OA p. 10, which says Matruh Garrison may have no assigned units and up to six attached, while September errata explicitly assigns Buffs and Hampshires. The prior provisional specific-erratum exception is an interpretation, not a source-resolved rule; this transcription does not settle it or infer placement solely from the erratum.
