# Review: recovery-counter identity and later setup

**Disposition:** No material transcription or scenario-scope error found in the [residual check](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-a-residual-scan.md). The similar names, unique rule counter and chronology strengthen a crosswalk, but the inspected sources do not explicitly equate all three names. Exceptional Graziani introduction remains unexhausted.

- [Axis §4.43b](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 2, places **Tank Recovery Squadron** in the German GT24 / OpStage 3 row. [Land §22.7](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 35, describes one **German Mobile Tank Repair Squad** counter, treated like the Commonwealth Desert Tank Delivery Squadrons except that it repairs only German, not Italian or captured Commonwealth, tanks. The rule does not explicitly name that track entry.
- [Counter PDF](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Counters.pdf), p. 2 Section 4, right block top row, shows a German-area counter labeled **TANK RECOVERY**, immediately after `DSKA` and before tank `A`; the header says one identical Section 4. The label does not print a date or the complete rule name. It supports a candidate, not a proven one-to-one identity or exhaustive inventory of all faces and printings.
- [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 3, §61.41 adds the **German Mobile Tank Recovery Squadron** in Tripolitania. [Scenario §§61.1–.2 and 61.41](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF pp. 5–6 / printed pp. 29–30, put that case in the later Desert Fox setup, with the race-for-Tobruk scenario starting GT26/3. GT24/3 is chronologically earlier. The erratum does not amend Graziani's §60.31 or independently establish a GT1–6 introduction. Section 60.22 ends Graziani GT6/3; no actual tow or repair follows.
