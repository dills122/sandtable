# September errata §§46.4, 49.13 and 49.4: source scope

**Result:** §49.13's corrected note is a detailed fuel-expenditure statement, not an express amendment of Land-only abstract rates. §49.4 adds fuel-less combat consequences, but its interaction with abstract availability remains open. §46.4 concerns anti-aircraft adjustment for aircraft density; its correction does not itself amend abstract convoy or truck-loss tables.
