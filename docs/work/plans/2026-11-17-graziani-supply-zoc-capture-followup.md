# Supply capture and enemy ZOC: bounded follow-up

**Finding:** Land §10.29 provides a distinct capture condition for a qualifying non-combat unit alone in an enemy ZOC during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase; §32.13 specifies capture of a Supply Unit when an enemy combat unit enters its hex. Neither checked passage expressly says whether the former applies to an abstract Supply Unit represented by a dump counter. No initial capture follows merely from adjacency or zero stacking value.

## Rules distinction

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 18 §10.29: truck convoys may not enter enemy ZOC unless the hex already contains a friendly combat unit. Non-combat units, including the listed bare HQs, Engineers and Squadron Ground Support Units, may not voluntarily enter an unoccupied hex in enemy ZOC. If **such a unit is alone** in enemy ZOC **at any time during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase** and has **no strength of any type**, that friendly non-combat unit is captured. The text does not express this threshold in stacking points, and does not define whether another non-combat counter or informational marker defeats “alone.” Do not replace it with “no friendly combat unit,” or equate zero stacking value with zero strength of any type.
- Printed p. 6 §3.33 says a bare HQ has zero stacking and is not a combat unit, yet can use parenthesized ratings. This illustrates why non-combat and zero stacking do not establish absence of all strength. Section 3.36 separately addresses an HQ with no combat values, parenthesized or otherwise, in a hex without combat units when an enemy combat unit places it in ZOC. Its HQ-specific wording does not redefine §10.29 “alone” or automatically govern supply. See [independent source review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-zoc-capture-followup-review.md).
- Printed p. 17 §§10.11–.15 and p. 18 §10.21 independently govern whether enemy ZOC actually exists and extends into the hex. §10.11 uses combat size/stacking and §10.15 excludes a hex totaling less than ten raw defensive close-assault points; cohesion and terrain/hexside restrictions also matter. These are **ZOC-existence conditions**, separate from §10.29's captured unit having no strength of any type. Source wording, stack interpretation and combat positioning constraints require their own evidence; adjacency alone is insufficient.
- Printed p. 43 §32.13 says a Supply Unit has no combat capabilities and, if an enemy combat unit enters its hex, it is captured and its supplies used immediately and freely. It does not state an enemy ZOC or “alone” condition. §32.12 represents it with a Supply Dump counter; §32.14 gives it zero stacking value. These statements do not expressly resolve whether “such a unit” in §10.29 includes the abstract Supply Unit, whether no combat capabilities satisfies no strength **of any type**, or whether a dump counter affects another unit being alone. Section 8.13's general restriction on entering an enemy-occupied hex adds a separate interaction requiring a ruling; do not assume a general entry exception from §10.29.
- If both provisions were determined to apply in a particular state, their triggers remain different: §10.29 is enemy-phase exposure to an existing qualifying ZOC; §32.13 is enemy combat-unit entry. The checked text does not settle simultaneous-event order, ownership bookkeeping, dummy discovery, or interaction with §32.17 destruction. Do not treat ZOC capture as proven automatic for supply or turn capture into destruction.

## Initial Graziani use

Scenario setup does not itself prove a qualifying exposed supply unit or an active enemy phase. The independently reviewed [Commonwealth contact stack](2026-11-17-graziani-commonwealth-contact-stack-review.md) distinguishes detailed §60.44 dump placement from Land-only §60.92(C)'s replacement, and identifies §59.66(C) as a conditional additional supply option. Those are placement possibilities, not proof of a chosen location, “alone,” qualifying ZOC, strength status, or a completed capture. Any initial or later application must establish mode, actual placement, occupancy, ZOC geometry and threshold, phasing side, and the unresolved §10.29/§32.13 relationship.
