# Bardia C4321 location classification

Original Map A's **Summary of Important Locations** explicitly lists Bardia `C4321` with an `X` in each of **Major City**, **Port**, **Assigned Garrison**, and **Victory Condition** columns. This table directly associates both city and port classifications with C4321; it does not itself establish rules effects or scenario setup.

The same scan's Map C depicts blue city blocks and a circled anchor at Bardia. Map A's terrain key identifies these symbols as Major City and Port. The anchor is visible in blue water **within the inferred C4321 hex**, close to its upper-right boundary with C4421; being in water does not place it in the adjacent sea hex. Artwork obscures the central hex number, so identification of the drawn hex follows surrounding numbered cells and the location table, not a legible `4321` label. Portions of city blocks are covered by Axis figures. The summary provides stronger explicit evidence for the classification than symbol position alone.
