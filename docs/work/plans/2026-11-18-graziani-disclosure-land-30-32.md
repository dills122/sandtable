# FG-01: disclosure census for Land §§30–32

**Scope:** Bounded pass through [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 41–45, for disclosure, secrecy, announced actions, hidden plans and discovery in §§30.0–32.9. It extends the partial [limited-intelligence inventory](2026-11-18-graziani-limited-intelligence-source-inventory-review.md); it does not establish a complete digital visibility contract or scenario reachability. Section 32.0 applies when playing Land **without both** detailed Air and Logistics. General §3.61–.62 restrictions and other cases still require their own interaction checks.

| Source / trigger | Printed information and audience boundary | Remaining uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §30.42, p. 42: planning a chariot raid | Axis player records the date planned and execution date on paper. §30.41 starts this procedure in July 1941; §30.45 restricts it to the Campaign Game, once. | A written plan is not by itself an express secrecy or opponent-disclosure instruction. This detailed raid is not a Graziani six-turn action. No audience for the paper record is specified here. |
| §30.44, p. 42: executing chariot raid | Axis player **announces** the Frogman Raid and rolls one die on the Chariot Table. The announcement is part of the two-player procedure; the text does not enumerate digital recipients or its exact contents beyond the raid. | This execution announcement does not authorize publishing the earlier plan at planning time or all preparatory details. Detailed-Air phase and campaign restrictions are separate. |
| §32.18, p. 43: dummy Supply Unit discovery | Players may place the specified dummy units to confuse opponents; the dummy moves like a real unit with less motorization and is removed **upon discovery**. | This clause does not define every discovery trigger, recipient or observation of a type before discovery. A displayed counter is not proof its actual contents are public. Compare §§32.13 and .32 and any applicable discovery source, rather than inventing a revelation event. |
| §32.13, p. 43: enemy combat unit enters a Supply Unit hex | The unit is captured and its supplies are “used immediately and freely.” | The [review](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-30-32-review.md) notes that whether this wording is mandatory or permissive remains interpretive. The sentence does not independently specify the opposing side's precise inventory record, other counters in the hex or disclosure timing. Real/dummy and capture interactions require separate source review. |
| §32.63, p. 44: Axis convoy arrival in Naval Convoy Phase | Axis player **announces which convoy lanes are being used**; Commonwealth may attack each arriving convoy separately. This is a lane announcement within the two-player procedure, not an express cargo manifest. | The clause does not state that cargo category, quantity, loss allocation, planned future lanes or a complete internal result is disclosed. Its attack and audience limits remain as in [convoy census](2026-11-18-graziani-convoy-section-census-review.md). |
| §32.72, p. 44: scenario fleet-attack allowance | Axis player rolls one die **secretly** to establish the scenario's number of allowed attacks, adding two for a Campaign Game. | Do not publish the raw secret die through a log or other side view by assumption. The sentence does not explicitly specify later disclosure duration, whether the derived allowance is separately announced, or an actual result. |
| §32.73, p. 44: planning a fleet attack | Axis plots each attack **secretly** during the Commonwealth Fleet Assignment Segment; after planning, it occurs in that Operations Stage's Tactical Naval Movement Segment. At most one per stage. | A hidden plotted attack is distinct from its later execution and damage; this case does not itself define exactly what is disclosed at execution, how long prior secret plans remain hidden, or the digital event audience. Segment reconciliation and selected sequence remain separate. |

Other potentially misleading records do not, by their mere existence, authorize opposing access: §30.34 directs players to track ship Gun Ratings and damage on the Ship Assignment Sheet; §§32.43–.45 describe advance supply planning; and §32.64 describes a Commonwealth attack calculation. Those individual passages do not explicitly state a general recipient for every underlying record. Conversely, their silence does not make every observable map counter, damage result or action forever secret. This is a source boundary, not a selected policy.

## Search and limits

Visually inspected the printed sequence §§30.0–32.9 on Land pp. 41–45, including §32.8 and its continuation on p. 45. Searched the retained [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) extracted text for numbered §30/§32 entries and `secret`, `reveal`, `announce`, `discover`, and `hidden`; inspected the corresponding p. 2–3 numbered entries. The §30.57 correction concerns port supply capacity, and §32.0 contains a note about the abstract rules; neither supplies a direct correction to the named disclosure triggers. Text search can miss OCR or an indirectly worded correction; this is not a later-edition or exhaustive cross-rule audit. Detailed strategic convoy and Air procedures outside these Land sections were not imported automatically.
