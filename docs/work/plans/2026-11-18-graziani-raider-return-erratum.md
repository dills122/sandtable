# Raider return and SAS cross-reference: September errata

**Result:** Errata §27.16 corrects an example's old calendar labels to Game-Turns **55 and 59** and requires returning LRDGs to be formed under §27.13. The printed four-Game-Turn delay can overlap Graziani's GT1–6 only if an LRDG is first validly formed and eliminated early enough; neither event is established. Errata §27.88 changes its reference to §27.32, the secret-movement procedure. The printed Commonwealth reinforcement schedule places the SAS Brigade at GT47/OpStage 2, outside Graziani; no alternative early SAS entry is established by this bounded check.

## Return after elimination

[Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 38, §27.16 says that after an LRDG is eliminated a new one **may** be formed from that eliminated counter four Game-Turns later. Its old example describes elimination in OpStage 2 and formation in OpStage 3 of a later turn. [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 middle column, §27.16 says returning LRDGs are formed as per §27.13 and substitutes turns **55 for July II and 59 for August II** in that example. These are example dates, not Graziani arrival dates, an automatic return, or a replacement for the four-turn rule.

Land §§27.12–.13 require an existing Recce/armored-car unit that reached maximum Morale through training, and removal of a TOE point in the Organization Phase to form an LRDG. Section 27.11 allows at most two LRDGs at once. The [prior raider Reaction note](2026-11-18-graziani-raider-reaction-erratum.md) identifies 11th Hussars at C3020 as a potential formation lead, but does not establish its qualifying training, eligible point, actual formation or elimination. If those conditions and elimination in GT1 were independently established, four Game-Turns later is GT5, within the scenario's GT1/1–GT6/3 window. That arithmetic is **conditional**, not a reachable witness or a ruling on the exact return OpStage; the source's example should not silently become a universal same-stage or next-stage rule. An actual return also needs the §27.13 formation conditions and available counter. Initial presence, earlier elimination, and other potential eligible units were not exhaustively audited.

## SAS cross-reference and timing

Land p. 40 middle column §27.88 says that if the Commonwealth player stacks SAS Brigade with an LRDG, SAS obtains a 30-CP allowance and is removed from physical sight and moves in the manner of an LRDG, with the LRDG escorting it until it returns to the map. The [review](2026-11-18-graziani-raider-return-erratum-review.md) notes that the rule does not separately specify the 30-CP allowance's duration. Its original reference to §00.00 is corrected by September errata p. 2 §27.88 to **§27.32**. Land p. 38 §27.32 says after initial formation the owner records raider location and movement secretly and normally follows Recce vehicle movement rules; §§27.33–.34 separately address revelation and ZOC. The corrected citation supports the hidden-movement connection, not a new SAS or LRDG, a route, unlimited movement, or removal of the escort and other requirements.

[Commonwealth reinforcement schedule](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 3, lists **Special Air Service Brigade [I]** at GT47/OpStage 2. [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed pp. 27–28 / PDF pp. 3–4, §§60.22, .41 and .47, ends Graziani at GT6/3, gives its initial Commonwealth deployment, and directs use of the printed reinforcement track. This schedule entry is outside the six-turn window, and the inspected initial setup does not list SAS. These are bounded source observations, not an exhaustive proof that no other rule or source can ever create an SAS counter. The correction alone supplies no eligible SAS/LRDG pairing or completed movement.

Land §32.0/.11 and scenario §60.92 describe the Land-only abstraction; their interaction with §27.35's detailed raider resupply remains open in the [Reaction analysis](2026-11-18-graziani-raider-reaction-erratum.md). Neither correction itself resolves that interaction, waives other movement restrictions, or establishes a special Land-only arrival. Test synthetic formation/elimination/return timing and hidden escort only after the necessary eligibility and mode decisions, without claiming those tests ran.
