# ML-03: independent direction evidence search

**Result:** The September 1979 erratum identifies the corrected edge as **Map D 2228/2328**, but gives no high side. This bounded search did not find an independently sourced original map, dated official direction correction, or verified geographic registration capable of determining that side. Keep direction **unknown**; no new map or runtime admission follows.

## Actual correction and source identity

Visually inspected [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1, MAPS entry. Local PDF SHA-256 checked before rendering: `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`. The entry specifies an escarpment on D hexside 2228/2328; it gives no slope, high hex, glyph, or crossing. This checks the correction itself, not the high side. The [Grognard transcription](https://grognard.com/errata1/cna.html) reproduces the same September entry and likewise supplies no orientation; it is not a separate correction or independent map evidence.

## Alternative-source search

- [VASSAL module listing](https://vassalengine.org/wiki_old/wiki/Module%3AThe_Campaign_for_North_Africa%3A_The_Desert_War_1940-43) says v0.93 replaced the map with a copy of the game map and v0.94 **added** this edge under the errata; v1 changed aspect ratio. The listing does not report the source printing, scan lineage, or high side. Its downloadable module image was not inspected here. A later edited edge cannot by itself establish that its chosen direction was printed or official.
- [Living Rules](https://basmith.net/cna/) describes a provisional unaffiliated redraw. Its map page could not be opened in this search; the earlier [alternate-map search](2026-11-18-graziani-alternate-map-source-search.md) describes a schematic redraw derived from VASSAL and checked against the SPI scan. It is not independent original topographic evidence.
- Exact-edge public searches combining the game name, D2228/D2328, escarpment and direction returned the September transcription and derivative leads above, not a verified directional correction. This is a search-index and source-access limit, not proof that none exists. A separate later physical printing, historical contour map or designer record was not located or inspected. Geographic elevations could not safely be assigned to these hexes without a verified map-to-geography registration and evidence that the game edge follows that feature.

The existing [direction review](2026-11-17-graziani-d2228-escarpment-direction-review.md) records a plausible D2328-high continuity inference from a different neighboring printed side, not proof for the corrected side. This follow-up did not re-render or re-interpret the ambiguous Map D image. The independent scan audit in the [alternate-map search](2026-11-18-graziani-alternate-map-source-search.md) finds the retained all-in-one and separate PDFs embed identical Map D image bytes; those packages cannot provide independent pixels. A provenance-identified original or official corrected map with an unambiguous direction glyph, or an explicit dated designer clarification, is the discriminating next evidence. Keep confirmed edge presence separate from unknown high side and direction-dependent movement. No code, map data or gameplay tests changed.
