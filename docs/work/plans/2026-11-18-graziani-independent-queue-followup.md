# Abstract air supply: arrival timing boundary

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Bounded source follow-up to the [scenario extent research queue](2026-11-18-graziani-scenario-extent-crosswalk.md) and [facility geography / mode scope](2026-11-18-graziani-facility-geography-mode-scope.md). Those documents retain conditional convoy arrivals and abstract-air endpoint eligibility without proving a shipment. This check concerns their **within-stage ordering**, not route legality, initial-stock sufficiency, or an actual Graziani delivery.

Land §5.2 places the Operations Stage Organization Phase C before Naval Convoy Arrival Phase D. Land §32.37 permits abstract-air movement during an Operations Stage Organization Phase, while §32.42 says Axis Supply Units arrive as per convoy. Therefore, **for an Axis Supply Unit whose arrival is actually resolved in that stage's Phase D**, the earlier Phase C cannot have transported that newly arrived unit or consumed its newly arrived fuel: both must exist at the relevant origin before the air movement. This is an inference from the printed ordering and the particular arrival event, not a claim that all supply or fuel at that hex is newly arrived. Pre-existing units and fuel remain separate candidates.

A later Operations Stage has a new Organization Phase, but that alone does not authorize a transport: §32.37 also limits each player to one Supply Unit per Game-Turn, requires a qualifying origin and destination, fuel at origin, a qualifying friendly destination HQ, the enemy-proximity and directional-division checks, and prevents the transported unit and its supplies from moving during the transport OpStage. If the player already used the Game-Turn allowance, a later stage does not reset it. §32.43 excludes the scenario starting Game-Turn from its ordinary planned-arrival statement; this analysis does not create a GT1 convoy or establish which later stage receives one. Commonwealth arrivals under §32.42 are not assumed to have the same Phase D timing without a separately established scheduling rule.

**Useful conditional comparison:** with all other gates assumed satisfied, a unit already present before Phase C can be a candidate then; a distinct unit first arriving in Phase D cannot be that earlier cargo. At the next stage's Phase C the latter may be considered if it has arrived and the Game-Turn allowance and all other checks permit it. Preserve event order and separate arrival identity from inventories so later delivery cannot be read backward into an earlier snapshot. This is a proposed verification vector, not an executed test or an assertion of a legal action.
