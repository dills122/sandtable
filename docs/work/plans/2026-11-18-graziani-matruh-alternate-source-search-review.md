# Matruh alternate-source search: verification

**Disposition:** Supports the [bounded search](2026-11-18-graziani-matruh-alternate-source-search.md)'s source hierarchy and its conclusion that no later authoritative Matruh-specific clarification was established by those leads. This is not proof none exists. The checks below re-open public material and repeat selected text searches; they do not establish a different original edition or license.

- [September 1979 errata PDF](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) opens as four pages and identifies itself as September 1979. [Grognard's transcription](https://grognard.com/errata1/cna.html) identifies the same dated addenda. A transcription or second host of that package is not evidence of a later correction.
- [Integrated Land PDF](https://friendorfoe.com/d/CfNA/CNA%20-%20Rules%20Land.pdf) opens and is searchable. Its §32.14 retains the three-unit wording for another type of hex “off-map”; §32.37 retains pre-transport payment from any Supply Unit in the origin hex. [Friend or Foe](https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/) credits Clay Stone for PDFs with errata applied. This community-hosted integration repeats the phrases but does not resolve their interpretation or independently establish a publisher ruling.
- [NJHarman's house-rules page](https://friendorfoe.com/war/cfna/houserules/) opens, labels corrections, clarifications, interpretations and changes, and records a 2022–2025 history. Exact rendered-text searches for `32.14`, `32.37`, `60.92` and `Matruh` returned no match. This does not exclude indirect discussion or a differently worded case, and the page is not publisher authority.
- [CNA Living Rules](https://basmith.net/cna/) opens, describes its own restatement and reasoned rulings, and disclaims affiliation with SPI or successors. This confirms its comparison status; it does not prove that every page lacks relevant content.
- [SPI-hosted work-in-progress archive](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv9/WIP_CNA_Done.pdf) opens as a 13-page extracted-text PDF. Exact searches for `1980` and `Moves 50` returned no match. No page-by-page visual audit or claim of historical completeness follows from those searches.
- The [Enterprise Games listing](https://enterprisegames.com/spi-parts/) and [Limey Yank counter listing](https://limeyyankgames.co.uk/die-cut-counter-showroom) both opened during this review, extending the candidate's search-excerpt-only access check. The former lists CNA Maps A and E, not Map D in that row; the latter describes aftermarket counter sheets incorporating known counter errata. Neither listing identifies a Matruh stacking or abstract-air correction. Inventory and availability remain unverified, and no purchase or inquiry was made.

A separate exact-term web search for the game with §§32.14, 32.37 and 60.92 did not establish a new publisher-authored correction. Search indexes, unindexed periodicals, correspondence, inaccessible materials and editions remain outside this bounded result. Existing Matruh supply, payment and stacking uncertainties therefore remain; neither public availability nor a matching text supplies distribution rights. No scan was added to Git, no data/runtime changes made, and no gameplay tests run.
