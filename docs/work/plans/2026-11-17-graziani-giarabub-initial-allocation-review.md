# Giarabub initial allocation: independent same-scan review

**Disposition, 2026-11-17:** No material error found in the [allocation note](2026-11-17-graziani-giarabub-initial-allocation.md)'s bounded reading. The cited provisions establish a general player-distributed resource and an abstract conversion, but do not select Giarabub's initial allocation. Treat pregame attachment of second/third-line-derived points as an unresolved interpretation, not a proven permission or prohibition.

- Scenario §60.31 lists Giaribub Garrison at C1014 without a truck figure on that row; §60.33 lists 30 light, 100 medium and 25 heavy second/third-line truck points for anywhere in Libya, subject to restrictions. This is evidence for an owner-distributed pool, not proof that any or all of it is placed at C1014 or assigned to this battalion. A partial placement elsewhere or at C1014 is not excluded merely by the row; legality of a particular split and attachment remains unproved.
- In the detailed truck game, §§59.44–.45 and 60.33 allow owner distribution subject to placement restrictions and allow trucks to start motorizing troops without cost or penalty. The [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 3, reinforces §59.45's broad initial loading rule. Neither selects a specific destination, proves every split legal, or explicitly resolves the abstract conversion's attachment status.
- Under abstract logistics, §59.63(A) calculates **attached** points from medium/heavy first-line trucks by hex; §59.63(B) describes points calculated from second/third-line trucks as **unattached**. The 100 medium plus 25 heavy in the Libya row yield 125 such points once, not an additional 125 alongside retained trucks. Section 59.64 says initial distributions may be altered once play starts under Land attachment/detachment rules, except as §59.66(B) specifies. Its wording does not expressly grant a pregame transfer of §59.63(B) points into the attached category. Nor does that observation itself prove that all other setup wording forbids such a transfer.
- Land §32.54 requires enough motorization points assigned to all historically motorized `8+` or `10+` infantry before assignment to other infantry types. It does not name a source pool or expressly specify when and how pregame attachment overrides §59.63(B). It is a meaningful priority constraint, not proof of the battalion's exact allocation; reconciling its scope and timing with initial unattached points requires a stated policy. It does not establish priority over every non-infantry use.
- A placement choice and an attachment choice need separate evidence. Neither a missing first-line figure on the Giaribub row nor the presence of a general pool proves zero effective initial transport; co-location alone does not establish attachment or sufficient transport. Retain unknown status until a valid allocation and applicable mobility rules are established.
