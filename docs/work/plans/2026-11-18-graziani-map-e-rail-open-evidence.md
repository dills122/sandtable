# Map E rail: evidence needed for twelve open pairs

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Disposition:** Evidence triage only, using the [unknown reconciliation](2026-11-18-graziani-map-e-rail-unknown-reconciliation.md) and its linked rechecks and reviews. All twelve named sides remain unknown. No new scan inspection, promotion, map-data change, or claim about real-world railway existence follows.

## Reason groups

Groups overlap: a vertex can also be overprinted. Membership describes the documented obstacle, not a new visual finding.

- **Vertex attribution:** `E2428–E2429`, `E2529–E2628`, `E2829–E2830`, `E2829–E2930`, `E2905–E3004`, `E3210–E3211`, `E3320–E3419`, `E3320–E3420`. Distinguish the named side from its neighbors with a legible centerline and grid at the common vertex, plus an applicable printed convention if the line actually passes through the vertex. A clearer image alone may not decide a truly exact vertex.
- **Overprint or line identity:** `E1930–E2029`, `E2428–E2429`, `E2529–E2628`, `E2829–E2830`, `E2829–E2930`, `E3320–E3419`, `E3320–E3420`, `E3513–E3613`, `E3613–E3714`, `E3714–E3715`. Distinguish rail from road, river, grid, city and figure ink with legible ties and centerline on the named side; continuity on either side alone need not prove the obscured intersection.
- **Sheet edge:** None of these twelve is documented as unresolved because of a sheet edge. The separate open `E3101|W` record is outside this set; an adjoining sheet or full untrimmed sheet with matched grid and edition could address its destination and crossing.
- **Source conflict:** None of the twelve has a documented independent-source conflict. `E2905–E3004` is pinned but lacks a distinct visible branch; that is a pin-versus-visibility uncertainty, not proof of conflicting sources. The separately bounded `E1927–E1928` apparent source/pin mismatch and unpinned `E2927–E3027` discrepancy are outside this set.

All twelve have pinned `railroad` entries; a pin is a data assertion, not independent proof of a printed crossing or a real railway. Nearby visible rail, and supported road crossings where noted, establish only those local printed features. They do not establish rail on the named side, junction connectivity, a historical railway, service, construction or damage state, control, occupancy, or movement legality. Unknown is neither crossing nor absence. The selected-pin set is not an omission survey.

Useful future evidence would include a better-preserved physical copy or independent, provenance-identified scan of the **same map edition**, showing the full side, labels, key and neighboring vertices; a publisher's original artwork or contemporaneous authoritative clarification could be stronger where ink or vertex convention is intrinsically ambiguous. Record edition, sheet, source provenance and any discrepancies before comparison: a different edition may reflect a revision rather than resolve the original. A historical railway map may corroborate real-world existence, but cannot by itself establish what this game map prints or which hexside its rules assign. An independent source showing no mark across a fully legible side could support a negative reading; absence from an obscured area cannot. Re-rendering or enlarging the existing scan adds no independent detail, and prior same-scan challenges were informed by candidates and earlier readings.
