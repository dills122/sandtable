# Independent review: victory and shared ZOC control

**Disposition:** The [follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-victory-remaining-source.md) accurately distinguishes shared ZOC control from §60.81's occupation and take/hold wording. Its example and the earlier result-order exclusions are conditional; neither establishes a reachable objective or a simultaneous result.

I visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 18, §§10.21 and 10.26–.27. Section 10.27 expressly treats a hex receiving ZOCs from opposing units as controlled by both players; §10.21 limits projection through specified hexsides and into adjacent hexes a unit could not enter. Section 10.26 negates an enemy ZOC's effect in a friendly-combat-occupied hex **for all movement purposes**, and says friendly ZOCs never affect friendly units. It does not itself define exclusive victory possession or negate the shared-control statement for all other purposes. A friendly unit's presence and ZOC status are separate facts.

I also visually inspected [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, §60.81. Italian decisive explicitly requires taking and holding **(occupying)** both Mersa Matruh and Siwa with a combat unit that can be supplied by convoy from Tobruk. Italian tactical separately requires taking and holding (occupying) Sidi Birani and retaining possession of Sollum, Fort Maddalena and Giarabub in supply. Commonwealth rows use take/hold and supply wording. A jointly ZOC-controlled hex without the required Italian occupying combat unit cannot establish that named occupation merely from shared control; this does not determine the full meaning of take, hold, retain, supply, or an actual state.

The [earlier independent result-order review](2026-11-18-graziani-victory-result-order-followup-review.md)'s four cross-side row exclusions still follow only under its stated **exclusive same-hex, same-snapshot possession** assumption. Generic ZOC control alone cannot establish that assumption. Neither §10.27 nor this review proves shared occupancy, mutual victory, a tie-break, or that exclusive victory possession is impossible. The strategic rows and scenario reachability were not reassessed here.
