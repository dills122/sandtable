# Alexandria repair and possible rain: source review

**Disposition:** Supports the [conditional scenario case](2026-11-18-graziani-weather-scenario-case.md). The source names actual starting broken-down vehicle types and gives Major Repair Facilities a weather exception, but neither a rain result nor a particular eligible repair is established. Same-scan informed recheck, not an independent edition or an admitted setup/repair ruling.

- **Starting vehicles and facility:** Scenario §60.41 lists Alexandria with **2 TOE A9 Cruiser tanks and 1 TOE A10 Cruiser tank**, broken down. It does not pick the individual tank-point hexes. Land §22.31 lists major facilities at Alexandria and Cairo followed by “all hexes”; the [facility review](2026-11-17-graziani-cw-facility-setup-review.md) preserves whether that parenthetical covers both cities. Scenario §60.44 names a major facility in Alexandria; §60.92(C) replaces §60.44 for Land-only supply. Co-location, facility placement and replacement scope remain choices, not source-proved facts for an individual tank point.
- **Weather:** Land §29.1 determines weather each Operations Stage and, after rainstorm or sandstorm, calls for a location die. Common-chart §29.7 maps location die **3 to D and E**. Its asterisk makes a **sandstorm** in a Nile Delta hex normal; it does not state an analogous rainstorm exception. The chart's fall row includes rain outcomes, but applying fall to GT1–6 is the documented [planning selection](2026-11-17-graziani-weather-schedule-review.md), which retains the season-chart and errata tension. No actual roll, affected hex or startup snapshot follows.
- **Repair:** Land §22.13(d) excludes Major Repair Facilities from its rainstorm/sandstorm ban, and §22.36 states major facilities are not affected by weather. Section 22.32 requires a vehicle to begin the Maintenance Segment in a facility hex and not have been towed during that Operations Stage. Control, other §22.13 conditions, facility state, supplies and the applicable abstract-mode interpretation still require separate evidence. This establishes only that rain alone does not bar a qualifying major-facility repair; it is not a successful die result or proof of a legal command.
- **Field comparison:** §22.27 refers field repairs to §22.13(d). Without a qualifying major facility, the major-facility exception does not independently remove the weather restriction; field-repair companion, towing and supply rules remain separate. The passages do not expressly resolve an elective field procedure in a hex that also contains a qualifying major facility. The one-point 10% chart/rule discrepancy remains as described by the facility review, not an assumed repair outcome.
