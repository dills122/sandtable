# Review: probe announcement timing

**Disposition:** The identified pre-resolution versus delayed-announcement tension is supported. The source note preserves the key distinction that §15.94 permits withholding; it does not require secrecy until resolution. No source precedence or actual probe follows from these checked passages.

- Land §15.22 p. 23 has the phasing player announce assaulted hexes, both players determine their respective total Points, and the phasing player state Close Assault or Probe before individual assaults are resolved sequentially. Determining total Points is not an express disclosure of all constituent identities or weapon systems.
- Land §15.94 p. 25 says the probing player **does not have to announce** that the assault is a Probe until after the assault is over. Section 5.2 III.G.3(d)–(f) p. 12 has both players secretly assign TOE points, lets A withhold its probe decision at assignment, and says A announces which assaults were probes after each resolves. These support the candidate's delay reading and conflict with a literal mandatory pre-resolution statement; they do not prove a publisher hierarchy or a ban on voluntary earlier announcement.
- Minor wording refinement: in the final discriminator, “permits its disclosure only afterward” could sound like an obligation to conceal earlier. Prefer **“permits withholding its disclosure until afterward”** to match the note's explicit no-mandatory-secrecy qualification. This does not change the documented conflict.
- September errata pp. 1–2 visibly correct the §15.25 example, §15.26 references and §15.27 die application, with other numbered §15 corrections; the inspected entries do not expressly settle §§15.22/.94 timing. No exhaustive indirect/later correction search or independent recheck of every OCR term is claimed.
