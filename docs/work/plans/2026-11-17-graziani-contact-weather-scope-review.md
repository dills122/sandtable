Given that a foul result has occurred and a **fair six-sided** location die is used, C is selected with probability `3/6 = 1/2`; fairness is an assumption. For a single reached stage, if the disputed season hierarchy selects the fall row, the base weather dice are independent fair ordered d6 and the fair location die is independent of them, the chart p. 6 §29.61 yields sandstorm `55, 56, 61` and rainstorm `62–66`. The resulting conditional-model probabilities for these inland Map C hexes are respectively `3/36 × 3/6 = 1/24` and `5/36 × 3/6 = 5/72`. These are model calculations, not an actual roll or proof of fall-row selection; the printed chart's GT rows and season/errata wording conflict as documented in [schedule review](2026-11-17-graziani-weather-schedule-review.md). Do not multiply per-hex storm probabilities as if the five locations rolled separately, and do not extrapolate across 18 stages without separately assuming temporal independence and stage reachability.
