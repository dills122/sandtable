# Initial contact border rules: source review

**Disposition:** The [border note](2026-11-17-graziani-contact-border-rules.md) appropriately treats the political-boundary interpretation as a lead and the absence of a separate border effect as a bounded search result. The checked text does not certify free movement or ZOC across these sides.

- [Map C](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf), PDF p. 3, shows blue checkered ink on the common `C3019–C3020` side at Fort Maddalena; the line continues between large Libya and Egypt labels. This geographic context supports a national-boundary interpretation but does not prove symbol meaning. The PDF p. 1 terrain key's `Border` illustration is a plain gray side with adjoining grid rays, visually different from the blue checkered mark. No direct key identity or independent edge clearance follows.
- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) p. 14 §8.19 prohibits Commonwealth land units moving west of Marble Arch `A2109` for any reason. It is a named geographic restriction, not text banning the Libya–Egypt line; the three cited contact sides lie on Map C. Other applicable route and unit rules remain separate.
- Map PDF p. 1 §8.37 chart lists terrain and side effects, including ridge, slope, escarpment, wadi and rivers, without a separate national-border row. Land §10.21 p. 18 lists all-sea, major-river, lake and escarpment sides, then independently excludes projection into an adjacent hex the unit could not enter from its current position. Political borders are not among the listed per-side exclusions. This is not a source-wide proof that no nationality or scenario restriction exists and does not decide whether some other feature coincides with a marked side.
- Scenario §60.31's `Anywhere in Libya` setup wording is a placement constraint, not itself an ongoing crossing or ZOC prohibition. Its application to a selectable placement and country classification is independent of the specifically printed `C3419` garrison position. The note's other Sheferzen side observations and its errata result rely on linked prior checks; they are not independently certified by this review.

For any admission, retain original ink and uncertainty, independently establish unit-specific entry, direction, actual ZOC prerequisites, terrain and scenario rules, and avoid treating this bounded negative check as a general passability policy.
