# Graziani facility geography and abstract air supply

**Date:** November 18, 2026. This is a bounded documentary comparison of the 30 additional on-map §60.5 facility coordinates in the November 18 map batches, not a legal-move inventory. **Disposition:** retain their map evidence for shared settlement and other geographic questions; facility status alone neither makes an abstract-air endpoint eligible nor makes its geography irrelevant to Land-only play. No candidate below is a proven shipment or finally excluded solely by the evidence in this table.

## Mode and endpoint rule

[Land §32.0 and §32.37](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43, govern abstract logistics and air when playing Land without Air and Logistics. The [prior source reading](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness.md) and [independent same-scan visual review](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness-review.md) read §32.37 as permitting one Supply Unit per player per Game-Turn in an OpStage Organization Phase. Both endpoints must be a **major city, village, or Tripoli/Tunisia box**; a friendly division or brigade HQ counter must be at the destination, and a battle-group HQ does not qualify. The text does not independently require an airfield, aircraft, or detailed-Air capacity. A village with no air facility can therefore be a geographic candidate; a listed facility outside a qualifying settlement or box does not qualify merely by being a facility. Whether another applicable rule adds a constraint requires separate review.

Before transport, expend 12 Fuel from a Supply Unit in the origin hex; no enemy combat unit may be within five hexes of the origin. Axis cannot go farther east, Commonwealth farther west, than the respective extreme hexrow occupied by a friendly division, shell or full. The transported unit and its supplies cannot move that OpStage, although §32.16 draw is allowed. Establish actual cargo, fuel source, ownership, timing, allowance, directional division, enemy positions, destination HQ, applicable capacity and snapshot separately. Geographic eligibility alone does not establish any of them; an initially unoccupied candidate can become relevant later after legal movement. See the [witness](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness.md) for its unresolved capacity and setup questions.

[Scenario §§59.61, 60.91–.93](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF pp. 2, 4–5, distinguish Air abstracted, Land-only, and Land-and-Air with abstract logistics. When Air alone is abstracted, §59.61 excludes trucks and supplies available at or for air facilities; Land-only uses §60.92's own replacement setup. Do not import detailed aircraft placement or facility supplies, or §60.93 conversions, into Land-only merely because a facility is listed. Detailed-Air placement remains relevant under its own mode and capacity rules. Initial facility ownership by country, current control, actual aircraft and HQ occupancy, and settlement geography are distinct facts; §60.5's country statement does not certify a particular cell's country or a later state.
