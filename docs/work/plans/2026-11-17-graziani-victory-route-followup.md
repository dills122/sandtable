Section 60.81 states strategic Italian supply by convoy from any map-D hex, decisive Italian supply by convoy from Tobruk, tactical Italian supply “as above, to Tobruk,” and Commonwealth tactical trace by truck convoy back to Alexandria or Cairo, with decisive and strategic supply “as above.” Its different subject and possession wording is material. The source does **not** specify a complete Land-only routing procedure. Section 32.16 measures **local draw** to a supply unit by medium-truck movement, except non-motorized infantry, and prohibits impassable terrain and enemy ZOCs unoccupied by friendly units. Sections 32.32–.33 still use motorization and the Truck Convoy phase. Section 8.13 prohibits entering an enemy-occupied hex, §8.19 restricts actual Commonwealth Land movement west of Marble Arch, and §§8.31–.33 and 8.41 govern terrain, hexsides, roads and rainstorm wadis. None explicitly converts those provisions into the complete victory predicate. The one-game-turn trace in §60.82 belongs to the longer Italian Campaign and is not imported.

## Candidate `gra-victory-route-v1`

The following is a project interpretation, with deliberate choices beyond printed text. Adopt only as a single versioned policy together with map, possession and end-boundary contracts; otherwise return `unknown`.

1. **Purpose and snapshot.** At the accepted terminal snapshot after all turn 6 stage 3 procedures, test hypothetical continuous medium-truck connectivity between each required subject and an eligible named source. Do not require a historical shipment, present truck or motorization inventory, supply-unit stock, fuel, a supply-unit relay or §32.16 local draw. Do not impose CPA, CP cost, duration or route-length limit. These choices preserve a named-source route rather than equating it with local draw, but they are not printed rules. Select a deterministic witness path only for replay/audit; existence, not the choice of path, determines the result.
2. **Subjects and sources.** Resolve objective identity and possession under separately accepted rules first. For Italian strategic, require a qualifying Italian combat unit at an eligible Alexandria **or** Cairo objective hex and one route from that unit's hex to **at least one** eligible map-D hex. For Italian decisive, require a qualifying Italian combat unit at **each** of Matruh and Siwa, each with a route to Tobruk. For Italian tactical, apply the §60.81 possession predicates separately and require a route from each of Sidi Birani, Sollum, Fort Maddalena and Giarabub to Tobruk. For each Commonwealth row require a route from **each** listed objective to Alexandria **or** Cairo; each objective may choose either source independently. Applying supply to each tactical location and each Commonwealth objective, requiring combat occupancy where the applicable possession contract says so, and allowing different Commonwealth sources are conservative interpretations of plural and “as above” wording, not explicit source text. Do not import the longer scenario's definition of combat unit without an independent applicable definition.
3. **Source eligibility.** A map-D origin may be any certified hex belonging to map D, including the subject hex if applicable, with no extra friendly-ownership requirement; it must satisfy the same endpoint obstruction rules as every route hex. City sources must be a certified hex of the named city, controlled by the tracing side under an accepted control rule, and satisfy endpoint obstruction rules. The named-source control requirement is an added interpretation; §60.81 does not say it. Alexandria/Cairo multi-hex sets, Tobruk identity, map-sheet membership and control are inputs, never inferred from place names. Source selection is existential, not a requirement that all map-D hexes or both cities work.
4. **Route graph.** Route is a finite sequence of certified adjacent hexes including subject and source. A zero-edge route is allowed only when the subject itself is an eligible source and passes all endpoint checks. Use the accepted medium-truck terrain/hexside traversability at terminal-stage weather, including any source-supported rainstorm restrictions and road/track crossings; ignore finite CP costs while respecting impassability. Reverse traversal is allowed only if the applicable truck/terrain rules permit it in that direction. Road benefits require certified connecting hexsides, not merely a road symbol in each hex. Weather selection and truck movement for this hypothetical test are interpretations, not explicit §60.81 substitutions.
5. **Blockers.** Reject every route hex, including subject and source, containing any enemy unit; a friendly occupant does not cancel enemy occupancy. Reject an enemy-ZOC hex unless occupied by a friendly combat unit. This uses §32.16's friendly-unit exception as analogy but narrows it to combat units by choice; the original local-draw text says friendly **units**, and does not explicitly supply the victory rule. Friendly supply units, dummies and markers alone do not neutralize ZOC under this candidate. ZOC projection must use the accepted rules and complete state, including incoming boundary ZOC. Evaluate occupancy and ZOC on the same snapshot. Do not use knowledge filtered for either player to decide authoritative connectivity.
6. **Western limit.** Apply §8.19 to Commonwealth hypothetical route vertices: no vertex may be west of Marble Arch, using an accepted map-coordinate interpretation of “west.” The printed rule restricts movement of Commonwealth Land units; extending it to a hypothetical trace is a policy choice. If the west relation or map identity is unresolved, return `unknown` where it affects the answer. No corresponding Axis restriction is implied here.
7. **Unknown versus no route.** A certified valid path proves `match` for that route even if another candidate path crosses an unknown seam. Return `no-match` only after a complete, bounded search proves all eligible paths blocked, including all source candidates and relevant incoming ZOCs. Return `unknown` if unresolved source identity/control, terrain, edge, map coverage, weather or occupation could change existence; missing edge is not automatically a clear edge or an impassable physical boundary. Unbounded or unverified outside-map continuation cannot be assumed absent. For a required conjunction, a known false required condition gives `no-match`; otherwise any unknown required condition gives `unknown`, consistent with [result proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-independent-decision.md). Preserve all six rows and do not publish a final verdict if any remains unknown under the selected result policy.

## Alternatives requiring review

- An actual or currently feasible shipment would require transport, timing, capacity, route length and possibly relay rules; it could fail while this candidate succeeds. §32.58's CPA 15 describes motorization transporting a supply unit, not a printed hypothetical route limit.
- §32.16 friendly **any-unit** ZOC neutralization is a closer textual analogy than the combat-only candidate; no-neutralization is another. Each changes routes through ZOC occupied only by a supply unit. Including dummy identity in public diagnostics would leak hidden information.
- Interpret the Italian tactical supply phrase as applying only to retained places, or only Sidi Birani; interpret “as above” as requiring one supplied holder per row rather than every objective. These materially differ from item 2 and are not disposed of by a route algorithm.
- Require map-D origin control, an actual dump, or a specific source unit; allow an enemy-held city as source; use one common Alexandria/Cairo source for all Commonwealth subjects; or apply neither city control nor the western limit. Each is a separate policy choice. A path ending at an occupied hostile source must not accidentally become valid merely because the path is otherwise open.
- Treat a zero-edge source/subject coincidence as invalid, impose a length limit, require a traversable return trip, or assess weather at another specified time. None follows automatically from “by convoy.”

## Distinguishing acceptance vectors

These are proposed tests, not executed results or evidence of historical reachability.

- Strategic Italian: two map-D candidates, first blocked and second open; all blocked with full coverage; one unknown seam potentially reaching another candidate; subject itself in map D and an obstructed zero-edge endpoint.
- Decisive Italian: Matruh route valid but Siwa route blocked; both valid through distinct routes; a friendly-held Tobruk source versus hostile-controlled or unknown-control source. Tactical: all four valid, then each independently disconnected while possession remains true.
- Commonwealth: two objectives each connected to different eligible cities; one objective disconnected from both; one source uncertified; a route that crosses west of Marble Arch and an alternate that does not. Evaluate explicit map-coordinate semantics before assigning expected outcomes.
- Enemy occupancy with friendly occupant; enemy ZOC with no occupant, a friendly combat unit, only friendly supply, and only a dummy. Compare candidate result with §32.16's broader local-draw wording. Assert opponent-visible failures do not disclose hidden units or dummy identity.
- Certified passable road crossing versus nonconnecting road, blocked escarpment, rainstorm wadi with and without road, missing seam and incoming ZOC beyond coverage. Test directed traversability if terrain rules make direction material. A known complete witness remains valid despite an irrelevant unknown seam; no-witness with potentially useful unknown remains unknown.
- §32.16 local draw without named-source route and named-source route without local draw; no available trucks; a path longer than one stage; each independently distinguishes this candidate from alternatives. Preserve deterministic witness selection and verdict after replay, with stable ruleset identity and filtered player views.

Affected artifacts: versioned scenario victory and movement/map contracts; Core predicate, terminal snapshot and state schemas; map/terrain/control evidence; Host replay and privacy tests. No code, runtime state or readiness flags changed; no gameplay tests run. Acceptance requires source/ruling review, exhaustive route-space boundary evidence and the separate terminal ordering decision.
