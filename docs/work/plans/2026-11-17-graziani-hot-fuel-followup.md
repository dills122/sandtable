# Graziani hot fuel: source precedence and fractions

**Disposition:** Unresolved source interaction. Retain the [existing exact-only candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-hot-supply-next.md) as a conditional project interpretation, not an established rule or playable complete effect. Neither a zero debit nor a rounded integer debit is established by the inspected passages. No runtime or readiness change.

## Evidence and competing readings

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) p. 41 §§29.1, 29.31, 29.34: hot weather affects all game-map sections; weather excludes Malta, Italy, Sicily, Crete and Tunisia–Tripoli, with a separate Map E provision for Allied off-map airfields. During Weather Determination, hot weather reduces fuel and water levels by 5%, including stocks in dumps and trucks. Water in wells/oases and fuel/water actually being consumed by a unit are excluded. A transported stock is not necessarily fuel actually being consumed.
- Land p. 43 §32.0 applies when playing the Land Game “without the Air and Logistics Games.” That wording does not itself enumerate every mixed-game combination; resolve each mode against its own applicable rules before extending this analysis. It replaces Land and Air supply expenditures with listed abstract ammunition and fuel costs. Section 32.11 directs players to ignore references to usage and expenditure of ammunition, fuel, stores and water, and says only its following supply rules apply. Section 32.12 makes Supply Units abstract substitutes for those points and represents them with Supply Dump counters; §32.15 retains tracked fuel and ammunition and removes a Supply Unit when both reach zero. Section 32.18 describes dummy units. Section 32.32 permits motorization to carry real or dummy units.
- The 5% weather reduction is not expressly called an expenditure or usage. That distinction, retained fuel balances, and dump-counter representation support applying §29.34 to eligible actual Supply Unit stock. Conversely, §32.11's broader statement that only following supply rules apply supports treating §32 as exhaustive and omitting the general weather debit. Neither passage explicitly settles their interaction; a dump counter representing an abstraction does not alone establish that every detailed dump rule survives. The latter reading does not prove that all weather effects disappear. An accepted project ruling or further authority is needed.
- [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) p. 2 §32.0 warns that abstract rules were untested; this is a reliability warning, not a repeal or precedence direction. Its visible §29 entries concern dating and the weather table, not §29.34. No direct §29.34 or §32.11 resolution was found in this bounded check and the earlier [candidate's](2026-11-17-graziani-hot-supply-next.md) errata text search. This does not exclude indirect, later or other-edition authority.

## Fraction and decision boundary

Conditional on the effect applying to an eligible single Supply Unit with 50 fuel, exact arithmetic gives a **2.5-point loss and 47.5 remaining**. [Scenario §60.92(B)](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) supplies initial 50-fuel Tobruk examples, but does not establish a hot roll or a later balance. Section 29.34 does not specify per-counter versus aggregate calculation, fraction representation, or rounding direction. Visual review of Land glossary pp. 5–7 found no general fuel-fraction rounding rule; this bounded check is not an exhaustive search of every rule or chart. A rounding instruction in a different procedure cannot by itself decide this one.

Per-unit, per-location and aggregate grouping can differ: two 10-fuel units each lose 0.5 before rounding, whereas their aggregate loss is 1 before allocation. Integer floor, ceiling or nearest, preserving fractions, and aggregating first are distinct policies. The existing candidate's per-unit exact-only gate is a conservative implementation proposal: for an integer-only state it blocks the whole applicable effect on 2.5, rather than inventing a rounded answer. It is not a source-mandated rounding rule and is not sufficient for playable historical execution.

Before admission, choose and version precedence, subject inventory, grouping and fraction treatment; classify all applicable stocks, loaded and off-map holdings, actual-consumption exclusions and water. Fractional representation also requires versioned serialization and later spending and comparison semantics. For a drawn hot result, durably preserve the result, stage identity, policy version and RNG cursor through retry or crash; never redraw or advance while the effect is unresolved. A pre-draw gate may also block a non-hot outcome and needs its own privacy review.

Whole-effect blocking prevents partial mutation but can expose private stock or dummy identity through an observable stop. Redacting diagnostics alone is insufficient. Define authorized observations and pending or uniformly exposed behavior, then test paired histories indistinguishable to each authorized audience across legal actions, events, retries, timing, and operator and intelligence-plane projections. If that behavior is infeasible, keep the effect unadmitted until a disclosure policy is accepted. These are project admission gates, not printed rules. See [independent follow-up review](2026-11-17-graziani-hot-fuel-followup-review.md) and [earlier review](2026-11-17-graziani-hot-supply-review.md) for further test vectors.
