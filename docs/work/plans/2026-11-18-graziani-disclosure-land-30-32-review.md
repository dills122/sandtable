# Land §§30–32 disclosure census: source review

**Disposition:** No material misstatement of the printed announcement, secrecy or discovery triggers found in the [census](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-30-32.md). Its audience and mode qualifications are appropriately bounded. The source wording on capture/use warrants the precision noted below; it does not establish a digital disclosure.

- Land §§30.41–.45, printed/PDF p. 42, starts chariot raids in July 1941; the Axis records the plan and execution dates, and **announces** the Frogman Raid when executing. The procedure is once per game and Campaign Game only. A recorded plan is not expressly declared secret by §30.42, and execution announcement does not prove earlier disclosure or a Graziani six-turn raid.
- Land §32.18, p. 43, describes dummy Supply Units as intended to confuse, moving like real units with less motorization and removed **upon discovery**. It does not enumerate all discovery triggers, discovery recipients or observations. Section 32.13 states an enemy combat unit entering a Supply Unit's hex captures it and the supplies are **“used immediately and freely.”** The census paraphrases this as supplies “may be used”; retain the printed phrase when resolving any mandatory-versus-permissive and actual stock-debit question. It does not specify a complete opponent-facing inventory or decide dummy/capture interaction; see [capture-use review](2026-11-17-graziani-captured-supply-use-review.md).
- Section 32.63, p. 44, explicitly has Axis announce which convoy lanes are being used; Commonwealth may attack arriving convoys separately, regardless of map weather. It does not expressly announce the full manifest or future lanes. §32.72 calls the **scenario allowance die** secret; §32.73 calls each attack plot secret during Commonwealth Fleet Assignment, limits attacks to one per OpStage, and names Tactical Naval Movement for execution. The source note properly separates those secrets from later attack effects and leaves timing and audience mapping open.
- Section 32.0, p. 43, is scoped to Land without both Air and Logistics. Section 32.7's simplified fleet attack and §30.4's campaign chariot procedure have their own different scope and timing; neither is evidence of a particular target, dice result, phase mapping or legal scenario action. Section 30.34's instruction to track ratings and damage is not itself a general opposing-view rule.
