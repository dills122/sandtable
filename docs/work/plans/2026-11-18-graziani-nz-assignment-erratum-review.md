# Independent review: NZ assignment example

**Result:** The erratum and underlying example support the note's hypothetical assignment/attachment distinction. They do not direct a Graziani transfer or correct the unrelated 6th NZ Brigade arrival conflict. Scenario and OA identities, locations and dates match the note.

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 29, §19.14 example names 1st King's Royal Rifle Corps, hypothetically detached from 7th Armored and absorbed by 2nd NZ. [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2 left column, corrects its last line to attached to NZ and assigned to 7th Armored. Section 19.13 separately permits one assigned and one attached parent, possibly different; attached units are represented by their parent's counter in the same hex, while assignment does not require co-location. Section 19.14's general rule addresses a parent formation's own higher-level relationship; its example does not itself execute any setup attachment.
- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), printed p. 27 / PDF p. 3, §60.41 places 1 KRRC at C3922 with `7Spt/7`, 7th Armored at D3612 with previously listed division units detached, and NZ HQ, 4th NZ Brigade and named support at Alexandria E3613 and/or E3714. [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 6, lists 1 KRRC in 7th Armored's organization under the 7th Support Group area; note d expands its name. The source example's division-level description does not by itself settle the immediate-parent representation of that nested organization. Physical separation and the example do not prove a later attachment.
- Land §19.42 says a non-assigned unit may attach subject to the stated limitations, co-location and two CP for **both** units in the owning player's Reorganization Segment only, with a prior Movement Segment described for co-location. Section 19.46 on p. 30 adds a nested-attachment condition and exception, so eligibility and any parent identity must be checked for a concrete case; the note appropriately refrains from claiming a legal move or available slot.
- Commonwealth schedule PDF p. 3 lists 6th NZ Field Artillery at GT2/OpStage 3 and 6 NZ Brigade at GT6/OpStage 3; OA p. 18 prints `3/2` for artillery and `3/6` for brigade HQ and its 24th–26th battalions. Land p. 31 §20.11's example places both in September `3/2`. The §19.14 correction is about 1 KRRC and does not correct those distinct dates. This review does not choose between conflicting arrival statements.
