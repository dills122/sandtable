# Matruh attachment capacity: continuation review

**Disposition:** The printed Matruh OA establishes a normal ceiling of six attached units, subject to the Maximum Attachments Chart. It does not establish a six-unit scenario starting roster. The 1979 assignment erratum creates a separate question about how that ceiling interacts with assigned units; do not adopt either six total or eight total as a resolved post-erratum rule. No setup or runtime admission follows.

## Source findings

- [Commonwealth OA, Matruh entry](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 10, says no units may be assigned, but up to **six units may be attached**, referring to the [Maximum Attachments Chart §19.5](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF p. 6. Those attachments count as Matruh garrison units. The chart's Matruh GT 1–12 row specifies six units, no tank, one gun class. Its unit definition and company-equivalent provisions qualify how those units are counted, as described below. The six is not a count of named scenario battalions. The OA says maximum attachments change when the counter becomes Selby Force in GT 12, Operations Stage 2. This review does not infer the later limit.
- [Land §19.4](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) distinguishes assigned attachments from additional non-assigned attachments. [September 1979 errata §19.5](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 2, clarifies that chart allowances are for attachments **in addition to normally assigned units**, even when those assigned units are elsewhere. The Matruh OA was written with zero assignable units; [errata §4.44b item 2](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 1, makes Buffs and Hampshire starting assignees contrary to that prohibition. Whether the OA's six is a special total cap that survives this exception, or whether §19.5 allows six additional attachments alongside the two assigned units, requires explicit source precedence. Six total is a reasonable conservative candidate, not an established post-erratum reconciliation.
- [Scenario §59.65](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, concerns **supply-unit stacking** in abstract logistics: initial placement may exceed hex stacking limits only if unavoidable under scenario distribution instructions. No additional supply unit may stack there while over limit; once compliant, normal stacking restrictions apply thereafter. Motorization points transporting supply units do not count against hex stacking limits. This does not authorize over-capacity formation attachments.
- **Scenario §59.2 does contain a distinct formation exception**, on the same printed page: a listed unit exceeding formation organization limits may operate normally, including itself being assigned or attached; no new subordinate may be assigned or attached, and a detached subordinate may not be replaced in violation of the limits. This requires an actually listed over-limit formation; it does not authorize an excess newly created by the owner's free same-hex setup reorganization. It does not itself establish that Matruh starts over limit, add unidentified battalions, or settle whether six is a total or additional-attachment allowance.
- [Scenario §60.41](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, expressly lists Essex, Durham Light Infantry and South Staffordshires as attached to Matruh; §59.2 defines `Att` as attached but not assigned. These are three named non-assigned battalions at the listed setup, before any player setup reorganization; their chart cost needs separate verification. The erratum establishes Buffs and Hampshire as **assigned**; it does not explicitly say either is attached or identify either location. If independently established to start in Matruh's hex, §4.25's default may make each attached absent a specific exception. Those assignments alone do not add two to the count of non-assigned attachments.

## Consequences and limits

If both erratum assignees are independently established to be co-located and attached, the hypothesized starting roster has five named attachments: three explicitly attached plus two assigned and attached. That is a roster count, not proven chart-capacity arithmetic. The chart defines a unit using a full-strength battalion- or company-equivalent counter; three company-equivalents equal one battalion-equivalent. Division- and brigade-equivalent units may carry two non-shell company-equivalents without charge against the maximum, subject to type restrictions. The OA's brigade-level HQ description alone does not establish brigade-equivalent eligibility. Applicable strength, equivalence, nesting and counting need review, along with setup detachment and stacking; see [capacity follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-followup.md). No sixth battalion is identified by the capacity figure.

The [prior setup review](2026-11-17-graziani-continuation-setup-review.md) retains D3714 as conditional and separately records the assignment/placement ambiguity. This continuation does not choose a different placement, assert an official ruling, or infer that §59.65 permits an over-limit formation. Chart qualifiers and the unavoidable condition incorporate the visual source checks in the [independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-continuation-review.md). No exhaustive later-errata search was performed.
