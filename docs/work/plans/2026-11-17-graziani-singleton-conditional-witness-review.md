# Conditional Commonwealth singleton witness: source review

**Disposition:** The [candidate note](2026-11-17-graziani-singleton-conditional-witness.md) is appropriately conditional. Scenario rows and first-line truck allocations support the proposed local arithmetic, but do not alone establish a complete legal setup or prove the actual hexes are singletons.

## Checked sources

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27 §60.41, lists 1 RTR at `C3520` with five medium trucks, 2nd Rifle Brigade at `C3320` with ten, and 11th Hussars at `C3020` with three. It separately places 7th Armored Division at `D3612` with its listed division units above detached. This establishes the printed row locations and detachment instruction, not every represented constituent or all possible co-locations.
- Scenario PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26 §59.42 assigns listed first-line trucks to units in their listed hex, allowing the player to divide them among units there. The candidate assigns each row's trucks to its sole assumed local combat unit; that is consistent with this clause if the underlying singleton and unit eligibility hold. §59.2 permits same-hex units to be organized subject to formation and stacking limits; it is not a blanket exemption from them. §§59.63 and .66 govern abstract motorization and optional initial supplies; converting or loading them does not establish that a complete legal allocation has been constructed.
- [Commonwealth OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 6, lists 1 RTR `g`, `7 × A9`; 2 Rifle Brigade `n`, `N`; and 11 Hussars `ll`, `U@6 TOE` beneath 7th Armored Division. Their distinct parent labels and printed hierarchy do not, without application of setup and attachment rules, establish that no additional represented unit is present. The 6/7/6 baseline and raw `12/21/18` calculations rely on [previous same-scan unit reviews](2026-11-17-graziani-singleton-zoc-threshold-review.md), including their chart/strength interpretation; they were not freshly recalculated here.
- [Land rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed pp. 16–17 §§9.16 and .29, distinguish location-specific garrison free-stacking from ordinary units and exclude first-line attached trucks from organizational size and road-space stacking limits. Those rules do not turn a listed truck quantity into an extra combat constituent. Free-stacking and convoy capacity language does not itself settle every possible §10.11 mixed-hex total. Under the stipulated actual one-point isolated combat stack, §10.11's strict greater-than-one condition fails; actual occupancy and effective stacking remain prerequisites, not inferred facts.

## Remaining scope

Check every mandatory deployment, local attachments and represented children, actual terrain capacity, optional supply/truck placement and selected mode, along with timing relative to organization. The candidate's choice to place discretionary pieces elsewhere needs a complete legal placement, not merely a lack of a fixed extra combat row in those three hexes. No affirmative legal witness, operational ZOC or contact outcome is established by this review.
