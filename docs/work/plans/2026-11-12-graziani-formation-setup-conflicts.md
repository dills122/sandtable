## Matruh Garrison: assignment versus attachment

- Scenario §60.41 places Matruh Garrison at D3714 with three named attached
  infantry units and listed trucks.
- Errata §4.44b says 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshires start the campaign and Italian
  scenarios as part of, explicitly “assigned to,” Matruh Garrison.
- The Commonwealth OA sheet (PDF p. 10) describes Matruh Garrison as a
  headquarters with no units assigned, while allowing up to six attachments.
  The unassigned infantry sheet (PDF p. 11) marks 1st Buffs and 1st Hampshire
  deployed at start. Thus both named units must be considered, but the printed
  relationship terminology conflicts. `assigned` and `attached` are not silently
  interchangeable domain values.

Candidate readings: treat the erratum as a specific exception to the OA's general
no-assignment rule; interpret its parenthetical as loose wording for attachment;
or preserve the conflict until further authority is found. Each must be checked
against formation organization, scenario placement/attachment choices, stacking
and exact unit names before selecting and versioning a ruling. The correction is
not a license to add duplicates if the units appear elsewhere in a setup expansion.

An [independently maintained CNA project](https://tangled.org/glassparency.bsky.social/cna-agent-sim/blob/main/informatiion/CURRENT-CHECKPOINT.md)
reports applying the erratum as an assigned-unit exception and removing Matruh's
no-assignment restriction. This is a separately reported interpretation, useful
for comparison; its conclusions and implementation are not authority for
Sandtable, proof of an official clarification, or a field-by-field second entry.

### Interaction with setup and organization rules

Original Land §19.11–19.13 defines assignment and attachment separately: an
assigned unit can be physically elsewhere, while an attached unit is represented
with its parent in the same hex; the same unit can have different assigned and
attached parents. Section 19.41 permits attachment of an assigned unit at one CP
per unit, while §19.42 imposes a different timing/cost for an unassigned unit.
Section 19.43–19.44 likewise differentiates detachment. A decision here therefore
changes legal actions and costs, not just displayed organization.

Land §4.25 says assigned units that begin in their parent's hex are attached by
default unless specifically stated otherwise. However, scenario introduction
§59.2 also allows units initially in the same hex to be organized, including
attachment/detachment, as the owning player chooses within applicable limits.
The exact precedence of an explicit starting attachment, the default and this
setup choice must be resolved before requiring all five named units to begin
attached. Preserve source-stated relationships separately from any legal setup
choice and validate the selected result.

September errata §19.5 explains that extra attachments are in addition to units
normally assigned. The Matruh OA sheet says up to six units may be attached and
refers to the maximum attachment chart. If §4.44b creates an exceptional assigned
status, whether the two exceptional assignees consume any of Matruh's six slots
is an additional source interaction; do not infer a numeric free-slot count from
the setup list alone. Check the specific chart, any conflicting usage of “all
attachments,” nesting and the selected relationship ruling. Test independently
assigned-but-detached, assigned-and-attached, unassigned-and-attached, limits,
CP costs, setup choice and post-creation reorganization.

The OA sheet also restricts voluntary movement of the garrison headquarters until
it becomes Selby Force or the operations stage in which it is close-assaulted;
its normal conversion occurs in turn 12, outside the six-turn scenario. Do not
conclude movement is impossible in all six-turn states: the close-assault exception
could apply. Establish reachable conditions and test both blocked movement and the
source-defined exception. Parent/child capabilities must use the accepted
relationship semantics and owner choices.

## Axis corps artillery duplication

Errata §60.31 says the XXI Corps artillery appears twice in the scenario's
“Anywhere in Libya” list. The printed setup indeed includes that repeated item.
Treat the duplicate as a correction question in the source ledger; record one
underlying identity only after checking the relevant OA and counter data. Do not
collapse two distinct corps units merely because their abbreviations resemble one
another, and do not count the repeated text as proof of two counters.
