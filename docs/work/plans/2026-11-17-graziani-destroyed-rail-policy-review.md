# Graziani destroyed railroad witness policy: independent critique

**Disposition:** Retained-witness is a coherent, explicitly versioned planning interpretation, supported but not settled by the reviewed text. No source-based blocker to that limited selection found. It is not an executable rule or evidence of historical reachability. Timing, geographic west, construction provenance, complete survey, competing rail gates and an observation-safe action protocol remain admission blockers.

## Findings and distinguishing cases

1. **History and identity must be independent of present damage.** The [source review](2026-11-17-graziani-destroyed-rail-identity-review.md) supports distinguishing built-then-destroyed from never-built track, while acknowledging that §8.78 does not expressly settle the witness question. For the same established qualifying track, occupant and west relation, intact and destroyed states both block under retained-witness; destroyed does not permit rail entry. A marker, map ink or railhead name alone does not establish a legal construction or destruction history. Test an initial constructed track as well as a later legally established construction; do not require an invented build event for track established at setup. Repair and destruction transitions need separately legal prerequisites and snapshot ordering.

2. **Historical ink and overlapping track are distinct claims.** Excluding historical Soluch–Benghazi–Barce ink alone is a versioned application of §§8.71 and 24.6, not an express §8.78 exception. Independently established qualifying track at the same hex can still witness, even if destroyed under retained-witness; unknown overlap cannot be treated as absent. The September errata's §54.4 reference leaves its interaction with this exclusion unresolved. Neither this policy nor that erratum proves a general ban on Axis rail use. Test historical-only, independently constructed overlap intact and destroyed, and unknown overlap with all other witness conditions held true.

3. **Three-valued existential semantics need explicit controls.** A candidate with all required conditions true blocks; any proven-false required condition excludes that candidate even when another is unknown. Without a known witness, unknown identity, construction, combat status, west relation or incomplete survey prevents proof of absence. A proven witness dominates other unknown candidates. In particular, under retained-witness, unknown damage alone does not make an otherwise proven previously constructed witness unknown; under operational-witness, unknown damage can matter. Test these cases separately, including an unbuilt candidate with unknown occupancy and a known witness alongside an unknown candidate. This establishes only the §8.78 result, not whole-action legality.

4. **Define the alternative before comparing results.** “Usable witness track” could mean physically undestroyed track or full legal usability, including occupancy, ZOC, connectivity and other action gates. Those meanings need not agree; an Axis-occupied witness is itself unavailable for Allied rail entry under §8.73. For a meaningful comparison, specify whether operational-witness means physical track condition alone and state how unknown condition is handled. If a broader interpretation is considered, name and test it separately. Compare policies on identical snapshots: intact, destroyed, unknown condition and repaired, with independent legal prerequisites and all other witness conditions fixed.

5. **West is a relation from used hex to witness, not travel direction.** A qualifying eastern off-route witness blocks a western used rail hex in either direction of travel, subject to the chosen geographic relation and use semantics. Test the converse explicitly: a witness west of the used hex does not block that hex under this predicate; equal-west remains dependent on the selected relation. Survey all potentially qualifying eastern locations for each used hex across relevant sheets and branches; lack of a connected route or a completed eastern inventory does not prove no witness. Check enemy occupancy and ZOC separately, including the unresolved §8.73/§10.26 interaction.

6. **Privacy covers consequences and transitions, not just error text.** Compare complete side-visible traces for equal authorized histories with differing hidden occupancy, witness identity and construction or condition, including action enumeration, submission, acceptance, state changes, events, later observations, ordering, timing and replay. An identical pending message or fixed delay alone cannot prevent disclosure if eventual admission or completion differs. Specify authorized disclosure or an observation-independent protocol; until then hold the public action boundary unavailable. Internal unknown and block remain distinct for adjudication, not public explanations. Any separately justified disclosure, including under §10.16, requires its own evidence and audience rule.
