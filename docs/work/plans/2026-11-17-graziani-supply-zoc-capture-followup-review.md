# Supply ZOC capture: independent source review

**Disposition:** The [follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-zoc-capture-followup.md) correctly distinguishes §10.29's conditional ZOC capture from §32.13's entry capture. Neither absence of combat capabilities nor zero stacking alone settles the former. Section 3 supplies useful comparisons, not a definition that conclusively classifies an abstract Supply Unit or resolves “alone.”

## Source check

- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 18 §10.29, prohibits convoy entry to enemy ZOC unless already occupied by a friendly combat unit; it separately forbids voluntary non-combat entry to an unoccupied hex in enemy ZOC. If **such a unit is alone in an enemy ZOC at any time during the enemy Movement/Combat Phase and has no strength of any type**, it is captured. The listed examples include bare HQs, Engineers and Air Squadron Ground Support Units, followed by “etc.” The passage does not specifically list Supply Units, define whether “alone” includes non-combat counters or markers, or use a zero-stacking threshold. Do not replace that wording with a hex merely lacking friendly combat units.
- Printed p. 6 §3.23 lists combat-unit types but says certain non-combat units can count as combat units for particular determinations. §3.33 says an HQ without attached combat units occupies zero stacking points and is no longer a combat unit, yet may use parenthesized ratings. This demonstrates why zero stacking and non-combat status do not alone establish absence of strength **of any type**. Section 3.36 has a distinct HQ capture condition: no combat values with or without parentheses, in a hex without combat units, when an enemy combat unit places the HQ in ZOC. Its wording does not rewrite §10.29's “alone,” or automatically extend that HQ-specific case to supply.
- Printed p. 7 §3.5 distinguishes TOE strength and combat characteristics from CPA; it does not expressly determine whether a Supply Unit's cargo constitutes strength for §10.29. Section 4.23 p. 8 depicts a Supply Dump counter, while §32.12 p. 43 uses that counter to represent an abstract Supply Unit; representation by itself does not decide whether the unit falls within §10.29's “such a unit.” §32.13 says no combat capabilities, and captures it on enemy combat-unit entry with supplies used immediately and freely; §32.14 gives zero stacking. These facts support the question, not an express answer about §10.29 or its consequences.
- §10.21 p. 18 restricts projection through certain hexsides and into inaccessible adjacent terrain; §§10.11 and .15 p. 17 independently restrict ZOC exertion. Confirm actual qualifying enemy ZOC and correct enemy phase before testing any capture predicate. §10.26's movement-purpose negation by a friendly combat unit is another distinct interaction; it does not itself define §10.29's “alone.” Section 8.13 p. 14 generally bars enemy-occupied entry. A specific entry exception or simultaneous-event priority needs a reviewed choice.

The source supports neither automatic capture from mere adjacency nor a general exemption for Supply Units. A later interpretation needs explicit answers for unit classification, no-strength and alone predicates, active phase, ZOC and edge geometry, entry versus ZOC ordering, real/dummy discovery, assigned carriers, stock use and side-visible traces. §32.13 does not expressly extend its immediate/free use wording to the independent §10.29 trigger. Initial scenario placement and actual reachability were not independently audited here.
