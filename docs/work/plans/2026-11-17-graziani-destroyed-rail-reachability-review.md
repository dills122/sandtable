# Graziani destroyed-rail reachability: independent review

**Disposition:** Accept the bounded planning conclusion, with one wording qualification: §8.78 states a prohibition on western use, not a requirement that a move actually be attempted or completed. A separately identified, otherwise legal western use is needed to demonstrate a practical effect or distinguish policies, not to make the prohibition exist. No setup witness, earliest turn, legal six-turn sequence, or six-turn impossibility is established.

## Source check

- [Scenario §60.7](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28, says the railroad runs to Mersa Matruh D3714 and ends there at game start. It does not specify a destroyed railroad hex or expressly certify every initial rail hex undamaged. The endpoint supports initial construction extent; it does not settle each hex's condition, topology, control, occupancy, or legal movement. Initial damage is **unknown on this evidence**, not established present or absent. A fuller setup, map, and applicable errata review could change that evidentiary status.
- [Land §§8.76–8.78](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 16, separately bar entering destroyed rail by rail movement until repair and bar Allied railroad movement use of any rail hex west of a rail hex occupied by an Axis combat unit. Neither expressly determines whether destroyed constructed rail counts for that nonlocal witness. Retaining it under RUL-GRA-26 is a selected interpretation; requiring an operational witness remains an alternative. Neither makes destroyed track traversable.
- Land §24.66, p. 37, permits destruction by air bombardment, artillery barrage, or an eligible engineer, engineer-capable HQ, or infantry unit of at least three TOE strength points spending a complete OpStage in the hex without expending capability points. It requires a destroyed-rail marker. Those possible mechanisms do not prove their prerequisites, outcomes, ordering, or occurrence in a particular scenario. Section 24.67 separately requires construction westward without skipping, and unbuilt rail does not function. Section 24.65 forbids building or rebuilding enemy-controlled or enemy-occupied rail; it is not a prohibition on destruction.

## Timing and comparison

For a setup candidate, independently establish a constructed qualifying rail hex, destruction and Axis combat occupancy at the selected snapshot. A railhead statement alone does not establish that conjunction or rule it out. For a later candidate, prove the full legal event history, including location, eligible actor or attack, applicable resources and mode, destruction result, occupancy and snapshot order. A ground unit's complete OpStage condition does not establish that it starts in position, survives, and satisfies all other conditions; bombardment does not automatically succeed. Compare all earlier candidate snapshots before asserting earliest occurrence or impossibility within six turns.

For a useful comparison of retained-witness and operational-witness planning policies, hold fixed the same proven constructed-and-destroyed eastern hex, qualifying Axis combat occupant, snapshot, western relation, and otherwise legal western rail use. Change only whether that destroyed hex qualifies as witness. A synthetic state can distinguish interpretations; it cannot prove a historical legal sequence. If initial damage, occupancy, or earlier actions are unknown, keep the reachability result unknown. Actual western movement is not needed for the rule's prohibition to apply; a legal candidate use demonstrates the consequence.
