# Graziani six-turn exclusions: source review

**Disposition:** No incorrect timing bound found in the selected rows. Retain the
ordinary-schedule and conditional-arrival qualifications; this review does not
establish global absence, legal arrivals, or an exhaustive exception audit.

## Checks

- Scenario §§60.22 and 60.47 place play at GT1 OpStage 1 through GT6
  OpStage 3 and prohibit Commonwealth replacement-point arrivals before
  November 1940. The Axis turn track places GT1–2 in September and GT3–6 in
  October. Section 60.47 also refers separately to unit reinforcements and
  their training, so the replacement-point restriction does not empty those
  subjects. Section 60.41 includes a Commonwealth division already in training.
- Axis land reinforcement schedule §4.43b begins at GT9 OpStage 2. This
  supports the absence of entries in that schedule during GT1–6, not the
  absence of all Axis forces or shipments. Scenario §60.37 requires September
  convoy planning before play.
- Chart §20.66 gives first planning turns of 5 for Italian infantry, 9 for
  the five listed artillery types, 10 for 47/32 and 75/46, 31 for armored
  reconnaissance, and 38 for German infantry, the earliest German row. Its
  two-turn rule gives the document's corresponding nominal arrivals. Truck
  chart §20.66a gives light 4→6, medium 6→8, and heavy 5→7; Italian Light AA
  is 2→4 and CV L.3/CV 33/35 is 3→5. These are earliest chart-derived dates,
  not evidence of actual plans, transport, arrival stage, or completion.
- Scenario §60.31 separately permits two starting Autoblinda 40 points as
  attached points or trained and fueled replacements at Tobruk. The chart's
  GT31 planning date cannot erase them. The French 149 mm and 155 mm chart
  rows instead become available in Tunis at GT39 without the usual two-week
  delay; neither creates a GT1–6 receipt. September 1979 errata §20.66 changes
  the explanatory note's tank designation, not these dates.
- Section 60.6 assigns Italian initiative for all of GT1, with normal
  determination beginning GT2. It does not itself suppress stage choices or
  weather. Six stages from GT5 OpStage 1 through GT6 OpStage 3 is inclusive
  counting only; it does not decide training credit or legal completion.
