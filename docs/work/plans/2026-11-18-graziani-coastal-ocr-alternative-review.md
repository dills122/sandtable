# Coastal geometry alternative search: review

**Disposition: one wording correction requested.** The [alternative search note](2026-11-18-graziani-coastal-ocr-alternative.md) correctly describes the newly checked cases and bounded negative, but its paragraph describing §30.21 as “coastal versus inland bombardment” risks reinstating the inland-only limitation rejected in the [earlier follow-up review](2026-11-18-graziani-coastal-ocr-followup-review.md). The original §30.21 permits specified ships to bombard **any adjacent hex** to the occupied coastal hex. Say “occupied coastal hex versus adjacent-hex bombardment” instead.

Visually checked Land p. 18 §10.21(a): ZOC does not extend through all-sea, major-river or lake hexsides. This is a hexside restriction; it does not by itself settle whole-hex coastal membership or whether adjacent sea suffices. The neighboring §10.21(c) also imposes a terrain-entry constraint for ZOC, which does not supply a definition of coast. Land p. 15 §8.42 describes the Libyan Plateau rising from a strip bordering the Mediterranean and then addresses escarpment movement; it gives no general adjacency test. Addendum p. 45 §3.4(C) discusses shoreward targets and Commonwealth warship movement, without assigning the candidate cells' coastal status.

Repeated selected alternative literal searches against the existing 47-page OCR found the cited all-sea, Mediterranean and shoreward passages. Their meanings are positive source observations; OCR recognition, omitted synonyms, other books and later clarifications remain search limits. The separate map-key mixed land/water example is not a proof that every other geometry is excluded. No candidate geometry or field correction follows from these passages.

## Recheck

The updated note now describes §30.21 as occupied-coastal-hex versus adjacent-hex bombardment, without an inland-only qualifier. The requested correction is complete.
