# Fleet rest/repair policy audit: independent review

**Disposition:** No material contradiction or false source-mandated overlap found in the [policy audit](2026-11-18-graziani-fleet-repair-policy-audit.md). Its stage-6 versus stage-8 comparison is conditional arithmetic, and RUL-GRA-15 selects repair-before-attack ordering without settling whether particular stages qualify for repair or rest credit.

- [RUL-GRA-15](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-15-abstract-fleet-attack-order-planning-interpretation) selects Fleet Assignment, completed Fleet Repair, then valid abstract attack before Reserve Designation. Its [linked proposal](2026-11-17-graziani-fleet-sequence-next.md) explicitly applies a qualifying same-stage repair completion before that attack and retains separate progress rules. This is a planning choice for the missing §32.73 segment, not an admitted or printed source ordering. Neither text expressly selects shared or disjoint rest/repair credit.
- Land §30.37, p. 42, requires six **consecutive** Operations Stages in Alexandria to repair one Gun Rating level, no engineer or supplies, and all work restarts if the ship moves before completion. Section 30.36 makes a ship with its entire Gun Rating eliminated sunk and unrepairable; §30.38 allows AA fire even during repair. The inspected repair case does not expressly decide new-damage reset, a partial-stage accrual boundary or AA restoration.
- Land §30.16, p. 41, states two port OpStages for every one in action and at most three consecutive at sea. Section 30.25, p. 42, separately requires the next two stages **in Alexandria** after offshore bombardment and excludes AA fire from that requirement. Their coexistence with §30.37 does not expressly state whether one stage may satisfy both rest and repair or that these duties must be disjoint. The audit's examples assume an already damaged, unsunk ship, an eligible continuous stay and a specified order of credit; stage 8 is not presented as a mandatory source rule.
- **Attribution refinement:** The audit says the ruling “expressly requires a separate repair contract.” RUL-GRA-15 expressly requires admission contracts and **damage/repair ordering**, while the linked proposal says to respect applicable repair-progress rules and blocks on unknown repair prerequisites. This supports the audit's open repair-contract gate, but the ruling does not literally prescribe a separately named standalone contract or decide its clock. Keep the requirement described at that level of precision.
