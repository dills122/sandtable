The C render includes the Bir Buq–Sidi Barrani coastal context and the Bir
Emba, Piccadilly Circus and Oxford Circus labels. These distinguish the
locality from an arbitrary ridge and line elsewhere. Those labels are not
repeated visibly on the inspected D edge, so they identify the C-side setting;
they are not independent name matches across sheets. The coast, ridge and
railway form a relative spatial pattern, but their similarity remains a single
scan's evidence and does not prove physical registration.

## Candidate and limits

Under the [33-column geometry hypothesis](2026-11-17-graziani-seam-geometry-comparison.md),
printed C3534 occupies the position of D3501, and C3533 neighbors D3501;
C3633 neighbors D3601. The observed pattern is consistent with that placement.
It does not prove C3534/D3501 identity, exact endpoints, or a feature on any
particular shared side. The original Land §4.1 wording and the candidate
geometry remain in tension as described in that comparison. The
[independent edge transcription](2026-11-17-graziani-seam-cd-independent.md)
and [identity pilot](2026-11-12-graziani-map-identity-pilot.md#independent-cd-sheet-edge-check)
contain broader observations and the existing bounded contract; this follow-up
adds no admitted pair or terrain feature. The unfinished railway is not a
usable rail route merely because its ink appears continuous.

No observation here establishes that a Siwa–Tobruk route must cross this seam,
that either dashed line connects to Siwa or Tobruk, or that a convoy may use
it. A positive route requires a complete ordered adjacent-side witness and
applicable terrain, state and convoy rules; a negative result requires an
exhaustive eligible search including unresolved boundary coverage. See the
[Siwa local source review](2026-11-17-graziani-siwa-route-source-review.md).
Next, inspect larger overlap areas and original-sheet geometry at higher
resolution, independently check feature endpoints and corrected overlay rules,
then trace candidate routes under an explicitly selected convoy policy.
