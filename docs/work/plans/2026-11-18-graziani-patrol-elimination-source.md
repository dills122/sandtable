# Patrol elimination: objective-loss source check

**Result:** No explicit precedence instruction resolving §16.33's “goes no further” against §16.34 was located in the bounded Land/September-errata check. The original rule has additional internal support for checking objective losses after complete **elimination**: §16.31 says both players must check possible losses, §16.34 expressly names the all-eliminated branch, and the §16.8 chart provides a special result for a completely eliminated patrol. These statements do not expressly settle whether **all captured**, or a mixture of captured and killed with no survivors, counts as eliminated. No policy is selected.

## Source interaction

- [Land §16.0 procedure](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), p. 26, says remaining patrol points lead to a reconnaissance roll and information; its final sentence then calls for an Objective Loss Table roll. Its paragraph structure does not expressly resolve whether that final sentence is confined to the survivor branch.
- Section 16.31 says **both** patroller and patrollee must check for possible combat losses, at different times. Section 16.33 says if no Patrol Points survive, the phasing player “goes no further” and receives no information. Section 16.34 separately says that after obtaining information **or after all units are eliminated**, whichever occurs first, the phasing player rolls for objective loss regardless of own losses. These passages support a narrower no-further-*information* reading, but §16.33's broader words do not themselves state that limitation. They do not explicitly rank one case over another.
- [Common chart §16.8](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF p. 3, lists objective killed/captured `0/0` for 1–4, `0/1` for 5 and `1/0` for 6. Its footnote converts the die-5 capture to one killed if the **patrolling force was completely eliminated**; §16.34 likewise converts captured to eliminated in that case. This is an explicit consequence for a completely eliminated patrol, not an explicit definition of elimination as including all captured or mixed captured/killed losses.
- Section 16.32's no-combat-target branch skips the Survival Table and patrol losses; AA/Flak and engineers do not count as combat units for that specific check. It does not itself settle the later objective-loss question. [Prior numerical follow-up and review](2026-11-18-graziani-patrol-interaction-followup-review.md) separately document the one-point over-allocation of a killed/captured result and the two players' distinct loss choices; those questions are not resolved by the present reading.

**Conditional discriminator:** With an otherwise legal patrol, a target for which §16.32 requires survival, and an accepted loss interpretation yielding all points **killed**, compare a die-5 objective outcome under (a) stopping all steps at §16.33 and (b) proceeding to §16.34 and the chart's conversion. The former has no objective roll; the latter yields one killed objective point, subject to source-specified eligible loss allocation. No actual patrol, roll, legal point assignment or source-mandated priority is asserted. An all-captured no-survivor example is a separate classification question, not automatically the same branch.
