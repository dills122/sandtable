# Review: Cairo instructor candidate

**Disposition:** The source supports a conditional 2 KOR instructor candidate; no legal co-location or credited training stage is established. One wording qualification avoids treating a missing training label as proof of the unit's state.

**Qualification:** The [candidate](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-c-source-3.md) calls 2 KOR “non-training.” Scenario §60.41 lists 2 KOR in Cairo separately from the 6th Australian Division's “in training” entry; it does not expressly state that 2 KOR is barred from, or actually not undergoing, training at a later checkpoint. Prefer “not listed as in training” and continue requiring its actual morale, eligibility and presence. This does not change the conditional arithmetic or establish that 2 KOR must train.

## Source checks

- Scenario §60.41, PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, lists 6th Australian Division **in training at Cairo and/or Helwan (E1430)** with the named exclusions; separately lists 2nd King's Own Royal Regiment at Cairo. Neither specifies a shared Cairo hex or a particular eligible trainee. The “and/or” choice cannot be reduced to automatic co-location.
- Commonwealth OA p. 11 places `2 KOR` under unassigned British infantry-type units with `m`, `N`, `D` and sheet Basic Morale `+1`. OA key p. 5 defines `N` as maximum TOE for a non-tank/non-gun unit and `D` as deployed GT1 stage 1; characteristic chart p. 22 places `m` among infantry battalion-equivalents with maximum TOE **6**. The single sheet morale and source-baseline strength are evidence, not a certified later morale, actual point allocation or parent state.
- Land §17.35 p. 28 requires a combat battalion at its designated Basic Morale in the trainee's hex during each training Operations Stage, with at least one-third assigned TOE strength. It permits infantry divisions or brigades to use an infantry-type battalion and requires a tank battalion for units with tanks. Conditional on six assigned and six present points, six exceeds the two-point threshold; that arithmetic does not prove every other requirement. The instructor does not count against stacking, but the trainee and other occupants still require their own applicable eligibility and stacking checks.
- Section 17.33's no-CP provision concerns the **training unit**, as the candidate states; do not automatically apply it to every possible instructor activity. Determine instructor presence over the relevant stage and interruption/phase semantics separately. Section 17.36 allows eligible units to fight at lower untrained morale; source setup's training designation is not proof of a completed increment. Replacement-point training has separate scope.
