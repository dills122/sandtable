# ZOC raw defense: further source qualification

**Disposition:** This follow-up found no inspected original passage explicitly deciding whether §10.15 counts nominally rated TOE or only points presently able to participate in a hypothetical Close Assault. The rules do establish some current-state exclusions and distinguish anti-tank **unit type** from other weapons possessing an anti-armor rating. They do not define an ordinary artillery position or allocation decision at the pre-combat ZOC boundary. Keep nominal and available-strength interpretations open.

## Source evidence

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed pp. 17–18, §10.15 says unit(s) in a hex totaling less than ten **Raw Defensive Close Assault Points** do not exert ZOC; it explicitly presumes **anti-tank units** Up Front for this purpose. Section 10.14 separately says units with cohesion −26 or worse do not exert ZOC. The latter is an explicit current-state rule, but it does not expressly say whether or how such a unit's nominal points participate in another unit's mixed-stack §10.15 threshold. Likewise §§10.11–.13 specify size/stack and non-projecting types; satisfying ten raw alone is insufficient.
- Land p. 6 §3.4 **Anti-Tank Units** describes units of that type and separately notes other TOE points that have anti-tank capability, including some artillery and AA weapons. An anti-armor rating or an anti-tank-capable weapon is not by itself an express unit-type conversion. §10.15's specific presumption names anti-tank **units**, so extending it to every gun, mixed unit or weapon with that capability is a project choice requiring further authority. Conversely the unit's own classification and composition must be evaluated before denying its presumption. No general answer is supplied for mixed parent formations. Section 3.23 also states certain non-combat units may be considered combat units for specified determinations including ZOC; do not apply a blanket counter-type shortcut.
- Land p. 19 §11.3 defines raw combat strength as TOE strength times rating for the function, before division by ten; §11.34 totals raw before conversion. Section 11.36 calls Raw and Actual strength totals secret until attack, while §10.16 p. 18 separately requires disclosure of whether a potentially projecting unit exerts ZOC when the phasing unit begins a Movement Segment in, or moves into, the hex. Strength secrecy and required ZOC disclosure coexist; neither mandates publication of all factors used in the computation. This supplies no position or ammunition default at the ZOC check.
- Land p. 22 §15.12 says pinned units and units retreating before assault into a hex under Close Assault cannot add ratings to defensive Close Assault; the case specifies this for non-phasing defenders. Sections 15.13–.15 separately address Back versus Forward guns, uncommitted units and units out of ammunition. Section 12.11–.12 p. 20 positions artillery involved in combat at the Combat Segment boundary; §12.16 has a separate Back heavy-weapons restriction, and §12.18 requires ratings and ammo for Forward artillery participation. These provisions give the immediate-availability interpretation evidence, but do not expressly extend all combat participation conditions to an earlier movement-time ZOC threshold. Nor do they prove that every nominal rating is currently effective or that missing position means zero. See [prior independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-zoc-raw-gun-position-review.md).
- Original §3.4's parenthesized-strength rule on p. 6 and [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) PDF p. 1 §3.4 concern use when attacked and specified neighboring strengths; the errata clarifies attack while alone or with only parenthesized-strength units. Errata §23.11 separately addresses engineers alongside friendly combat units. These are conditions on use in their described contexts; they do not expressly specify if parenthesized ratings are included in a pre-attack §10.15 calculation. The Axis gun chart key adds an ammo/non-parenthesized-rating condition, so chart versus errata hierarchy remains a separate question. Do not assume all parenthesized ratings are always usable or excluded for ZOC solely from these texts.

## Decision and tests

A coherent versioned policy must separately define raw-TOE eligibility, unit classification, current cohesion, parenthesized ratings, ammo and position/time representation, including the explicit anti-tank-unit presumption, then combine that result with §10.11 and directional §10.21. Possible choices remain potential nominal strength, combat-eligible strength with a specified snapshot/default, or an unknown result when needed predicates are unavailable. Record the actual proof and expose only information permitted by the separate §10.16/observation contract. Do not project a later combat commitment or position backward into an already committed movement event without an accepted correction/replay protocol.

Synthetic checks: anti-tank-type unit versus artillery with anti-armor rating; a mixed unit; nominally 9/10 raw; −26 cohesion alone and in a mixed stack; known/unknown ammo, Forward/Back and pinned/retreated states; parenthesized-only versus mixed ratings; pre-first-combat and successive combat boundaries; Land-only ammo; and equal authorized histories with required ZOC disclosure but private strengths. None is a claim that actual setup, route or combat is reachable.
