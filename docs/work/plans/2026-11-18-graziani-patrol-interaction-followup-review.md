# Patrol one-point loss interaction: source review

**Disposition:** Supports [follow-up](2026-11-18-graziani-patrol-interaction-followup.md)'s numerical reading and unresolved one-point conflict. No candidate cap, allocation or elimination interpretation is selected. Same scans, informed second reading, not an independent edition.

- Land §§16.12–.14 permit assignment of eligible TOE points and give maxima of two from one hex and three against one target; the checked text specifies no two-point minimum. A one-point patrol remains hypothetical and must separately meet type, cohesion, target, path, weather and stage restrictions. The [prior independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-patrol-details-independent.md) records §16.11's errata-expanded types; this review did not recheck errata.
- Common chart §16.6 shows killed/captured `0/0` for adjusted 0–3, `0/1` for 4, `1/0` for 5 and `1/1` for 6. Its all-recce −1 adjustment makes raw 6 yield 5; an otherwise eligible non-recce one-point patrol reaching survival die 6 encounters two listed losses for one assigned point. §16.33 gives the phasing player a choice **as to unit type**, but does not explicitly say to choose kill versus capture or how to cap the one-point result. Its reference to losses including trucks does not establish a truck conversion formula.
- Section 16.32 skips the survival roll and its patrol losses when the target has no combat units, with AA/Flak and engineers noncombat for that check. Section 16.33 says no surviving Patrol Points means no further information; §16.34 separately directs an objective roll after information or after all patrol units are eliminated. The scope of “eliminated” and the relationship between these passages remain interpretation questions, including all-captured and mixed-loss cases. No-combat skip alone does not expressly cancel objective resolution.
- Objective chart §16.8 gives killed/captured `0/0` for 1–4, `0/1` for 5 and `1/0` for 6; its footnote and §16.34 convert the capture result to a killed/eliminated point if the patrolling force is completely eliminated. Section 16.34 also assigns objective loss choice to the non-phasing player, from a combat unit; absent combat units it lists trucks, AA/Flak, engineers, then HQ in priority order. That separate choice does not settle patrol-loss priority.
- Chart §6.3 lists Patrol at zero CP, while Land §32.25 separately waives patrol supply expenditure in Land-only mode. Neither waives other restrictions. The candidate's dummy-only target and prisoner distinctions remain unresolved; no actual eligible patrol or target follows from the numerical example.
