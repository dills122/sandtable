# LA-01: Land-only §32.3 supply movement source census

November 18, 2026. Compared every numbered case in Land §32.3 with [register LA-15](2026-11-12-graziani-source-register.md) and the [remaining research queue](2026-11-17-graziani-remaining-source-research.md). Printed p. 43 contains **§§32.31–.37**, followed by §32.4; there are no further numbered §32.3 cases on that page. This is a documentary source-coverage census, not a source-complete rule admission, route proof or runtime audit.

Land §32.0 applies when the Land Game is played without **both** Air and Logistics; it introduces the abstract supply system. The explicitly printed abstract-air transport in §32.37 is therefore not automatically absent with the detailed Air Game. Do not extend these cases unchanged to every mixed-game mode. The September §32.0 erratum warns the abstract rules were untested; it does not decide a particular movement conflict.

No numbered §32.31–.37 case is missing from LA-15's source range; the matrix is an original reading and its README's older blank-review status is superseded by the attributed independent entry and [disposition](2026-11-17-graziani-independent-reviews.md). That entry and later reviews establish bounded transcriptions, not complete applicability or resolved raw differences. The concrete §32.35 ship-capacity/phase check remains useful alongside the named interpretation and reachability gates; this census does not claim other unexamined source cases do not exist.
