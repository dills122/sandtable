# Matruh route endpoints: independent source review

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Disposition:** Retain the [plan](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-route-endpoints.md) as a bounded research queue, not a route or legality proof. The source distinguishes the named rail line from victory convoy conditions. Clarify that a complete rail survey is a research goal, not a prerequisite imposed by the rules on every actual rail movement. This review follows the plan and earlier readings of the same scan; it is not blind or independent-edition corroboration.

| Question | Source check and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Rail endpoints | Land §8.71 names the active Alexandria–Mersa Matruh line; scenario §60.7 says the railroad runs to Mersa Matruh D3714 and ends there at setup. Scenario §60.41 permits its listed Alexandria deployment at E3613 and/or E3714; §60.5 lists airfields in both. Neither placement establishes which city hex has the relevant rail crossing. A full D/E seam and branch survey can reconstruct the named line, but §8.77 permits pickup and dropoff along it: a particular movement needs its selected path and state, not necessarily a complete survey of the entire named line. |
| Italian decisive | Scenario §60.81 calls for occupying both Mersa Matruh and Siwa with a combat unit that can be supplied by convoy **from Tobruk**. Scenario §60.5 identifies Matruh D3714, Siwa C0127 and Tobruk C4807 as airfield locations; §60.31 also places the Tobruk garrison at C4807. These are strong location evidence, not proof of occupancy or a functioning route at victory evaluation. The wording does not settle how to bind the unit and supply clause to both locations, whether a shipment or available vehicle is required, or the exact directed movement test. Examine both objectives; do not replace the Siwa condition with a Matruh witness. |
| Direction and scope | Italian strategic says supply by convoy **from any hex on map D**; Commonwealth tactical says truck-convoy supply **back to Alexandria or Cairo**, with the other Commonwealth conditions using supply as above. These are distinct wordings, not a publisher ruling that a prospective delivery's terrain restrictions run in the opposite direction. Source control, direction and convoy modality need explicit rulings. No text here mandates the coastal road, this seed or a performed convoy. |
| Negative and positive proofs | An accepted existential rule can be shown by one fully valid witness, with terrain, relevant state, applicable transport rules and ordered sides covered. A negative requires complete coverage of every eligible source, destination and continuation within a justified search domain; absence of a pinned side, a blocked road or an unexamined branch proves neither impassability nor failure. This remains conditional on the selected rules and privacy policy. |
| Local map observations | Inspected the existing Matten Baggush crop: blue cross-tied rail and a parallel road are visible, with grid, labels and artwork near junctions. This supports a focused side survey, not an independently computed route or verification of every pair in the queue. Existing linked reviews support qualified local readings; at C4807–C4707 converging marks and overprint leave exact feature/side intersections unresolved. Recheck before describing each feature as a certified crossing. |
| Rail legality | Land §§8.73, 8.76–.78 distinguish start and ZOC/occupancy restrictions, destroyed or unbuilt rail, pickup/dropoff and rail west of an Axis combat-occupied rail hex. The friendly-combat-unit ZOC interaction, current state, capacity and errata require separate treatment. §8.47 treats unconstructed roads as tracks; do not equate unfinished road ink automatically with an impassable side. |
