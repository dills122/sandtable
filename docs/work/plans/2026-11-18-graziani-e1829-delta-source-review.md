**Disposition:** The [source note](2026-11-18-graziani-e1829-delta-source.md) accurately reports a bounded observation. The inspected crop does not justify either a finding that E1829 contains no green anywhere or a definitive delta classification.

- In the reproduced Map E crop, the cell immediately upper-left of E1730 is the identified **E1829** candidate. Its own numeral is obscured by the Cairo city, figures, river and route markings; coordinate identification follows the neighboring grid and the [six-side review](2026-11-17-graziani-e1730-six-sides-review.md), not an independently readable `1829` numeral. The map's summary lists Cairo at `E1730/1829/1830/1930/1931`, supporting the city grouping without independently exposing that obscured label.
- The Map A key labels its green partial-fill sample **Delta**. In the E1829 candidate, exposed interior areas appear pale with blue city and river artwork and dark overprint. Green fill reaches the outside of the western boundary; I cannot distinguish a clear green patch in its unobscured interior in this crop. Artwork and print overlap obstruct portions of the cell, so that bounded observation is **not an absence proof**. Neighboring green does not establish E1829 terrain, and the key does not supply a whole-hex mixed-terrain threshold or city/delta precedence.
