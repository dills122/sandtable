## Decisions still needed

- **Trace subjects:** Decide whether each listed objective, its holding unit, one row-level force, or another subject must trace, and how “as above” applies to each later row. Per-objective routing is already a choice; city selection cannot settle this upstream quantifier.
- **City choice across traces:** If a ruling requires several traces, either each may select Alexandria or Cairo independently, or the row may require one common city for all. The singular alternative identifies two candidate cities but does not explicitly specify a shared selection or independent selections across several subjects. The [route candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-route-followup.md) proposes independent choices; it remains unadmitted. One trace to each city distinguishes the policies only if all other predicates and both endpoints are valid.
- **Hex choice:** Separately decide whether each trace may end at any certified eligible hex of its selected city, only at a designated hex such as the chart representative, or at another defined source. Even a common-city policy could allow different hexes within that city; a common-hex policy is a further restriction. The Italian parenthesis does not automatically transfer to Commonwealth wording.
- **Eligibility and control:** Define source membership, friendly control, endpoint occupancy and obstruction, and treatment of incomplete map/control evidence. Requiring friendly control is a candidate choice, not an express §60.81 condition. Neither a named city nor a chart distance establishes an available route.
- **Route and timing:** Select a Land-only convoy interpretation, direction, terrain and interference, complete proof boundaries, and the terminal snapshot. Section 32.16 local supply-unit access alone does not prove connection to a named city. Missing evidence stays unknown, subject to [RUL-GRA-07](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-07-bounded-facility-and-victory-planning-policies)'s known-false dominance within a row.
