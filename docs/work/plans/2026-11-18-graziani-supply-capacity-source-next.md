# Supply capacity: later-scenario placement comparison

**Finding:** A later Land-only scenario supplies a specific two-regular-unit placement limit at named locations, but it does not correct §32.14 or decide whether that case's three-unit clause applies to ordinary on-map hexes. The ambiguity and [RUL-GRA-08](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-08-supply-stacking-and-initial-overstack-planning-policy)'s status as a planning interpretation remain unchanged.

In [Desert Fox scenario §61.72(D)](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF pp. 6–7 / printed pp. 30–31, the Land-only Commonwealth setup specifies nine regular and four dummy Supply Units distributed among named locations, with no more than **two regular** Supply Units at any one of those locations. It separately places five regular units at Tobruk and two each at Cairo and Alexandria. The two-unit restriction is stated for the named distribution and regular units; it does not expressly supply a general three-unit cap, specify dummy inclusion in that restriction, or amend the separate Tobruk/Cairo/Alexandria allocations. This is a separate scenario's setup and does not change the five fixed Matruh units or optional placements in Graziani §60.92(C).

The [earlier source review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-stacking-source-scope-review.md) already checked original §32.14, §59.65–.66, Graziani setup, map key and September errata; it preserves the printed **off-map** clause and the difference between ordinary stacking points and a separate supply-counter count. The Desert Fox placement is compatible with several interpretations, including a scenario-specific tighter limit. It is not a worked post-setup stacking example, an explicit on-map cap, or proof that §59.65's unavoidable-excess exception applies to any particular initial placement. No new official correction found in bounded searches of §32.14 and supply-unit stacking; this is not an exhaustive census of later rulings.
