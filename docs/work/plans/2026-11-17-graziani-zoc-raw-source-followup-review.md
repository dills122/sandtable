# ZOC raw-defense source follow-up: independent review

**Disposition:** corroborate the [source follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-zoc-raw-source-followup.md)'s central distinction: §10.15 presumes **anti-tank units** Up Front, not expressly every point with an anti-armor rating; no inspected passage specifies a complete pre-combat ZOC calculation for gun position, ammunition or parenthesized ratings. Two wording qualifications below should be preserved before policy selection.

## Source checks and qualifications

1. **Raw as well as actual strengths are secret.** [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) PDF/printed p. 19 §11.36 describes both Raw and Actual strength totals and says these strengths are secret from opponents, revealed upon attack. The follow-up mentions Actual strengths, which is incomplete if read as excluding raw totals. Section 10.16 p. 18 separately requires the non-phasing player to disclose whether a particular potentially projecting unit exerts a ZOC when a phasing unit starts a Movement Segment in or moves into the relevant hex. This supports that narrow disclosure without an automatic right to its raw components, TOE, ammunition, positions or a generalized explanation of every rejected action. A concrete audience/timing and downstream-effects contract is still required; this observation does not prove one.
2. **Qualify pinned and retreat cases.** Land §15.12 p. 22 specifically says pinned units and units that retreated before assault **into a hex under Close Assault attack** cannot add their ratings to defensive Close Assault, while their TOE participates in the stated defending-loss calculation; it says that provision pertains only to non-phasing defending units. Avoid generalizing this to every pinned or retreated unit and every action. Sections 15.13–.15 separately address gun position, commitment and out-of-ammunition participation. These combat rules do not expressly settle whether their conditions are applied to a movement-time §10.15 raw threshold.

Land §3.4 p. 6 describes anti-tank units, then acknowledges non-anti-tank TOE points with armor-piercing capability. Section 10.15 pp. 17–18 says **anti-tank units** are presumed Up Front for that ZOC purpose. It does not expressly expand that presumption to all artillery or AA with an anti-armor rating, and mixed parent/unit classification needs its own decision. Land §3.23 p. 6 also says certain non-combat units may be treated as combat units for specified determinations including ZOC; do not apply an assumed classification shortcut to every counter.

Section 10.14 says units at cohesion −26 or worse do not exert ZOC. It does not expressly determine whether their raw ratings count toward a different co-located unit's §10.15 total; single-unit non-projection and mixed-stack aggregation are different questions. Section 10.15 bars **less than ten** raw defensive points regardless of §10.11, so an exact ten meets that numerical threshold but not necessarily size, eligibility, terrain or directional entry requirements.

Section 3.4 p. 6 describes parenthesized strengths as usable when attacked without friendly combat units with appropriate non-parenthesized ratings; September errata p. 1 §3.4 clarifies the attacked-alone or all-parenthesized case. Axis chart-key and engineer-specific errata interactions remain separate, as the follow-up states. None expressly determines whether a parenthesized nominal rating counts before any attack for §10.15. Preserve nominal-versus-available alternatives, actual-event timing, and unknown when necessary evidence is missing; do not infer either a permanent zero or an automatic usable value.

Future distinguishing tests should cover raw versus actual secrecy and precisely authorized §10.16 disclosure, anti-tank **type** versus a different unit with an anti-armor rating, mixed classification, −26 single/mixed cases, raw 9/10, Forward/Back and pre-combat timing, §15.12's specific non-phasing defense context, and parenthesized/engineer/ammo alternatives. Tests are not executed evidence or a historical reachability claim.
