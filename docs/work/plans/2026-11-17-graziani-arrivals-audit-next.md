# Graziani Commonwealth land arrivals: source audit

**Result:** the original Commonwealth land schedule lists **six rows** in
GT1–6, expanding to **21 OA rows** in the checked source. Their ordinary
schedule/OA arrival markers agree; the Land §20.11 example conflicts on the
6th NZ Brigade. French Motor Marine's corrected starting company is separate
from those six rows. These are printed schedule identities and dates, not proof
of 21 independent counters on the board, complete scenario obligations,
admitted initial state or actual movement eligibility. This audit rereads the
same edition and compares the existing independent packets; it is not a new
edition or a blind double entry.

is `4 × 18-pounder`; other stated `N` values require the applicable OA key and
characteristic chart. Several Polish rows share abbreviation `1 Pol` but have
different identities/types; do not deduplicate by abbreviation alone. OA
indentation, starting parents, counter representation and actual attachment
choices need their own crosswalk. The 2 RTR OA note says it is assigned to
4/7 Armored Brigade upon arrival; it does not itself prove co-location or an
automatic same-snapshot attachment. The 3rd Hussars track's `[7]` and OA's
later assignment note need hierarchy/time interpretation, not a silent parent

## Conflicts, duplicates and exclusions

- **6th NZ:** Land §20.11's example places both the brigade and 6th NZ Field
  Artillery in GT2 OpStage 3. The explicit schedule and OA instead put the
  **artillery** at GT2 OpStage 3 and the **brigade HQ and three battalions**
  at GT6 OpStage 3. [RUL-GRA-04](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-04-6th-nz-brigade-arrival-working-interpretation)
  selects the schedule/OA reading for planning; it is not a printed correction.
  Do not count the field regiment as a second brigade or deliver brigade members
  at both dates. Preserve the example alternative and use one versioned
  resolution for every constituent and its listed GT6 trucks.
- **French Motor Marine:** scenario §60.41 places the 1st Company at C3926;
  Commonwealth OA p. 21 prints that company as `1`, `v`, `N`, `3/65`, but
  September errata §4.44b item 3 changes its arrival to `D`. This supports a
  **single starting company** under the corrected reading, not a seventh
  GT1–6 track row or a duplicate arrival. Separate later similarly named OA
  rows have different dates/IDs; the inspected notes do not establish a
  substitution or lifecycle transition. See [marine lifecycle review](2026-11-17-graziani-marine-lifecycle-review.md).
- **Other GT1–6 track rows:** the checked schedule p. 3 prints no additional
  Commonwealth **land reinforcement schedule** entries in those turns.
  That is not proof that unscheduled scenario overrides, setup, supply,
  convoys, replacement production, aircraft/fleet or other stage obligations
  are empty. Scenario §60.47 says no Commonwealth Replacement Points arrive
  before November; GT1–6 are September III through October IV under the map
  track. This bounds that subsection, subject to other explicit exceptions and
  source hierarchy; it is not a ban on Axis replacements or real Supply Units.
- The September errata §4.43a correction checked on PDF p. 1 adds later GT28
  and GT76 schedule corrections; it does not explicitly change these six
  entries. Its §4.44b item 3 addresses the French company. No explicit §20.11
  correction was identified in the bounded errata text/page check. Later
  errata, unindexed corrections and other scenario exceptions remain possible.

## Deployment, movement and training gates

Land §§20.12–.14 put whole-unit reinforcements at the beginning of Naval Convoy
Arrival, do not charge CP for disembarkation or the initially placed hex, and
permit movement in the arrival OpStage subject to other rules; the default
Commonwealth arrival location is Cairo with printed exceptions. **Errata
§8.86 corrects the contrary restriction on reinforcements/replacements arriving
in Tunis or Tripoli**; it is not itself a blanket Commonwealth Cairo rule.
Do not infer an arrival-stage lock from that original restriction. Source-map
hex identity, capacity, actual phase, side control, placement and legal
movement still need state and a versioned command contract.

Scenario §60.47 warns some arrivals need training; Land §17.36 permits units
to fight at their untrained morale instead of training. OA p. 21 lists Polish
Brigade morale `+1(−1)` and p. 15 4th Indian Division `+1(0)`; p. 18 lists
2nd NZ Division `+1` without a parenthesized untrained value. These group
markings are evidence for different training cases, **not** proof of each
constituent's current morale after assignment/attachment changes or an
automatic mandatory training lock. Land §5.2 puts Organization/Training before
Naval Arrival; §20.43 expressly permits replacement-point training in its
arrival stage. [RUL-GRA-13](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-13-arrival-and-training-order-planning-interpretation)
chooses no same-stage reopening for whole-unit morale training and preserves
the specific replacement exception, pending admission. Thus the scheduled GT6
stage-3 brigade has no *later in-scenario* Organization step under that choice;
this does not prove its units need training or cannot fight/move on arrival.

## Distinguishing checks

- Exercise all six positions with stable source identities, grouping and
  expressly listed trucks. Verify the remaining 12 positions against this
  **track** while separately evaluating other stage duties; do not convert a
  missing track row into a whole-stage no-op.
- Test all four 6th NZ brigade OA members and trucks at selected GT6/3, the
  field regiment at GT2/3, and replay across both dates; compare the competing
  §20.11 example explicitly. Reject duplicate identities and duplicate
  deliveries without rejecting distinct same-abbreviation Polish units.
- Test corrected French company once at setup and no fabricated later
  arrival, while keeping later distinct OA identities separate. Compare
  arrival-stage movement with eligible/no-training, optional morale training,
  invalid location and unknown attachment state; verify no CP charged for
  initial placement and no unjustified retroactive training step.
- Validate absent versus zero truck data, setup/truck conversion versus
  later arrivals, owner choices, control/capacity, idempotent replay and
  source-authorized observations. These are proposed synthetic tests, not
  executed results or an assertion of six-turn playability.
