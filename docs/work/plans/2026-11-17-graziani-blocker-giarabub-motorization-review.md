# Giarabub motorization: independent same-scan review

**Verdict, 2026-11-17:** [Source recheck](2026-11-17-graziani-blocker-giarabub-motorization.md) accurately separates printed `8+`, historical motorization, and an unestablished initial assignment. No evidence in the inspected passages identifies a unit-specific initial motor pool or establishes its actual effective initial CPA. `unresolved-no-scenario-pool` is supportable only with the note's narrow qualification; it must not mean no general pool, no motorized unit type, or no printed CPA.

## Independent source checks

- [Scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, §60.31 places Giaribub Garrison at C1014 without a first-line truck quantity on that row. Section 59.42, PDF p. 2, assigns listed first-line trucks within their hex; §59.63(A) converts medium and heavy first-line trucks hex by hex into attached motorization points in abstract logistics. Neither establishes a unit-specific pool here. Other eligible co-located units and owner choices need separate checking.
- Section 60.33 separately lists 30 light, 100 medium and 25 heavy second/third-line trucks anywhere in Libya, subject to setup restrictions. Sections 59.44 and 59.63(B) distinguish their placement and unattached abstract points. Section 59.64 allows initial distributions to change after start subject to its exception and attachment rules. This is a general player-distributed resource, not proof of an initial allocation to Giarabub. It defeats any blanket claim of no available truck pool.
- [Axis OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsAxisPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 14, identifies Giarabub Oasis Mtr MG Bn as `Grbub`, ID `hh`, `N`, `D`; its chart key on p. 4 defines those abbreviations. Section 4.46b, pp. 26–27, prints `hh` CPA **8+**, maximum TOE 1 and close assault 5/7; `+` marks historical provision of sufficient trucks and a CPA change while motorized. It does not give a truck quantity or select its current state. This is affirmative mobility classification and printed rating, not an absent numeric fact.
- [Air counter sheet](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 44 / printed p. 40, section 2, shows the `Grbub` motor-MG symbol, `G`, `II`, and **0**. [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) §§4.21–4.23, pp. 7–8, identify symbol and counter fields; §9.16(a), p. 17, gives the home-garrison stacking exception. Zero is a stacking value, not a motor-pool or CPA value. This verifies classification, not a current truck assignment.
- Land §32.54, p. 44, prioritizes sufficient motorization points for all historical `8+`/`10+` infantry before other infantry types. It does not by itself identify which pool is attached at setup. Sections 8.92–8.93, p. 16, distinguish historically motorized infantry from actual transport: when carried, a non-motorized unit takes the transporting truck's appropriate CPA. Sections 8.96–8.97 describe truck attachment and organization-phase timing, subject to their stated conditions. Thus `8+` remains the printed chart entry; a changed effective allowance needs actual sufficient transport and applicable truck characteristics. Do not infer a specific changed value from `+` or assume the separate pool is already attached.

## Critique and limits

No material factual error found in the source recheck's cited conclusions. For implementation planning, make the profile and timing explicit: §32.54 is an abstract-logistics priority, while §8.97 concerns attachment; neither alone proves how setup priority and first-line versus second/third-line allocations reconcile. The note correctly leaves that decision open. Its phrase “exact current allowance” should remain distinct from known printed `8+`; neither claim establishes an effective initial value. A bounded source check cannot certify all possible later transfers, eligible co-location, errata or editions.
