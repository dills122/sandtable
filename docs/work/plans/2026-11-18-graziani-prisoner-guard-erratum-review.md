# Independent review: prisoner guard erratum

**Result:** No material source error found. Land §28.17's first sentence requires one Guard Point per detention camp. Its moving-prisoner sentence prints one guard per **1** Prisoner Point; September errata replaces the line-four **1** with **5**, yielding one guard per five prisoners in that sentence. The correction does not change the separate camp requirement. The inspected wording supplies no explicit nonmultiple rounding method; the [note](2026-11-18-graziani-prisoner-guard-erratum.md) appropriately leaves it open.

Section 28.11 ties Prisoner Points to surrendering infantry TOE, and §28.22 forms guards by removing infantry-type TOE rather than creating free strength. Scenario §60.22 sets GT1/1 through GT6/3; §§60.31 and 60.41 list infantry formations. Those entries establish candidate guard sources, not an actual surrender, guard, camp or movement. The note limits its absence claim to inspected starting entries and does not infer six-turn reachability.

Section 28.15 specifies Store expenditure for prisoners. Section 32.0 applies when playing Land without Air and Logistics, and §32.11 replaces ordinary supply expenditure, including Stores and Water. The note correctly declines to debit §28.15 merely from the detailed-game text, while leaving any distinct availability or escapee-survival interaction for interpretation. Neither that supply substitution nor the ratio erratum supplies an automatic escape, death or rounding policy.
