# Abstract-air origin: enemy combat-unit classification

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Source follow-up to the [Matruh air-supply candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness.md), its [review](2026-11-17-graziani-air-supply-witness-review.md), and the [Land-only anti-air inventory](2026-11-17-graziani-abstract-aa-fleet-next.md). The candidate leaves enemy proximity open; this note narrows what must be classified for that one gate. It does not establish an enemy position, distance, complete map, or legal shipment.

Land §32.37 bars transporting a Supply Unit **out of an origin hex** if any enemy combat unit is within five hexes of that origin. It is a presence test, not a demand that the enemy be attacking or exerting a Zone of Control; it does not apply the separate §16.32 patrol-survival exception. General §3.23 includes anti-aircraft-type units among combat units, while §16.32 explicitly excludes AA/Flak and engineers only **for its own no-combat survival check**. Thus an AA unit's patrol treatment is not by itself evidence that a surviving enemy AA unit should be ignored at an air-supply origin.

The selected mode and actual state still matter. Land-only §32.81 removes Commonwealth anti-air units and specified points; §32.82–.84 give distinct German and Italian removals and exceptions. In particular, §32.84 says these restrictions do not affect non-AA points merely because they have an AA rating. Establish the actual type, nationality, presence and applicable removal before counting a counter: no removed unit may be used as a hypothetical witness, and an AA rating alone is not proof that the whole unit is an AA unit. The [anti-air inventory](2026-11-17-graziani-abstract-aa-fleet-next.md) retains unresolved setup and arrival lifecycle details; this note does not settle them.

Headquarters and dummies require separate, context-sensitive handling. §3.1 says HQs, engineers and Dummy Tank Formations are combat units in certain situations; §3.31 treats an HQ counter as combat when combat units are attached, while §3.33 describes an HQ without attached combat units as no longer combat. §32.37 does not explicitly resolve every dummy or engineering case, and the [dummy patrol analysis](2026-11-18-graziani-patrol-dummy-followup.md) cannot be imported as an air-supply ruling. A Supply Unit itself has no combat capabilities under §32.13, but whether a particular exceptional classification applies requires its own evidence. Preserve actual formation contents and location; a name, assignment elsewhere, or visible counter label alone may not settle the origin test.

Conditional boundary checks, assuming a validated hex-distance interpretation and all other transport requirements independently satisfied:

1. An actually present, surviving enemy unit established as combat at distance **five** blocks departure; distance **six** does not block under this particular five-hex condition. Do not derive cross-sheet distance from unverified seam registration.
2. A scenario-listed AA unit removed before the relevant snapshot is not present merely because the setup list names it. A surviving non-AA artillery unit with an AA rating needs its own combat classification; the rating is not an instruction to remove it under §32.84.
3. Compare a combat-bearing HQ with an HQ shown to have no attached combat units, and a dummy-only candidate whose classification remains unresolved. The latter cannot be silently counted or excluded from this gate without a ruling.

These are synthetic source-boundary cases, not actual Matruh placements or executed tests. A legal-action or error response also needs an independently designed information boundary; the opponent's hidden unit identity must not be exposed merely to explain a failed proximity check.
