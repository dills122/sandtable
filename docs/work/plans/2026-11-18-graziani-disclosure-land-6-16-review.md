# Review: Land §§6–16 disclosure pass

**Two material qualifications:**

1. [Land §12.0 procedure](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 20, explicitly says that **when resolving a barrage**, the barraging player states both its target (type and temporary number, or specific unit identification if known) **and the number of Actual Barrage Points directed at that target**. The [candidate](2026-11-18-graziani-disclosure-land-6-16.md) describes the earlier secret choice and §12.23–.24 target count/class response, but omits this distinct later declaration. Add the statement with its resolution trigger; it does not expose all gun-unit identities, weapon types, withheld choices or entire formation strength.
2. Section 16.33 says a patroller with no surviving points “goes no further” and gets no information, while §16.34 separately requires an Objective Loss Table roll **after getting information or after all patrol points are eliminated**, regardless of own losses; if entirely eliminated, its Captured result becomes Eliminated. The candidate's no-report conclusion is supported, but “goes no further” must not imply no later loss roll. Record the two printed steps and their wording tension, preserving the separate §16.32 no-combat-unit notice and not inventing an objective result.

**Other source checks:** Visually inspected Land pp. 18–21 and 25–27. Section 11.0 specifies each player's secret gun positions, target notes and later anti-armor/close-assault assignment; withholding specifically concerns unpinned armor or gun-class units. Section 11.36 makes recorded parent-organization raw and actual point totals secret from opponents until attack; its interaction with the §3.62 combat exceptions needs the candidate's qualification. Section 12.23 limits the opponent's target disclosure to count and Gun/Armor/Infantry class in each hex adjacent to a firing hex, without mandatory strength, exact type or designation; §12.24 provides blind targeting and temporary numbers except for specifically known units. Section 14.23 describes the firing player's uncertainty and conditional reassignment, not a general armor-information query. Section 15.94 permits delaying a probe announcement until after assault; [§5.2](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) p. 12 separately says Player A announces probes after each resolution.

Section 16.0 describes announcing patrol point count and target hex; §§16.44–.46 permit the stated deception and prescribe distinct dummy elimination triggers, with §16.43's Italian restriction separate. The existing [limited-intelligence review](2026-11-18-graziani-limited-intelligence-source-inventory-review.md) already records §10.16 and §§16.32/.51–.55; these are not newly discovered by the candidate. None of these passages alone proves a concrete legal scenario action, remote viewer or complete digital projection.
