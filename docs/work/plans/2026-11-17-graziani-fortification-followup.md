# Graziani initial fortifications and minefields: source follow-up

**Disposition:** Preserve RUL-GRA-07's planning interpretation of inherent city levels, explicitly conditional on admission. Section 60.7 does not explicitly exempt them. Its no-minefields statement also carries a railroad parenthetical whose scope remains unresolved; treating it as a categorical zero without resolving that exception would overstate the printed source. Minefields and city fortifications are distinct representations and require separate decisions.

## Source evidence

Visually inspected [scenario booklet](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 4 / printed p. 28 §60.7, and [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), PDF/printed pp. 36 and 38, §§24.3–.4 and 25.0–26.26. Searched the text of [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) for relevant section numbers, minefields, fortifications and railroad. Local SHA-256: scenario `fa12488e59552d7b0ff0d5abefa891d4b81af0492256bec2d25f8d61ef040a66`; Land `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`; errata `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`. Same scans used in prior research, not another edition or exhaustive search for later rulings. Errata §25.15 changes its repair reference to §22.34; this bounded search found no explicit correction of §§26 or 60.7.

- Under **Construction on the Map at the Start of the Game**, §60.7 states no minefields, fortifications or pipeline, followed by “other than those provided by the RR”; the next sentence says the railroad runs to Mersa Matruh and ends there. Neither the parenthetical's grammatical reach nor what the railroad provides is identified by that sentence alone. Do not infer railroad minefields or immunity from suppression without evidence.
- Land §26.11 defines real and dummy minefields as counters with the same front and different reverse. Section 24.31 permits engineering units or Commonwealth HQ engineers to construct them; §24.32 takes one full Operations Stage, completing at the next Construction Segment. Section 24.35 restricts terrain, allows one real or dummy field per hex, and prohibits construction in major cities; §24.36 prohibits construction in enemy-controlled hexes. These are future construction rules, not evidence of an initial field or of an inherent city minefield. Resource and Land-only abstraction rules require separate review.
- A present minefield has its own effects: §26.15 reveals its type upon enemy entry; §§26.21–.25 give movement and vehicle consequences, and §26.26 a conditional combat column adjustment. Real-field removal under §26.13 requires qualifying engineering presence for a full Operations Stage, no movement CP and remaining in the hex. These effects do not apply merely because a city is fortified. Sections 24.38, 26.14 and 26.23 describe dummy removal with different timing language; reconcile them before implementing a dummy lifecycle.
- Land §§25.0 and 25.12 treat major cities as fortifications, normally level 2 and Cairo/Alexandria level 3; villages have no such inherent level. Section 24.41 distinguishes city built-in levels from counters for constructed fortifications. The construction heading and distinction support retaining inherent levels while excluding separate constructed starts, but §60.7 does not expressly say that. The literal all-fortifications-absent reading remains plausible. Neither interpretation establishes actual map classifications or a specific legal attack.
- Sections 24.44–.45 prohibit new construction in major cities but permit rebuilding levels destroyed by bombardment or barrage; §24.47 limits removal; §§25.14–.16 permit reduction to zero. A scenario-suppressed initial level is not expressly identified as destroyed or automatically restored. Specify restoration eligibility separately if selecting initial suppression.

## Decision boundary

[RUL-GRA-07](2026-11-12-graziani-ruling-decisions.md#rul-gra-07-bounded-facility-and-victory-planning-policies) selects `graziani-city-fortifications-v1` for planning: built-in city levels retained, separately constructed fortifications absent. The [source analysis](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-city-fortifications.md) and [same-scan independent review](2026-11-17-graziani-rules-city-fortifications-review.md) document the contrary reading and lifecycle uncertainty. This follow-up does not establish an official correction or new admission. Apply railroad qualification to constructed-fortification absence as well until its scope and provision are established.

For minefields, record initial real and dummy absence as a **candidate subject to railroad exception resolution**, not proof that every hex is empty or that future construction is prohibited. Resolve §60.7's parenthetical against railroad and setup sources, including any specific exception or later ruling; document applicable mode and source hierarchy. If evidence remains insufficient, choose and version an explicit project interpretation, retaining the alternative as unresolved. No railroad-provided minefield has been demonstrated in this check.

Future synthetic checks should distinguish real, dummy and absent fields; initial versus legally constructed fields; certified major city, village and non-city hex; inherent versus constructed fortification; both setup policies; actual damage versus scenario suppression; and railroad exception evidence. Check mode-specific costs, reveal/removal timing, legal terrain and deterministic replay under the selected ruleset. No runtime, map certification, scenario outcome or gameplay test changed here.
