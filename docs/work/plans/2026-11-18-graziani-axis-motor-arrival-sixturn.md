# LA-02: Axis six-turn motorization arrival bounds

**Result:** The original Axis truck-production chart's ordinary first-planning dates imply **GT8** as the earliest ordinary newly planned medium-truck replacement arrival and **GT7** for heavy, after Graziani ends GT6/3. Light trucks have a conditional GT6 ordinary arrival, but Land-only §60.92(A) names medium-truck ability, not light or heavy, for incoming Motorization Points. These bounds do not establish a zero Axis motorization arrival: scenario preplanning, other sources, timing and the abstract conversion remain separate questions.

| Other state changes | Starting §59.63(A) conversion counts both medium and heavy first-line trucks, separately from incoming ability. §32.56 gives capture of validly assigned motorization when its unit is captured. | Initial inventory and battlefield capture are not new medium replacement-pool arrivals; do not count either as a fresh convoy delivery. Check any specifically identified scenario reinforcement, repaired/returned or other introduction independently. |

A conditional comparison is available for review: an ordinary medium plan initiated at its first listed GT6 slot targets GT8, outside the horizon; an ordinary light plan at GT4 could target GT6, but is not itself medium-based Land-only motorization. The exact plan, lane, successful arrival and accepted mode crosswalk remain absent. This does not adjudicate whether §60.37's pre-start instructions can authorize any earlier production class; a source-backed hierarchy or directly applicable correction is needed if a concrete earlier plan is proposed. Whether the Commonwealth §60.47 pre-November Replacement Point restriction covers its own abstract motorization is a separate side-specific question and is not a general Axis ban.
