# RUL-GRA-26: bounded ruling audit

**Disposition:** RUL-GRA-26 faithfully selects the reviewed retained-witness
interpretation for planning. No blocking discrepancy found against the
[policy](2026-11-17-graziani-destroyed-rail-policy.md) and its
[independent critique](2026-11-17-graziani-destroyed-rail-policy-review.md).
It is not an executable contract, official ruling, evidence of historical
reachability, or admission of a rail action. Preserve the following details
when drafting that contract; the linked policy and critique carry them even
where the compact ruling does not repeat them.

1. **Three-valued witness evaluation.** For each candidate, all required
   conditions must be true to prove a witness; any false condition excludes
   it, even if another is unknown. A true candidate blocks despite other
   unknown candidates. With no true candidate, absence requires complete
   relevant coverage and false results for all candidates; otherwise the
   result is unknown. Under retained-witness, unknown damage alone does not
   affect an otherwise proven constructed witness. Unknown construction is
   not proof of either unbuilt or built. These results concern §8.78 only;
   unknown does not authorize an action or prove a block.
2. **Alternative requires a definition.** The ruling points to the
   operational-track alternative without adopting it. Before comparison,
   define whether operational means physically undestroyed track or full
   action legality. The latter can differ because enemy occupancy independently
   bars Allied rail entry. Fix all other witness conditions and compare
   intact, destroyed, unknown condition and repaired states on identical
   snapshots; under a physical-condition alternative unknown damage can
   matter. Retain both interpretations and version any later change.
3. **Historical ink is not all track at a hex.** Exclusion of the historical
   Soluch–Benghazi–Barce line as a witness applies §§8.71 and 24.6 to §8.78;
   it is not an express §8.78 exception. Independently established overlapping
   qualifying track remains a separate candidate, including when destroyed.
   Unknown overlap stays unknown absent another proven witness or a false
   required condition. September errata refers to Axis rail use under §54.4;
   its interaction with the historical-line exclusion remains open. Do not
   infer a general ban on Axis rail use.
4. **Source facts and provenance.** The reviewed Land text distinguishes
   unconstructed track, ignored until built, from previously built destroyed
   rail that cannot be entered by rail until repaired. The continuing identity
   inference is supported but §8.78 does not expressly decide the nonlocal
   destroyed-witness question. Initial established construction need not be
   backed by an invented build event; printed ink, a named railhead or a
   marker alone does not prove construction history or a legal destruction.
   Section 24.65's enemy-control and occupancy restrictions apply independently
   to building or rebuilding. A scenario override, further errata or better
   evidence may change the interpretation.
5. **Disclosure and replay are admission gates.** The ruling calls for a
   side-safe boundary and versioned replay, but implementation must specify
   the complete side-visible trace: action enumeration and submission,
   acceptance, resulting state changes, events, later observations, ordering,
   timing and replay. Compare equal authorized histories with different
   hidden witness states. A uniform pending message or delay alone cannot
   mask differing eventual outcomes. Until an authorized disclosure or
   observation-independent protocol is established, hold the affected
   public action boundary unavailable. Record policy identity, ruleset and
   configuration version, authoritative snapshot and event order so replay
   does not silently reinterpret prior decisions.

**Evidence and limits:** Compared RUL-GRA-26 with the linked policy, its
independent critique and [source review](2026-11-17-graziani-destroyed-rail-identity-review.md).
This audit relies on those source readings; it did not independently inspect
the scans, §54.4, all scenario rules, later errata or maps. Same-scan reviews
are not independent editions. Proposed cases are contract obligations, not
executed tests. Documentation only; no runtime or data changes. Audit date:
November 17, 2026.
