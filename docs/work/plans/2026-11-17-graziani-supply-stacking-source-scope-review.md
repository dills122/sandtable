## Source readings

Visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) PDF/printed pp. 9, 16 and 43, [scenario](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF pp. 2, 4–5 / printed pp. 26, 28–29, [common charts](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf) PDF/printed p. 2, original [map](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Maps.pdf) PDF pp. 1 and 4, and [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) pp. 1–4. SHA-256 reverified: Land `b362870368b9fb8abe6918195fdcf878d4a8041c557b835b9a1bdde3c8b76e99`; scenario `fa12488e59552d7b0ff0d5abefa891d4b81af0492256bec2d25f8d61ef040a66`; common `51aa5a5bfdaca3d23794da71a45a830d98b798d060097cde6418126d6bd63bb0`; map `8830ec78df489607a2cc7e2cbc8ddcc9457ecbad3b11df071a60d825dadfd030`; errata `5db539e1fac4aea3ef152370fb732f1938307fe4b2d47ad93eb605db0a09f0fb`.

Land §32.14 p. 43 gives a Supply Unit stacking value of zero, then says five may be placed in a Major City hex and three in any other type of hex **off-map**; it separately permits unlimited Supply Units in the boxes of the Tripoli/Tunisia region. The `off-map` word is visibly printed. The passage does not expressly extend three to ordinary on-map non-major-city hexes or establish dummy inclusion; a zero stacking-point value does not itself decide the separate unit-count cap.

Land §8.81 describes large regional boxes and smaller In Transit boxes; §8.87 says there are no stacking restrictions in the Tripoli/Tunisia boxes and the In Transit boxes. That is affirmative general stacking text. Whether §32.14's separate Supply-Unit count is covered by the general stacking exemption, and exactly which boxes belong to the §32.14 regional-box exception, remain interpretive questions. Scenario §59.66(C) explicitly treats Tripoli/Tunis boxes as hexes for purposes of **that Case**; it is not a general declaration that all off-map facilities or boxes are hexes for §32.14. Land §4.26 describes holding boxes as space for counters located in a represented on-map hex; do not treat them as independent region boxes.

Scenario §59.65 allows initial supply placement to exceed stacking limits **only if unavoidable due to scenario distribution instructions**, prohibits additional supply units in the noncompliant hex and requires normal restrictions once compliance is reached. Section 60.92(C) lists five regular Supply Units at Mersa Matruh D3714. Map D shows a blue settlement dot at that coordinate; Map A's key names the dot Village/Bir and separately shows a Major City block cluster. This corroborates the prior [Matruh map classification](2026-11-17-graziani-map-matruh-classification.md), not an inferred major city from the quantity five. Under the separately proposed three-on-map reading, the fixed five require evaluation under §59.65's unavoidable-distribution exception; its scope does not automatically admit additional owner-selected loads. Under the literal `off-map` reading, an ordinary on-map cap is unspecified, not established as three or unlimited.

Scenario §60.5 lists off-map facilities such as Abu Seier `E3433`. This shows off-map wording is used outside the named Tripoli/Tunisia box listing, but does not prove that such a facility is a supply-eligible hex or what cap applies. Common §9.4 concerns stacking points, not the distinct §32.14 count. Errata §32.0 warns abstract rules were untested; no direct §32.14 correction was found in the four-page package. Other and later authority may exist.
