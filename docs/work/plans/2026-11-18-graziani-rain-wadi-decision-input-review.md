# Rain-wadi decision input: source review

**Disposition:** The [decision input](2026-11-18-graziani-rain-wadi-decision-input.md) presents supportable, unselected alternatives and conditional arithmetic. The original cases do not expressly rank the specific rain-wadi breakdown statement against general rain treatment. None of the three numbers establishes a legal crossing or actual breakdown.

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 14–15 §8.41, give a road crossing of a rain-swollen wadi an additional 2 CP and no additional breakdown, still describing the rate as ½ BP. Section 29.56 p. 41 treats road hex movement cost and breakdown rate as track in rain; §21.37(c) p. 34 treats all road breakdown point values as track values in rain. Those wordings make the specific ½ rate and the broader track rate competing evidence, not an explicit priority rule. Section 29.55 additionally limits rainstorm wadi crossings to roads; chart note 10 includes railroad treated as road for that crossing purpose. Their relationship and the scope of the latter equivalence remain explicit interpretations.
- [Terrain Effects Chart](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf), PDF p. 23 §8.37, prints 4 BP for clear terrain, ½ BP for road and +8 BP for a wadi. Road note 6 negates hexside entry costs and BP values; track note 8 halves hex and hexside BP values, with the down-escarpment exception. Thus the note's 2 BP track-clear contribution and 4 BP track-wadi contribution are arithmetically correct under its assumptions. `2 + 0 = 2` and `2 + 4 = 6` are conditional compositions, not source-declared totals: whether road note 6 survives rain's track treatment and whether hex and side values are added must be resolved. The ½ BP candidate follows the specific §8.41 rate, subject to the same unresolved hierarchy.
- Land §32.55 p. 44 exempts Motorization Points themselves from breakdown and immediately says all other breakdown rules apply. Do not turn that into a blanket exemption for other vehicles or attached units. The fixture explicitly assumes a breakdown-eligible vehicle and makes no claim about an actual breakdown check, loss, weather, route, or rounded result. The additional 2 CP is not a complete crossing cost.
- Deferral without committing movement or BP is a proposed implementation behavior, not a rulebook outcome. The candidate set is bounded, not proof all interpretations are exhausted. Railroad equivalence for the crossing does not establish rail-movement eligibility; separately establish road/rail connections and other conditions for any concrete witness.
