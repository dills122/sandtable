# Graziani: C0716 setup radius

**Finding:** [Scenario §60.31](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 3 / printed p. 27, places **Saharan Det (I)** within three hexes of C0716 and lists **five light trucks** on that row. This is a player-selected area, not a fixed placement at C0716. The separate §60.34 detailed-supply table places a dump at C0716; that does not fix the detachment there. Section 60.92(B), PDF p. 5 / printed p. 29, separately supplies an abstract-supply entry at C0716. Do not merge these different rules or turn the five light trucks into medium trucks.

## Limits and unresolved choices

- §60.31 names the detachment as a formation; it does not by itself specify every constituent, its TOE, whether constituents can be distributed independently across eligible cells, or any chosen placement. Consult Axis OA and general setup/organization rules before expanding it; same-hex organization under §59.2 is not proof of unrestricted relocation. The row does not say “anywhere in Libya”; do not silently add that restriction solely from the adjacent, separate row.
- This table is geometric only. Map C depicts varied terrain, including the colored desert area and oasis symbols near the radius. It is not an adjudicated list of enterable or legal deployment hexes. Terrain definitions, restrictions, stacking, occupancy, national boundaries, attachment and any initial setup exceptions require their own source checks. No candidate is admitted to runtime by this note.
- The [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF p. 3, §60.31 addresses duplicate XXI Corps artillery in the separate Libya row; it does not mention this radius. This check does not exclude other editions or corrections.
- Review used the scenario scan and map scan already used in repository research; it is not an independent edition or blind transcription. The C0716 origin, boundary interpretation, each candidate's terrain and actual placement remain distinct claims. No scenario-ready map, movement route, supply line, zone of control or combat outcome follows from the disk.
