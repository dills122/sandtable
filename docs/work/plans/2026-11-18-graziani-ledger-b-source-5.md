# VI-07: dummy-tank source scope for victory qualification

**Result:** Land §16.43 expressly prohibits **Italian formations** from using dummy tanks, including when mixed with German formations. This narrows an Italian-formation candidate but does **not** establish that all Axis dummy use is prohibited, that a dummy is universally a noncombat unit for victory, or that any six-turn dummy is present. The actual objective predicate and formation state remain decisive.

## Source comparison

I visually inspected [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) printed/PDF pp. 5–6 and 26–27, §§3.1, 3.23, 16.0 and 16.41–.46; and the [scenario book](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) PDF pp. 3–4 / printed pp. 27–28, §§60.31 and 60.81. The existing [victory classification review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-combat-units-review.md) already checks the general glossary and warns against assuming dummies qualify. This check adds the **specific §16.4 use and elimination language** to that victory question; it does not resolve the general definition by assertion.

| Source | Bounded fact | Consequence and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Land §3.1, p. 5 | Glossary describes a combat unit by ability to engage and says HQs, engineers and dummy tank formations count as combat units **in certain situations**. | This is an explicit qualification, not a rule that every dummy qualifies at every objective. |
| Land §§16.41–.45, p. 27 | A dummy is tank-battalion-equivalent in size, has zero CPA and is worth nothing in combat; its stated function is confusing enemy intelligence. §16.43 bars Italian formations from using dummies, even mixed with German formations. §16.45 says dummies are worthless alone in a hex. | A claimed Italian-formation use must confront that explicit ban. Do not extend the wording to all German formations, every Axis-controlled situation, supply dummies or a general §60.81 disqualification without further evidence or a ruling. |
| Land §16.46, p. 27 | A dummy tank battalion is eliminated if its formation undergoes any close assault; the case also describes a separate anti-armor condition. | A hypothetical lone or mixed dummy cannot be treated as a persistent ordinary tank through those specified events. This does not establish that an assault occurred, or decide a pre-assault victory snapshot. |
| Scenario §60.81, p. 28 | The Italian strategic and decisive rows explicitly refer to combat units; other rows use their own take/hold or retain and supply wording. | Do not impose a combat-unit condition on every row solely because the strategic and decisive rows state one. Interpret the specified predicate and actual unit status before awarding any result. |
| Scenario §60.31, p. 27 | Initial Axis land deployment is listed as Italian forces and includes named Italian formations; the [ordinary Axis track review](2026-11-18-graziani-benghazi-diversion-source-next-review.md) finds its first entry after GT6. | This is not an exhaustive proof of every possible German introduction, scenario exception, dummy formation or later state. It does not transform §16.43's Italian-formation language into a universal Axis ban. |

A discriminating case would identify an actual formed dummy, its controlling/represented formation, location and applicable terminal predicate, then separately check the §16.43 use restriction, eligibility and any intervening elimination. The stores cost in §16.42 belongs to a distinct creation procedure; its Land-only interaction with §32.11 must be resolved before claiming a legal creation or cost. No specific dummy, legal creation, objective occupation, assault or victory has been established. Supply-unit dummy markers are a different case and must not inherit these tank rules by name.
