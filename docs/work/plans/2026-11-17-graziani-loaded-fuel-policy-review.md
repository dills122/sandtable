# Loaded fuel policy: independent review

**Disposition: Useful decision framework; revise before gameplay admission.** The alternatives are policy choices, not established source rulings. Partial payment, mixed stock, competing claims and air prepayment remain open. Neither precedence choice is selected here.

## Findings

1. **State the transfer prohibition as source text, not merely absent permission.** Scenario §59.66(B) expressly says these initial supplies may not be transferred to a Supply Unit. The policy's statement that neither option establishes permission is safe operationally but understates the rule. Preserve this prohibition under A; any B treatment that permits transfer would be a further explicit departure, not a consequence of §32.11. The scenario also restricts detachment of a loaded point to attachment to a subsidiary combat unit of its parent; its duration note addresses that detachment restriction, as the plan correctly says.
2. **Make visibility and replay admission gates concrete.** Record authoritative owner selection, point-to-unit attachment and initial resource identity/amount, policy and ruleset version, selected sources, ordered debits, timing and outcome, including failed or deferred attempts and any later continuation. Verify replay from setup and snapshots produces identical balances, attachments and action outcome without double spending; retries must be idempotent. Restrict exact opposing cargo, source identities, stock, failed-draw reasons and event details to authorized views; sanitize public events, legal-action responses and intelligence requests so their content or denial distinctions do not expose hidden state. Establish disclosure after observation or capture separately. These are project requirements, not printed rule claims.
3. **Bound the 5-Fuel illustration.** Section 32.23 describes individual fuel-requiring units and one or two points for first movement, with further cost at the instant CPA is exceeded; §32.24 specifies categories. Label 5 as an assumed aggregate of separately eligible charges, with participating units, payment timing and allocation deliberately unspecified. Do not imply an individual unit has a source-established 5-point charge, that simultaneous aggregation is permitted, or that a failed additional payment automatically reverses earlier movement. Option 2 needs an explicit deadlock/other-expenditure disposition; option 3 needs a durable pending state and an interruption/retry rule, as well as the already noted rollback and continuation choices.
4. **Tighten prepayment tests.** The source's singular “any supply unit” supports a single-payer reading; direct 7 + 5 is a proposed extension, not equally explicit authority. A same-hex Organization rearrangement remains distinct: validate capacity, ownership/eligibility, surviving payer, timing and ordering before committing payment and transport. On self-payment, check both zero and positive Ammo: 0 Ammo/12 Fuel removes the counter after payment, but positive Ammo/12 Fuel leaves it in play. Test failure and replay of each step, once-per-player-per-game-turn limit and no partial debit. No conclusion on shipment with a removed counter follows solely from arithmetic.

## Source and policy boundary

Scenario §59.62 applies when Logistics is abstracted whether or not Air is also abstracted. Section 59.66(B) permits each qualifying attached point initially carrying neither men nor guns to start with 1 Ammo or 3 Fuel; the unit must consume those supplies first for any supply expenditure and may not draw on a Supply Unit in that context. The text does not specify matching-resource versus all-cargo duration, partial shortages or allocation. Section 32.0 applies specifically to Land without Air and Logistics; §32.11 ignores outside supply usage/expenditure references, while §§32.15–.16 and .23 prescribe Supply Unit stock, access and movement payment. Their interaction with the scenario provision is an actual precedence choice. A's preservation and B's displacement each need an explicit versioned ruling; neither follows conclusively from the passages alone.

Mixed-cargo tests should distinguish one eligible unit with multiple attached points carrying different resources from an individual point, which starts with Ammo **or** Fuel. Compare matching-only and all-cargo restrictions for both fuel and Ammo expense; establish which unit owns and may use each attached point's stock before allocation. Check zero, partial, exact and surplus matching stock, no eligible source, inaccessible or insufficient source, contention, conservation and no negative balances. These are proposed synthetic tests, not source-proven results.
