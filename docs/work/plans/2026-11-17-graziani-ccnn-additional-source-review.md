# 1st CCNN additional sources: independent review

**Disposition:** The bounded no-correction conclusion is supported by the checked public leads, with two attribution qualifications. Neither checked lead establishes 1st CCNN HQ's initial gun or strength.

- [Grognard listing](https://grognard.com/m-article-page-list.aspx?GameID=c045) calls the September 1979 addenda available **by courtesy of** Decision Games; this supports courtesy, not a claim that Decision Games authored the historical addenda. The linked [four-page scan](https://grognard.com/scans/CNA%20Addenda%20Sep%2079.pdf) has the same stated date and page count as the [SPI-hosted errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf); this check did not hash-compare or visually compare every page, so do not assert byte identity. The listing also links an [HTML errata transcription](https://grognard.com/errata1/cna.html), which likewise identifies September 1979. Its §4.44b entries and SPI-hosted PDF's corresponding entries do not identify the 1st CCNN division HQ gun.
- [Limey Yank showroom](https://limeyyankgames.co.uk/die-cut-counter-showroom) lists CNA counters under John Edwards and describes a separate errata sheet as correcting known counter errata. “SPI” in the entry identifies the game publisher; it does not establish that this modern sheet is an SPI- or designer-issued rule ruling. The listing text identifies no 1st CCNN division HQ weapon assignment. Image content was not inspected and no sheet purchased; absence from listing text is not proof about the sheet.
- Repeated targeted web queries for title plus `1 CCNN`, `65/17`, `4.44`, headquarters and errata surfaced existing scans and other leads, with no identified dated publisher/designer ruling resolving the specific issue. Search results and inaccessible or unindexed material cannot establish nonexistence. A [designer work-in-progress compilation](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv9/WIP_CNA_Done.pdf) mentions 65/17 among many rated artillery pieces, not this HQ's initial assignment; it is not a correction. The reviewed note's constrained conclusion remains appropriate.
