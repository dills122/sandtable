# Vehicle-marker contents and fog of war

**Finding:** Land addendum §3.6 distinguishes inspectable map counters from the secret contents of broken-down, destroyed and abandoned vehicle markers. The explicit secrecy rule does not by itself specify what a captor learns, which prior combat disclosures remain known, or how to project internal marker identities. Capture and repair require authoritative lot details without automatically making every lot field public.

## Source and audience matrix

| Situation | Printed source | Bounded audience consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marker on map | Addendum §3.6, p. 45: a player may examine any stack of counters on the game map; what is in counters is another matter. The vehicle entry says contents of broken-down, destroyed and abandoned vehicle markers are **secret**. | Marker/counter presence can be represented where the source-authorized map stack is visible; underlying vehicle models, counts and cargo are not disclosed merely by observing or examining that counter. The text does not define a universal digital visibility model for off-map or unrevealed states. |
| Owner records a breakdown | §21.42 places a numbered Broken Down Vehicle marker and records necessary details on the owner's Broken Down Vehicles Sheet. | Owner's authoritative log needs sufficient actual composition and location. Number on the physical marker aids bookkeeping; a digital stable ID must not become a channel to hidden lot details or unpermitted linkages. |
| Combat reveals some facts | Addendum §3.6 Anti-armor Fire allows the types of tanks and self-propelled guns and presence of armored recce/halftracks actively participating, but not their numbers, origins or which TOE points absorb damage. Its Close Assault disclosure is separately scoped. | A previously authorized combat fact is not erased merely because a marker is created, but it is not permission to publish the new marker's model-by-model losses, per-unit count or origin. Keep observation history separate from current private marker truth. |
| Breakdown capture | §§21.52–.55 allow a battalion-size-or-larger friendly combat unit moving adjacent to capture a qualifying broken-down marker, subject to protection by friendly units/HQs/engineers or a friendly-controlled hex and passable adjacency. Captured vehicles may be moved up to three hexes immediately under the stated path restrictions. | Distinguish capture legality, public movement/marker events, change of control and actual composition. Section 21.5 does not expressly enumerate what hidden contents become visible to the captor; do not fabricate full disclosure or deny information needed for an authorized captured-vehicle action without a separate ruling. |
| Destroyed tank capture | §§14.46, 14.51–.53 mark destroyed tanks and permit capture of enemy destroyed tanks when the stated entry/occupancy requirements hold, with a choice to eliminate permanently or consider captured. §14.53 says captured tanks **may be moved three hexes** at capture. | This is distinct from §21.52's adjacency trigger for breakdowns. Reading “three hexes” as **up to** three is plausible but not explicit as in §21.55's maximum-three wording; resolve shorter relocation separately. Receipt is not proof of general public disclosure of contents. Destroyed non-tank vehicles are removed immediately under §14.51 and should not be invented as repairable destroyed-tank lots. |
| Towing / split marker | §21.63 permits towing broken-down and destroyed-tank markers. Its p. 45 addendum expressly permits towing any portion of the **broken-down vehicles**; when only some are towed the player adds a marker and adjusts the records. | Authoritative state must conserve constituent lots through split and movement. Partial splitting of a destroyed-tank marker requires separate authority or a selected interpretation. The source does not say the new marker's composition or origin becomes public; visible marker changes and secret content are separate. |
| Repair | §§22.11–.14 allow a player to attempt repair of controlled eligible broken-down vehicles and destroyed tanks, subject to phase, control and other restrictions. §§22.23–.25 and 22.33–.34 use vehicle categories/types; §22.5 treats repaired vehicles as replacements. | Authority needs actual type, control, amounts, attempts, RNG and result to adjudicate, including captured vehicles separately where required. The rules inspected do not prescribe opponent disclosure of every chosen vehicle, result or stock entry; don't derive it from a public repair event. Exact eligible observer disclosures remain a separate policy gate. |

This review did not identify the complete abandoned-vehicle capture procedure, so keep it unsupported rather than inventing a trigger or revelation. |

## Consequences and tests

1. Two authoritative histories with identical authorized observations and outward actions, but different hidden models, counts and cargo, should produce equal unauthorized observation, action availability, error shape and event metadata until a separately authorized disclosure. A partial tow can visibly create a second counter, so different outward actions need not produce identical event streams. Do not place true lot details in public IDs, event count/order or intelligence-plane requests.
2. Record previously authorized combat disclosures as observations, not a truth mirror. Test identical known participant types with different hidden loss allocations; do not publish which TOE points were destroyed solely from §3.6 combat reporting.
3. Exercise breakdown capture with valid versus forbidden adjacency, protective unit, friendly-controlled hex and impassable side. Exercise destroyed tanks separately with actual entry/occupancy and captor choice. Do not assert content disclosure at either trigger until a sourced or versioned visibility policy exists.
4. Split and tow part of a mixed **broken-down** marker; conserve per-type quantities and assigned ownership in authoritative replay, adding a marker as the addendum states. A partial split of a destroyed-tank marker is unsupported until separately sourced or ruled. Compare views under identical authorized outward histories; no duplicate loss or repair on retry. Separately resolve any revelation required to choose an authorized captured type.
5. Repair controlled friendly and captured lots separately, enforcing phase and applicable facility/field restrictions. Repair failure, junking, return as replacements and towing order must not create unexplained public model/count disclosures. Abandoned-marker capture is a separate unsupported case until its own source procedure is verified.

The Land-only rule §32.55 says **Motorization Points** do not break down; that does not itself establish that other vehicles never do, or apply detailed truck/cargo representations unchanged in every mode. Scenario reachability, capture-side-specific information access, actual starting markers and all exceptions require separate evidence. These are conditional tests, not executed gameplay.
