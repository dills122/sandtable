# Loaded fuel mode precedence: source review

**Disposition: Revise decision vector 4.** Visual source review supports the
plan's mode boundaries and conditional treatment of precedence. One omitted
rule creates a distinct way to fund an air shipment without deciding whether
§32.37 permits direct split payment.

## Finding

Land §32.15 permits supplies to be rearranged at no cost among Supply Units
occupying the same hex during the Organization Phase. Section 32.37 places air
transport in that phase. Thus two origin Supply Units each holding less than
12 Fuel may be able to consolidate 12 in one unit before prepayment, subject
to capacity, phase ordering, stock and other applicable conditions. This is
not evidence that §32.37 permits payment directly from multiple units. Vector
4 should distinguish direct split payment (unresolved) from a separate
consolidation-then-payment case; preserve uncertainty about any ordering
constraint not established by these passages.

## Checked conclusions

- Scenario §59.62 enables the following initial setups when Logistics is
  abstracted with or without Air. Section 59.66(B) establishes optional
  carried Ammo or Fuel, first consumption, a Supply Unit draw restriction,
  no transfer to a Supply Unit, and the qualified detachment restriction.
  Its explicit duration note addresses detachment; it does not independently
  settle duration of the draw restriction.
- Land §32.0 applies with both Air and Logistics absent. Section 32.11's
  broad exclusion and §32.23's Supply Unit source compete with §59.66(B);
  these passages do not expressly settle precedence. The proposed special
  case remains a candidate, and the Air-played mode requires separate rules.
- Section 32.16 governs eligible combat-unit draw and its half-CPA route;
  §32.37 instead requires the owning player to prepay 12 Fuel from an origin
  Supply Unit. Carried cargo is not established as payment. Direct splitting,
  self-payment, zero-balance removal and subsequent shipment remain separate
  questions; aggregate stock alone does not prove legality.
