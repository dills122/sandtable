# §32.6 convoy census: independent source review

**Disposition:** No material transcription error found in the [census](2026-11-18-graziani-convoy-section-census.md). Its six numbered cases, two §32.65 branches and limited claims about coverage, 5% and reachability are supported. Two precision points below should remain explicit when turning the census into a rule.

- **§§32.61–.64:** Land p. 44 prints Supply Units, Motorization Points and Replacement Points; no tonnage limitation; a per-lane, per-Naval Convoy Stage maximum of three Supply Units and 20 Motorization Points; and separate Game-Turn Replacement Pool limits. Commonwealth may attack each arriving convoy, regardless of map weather, with sequential dice after the lane and division lookup. None of these states an actual arrival, cargo, choice or result.
- **§32.65:** Branch A expressly addresses 10%, applies only to Motorization and Replacement Points, and rounds them down and up respectively. B addresses 20% or more, rounds down, and has the awkward printed minimum “at least one point of each and Supply Unit”; the census correctly leaves its interpretation open. The following nationality-grouping sentence and tank example are printed after B, but the **example itself explicitly uses a 10% loss**, not 20% or more. Avoid attaching the grouping/example exclusively to the B threshold merely because of its placement; its application and grouping remain a separate question.
- **§32.66:** Axis p. 34 has six route columns and ten bomb-point rows. Its qualifying division is non-shell and east of or in the same east–west hexrow as at least one other non-shell Allied division; the census preserves direction and the unresolved “beyond which” threshold. Land §32.64 calls it a qualifying **Commonwealth** division, while the chart key says **Allied**. The census quotes the chart's Allied wording; preserve this wording difference when selecting the eligible population instead of treating the two labels as automatically identical. No actual qualifying pair, route or snapshot follows from the printed key.
- **5% and errata:** Common chart §41.5 prints 5% at Axis Naval Convoy, Bomb Points 21–40, ordered dice 55–64. Neither §32.65 A nor B explicitly gives a 5% branch; existence of the cell does not prove an eligible attack or a rounding policy. September errata §41.5 changes Barrage heading placement and a result label, not that named Bomb Points cell; §41.67 describes cargo allocation under detailed Air rules, while §§56.25 and 56.29 address tonnage staging and optional letter ordering. Their abstract-mode application remains a scoped question. Numbered errata search is not exhaustive proof of no indirect correction.
