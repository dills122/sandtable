# AR-06: arriving first-line trucks and Cairo stacking

**Result:** A specific source cross-check constrains, but does not certify, reinforcement placement. The Commonwealth schedule lists trucks with GT5 and GT6 brigades and identifies listed trucks generally as first-line attached trucks. Land §9.29 distinguishes attached trucks from unattached convoys for road-space stacking; however, the [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-ledger-b-source-next-review.md) confirms §32.9 waives §9 road/track stacking-point restrictions in Land-only play. The §9.29 distinction must not be applied as a Land-only road-space discriminator. Ordinary hex capacity, organization, truck conversion and placement remain separate questions.

## Bounded original-source comparison

I visually inspected the [Commonwealth chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF pp. 3–4, §4.43a; and [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF pp. 16–17, §§9.0, 9.12, 9.14–.16, 9.21, 9.25 and 9.29–.33. The source-specific cross-check is the relationship of the schedule's attached-truck key to §9.29; previous [arrival placement review](2026-11-17-graziani-organization-batch-followup-review.md) already checked the schedule's Cairo and parent-placement text. This is not a new edition or an independent review of that earlier work.

| Source | Reading and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Chart §4.43a p. 3 | GT5 stage 1 lists 7 Indian Brigade with 5 light and 20 medium trucks; GT6 stage 3 lists 6 NZ Brigade with 25 light, 85 medium and 5 heavy. These are schedule entries, not proof that arrivals occurred or a resolution of the separate 6 NZ date conflict. |
| Chart p. 4 key | Listed trucks are first-line (attached) and may be freely divided amongst units unless otherwise indicated. The same page directs reinforcements to Cairo unless specified otherwise; co-location and separation are subject to its parent and stacking terms. It does not name a chosen Cairo hex or prove a legal distribution. |
| Land §9.29 p. 17 | Attached first-line trucks do not count toward organizational size **or road-space stacking limits**; unattached truck convoys in a hex do not count against stacking limits **except** those concerning road space. This distinction concerns the stated categories and does not settle every possible detachment or conversion. |
| Land §§9.12 and 9.21 p. 17 | An HQ with no attached combat units has zero stacking points; a division's stacking representation depends on units attached to, not merely stacked with, its HQ. Thus unit attachment and current composition still matter; source does not permit counting every listed constituent independently without evaluating parent representation. |
| Land §§9.16 and 9.25 p. 17 | Pure AA/Flak units stack freely in major cities. A separate rule generally limits unattached/unassigned zero-stacking companies/batteries to five in a hex, with no numerical limit for those units in a major-city hex. These are category-specific exceptions, not proof that a particular arrival qualifies or that all stacking restrictions disappear. |

The originally proposed attached-versus-convoy road-space comparison is not a Land-only discriminator because of §32.9. A mode-specific check may compare these categories under detailed rules separately, while testing ordinary hex capacity and organizational composition under the Land-only waiver. Cairo membership, actual current stacks, parent choices, other arrival rules, chart terrain limits, timing and applicable errata remain gates; no numeric capacity or accepted placement follows from §9.29 alone.
