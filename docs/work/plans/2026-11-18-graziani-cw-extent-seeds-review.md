## Findings

- **Off-map air candidate set needs an explicit warning.** §60.42 permits placement at Commonwealth North African air facilities within capacity; §60.5 includes off-map airfields Abu Seier, Deversoir, Fayid, Ismailia and Kabrit, and off-map Port Said flying boat basin, in addition to on-map facilities. Initial control is described by country, so eligibility and facility type still need checking; this is not proof of actual occupancy at each facility. Tripoli/Tunisia boxes are also listed but initially Italian, not automatic Commonwealth candidates. A geographic inventory must not silently restrict the §60.42 candidate set to map hexes.
- **Repair facilities omitted from supply geography.** §60.44 places the Commonwealth major repair facility at Alexandria and a temporary facility at Mersa Matruh. These are distinct fixed facility seeds, not extra land units or supply dumps. Include if inventory purports to cover all initial logistical facilities; otherwise state exclusion.

## Checked scope

- §60.41: all eleven fixed land-location rows and four flexible location rows appear in the inventory, including detached/attached qualifications and broken-down tanks at Alexandria. No off-map land deployment box appears in §60.41. First-line truck numbers are deliberately deferred to linked motorization review; not a complete truck-by-row transcription here.
- §60.22 starts GT1/OpStage 1 and ends at conclusion of GT6/OpStage 3. Chart §4.43a GT1–6 entries, stages and explicit trucks agree; none bears `WD` or `Rtn`. Default Cairo entry and conditional separate-hex/stacking language agree. This does not exclude withdrawal, movement, removal, or alternative timing under other rules or sources.
- §60.43 placement categories and quantities match. §60.44 detailed supply geographic placements and §60.92(C) Land-only replacement locations and grouping match; the two profiles must not be combined. §60.93(C) prints `60.82 “C”`, as flagged, and adds two regular units among Commonwealth air facilities. No resolution of that pointer inferred here. §60.92(A) also changes motorization conversion; linked review is needed for its effect.
- §60.45 fleet and §60.46 Malta forces are outside the reviewed initial land/supply inventory; §60.42 air candidate description is not an exhaustive fleet or Malta inventory. Scenario §60.5 lists air facilities, including off-map facilities, and should not be read as proof all are initially Commonwealth-controlled or eligible for every aircraft.
