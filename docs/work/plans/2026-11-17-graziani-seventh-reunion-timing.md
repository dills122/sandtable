# 7th Armoured reunion: earliest conditional timing

**Date:** November 17, 2026. **Finding:** Under the reviewed default setup and direct GT1 Operations Stage 1 entry, 1 RTR cannot already be attached to 7th Armoured Brigade at the initial snapshot. If a legal in-stage move brings the assigned battalion and its parent together during OpStage 1, Land §19.41 allows their immediate attachment; the next Naval Convoy Arrival Phase is GT1 OpStage 2. This is an earliest **conditional sequence opportunity**, not proof that the reunion, a non-shell division, or a 5% convoy result can actually occur then. No complete movement path has been established.

## Movement and formation limits

[Commonwealth chart §4.46a](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 22, gives tank-battalion code `g` a CPA of **25**; OA identifies 1 RTR with that code. The 7th Brigade HQ is code `d`, CPA **20**; with only its code-`g` 8 Hussars attached, the §6.15 lowest-component rule gives a conditional brigade CPA of 20. Other attachments or changes require recomputation. CPA is a stage allowance, not a fixed route radius or proof of unused points. Land §§6.13–.16 permit expenditure beyond CPA at disorganization cost, count spending across both sides' portions of the stage and do not carry unused CP into the next stage. The parent formation's CPA is the lowest of its actual components under §6.15; the rule's 7th Armoured example gives 10 with unmotorized 1 KRRC, which is separately detached at this setup and is not evidence of the present parent's CPA. One CP per participant for attachment must be included along with movement, any reaction or combat, and any earlier actions; no actual GT1 CP expenditure ledger is established here.

A line between C3520 and D3612 is not a route or CP total. The map-sheet seam, terrain and hexside features, usable tracks, weather, motorization, supply, enemy occupancy and ZOC, contact, breakdown, stacking and the moving parent's actual composition remain to be checked. The [C3520 six-side review](2026-11-17-graziani-c3520-six-sides-review.md) bounds only local visual evidence; it does not certify a complete path. Both moving 1 RTR toward the parent and moving the parent toward 1 RTR are source-permitted attachment patterns only if each intervening action is legal. No stage-1 reachability or impossibility is claimed.

At the convoy snapshot, the earlier [candidate](2026-11-17-graziani-seventh-armoured-convoy-witness.md) additionally needs qualifying retained strengths, legally attached 4th and 7th Brigades and a non-shell division; a second qualifying division, the correct westward order statistic and map-row policy; and a real permitted lane-6 arrival, attack and qualifying dice. Seven conditional index changes in that candidate do not establish travel distance, movement cost or an attainable endpoint. Convoy loss interpretation remains separate.

Visually checked same-edition scenario PDF pp. 1–3, Land pp. 12 and 29, and Commonwealth OA p. 6 and chart p. 22. Source hashes are recorded in the linked reviews. No exhaustive setup or route search, independent edition, runtime change or gameplay test.
