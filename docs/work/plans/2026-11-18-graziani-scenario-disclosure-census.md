# Graziani scenario §§59–60: disclosure census

**Result:** The inspected scenario cases supply setup choices, named dummy objects and an initial initiative holder, but no new explicit opponent-facing announcement, secret-choice procedure or general visibility grant was identified in §§59–60. The important interaction is mode-specific: detailed-game dummy **dumps** and Land-only dummy **Supply Units** are separately specified. Their existence or owner choice does not by itself make the real/dummy identity observable to the opponent.

## Method and limits

Visually read [scenario PDF](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf) pp. 1–5 / printed pp. 25–29 through §60.93, including numbered §59.1–.66 and §60.1–.93; the last page also starts §61, outside this census. Searched existing OCR for `secret`, `reveal`, `announce`, `inform`, `tell`, `hidden`, `unknown`, `identify`, `know`, `show`, `disclose`, `dummy`, `choice`, and ownership/placement leads, then checked the listed cases visually. Search terms and OCR can miss synonymous or indirect provisions; the negative is limited to explicit triggers found in these sections and does not settle other Land, Air/Logistics or scenario-group rules. [September errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf) extracted text checked for §§59–60 entries and secret/announce terms; OCR and indirect/later corrections remain possible.
