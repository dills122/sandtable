## Still blocked or already decided

Publication versus pending can itself leak hidden state; fixed deadlines or identical pending text alone do not solve conditional publication. A witness rule does not fill these gaps. See [victory review](2026-11-17-graziani-victory-policy-review.md).
- **Tobruk facility lifecycle:** scenario §60.33 gives a temporary facility; Land §22.31 lists a major facility. Neither establishes coexistence, replacement or transition. Section 24.81's permissive destruction wording leaves actor, trigger and timing unspecified. Control is not presence. Initial temporary facility remains the bounded RUL-GRA-07 profile; later type and destruction need separate choices and event/coverage contracts. See [lifecycle review](2026-11-17-graziani-tobruk-facility-lifecycle-policy-review.md).
- **Convoy and rail admission:** missing cargo, division, threshold, lane and geometry coverage, ordered dice, allocation and atomic recovery block convoy effects. Rail needs complete phase sequencing, track and directional coverage, incoming ZOC and occupancy, stage-wide CP, classification and side-safe observations. Unknown cargo or track is not empty or clear. See [matrix](2026-11-17-graziani-decisions-batch-matrix.md).
- **Destruction timing:** the proposed sub-segment mapping is reviewable but the genuine-cycle commitment, cancellation, actor and resource semantics remain unspecified. Do not infer an executable retry window. See [timing review](2026-11-17-graziani-destruction-timing-policy-review.md).

## Required distinguishing checks after selection

1. **Victory:** same versus split witness; true witness with unrelated unknown; blocked path with unknown alternative versus certified exhaustion; false conjunct plus unknown; replay and paired audience histories including terminalization and later actions.
2. **Convoy:** empty versus unknown cargo; separate versus pooled groups and ceiling versus floor; 5%, 10% and 25%; ambiguous model allocation; interrupted post-draw recovery and duplicate command without reroll or partial delivery.
3. **Rail:** origin and entered hex for both traveler classes, separate combat and non-combat occupants, certified absence and incomplete coverage; ordinary contact/break-off comparison; enemy occupation, track, stage-wide CP, replay and hidden-state pairs.

These are proposed synthetic tests, not executed gameplay tests or historical reachability evidence. No runtime, readiness or ruling ledger changed. Source claims above rely on the linked reviews; this triage did not independently reread scans or search later errata.
