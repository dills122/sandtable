# Tobruk initial abstract supply: redistribution boundary

Scenario §60.92(B) lists three regular Axis Supply Units at **Tobruk C4807**, each initially **15 Ammo / 50 Fuel** in the Land-only replacement setup. Land §32.15 permits rearrangement without cost among Supply Units in the same hex during the Organization Phase, caps each at **40 Ammo / 60 Fuel**, and removes a unit only when both amounts reach zero. The [independent supply review](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-capture-independent.md) previously checked the Tobruk row; the [capture/use review](2026-11-17-graziani-captured-supply-use-review.md) checked §32.15. These are initial source values and a conditional rule interaction, not evidence of a later stock position.

The [independent review](2026-11-18-graziani-axis-supply-source-next-review.md) notes §32.15 also gives 40/60 as an initial amount. The scenario's Land-only replacement entry supplies strong specific evidence for its printed 15/50 values; the interaction is stated explicitly rather than silently discarded.

A discriminating boundary follows if those three units remain friendly and together at a valid Organization Phase, with **no intervening expenditure, other stock, or transfers**. Their combined 45 Ammo / 150 Fuel can be redistributed to `(40,60)`, `(5,60)`, `(0,30)`: each unit stays within both caps, no unit has both values zero, and totals are conserved. Thus a full 40/60 unit is possible under that conditional rearrangement, despite none starting full. Conversely, producing two 40-Ammo units from these three units alone would require 80 Ammo; it cannot be done from 45 without additional stock. The missing 35 cannot be inferred from optional loads, arrival or another dump merely because those rules exist.

This is a proposed source-constrained arithmetic witness, not a certified legal sequence: initial placement and phase timing, ownership, unchanged balances, occupancy and any applicable additional restrictions must be established for a particular state. Section 32.15 authorizes rearrangement in Organization, not an automatic setup rewrite or a free transfer from other hexes. The proposed result does not settle capture, movement, distribution across separate hexes, historical reachability or external supply.
