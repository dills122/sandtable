# Abstract-air origin proximity: Tobruk starting boundary

**Date:** November 18, 2026. Second named Axis origin after the [Bardia starting witness](2026-11-18-graziani-air-proximity-start-witness.md). **No positive Commonwealth proximity witness identified for Tobruk in this bounded check.** This does not establish that no enemy combat unit is within five hexes, that the origin gate passes, or that any transport is legal.

Scenario §60.92(B) places three regular Axis Supply Units at **Tobruk C4807**, each with 15 Ammo and 50 Fuel, in the Land-only replacement setup. Scenario §60.41 places Commonwealth **2nd Scots Guards** and 31st Field Artillery Regiment at **Sidi Barrani C4131**. Infantry is among the combat-unit categories in Land §3.23. Their listed presence is a starting fact, not a certified later phase snapshot. Map C's Tobruk and Sidi Barrani identities and their local printed hex neighborhoods are documented in the [C4807 reading](2026-11-17-graziani-c4807-six-sides.md) and [C4131 reading](2026-11-17-graziani-c4131-six-sides.md), with the first number obscured by Tobruk artwork but supported by the map's location summary and neighboring cells.

Using the ordinary same-sheet printed hex-grid convention, each shared-side step changes the first two-digit row number by at most one. The first number differs by **seven** between C4807 and C4131; at least seven side steps are therefore required, regardless of the other coordinate. If this geometric convention is accepted for §32.37, the listed C4131 infantry alone cannot witness the five-hex origin bar. This is a **lower bound**, not an exact distance or a traversable land route. Obscured vertices, terrain, water and ZOC do not become certified by the arithmetic. No C/D seam is used for this one pair.

The other fixed Commonwealth Map C locations printed in §60.41 include C3926, C3922, C3721, C3520, C3320 and C3020; their first two-digit rows likewise differ from 48 by more than five under the same regular-grid convention. This limited fixed-list comparison is not a sweep of the entire five-hex disk, the opposing order of battle, or future movement. Scenario choices include forces anywhere on Map D or E and city/area alternatives; exact chosen positions, unit attachments, exceptional entry and cross-sheet registration require independent checks. Other actual or later enemy positions could provide a witness. Do not infer an empty radius from the inspected locations or a missing map record.

Land §32.37 tests enemy combat-unit presence within five hexes of the **origin**, independently of destination, and also requires eligible endpoints, 12-Fuel prepayment, an eligible destination HQ, directional limit, available Game-Turn allowance and Organization timing. If an actual surviving enemy combat unit is established at distance five or less, that snapshot supplies a blocking witness; no such witness is claimed here. Other gates and hidden-information handling remain independent. No map traversal, supply path, scenario placement choice or successful air movement is asserted.
