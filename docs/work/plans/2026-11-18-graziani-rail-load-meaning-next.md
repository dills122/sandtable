# Graziani rail load: bounded original-source search

**Disposition:** No inspected official example or limiting wording resolves
whether §32.34's two-unit stage load counts all distinct Supply Units carried
or the maximum simultaneously aboard. Do not select either reading on this
evidence.

## Further source check

- [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf),
  PDF/printed pp. 15–16 §§8.72–.77 and p. 43 §§32.31–.34: the printed
  example in §8.72 concerns movement in opposite directions, with a unit in
  one direction and supplies in the other. It does not show replacement cargo
  along a route. Section 8.77 permits pickup/dropoff subject to stacking and
  direction restrictions, while §32.34 says two real and/or dummy Supply Units
  in one direction as the stage's total load and retains normal rail rules.
  No worked example defines whether an unloaded unit frees one of those two
  supply-unit places. These provisions remain subject to the precedence and
  movement gates in the [earlier source note](2026-11-18-graziani-rail-capacity-source-next.md).
- [Air/Logistics rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_AirGameRules.pdf),
  PDF p. 27 / printed p. 23 §§54.31–.35: detailed logistics has a 1,500-ton
  stage allowance in either direction, permits one kind of supply at a given
  time, and says supplies move from one spot to another and, when unloaded,
  may not be moved that Operations Stage. The last rule constrains **the same
  unloaded supplies** in that system; it does not say whether a different
  cargo can take a freed place or interpret the abstract two-counter total.
  It concerns detailed supplies, not the Land-only §32.34 Supply Unit
  allowance; importing its restriction would need a separate mode-scope and
  precedence decision. No turnover example appears in these cases.
- [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf)
  PDF p. 1 §§8.71 and 8.73 addresses Axis railroad use and reorganization
  points, respectively; p. 2 §32.0 comments on the abstract rules generally.
  Indexed text searches for §8.75, §8.77, §32.34, §54.3 and railroad found no
  correction defining turnover. This is a bounded text search, not proof that
  all editions, later rulings or unindexed corrections lack an answer.

For a discriminating test, A, B and C are distinct Supply Units moved
sequentially in one direction with A dropped before B boards and B dropped
before C boards. Peak aboard is one; distinct units moved total three. Neither
policy's answer certifies legal track, endpoints, stacking, CP, stage order or
phase. Separately define repeated boarding of the *same* unit if ever needed;
a boarding-event cap and a distinct-identity cap are different choices.
