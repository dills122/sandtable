# C0716 starting supply: conditional air-fuel counterexample

**Date:** November 18, 2026. A bounded source consequence for a fixed Graziani setup entry, distinct from the Bardia/Tobruk proximity and Alexandria capacity checks. Scenario §60.92(B) gives the Axis **one regular Supply Unit at C0716, with 20 Ammo and 6 Fuel**. Land §32.37 requires **12 Fuel before air transport**, from a Supply Unit in the origin hex. Consequently, in a snapshot where that listed counter remains at C0716 with its printed 6 Fuel and **no other eligible origin-hex Fuel is present**, the stated payment cannot be met: even aggregate local Fuel is six short. This is a conditional counterexample to treating the existence of an initial Supply Unit as sufficient air-transport fuel, not proof of an actual legal or illegal move in every allowed setup.

The no-other-Fuel premise is not established by the single fixed row. The same §60.92(B) gives the Axis two three-unit dump groups, each unit with 40 Ammo and 50 Fuel; each group is placed in one qualifying Map C Libya hex outside the stated Commonwealth proximity restriction. The text does not expressly require the two groups to occupy different hexes. Whether a dump can actually be placed at C0716 depends on country, opposing positions, distance and other setup rules; none is selected here. Other eligible starting units, rearrangement during Organization under §32.15, and prior events could also alter the origin stock. Conversely, a source in a different hex does not meet §32.37's stated origin-hex wording merely because it is reachable for a different supply purpose. Splitting across counters, self-payment, operation ordering and shortage disposition remain unresolved in the [shortage continuation](2026-11-17-graziani-supply-shortage-continuation.md) and its review.

C0716's map mark is an **oasis**, per [source reading](2026-11-17-graziani-c0716-six-sides.md) and [same-scan review](2026-11-17-graziani-c0716-six-sides-review.md); §32.37 names a major city, village or Tripoli/Tunisia box as endpoint categories. The oasis observation alone does not prove a qualifying village or certify absence of overlapping settlement evidence. Endpoint eligibility remains a separate unresolved gate. The Saharan Det may begin **within three hexes** of C0716 under §60.31, not necessarily in C0716; its formation, the five light trucks in that setup row, and the fixed supply counter are distinct entries. Do not create a counter, pool or co-location from their proximity.

For a future source-boundary check, compare the printed `6 Fuel only` snapshot with a separately validated snapshot having a co-located eligible counter with at least 12 Fuel, holding every other gate unknown or independently satisfied. The latter satisfies the payment amount from one counter but does not prove endpoint, enemy proximity, destination HQ, direction, allowance, timing or movement legality. These are not executed tests.
