# Rail arrival decision input: independent source review

**Disposition:** The [decision input](2026-11-18-graziani-rail-arrival-decision-input.md) correctly separates literal enforcement, an explicit arrival exception, and deferral. Its fixture distinguishes those policies without proving a reachable trip. No inspected rule expressly chooses precedence; the air-transport control is a separate restriction.

- [Land](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf), printed/PDF p. 43 §32.31, permits a Supply Unit to move by land or rail in the Operations Stage in which it arrives. Section 32.34 permits the Commonwealth two real and/or dummy Supply Units in one direction as the stage's total rail load, and says normal rail rules apply. Page 15 §8.73 requires a rail user to start the Stage on a rail hex, with separate CP and enemy-ZOC conditions. None of these clauses expressly states that the arrival permission cancels the start condition or that every arrival satisfies it. The conflict turns on receipt and placement timing; a convention placing a counter at Stage start requires separate support.
- Page 12 §5.2 places Naval Convoy Arrival after Initiative Declaration, Weather and Organization, and before Commonwealth Rail Movement. Arrival's printed list names reinforcements, replacement points and ammunition points; it does not expressly place abstract Commonwealth Supply Unit receipts there. Section 32.42 specifies Commonwealth placement in Cairo and conditional Alexandria placement, but does not by itself identify an OpStage placement phase. The hypothetical post-start, pre-rail receipt is useful for policy comparison; these passages do not establish that it occurs in Graziani or that a suitable destination and route exist.
- Literal rejection of the hypothetical unit, an express exception to its start-location requirement alone, and deferral for unresolved precedence are distinguishable outcomes. The decision table properly retains other rail conditions for the exception. “Unsupported” describes an implementation's unresolved interpretation, not a printed game result. The start-on-rail control tests the location predicate only; it does not prove that the counter qualifies as an arrival or is otherwise eligible.
- Section 32.37 separately places air transport in Organization, bars further movement by the owning player in that Stage, and still permits drawing upon that unit. An air-transported unit is therefore an appropriate negative control for owner rail movement, not proof that all transport is arrival or that every form of involuntary movement is barred. The exception under consideration must not silently override this distinct rule.
