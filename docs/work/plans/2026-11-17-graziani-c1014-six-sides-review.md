# C1014 six sides: independent source review

**Disposition:** The [C1014 source note](2026-11-17-graziani-c1014-six-sides.md) is supported with its stated visibility limits. Four dashed crossings and two one-sided brown bands match the four literal pinned records. The C0914 vertex overlap alone cannot establish another slope. Ink obscures portions of the sides; this is not a complete edge or movement certification.

[Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf) p. 14 §8.35 place the slope splash on the down side. Thus visible splash supports C1014 down and the respective neighbors up, though overprint and shared vertices limit precision. Pinned C1014 hex is `clear`, `village`, `coastal: false`, consistent with the dot and pale interior. No literal side entry is a sparse-data observation, not proof of no hidden feature.
