# Sequential dice: independent Land-source check

Visually checked [Land rules](https://www.spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_LandGameRules.pdf)
printed/PDF pp. 5 and 24, §§3.1 and 15.73(a). The glossary names the large
die as the tens digit. The later example expressly reads a **2 on the large
die and 5 on the small die as 25**, followed by an example using 34. This
supports distinguished die roles; it excludes sorting numerical face values
high-to-low in that example. Section 32.64 separately calls for sequential
dice for the simplified convoy table, supporting the same glossary reading
there. Do not infer a probability distribution without an RNG assumption.

The [common chart](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf)
§41.5 p. 12 confirms the Axis convoy 21–40 bomb-point `55–64` cell is 5%,
and the 201–260 `64–66` cell is 25%. Treating each die as a distinct fair,
independent d6 gives six and three ordered results out of 36, respectively;
source table entry alone does not prove dice fairness, a reachable bomb column
or a legal load. The separate [mode-threshold review](2026-11-17-graziani-convoy-mode-rounding-review.md)
retains the unresolved loss and rounding questions.
