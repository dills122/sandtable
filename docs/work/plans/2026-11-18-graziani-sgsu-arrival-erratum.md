# SGSU arrival erratum: Land-only and training scope

**Finding:** September errata §20.3 removes the Squadron Ground Support Unit (SGSU) entry from the Replacement Point Conversion Chart: SGSUs arrive as desired under Air §34.82 and need no Replacement Points. It does **not** authorize an unconstrained arrival or establish an SGSU in Graziani Land-only. Nor does the checked text make an SGSU a §17.35 combat-battalion instructor or settle replacement-training capacity.

- Common chart §20.3, PDF p. 7, originally lists an SGSU with `1 Inf & Supplies`; errata p. 2 §20.3 says ignore that entry and points to §34.82. Air rules p. 5 §34.82 places arrivals in the Naval Convoy Arrival Phase: Axis in a Tripoli/Tunisia box, Commonwealth in Alexandria/Cairo. Its “as desired” permission is subject to stated restrictions, including replacement delay for enemy-action losses, nationality restrictions, and aircraft-based limits; it is not proof of an actual arrival or unlimited counters. Those restrictions belong to the detailed Air procedure, not an independently established Land-only arrival mechanism.
- Scenario §§60.32 and .42 list 39 Italian and 14 Commonwealth SGSUs respectively as part of initial air strengths, with air facility placement and capacity constraints; §59.35 specifies owned facility and readying-capacity conditions. They are real detailed-air setup evidence, not evidence of a selected facility or an automatic Land-only land-counter deployment. Scenario §60.92 labels the Air-and-Logistics-abstracted option Land Game Only; Land §32.0 sets that mode, and §32.81–.84 removes or modifies specified anti-air units and points. The latter cases do **not** expressly say SGSUs are removed. Prior [Axis setup review](2026-11-17-graziani-axis-flexible-setup-batch.md) treats air placement as detailed-mode evidence; no checked Land-only clause affirmatively imports §34.82 or the §60.32/.42 pools as standalone SGSU arrivals. Carry that as a mode-boundary interpretation, not a quote saying all SGSUs are explicitly removed.
- Land §17.35 requires a qualifying **combat battalion** with designated Basic Morale and at least one-third assigned TOE strength for a unit's training, with specified type and co-location conditions. Air §35.0 calls SGSUs noncombat aircraft-service units; the erratum creates no combat battalion or instructor. Scenario §60.41's 6th Australian Division in training does not establish its instructor or credited stages. Section 20.43 cross-refers replacement training to §17.3, but its application to each replacement class and capacity remains unresolved in the [replacement-training follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-replacement-training-followup.md). Do not convert “no Replacement Points” for SGSUs into a new instructor, free replacement stock, or a training-space exemption.

Scenario §60.22 ends at GT6/OpStage 3. The erratum's absence of a fixed date in the detailed-air arrival system cannot prove that no SGSU can arrive in six turns **when that system is played**, or that one actually arrives; the stated restrictions, aircraft counts, previous losses and player choices would have to be established. In the Land-only profile no specific SGSU or legal independent arrival was established by these sources. This result does not exclude other source-specific provisions or decide every field-repair, air-facility or abstraction interaction.
