# Matruh attachment capacity continuation: independent review

**Disposition:** Revise §59.65 summary before relying on it. Core distinction between listed over-limit formations and supply stacking is supported. Keep six-total versus six-additional reconciliation unresolved; chart counting and modification also remain open. No runtime or setup admission.

## Findings

1. **§59.65 omits a necessary condition.** [Scenario rules](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_Scenarios_DupFromAirRules.pdf), PDF p. 2 / printed p. 26, permit initial supply-unit placement over stacking limitations **only if unavoidable due to scenario distribution instructions**. While over, no additional supply unit may be stacked in that hex; once distribution meets restrictions, normal stacking restrictions apply from then on. Motorization points transporting supply units do not count against hex stacking limits. Current wording “prescribed supply units may remain temporarily over” omits the unavoidable condition and can suggest every prescribed excess is permitted. The conclusion that §59.65 does not authorize formation attachment excess remains correct.
2. **Chart conditions qualify the six and slot arithmetic.** Visual check of [Maximum Attachment Chart §19.5](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsBothPlayers.pdf), PDF p. 6, shows Matruh GT 1–12: six units, no tank, one gun class. Chart defines unit by a full-strength battalion- or company-equivalent counter and permits division- and brigade-equivalent units two non-shell company-equivalents at no cost to maximum, subject to type restrictions; three company-equivalents equal one battalion-equivalent. OA's brigade-level HQ description does not by itself settle brigade-equivalent eligibility. Three explicitly attached battalions are a roster count; “three slots” and “five total attachments” as capacity arithmetic depend on applicable strength, equivalence, nested-unit and counting rules. State this qualification and cross-link [capacity follow-up](2026-11-17-graziani-matruh-capacity-followup.md), which tracks these open questions.

## Confirmed scope

- Scenario §59.2's **listed unit exceeds formation organization limits** paragraph permits that unit to operate normally, including being itself assigned or attached; it prohibits assigning or attaching new subordinates, and prohibits attaching replacements in violation of limits after detachment. This exception requires an actually listed over-limit formation; it does not excuse an excess newly created by free same-hex setup reorganization. The separate same-hex provision limits organization by formation and stacking rules. Continuation captures the distinction; make the listed-excess and no owner-created excess conditions explicit if using it operationally.
- [Matruh OA](https://spigames.net/PDFv10/CNA_ChartsCWPlayer.pdf), PDF p. 10, explicitly says no units may be assigned and up to six attached; [September 1979 errata](https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/ERR_CampaignforNorthAfrica.pdf), PDF pp. 1–2, assigns Buffs and Hampshires to Matruh and explains chart attachments are in addition to normally assigned units. These texts do not expressly reconcile Matruh's specific attachment wording with exceptional assignees. Both six-total-attached and six-additional readings remain candidates, not rulings. Assignment alone proves neither co-location nor present attachment. Scenario §60.41 lists Essex, Durham Light Infantry and South Staffordshires after `Att`, which §59.2 defines as attached but not assigned.

Source check: independently rendered scenario PDF p. 2, Commonwealth OA PDF p. 10, and common chart PDF p. 6; checked errata text and prior linked research. No exhaustive later-errata search, legal-action validation, implementation change, or test run.
