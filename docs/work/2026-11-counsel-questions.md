# Rights and provenance: questions for counsel

Prepared 2026-11-28 for the Beta 0 feasibility phase. **Questions only, not legal advice.**
The owner has selected an engine-only distribution with locally supplied content packs.
The [rights memo](plans/2026-11-25-rights-and-provenance-memo.md) and
[content separation design](plans/2026-11-25-content-pack-separation-design.md)
describe the current provisional position. No public release or content migration is
authorized by this document.

1. Who holds the relevant rights today, and whom should we approach regarding the
   original game, later editions, third-party map redraws, and derivative contributions?
2. In the relevant jurisdictions, how should we distinguish independently written
   mechanics code, individual facts, structured datasets, expressive tables, and
   close paraphrases? What permission is needed for each?
3. What rights, if any, can the downstream CC0 dedication convey for derived map
   data? Which upstream permissions or provenance documents are missing?
4. What restrictions apply to previous acquisition from public scans and new
   acquisition from a lawfully owned physical copy? May a reader create private working images?
5. Does an engine that imports locally transcribed player packs materially change
   risk compared with distributing populated packs? What loader documentation or
   pack-sharing features should be avoided or permitted?
6. What use of the game name, attribution, and non-affiliation wording is appropriate
   in README, NOTICE, package names, site, and announcements?
7. Given existing public Git history, what tip-only remediation, fork/cache handling,
   preservation, notice, or history remediation is appropriate? Which actions should
   wait for explicit owner authorization and advice?
8. Can existing transcriptions, review packets, and conformance fixtures be retained
   in an access-controlled private store? What storage, access, backup, and retention
   controls or deletion obligations apply?
9. What terms and copy-ownership attestations should named testers and independent
   readers sign? Can packs or binaries containing data ever be shared privately?
10. What contributor policy, provenance declarations, and review procedures should
    govern code, synthetic fixtures, proposed data corrections, and incoming PRs?
11. What licence, attribution, warranty, third-party notices, and first-run wording
    should accompany a synthetic-only engine package?
12. What restrictions apply to publishing build artifacts, screenshots, issue
    attachments, saved games, and diagnostics that might reproduce pack data?
13. What takedown response, notification contact, preservation obligations, and
    withdrawal process should the owner establish before announcement or distribution?
14. Should charging, donations, external playtesting, or marketing remain deferred
    pending written clearance, and what evidence would counsel require before
    approving each distribution class?

Ask counsel to identify jurisdiction-specific assumptions, priority actions, and
written release conditions. Keep advice and supporting content in the appropriate
private channel; publish only owner-approved conclusions and metadata.
