# Graziani GT1 Benghazi convoy

This source-runtime slice covers only a sole lane 3 supply convoy in GT1
operation stage 1. The Axis announces the convoy; the Commonwealth may
decline its attack; only then are its supply counters delivered. Mixed lanes,
actual convoy attacks and later arrivals remain pending. The source turn and
Host journal must retain the choice and replay both world successors.

The scenario §60.31 and §60.92B print Benghazi as B4827. The original map,
Air/Logistics §37.42 and important-locations summary identify A4827. The
explicit provisional ruling RUL-GRA-05 adopts A4827 for this runtime slice;
it is not release acceptance of that ruling. The initial source checkpoint
continues to preserve B4827 and its existing bytes. On delivery only, runtime
supply version 2 moves the one existing Benghazi supply counter to A4827 and
adds the arriving counters there, preserving their identities and stocks. It
records the provisional ruling and the original conflict in its canonical
state. Replaying an already delivered convoy or exceeding the five-counter
major-city limit in Land §32.14 is rejected.

Scenario §60.31 places the Axis Benghazi garrison at setup; no land movement
precedes the first operation stage's Naval Convoy Arrival. Scenario §60.7
states that ports begin at their listed efficiency except Tobruk. The general
Land-only Axis convoy rules in §32.61–32.63 determine lane and supply-unit
limits. This slice does not adjudicate the separate Tobruk efficiency conflict.
