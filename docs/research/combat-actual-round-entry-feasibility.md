Decision owner: coordinator/human, under the [canonical Combat plan](../design/combat-cycle-implementation-plan.md) and [REL-AUD-02](2026-10-05-first-release-audit.md).

Cancellation may traverse the remaining steps to the same cycle's Reserve Release, preserving the no-attack meaning.
